## F_n INVERSION ANALYSIS (completed, not fixed)

The quarantine backtest shows 81% WR with F_n <= 1.65. Production F_n median is 1.80 (73.5% above 1.65).

**Root cause is NOT the 252-bar cap.** Tested at 100/252/504/756/1000/unlimited — F_n barely changes. The x_m integrator (A_m=0.98) saturates in ~34 bars regardless.

**Real cause: temporal selection bias.** Quarantine evaluates F_n at every gate boundary across 5 years of history per symbol. Low F_n occurs transiently during integrator warmup. Production only sees the latest snapshot where F_n has long saturated.

**Decision:** F_n gate disabled. Fix exits first. Per-date rolling evaluation (Option A) is the path to closing the gap from 67% toward 81%.
