# Condemned ideas — never rebuild these

Joe has zero tolerance for recycled failures ("you invented it months
ago - it failed then and it will fail again"). Before building any
construction, check it is not one of these wearing new words.

## CONDEMNED (hard rule)
- **The per-stock spring triad**: "energy/compression/release",
  drawdown + quiet + trigger, coil/loaded-spring language — ANY
  per-stock condition checklist IS this idea. Failed months ago,
  failed again 2026-07-31 at daily/hourly/15-min (anti-selective
  intraday). Single-signal quant flattening.
- **Close-of-bar harvests of gate displacements**: per-gate step,
  cycle read (opposing-majority exit), morphology read
  (gates-to-peak) — ALL null-proven under live timing at 3 rungs.
- **Ride-unbounded collection**: falsified at every WR tier (collapse
  tail is unbounded).
- **Quant-signal frames**: conditional-return scans, indicator
  majorities, "training years"/test splits. There is no training —
  only geometric structure over an evaluation period.
- **Descriptor band-mining**: scanning conditional returns on
  descriptor bands then blaming the physics when it fails.
- **Trigram + pooled spellings** (daily law work): falsified.
- **Naive cycle boundary / arc phase morphology / age-conditioned
  yield / global meadow bands**: all falsified, filed in the waypoint.

## What IS proven (build from these)
- Species law: bigram gate-class species predict next-gate direction
  58-69% EVERY year at daily/hourly/15-min under strict timing.
- Herd conditioning (pedigree-cell energy x greed) improves everything
  it touches and replicates cross-rung; the only surviving (modest)
  money edge rides it: herd_kgate_v1.
- The joint field / population physics is the object. Relations
  between stocks, not per-stock scalars.

## Instinct check
If a proposed finder reduces to "stock satisfies conditions A+B+C",
stop — that is the condemned shape regardless of how physical the
vocabulary sounds. Reread the spec and the herd machinery instead.
