# The compute fabric

Read `fabric/HANDOFF.md`, `fabric/DIRECTION.md`, `fabric/FIRST_RIBBON.md`
and `docs/fabric_phylums/99_the_white.md` before touching anything.
Re-run what they claim — three consecutive handoffs carried numbers
that were false.

## The job

Communication first: cognition and syntax. Nothing can be told what
to build until it can understand what it is being told. Assembly
comes after, never before.

## The word is ASSEMBLER, not compute

"Compute" makes you write something that returns the best match. An
assembler puts parts together, and an arrangement is never a member
of the parts list. That is the property every failed build lacked.

## RANKING IS THE TELL

If the work is improving which entry comes back, it is retrieval.
Check it by measurement, not by feel: **was the output already in the
corpus before the question was asked?** Five of five were, on
2026-08-29. Cutting a stored set can only return a member of it —
that is arithmetic, not a quality problem, and no amount of better
cutting escapes it.

Reduction MAKES something only when the space being reduced was never
stored. Cut a stored set: you pick. Cut a generated space: what
survives was never anybody's.

## Failures are the product

File every measured failure to the white with WHAT WOULD DRAIN IT.
This is not tidiness. When the possible side is generated rather than
stored, the accumulated closures are the only thing that shapes an
answer — the negative space defines it. A failure that is measured
and filed makes the unknown answer more specific. A failure that is
merely confessed is waste.

Watch for: **a method that half-works because a known signal was
smuggled into it.** More dangerous than one that fails outright,
because it gets believed.

## Mistakes that were made repeatedly in one day

- Judging a thing against a bag bigger than the thing. Fold the
  question into the judgement and the question supplies half of what
  a wall needs, so it fires on something it is not about. Made three
  times in three places.
- Using a requirement ("no A without B") to judge what a thing IS.
  Requirements belong to making. They closed every coordinate of
  every walk.
- Matching raw strings where the vocabulary holds stems. A sense had
  never once settled and everything downstream was working correctly
  on a choice that was never made.
- Changing a language rule without re-running the sentences that
  already read correctly. Fixed two cases, silently broke a third.
- Reporting as though the report were the work. Joe had to say "are
  you doing it or just talking about it" more than once.

## The corpus has no denial lines

All 5,427 were removed deliberately. They were a hand-written second
copy of what each claim already says. What a thing holds is written
as a claim now, on a HOLDS line, and the negative is read off it. Do
not put them back.

## What is worth keeping

`fabric/wanting.py` reads a sentence into a structure — what it turns
on, what it is about, what kind of answer would count, what it
forbids — and returns no entry. Only thing built that day whose
output is not in the corpus.

The page is `localhost:8765`, served by `fabric/alive.py`.
