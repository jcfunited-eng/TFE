## What this result means

This is **consequence-shaped reciprocal vocal learning**.

The tutor's consequence deterministically selects and preserves Guala's own
body program in the card's causal THING mosaic. After cold restore, the card's
sight can evoke newly synthesized pressure from that program.

This is not a chatbot response, transcript lookup, named vocal profile, audio
replay, score, similarity threshold, probabilistic model, Chi/Atlas identity,
or scripted semantic mapping.

## What this result does not mean

It does **not** yet prove that Guala learned or spoke an intelligible human
word.

The current body program is created before tutor pressure arrives. The
tutor's physical consequence selects that program, but the substrate does not
yet derive or reshape the larynx/tract program from structural correspondence
between:

- the tutor's complete heard acoustic field; and
- Guala's complete self-generated, self-heard acoustic field.

That absent mechanism is **endogenous acoustic correspondence**. It is
separate from the now-implemented lawful external causal shaping.

The truthful distinction is:

- external consequence shaping: implemented and cold-restored;
- sight-to-fresh-body reciprocation: implemented and cold-restored;
- tutor-form imitation: not implemented;
- independently intelligible intended word: not proven;
- broad word recognition, continuous speech, varied speakers, and dialects:
  not proven.

No result may be described as learned human-word speech until emitted pressure
is independently intelligible as the intended form.
