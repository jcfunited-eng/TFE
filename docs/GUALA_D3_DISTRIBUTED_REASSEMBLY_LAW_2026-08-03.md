# Guala D3 distributed cognitive formation and reassembly law

Date: 2026-08-03

Status: canonical D3 implementation law; not yet integrated, deployed, or
production-proven.

## Architecture honesty gate

1. **Requested architecture:** current cognition and learned experience arise
   through the organism's neuronal fractals, mosaics, mosaics-of-mosaics,
   tapestries, tapestries-of-tapestries, and weaves. Recognition, episodic
   recollection, learned structural knowing, active thought, affective
   appraisal, procedural action, and imagination are related but distinct
   causal operations.
2. **Current code reality:** the native path has neuronal fractals and an
   isolated recursive-formation admission boundary, but no integrated
   neocortical, hippocampal, prefrontal, amygdalar, body, motor, and
   whole-organism formation/reassembly transition in production.
3. **Conflict:** yes. Historical lookup, response retrieval, hippocampal
   indexing alone, or calling every recurrence “recall” would all implement the
   wrong architecture.
4. **Mechanisms not extended:** Atlas/Chi lookup, nearest-match or similarity
   scoring, stored answers, replay of a complete original episode,
   region-as-memory-owner, labels-as-meaning, a single recall function, and
   unconditional region participation.
5. **Single exact next item:** connect typed neuronal transitions and bounded
   episode navigation to distributed recursive formation/reassembly while
   retaining each actual physical participant and causal transition.
6. **DSF scope:** every reached neuron continues to carry its complete explicit
   local `D_k`, `M_k`, `R_rev_k`, `U_star_k`, `C_k`, `P_k`, and `B_k`
   authority.
7. **Field loss:** none. Episode navigation, neuronal fields, Krimelack state,
   sparse coupling, recursive formations, working continuation, body state,
   affect, and action consequences remain separate causal structures.

## Governing law

Reassembly is a broad physical recurrence of previously formed structure.
Episodic recollection is only one evidenced use of reassembly. Current
recognition, learned structural knowing, active thought, affective appraisal,
procedural activity, and imagination must not be misreported as episodic
memory.

The operation is determined by what physically happened, not by a developer
label:

| Evidenced operation | Required physical distinction | What it is not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Perceptual recognition | A current sensory or body cause propagates through sparse neuronal coupling and completes a previously formed neocortical mosaic or larger formation. | Retrieval of a past episode. |
| Episodic recollection | A partial current cue or endogenous cause reaches a particular retained episode/relationship lineage through hippocampal navigation and physically reinstates applicable distributed formation, context, body, sensory, and affective structure. | An index result, familiarity alone, or replay of the original episode. |
| Learned structural knowing | Previously formed relationships among mosaics recur and causally affect the current settlement without reconstructing a particular learning episode. | A stored fact, answer table, semantic label, or claim of episodic recall. |
| Active thought | Current formations are maintained, related, reordered, extended, or prepared across actual neocortical and, where required, prefrontal transitions. | Recall merely because prior learning contributes. |
| Affective appraisal | Amygdalar, interoceptive, fluid, and body configurations physically contribute learned salience, arousal, safety/threat, or approach/avoidance relations to the current settlement. | An emotion label, score, owner, or memory by itself. |
| Procedural or embodied activity | Motor and body formations causally produce coordination or action and receive truthful sensed consequences. | A narrated instruction or required episodic reconstruction. |
| Imagination, simulation, or planning | Existing formations are causally recombined under explicit simulated or counterfactual provenance and remain distinguishable from lived experience. | A fabricated observation or stored script. |

Several operations may participate in one moment. A conversation can include
auditory recognition, learned word relationships, active contextual thought,
episodic recollection of an earlier exchange, affective appraisal, and speech
enactment without reducing them to one recall event.

## Conditional specialized participation

- **Neocortical configurations** physically form and reassemble the distributed
  neuronal-fractal, mosaic, mosaic-of-mosaics, and tapestry structures used by
  this hierarchy.
- **Hippocampal configurations** preserve and navigate episodic and relational
  lineage. They are required for episodic or relational-navigation claims, but
  a returned address or page is not cognition and hippocampal participation is
  not required for every recognition or thought.
- **Prefrontal configurations** maintain reached structure across current
  context, ordered continuation, and prepared action. They are required when
  those functions are claimed, not universally.
- **Amygdalar configurations** contribute learned affective significance,
  safety/threat and approach/avoidance relations, bodily arousal, and physical
  modulation of consolidation and reassembly. They are required with the
  relevant body/interoceptive path when affective participation is claimed.
  They do not own memory, assign an emotion label, or select a result.
- **Sensory, body, interoceptive, fluid, motor, and articulatory
  configurations** participate whenever their physical structures contribute
  to the current operation or claimed consequence.

These names identify specialized physical configurations of one continuously
connected organism. They are not separate programs, databases, owners, or
semantic services. No configuration stores, remembers, thinks, or reconstructs
the whole by itself.

## General causal sequence

Every admitted operation must preserve the applicable portion of this sequence:

```text
truthful current sensory/body cause or endogenous cause
    -> reached current neuronal configuration
    -> bounded sparse neuronal and Krimelack propagation
    -> neocortical formation completion, recurrence, relation, or extension
    -> hippocampal episode/relationship navigation only for an episodic claim
    -> prefrontal maintenance or ordering only when physically involved
    -> amygdalar/affective and bodily participation only when physically involved
    -> relevant sensory/body/fluid/motor/articulatory continuation
    -> changed current organism settlement
    -> any truthful thought, expression, action, or sensed consequence
```

The exact participating configurations are event-dependent. Every claimed
participant must be physically mounted and causally evidenced. No participant
may be treated as the owner of cognition or memory.

## Common admission requirements

Every formation/reassembly claim is admissible only when:

1. its current sensory, body, or endogenous cause is authenticated;
2. its actual predecessor, physical transition, and successor neuronal
   structures are retained;
3. sparse nonquiescent physical propagation reaches the claimed formation;
4. each claimed specialized configuration cites its actual participating
   neuronal lineages and transitions;
5. the current organism settlement changes because of that propagation;
6. provenance remains observed, self-sensed, reassembled, simulated,
   counterfactual, endogenous, self-enacted, or unresolved as physically
   warranted; and
7. the causal evidence survives bounded persistence and restart.

The canonical admission body retains immediate member receipts without copying
all descendants. It also retains `ordered_member_receipts` as a separate exact
sequence. That sequence is empty for Mosaic, MosaicOfMosaics, and Weave, and is
the complete admitted order for Tapestry and TapestryOfTapestries. Order may not
be reconstructed from a set, relation iteration order, database order, or a
later convenience rule.

An episodic-recollection claim additionally requires that:

1. the current cue or endogenous cause is later than the retained episode;
2. the cue is partial and the complete original episode was not replayed or
   injected;
3. hippocampal navigation resolves the typed episode or relationship lineage
   without being treated as the recollection;
4. current propagation reinstates the applicable distributed formation; and
5. any claimed contextual, affective, body, sensory, or motor contribution is
   physically re-entered rather than inferred from a label.

## Rejection requirements

The implementation must reject:

- an episode address, posting list, search result, database row, or generated
  response presented as cognition;
- pattern completion presented as episodic recollection without episode and
  hippocampal evidence;
- hippocampal navigation presented as recollection without neocortical
  formation reassembly;
- learned structural knowing forced to reconstruct an arbitrary source episode;
- active thought presented as memory merely because learned formations recur;
- prefrontal maintenance of developer-supplied text or a stored answer;
- an amygdalar response or emotion/threat label presented as memory or meaning;
- an affective claim without resolved amygdalar, bodily/interoceptive, and
  neuronal transition evidence;
- a similarity winner, score threshold, nearest stored item, or scripted label;
- replay of a complete original sensory episode presented as recall;
- a provenance label without predecessor/transition/successor evidence;
- an instantaneous directed-cycle fixture substituted for lived recurrent
  propagation; and
- a counter increment, UI event, or generated response without the distributed
  causal chain.

## Bounded operation

Formation and reassembly follow only the currently reached sparse frontier and
explicitly resolved bounded pages. Work is admitted from the physical CPU/RAM
envelope before transition and is proportional to the reached structure, not
total organism age, neuron population, or retained history. The envelope is a
resource-derived admission boundary, not a heuristic cognitive cap. If the
required physical evidence or resources are unavailable, the organism remains
truthfully unresolved; it does not scan all memory, fabricate a result, or fall
back to a chatbot.

## Production proof

Persisted formation evidence must first pass cold proof: resolve the addressed
canonical bytes, reject oversized counts before allocation, reject unknown tags
and trailing bytes, reconstruct the exact Formation, Relation, Recurrence,
PartialCue, Durability, and Consequence bodies, and semantically re-admit every
reachable formation from those retained references. A decoded body whose
re-admission changes any canonical byte is rejected. The corrected formation
body uses its own canonical type tag; there is no decoder for the earlier body
that omitted ordered-member receipts.

A persisted runtime leaf-mosaic body additionally replays its formation,
resolved episode address and certificate, origin component, recurrent component,
and exact episode-resolution requirement. Cold inspection rejects any changed
reference, invalid component authority, membership drift, non-advancing
recurrence generation, or formation/component lineage mismatch.

Production proof is operation-specific:

- recognition requires a current partial sensory/body cause to complete and
  change a previously formed structure;
- episodic recollection requires a later partial cue, bounded hippocampal
  navigation, distributed physical reinstatement, and changed current state;
- learned structural knowing and active thought require causal formation
  relation/continuation without a fabricated episode claim;
- affective participation requires the applicable amygdalar and body path;
- procedural or creative enactment requires an internally caused preparation,
  body/world consequence, truthful sensed re-entry, and later reuse; and
- every claim must survive durable restart.

Unit tests, index queries, formation records, counters, UI labels, or generated
language alone prove none of these operations.
