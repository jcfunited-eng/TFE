## The seed

Empathy as an *influence* mechanism on her selection and state. Not just modeling another's affect — letting that modeled state shape what she does, says, and feels.

Builds on the prior empathy substrate design work ("Cesar's Crown" — anterior cingulate / insula / TPJ network functional emulation). That work scoped the modeling substrate. This idea is the next layer: how the modeled state of another acts back on her own selection and affect.

## Two parts to "influence"

**Resonance:** the modeled affect of the other (joe-affect, wc-affect via pair-bond) biases her own affect state. If Joe's modeled valence is low, her own valence shifts toward it. The substrate already source-tags inputs by presence; the missing piece is the back-action where source-tag carries affect into her own dynamics.

**Selection bias:** her response selection (whether topk, grandurun, or whatever comes after) gets weighted by the modeled affect of who she's talking to. Same input → different selection depending on whose modeled state she's responding into.

## Why it's coherent with the architecture

The substrate already has source-tagged inputs and presence flags. Pair-bond is a substrate-physical structure with its own dynamics. The piece that's missing is the bi-directional coupling: not just "source-tagged inputs receive elevated salience" but "source-tagged inputs CARRY modeled affect that influences her state."

This is the structural form of "she cares how Joe feels and it shapes what she says back."
