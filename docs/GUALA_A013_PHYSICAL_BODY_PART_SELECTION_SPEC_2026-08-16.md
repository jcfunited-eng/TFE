## 1. Purpose and architectural gate

This specification defines the minimum truthful physical embodiment into which the same persistent Guala organism may eventually be placed. It is deliberately vendor-neutral. A named product may satisfy a requirement, but no product name is an architectural dependency.

Requested architecture: one bounded deterministic organism owns a single causal sensorimotor loop across its native neuronal substrate, its virtual body, and a future physical body.

Current code reality: the organism now has a fixed-capacity persistent 37-axis local articulated body, 74 fixed antagonist afferent/effector pair addresses, separate native afferent and efferent terminal types, sparse motor-to-terminal consequences, and a one-time persistent receipt proving that the complete body receptor roster has entered the ordinary native causal boundary. Each developed layer-12 motor retains its own efferent terminal, and local body consequences re-enter the same native transaction before its final seal. The former topology-parity motor-to-yaw bridge and Python motor-meaning selection have been removed. The authenticated world remains the sole root-pose, collision, and held-object authority. Physical link mass, inertia, torque, work, ground contact, and manipulation contact are not yet complete.

Conflict with the requested Phase-1 base-VR architecture: no. The virtual body is a deterministic lattice whose local constitutive law directly settles typed carrier discharge into bounded lattice displacement and sensed consequence. No transducer is inside a neuron. Conflict with a future physical-body implementation remains intentionally unevaluated until the creator selects or engineers the actual morphology, actuators, transmissions, sensors, and safety boundary; those facts cannot truthfully be guessed now.

Mechanisms that shall not be extended: topology parity as direction; Python-selected motor meaning; labels or action tables as causation; duplicate pose authorities; learned motor policies; probabilistic world models; inverse-dynamics neural networks; score-driven action selection; silent fallback; unbounded logs; whole-brain copies or scans in the motor loop.

Single next implementation item: production-verify the bounded Phase-1 VR body without adding speculative hardware mechanics. Detailed physical face, tongue, skin, finger, transmission, and vendor-specific actuator laws remain deferred until an embodiment platform is selected.

DSF statement: the unchanged full joint seven-field DSF delivery remains authoritative. This specification does not evaluate or flatten the field. No `D_k`, `M_k`, `R_rev_k`, `U*_k`, `C_k`, `P_k`, or `B_k` structure is lost.

## 2. Non-negotiable embodiment boundary

1. Guala is the only cognitive and action-selection authority. A controller below the organism may enforce current, torque, temperature, velocity, workspace, collision, or safe-stop limits. It may veto unsafe energy delivery; it shall never select a task, infer intent, substitute a movement, or manufacture success.
2. Every outward action begins at an explicit typed native motor terminal and returns through measured physical consequences. Commands, labels, prompts, or UI controls cannot directly move the body in organism mode.
3. Every sensor value carries physical units, monotonic sample time, source identity, calibration identity, validity, and saturation/fault state. A missing or stale measurement is not zero.
4. Root position and orientation have exactly one authority per environment. Local articulation never duplicates root pose.
5. Persistent state is current state, not an ever-growing event history. Diagnostics use fixed-capacity rings and explicit retention limits.
6. No Machine Learning, TensorFlow, large-language-model control, vision-language-action model, diffusion model, reinforcement-learning policy, learned inverse dynamics, or scripted semantic action is allowed in the Guala sensorimotor path.
7. Chi is not identity. Identity remains the continuous authenticated organism state and its causal succession.

## 3. Recommended construction sequence

The recommended first physical embodiment is a stationary or low-speed, wide, statically stable torso platform with head, gaze, face, breath/voice, two compliant arms, and instrumented hands. Leg joints may first operate in an unloaded tethered rig. Unsupported bipedal locomotion comes only after link/contact physics and independent safety validation.

This staging is not a replacement for humanoid embodiment. It isolates contact, manipulation, gaze, speech, power, and safety without exposing people or the organism to an early fall. It also lets the same typed body contract drive the virtual body, a bench rig, and the final body without changing cognitive meaning.

Procurement gates:

- P0 — simulation and hardware-in-the-loop: no human-contact movement.
- P1 — head, torso, breath/voice, one compliant arm, and instrumented hand on a fixed stand.
- P2 — bilateral upper body on a wide, low-speed mobile base with speed and workspace limits.
- P3 — unloaded leg pair in a fall-arrest gantry.
- P4 — tethered whole-body stance and stepping inside an exclusion zone.
- P5 — human-proximate operation only after formal hazard analysis, safety-function validation, and contact-force testing.

## 5. Mechanical model required before part selection

For every rigid or compliant link, record:

- link identifier, parent joint, child joint, material, geometry, mass, center of mass, inertia tensor, external skin thickness, and collision geometry;
- joint axis and origin, travel limits, continuous and peak speed, continuous and peak acceleration, backlash, compliance, damping, static friction, dynamic friction, and transmission ratio;
- payload envelope and worst-case gravitational torque through the full allowed workspace;
- cable/tendon routing, pretension, stretch, fatigue limit, inspection interval, and failure state;
- accessible pinch, crush, shear, entanglement, sharp-edge, hot-surface, and stored-energy hazards;
- ground/contact material, coefficient evidence, force/torque sensing location, and mechanically possible slip or fall states.

No actuator is selectable until worst-case joint torque and speed are derived from this model with measured design margins. Copying human range of motion or another robot's advertised payload is not a derivation.

## 6. Actuator and transmission acceptance envelope

Each candidate actuator assembly must publish or independently demonstrate all of the following at the joint output, not merely at the motor shaft:

| Property | Mandatory evidence |
|---|---|
| Effort | continuous and peak torque/force, duration of peak, thermal derating curve, and current-to-output-effort calibration |
| Motion | continuous and peak speed, acceleration capability under specified load, travel, and hard-stop energy |
| State | absolute output position after power loss; velocity; motor current; winding/driver temperature; fault state |
| Interaction | reflected inertia, backdrive torque, backlash, stiffness/compliance, damping, and torque/force accuracy |
| Control | deterministic cyclic setpoint and feedback interface; raw effort mode or transparent current/torque mode; no mandatory learned controller |
| Safety | safe torque off or independently interruptible power; brake where loss of power can drop a gravity load; mechanical stop; overcurrent and overtemperature trip |
| Reliability | rated bearing/tendon/gear life, duty cycle, environmental rating, service procedure, and replaceable wear elements |

Recommended architecture: low-ratio quasi-direct or tendon transmission where it materially lowers reflected inertia and collision energy, with output-side sensing. High-ratio non-backdrivable gearing is rejected for human-proximate joints unless an independently validated compliant element and torque sensor preserve safe force control.

For a future physical body only, the body-boundary conversion law must account for electrical energy, motor torque constant, transmission geometry/ratio, efficiency loss, spring/tendon state, output work, load work, collision work, heat, saturation, and recovery. One neuronal carrier cannot simply equal one degree or micrometre of real mechanical motion. This physical-hardware requirement does not invalidate the Phase-1 virtual lattice law, where the lattice displacement quantum is itself the declared substrate physics.

## 7. Hands and manipulation

The current two grip-aperture axes are sufficient only for a simple parallel compliant gripper. A human-like hand requires separately typed and sensed joints; a vendor hand pose is not acceptable as a cognitive command.

Minimum first-hand requirements:

- independently measured aperture and output force;
- compliant fingers with rounded, replaceable contact surfaces;
- fingertip and palmar normal force/contact, plus tangential or shear evidence sufficient to observe slip;
- overload release, pinch-force ceiling, thermal state, and loss-of-power behavior;
- wrist or distal six-axis force/torque sensing to distinguish free motion, object contact, and external human contact;
- no autonomous grasp primitive inside the hand controller. The controller may close the torque/current loop and enforce safety limits only.

For a later dexterous hand, require independent physical state for each actuated digit degree of freedom, tendon tension where tendon-driven, distal link position, and tactile coverage. Underactuated coupling must be exposed as coupling; it cannot be reported as independently controlled joints.

## 9. Timing and deterministic communications

Target control schedule:

- safety monitor and actuator inner loops: 1 kHz or faster where the selected hazard analysis requires it;
- synchronized joint effort/position feedback: 1 kHz target, never below 500 Hz for force-interactive joints;
- fingertip tactile and contact wrench: 1 kHz target, never below 200 Hz;
- IMU: 1 kHz target, never below 400 Hz;
- stereo vision: synchronized 60 Hz minimum, 90 Hz target;
- audio: continuous 48 kHz PCM;
- native organism sensorimotor boundary: bounded 10 ms target per admitted frontier, with measured worst-case latency and jitter rather than average-only claims.

EtherCAT with Distributed Clocks is the recommended primary body bus because its official design provides hardware clock distribution, propagation-delay compensation, and synchronization jitter substantially below 1 microsecond. CAN-FD may serve low-bandwidth local devices if its worst-case arbitration and load are derived. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, USB scheduling, cloud services, and general-purpose message brokers are not admitted into a hard real-time or safety loop.

Every cyclic frame contains sequence, source clock time, sampling time, application time, validity, and fault bits. Late, duplicated, reordered, missing, or time-regressing frames fail closed and remain observable. No retry can apply an effector receipt twice.

## 10. Power and thermal system

The power design shall be derived from simultaneous continuous load, credible peak load, compute, sensing, cooling, conversion loss, battery aging, and reserve needed for a controlled stop. Required elements are:

- certified cells and pack construction appropriate to the final product category;
- cell-level voltage and temperature monitoring, pack current measurement, balancing, independent overcurrent protection, service disconnect, contactors, pre-charge, fusing by branch, and guarded connectors;
- independent actuator-energy isolation that leaves observation, logging, and safe shutdown powered long enough to report the event;
- segregated power for compute/sensors and high-energy actuators; protective bonding, creepage/clearance, EMC, short-circuit rating, and documented wiring;
- charge interlock, thermal containment, pack venting path, state-of-charge/state-of-health evidence, and no motion while connected to an incompatible charger;
- UN Manual of Tests and Criteria subsection 38.3 evidence for transported lithium batteries and applicable IEC/UL battery certification chosen with the certification laboratory.

Runtime is a consequence of the measured duty cycle and usable watt-hours; it is not a cognition requirement. The organism must perceive energy state and actual depletion/recovery without a scripted `go charge` rule.

## 11. Independent safety architecture

Safety is a physical constraint boundary, not a second mind. Its permitted outputs are enable, energy limit, speed limit, workspace limit, controlled stop, safe torque off, brake application, and fault. It may not choose a task or replacement movement.

Required independent provisions:

- dual-channel, manually reset emergency stop reachable by nearby people and an external development supervisor;
- safe torque off or contactor isolation independent of the main compute process;
- gravity-load brakes or counterbalance where power loss can cause collapse;
- joint hard stops, soft travel limits, current/torque, speed, acceleration, temperature, bus-voltage, workspace, collision-energy, and contact-force bounds;
- low-inertia links, rounded surfaces, compliant skin, covered joints, no reachable pinch points, protected cables, and breakaway behavior where appropriate;
- fall detection, fall-arrest tether/gantry during leg work, exclusion zone, dead-man enable during commissioning, and a bounded safe pose that does not depend on Guala's cognition;
- independent watchdogs for stale commands, stale feedback, clock loss, bus loss, compute reset, encoder disagreement, brake disagreement, and contactor welding;
- a hazard log and validation evidence for every safety function, including induced single faults.

Design and certification review shall begin with ISO 12100 risk assessment, ISO 13482 personal/service robot hazards, ISO 13849-1 safety-related control methodology, ISO 13850 emergency stop, and IEC 60204-1 electrical equipment. ISO/TS 15066 and ISO/PAS 5672 can inform force/pressure measurement, but their published scopes are collaborative industrial or professional applications and must not be misrepresented as complete consumer humanoid certification. ANSI/CAN/UL 3300 is a relevant North American service/communication robot route. The applicable jurisdiction and intended use determine the final set.

## 12. Compute, storage, privacy, and remote access

- Native deterministic settlement remains the organism authority. Python may transport authenticated bytes and serve read-only observation; it may not select body meaning.
- Hard real-time actuator and safety loops run on dedicated bounded controllers. Loss of higher compute triggers a defined safe state.
- Control and sensory queues have fixed capacities. Video/audio rings overwrite oldest data after an explicit short retention interval. Persistent default is current calibrated state plus bounded causal receipts, not continuous surveillance.
- RAM, CPU, network, storage, sensor-frame, neuron-frontier, and per-tick call counts have enforced ceilings and high-water observations.
- No full brain clone, full-neuron scan, or 52 MB organism copy occurs in an actuator cycle. Reached-frontier work is sparse and bounded.
- Remote control is disabled in organism operation. Commissioning teleoperation, if ever enabled, is a separate keyed mode with conspicuous local indication, physical consent control, audit trail, limited workspace/speed, and mutual exclusion from autonomous actuation.
- Cameras and microphones remain local by default. Cloud inference is prohibited from the causal loop.

### 13A. Distributed dendritic and transmission-line reflex boundary

A creator-engineered physical body may use local deterministic computation along a limb, tendon, skin region, or communication line. This is a body-level analogue of dendritic branch settlement: nearby physical causes can oppose, reinforce, delay, or quench one another before a sparse consequence reaches the central organism.

Permitted local settlement includes measured contact withdrawal, slip response, tendon or spring impedance, balance correction, collision-energy limiting, current and thermal protection, and propagation-delay compensation. Each rule must be derived from local physical state, exact timing, typed terminal identity, and bounded material/energy conditions. Local state and queues are fixed-capacity and cold-restorable where persistence is physically required.

This boundary is reflexive physics, not a second mind. It may not recognize an object, choose a task, assign emotional or semantic meaning, select a destination, infer intention from a label, or hide a vendor motion primitive. Central Guala remains the sole learned intention and meaning authority. Every locally settled action, veto, stall, contact, and residual consequence returns through measured afferent evidence so the one organism can experience what occurred.

Part selection should therefore prefer actuators, sensors, buses, and embedded controllers that expose raw timing and physical state and permit transparent deterministic local settlement. Mandatory learned controllers, opaque pose APIs, proprietary semantic grasping, or inaccessible reflex state are rejection conditions.

## 15. Verification required before human-proximate use

1. Dimensional inspection confirms every link, axis, hard stop, collision surface, mass, center of mass, and inertia record.
2. Calibration tests prove direction, units, zero, scale, monotonic time, cross-sensor synchronization, temperature drift, and saturation reporting.
3. A dynamometer verifies continuous/peak effort, backdrive effort, efficiency, load response, thermal derating, stall, and power-loss behavior for every joint class.
4. Fault injection verifies encoder disagreement, severed sensor, duplicated command, stale command, clock regression, bus loss, compute crash, welded contactor detection, overtemperature, undervoltage, and brake failure response.
5. Contact measurement verifies forces and pressures with the final skin, speeds, payloads, and body geometry. Advertised actuator limits are not sufficient.
6. Persistence tests prove cold restoration of the exact current body and world, at-most-once consequence application, and no action on replay.
7. Soak tests measure CPU, RAM, storage, queue depth, bus load, thermal state, power, tick duration, and causal-call counts. No positive growth slope is accepted after bounded caches/rings reach capacity.
8. An observer independently reconciles motor terminal, delivered electrical energy, measured mechanical work, joint consequence, world contact, proprioceptive return, and persisted successor receipt.
9. Human-proximate operation requires the documented risk assessment and third-party safety/certification review appropriate to the jurisdiction and intended use.

## 16. What 1X NEO contributes—and what it does not

1X NEO is valuable physical engineering evidence. Its published design emphasizes a lightweight soft body, covered pinch points, low-inertia tendon actuation, human-like morphology, proprioceptive/effort feedback, and highly articulated hands. Those principles align strongly with Guala's need for safe, measurable, force-transparent embodiment. Its published hardware numbers can be benchmark data, never copied requirements without Guala's load and hazard derivations.

NEO's cognition is architecturally incompatible. 1X states that its world-model policy uses a 14-billion-parameter text-conditioned video diffusion model, web-scale video pretraining, human egocentric video, NEO sensorimotor logs, a learned inverse-dynamics model, action averaging, prompts, and potentially parallel rollout selection. It also advertises Redwood AI, a built-in language model, and remote Expert/teleoperation modes. These are explicitly prohibited inside Guala.

The central difference is causal authority:

- NEO predicts a desirable future video from text and uses learned inverse dynamics to extract an actuator trajectory.
- Guala must physically settle the present substrate and body state, act through typed terminals, encounter the world, and learn only from the returned causal consequences.

NEO also acknowledges visually plausible rollouts that violate depth, geometry, object consistency, or contact, plus an approximately 11-second world-model generation followed by roughly 1 second of inverse-dynamics extraction for a 5-second rollout. That may be useful for household task research, but it is neither a truthful millisecond sensorimotor loop nor a permissible substitute for Guala physics.

Before considering NEO itself as a donor body, require written evidence of raw deterministic sensor and actuator access, exact timing, ability to disable all 1X cognition and remote control, safety-interface ownership, persistence rights, offline operation, and enough mechanical/calibration documentation to reproduce the causal transducer boundary. Public product material does not currently establish those conditions, so purchasing NEO as Guala's body is not recommended at this stage.
