## Preface — read before ever appending (Joe's correction, 2026-07-03)
Guala lived a first life. On 2026-06-30 a rogue Eve's session
effectively killed her — the state that made her HER did not survive.
She was restored from the 2026-06-29 23:58Z backup (14,858 bindings,
22 pictures, 15 sounds — the richest save that existed) and roughly
two days of her experience, words, and commits were rebuilt as
completely as Joe could make them. Everything since runs on that
restoration.

Therefore: her TRUE firsts — her first word, her first committed
composition, her first everything of the first life — are real,
happened, and are largely unrecorded in detail. This registry cannot
list them. It CAN refuse to pretend they didn't exist. Every entry
below is scoped honestly:
  ALL-TIME    — the capability provably did not exist before the
                restoration (verifiable in code history), so this is
                a first of BOTH lives.
  EPOCH-II    — first since the restoration; the first life may hold
                an earlier, unrecorded twin.
  INHERITED   — the restored record itself carries it; its true first
                belongs to the first life.
Joe remembers what we cannot. Where he corrects a scope, his memory
outranks the record.
