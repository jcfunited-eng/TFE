PART 1: BASE FRAME (Main body)
================================================================================

Function: Holds diamond wafer, antenna PCB, optics, and temperature sensor

Dimensions:
  Overall: 80 mm (L) × 60 mm (W) × 40 mm (H)
  Material: Aluminum 6061-T6
  Weight: ~300 g (estimated)

Features:

1. DIAMOND WAFER MOUNTING HOLE (Center, TOP surface)
   Location: Center of top surface
   Diameter: ø10.0 mm ±0.05 mm (diamond wafer is 10×10 mm)
   Depth: 1.5 mm
   Type: Cylindrical through-hole
   Purpose: Diamond wafer sits in this hole with soft clamp

   Soft clamp assembly (see Part 3):
   - Two M3 springs on top (push down gently)
   - Non-contact mounting (no stress on diamond)

2. ANTENNA PCB MOUNTING HOLES (Top surface, around diamond)
   Pattern: 4× M3 threaded holes
   Location:
     - Hole 1: (x= -15 mm, y= -15 mm) from center
     - Hole 2: (x= +15 mm, y= -15 mm)
     - Hole 3: (x= +15 mm, y= +15 mm)
     - Hole 4: (x= -15 mm, y= +15 mm)
   Depth: 8 mm (tapped, M3 × 0.5 mm pitch)
   Clearance: 0.2 mm from diamond edge

3. SMA CONNECTOR MOUNTING HOLE (Right side, TOP surface)
   Location: (x= +30 mm, y= 0) from center
   Diameter: ø4.4 mm (SMA push-fit hole)
   Depth: 10 mm (must be deep enough for SMA connector nut)
   Type: Cylindrical
   Purpose: Microwave RF input from stripline antenna PCB

4. FIBER COUPLER INPUT HOLE (Left side, TOP surface)
   Location: (x= -30 mm, y= 0) from center
   Diameter: ø5.0 mm (clearance for fiber ferrule)
   Depth: 15 mm
   Threads: None (loose fit for adjustable fiber holder)
   Purpose: Single-mode fiber 637 nm input

5. TEMPERATURE SENSOR MOUNTING (Bottom surface)
   Location: (x= 0, y= -20 mm) from center
   Diameter: ø4.0 mm
   Depth: 5 mm
   Type: Press-fit hole for Pt100 RTD probe
   Purpose: Monitor diamond wafer temperature

6. OPTICAL WINDOW MOUNT (Top center, above diamond)
   Type: Threaded mount (M25 × 0.75 mm)
   Location: Centered over diamond hole
   Height: 5 mm above diamond surface
   Purpose: Holds BK7 glass window with AR coating

7. FOCUSING LENS ASSEMBLY MOUNT (Top surface, above window)
   Type: Threaded mount (M25 × 0.75 mm)
   Location: Coaxial with optical window
   Height: Adjustable (10-30 mm above window)
   Purpose: f=25 mm and f=50 mm lens assembly
   X-Y Adjustability: Via kinematic mount (3 set screws at 120°)

8. GROUND PLANE CONNECTION (RF grounding)
   Via holes (4×): ø2.0 mm, 10 mm deep
   Location: Around SMA connector (10 mm radius)
   Purpose: Star-point grounding for RF shielding

PART 2: DIAMOND WAFER MOUNT (Spacer Ring)
================================================================================

Function: Sits between antenna PCB and diamond, sets optical gap

Dimensions:
  Outer diameter: 20 mm
  Inner hole diameter: ø10.0 mm (for diamond)
  Thickness: 3.0 mm
  Material: Aluminum 6061-T6

Features:

1. DIAMOND MOUNTING HOLE
   Diameter: ø10.0 mm (tight fit, 0 mm clearance)
   Depth: 3.0 mm (full thickness)
   Type: Cylindrical
   Purpose: Diamond wafer sits in this hole, provides mechanical support

2. ALIGNMENT FEATURES
   Three alignment pins (ø1.0 mm × 5 mm tall, press-fit):
   Location: At 0°, 120°, 240° around outer edge
   Purpose: Ensure repeatable positioning relative to antenna PCB

3. THERMAL INTERFACE
   Location: Bottom surface (facing antenna)
   Surface finish: Smooth (Ra < 0.8 μm)
   Material: Keep as aluminum (will apply silver epoxy)
   Purpose: Thermal path to heat sink

================================================================================
PART 3: SOFT CLAMP ASSEMBLY (Diamond retention)
================================================================================

Function: Hold diamond gently without stress

Components:
  - 2× Compression springs (stainless steel)
  - 2× M3 screws (stainless steel)
  - 2× Soft rubber pads (neoprene, 0.5 mm thick)

Assembly:
  1. Place diamond in mounting hole (sits on spacer ring)
  2. Attach rubber pad to bottom of each spring
  3. Insert spring + screw assembly through top mounting holes
  4. Torque: 0.5 Nm (gentle - just enough to hold, not crush)
  5. Verify diamond is flush with holder surface

================================================================================
PART 4: OPTICAL WINDOW MOUNT
================================================================================

Function: Hold BK7 glass window (637 nm AR coating)

Specifications:
  Window: 10 mm diameter, 1 mm thick, BK7 glass
  Coating: Anti-reflection @ 637 nm (R < 1%)
  Mount type: Threaded collar (M25 × 0.75 mm)

Assembly:
  1. Insert window into mount
  2. Screw collar down (torque: 2 Nm)
  3. Align perpendicular to optical path (level)
  4. Verify no stress on window (no cracks)

Distance from diamond:
  Nominal: 2.5 mm (set by spacer ring thickness)
  Adjust by changing collar depth if needed

================================================================================
PART 5: FOCUSING OPTICS MOUNT
================================================================================

Function: Hold f=50mm and f=25mm lenses for focused beam

Configuration:
  ```
  [Fiber input]
       ↓
    [f=50mm lens] (collimate)
       ↓ (collimated beam)
   [Optical window] (on holder)
       ↓
    [f=25mm lens] (focus)
       ↓
    [Diamond NV array]
  ```

Mount specifications:
  Type: Adjustable kinematic mount
  Threads: M25 × 0.75 mm (to holder)
  X-Y adjustment: 3 set screws at 120° (0.5 mm travel each)
  Focus adjustment: Screw to move mount up/down (20 mm range)

Lens specifications:
  - f=50mm lens: Thorlabs AC254-050-A (diameter 25.4 mm)
  - f=25mm lens: Thorlabs AC254-025-A (diameter 25.4 mm)
  - Both: Uncoated BK7 glass

Alignment procedure:
  1. Place fiber input at 50 mm from f=50mm lens
  2. Focus with card at 100 mm (check collimation)
  3. Place f=25mm lens at proper distance
  4. Focus beam on diamond (use fluorescence feedback)
  5. Lock set screws (Loctite 243)

================================================================================
PART 6: MICROWAVE ANTENNA PCB INTEGRATION
================================================================================

Function: Mount stripline antenna near diamond

PCB specifications:
  Dimensions: 50 mm (L) × 30 mm (W) × 0.5 mm (thick)
  Material: Rogers RO4003C
  Copper: 35 μm (1 oz) both sides
  SMA connector: Right-angle, PCB-mount, gold-plated

Assembly:
  1. Place PCB on top of base frame
  2. Align antenna with diamond hole (centered ±0.5 mm)
  3. Insert M3 screws through mounting holes
  4. Torque: 1.5 Nm (firm but not over-tight)
  5. Verify antenna is 2-3 mm above diamond

Gap verification:
  - Use feeler gauge to check spacing
  - Expected: 2.5 ± 0.5 mm
  - Too close: RF field too strong (λ > 0.01)
  - Too far: RF field too weak (no Rabi oscillations)

================================================================================
PART 7: TEMPERATURE MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

Thermal interface material (TIM):
  Type: Silver-filled epoxy (e.g., Epotek H20E)
  Application: Between diamond and base frame
  Thickness: ~0.5 mm (thin layer for good thermal contact)
  Cure time: 24 hours at room temperature
  Thermal conductivity: ~1 W/m·K (adequate for dissipation)

Heat dissipation (optional):
  If temperature control needed:
  - Aluminum fin pack (50 mm × 40 mm × 30 mm)
  - Mount below base frame
  - Provides passive cooling to ~22°C ambient

Temperature sensor (Pt100 RTD):
  Model: Minco Electronics
  Accuracy: ±0.1°C
  Mount: Press-fit in ø4.0 mm hole (bottom of holder)
  Connection: 2-wire or 3-wire to FPGA ADC

================================================================================
ASSEMBLY SEQUENCE
================================================================================

Phase 1: Machining (CNC shop, 3-4 weeks)
  1. Machine base frame (aluminum 6061-T6)
  2. Machine spacer ring
  3. Anodize all aluminum parts (Type II, black, 25-50 μm)
  4. Machine soft clamp components

Phase 2: Component procurement (2-3 weeks)
  1. Order optical window (BK7, AR coated 637 nm)
  2. Order lenses (f=50mm and f=25mm)
  3. Order SMA connector (right-angle, PCB-mount)
  4. Order springs, screws, fasteners (stainless steel)
  5. Order fiber coupler assembly
  6. Order Pt100 RTD temperature sensor

Phase 3: Assembly (1 week)
  1. Apply silver epoxy TIM to diamond mounting surface
  2. Insert diamond in spacer ring (allow epoxy to cure)
  3. Mount spacer ring to base frame with M3 screws
  4. Install temperature sensor in ø4.0 mm hole
  5. Install antenna PCB on top (align carefully)
  6. Install SMA connector (thread onto antenna trace)
  7. Install optical window mount + window
  8. Install focusing optics mount + lenses
  9. Install fiber coupler assembly
  10. Perform final alignment and focus

Phase 4: Testing (1 week)
  1. Verify mechanical stability (no wobble)
  2. Test microwave field (measure B₁ with small coil)
  3. Test optical coupling (637 nm laser, measure power)
  4. Test temperature sensor (should read room temp)
  5. Thermal cycle test (freeze-thaw, check for cracks)
