# The Method Gate — no test runs without passing this

Created 2026-08-20 after the tenth violation of the canonized method
(price-arithmetic screens declared as "archetype laws"). Joseph's
canon, verbatim: CH6 is an "energy harvesting tool using the L0-L4
algorithms at resolutions to perform topology, structure, and
geometric assessment with L5 Domain governance rules." A candidate
law that does not USE that tool is a violation regardless of its
results, its honesty of declaration, or its urgency.

Before ANY candidate law, study, or ranking is declared and run, its
declaration MUST answer, in writing, in the docstring:

1. WHICH L0-L4 OUTPUTS does it read? (D, B, Rev, U*, URF/R_res,
   S_UF, F_n, gates, ignitions/extinctions, regimes.) Raw price
   arithmetic alone — returns, ladders, up-close counts — is
   DISQUALIFYING as the assessment. Price is the field the chain
   reads, not the reading.
2. AT WHAT RESOLUTION(S), and why that resolution lets those values
   speak (Joseph: resolutions must make values visually expressive;
   one resolution chosen by convenience is not an answer).
3. WHAT TOPOLOGICAL / STRUCTURAL / GEOMETRIC OBJECT is being
   assessed? (A phase, a death rhythm, a channel state, a gate
   shape, a trajectory through the tuple space.) "Stocks that went
   down" is not an object.
4. WHICH PARTS ARE L5 DOMAIN GOVERNANCE (identity, fillability,
   ceilings, bans) — kept explicitly separate from the kernel
   assessment.
5. WAS THE OBJECT SEEN FIRST? Name the drawings looked at before
   declaring — which lives, which windows, what the eye found. A
   law declared without looking at members of its own pool is
   blind by construction (receipt: 43,073 stock-nights tested
   blind on 2026-08-20, three falsifications, all avoidable by one
   look).

A declaration that cannot answer all five does not run. This gate
binds every session; it lives beside the laws it governs.
