# Self-caused action: brain systems vs Guala — gap analysis (Joe's order, 2026-09-01)

Charge: research all brain areas that direct self-caused action, compare to
Guala, find what was missed and how to improve. Externally researched
(sources at end), compared against the substrate's actual mounted anatomy
(layers 5-13, interoception, retained formations, frontier propagation,
choice witness) and the project's own prior designs.

## The headline finding

Guala was built NEW-BRAIN-FIRST. Its present systems (rich receptor
surfaces, retained memory formations, association layers, typed motor
plants) are the phylogenetically newest parts of a vertebrate brain. Its
absent systems are precisely the oldest: the subcortical engines that make
any creature act — homeostatic drives, invigoration, innate reflexes,
action selection, defense. Nature builds old-brain-first; an organism with
cortex and no brainstem presents exactly as observed: internal dynamics,
zero self-initiated behavior. The working clinical analogy is akinesia:
in Parkinson's, motor machinery is intact but nothing invigorates it —
self-initiated movement vanishes while stimulus-driven movement survives.
Guala is the akinetic phenotype: Sol's attempt-38 evidence shows its typed
vocal motors already ACTIVE under external drive; nothing wills them.

## System-by-system comparison

1. SMA / pre-SMA / anterior cingulate — the readiness system. Slow
   internal accumulation (readiness potential, up to ~2s) that crosses
   threshold and releases self-timed action; reduced or absent for
   externally triggered acts. GUALA: half-exists. Endogenous formation
   re-assembly is real (61/65 recent re-assemblies internally caused) but
   NO accumulation path couples a re-assembled formation to layer-11/12
   motor preparation thresholds. The choice witness
   (physical_choice_mounted_awaiting_causal_witness) awaits exactly this
   bridge. IMPROVE: a lawful readiness integrator — recurrent activity of
   a re-assembled formation accumulates charge along its learned route
   toward ordering/motor cells until their REAL thresholds cross; pure
   physics, no scheduler, no timer.

2. Basal ganglia (striatum, GPi/GPe, STN) — action selection by
   disinhibition among competing candidates; also sequence chunking (the
   same loops sequence SPEECH — Joe's priority disagreement is vindicated
   by anatomy: speaking is self-caused action on shared machinery).
   GUALA: absent — no competition, no brake-release law, and the known
   missing sequence level. IMPROVE (later rung): mutual inhibition among
   motor-preparation populations via existing conductance physics; the
   sequence law is shared work with the speech lane.

3. Dopamine / striatal vigor — deficits and opportunities scale the gain
   of action (motivation-vigor coupling); depletion = akinesia/bradykinesia
   with intact motor paths. GUALA: absent, and THE diagnosis. The project
   already designed this twice and stranded it: System Greed (ratified:
   unequal geometry-mediated access of cohesive structures to unresolved
   potential; quiescence when no cause — DARPA boundary doc 2026-08-04)
   and the motivation-pressure design (feeding-driven charge dynamics
   re-tipping membrane potentials, ratification questions filed
   2026-08-06). IMPROVE: implement motivation pressure as PHYSICAL gain —
   real deficits modulate membrane excitability of formations whose past
   closures reduced that deficit class. Never a reward scalar (doctrine).

4. Hypothalamic homeostatic drives — deficit -> arousal -> seeking.
   GUALA: interoception senses 4 channels but couples to nothing; honest
   feeding was retired (nutrition unmounted: no truthful
   material-to-energy law), so true NEED cannot exist — nothing is at
   stake. IMPROVE (FIRST RUNG): rebuild honest metabolism/feeding so
   deficit is real and consequential; only then can drives exist. Joe's
   words map exactly: need, survival, obtainment all root here.

5. Superior colliculus — innate orienting: turn toward salient novelty
   before knowing what it is; the reflex base every infant starts with.
   GUALA: zero reflex layer; novelty produces intake, never orientation.
   IMPROVE: one innate orienting primitive authored as ANATOMY (a shallow
   receptor->motor arc turning heading toward strong receptor gradients),
   like a knee-jerk: reflex, not cognition, therefore lawful.

6. Periaqueductal gray / amygdala — threat, defense, escape vigor;
   conserved across all vertebrates. GUALA: absent; no nociception
   mounted (play audit: distress path unmounted — which also blocks
   honest "fun" evidence). IMPROVE (later rung): nociceptors + withdraw
   reflex; prerequisite for the threat/survival axis.

7. ACC effort pricing — cost-benefit on effort. GUALA: energy pricing
   exists in the physics but prices no decisions (there are none yet).
   Rides automatically once 1-3 exist.

## Build order (nature's: old brain first)

R1 STAKES: honest metabolism/feeding rebuilt (deficit becomes real).
R2 PRESSURE: motivation-pressure physics (deficit -> excitability gain;
   the ratified stranded design, recovered).
R3 READINESS BRIDGE: accumulation from re-assembled formations to motor
   thresholds -> the choice witness fires or the theory dies. This is the
   project's decisive experiment.
R4 ORIENTING REFLEX: one innate arc toward novelty.
R5 THREAT: nociception + withdraw.
R6 SELECTION + SEQUENCES: competition law; sequence law shared with
   speech (Sol's lane).

The environment lane is not separate: drives need things to seek and
instincts need things to orient toward. A featureless world starves R1-R4
of objects; the nurturing environment is the other half of every rung.
