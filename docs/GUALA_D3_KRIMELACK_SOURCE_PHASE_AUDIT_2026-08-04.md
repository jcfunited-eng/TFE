## Implemented source-boundary correction

The `GLJSRC01` schema already carried `normalized_signal`, `phase_turns`, and
`relevance`. The native parser previously validated all three and discarded the
first and last two after deriving the dimensionless L0 input. It now retains,
per source sample:

- the exact rational representation of the supplied finite binary64 normalized
  source;
- the exact reported phase rational; and
- the exact relevance rational.

The affine input map continues to derive the positive dimensionless L0 input.
The source evidence remains distinct; it is not replaced by the mapped field
and does not replace the full DSF delivery.

This correction is evidence preservation only. It does not make a normalized
value into a physical quantity, make a phase report into biological phase, or
provide energy, transduction, channel gating, membrane current, Krimelack, or
cognition.

- foveal sight uses `index / sample_count`, which is trajectory position;
- generic virtual receptors also use `index / count`;
- several modalities emit zero phase;
- auditory phase accumulates the argument of complex floating-point gammatone

These may remain observational or DSP evidence where truthfully labelled. They
cannot become exact resident Krimelack state merely because they are retained.

The virtual body already possesses exact yaw orientation in millidegrees.
Dividing that integer orientation by the defined full-turn extent of 360,000
millidegrees yields exact geometric turn position without a selected
coefficient. That occurrence describes body heading only. It is not, by itself,
the mechanical deformation of vestibular tissue, channel state, or Krimelack.

## Required physical boundary

The source boundary must eventually carry two separate products:

1. the exact physical source quantity with declared unit, calibration profile,
   causal time, and finite source range; and
2. an exact phase occurrence only when that receptor's physical transduction
   law derives it.

Resident admission must borrow both the complete D1 result and the distinct
physical occurrence. Only after one receptor supplies those quantities can a
Krimelack candidate retain bounded phase residue and topological winding
without inventing energy. D1 may constrain or structurally couple that physical
transition only through a separately dimensionally complete law; repeated
`U_star=Observed` or another constant trit cannot supply energy.
