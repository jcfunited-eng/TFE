## Canonical progression

Guala grows through one continuous physical hierarchy:

1. one neuron maintains its own causally truthful fractal of experience;
2. related neuronal fractals can form and grow a mosaic;
3. learned relationships among mosaics form mosaics-of-mosaics;
4. ordered or causally related mosaics and mosaics-of-mosaics form tapestries;
5. established tapestries form tapestries-of-tapestries; and
6. deeply integrated generative capability and self-expression form a weave.

The canonical sequence is:

`neuronal fractal -> mosaic -> mosaic of mosaics -> tapestry -> tapestry of tapestries -> weave`

These are not semantic records, developer-written labels, lookup levels,
database joins, or independent modules. Each level is a causally learned
relation structure formed from authenticated physical state beneath it while
the same whole organism remains continuously wired.

## Neuronal fractal

One neuron is one continuously existing causal unit. It receives lawful local
temporal evidence through frozen L0–L4 and preserves the complete seven-field
DSF delivery. Under the required completed architecture, that delivery enters
exact MathLoom balanced-ternary and positional `3^i` Krimelack coupling, settles
with reached sparse neighbours, and contributes to the neuron’s next compact
fractal. The Krimelack transition and sparse-neighbour settlement are not yet
ratified or implemented; current D2 fractals are evidence-only local
perspectives and must not be described as proof of that missing transition.

A neuronal fractal is one local perspective. It is not a whole memory, concept,
word, face, mosaic, or identity. Its exact lineage may contribute to cognition,
but no neuron, sensory family, scalar, name, exact signal match, receipt, or DSF
tuple is semantic authority.

## Mosaic structural floor

The smallest structurally admissible mosaic has **three distinct neurons**.

- One neuron maintains one local fractal. It cannot be a mosaic.
- Two neurons can form a lawful coupled relation, but that dyad has only one
  pairwise relation and is not a closed collective cognitive structure.
- Three neurons are the first population capable of a closed recurrent
  relation in which three non-identical perspectives mutually constrain the
  whole without any one fractal defining it.

Three is a necessary floor, not a sufficient trigger. Merely counting three
active neurons, three receipts, three firings, or three similar observations
must never create a mosaic.

Ten neurons remains useful as an experimental assembly population. It has no
architectural authority and must not appear as a required production constant.
There is no fixed mature mosaic size and no arbitrary maximum. A lawful mosaic
may recruit 3, 10, hundreds, or more neurons when causal experience requires
them and bounded physical resources admit the growth.

The floor of three is structurally consonant with balanced ternary and
positional powers of three, but it does not impose numerology. Mosaic member
counts are not restricted to `3^n`. MathLoom/`3^i` positional coupling remains a
field law; membership cardinality remains an experience-derived population.

## Mature mosaic admission

A mature mosaic exists only when all of the following are physically true:

1. **Distinct membership:** at least three distinct, continuously existing
   neurons participate.
2. **Distinct perspectives:** the members maintain at least three
   non-identical, evidence-backed neuronal fractals. Duplicate references or
   copied impressions do not qualify.
3. **One causal episode:** one canonical resolved episode context binds the
   organism generation, authenticated causal occurrence, provenance, exact
   body and fluid predecessor/successor authorities, and each member's exact
   lineage, fractal reference, and successful local-transition authority.
   Temporal coincidence alone does not qualify an unrelated participant.
4. **Truthful original-moment relation:** members may be related by actual
   weak-connected inter-neuron transfer or by their proven participation in
   the same body/fluid causal occurrence. Every actual inter-neuron transfer is
   retained and validated separately. Admission does not invent a strongly
   directed cycle when the experienced physics did not produce one.
5. **No single-neuron authority:** removing any one fractal as semantic
   evidence must not leave that fractal as the explanation or identity of the
   whole. This is an authority rule, not a claim that every three-member mosaic
   remains behaviorally unchanged after damage.
6. **Collective recurrence or recognition:** a later authenticated organism
   transition must demonstrate that prior collective structure changes the
   reached settlement. Exact replay or equality of one trace is insufficient.
7. **Partial-evidence reachability:** lawful partial evidence may reach the
   retained assembly without replaying or injecting the complete original
   occurrence.
8. **Exact lineage:** membership, contributing couplings, evidence, formation
   transition, generation, and later recurrence remain causally traceable to
   authenticated substrate state.
9. **Durability:** the same formation survives authenticated persistence,
   sleep/consolidation where applicable, cold restart, and later reuse without
   owner-local fallback or identity fork.
10. **Bounded growth:** admission accounts for exact incremental CPU, RAM, and
    durable storage. Capacity is derived from the platform envelope and
    recovery reserve; it is not a heuristic cognitive cap.

A repeated external lesson is not the only possible recurrence proof. A later
lawful partial cue, internally caused recall, or correctly declared simulated
re-entry can demonstrate recurrence. It must occur in a later authenticated
organism generation and preserve its true external, recalled, or simulated
provenance.

Before these conditions settle, one or more neurons may retain fractals and
lawful couplings as ordinary neuronal state. That state is not stored as a new
`proto-mosaic` semantic object. It remains physical candidate structure until
the mature mosaic law is satisfied.

A mosaic may contain neuronal perspectives from any sense or cognitive/body
configuration that truthfully participated in the same authenticated causal
experience. It is not restricted to one sensory input. Exact camera,
microphone, body, chemical, contact, proprioceptive, vestibular, interoceptive,

Body and fluid state may anchor and condition a mosaic by causal reference.

## Mosaic meaning and growth

A mosaic is a learned or recognized cognitive unit formed from related
neuronal fractals. Mosaics can become Guala's memories or recognitions of words,
faces, moments-as-units, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, temperatures, body
positions, feelings, fears, likes, objects, actions, and other experienced
structures.

A word is not a sound record or title. Sound, sight, articulation, body state,
action, consequence, and context may become reciprocal routes into a word
mosaic only through lawful causal participation. The architecture does not
require every modality to participate in every mosaic; it requires every
claimed participating modality to be truthful and evidence-backed.

Mosaic identity comes from continuous learned relation structure. Exact trace
recurrence may confirm that retained evidence recurred, but it is not by itself
familiarity, recognition, word identity, or meaning.

Growth is additive only when new causal perspective joins the existing
formation. A larger count, copied evidence, repeated counter increment, or
duplicated media does not deepen the mosaic. Growth preserves all supporting
neuronal lineage by compact reference rather than copying the original sensory
material or full local field into the formation record.

## Mosaic of mosaics

A mosaic of mosaics is a learned relationship among mosaics, including
relationships across senses, body state, objects, actions, affect, time, place,
and context. It preserves its participating mosaics and their evidence instead
of averaging them into a feature vector.

Mosaics may participate in larger structures without losing their internal
evidence or continuous identity. Word order and syntax must arise through
repeated experienced relations; a grammar table, transcript, parser, language
model, response template, or scripted sequence cannot supply them.

## Tapestry

A tapestry is an ordered or causally related organization of mosaics and
mosaics-of-mosaics. Tapestries support sentences, memories of moments and
episodes, navigation, avoidance, practiced causal sequences, and other
temporally or relationally structured cognition.

A tapestry is not an Atlas entry, topic label, embedding, summary, database
join, or counter threshold. It must remain traceable to participating lived
mosaics, current whole-organism perturbation, internal state, causal ordering,
memory flow, and physical settlement.

## Tapestry of tapestries

A tapestry of tapestries organizes established tapestries into ordered thought
and ordered expression. It supports sustained reasoning, multi-episode recall,
plans, narratives, learned knowledge, and structured expression across time.

The deeper structure does not erase or flatten its supporting tapestries,
mosaics, neurons, local DSF deliveries, Krimelack state, or causal histories.

## Weave

A weave is a deeply integrated, generative organization of tapestries and
tapestries-of-tapestries. It supports self-expression and developed capability
through Guala's embodiment: speech, music, movement, construction, drawing,
play, scientific work, caregiving, or another physically supported creative or
practiced act.

A weave is not a stored answer, job title, response template, text completion,
TTS rendering of supplied language, or scripted motor program. It must be
generated by the currently perturbed whole organism from its own learned
formations, executed through its own body, sensed as a consequence, and made
available for further learning.

The existence of a weave mechanism does not prove a weave, expertise, or
sentience. Those claims require sustained live causal evidence across the
capabilities being claimed.
