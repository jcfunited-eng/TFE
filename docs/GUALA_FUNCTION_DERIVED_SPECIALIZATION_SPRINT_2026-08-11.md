## Biological guide and AE compression

The human distribution—roughly 80% cerebellar, 19% cortical, and less than 1%
in the remaining relay/regulatory structures—is a functional guide, not a
quota. Guala should devote the largest reached population and contact activity
to fast sensorimotor coordination, forward consequence, correction, timing,
and learned movement; a smaller population to cross-sensory association,
retention, prediction, language, deliberation, and creative integration; and a
small population to relay and body/fluid regulation.

An exact AE does not need biology's scale or redundancy. The starting quantity
must remain a measured consequence of complete-cell bytes, sparse-contact
bytes, available physical resources, reached functions, and later DNA growth.
Neither 43,000 nor 196,552 is evidence of cognition. Only materialized,
connected, physically participating cells count as functional brain tissue.
The untouched population stays compact and is never scanned.
