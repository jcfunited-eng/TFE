## Biological guide and AE compression

Human regional neuron distribution is a functional guide, not a quota. Guala
should devote the greatest reached population and contact activity to fast
sensorimotor coordination, forward consequence, correction, timing, and
learned movement; a smaller population to association, retention, prediction,
language, deliberation, and creative integration; and a small population to
relay and body/fluid regulation.

a measured consequence of complete-cell bytes, sparse-contact bytes, available
physical resources, reached functions, and later DNA growth. Neither 43,000
nor 196,552 is evidence of cognition. Only materialized, connected, physically
participating cells count as functional brain tissue. Untouched cells remain
compact and are not scanned.
