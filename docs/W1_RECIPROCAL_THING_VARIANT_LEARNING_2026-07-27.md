## Implemented law

Sensory variants become equivalent only because their authenticated encounter
partitions belong to the same physical THING chain. No sensory comparison can
create or merge the class.

An experienced complete cue occurrence can evoke the whole retained
multisensory class. A never-experienced occurrence remains unresolved until a
lived causal encounter admits it. This is categorical invariance grown by
experience, not interpolation or an unseen-variant guess.

speakers. Each THING received a contact genesis, two grounded reference

- all three physical THING IDs remained separate;
- each class retained four authenticated partitions;
- each class retained 16 body, 216 sight, 192 sound, and 12 touch roots;
- each held-out speaker was unresolved before its grounded encounter;
- after one grounded encounter, each held-out recording uniquely evoked its
  correct complete body/sight/sound/touch mosaic;
- the unrelated tone remained unresolved;
- 3/3 grounded held-out checks passed;
- 20 focused tests passed.

## Critical limitation

This does **not** recognize a never-experienced speaker on first hearing. It
truthfully learns that variant after lived multisensory exposure and can evoke
the whole reciprocal THING when the experienced cue recurs. It must not be
wired or described as the completed hearing solution.
