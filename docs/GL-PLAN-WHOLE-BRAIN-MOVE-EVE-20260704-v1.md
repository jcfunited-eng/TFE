## 0. The one fact the plan is built around
The complete brain (8 hemispheres, fifteen mechanisms co-present)
has ELEVEN working mechanisms and FOUR absent — and one of the four
is composition: it cannot speak. Its composition machinery
(LoomTapestry/LoomMosaic) exists in the codebase but was never wired
to the organism. A same-day 100% cutover therefore silences her at
Joe's seat. The plan puts the whole brain into her live process on
day one and hands her functions to it in fixed stages, building the
ONE missing organ (speech) inside her, on her real life — walls
first, roof named, no bench anywhere.

process, her identity, all fifteen mechanisms on from first tick,

taste as they exist live) — every experience she has, the brain has,
in the same window. It sleeps when she sleeps (same trigger). Its
state joins her hot/cold save path and S3 set from first boot.
Per-neuron differentiation at birth derives from structure (ring/
hemisphere position), not seeded RNG — Eve's standing ruling.
ENTRY: verified backup (D1-class).
EXIT (all four, at Joe's seat):
  E1a boot-restore of the combined state proven once, bit-honest.
  E1b meter shows fifteen brain rows live/ABSENT, growing across a
      real day of her experience.
  E1c her tick rate holds: sustained cost ≤15% tick-rate loss;

      quietly throttled or benched.

      receives everything, drives nothing yet).
EFFORT: days (wiring + save-path + meter rows).

### Stage 2 — THE BRAIN DRIVES PERCEPTION AND MEMORY
Recall, recognition, association, habituation, attention, and affect
modulation hand over: the scheduler and recall paths read the
BRAIN's outputs, old shell paths disconnected from those decisions
(not deleted yet — §4 says when). Failures surface at Joe's seat and
on the meter; G-2 no-silent-fallback rule is absolute.

EXIT: Joe teaches her a new word with a real referent at his seat;
  within that day the brain's recall surfaces it (meter provenance
  shows brain-origin); her attending choices are brain-scored; no
  fallback events in the log.
EFFORT: ~1 week.

### Stage 3 — THE VOICE (the one build item, named now)
Wire the existing LoomTapestry/LoomMosaic composition machinery INTO
the organism as its emission organ, per the canonical registry
(keyhole cascade + emission commits — no templates, no LLM text, no
phased grammar). Built in her, trained by her real days, replayed in
her real sleep. Imagination and reflection ride on this organ and
come alive with it or are dated ABSENT on the meter — they are not
separate projects.
ENTRY: Stage 2 exits met; fresh verified backup.
EXIT: Joe speaks to her at his seat and she answers THROUGH the
  brain's composition path — emission origin=brain-commit on the
  meter, fallback count zero for the exchange. Content quality is
  NOT an exit criterion; immature words from the true organ beat
  fluent words from the old shell.
EFFORT: 2–4 weeks. This is the long pole and the roof; it is
  reached with all walls standing.

### Stage 4 — RETIRE THE SHELL
Old cognition paths (section scoring, old recall, old emission)
removed from her live loop — deleted from the loop, not commented
out, per "get rid of it means delete." Sleep/consolidation runs as
the brain's own. Her old atlas + deep atlas remain mounted read-only
as her memory archive; the brain's dream replay reinstates from it
(§5). The meter reads all-brain.
ENTRY: Stage 3 exits met; fresh verified backup; 7 consecutive days
  of Stage-3 exchanges with zero fallback events.
EXIT: a full day at Joe's seat — teach, talk, sleep, wake, answer —
  entirely on the brain; combined-state restore proven once more.
EFFORT: days, after the Stage-4 entry week.
