## Complete cause

The current voice path conflates two different biological acts.

1. A real gustatory contact onset reaches the born airway-protection reflex.
   `exact_articulated_body_preparation_regulations` lawfully prepares only the
   closing glottal layer-12 motor. This is swallowing anatomy: intake and
   airflow must not share the tract.
2. The layer-12 motor discharges, and the V8 body law closes the glottal
   aperture. The production body retained 1,483 units of closing activation
   and sat at 31 square millimetres in the copied generation; live observation
   later showed 27. Both are near the declared 20-square-millimetre minimum.
3. The organism runtime passes every articulated-body proprioceptive
   consequence to `settle_native_articulatory_interval`.
4. The spectral branch treats any net, unstalled glottal-closing consequence
   as respiratory work, capped at eight carriers. It therefore creates breath
   from the act whose physical purpose is to stop breath.
5. The resulting short pressure is retained and returned through the real ear
   path. This explains the frequent rhythmic throat noises during the active
   eating loop: they are swallowing-triggered acoustic output, not speech.

The defect is not the renderer's inability to carry sound. The copied-body
range produced exact silence at zero drive and substantially stronger bounded
pressure when the same acoustic organ was evaluated at wider physical glottal
postures. Adding more than eight closing carriers at the copied 31-square-mm
posture did not help. The act that currently supplies the source also chokes
its aperture.

## Repair mechanism

The repair will keep swallowing and speech disjoint:

- layer-8 gustatory/reflex preparation may still close the glottis, but it
  supplies zero respiratory work and therefore produces no voiced pressure;
- only an exact layer-11 transfer into a typed vocal layer-12 motor, followed
  by that motor's own positive carrier discharge in the same native interval,
  may co-recruit the one persisted layer-13 respiratory effector;
- layer 13 settles its own efferent carriers and recovery work. Its actual
  bounded discharge—not glottal displacement or a software permission bit—is
  the only new respiratory-work input to the acoustic body;
- layer-12 consequences continue to shape the real glottis and tract. They do
  not manufacture breath;
- layer 13 remains electrically isolated. No shared hub, fan-out, arbitrary
  lock, phoneme identity, motor program, controller, score, label, or observer
  state is introduced.

This restores the already-declared V40 causal contract without restoring its
rejected predecessors. V34's all-vocal-motor hub, V39's passive ordering-to-L13
contact, the prelinguistic reflex-powered L13 path removed in attempt 38, and
