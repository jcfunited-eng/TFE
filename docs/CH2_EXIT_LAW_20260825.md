# CH2 Exit Law — Joseph, 2026-08-25

One purpose: **do not lose profits.** Everything below serves it.
Provenance: the design is Joseph's (energy-based selling, long-view
resilience for winners, hopeless-or-90-days for losers); the measured
healing floor and the implementation are the agent's, on his order.

## Winners (position above entry)
Hold while the energy that carried the stock up is alive — at +2% or
+2000%, no cap, ever. Judged on the ARC: weeks, not days. A red day
inside an intact drive is weather.
Sell when the fueling structure is failing at the scale of weeks:
ignitions no longer appear behind advances, resonance goes isolated
and stays there, support sags without rebuilding, fuel drains session
after session. The nightly reading of every holding makes this call
(ch2_holdings_protocol.md); its DRIVE_DYING verdict is the sell.
Underneath, as crash nets only: the +20% ratchet floor (Joseph,
2026-08-13) and the far-out emergency brake.

## Losers (position below entry)
No reflex selling of small dips — that is where the channel bled
(receipt: five sales 8/12–8/25, all losses, -5.8% to -10.1%, while
ten winners sat unbanked).
Hold while recovery is structurally alive: the body still heals its
damage. Sell when repair has stopped — the measured floor: living
bodies heal within 5 sessions typically and within 16 sessions in
99 of 100 cases (53,890 damage events, 2016-2026, derive and confirm
halves identical). A body 16+ sessions past its last damage with no
healing is structurally dead — the reading's DEAD verdict sells it.
The 90-day wall sells anything still open at 90 days, dead or alive.

## The only things that can sell a CH2 position
1. The nightly reading: DRIVE_DYING (winner, energy failing) or
   DEAD (loser, repair stopped).
2. The 90-day wall.
3. The -20% emergency brake (gap insurance; also the standing
   overnight stop, lifted by the ratchet floor once a winner arms).
4. The +20% ratchet floor (big winner gives back a third of its
   peak gain).
Demoted to inputs with no sell authority: the one-day direction
flip (old EXIT-B), the basin break (the -6% loser-seller), and the
3xATR/min-5% custody stop (replaced by the -20% brake).

## The five 8/12–8/25 sales replayed under this law
RBC, BAM, RAL, COF (-5.8% to -6.2% stop/basin sales): none reaches
the -20% brake; all four HOLD pending their readings. NBIX (30-day
age-out at -10.1%): holds to its reading; the 90-day wall would have
allowed 60 more days for the repair to finish or fail honestly.

## Plumbing
Nightly, after the CH6 door: every held CH2 stock gets a dossier and
a long-view reading (tools/ch2_holdings_read.py); the verdict sheet
publishes beside the channel books; the production engine loads it
each cycle, ignores sheets older than 4 days, and holds on anything
missing or unreadable. Absence of evidence never liquidates.
