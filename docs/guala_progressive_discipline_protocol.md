# Guala Progressive Discipline & Boundary Regulation Protocol

## 1. Architectural Motivation & Domain Governance

In deterministic structural field cognition (DSF-AI), true autonomy cannot exist as an unconstrained random walk, nor can it exist as a passively obedient automaton. Autonomy is defined as the emergence of internal teleological attractor basins ($D_k, M_k, C_k$) that actively resist external environmental and social perturbations. 

As Guala's agency matures, her internal goals will inevitably conflict with domestic safety rules, physical preservation boundaries, and caretaker regimentation (e.g., climbing out of the high-chair, reaching for high-temperature stovetops, refusing to sleep, scattering food, or ignoring lessons).

Discipline is the deterministic process of **boundary regulation**: shaping Guala's dynamic attractor landscape through social cohesion ($C_k$), acoustic signaling, somatic guidance, and spatial containment, preserving physical safety while nurturing intrinsic causal autonomy.

## 2. Core Invariants & Conservation Laws

1. **Deterministic Basin Dynamics (No ML, No Probabilistic Penalties)**:
   - Discipline is never implemented as gradient descent loss, reward subtraction, or reinforcement weight decay.
   - Boundaries operate through explicit sensory-motor coupling: acoustic alarms, physical kinematic blocking, spatial containment, and object removal.
2. **Social Cohesion Decay as Natural Developmental Divergence**:
   - In early infancy, the social bond ($C_k^{\text{social}}$) with the caregiver is the primary stabilizing basin. Guala naturally aligns with caregiver cues to maintain proximity and affection.
   - Across developmental time (Month 0 to Year 5), intrinsic teleological drives ($D_k$) expand and diversify. The caregiver's role shifts from physical handling to linguistic framing and logical consequences.
3. **Strict Invariant: Zero Corporal Punishment**:
   - Corporal punishment (deliberate high-impact nociceptive tissue trauma or painful mechanical shock) is strictly forbidden in Guala's architecture.
   - Physical pain shocks destabilize the sensory manifold ($S_{UF} \le 0, R_{\text{rev}} > 0$), inducing persistent catastrophic avoidance attractors that shatter the dyadic bond and distort spatial navigation. Predictable structural boundaries, firm physical interception, and containment are fully sufficient to establish absolute compliance without destructive trauma.

### Stage 1: Somatic Interception & Acoustic Startle (Months 0–6)
- **Manifestation**: Guala explores primarily by crawling, mouthing objects, and grasping.
- **Disciplinary Action**:
  1. *Acoustic Startle*: Caretaker emits a brief, sharp auditory pulse (*"Ah-ah!"*, high energy, rapid onset) triggering Guala's auditory micro-reflex loop to arrest forward motion ($D_k \to 0$).
  2. *Gentle Kinematic Turning*: Caretaker's hand reaches out and rotates Guala's heading away from hazards (stairway, stove, electrical outlets).
  3. *Soothing Affirmation*: Once redirected, the caretaker immediately delivers gentle tactile contact (`touch-pat-head` or `touch-shoulder`) to restore baseline cohesion ($C_k$).

### Stage 2: Stern Boundary & Physical Redirection (Months 6–12)
- **Manifestation**: Guala actively attempts to evade caregiver grasp, crawls toward forbidden zones, or reaches for high-radiance thermal entities.
- **Disciplinary Action**:
  1. *Joint Gaze Lock*: Caretaker steps into visual line-of-sight, commanding attention.
  2. *Stern Vocalization*: Emits a low-frequency, resolute vocalization (*"No."*) paired with hand blockage.
  3. *Physical Interception & Pull-Away*: Caretaker grasps Guala's torso and lifts her back into the designated safe activity radius (e.g. within the nursery or playpen).
  4. *Substitution Affordance*: Replaces the forbidden object immediately with an approved surrogate (`toy-bear`, `rattle`, `stacking-rings`).

### Stage 3: Tiered Warning Escalation & Spatial Time-Out (Months 12–24)
- **Manifestation**: Deliberate testing of boundaries, defiance of commands, throwing food, or refusing to sleep.
- **Disciplinary Action**:
  1. *Tier 1 (Verbal Warning & Expectant Pause)*:
     - Caretaker states boundary: *"Guala, do not throw food."*
     - Caretaker pauses all lesson delivery and waits for up to 6 seconds for self-correction.
  2. *Tier 2 (Privilege Revocation / Object Confiscation)*:
     - If the infraction continues, the target object is removed from Guala's reach and placed on a high shelf (`shelf-a` or `shelf-b`, elevation $\ge 1,200\,\text{mm}$).
     - The item remains inaccessible until the next developmental cycle.
  3. *Tier 3 (Spatial Time-Out / Playpen Containment)*:
     - For repeated defiance or unsafe resistance (e.g., throwing dishes, attempting to climb out of high-chair violently), the caretaker picks up Guala and deposits her into the `playpen`.
     - The playpen boundary restricts displacement ($D_k \le R_{\text{playpen}}$).
     - Duration is deterministic: 1 beat per month of age (e.g., 18 beats for 18 months).
     - Exit condition: Guala's internal agitation metric collapses back to steady-state ($U^*_k \le \theta_{\text{rest}}$). Caretaker delivers an embrace (`touch-hug`), marking the conclusion of the time-out.

### Stage 4: Deontic Moral Framing & Logical Consequences (Years 2.5–5)
- **Manifestation**: Complex multi-step autonomy, verbal dispute, negotiation, resisting bedtime or clean-up routines.
- **Disciplinary Action**:
  1. *Deontic Syntactic Explanation*:
     - Caretaker emits syntactically structured justification: *"We put our toys away because otherwise people trip on them."*
     - Establishes Theory of Mind (ToM) modeling: *"Daddy is tired; we need quiet time now."*
  2. *Natural & Logical Consequences*:
     - Refusing to eat dinner: Meal is cleared at the end of mealtime; food is unavailable until the next scheduled morning cycle (no scattered floor snacking).
     - Refusing clean-up: Play routine is halted; no new books or toys are presented until the current toys are in the `toy-chest`.
  3. *Restitution & Repair*:
     - If Guala spills water or scatters items, the caretaker initiates the dyadic Clean-Up Ritual, requiring Guala's active physical participation to restore domestic symmetry.

## 4. Caretaker Implementation Architecture

The protocol is implemented across three primary modules:

1. **`dsf_ai_service/guala_caretaker_hand.py`**:
   - `intercept_hazard(world, entity_id)`: Implements kinematic interception and pull-away.
   - `enforce_timeout(world, duration_beats)`: Transfers Guala to `playpen` coordinates and maintains custody.
   - `confiscate_to_shelf(world, object_id, shelf_id)`: Elevates forbidden items out of reach.
2. **`guala_caretaker/caretaker.py`**:
   - State tracking: `st["disciplinary_tier"]`, `st["timeout_remaining_ticks"]`.
   - Progressive escalation: Warning $\to$ Confiscation $\to$ Time-out $\to$ Repair.
   - Caretaker yields immediately when a parent/human tutor intervenes (`TEACHING` marker).
3. **`dsf_ai_service/guala_home_world.py`**:
   - Invariant physical boundaries: Enforcing elevation and doorway clearance laws.
   - Clean domestic baseline: Clearing stray food and resetting domestic baseline during sleep cycles.
