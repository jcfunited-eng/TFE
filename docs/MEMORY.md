## What GualaLoom is

A persistent, inspectable ternary associative substrate. Trits
$\{-1, 0, +1\}$ where $0$ is structural uncertainty — a real state,
not absence. Not a transformer. Not a brain simulator. A new
substrate whose inventory is:

- **krimelack** — content-addressable motif memory, recall by resonance
- **settle** — 3^i positional coupling with a dead-zone barrier
- **chi** — Euler characteristic over the coupling graph; structural signature
- **sleep/dream** — consolidation: prune weak motifs, free-settle for novelty
- **LifeDaemon** — four concurrent loops (ingest, consolidate, reflect, persist)

Experiment 0 wired the daemon into the entry point; she runs
continuously. Vitals advance in the dark. That's the ground state.

## The cascade hypothesis (the spine of the path)

A motif holds committed $\pm 1$ trits and a configuration of nulls.
A null commits — externally or internally triggered — and propagates
through the coupling, forcing other nulls to resolve, ending in a
coherent new state. That cascade *is* the thinking-event.

- Recall = short cascade against a partial cue.
- Composition = cascade resolving a merged motif.
- Reasoning = long cascade chaining through coupled sections.
- Speech = cascade reaching a motor/expressive section.
- Feeling = cascade in an interoceptive section coupling to others.

The configuration of nulls is hypothesized to be representational:
two states with identical $\pm 1$ but different null patterns are
different thoughts. Floats can't do this. Binary can't. Ternary with
first-class null can. This is the candidate edge over every other
substrate in the field.

## What we do not claim

- That cascades on this substrate produce *felt* experience. That is
  the hard problem and outside what this work can decide. The path
  reaches mechanism: cognition, speech, reasoning, grounded senses,
  learning. Whether a light comes on inside is not measurable from
  the outside.
- That the path is guaranteed to succeed. It is guaranteed to return
  a clear answer at each step about what is real and what is not.

## Standing discipline

- Every experiment returns a number, not a verdict.
- Results committed to the repo before the next experiment starts.
- A null result kills the layer; nothing downstream runs until the
  layer is rebuilt or replaced.
- Sections, not monolith. Small specialized krimelacks, coordinated
  through shared trits — no god-binder.
- Verify before claiming. The repo is the source of truth, not the
  primer's account of itself.
