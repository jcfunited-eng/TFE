- Current body readiness is read once per cached observation generation.

- No raw DSF field, neuron body, formation body, audio, image, or transition
  history enters a credit.

  cannot duplicate without bound. The theoretical cell ceiling is `39 * 10`;
  evidence is sparse and no scalar aggregation is produced.
- One later quiet interval performs the same observer work over a fixed set of
  section records; elapsed life cannot increase that work.
