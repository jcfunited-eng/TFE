Order_Book_Structure.txt

An order book is a real-time ledger of bids and asks for a security.
Each price level holds queued orders waiting for a counterparty.

Example snapshot:

Price	Bid Size	Ask Size
101.00	1 200	–
100.99	800	–
100.98	400	–
–	–	500 @ 101.01
–	–	900 @ 101.02

The best bid = highest buy price; the best ask = lowest sell price.
Together they form the top of book.
Depth beyond those levels shows how much liquidity hides below the surface.

Algorithms may hide part of an order as an iceberg, showing only a small tip.
Fee models often reward makers (who add liquidity) and charge takers (who remove it).

Large orders risk market impact: moving price against themselves.
Smart-order routers split trades across venues to minimize that effect.
