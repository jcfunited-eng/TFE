Behavioral Finance and Market Psychology.txt

Markets are moved by minds, not formulas alone.

Common biases

Loss aversion: pain from loss exceeds joy from gain.

Anchoring: clinging to initial reference points.

Overconfidence: overestimating skill or information.

Herd behavior: following the crowd into bubbles or panics.

Emotional cycle of markets
Optimism → Euphoria → Anxiety → Fear → Capitulation → Hope → Recovery.
Knowing the cycle doesn’t always stop it, but awareness tempers extremes.

Prospect theory shows investors prefer a sure small gain over a risky larger one, yet will gamble to avoid loss.
Case sketches:
The dot-com boom (2000) and the 2008 crisis both revealed mass overconfidence followed by collective fear.
Disciplined processes—checklists, automation, and diversification—help counter emotion.
