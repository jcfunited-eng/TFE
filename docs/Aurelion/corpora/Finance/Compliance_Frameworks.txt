Compliance_Frameworks.txt

Financial markets run on trust enforced by rule.
In the U.S., the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) sets disclosure and fair-dealing standards; FINRA oversees broker-dealers; the CFTC governs derivatives.

Key pillars:

Reg NMS – requires best execution across venues.

Short-Sale Rule (Reg SHO) – prevents unchecked downward pressure.

Know-Your-Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money-Laundering (AML) – verify identities and trace funds.

Suitability – recommendations must fit the client’s risk tolerance.

In Europe, MiFID II harmonizes transparency and investor protection.
Firms must record communications, disclose costs, and publish execution quality.

Compliance staff act like the nervous system’s reflex arcs: constantly monitoring, correcting, and reporting to keep the organism healthy.
