Risk_and_Settlement.txt

Risk is the shadow of opportunity.
Markets classify it so they can measure and manage it.

Market risk – prices move unfavorably.

Credit risk – a counterparty defaults.

Liquidity risk – positions cannot be exited quickly.

Operational risk – system or human failure.

Clearinghouses demand margin—collateral posted by both sides—to absorb potential losses.
If losses exceed margin, a margin call occurs.
Daily mark-to-market ensures accounts stay solvent.

Settlement finalizes ownership transfer; in modern systems it is largely electronic.
Central securities depositories like DTCC or Euroclear record the definitive ledger entries.

Risk cannot be eliminated, only transformed.
The art of finance is to choose which risks to keep and which to lay off.
