Global_Markets.txt

Trading never truly sleeps.
When New York closes, London is already handing off to Tokyo and Hong Kong.

Major equity venues:

NYSE, NASDAQ – U.S.

LSE, Euronext, Deutsche Börse – Europe

TSE, HKEX, SGX – Asia-Pacific

Index families (S&P 500, Dow, Nikkei 225, FTSE 100) summarize regional performance.

Beneath equities lies a web of derivatives, commodities, and foreign-exchange markets:

CME Group, ICE, Eurex – futures and options

FX interbank network – global currency pairs traded 24 h a day

Clearinghouses such as DTCC or LCH Clearnet sit at the center, reducing counter-party risk by standing between buyer and seller.
