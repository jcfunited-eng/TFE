### Lesson Plan: Introduction to Grammar

#### Objective:
By the end of this lesson, students will understand the basics of grammar, including sentences, parts of speech, and punctuation.

---

### Section 1: What is Grammar?
**Explanation:**  
Grammar is the set of rules that helps us use words correctly when we speak or write. Think of it as a guide that helps us put words together in ways that everyone can understand.

---

### Section 2: Sentences
**Explanation:**  
A sentence is a group of words that express a complete thought. Every sentence needs a subject (who or what the sentence is about) and a verb (what the subject is doing).  
**Example Sentence:** "The cat (subject) runs (verb)."

---

### Section 3: Parts of Speech
**Explanation:**  
Words can be classified into different types called parts of speech. Here are the main types:  
1. **Nouns** - Names of people, places, or things (e.g., dog, city).  
2. **Verbs** - Action words (e.g., run, jump).  
3. **Adjectives** - Words that describe nouns (e.g., blue, tall).  
4. **Adverbs** - Words that describe verbs (e.g., quickly, softly).

---

### Section 4: Punctuation
**Explanation:**  
Punctuation helps us know how to read sentences. It includes marks like periods, commas, and question marks.  
- **Period (.)** - Ends a statement.  
- **Comma (,)** - Tells us to take a small pause.  
- **Question Mark (?)** - Shows that a sentence is asking something.

---

3. The children play in the park.
