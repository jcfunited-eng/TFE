Speech 2: The Coffee Bean Principle
Topic: Responding to adversity with ethical strength.
"Friends and colleagues, we all face adversity. It's a part of life. When challenges arise, we have a choice in how we respond. We can be a potato, an egg, or a coffee bean.
Imagine these three items dropped into a pot of boiling water, a metaphor for a tough ethical dilemma or a crisis.
The potato goes in strong and hard, but the boiling water makes it soft and weak.
The egg is fragile, its liquid interior protected by a thin shell, but the boiling water makes it hard inside.
The coffee bean, however, is unique. After it is exposed to the boiling water, it changes the water and creates something new and rich.
Our ethical framework is what determines our response. Are we weakened by pressure? Do we become hard and inflexible? Or are we like the coffee bean, using the heat of adversity to transform our environment for the better?
In the face of ethical challenges, we have the power to 'change the water'—to improve the situation through integrity, fairness, and concern for others. The only thing that truly matters is what happens within us. Let's choose to be the coffee bean."
