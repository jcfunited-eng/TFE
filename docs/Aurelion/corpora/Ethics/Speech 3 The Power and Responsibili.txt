Speech 3: The Power and Responsibility of Communication
Topic: Ethical obligations in how we speak and listen.
"Public speaking and communication are powerful tools. They carry significant ethical obligations. We advocate for truthfulness, accuracy, honesty, and reason as essential to the integrity of communication.
This means more than just avoiding outright lies. It means actively seeking to understand others before judging them. It means respecting a diversity of perspectives and tolerating dissent to foster informed and responsible decision-making.
As ethical communicators, we must also accept responsibility for the short- and long-term consequences of our words. Unethical communication—such as distortion, intimidation, or the expression of hatred—degrades individuals and humanity.
Whether in a formal speech or casual conversation, let us promote climates of caring and mutual understanding. Our words have impact. Let us ensure that impact is a positive one, building bridges of understanding and justice in our communities."
