NASA confirmed that the Artemis II mission remains on schedule for its upcoming crewed lunar flyby, marking the first time astronauts will travel beyond low-Earth orbit since Apollo 17. 
Engineers successfully completed thermal vacuum testing of the Orion spacecraft, validating its life support and power systems under simulated deep-space conditions. 
Mission control teams are finalizing trajectory simulations and emergency abort procedures, ensuring full compliance with updated spaceflight safety protocols. 
The agency emphasized its continued partnership with international and commercial partners to advance the broader Artemis program and eventual surface landing objectives.
