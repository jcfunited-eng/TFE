The National Weather Service reports an approaching low-pressure system moving across the Midwest, expected to bring scattered thunderstorms and heavy rainfall through late Sunday. 
Meteorologists warn of potential flash flooding in low-lying areas and localized wind gusts exceeding 40 miles per hour. 
Temperatures are forecast to remain above seasonal averages, with warm, humid air mass conditions persisting into early next week. 
A secondary cold front is likely to follow, bringing cooler, drier air by Wednesday.
