### Lesson Plan: Have You Learned?

**Objective:** To help students reflect on what they've learned and to encourage self-evaluation.

---

#### 1. Gentle Explanation
Learning is a journey where we discover new things every day. Sometimes, it’s important to check in with ourselves and think about what we’ve learned. This helps us understand our progress and feel good about our efforts. Today, we will explore different ways to ask ourselves and others, "Have you learned?"

---

#### 2. What Does "Have You Learned?" Mean?
- This phrase can be a question we ask ourselves or others.
- It helps us think about knowledge, skills, or experiences we've gained.
- It’s a way to celebrate our achievements, no matter how big or small.

---

#### 3. Ways to Reflect on Learning
- **Think about successes:** What did you do well? Celebrate those small wins!
- **Identify challenges:** What was hard? Understanding difficulties helps us grow.
- **Consider changes:** How have you changed since learning something new? Growth can be exciting!

---

#### 4. Super Easy Questions to Ask Yourself
- What is something new I learned today?
- How did I feel when I learned this?
- Would I want to learn something similar in the future?

---

#### 5. Closing Thoughts
Learning is not just about getting the right answers; it's also about understanding ourselves better. By asking "Have you learned?" we can appreciate our journey, identify areas for growth, and keep moving forward with curiosity!

---

### Exercises

1. **Personal Reflection:** Take a few minutes to write down one thing you learned this week. How did you feel when you realized it?

2. **Partner Reflection:** Share your learning experience with a friend or family member. Ask them what they learned this week too. Discuss your thoughts together!

3. **Goal Setting:** Think of one thing you would like to learn next. Write down how you plan to learn it. Will you use books, videos, or ask someone for help? 

---

Feel free to take your time with each section, and remember, learning is all about enjoying the process and growing step by step!
