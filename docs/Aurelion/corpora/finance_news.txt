The S&P 500 closed slightly lower on Friday as investors evaluated mixed earnings from major technology firms and the latest labor market data. 
Bond yields remained elevated after the Federal Reserve indicated that interest rates may stay higher for longer. 
Energy stocks gained as crude oil prices climbed above $80 a barrel, while semiconductor shares lagged following weaker chip export data from Asia. 
Analysts noted that overall market sentiment remains cautious but stable, with institutional investors watching inflation indicators and supply chain trends closely.
