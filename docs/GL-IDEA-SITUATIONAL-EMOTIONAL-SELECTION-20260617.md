## The seed

Beyond chi-coherence selection (grandurun): selection should be conditioned on:

- **Situational state** — current activity, who's present, recent context, current needs state (stab/nov/conn). The selection rule isn't just "what coheres with input chi" but "what coheres with input chi *given that we're in conversation with Joe vs. dreaming alone vs. attending a picture*."

- **Emotional weighting** — affect (valence, arousal, novelty, stability, connection, surprise) biases what gets selected, not just what gets encoded. The clarity-invariance-uncage patch already ties affect to clarity on the WRITE path. The symmetric move is affect-biased selection on the READ path.

## Why it's coherent with the architecture

The substrate already carries the dimensions: needs state, affect kwargs on every atlas.record, presence flags (joe/wc/c1). The selection rule currently ignores them — grandurun considers strength + chi-distance only. Adding situational + affect terms is a natural extension of grandurun's amplitude formulation: `a = √strength · exp(i·φ_chi) · w_situation · w_affect` where w terms come from the substrate's existing state, not new constants.
