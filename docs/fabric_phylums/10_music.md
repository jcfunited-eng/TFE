# 10 MUSIC — the wave laws played on purpose
Physics / waves add, worn as art. The raw material is not sound —
it is expectation.

ESSENCE: pitch is vibration speed — a string shaking 440 times a
  second IS the note A; faster is higher. (pitch = frequency)
ROOT: physics / waves — sound is air rippling; the ear counts the
  ripples.
CANNOT: no pitch without repetition — a single clap has loudness
  but no note; noise is vibration that keeps no steady count.
THREAD: engineering (a bridge has a note — march in step and you
  can play it to death), radio (tuning a dial is choosing a pitch
  of light), the body (the inner ear is a coiled keyboard, one
  station per speed).
ASKED-AS: guitar tuning high low deep squeaky bass treble hertz humming whistling voice

ESSENCE: harmony is ratio — two pitches agree when their speeds
  relate by small whole numbers (2:1, 3:2), and grind when they
  don't.
ROOT: physics / waves add — small ratios let the summed wave repeat
  its pattern quickly. UNSURE why nerves prefer the tidy sum, but
  the preference spans most cultures.
CANNOT: no instrument can have every interval pure at once — the
  ratios refuse to tile the octave; every piano is a negotiated
  compromise, all its fifths slightly and deliberately wrong.
THREAD: mathematics (harmony was the first physics ever written as
  number — Pythagoras heard arithmetic), machines (gear teeth mesh
  in small whole ratios or grind — a gearbox is a chord).
ASKED-AS: chords clashing dissonance singing duet sweet sour thirds blending consonance grating beating

ESSENCE: rhythm is expectation and its games — a beat teaches the
  body when the next hit lands, then music earns its thrills by
  paying on time, early, late, or not at all.
ROOT: the body / brains are prediction engines; a steady pulse is
  the cheapest promise there is.
CANNOT: no surprise without a promise first — syncopation over no
  beat is just noise; an expectation never built cannot be cheated.
THREAD: humor (setup, then the twisted payoff — comic timing IS
  this), sports (a feint only works on a defender you have taught
  the rhythm to), markets UNSURE (crowds taught a pattern, then
  paid or broken).
ASKED-AS: drums dancing clapping tempo groove offbeat swinging toe tapping metronome shuffle drops

ESSENCE: the octave is the same note wearing a higher hat — double
  any vibration and every ear files it as kin (2:1); music's whole
  map folds at that seam.
ROOT: physics / waves add — at 2:1 the fast wave nests exactly
  inside the slow; near-universal across cultures, UNSURE only at
  the edges (some traditions stretch it slightly).
CANNOT: pitch is a spiral, not a ladder — climb and you arrive
  above your own start; no melody rises forever (the endless-rise
  trick is a barber pole, not a staircase).
THREAD: mathematics (clock arithmetic — numbers that fold and
  return home), color (the color wheel borrows this fold; light,
  barely one octave wide, never truly repeats — UNSURE).
ASKED-AS: men women singing together keyboard higher lower soprano bass matching circle round

ESSENCE: tension and release is the engine — music leaves home (the
  key's rest note), builds strain, and pays it off by returning; a
  cadence is a promise kept.
ROOT: rhythm's law (this file) widened from time onto pitch.
CANNOT: no resolution without departure — a piece that never
  leaves its rest note cannot end, only stop. All release is
  bought with tension first, at full price.
THREAD: storytelling (every plot is a long cadence: home, trouble,
  home), sports (the held breath before the penalty kick is the
  unresolved chord), cooking (appetite built, then fed).
ASKED-AS: chorus buildup drop ending satisfying progression suspense hanging unfinished song verse closure

ESSENCE: a scale is a chosen subset — from the smooth continuum of
  all pitches, a culture picks a handful per octave and calls them
  home; the choice is the flavor.
ROOT: the octave's fold plus harmony's ratios — the picks cluster
  near the small-ratio landmarks, then differ in the details.
CANNOT: no wrong notes without a fence — the continuum permits
  everything, so it expresses nothing; only a chosen subset makes
  "off-key" possible, and music is spent against that fence.
THREAD: language (each tongue picks ~40 sounds from the mouth's
  continuum — same fence, same foreign accent when crossed),
  cooking (a cuisine is a pantry's chosen subset), painting (a
  palette is a scale for the eye).
ASKED-AS: major minor pentatonic blues sharps flats modes exotic foreign eastern happy sad

ESSENCE: silence is part of the music — a rest is played, not
  skipped; a note's meaning lives partly in the gaps around it.
ROOT: rhythm's law — expectation runs on through the gap, so the
  gap carries load.
CANNOT: no phrase without gaps — sound with every space filled
  cannot speak, only roar; fill all the silences and the melody
  drowns in itself.
THREAD: painting (negative space does half the work), building (a
  room is walls bought to own an emptiness), conversation (the
  pause before the answer says more than the answer).
ASKED-AS: quiet dramatic breath spacing phrasing stopping breaks empty air hush suspense drop

ESSENCE: loudness is not meaning — volume is the wave's height,
  meaning is its shape; the quietest bar can be the heaviest.
ROOT: physics / waves — height and shape are separate dials on the
  same wave.
CANNOT: no fixing a dull phrase by playing it louder —
  amplification scales what was said, it cannot add unsaid. And
  all-loud is as flat as all-soft: contrast carries, level cannot.
THREAD: language (italics on every word are italics on none),
  teaching (the quiet correction outlives the shouted one), law
  UNSURE (the loudest argument is not the strongest).
ASKED-AS: turning up soft whispering shouting dynamics blasting powerful emotional speakers concert headphones

ESSENCE: timbre is the recipe of overtones — every real note is a
  chord in disguise: one main vibration plus quieter multiples
  sounding together, and the mix is the voice.
ROOT: physics / waves add — a string vibrates whole, in halves, in
  thirds, all at once; the blend is the fingerprint.
CANNOT: no rich voice from a lone pure vibration — strip the
  overtones and violin, trumpet, and mother all collapse into the
  same lab beep.
THREAD: color (white light is a chord of colors; a prism is an ear
  for it), the body (you know a voice before its words arrive —
  the overtone recipe signs it), cooking (a stock: one taste built
  from many quiet ones).
