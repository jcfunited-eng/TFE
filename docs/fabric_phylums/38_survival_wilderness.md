# 38 SURVIVAL AND WILDERNESS — physics with the padding removed
Nothing new happens in the wild. The house's laws apply, minus the
house — and the deadlines are real.

ESSENCE: survival runs on the rule of threes — about three minutes
  without air, three hours without shelter in killing weather,
  three days without water, three weeks without food. The order of
  dying sets the order of working.
ROOT: medicine + the body / the body's reserves come in different
  sizes — a breath of air, a night of heat, days of water, weeks
  of fat.
CANNOT: no willing the clocks longer, and no working them out of
  order — gathering food on the three-week clock while the
  three-hour clock runs is the classic way to die.
THREAD: medicine (airway, breathing, circulation — same list, same
  order), money (a business suffocates on cash flow before it
  starves on profit), strategy (triage: fight the nearest deadline).
ASKED-AS: lost hiking stranded desert freezing thirst hunger panic emergency priorities first steps

ESSENCE: shelter before thirst, thirst before hunger — the cold
  night kills tonight, the dry throat this week, the empty belly
  next month; so build, then drink, then hunt.
ROOT: the rule of threes, turned from clock into work order.
CANNOT: no reordering by appetite — hunger shouts loudest and
  matters least. The killer on the schedule is the quiet one.
THREAD: building (roof before furniture), maintenance (the leak
  before the paint), medicine (the bleeding before the diet).
ASKED-AS: stranded camping first priority freezing exposure forage eating canteen sequence checklist plan

ESSENCE: fire is the wilderness multi-tool — one skill yields
  heat, light, dry clothes, safe water, cooked food, a signal, and
  courage.
ROOT: physics + chemistry / burning releases stored sunlight;
  boiling kills through cooking's one-way door.
CANNOT: no fire without fuel, air, and heat together — remove any
  one and it dies. Wet fuel spends the flame's own heat drying
  itself before giving any back.
THREAD: cooking (sterilizing is the same door), the mind (fear
  shrinks in firelight — the signal is aimed at yourself too), the
  human journey (the hearth was the first address).
ASKED-AS: campfire matches lighter kindling tinder starting smoke sparks flint damp wood warmth

ESSENCE: water runs downhill, and so does the way home — walk down
  the slope to the trickle, the trickle to the stream, the stream
  to the river; rivers collect water from uphill and people from
  downhill.
ROOT: physics / gravity — every drop obeys, so the land is
  organized around its drains.
CANNOT: no stream that climbs a ridge — walking down cannot miss
  the drainage. UNSURE past that: in truly empty country the river
  leads to swamp and gorge, not town; the second half of the law
  needs inhabited land.
THREAD: movement of goods (cities sit where rivers meet — the same
  law, next file), the deep earth (a canyon is this law given
  time), the living world (every path in dry country bends toward
  the waterhole).
ASKED-AS: lost woods finding follow creek civilization mountains valley hiking direction escape trail

ESSENCE: lost means stop — the moment you know you are lost, sit
  down; walking multiplies the error, spends the heat and water
  budgets, and moves you off the map the searchers will draw.
ROOT: chance + risk / an unguided walk spreads — every blind step
  widens the circle someone else must search.
CANNOT: no walking out by feel — without references the body veers
  in arcs it cannot feel, and the confident march inscribes a
  circle. Confidence is not a compass.
THREAD: money (the first law of holes: stop digging), strategy
  (the lost column that keeps marching), the mind (panic's first
  command is always "move," and it is always wrong).
ASKED-AS: missing person rescue team wandering stay put found trail hug tree waiting

ESSENCE: the body is a furnace on a budget — water strips heat
  many times faster than air, wind carries off the warm layer as
  fast as skin rebuilds it, and wet plus wind outkills bare cold.
ROOT: cooking's first law / heat flows hot to cold — faster by
  touch, faster still by moving flow.
CANNOT: no staying warm wet and windblown — the two thieves
  together outdraw the furnace. Cloth that holds water cannot
  insulate wet: it presses the thief against the skin.
THREAD: the body's own trick (sweating hires exactly these thieves
  on purpose), engineering (fins and fans on every radiator),
  cooking (blowing on the spoonful — the same physics in miniature).
ASKED-AS: hypothermia soaked rain shivering windchill jacket parka cotton wool drenched freezing swim

ESSENCE: navigate by handrails — a river, a ridge, a coastline, a
  held bearing; progress is only real along references that do not
  move, checked often.
ROOT: astronomy + navigation / position exists only against fixed
  things; the walker carries no fixed point inside.
CANNOT: no honest reckoning by body-feel — legs miscount and the
  inner compass drifts. A few degrees of unchecked error, held for
  a day, is a valley missed.
THREAD: sailing (the whole history of navigation is better
  handrails — coast, then stars, then clocks), money (a budget is
  a handrail for spending), evidence (a measurement without a
  fixed standard wanders the same way).
ASKED-AS: lost straight line landmark map north gps direction sense orienting shortcut woods

ESSENCE: tracks are the ground's memory — weight, direction, gait,
  and time press into soil, snow, and bent grass, and the writing
  can be read back for hours or days.
ROOT: physics / pressed matter stays pressed; sun, wind, and rain
  erase slowly enough that the fading itself tells the age.
CANNOT: no crossing ground without writing on it — weight cannot
  be withheld, only placed where the page is poor: rock, water.
  And the track records what happened, never what was intended.
THREAD: evidence (forensics is tracking indoors — the fingerprint
  is a track), computing (logs are tracks), the deep earth (strata
  and craters — tracks with ages in millions).
