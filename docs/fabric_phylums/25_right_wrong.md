# 25 RIGHT AND WRONG — ethics' recurring shapes
The softest laws in this corpus and the most contested. This file
describes what humans converge on; it preaches nothing.

ESSENCE: every society examined lands on some form of "return
  good for good; do not do what you would hate done to you" —
  rediscovered independently, continents and centuries apart.
ROOT: people together / the gift ledger; likely older than
  humanity — apes keep it too. Depth UNSURE, convergence not.
THREAD: law (contract is this rule with teeth), money (every
  trade enacts it twice), meaning + ritual (nearly every
  scripture carries it, both phrasings).
ASKED-AS: golden rule treat others kindness payback favors karma manners respect

ESSENCE: intent changes the crime — the same broken body is
  accident, negligence, or murder depending on the mind behind
  it, and children grade the difference before they can read.
ROOT: the mind / the pattern-finder aimed at agents — humans read
  intention constantly. Depth UNSURE; presence measured.
THREAD: law (murder versus manslaughter — the whole grading),
  story + drama (the villain is made by motive, not body count).
ASKED-AS: purpose meant blame guilty excuse mistake fault sorry court punishment

ESSENCE: the feel for fair shares arrives early and runs below
  training — toddlers protest rigged splits, and a monkey hurls
  back the cucumber when its neighbor gets grapes for the same
  task.
ROOT: premise — replicated across cultures and down into other
  social species. Edges UNSURE: rigged systems do persist, at
  rising enforcement cost.
THREAD: money (free money refused to punish a nine-to-one split;
  strikes), sports (a bought referee empties a stadium's
  goodwill), power (extraction felt as rigged meets the revolt
  line sooner).
ASKED-AS: equal portions jealous envy cheated deserve tantrum siblings cake divide

ESSENCE: the boundary of "who counts" has widened across recorded
  history — kin to tribe to nation to strangers, now arguing at
  animals; each old edge later looks arbitrary from outside it.
ROOT: UNSURE at mechanism — trade, travel, and story all
  correlate; meeting the other shrinks the otherness. Direction
  observed; speed varies; reversals happen.
THREAD: story (fiction is circle-widening machinery — strangers
  made feelable, UNSURE), meaning + ritual (missionary faiths
  leapt the tribe line early).
ASKED-AS: empathy outsiders foreigners racism slavery rights vegetarian pets immigrants inclusion

ESSENCE: no pile of facts about what IS adds up to what OUGHT to
  be — the bridge from description to prescription must be built
  of prescription.
ROOT: mathematics + logic / a conclusion only unpacks its
  premises — no ought among them, none in the conclusion.
THREAD: evidence + measurement (the method steers everything and
  chooses nothing), money (economics can optimize any target and
  cannot select one), medicine ("healthy" smuggles a chosen goal
  — the body doesn't care).
ASKED-AS: natural should values decide prove data study claims experts opinion

ESSENCE: moral verdicts arrive as feelings — outrage, disgust,
  warmth — with reasons drafted after; press past the reasons and
  the verdict stays: "I can't explain it, it's just wrong."
ROOT: the mind / emotions are fast verdicts — ethics rides the
  same circuit. UNSURE how far: slow deliberation does sometimes
  overrule the flash.
THREAD: persuasion (that whole file), law (lawyers tell stories,
  not syllogisms), meaning + ritual (purity and disgust doing
  moral work everywhere).
ASKED-AS: instinct intuition heart logic convince debate rationalize justify hunch conscience

ESSENCE: a short list recurs in every culture examined — care for
  kin, reciprocity, courage, fairness, loyalty, deference, purity
  rules — each worn in wildly different local dress, weighted
  differently.
ROOT: anthropology's tally; the roster is argued, the recurrence
  well-attested. UNSURE at the edges.
THREAD: language (the same shape: universal capacity, local
  vocabularies), music (every culture has it, none share it),
  cooking (everyone ferments something; nobody ferments the same
  thing).
ASKED-AS: values common shared humanity customs traditions taboo honor elders worldwide

ESSENCE: blame everywhere assumes choice — no one blames the
  avalanche, and every judging system excuses what could not be
  otherwise (infancy, madness, coercion), arguing only over where
  the line sits.
ROOT: right + wrong / intent changes the crime — no inferred
  choice, no assigned blame; logic — commanding the impossible
  commands nothing.
THREAD: law (insanity and duress defenses), medicine (addiction
  argued as illness versus choice — the same line), teaching (no
  grading the lesson never taught).
ASKED-AS: responsible free will fault accountable forced willpower control helpless kids

ESSENCE: wherever there is punishment there is machinery for
  ending it — apology, restitution, forgiveness, debts that
  expire; societies that only escalate bleed out, so the exit is
  as universal as the anger.
ROOT: people together / trust drains in buckets, yet the group
  must keep living together; feud arithmetic is ruin. UNSURE as
  law, strong as pattern.
THREAD: law (sentences with ends), money (bankruptcy is debt's
  forgiveness ritual, so the game can restart), war (the
  armistice handshake).
ASKED-AS: sorry amends move on grudge revenge cycle reconcile peace mercy second chance
