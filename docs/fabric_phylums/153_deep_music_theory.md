# 153 DEEP MUSIC THEORY — why organised sound works
Music holds the raw laws: pitch is speed, harmony is ratio, rhythm is
expectation. This is the floor built on top of them — what a tradition
constructs once it has those: the carving of scales, the compromises in
tuning, the grammar of chords and lines, and the memory management that
turns a stretch of sound into a piece. Not taste. Every rule here also
forbids something, and where the evidence is unsettled it says UNSURE.

ESSENCE: the quiet multiples inside one note arrive in a fixed order —
  octave first, then the fifth, the fourth, the major third — and that order
  is the ranking music has used ever since. The intervals were never chosen.
  They were found already sounding inside every note.
ROOT: physics / a string fixed at both ends can only hold whole numbers of
  half-waves, so its available vibrations are the whole-number multiples and
  nothing in between.
THREAD: light (a hot gas emits fixed lines for the same whole-number
  reason), machines (a shaft's dangerous speeds are its multiples), the
  mouth (a sung vowel is this same stack with some members boosted).

ESSENCE: consonance and dissonance are not beauty and ugliness, they are
  stability and wanting-to-move. Rank the intervals by how simply their
  speeds relate and you get a ladder, not a fence, and every style draws its
  own line across that ladder.
ROOT: music / harmony is ratio; the mind / a state the ear cannot predict a
  landing for is filed as unfinished rather than as unpleasant.
THREAD: arts (nothing is perceived except against its surround), language (a
  swear word's force belongs entirely to its era), law (what is scandalous
  is a date, not a fact).
ASKED-AS: dissonance clashing harsh chords ugly modern jazz weird rules changed tension unresolved

ESSENCE: a pitch carries two independent facts — which of the twelve names
  it is, and how high it sits — and only the name travels. That is why a man
  and a child singing one tune are singing one tune, and why a scale is
  defined once and copied up and down forever.
ROOT: music / the octave folds the map; the mind / a category survives any
  change that leaves its defining relation intact.
THREAD: mathematics (clock arithmetic returning home), colour (a wheel that
  comes back round), families (the same name at three generations).
ASKED-AS: octave same note higher lower another name doubling voices men women register

ESSENCE: a scale is not only a chosen handful of pitches, it is a handful
  spaced unevenly on purpose. Because the steps differ in size, every note
  has a unique address — you can hear which one you are on — and equal steps
  throw that away and leave the ear with no position at all.
ROOT: music / a scale is a chosen subset of the continuum; the mind / a
  location is recognised by its neighbourhood, so a neighbourhood identical
  everywhere carries no location.
THREAD: navigation (a coastline of identical bays cannot be fixed on),
  language (a tongue's sounds spaced so they do not collide), maps (a grid
  with no landmarks in it).
ASKED-AS: scale seven notes why steps uneven whole tone chromatic lost key spacing major

ESSENCE: the pure ratios refuse to fit inside the octave, so somebody has to
  decide where the leftover goes. Pile it into a few keys and most are
  lovely while two are unusable; spread it evenly and every key is slightly
  wrong and every key is playable. The second bargain won.
ROOT: music / no instrument can have every interval pure at once;
  mathematics / a remainder can be moved around but never deleted.
THREAD: engineering (a tolerance that has to be allocated somewhere in an
  assembly), money (rounding error that must land in some account), law (a
  rule made uniform loses fit everywhere and gains portability).
ASKED-AS: tuning piano equal temperament out of tune keys just intonation frets compromise wolf

ESSENCE: a key is a home note plus the habit of returning to it, and a few
  bars in, the listener is holding that note whether they know it or not.
  Everything after is measured from it — the same chord is arrival or
  departure depending only on which note the ear decided was home.
ROOT: the mind / prediction builds a reference point from early evidence and
  then judges everything against it; music / tension and release needs a
  home to leave.
THREAD: arts (an element read against its field), language (a pronoun
  meaning entirely whoever was named last), navigation (every bearing
  needing a declared north).
ASKED-AS: key signature what means home note tonic sounds finished major minor centre

ESSENCE: an interval has a sound of its own and a job inside a key, and
  those are two questions. A minor third is a colour; that same distance
  between the sixth degree and home is a route. Learners hear the colours,
  players hear the jobs, and the jobs are what predict.
ROOT: this file / a key centre turns every note into a position rather than
  just a pitch; the mind / a thing is filed twice, by what it is and by what
  it does.
THREAD: language (a word's part of speech is a job, not a spelling), chess
  (a piece worth what its square makes it), law (one act as a role in one
  contract and a breach in another).
ASKED-AS: intervals third fifth sound sad happy ear training function degrees steps naming

ESSENCE: a chord is intervals piled on one another, and this tradition piles
  thirds because a third is the widest step that still fuses into one
  object. Stack seconds and the ear hears several notes fighting; stack
  thirds and three notes become one thing with a colour.
ROOT: this file / consonance is how simply the speeds relate, and the third
  is the first step small enough to stack and simple enough to merge; the
  mind / parts that agree are grouped into one object.
THREAD: building (brick courses sized so a wall reads as a surface), cooking
  (ingredients that merge into a sauce against ones that stay separate),
  design (spacing that groups against spacing that separates).
ASKED-AS: chords stacked thirds triad notes together cluster fourths quartal harmony building blocks

ESSENCE: three notes, and only the middle one moves. Put the small third
  underneath and the chord is major; put it on top and it is minor. That one
  semitone is the whole difference between the two moods a tradition runs
  on, and squeezing the frame gives the chords that cannot rest.
ROOT: this file / a chord is stacked thirds and a third comes in two sizes;
  the mind / a large perceived difference can sit on a tiny physical one
  when everything else is held equal.
THREAD: chemistry (an isomer with identical atoms and a different property),
  language (one changed sound flipping a word), light (two pigments
  differing in a single component).
ASKED-AS: major minor chords happy sad difference triad diminished augmented one note quality

ESSENCE: add a fourth note a third above and the chord stops being a place
  and becomes a direction. The seventh is a dissonance built into the chord
  itself, so it now carries unfinished business and the listener starts
  waiting for the answer before anything else happens.
ROOT: this file / dissonance is instability with a preferred direction;
  music / tension is bought before release can be spent.
THREAD: story (a scene ending on a question), selling (a proposal left
  deliberately open), engineering (a spring loaded at assembly so the
  mechanism has a preferred direction).
ASKED-AS: seventh chord jazz bluesy dominant tension unresolved four notes wanting resolve sounds

ESSENCE: turn a chord upside down — move its bottom note up an octave — and
  it is the same chord with the same name and completely different footing.
  Root in the bass is planted, the third leans, the fifth is unstable.
  Identity never changes. Weight always does.
ROOT: this file / the octave fold means the set of notes survives moving one
  of them; the ear / the lowest voice is heard as the ground everything else
  is measured against.
THREAD: building (the same stones as a wall and as a heap), typography (a
  headline placed at the foot of a page), photography (a horizon set high or
  low in the same landscape).
ASKED-AS: inversion chord bass note slash chords first second position same different weight

ESSENCE: when one chord follows another, each voice should move as little as
  it can and common notes should simply stay put. Small moves let the ear
  keep following the same lines through the change; large jumps in every
  part make it hear a new object instead.
ROOT: the mind / a thing is tracked through change by continuity, so a part
  that moves far is heard as a different part starting; this file / chords
  are made of lines and only look vertical on the page.
THREAD: animation (an object that jumps reads as a cut, not a movement),
  dance (weight shifted rather than thrown), writing (a paragraph that moves
  one idea at a time).
ASKED-AS: voice leading chords smooth clunky connecting progression common notes moving parts jumpy

ESSENCE: endings come in strengths. The full stop is the pull chord falling
  home with the home note on top; a weaker version stops on the pull chord
  and reads as a comma; the deceptive one goes anywhere except home and
  reads as a page turned rather than a book closed.
ROOT: this file / a key centre makes return the only real arrival; language
  / a structure needs marked ends or its parts cannot be counted.
THREAD: writing (full stop against semicolon against dash), meetings (a
  decision announced against a topic drifting), law (a judgment that closes
  against one that sends it back).
ASKED-AS: cadence ending phrase finished half deceptive resolution sounds complete stopping punctuation comma

ESSENCE: the chord a fifth above home carries two notes that both want to
  move, in opposite directions — one squeezing up a semitone into home, one
  sliding down into the third. Exactly one chord answers both at once, so
  hearing it is very nearly hearing the answer already.
ROOT: this file / small gaps pull hardest, and a dissonance is an
  instability with a direction; the mind / a prediction with one available
  completion is experienced as near-certainty.
THREAD: chemistry (a molecule strained into one available reaction), story
  (a setup with a single satisfying payoff), games (a threat with exactly
  one defence).
ASKED-AS: dominant chord fifth pull resolve home leading note seventh wants strongest progression tension

ESSENCE: change which note the ear is calling home and every chord in the
  piece is re-priced without one of them changing. That is modulation — not
  new material, new gravity — usually done with a chord belonging to both
  keys and then a cadence that lands in the new one.
ROOT: this file / a chord's meaning is its position relative to home; the
  mind / a reference point can be replaced by evidence, and the replacement
  is not noticed while it is happening.
THREAD: language (a conversation whose topic quietly shifts), politics (a
  window of the normal moving while everyone holds position), travel (waking
  in another time zone and the day still fitting).
ASKED-AS: modulation key change song lifts pivot chord returning home moving keys bridge

ESSENCE: take one set of seven notes, decide a different one is home, and
  you get a different world out of identical material. The notes did not
  move. The pattern of steps around the centre moved, so every distance from
  home changed — and the distances were where the character lived.
ROOT: this file / a scale's character is its uneven spacing measured from
  the centre; this file / a key centre re-prices everything relative to
  itself.
THREAD: mathematics (rotating a sequence into a different object), design
  (the same shapes reading differently once one is made dominant),
  households (the same people, a different head).
ASKED-AS: modes dorian mixolydian same notes different sound white keys folk church centre

ESSENCE: how often the chords change is a rhythm of its own, felt as pace
  even when the notes on top are flying. Two chords a bar feels urgent; one
  chord held eight bars feels like standing still — which is why a fast solo
  over slow harmony still sounds calm.
ROOT: music / rhythm is expectation, and a chord change is an event the ear
  counts; this file / a chord is a place, so changing it is moving.
THREAD: story (scene changes against sentence length), driving (engine noise
  against actual speed), business (activity against decisions taken).
ASKED-AS: chord changes fast slow pace song busy solo static one chord movement feels

ESSENCE: a leap in a melody creates an obligation. Jump a wide interval and
  the ear expects the next move to be a step back inside the gap you opened;
  leap repeatedly one way and the line feels reckless. Melodies are mostly
  steps with a few leaps, and the leaps are the events.
ROOT: the mind / a pattern that opens a gap is held open until something
  fills it; the body / melody was invented on voices, and a voice pays for
  large jumps.
THREAD: writing (a long word among short ones), dance (a jump that lands
  back into the phrase), speech (a raised voice that has to come down).
ASKED-AS: melody leaps steps singable jumpy hard sing memorable line wide intervals writing

ESSENCE: the up-and-down shape of a tune survives things the exact notes do
  not. Change its key, its mode, even several of its intervals, and people
  still recognise it from the pattern of rises and falls. A melody has a
  silhouette, and that is what memory actually keeps.
ROOT: the mind / a rough shape is cheap to store and an exact sequence is
  not; music / the ear measures relations, and up-or-down is the cheapest
  relation there is.
THREAD: faces (recognised from a caricature that gets every measurement
  wrong), handwriting (a signature surviving a shaky hand), maps (a
  coastline recognisable at any size).
ASKED-AS: melody shape contour humming badly still recognise rises falls memorable outline singing tune

ESSENCE: music comes in breaths. A phrase runs about as long as a sung line
  — commonly four bars — and phrases pair up, so a two-bar idea asks and a
  two-bar idea answers, and four of those make the sixteen bars nearly every
  song in the tradition is built on.
ROOT: the body / a phrase is one breath long, because music was sung long
  before it was written; the mind / a chunk must fit inside what can be held
  at once, and that is a few seconds.
THREAD: language (a sentence sized to be parsed in one pass), poetry (the
  line as a breath), building (a bay repeated along a facade).
ASKED-AS: phrases four bars song structure sixteen verse length sections breaks odd counting

ESSENCE: the oldest shape in music is a statement with something answering
  it — a singer and a crowd, a horn section and a guitar, a hand and its
  echo. The answer works by being clearly the same size and clearly not the
  same content, so the pairing is heard as a pairing.
ROOT: people together / turn-taking is already how humans exchange anything;
  this file / phrases pair, and the second has to be heard as a reply.
THREAD: conversation (a question and an answer of matching weight), law
  (claim and reply), work songs (the labour setting the phrase length).
ASKED-AS: call response gospel blues answer phrase singer crowd trading fours echo conversation

ESSENCE: a whole piece can be grown from one tiny idea by doing simple
  things to it — repeat it higher, turn it upside down, stretch it, halve
  it, change the notes and keep the rhythm. The listener recognises the
  family without any conscious work, so it never sounds repetitive.
ROOT: the mind / recognition survives transformation as long as one relation
  is kept intact; this file / a melody is stored as a shape, and a shape can
  be moved and still land.
THREAD: writing (a phrase brought back altered at the close), mathematics (a
  set of operations that leave a thing itself), evolution (variations on a
  body plan that never restarts).
ASKED-AS: motif theme development variation same idea repeated inverted symphony building material song

ESSENCE: two melodies at once only works when each is worth hearing alone
  and they do not move the same way at the same time. Independence is
  manufactured — one leaps while the other steps — and the moment they move
  in parallel the ear fuses them and the second voice is gone.
ROOT: the mind / things that move together are grouped into one object, so
  separateness must be built out of difference in motion; this file / a line
  is tracked by its continuity.
THREAD: conversation (two people talking at once and one going unheard),
  design (two elements that must both read), teams (roles overlapping until
  one is redundant).
ASKED-AS: counterpoint two melodies bach parallel fifths forbidden independent lines voices rules together

ESSENCE: there are only a few ways to arrange several sounds. One line
  alone; one line with everything else supporting it; several lines of equal
  standing; or everyone moving in one rhythm as a block. The choice decides
  where attention goes before a single note is picked.
ROOT: arts / a work needs a figure and a ground, and texture is how many
  figures have been declared; the mind / attention follows one line well and
  several badly.
THREAD: arts (a figure needing servants), writing (a narrator against an
  ensemble), cities (a landmark that needs ordinary buildings).
ASKED-AS: texture melody accompaniment solo choir parts thick thin layers backing arrangement voices

ESSENCE: play a perfectly even row of identical clicks and nobody hears an
  even row — the ear packs them into twos or threes and hears one of each
  group as strong. The grouping is added by the listener, so a bare pulse
  already contains a metre that nobody played.
ROOT: the mind / perception imposes structure on uniform input instead of
  reporting uniformity; music / a pulse is a promise, and a promise needs
  units.
THREAD: vision (a row of dots seen in clusters), language (a run of
  syllables heard as words), counting (things counted in fives and dozens
  because a plain heap cannot be held).
ASKED-AS: beat counting clicks metronome grouping twos threes strong weak pulse ticking hearing

ESSENCE: a metre is a repeating pattern of strong and weak positions, and
  once established the positions carry weight regardless of what is played
  on them. A note on beat one and the same note on beat four are different
  events, and a silence on a strong beat is the loudest one.
ROOT: this file / the ear groups a pulse and then keeps the grouping running
  by itself; the mind / an expectation persists through evidence rather than
  restarting at every event.
THREAD: sound and song (a word's loud seat), calendars (a week's positions
  carrying weight only because everyone keeps it), ritual (a date made heavy
  by return).
ASKED-AS: time signature bar downbeat counting in strong weak metre waltz feel measure beats

ESSENCE: a syncopation is a note landing where the beat is not, and its
  whole effect comes from the beat still running silently in the listener's
  head. Keep it up with nothing holding the original and the ear gives in,
  re-hears the offbeats as the beat, and the thrill goes.
ROOT: music / no surprise without a promise first; the mind / a prediction
  is revised toward whatever keeps arriving, so the reference itself moves.
THREAD: comedy (a running joke becoming the baseline), markets (an anomaly
  that stops paying once everyone trades it), law (an exception used so
  often it becomes the rule).
ASKED-AS: syncopation offbeat funky groove lost beat backbeat drums against pulse displaced feel

ESSENCE: speed does most of the emotional work people credit to the notes.
  Halve the tempo of a cheerful tune and it becomes a lament with every note
  unchanged; the body reads pace against its own resting rates, so fast is
  arousal and slow is weight before harmony is asked.
ROOT: the body / heart rate and walking pace are the reference clocks every
  listener carries; music / expectation runs on a pulse.
THREAD: speech (the same sentence said fast or slow), film (a scene's
  cutting rate), driving (the speed a road is read at).
ASKED-AS: tempo speed fast slow song sad happy slowed remix bpm mood changing feel

ESSENCE: what carries is the direction of the loudness, not the level. A
  phrase that grows means something a loud phrase does not, and the drop
  into quiet after a climax does more than the climax did. Dynamics is a
  curve drawn over time, and a flat line is the only wrong answer.
ROOT: music / loudness is not meaning; the mind / senses report change, so a
  held level stops being heard as loud at all.
THREAD: speech (a speaker who starts shouting has nowhere left), theatre
  lighting (a stage that begins fully lit), spending (a life run at its
  ceiling with no gear in reserve).
ASKED-AS: dynamics loud quiet crescendo climax volume flat compressed mastering shape swell phrase

ESSENCE: most of what tells a piano from a violin lives in the first
  fraction of a second. Cut off the attack, play only the steady part, and
  listeners confuse instruments they have known all their lives. A sound's
  identity is in how it starts and stops, not in its middle.
ROOT: music / timbre is a recipe of overtones — and that recipe changes
  fastest at the onset, so the onset is where the information sits; the mind
  / a category is decided by its most variable feature.
THREAD: speech (a consonant is a transition, and vowels alone are hard to
  read), arts (a mark recording the movement that made it), tasting (a
  wine's first impression as its own event).
ASKED-AS: instrument recognise piano violin attack pluck synth fake sample tone start note

ESSENCE: the same notes given to different instruments are a different
  piece, and the main job is not colour but clearance — keeping the
  important line where nothing competes with it. Two instruments in one
  range doing different things is how an arrangement turns into mud.
ROOT: the ear / two sounds close in pitch and loudness mask one another and
  the louder wins; arts / a figure requires a ground made on purpose.
THREAD: design (two elements at identical weight), radio (channels spaced so
  they do not bleed), teams (two people holding one responsibility).
ASKED-AS: arrangement muddy mix instruments clashing same range space parts orchestration layering too much

ESSENCE: a piece's form is a plan for what the listener will still be
  holding at each moment. Verse and chorus, departure and return, theme and
  variations — all are schemes for spending new material and paying it back
  before it is forgotten.
ROOT: the mind / working memory is small and recognition is cheap, so
  structure has to be built from returns rather than from accumulation; this
  file / phrases pair, and pairs stack into sections.
THREAD: teaching (spaced review timed before forgetting), buildings (a plan
  you can hold in your head from inside), speeches (the phrase brought back
  at the close).
ASKED-AS: song structure verse chorus form sonata repeat sections remember long classical shape

ESSENCE: the same sound heard twice is not the same experience twice — on
  the second hearing the listener is predicting, and predicting is what
  turns sound into music. Repeat a spoken sentence enough times and it
  begins to sound sung. That is the conversion, caught in the act.
ROOT: the mind / attention shifts from identifying to anticipating once a
  thing is known, and anticipation is what music trades in; music / a beat
  is the cheapest promise there is.
THREAD: sound and song (a phrase that becomes a chant), ritual (an act made
  meaningful by being done again), advertising (a jingle owning a phrase by
  return).
ASKED-AS: repetition repeated song loops chorus again same part boring catchy speech sung

ESSENCE: a rest is not an absence, it is a position with nothing played in
  it, and where it falls decides what it means. Silence on a strong beat is
  an event as loud as a note; at a phrase end it is a breath; in the middle
  of a line it is a stumble. The grid gives the gap meaning.
ROOT: this file / metre makes positions carry weight whether or not they are
  filled; music / expectation runs on through a gap.
THREAD: speech (a pause mid-sentence against one between sentences), theatre
  (a beat held on a line), architecture (a gap in a colonnade at a
  structural position).
ASKED-AS: rest silence missing note pause dramatic stop gap timing space bars beat

ESSENCE: the ear follows the top line and the bottom line and treats
  everything between as filling. That is why a melody buried in an inner
  part disappears, why the lowest note decides how a chord feels, and why
  the outer two voices are most of what an arrangement is doing.
ROOT: the mind / the edges of any field are attended first, and pitch is a
  field with two edges; this file / the lowest note is the ground the rest
  is measured from.
THREAD: design (a page read from top and bottom), photography (the frame's
  edges as active decisions), speech (first and last words being the ones
  kept).
ASKED-AS: bass line melody buried middle parts arrangement top voice lowest note inner hearing

ESSENCE: in this tradition most chords play one of three roles — home, the
  approach, and the pull — and the strong tendency runs home to approach to
  pull to home. It is not a law of physics. It is a grammar a culture built,
  and it works because listeners learned it before speech.
ROOT: this file / the dominant's pull supplies one strong direction and
  everything else is arranged as approach to it; people together / a
  convention becomes an expectation once everyone has heard it since
  childhood.
THREAD: language (a word order that is a habit and feels like a law),
  manners (a greeting sequence), chess (openings that are conventions with
  reasons).
ASKED-AS: chord progressions order tonic subdominant dominant common changes rules pop songs grammar

ESSENCE: two keys are close when they share most of their notes, and moving
  by a fifth changes exactly one of them. That single fact orders all twelve
  keys into a ring, explains which modulations feel easy and which feel like
  a shock, and turns tuning arithmetic into a map.
ROOT: mathematics / stepping by a fixed interval through a folded space
  visits everything and returns to the start; this file / a key is notes
  plus a centre, so distance between keys is overlap.
THREAD: maps (adjacency defined by shared border), language (dialects
  nearest by shared vocabulary), family trees (distance counted in shared
  ancestors).
ASKED-AS: circle fifths keys related close distant sharps flats signatures modulation map order

ESSENCE: two keys can share every note and differ only in which is home —
  that pair sounds like one room in different weather. Two keys can share a
  home and differ in their notes — that is the same weather in a different
  room. Composers use the two swaps for different jobs.
ROOT: this file / a key is notes plus a centre, so there are exactly two
  things available to change; the mind / a change of reference and a change
  of contents are not the same experience.
THREAD: photography (the same scene relit against the same light on another
  scene), writing (one story from a new narrator against one narrator on a
  new story), cooking (the same ingredients by another method).
ASKED-AS: relative minor parallel same notes key change mood shift major switching songs bridge

ESSENCE: the minor scale comes in versions because its natural form has no
  semitone underneath home, and without that squeeze the pull home is weak.
  Raising the note fixes the cadence and opens an awkward wide step, so the
  melodic form raises its neighbour too on the way up.
ROOT: this file / the pull home needs a semitone leading into it; the body /
  singers avoid awkward intervals, and singers wrote these rules.
THREAD: engineering (a fix that creates a second problem needing its own
  fix), spelling (irregular forms marking where two rules met), law (an
  exception carved for one case and then generalised).
ASKED-AS: minor scale harmonic melodic natural three versions raised seventh sixth confusing theory

ESSENCE: a note outside the chord is acceptable when it is plainly on its
  way somewhere — passing between two chord notes, leaning on a neighbour
  and returning, or held over and then stepping down. The ear forgives what
  it can already predict the exit from.
ROOT: this file / dissonance is instability with a preferred direction; the
  mind / a state is tolerable when its resolution is already predicted.
THREAD: driving (a car crossing a lane with an indicator against without
  one), manners (an interruption that announces itself), writing (a
  digression signposted as one).
ASKED-AS: passing notes suspension wrong note sounds fine resolving neighbour held over theory tension

ESSENCE: some chords are built from equal steps the whole way round — the
  diminished seventh, the augmented triad — and a shape with no unequal step
  has no bottom. Any note can be heard as the root, so the chord means
  whatever the next chord decides it meant.
ROOT: this file / a position is known by an uneven neighbourhood;
  mathematics / a symmetric object has no distinguished point.
THREAD: navigation (a featureless plain), law (a clause readable four ways
  until a court chooses), design (a symmetric front with no marked
  entrance).
ASKED-AS: diminished chord scary augmented ambiguous pivot resolve horror tension anywhere sounds unstable

ESSENCE: being in tune is not a property of an instrument, it is an
  agreement between the people playing together. The reference pitch is
  arbitrary and has drifted across centuries; what is not arbitrary is that
  everybody adopts the same one, since the ear hears only mismatch.
ROOT: people together / a standard's whole value is shared adoption; music /
  harmony is a relation, so it can only be wrong between two things.
THREAD: money (a currency worth whatever everyone accepts), time (a clock
  standard that is a choice enforced everywhere), spelling (standardised for
  the same reason).
ASKED-AS: tuning concert pitch 440 out band guitar drift standard agree together temperature

ESSENCE: two notes at almost the same speed make the loudness pulse — a slow
  wobble that quickens as they separate and vanishes the instant they match.
  That wobble is the instrument tuners actually use: they do not listen to
  the pitch, they count the beats and remove them.
ROOT: physics / two waves added drift in and out of step, and the drifting
  is itself slow enough to hear; music / harmony is ratio, so near-misses
  have a signature.
THREAD: engineering (a strobe revealing a tiny speed difference), radio (two
  carriers producing an audible tone), rowing (two crews slipping in and out
  of step).
ASKED-AS: tuning beats wobble two notes close waver guitar strings unison strobe tuner interference

ESSENCE: a groove is a set of small systematic deviations from the printed
  grid — the second of a pair played late, the snare a hair behind, the bass
  a hair ahead — consistent enough to be measured. Play the same notes
  exactly on the grid and the music is correct and dead.
ROOT: this file / metre is an expectation living in the listener, so playing
  slightly against it is information; the body / ensemble playing is bodies
  coordinating, and bodies have consistent lags.
THREAD: speech (an accent living in tiny consistent timings), dance
  (partners a fraction ahead or behind), arts (a hand's signature carried in
  a mark's speed).

ESSENCE: two different groupings can run over one pulse at the same time —
  three against two is the simplest — and the ear holds it because the grids
  agree at regular meeting points. Which one you take as the main beat
  changes what you hear, and a piece can switch that with no notes.
ROOT: this file / grouping is imposed by the listener, so two groupings can
  live in one sound; mathematics / two cycles line up at their common
  multiple, and that is the pattern's period.
THREAD: gears (two wheels meeting at their common multiple), calendars
  (weeks and months sliding against each other), vision (a picture that
  flips between two readings).
ASKED-AS: polyrhythm three against two cross rhythm drumming confusing count feel switching hemiola

ESSENCE: hold one note underneath while the harmony above it changes and
