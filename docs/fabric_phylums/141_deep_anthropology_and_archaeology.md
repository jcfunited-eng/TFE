# 141 DEEP ANTHROPOLOGY AND ARCHAEOLOGY — reading peoples and their leavings
Two trades with one problem: understanding people whose reasons you do
not already share. One asks the living and cannot fully trust the
answers; the other asks the ground and gets no answers at all. Both are
rebuilding a whole way of life out of a handful of surviving marks.

ESSENCE: a baby carried to the far side of the world grows up speaking
  that side's language and finding its food ordinary. Almost nothing a
  people does travels in the blood. It travels down the arm, from the
  last generation, and it has to be handed over again every single time.
ROOT: teaching / what is not re-taught each generation is gone in one,
  so a way of life is a relay and never a possession.
CANNOT: no culture without transmission — stop the handing over for one
  generation and the thing is not weakened, it is finished. And no
  inheriting a custom through birth: a child of any ancestry raised
  anywhere takes up whatever is around it, which is why "it is in their
  blood" is always a statement about the watcher.
THREAD: language (a tongue lives only in mouths), farming (seed saved
  every year or the variety ends), keeping knowledge.
ASKED-AS: culture learned born with it raised elsewhere customs passed down generations blood traditions inherited

ESSENCE: there are two honest accounts of any custom — the one its own
  people give, which says what it means, and the one an outsider builds,
  which says what it does. Neither replaces the other, and using one
  where the other belongs is most of what goes wrong.
ROOT: the other mind / a reason is known from inside and inferred from
  outside, and the two accounts need not agree even when both are right.
CANNOT: no outsider's account being complete without the insider's — the
  meaning is a fact about those people, not decoration laid over a
  function. And no insider's account covering what the practice does for
  the group: nobody is positioned to see the effect of their own custom
  on a village they have never been outside of.
THREAD: medicine (what the patient calls the illness against what the
  chart calls it), law, evidence + measurement.
ASKED-AS: what it means to them outsider explanation why they do it their own words

ESSENCE: your own arrangements do not feel like arrangements. They feel
  like what any sensible person does — so another people's food, manners
  and gods arrive already marked as strange, and the marking is finished
  before any thinking starts.
ROOT: the mind / whatever is learned first is filed as the world rather
  than as one version of it.
CANNOT: no neutral first look at another way of living — the comparison
  is already running before you notice you are making one. And no fixing
  it by good intentions: it is corrected one custom at a time, by the
  specific work of finding out what that custom is for.
THREAD: language (an unfamiliar tongue heard as gabble), cooking (the
  smell that is delicious at home and rank abroad), the other mind.
ASKED-AS: weird customs strange food disgusting backward normal to them judging other countries different

ESSENCE: to understand a practice you have to stop scoring it first —
  every custom makes sense from inside some arrangement, and that sense
  is what you came for. But understanding is not agreeing, and a rule
  forbidding all judgement would silence the people inside a custom who
  are arguing against it.
ROOT: this file / the insider's account explains, and explaining is a
  different act from approving.
CANNOT: no understanding while scoring, and no scoring honestly before
  understanding — the two have to be done in order, not at once. And no
  consistent version of a total ban on judging: every society contains
  reformers, and the ban hits them before it hits any foreigner.
THREAD: right + wrong, law (a defence heard before a verdict), medicine.
ASKED-AS: cultural relativism judging other cultures who are we to say wrong tolerance limits customs

ESSENCE: before there were offices there were relatives. Who you must
  feed, who you may marry, who avenges you, who gets the field — all of
  it arrives wearing family words, so the family diagram is also the tax
  system, the army roster and the will.
ROOT: people together / a group has to hand out duties, and the oldest
  set of assignments already lying about is who is related to whom.
CANNOT: no small society running on offices — there is nobody spare to
  staff them, so duty must be carried by relationships that exist
  already. And no separating kinship from property where the land is
  held by a line: change the kin rule and you have changed the economy.
THREAD: law (inheritance), power + governance, money.
ASKED-AS: family tree relatives duties who inherits cousins clan uncle obligations kinship tribe rules

ESSENCE: many peoples use one word for the father and the father's
  brother, and a quite different word for the mother's brother — not out
  of confusion, but because those two men owe you different things. Kin
  words sort people by what is owed, and they split exactly where the
  duties split.
ROOT: this file / kinship is an assignment of duties, so its vocabulary
  is a vocabulary of duties rather than of biology.
CANNOT: no translating a kin term by a blood relation — "brother" in
  some systems takes in every man of your generation on one side, and
  the mistranslation makes nonsense of the whole chart. And no system
  marking a distinction it does not use: where two relatives owe the
  same thing, one word covers both.
THREAD: language (a word cuts the ground its speakers need cut), law,
  accounting (a category that exists because something is posted to it).
ASKED-AS: cousin called brother uncle different words family terms translation kinship names confusing systems

ESSENCE: for most of human history a marriage was not two people
  choosing each other. It was two groups signing a treaty with a person
  as the bond — which is why the rules about marrying out are so strict.
  The entire product is ending up related to the neighbours.
ROOT: people and power / a lasting peace needs something staked on both
  sides, and shared grandchildren are the oldest hostage there is.
CANNOT: no alliance out of a marriage inside the group — marry only your
  own and you have made no new relatives, so nothing has been bought.
  And no private choice of partner where land or peace rides on the
  match: whoever bears the consequence will make the decision.
THREAD: power (dynastic marriage is this at state scale), law
  (contract), farming (two fields joined by a wedding).
ASKED-AS: arranged marriage families choosing partner alliance marry outside village tradition love match parents

ESSENCE: every people forbids marriage inside some circle, and the
  circles are drawn in wildly different places — a first cousin is the
  ideal spouse here and unthinkable there. What is shared is not the
  boundary but the having of one, because a rule that pushes marriage
  outward is what makes a society bigger than a household.
ROOT: this file / marriage is an alliance device, and a device pointed
  inward builds nothing.
CANNOT: no closed group lasting on its own marriages — a household that
  marries itself gains no allies, and past a few generations pays in
  sick children too. And no explaining which relatives are forbidden by
  blood: the lists do not match the blood in any society ever described,
  and often exclude people with no shared ancestor at all.
THREAD: heredity, law, the living world (crossing out beats crossing in).
ASKED-AS: incest taboo cousins marrying forbidden relatives rules different countries why banned family close

ESSENCE: a gift is not the opposite of a trade. It is a trade with the
  clock taken out — handing something over with no price named leaves
  the other person holding a debt of no stated size and no due date, and
  that open debt is the thing actually being manufactured.
ROOT: money / an exchange settles and ends, while a gift deliberately
  fails to settle, and the unsettled part is the relationship.
CANNOT: no gift without obligation — refusing to accept is itself a
  message, and returning the exact value on the spot is a refusal in
  polite clothes. And no continuing tie between two parties who have
  squared up perfectly: paying in full is how you end things.
THREAD: money, people and power (favours as currency), diplomacy (the
  state gift that must be answered).
ASKED-AS: gift obligation owe them back present refusing rude paying back exactly favour debt strings

ESSENCE: there are three ways of giving and distance picks which. In the
  household nobody counts at all; among neighbours the books balance
  over months or years; with strangers each side takes what it can get.
  The same person runs all three before lunch.
ROOT: this file / a gift builds a tie, and the strength of the tie
  decides how loosely the accounts may be kept.
CANNOT: no counting inside a family without damaging it — handing a
  parent an itemised bill is understood, correctly, as ending
  something. And no open-handed giving to strangers as a standing
  system: with no expectation of meeting again there is nothing holding
  the unbalanced version up.
THREAD: money, people together, law (the contract is the stranger form
  of the same act).
ASKED-AS: sharing family friends strangers keeping score paying back haggling generosity distance closeness accounts

ESSENCE: the things a people will not touch are usually the things their
  own categories cannot place — the animal that is neither one kind nor
  the other, the stuff that was inside a body and is now outside, the
  food that belongs to a different meal. Dirt is largely matter out of
  place.
ROOT: the mind / thinking works by sorting, and whatever the sorting
  cannot file registers as wrong rather than as merely unfiled.
CANNOT: no taboo list explained by danger alone — the forbidden animal
  is often the safe one and the permitted one often riskier. And no
  society without such rules: every set of categories produces
  leftovers, and the leftovers always get marked.
THREAD: cooking (mixtures that are unthinkable rather than nasty),
  cleaning (dirt as a judgement about place), religion.
ASKED-AS: taboo forbidden food unclean disgusting pork shellfish rules not allowed dirty why avoided

ESSENCE: every change of standing is done in three moves. The person is
  taken out of ordinary life, held for a stretch in a strange in-between
  where the usual rules are suspended, then handed back with a new name
  and new duties. The uncomfortable middle is the working part.
ROOT: people together / standing is held by everyone else's treatment of
  you, so it cannot be changed privately — the change must be witnessed
  and dated.
CANNOT: no changing status by deciding to — a person who declares
  themselves an adult, a widow or a doctor without the ceremony has
  changed nothing anybody else is obliged to honour. And no rite without
  the gap: run the old standing straight into the new and the old one
  stays attached.
THREAD: teaching (graduation), law (the words that make a marriage),
  armies (basic training as separation, ordeal, and a new name).
ASKED-AS: initiation ceremony graduation wedding funeral coming of age ritual stages hazing status change

ESSENCE: some standing is handed out at birth and some is won, and every
  society mixes the two — but the mix decides what a life feels like.
  Where standing is given, effort changes nothing and everyone knows
  their place; where it is won, everything is open and every failure
  becomes a verdict on the person.
ROOT: people and power / positions have to be assigned, and a society
  can use a fact fixed at birth or a contest, each with its own bill.
CANNOT: no society purely of one kind — even the most open hands down
  language, money and contacts long before a child can compete. And no
  free version of the earned kind: making standing winnable is exactly
  what makes losing it feel deserved.
THREAD: power, money (a lead that buys the means of keeping the lead),
  teaching.
ASKED-AS: born into it inherited status earned self made caste class nobility merit fair birth

ESSENCE: a rite does its work by being performed, not by what anyone
  privately believes while performing it. People who agree on nothing
  can kneel at the same moment, and the kneeling is what binds them —
  which is why so many traditions demand the act and never inspect the
  mind.
ROOT: people together / whatever coordinates a group has to be visible,
  and belief is not.
CANNOT: no group held together by shared private conviction, because
  nobody can check it. And no rite doing its binding work in private:
  performed where nobody sees, it may comfort the performer and it ties
  them to no one.
THREAD: meaning + ritual, law (the oath said aloud in a room), armies
  (drill that trains nothing but doing it together).
ASKED-AS: rituals meaningless going through motions believe it prayer ceremony together custom pointless doing

ESSENCE: a made thing is a decision left lying about. The shape of a pot
  holds the maker's idea of what a pot is, and when three hundred pots
  from one valley share a shape nobody ever wrote down, that shared
  shape is a shared thought you can pick up and weigh.
ROOT: craft / a tool is an intention given a body, so the body can be
  read back toward the intention.
CANNOT: no making without a trace — technique leaves marks in the
  material whether or not anybody meant it to, so how a thing was made
  is nearly always recoverable. And no reading the reason exactly: the
  object records what was done, and many different intentions make
  identical pots.
THREAD: craft, engineering (a part's shape names its job), writing (a
  hand recognisable across a hundred documents).
ASKED-AS: pottery styles what objects tell us made things ideas design artefacts thinking shape copied

ESSENCE: dirt arrives in order. What lies lower went down earlier, so
  the layers stack into a calendar with no numbers on it — and an
  excavation is mostly the careful undoing of that stack, one sheet at a
  time, in the reverse of the order it was built.
ROOT: deep earth / material settles onto whatever is already there, and
  nothing lower can have arrived later unless something dug.
CANNOT: no reading a site dug in one lump — mix the layers and the order
  is gone, and no laboratory anywhere recovers it. And no layer taken on
  trust: a pit, a plough, a burrow or a later builder can put old things
  above young ones, so the rule holds only where the sheets are proved
  intact.
THREAD: deep earth (rock beds, the same law at a different speed),
  accounting (a ledger read by date), snow and ice.
ASKED-AS: digging layers deeper older excavation levels site how do they date order underneath

ESSENCE: the information in a find is mostly not in the object. It is in
  what lay beside it, how deep, which way up, and what else was in that
  room. A bead on its own is nearly mute; the same bead in a child's
  grave beside a copper knife is a sentence.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / a single reading says almost nothing, and
  meaning lives in the relations between readings.
CANNOT: no recovering context after the object is lifted without a
  record — the relations are not properties of the thing and they do not
  travel with it. And no second attempt: the digging destroys the
  arrangement it is reading, so a site is read exactly once.
THREAD: law (a weapon in a room against a weapon in a drawer), medicine
  (a lab number with no patient attached), evidence.
ASKED-AS: where it was found context digging record notes object alone museum information lost association

ESSENCE: nearly everything people made was string, basket, hide, wood
  and food, and nearly none of it is left. What survives is stone, bone
  and fired clay — so the ages get named after the leftovers, and a
  world of rope and timber is remembered as a world of flint.
ROOT: chemistry / decay runs at wildly different speeds by material, so
  survival sorts the record long before anybody digs it.
CANNOT: no even sample of what was made — the surviving set was chosen
  by rot, not by importance, so the missing half is missing by
  chemistry. And no arguing from absence in ordinary ground: the basket
  that is not there is not there because it was a basket.
THREAD: evidence (any set assembled by survival), cooking (a meal leaves
  nothing but its bones), craft.
ASKED-AS: stone age wooden tools rotted survived clay bone what people actually used missing perishable

ESSENCE: the rubbish heap is the most honest thing on a site. Nobody
  arranges a bin for posterity, so the bones, shells, broken pots and
  seeds in it record what was actually eaten and used — while the great
  building next to it records what somebody wanted said.
ROOT: evidence / a record made with no audience in mind cannot have been
  posed for.
CANNOT: no boasting in a midden and no lying in one, which is why the
  tip outranks the monument as testimony. And no rich record from a tidy
  people: where waste was carried off or burned, the most truthful
  archive was destroyed by good housekeeping.
THREAD: accounting (the receipts against the annual report), medicine
  (what a body shows against what a patient reports), history.
ASKED-AS: rubbish dump bones shells food remains what they ate trash pit evidence midden honest

ESSENCE: where the houses sit says what the society was. Scattered farms
  with no centre is one kind of life; a ring of dwellings all the same
  size is another; one large building with small ones huddled round it
  has already told you there was a ruler, before a word is read.
ROOT: people and power / a hierarchy has to be housed, and housing
  cannot be hidden.
CANNOT: no ranked society without ranked space somewhere — standing
  cannot be held privately, so it shows up in walls. And no reading rank
  from size alone: a big building can be a granary, a shrine or a hall,
  and which it was is a separate argument needing separate evidence.
THREAD: building, power, farming (fields deciding the shape of a
  village).
ASKED-AS: village layout houses sizes palace ruins settlement pattern who was in charge ancient buildings

ESSENCE: a body keeps its own accounts. Teeth record every childhood
  hunger as a line, joints record the work that was done every day,
  bone chemistry records which foods the fields actually gave — so a
  cemetery says what no chronicle ever does: how the ordinary people
  lived.
ROOT: living machinery / a body builds only what is demanded of it, so
  the demands stay legible afterwards in what got built.
CANNOT: no reading a population's health off its monuments — the
  building says what was affordable and the bones say who paid for it.
  And no skeleton naming its own cause of death in most cases: what is
  written into bone is the long load, not the last day.
THREAD: medicine, farming, evidence.
ASKED-AS: bones tell us diet health teeth skeletons graves hard work disease ancient people lived

ESSENCE: nobody counted them, so the count has to be built: floor space,
  or hearths, or roofed area, multiplied by a figure for how many people
  fit. Every such estimate is one honest measurement wearing an assumed
  constant, and it is the constant that decides the answer.
ROOT: measurement / a figure derived through a proxy inherits the
  proxy's assumption, and the assumption is usually the largest term in
  the sum.
CANNOT: no population figure from the ground without an assumed rate,
  and no way to check that rate using the same ground. And no comparing
  two sites whose estimates used different constants: the difference
  measured may live entirely in the arithmetic.
THREAD: evidence, money (an index resting on one chosen basket),
  astronomy (a distance built on a ladder of assumptions).
ASKED-AS: how many people lived there population estimate houses guess ancient city size numbers crowded

ESSENCE: stone knows where it came from. A blade of volcanic glass a
  thousand miles from the only mountain that makes that glass is a
  receipt for a journey nobody wrote down, and the trail of such
  misplaced material is most of what is known about who met whom.
ROOT: chemistry / a material carries the chemical signature of its
  source, and carrying it about does not change the signature.
CANNOT: no trade shown without a transferred material to show it —
  exchanges of songs, words, spouses and diseases leave no such receipt,
  so the trade map is always the map of hard goods. And no route proved
  by a single object: one import can be loot, a gift, or a traveller's
  pocket.
THREAD: chemistry, money, movement of goods and people.
ASKED-AS: obsidian amber traded far away source origin materials imported ancient trade routes proof distance

ESSENCE: the dead do not bury themselves. A grave is arranged entirely
  by the living, so it records what the mourners wanted said about the
  person and about themselves — which makes it fine evidence about
  belief and treacherous evidence about the life inside it.
ROOT: evidence / a record made deliberately reports its maker's aims
  alongside its subject.
CANNOT: no reading standing straight off grave goods — a lavish burial
  can mark a beloved child, an heir's claim, or a debt being cleared in
  public. And no society burying at random: whatever is done with a body
  is prescribed, so even the plainest grave is a statement.
THREAD: meaning + ritual, law (a will as the same act in writing), story
  + drama.
ASKED-AS: graves buried with treasure rich tomb funeral goods status beliefs afterlife burial archaeology

ESSENCE: a date out of the ground is never a year. It is a range with a
  confidence attached, and it dates the thing measured — when this tree
  stopped growing, when this clay was last fired — which can sit
  centuries away from the event anybody actually cares about.
ROOT: measurement / an instrument reports its own quantity, and the step
  from that quantity to the question is where all the error lives.
CANNOT: no point date from a decay measurement, so no argument that
  needs one to be exact. And no dating an event by an object that could
  have been old when it was buried: old timber in a young fire dates the
  timber, and heirlooms are common in every period.
THREAD: evidence, deep earth, astronomy (light that left long before it
  arrived).
ASKED-AS: carbon dating accurate how do they know years range old wood error margin dates

ESSENCE: speech remembers well when it is shaped to be remembered —
  verse, rhythm, formula, an audience that may correct you. Loose
  recollection drifts within a lifetime; a chanted line of ancestors
  runs for generations, edited whenever the present needs a new one.
ROOT: keeping knowledge / a store with no outside copy is maintained by
  repetition, and repetition is a live process open to the living.
CANNOT: no long oral memory without a fixed form and an audience
  policing it — casual telling drifts inside one lifetime. And no
  genealogy neutral about the present: the line that justifies who holds
  the land is the line that keeps being recited.
THREAD: music (a tune carries words that prose could not), law
  (precedent recited from memory), teaching.
ASKED-AS: oral history stories passed down accurate memory generations songs genealogy reliable myths remembered

ESSENCE: words are inherited like everything else, so where a hundred
  tongues share the same core handful — mother, two, water — changed in
  the same regular ways, they are one family with a parent nobody
  recorded. The shape of the family is a map of ancient movement.
ROOT: language / a word is handed over rather than worked out, so a
  shared word means a shared hand-off somewhere upstream.
CANNOT: no relatedness proved by a few lookalike words — chance and
  borrowing supply those between any two languages on earth. And no
  reading a language's spread as a people's spread: tongues are adopted
  as well as inherited, so a shared language is never by itself proof of
  shared ancestors.
THREAD: heredity (descent with change, the same tree drawn twice),
  history, keeping knowledge.
ASKED-AS: language families related words similar ancestor migration where did they come from spread

ESSENCE: what a thing was for can often be worked out; what it meant
  almost never can. Use leaves marks and meaning does not — which is why
  the label "ritual object" so often means "we cannot see what this was
  for", an honest confession dressed up as a finding.
ROOT: the other mind / a reason is inferred from what it left behind,
  and belief leaves the thinnest trace of anything people do.
CANNOT: no reading a belief off an object without a text or a living
  descendant to say so — the same little figure can be a god, a toy, a
  portrait, or a weight. And no argument from strangeness: how odd a
  thing looks measures our distance from it and nothing about its
  holiness.
THREAD: the other mind, evidence, language (a symbol is useless without
  its key).
ASKED-AS: ritual object religious meaning statue figurine what was it for guess unknown purpose symbolic

ESSENCE: a looted object is not so much stolen as silenced. The thing
  itself usually survives and is often well cared for; what is destroyed
  is the ground it came out of, and with it every question anyone could
  ever have asked. A market for antiquities is a market for holes.
ROOT: this file / the information lives in the context, so lifting the
  object cleanly out of it is precisely how everything is lost.
CANNOT: no recovering a looted piece's site by studying the piece — the
  record was in the dirt thrown aside to reach it. And no innocent
  buyer: demand pays for the digging, so a piece bought without papers
  has already funded the next hole.
THREAD: keeping knowledge (an archive scattered is an archive gone),
  money (a price calling a supply into being), law.
ASKED-AS: looting antiquities stolen artefacts black market museums provenance treasure hunting damage buying

ESSENCE: every generation digs up its own arguments. When empire was the
  question, prehistory filled with conquering migrations; when trade
  was, the same finds became exchange networks; when the argument turned
  to men and women, the little figures changed meaning again. The ground
  never moved.
ROOT: the mind / a search is pointed by the belief running it, and
  evidence this thin lets the pointing decide the answer.
CANNOT: no interpretation free of the interpreter's question — the finds
  do not choose which story they support, so something has to. And no
  cure by trying to be neutral: what works is stating the question
  first, so a later reader can see exactly what was assumed.
THREAD: evidence, history, story + drama.
ASKED-AS: archaeologists changed interpretation same finds different theories bias fashion reading past today argument

ESSENCE: you cannot learn a way of life by asking about it for an
  afternoon. It takes years of being present, badly and awkwardly, until
  people stop performing for you — and even then your being there is
  part of what you are watching, permanently and unfixably.
ROOT: evidence / an instrument that disturbs its subject cannot be
  subtracted from the reading, only declared.
CANNOT: no understanding a practice without long time inside it, because
  the parts that matter are the parts nobody thinks to mention. And no
  observer who is not also an event: honest work reports the observer
  instead of pretending them out of the room.
THREAD: medicine (a patient in front of a doctor behaves like a
  patient), physics (a measurement that touches the thing), teaching.
ASKED-AS: living with them fieldwork observing anthropologist years presence changed behaviour outsider watching visiting

ESSENCE: ask what the rule is and you get the rule; watch for a year and
  you get the practice, and the two are never the same. People describe
  the version they would defend, and the gap between it and what happens
  is not hypocrisy — it is where the exceptions live.
ROOT: the other mind / an account of one's own behaviour is composed
  afterwards and aimed at a listener.
CANNOT: no reading a society's actual arrangements off its own
  statements about them — a statement is a claim, not a record. And no
  discarding the statement either: what people say ought to happen is a
  real fact about them, and it steers what happens next.
THREAD: law (the statute against the enforcement), medicine (a reported
  diet against a weighed one), evidence.
ASKED-AS: what people say they do actually do rules exceptions interviews surveys behaviour observed claim

ESSENCE: no living people are anybody's ancestors. Every community on
  earth has exactly the same depth of history behind it, so a group
  hunting with spears today is not a window into ten thousand years ago
  — it is a modern answer to modern conditions, arrived at by its own
  long road.
ROOT: the living world / every line alive now has been running the same
  length of time, and none of them is an earlier draft of another.
CANNOT: no using a living people as a model of the past — they have
  their own millennia of change behind them, including the changes made
  by meeting us. And no ladder of societies from simple to advanced: the
  ladder was always drawn by someone standing on the top rung.
THREAD: evolution (no living species is ancestral to another), history,
  power.
ASKED-AS: primitive tribes stone age today living fossils ancestors backward advanced societies compare simple

ESSENCE: a culture is not a container with a lid. Inside any named group
  people disagree, borrow, and vary by valley, age and trade; the
  boundary was drawn by whoever did the naming, and nobody anywhere
  carries the whole of it in one head.
ROOT: people together / a shared practice is spread across many people,
  so no individual holds all of it and no line neatly encloses it.
CANNOT: no complete carrier of a culture — every member holds a
  different partial share, which is why "an authentic voice of a people"
  is always one person's share being promoted. And no census of
  cultures: they are not countable objects, so any count is really a
  count of somebody's categories.
THREAD: language (a dialect shading into the next valley's), the living
  world (where one species stops), sociology.
ASKED-AS: culture defined boundaries mixed groups authentic representative whole tribe varies inside disagreement members

ESSENCE: two peoples build the same thing and there are only two
  explanations — they met, or the problem has that answer. Telling which
  is genuinely hard, and the history of the field is a long swing
  between assuming everything spread from one place and assuming
  everyone worked it out alone.
ROOT: history / an invention waits on its parts, so wherever the parts
  are common the invention will be common too.
CANNOT: no proving contact from a shared solution — a bow, a pot and a
  pointed roof are what those materials and needs afford anybody. And no
  proving independence either: the absence of a route is nearly
  impossible to establish, so most such cases stay open forever.
THREAD: history, engineering (two firms arriving at one design), the
  living world (unrelated animals growing the same eye).
ASKED-AS: pyramids everywhere same invention spread contact independently discovered coincidence cultures similar borrowed

ESSENCE: every people ever described has kin words, food rules, music,
  jokes, stories about where things came from, a way of marking who is
  married, and a difference between what is said in public and in
  private. The contents vary wildly and the list of headings barely
  moves.
ROOT: living machinery / one species with one set of equipment and one
  set of problems keeps producing the same headings.
CANNOT: no society missing the whole list — no people has ever been
  found without music, without kin terms, or without stories of origin.
  And no predicting the content from the heading: knowing there are food
  rules tells you nothing whatever about which foods.
THREAD: language (every tongue marks who did what to whom), the mind,
  music.
ASKED-AS: all cultures have music stories marriage universal things every society common human across world

ESSENCE: the deepest rules are the ones nobody can state. How close to
  stand, how long to hold a look, when a silence turns rude, who eats
  first, how late is late — none of it is taught in words, all of it is
  enforced, and you discover your own only by breaking somebody else's.
ROOT: teaching / a skill practised from infancy runs wordless, and what
  runs wordless cannot be reported.
CANNOT: no asking a member for these rules — they never learned them in
  words, so what you get back is a guess. And no travelling without
  breaking them: they are invisible from inside, and the only detector
  is the other person's face.
