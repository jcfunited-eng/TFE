# 110 DEEP CLINICAL MEDICINE — the body's systems and what goes wrong
File 06 holds the body's laws and file 86 its machinery. This one holds
the failures: what each system looks like when it stops working, how it
is looked at, and what can honestly be done about it.

ESSENCE: an organ can fail in only a handful of ways. It can do too
  little, do too much, block, leak, or grow something that was not
  asked for. Naming the organ and then naming which of those is
  happening carries a diagnosis most of the way before any test is
  ordered.
ROOT: engineering / a part's failure modes are set by the job it does,
  not by what it is made of.
CANNOT: no useful complaint without a system attached — tired, thin,
  sore and dizzy belong to every organ at once, so they name nothing
  until an organ is chosen. And no failure mode outside the handful:
  what looks new is one of them wearing a local costume.
THREAD: engineering (a pump, a valve and a seal fail in their own few
  ways), computing (a service is down, slow, stuck or corrupt),
  plumbing.
ASKED-AS: what is wrong which organ too much too little blocked leaking lump growth doctor

ESSENCE: a failing organ is helped by the rest of the body, and the
  help becomes the disease. A weak heart is answered by holding salt
  and water and by squeezing the vessels tighter, which is exactly the
  load a weak heart cannot carry. Most of what is treated in long
  illness is the compensation, not the injury.
ROOT: engineering / feedback aimed at a number does not know what
  hitting the number costs elsewhere.
CANNOT: no compensation without a bill in another currency — so no
  organ that can be supported without the support being aimed at too.
  And no quick release: the corrections keep running for weeks after
  the cause is fixed, because they were set by a body that has not yet
  noticed.
THREAD: money (a shortfall covered by borrowing, and then the debt is
  the problem), engineering (a governor that fights its own machine),
  farming (watering harder on soil that will not drain).
ASKED-AS: heart failure fluid retention swollen legs why worse over time treatment tablets salt

ESSENCE: the same reading arrived at fast or slow is two different
  diseases. A blood count halved over a year leaves a person walking
  about; halved in an hour it kills. A body will live at almost any
  set point it was given time to walk to.
ROOT: living machinery / a held value is hunted toward and every
  correction takes time — so danger lives in the rate, not the level.
CANNOT: no reading urgency off a number without its slope. And no
  correcting a long-standing derangement quickly: a salt level that
  drifted over weeks and is put right in a day injures the brain, so
  slowness is part of the treatment.
THREAD: engineering (a load a structure carries slowly and not
  suddenly), oceans + weather, money (a slow devaluation and a crash).
ASKED-AS: blood test very low but walking around slow fast correct gradually danger level number

ESSENCE: whatever injures an organ for long enough, the ending looks
  the same: working tissue replaced by scar, the organ shrunken, hard
  and quiet. Liver, kidney, lung and heart all arrive there by
  unrelated roads.
ROOT: medicine / the body heals fast and crooked — repeated repair in
  a working organ is repeated patching.
CANNOT: no telling one cause from another once an organ has scarred:
  the end pictures converge, so the diagnosis that changes anything
  has to be made while the injury is still running. And no scar that
  keeps its shape — maturing scar shrinks, which is what strangles the
  plumbing running through it.
THREAD: building (a wall patched for thirty years), farming (a field
  salted slowly), materials.
ASKED-AS: cirrhosis fibrosis scarred kidney shrunken biopsy end stage too late reverse damage

ESSENCE: a pump that cannot keep up fails in both directions at once.
  Forward, the body is short of delivery — tired, cold, faint.
  Backward, the queue builds behind it, drowning the lungs on the left
  and swelling the legs and liver on the right. Nearly every patient
  shows some of both.
ROOT: medicine / blood is one closed loop, so a restriction anywhere
  is felt on both sides of it.
CANNOT: no failing forward without a queue behind — the fluid has to
  be somewhere, so there is no heart failure that shows as tiredness
  alone. And no pumping a body out of it: a wet lung is treated by
  taking load off the heart, not by driving it harder.
THREAD: water (a blocked culvert floods above and starves below),
  engineering (a pump run against a closed valve), money.
ASKED-AS: heart failure breathless lying flat pillows swollen ankles tired fluid lungs weak

ESSENCE: high blood pressure is not a feeling, it is a load. Every
  beat against a higher resistance thickens the heart wall and batters
  the smallest vessels in kidney, eye and brain, and the bill is
  presented decades later as failure in whichever of those was
  thinnest.
ROOT: engineering / a structure loaded past its design point does not
  break, it fatigues — and fatigue is silent until it is not.
CANNOT: no symptom to dose against, so no treating this by how a
  person feels today. And no organ spared: one pump makes one pressure
  for the whole body, so the damage is never local and never in one
  place.
THREAD: engineering (fatigue cracks under a load nothing complained
  about), living machinery (one line at one pressure serving every
  tap), building.
ASKED-AS: blood pressure high tablets forever no symptoms stroke kidneys eyes damage numbers

ESSENCE: the disease is in the artery wall, not in its bore. Fat and
  inflammation build a soft pocket under the lining, and for years the
  wall bulges outward and keeps the channel open, so nothing narrows
  enough to feel. The pipe is the last thing affected.
ROOT: living machinery / inflammation is a repair crew that wrecks the
  neighbourhood — here it has been running for forty years inside a
  wall.
CANNOT: no reading the risk off the narrowing, so no clean pipe as a
  treatment goal: the pockets that kill are often the ones too small
  to see. And nothing scrapes a wall clean — the work is cooling the
  wall and thickening the cap over the pocket.
THREAD: building (rot inside a wall while the paint holds), materials,
  engineering.
ASKED-AS: cholesterol plaque clogged arteries statins stent narrowing wall buildup scan heart

ESSENCE: two different chest pains. One is a fixed narrowing that
  cannot supply a working heart: it comes on with effort, goes with
  rest, and behaves the same way for years. The other is a pocket that
  split open and grew a clot across itself in minutes. That one does
  not settle, and that one is the killer.
ROOT: this file / the disease sits in the wall, and a wall can either
  narrow slowly or break suddenly — two mechanisms, one symptom.
CANNOT: no predicting the rupture from the narrowing, so no propping
  open a pipe that prevents the attack it was not aimed at. And no
  trusting a stable pattern once it changes: pain at rest, or at less
  effort than last week, means the mechanism has switched.
THREAD: earth (a slope that creeps and a slope that slides),
  engineering (wear against fracture), money.
ASKED-AS: angina chest pain walking uphill relieved rest unstable at rest worse suddenly stent

ESSENCE: the heart's wiring and its muscle fail separately. A perfect
  muscle with a broken rhythm delivers nothing, and a heart with a
  normal-looking trace can be barely pumping at all. The trace reads
  the electricity; only a pulse or a picture reads the pumping.
ROOT: living machinery / the heart makes its own beat, so its
  controller is a separate thing from its pump and can fail on its
  own.
CANNOT: no reading output off a rhythm strip — electrical activity
  with no pulse is a common way to die, and the monitor looks fine
  throughout. And no rate too fast to matter: past a speed the filling
  pause vanishes, so the speed itself is the failure.
THREAD: engineering (a motor's controller against its windings),
  computing (a heartbeat message still arriving from a dead process),
  music.
ASKED-AS: palpitations irregular heartbeat atrial fibrillation ecg normal no pulse racing pacemaker shock

ESSENCE: a clot that travels says nothing about where it was made. A
  leg vein sends its clot to the lung; a fibrillating heart sends its
  clot to the brain. Treat the landing site and ignore the launch site
  and there will be a second one.
ROOT: medicine / blood is one branching loop, so anything loose in it
  is carried until the pipes narrow.
CANNOT: no local treatment of a lodged clot that prevents the next,
  because the source is upstream and untouched. And nothing past an
  arterial plug has another supply: branches beyond it starve, which
  is why the damage is shaped exactly like the artery.
THREAD: water (debris caught at a grating far from where it fell in),
  movement of goods (a fault that shows at the destination),
  computing.
ASKED-AS: clot lung leg dvt stroke flying blood thinners where did it come from prevent

ESSENCE: breathing is air and blood arriving at the same place at the
  same time. Air with no blood beside it is wasted, and blood running
  past an airless patch comes back unloaded. Most lung disease is a
  mismatch between the two, not a shortage of either.
ROOT: physics / a gas crosses only where two sides are separated by a
  thin wall, so both sides have to turn up.
CANNOT: no loading blood that goes past a drowned or blocked patch, so
  no amount of extra oxygen rescues a lung with a large short circuit
  through it. And no telling which half is wrong from the breathing
  rate: the effort looks identical either way.
THREAD: engineering (a heat exchanger with one side blocked), weather,
  farming.
ASKED-AS: oxygen low breathing hard pneumonia collapsed lung ventilator mask saturation not improving

ESSENCE: a lung fails either because air will not come out or because
  it will not go in. Obstructed lungs empty slowly and sit over-
  inflated, so the work is in breathing out. Stiff lungs are small and
  hard to fill, so the work is in breathing in. Two opposite mechanics
  behind one complaint.
ROOT: engineering / a container's fault lies either in its walls or in
  its outlet, and the two produce opposite behaviour.
CANNOT: no separating them by breathlessness — the measurement is the
  diagnosis. And no reliever for a stiff lung: nothing opens an airway
  that was never closed.
THREAD: engineering (flow restriction against tank stiffness),
  building, cooking.
ASKED-AS: asthma copd emphysema wheeze breathing out fibrosis stiff inhaler spirometry tests

ESSENCE: the two gases fail on different clocks. Oxygen is hard to
  load and easy to run short of; carbon dioxide is easy to blow off
  and rises only when the whole bellows slows. So a person can be blue
  with a normal carbon dioxide, and pink and drowsy while dying of it.
ROOT: living machinery / the urge to breathe watches the exhaust, so
  the alarm follows one gas while the damage follows the other.
CANNOT: no reading ventilation off an oxygen number: the finger probe
  is silent about the gas that kills quietly. And no fixing a tiring
  bellows with oxygen — it corrects the reading and lets a person stop
  breathing in comfort.
THREAD: engineering (a furnace with a blocked flue), survival (a
  sealed room), chemistry.
ASKED-AS: oxygen saturation carbon dioxide drowsy sleepy retaining ventilator slow breathing blue lips

ESSENCE: a kidney that stops has three possible reasons and they sit
  in a line: not enough blood arriving, the filter itself damaged, or
  the drain blocked below. Two of the three are plumbing and fluid,
  fixable in an afternoon. Only the middle one is kidney disease.
ROOT: engineering / a stalled process is starved, broken, or backed up
  — and the cheap checks are at the two ends.
CANNOT: no treating a rising kidney number without asking which of the
  three: fluid poured into a blocked bladder makes it worse. And no
  waiting out a blockage — pressure backing up through a kidney
  destroys it in days, long before anything hurts.
THREAD: engineering (check the ends of a line before the middle),
  plumbing, computing.
ASKED-AS: kidney failure creatinine dehydrated catheter blocked prostate scan fluids dialysis urine

ESSENCE: the kidney's other jobs are why its failure looks like other
  diseases. It sets blood pressure, orders red cells to be made, and
  switches on the vitamin that hardens bone — so kidney failure
  arrives as high pressure, exhaustion and crumbling bones long before
  anything is odd about passing water.
ROOT: living machinery / a body reuses one organ for several unrelated
  jobs, so one failure reports itself in several places at once.
CANNOT: no diagnosing this from urine: most failing kidneys make a
  normal amount right to the end. And no treating the anaemia or the
  bones as separate problems — they are one problem, upstream, and the
  local treatments barely work while it stands.
THREAD: engineering (a shared subsystem whose failure shows everywhere
  except where it lives), money, computing.
ASKED-AS: kidney disease anaemia tired blood pressure weak bones dialysis urine normal vitamin

ESSENCE: a machine can do the kidney's filtering three afternoons a
  week at a small fraction of the rate a kidney works. That is enough
  to stay alive and not enough to be well — which is why the treatment
  is a life sentence and not a repair.
ROOT: living machinery / the kidney throws everything out and buys
  back what it wants, without pause; a batch process cannot imitate a
  continuous one.
CANNOT: no intermittent copy of a continuous organ without a sawtooth
  — loaded up before every session and wrung out after it, forever.
  And no machine for the kidney's other work: only the filtering is
  replaceable, so the anaemia and the bones are treated separately by
  hand.
THREAD: engineering (batch against continuous processing), farming
  (irrigating weekly instead of daily), money.
ASKED-AS: dialysis three times week tired transplant machine fluid restriction between sessions diet

ESSENCE: the liver makes the clotting proteins, clears the drugs,
  disposes of the ammonia and holds fluid inside the vessels. When it
  fails a person bleeds, becomes confused, swells, and can no longer
  be given ordinary doses of anything — four unrelated-looking
  failures out of one organ.
ROOT: living machinery / everything absorbed from the gut passes the
  liver first, so it sits upstream of a very great deal.
CANNOT: no supporting a failed liver the way a kidney is supported:
  nothing replaces a chemistry works with hundreds of products. And no
  safe prescribing into it — every dose in the book assumes a
  clearance that is no longer there.
THREAD: engineering (one works feeding a dozen assembly lines), money
  (a clearing house going down), computing.
ASKED-AS: liver failure jaundice confused bleeding swollen belly alcohol hepatitis transplant medication doses

ESSENCE: an abdomen goes to theatre for three things: a tube blocked,
  a tube perforated, or a piece of gut with its blood supply cut off.
  They feel different. Blocked comes in waves, perforated is constant
  and worse with every movement, and starved gut hurts far more than
  the belly looks.
ROOT: this file / an organ blocks, leaks or loses supply — the
  abdomen's version of the same short list.
CANNOT: no waiting on gut without blood: it dies in hours and then
  leaks, so the delay is the disease. And no reassurance from a soft
  belly in the old or the steroid-treated — the response that makes a
  belly rigid is one of the first things they lose.
THREAD: plumbing, engineering, farming.
ASKED-AS: stomach pain surgery bowel obstruction perforation appendix twisted vomiting rigid belly emergency

ESSENCE: the gut's own nerves cannot say where they are. An inflamed
  appendix is felt in the middle of the belly and only moves to the
  spot when it touches the lining. Irritation under the diaphragm is
  felt in the shoulder. The map is wired in, not learned.
ROOT: medicine / the body is renovations stacked on a fish — organs
  and skin that shared a place in the embryo share a nerve address for
  life.
CANNOT: no locating an internal organ by feel alone, so no dismissing
  pain for being in the wrong place. And no standard picture of a
  heart attack: jaw, arm, back and stomach are not exceptions, they
  are what it is for a large share of people.
THREAD: computing (a fault reported by the wrong subsystem), building
  (a leak that shows two rooms away), language.
ASKED-AS: pain moved appendix shoulder tip jaw arm heart attack referred belly button gallbladder

ESSENCE: a hormone problem has two directions and three addresses. Too
  much or too little — and the fault lies in the gland, in the
  pituitary that orders it, or in the tissue that should be listening.
  The test that separates them measures the order and the product in
  the same breath.
ROOT: living machinery / a slow chemical message with a matching lock
  can fail at the sender, in transit, or at the receiver.
CANNOT: no reading one hormone level alone: a low product with a high
  order is a broken gland, low with low is a broken boss, and the
  product by itself cannot say which. And no diagnosis from symptoms —
  tired, cold and heavy fits half the endocrine system and most of the
  well.
THREAD: money (an order unfilled: buyer, post, or warehouse),
  computing, law.
ASKED-AS: thyroid underactive tired cold weight gain hormone levels pituitary gland test feedback

ESSENCE: one raised sugar, two opposite diseases. In the first the
  factory has been destroyed and there is no insulin at all — young,
  fast, and fatal in weeks without replacement. In the second there is
  plenty of insulin and the body has stopped listening — slow, silent,
  and years old on the day it is found.
ROOT: living machinery / a signalling system fails at the sender or at
  the receiver, and the two failures share one reading.
CANNOT: no treating the first with anything but the hormone itself,
  ever. And no telling them apart by the sugar number: the same
  reading, two mechanisms, two opposite treatments.
THREAD: computing (no signal against a signal ignored), teaching,
  money.
ASKED-AS: diabetes type one two insulin tablets sugar thirsty weight loss children adults difference

ESSENCE: the harm of a high sugar is not the sugar. It is decades of
  it coating the smallest vessels and the longest nerves, permanently.
  Which is why one disease ends in blindness, kidney failure, numb
  feet and lost toes: those are simply the places where the vessels
  are finest.
ROOT: chemistry / sugar sticks to proteins slowly and does not come
  off, so the damage is level multiplied by years.
CANNOT: no feeling this happening — the nerve that would report the
  injury is among the things being destroyed, which is why a foot
  ulcer is found by looking and not by pain. And no undoing it:
  control slows what comes next and recovers nothing.
THREAD: chemistry (the slow browning that colours a crust),
  engineering, farming.
ASKED-AS: diabetes complications eyes kidneys numb feet ulcer amputation control blindness nerves years

ESSENCE: give a body its own stress hormone for a few weeks and the
  gland that made it shuts down and shrinks. Stop suddenly and there
  is nothing at all, in a body that needs more than usual because it
  is ill. That is why these courses are tapered, and why stopping one
  can kill.
ROOT: living machinery / a steady outside signal is answered by the
  body dismantling its own side of the loop.
CANNOT: no abrupt stop after a long course — this is the clearest case
  of a drug whose stopping is far more dangerous than its taking. And
  no waking the gland to order: it returns on its own timetable, over
  months, and nothing hurries it.
THREAD: money (a subsidy that closes the local industry), engineering,
  farming.
ASKED-AS: steroids prednisone tapering stopped suddenly adrenal crisis vomiting card sick day dose

ESSENCE: the brain is a map, so what a person has lost names the place
  before any scan. Speech gone with understanding kept, one side weak
  with the face spared, a missing quarter of the visual field — each
  of these belongs to a particular patch of tissue and a particular
  artery feeding it.
ROOT: living machinery / signals travel by their wiring, so a wire cut
  in a place removes exactly what ran through that place.
CANNOT: no scattered damage producing a clean single loss, so no
  writing off a precise deficit as tiredness or age. And no reading
  size from severity: a small stroke in a critical place outweighs a
  large one in a quiet one.
THREAD: computing (a fault localised by exactly what broke), electrics
  (tracing a circuit by what went dark), maps.
ASKED-AS: stroke weak one side speech vision loss numb face scan which part brain artery

ESSENCE: the skull does not stretch. Anything added inside it — blood,
  swelling, a growth — is paid for by squeezing something else out,
  and for a while the brain surrenders its fluid and some of its blood
  to make room, and nothing shows. Then the room runs out, and the
  fall is a cliff.
ROOT: physics / a fixed volume holding something that will not
  compress must displace whatever is already there.
CANNOT: no gradual decline inside a full skull, so no reassurance from
  a patient who is still talking — that is exactly what the last hour
  before collapse looks like. And no relief from outside: pressure in
  a closed box is treated by opening the box or taking something out
  of it.
THREAD: engineering (a sealed vessel), building, money.
ASKED-AS: head injury bleed brain swelling pressure skull talking then collapse drowsy pupils surgery

ESSENCE: a seizure is the brain's electricity falling into a
  synchronised runaway, and it is a symptom rather than a diagnosis.
  Low sugar, low salt, a fever, a bleed, a drug or the sudden absence
  of one, and a scarred patch of cortex all produce the identical fit.
ROOT: engineering / a network with feedback and no brake can collapse
  into one rhythm, and the collapse looks the same whatever tipped it
  in.
CANNOT: no seizure that names its own cause, so no drug for one whose
  cause is a chemistry that must be corrected instead. And no waiting
  one out past a few minutes: after that the brain begins injuring
  itself, and the fit becomes the emergency rather than the sign of
  one.
THREAD: engineering (a feedback squeal), music (a room that finds one
  note), computing.
ASKED-AS: seizure fit epilepsy shaking unconscious cause low sugar withdrawal brain scan medication

ESSENCE: a broken bone needs three things and nothing else: the ends
  near each other, held still, with blood reaching them. Remove any
  one and it does not join — which is why some breaks are plated and
  screwed, and why a fragment with a poor supply can fail to heal at
  all.
ROOT: living machinery / repair is a supply job before it is a
  building job, and nothing is rebuilt where blood does not reach.
CANNOT: no union across a gap, none across movement, and none in a
  starved fragment: three requirements, not three preferences. And no
  strength on schedule — new bone looks joined on a picture weeks
  before it will carry a load.
THREAD: craft (glue clamped, aligned and left alone), building,
  engineering.
ASKED-AS: broken bone healing plate screws cast not healing scaphoid blood supply weeks weight

ESSENCE: two joint diseases, and the story separates them before any
  test. A worn joint hurts more as the day goes on and eases with
  rest, in the one or two joints that carried the load. An inflamed
  joint is stiff for an hour every morning, warm and swollen, in
  several joints at once and often matching left and right.
ROOT: medicine / the defence force can be catastrophically wrong — one
  of these is mechanical failure and the other is the body attacking
  itself.
CANNOT: no anti-inflammatory that rebuilds a worn surface, and no rest
  that settles an autoimmune joint: treating either as the other costs
  years of joint. And no self-repair to wait for — cartilage has no
  blood supply of its own.
THREAD: engineering (wear against corrosion), materials, farming.
ASKED-AS: arthritis knees hands morning stiffness swollen worse evening rheumatoid osteoarthritis cartilage worn

ESSENCE: skin is the only organ that can be examined directly, and it
  reports on all the others. Yellowing, bruising, a rash that will not
  blanch under a pressed glass, spidery vessels, fingertips gone club-
  shaped — internal disease writes itself on the outside, where it can
  be read with no machine at all.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / the cheap instrument that is present
  beats the powerful one that is not.
CANNOT: no internal diagnosis from skin alone — these are pointers,
  never proofs. And no reading them through clothing or in bad light:
  the sign that is missed is nearly always the one nobody uncovered.
THREAD: engineering (an indicator lamp on the outside of a sealed
  unit), farming (leaves reporting on roots), craft.
ASKED-AS: rash spots skin colour yellow bruises nails clubbing internal disease signs looking

ESSENCE: lose enough skin and the problem stops being the skin. Water
  evaporates without limit, heat pours out, protein is lost, and every
  germ has an open door. Which is why a large burn or a severe skin
  disease is treated first as a fluid and infection problem and only
  then as a wound.
ROOT: living machinery / a body is a held difference, and skin is what
  holds it against the outside world.
CANNOT: no barrier organ that fails locally: the loss scales with
  area, so how much is gone matters more than how deep. And no
  hurrying the cover — skin grows back from the edges and from what
  survives in the depths, at its own rate and no other.
THREAD: building (a roof off), physics (evaporation as a heat drain),
  engineering.
ASKED-AS: burns skin loss fluids drip infection body surface percentage grafts losing heat water

ESSENCE: too few red cells is never the diagnosis. Either they are not
  being made, they are being destroyed, or they are being lost — and
  each has its own list. A man or an older woman low on iron is
  bleeding from somewhere until proved otherwise, and that somewhere
  is usually the bowel.
ROOT: engineering / anything running short is under-made, over-used,
  or leaking, and the three are asked in that order.
CANNOT: no treating anaemia by handing over iron: the tablet corrects
  the number and hides the cancer that was bleeding. And no comfort
  from an early normal count — the body restores the count long before
  it restores the store behind it.
THREAD: money (a falling balance: income, spending, or theft),
  farming, cleaning.
ASKED-AS: anaemia low iron tired pale tablets bleeding bowel cancer transfusion cause count

ESSENCE: in sepsis the germ is not what kills. The body's own all-out
  response does: vessels leaking and going slack everywhere at once,
  the pressure falling, clotting firing until it is exhausted, and
  organs starving inside a body still full of blood.
ROOT: living machinery / inflammation is a firebreak burnt on purpose
  — this is that tool turned on the whole body at the same moment.
CANNOT: no sepsis cured by the drug alone, since the cascade runs on
  for days after the germ is dead. And no source controlled from a
  drip: pus, dead gut or an infected line has to be physically taken
  out, or the fire is being fought while the fuel is still stacked.
THREAD: money (a panic that continues after the bad news is settled),
  war, engineering.
ASKED-AS: sepsis infection blood pressure confused fever antibiotics quickly organs failing source drain

ESSENCE: in an old and frail person almost any illness can arrive as
  confusion, a fall, or simply stopping eating. A urine infection, a
  heart attack and a broken hip can each present that way, with no
  fever, no chest pain and no complaint at all.
ROOT: this file / symptoms are made by the body's response, and an
  aged body's responses are blunted — so the illness turns up without
  its usual signature.
CANNOT: no ruling out serious illness because the classic features are
  missing: in this group they usually are. And no calling new
  confusion dementia — a change over days is a physical illness
  somewhere in the body until it has been found.
THREAD: engineering (an old machine whose warning systems fail first),
  family + lives, teaching.
ASKED-AS: elderly confused suddenly fall stopped eating no fever infection delirium dementia different presentation

ESSENCE: the examination has an order because each step spoils the
  next. Looking costs nothing and is done from the doorway. Touching
  changes what there is to feel. Listening needs a still, quiet
  patient, which is exactly what your hands have just prevented. Most
  of what is found is found by looking.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / a measurement that disturbs its subject
  is taken last, or it destroys the ones after it.
CANNOT: no examining what has not been uncovered and no listening
  through clothing. And no deep palpation that stays informative: once
  a belly is guarding, the finding you wanted is no longer available,
  so the gentle pass comes first.
THREAD: physics (a measurement that disturbs what it measures), craft,
  teaching.
ASKED-AS: examination doctor looking pressing stethoscope order undress abdomen tender chest signs missed

ESSENCE: a plain X-ray separates only four things: air, fat, water and
  bone. Everything soft is one shade of grey. That is why it shows a
  broken bone and a lung filled with air or water beautifully, and why
  it can be entirely normal in a belly full of disease.
ROOT: physics / the picture is made of what stopped the beam, so
  anything of similar density is invisible against its neighbour.
CANNOT: no soft-tissue detail on a flat film, so no ruling out water-
  dense disease sitting inside water. And no depth in one view: a flat
  picture of a solid body stacks everything along the beam, which is
  why two views at right angles are the minimum.
THREAD: light + seeing (a shadow gives outline and nothing else),
  photography, engineering.
ASKED-AS: xray chest broken bone normal film shadow two views cannot see soft tissue

ESSENCE: a scanner spins the same X-ray beam around the body and
  computes the slices, so it sees what a flat film cannot: fresh
  blood, free gas, bone detail, all in seconds. The price is a real
  radiation dose, many times a chest film, given to a person who may
  turn out not to have needed it.
ROOT: mathematics / enough views taken from outside can be solved back
  into a picture of the inside.
CANNOT: no scan without a dose, so no free repeating of them in the
  young. And no tolerance for movement: the picture is assembled from
  many separate moments, so a patient who cannot lie still is blurred
  out of their own answer.
THREAD: mathematics (an inside reconstructed from many outsides),
  photography, engineering.
ASKED-AS: ct scan cat radiation dose slices bleeding quick contrast dye how many scans

ESSENCE: MRI reads the water held in tissue, so it separates the soft
  things X-rays cannot tell apart: brain, cord, ligament, marrow. It
  is slow, extremely loud, and it lives inside a magnet that is never
  switched off, which is why an oxygen cylinder wheeled into the room
  becomes a missile.
ROOT: physics / a nucleus spinning in a strong field can be tipped and
  then listened to as it settles, and different tissues settle at
  different speeds.
CANNOT: no MRI on a body carrying the wrong metal, so no emergency
  scan without a safety check that takes longer than the scan. And no
  good picture of bone or lung: little water in them, so little to
  hear.
THREAD: physics, engineering (a magnet with no off switch), safety.
