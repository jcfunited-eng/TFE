# 29 CRAFT AND MAKING — hands, tools, and material in conversation
The material talks back. Craft is the discipline of listening to it
before it shouts.

ESSENCE: the material has a will — wood splits along its grain,
  steel holds the temper it was given, cloth stretches on the
  slant — and the maker's first job is learning that will and
  working with it.
ROOT: physics + chemistry / inner arrangement decides outer
  behavior — which ways a thing bends, splits, and holds is set
  before the tool arrives.
CANNOT: no ordering wood to be strong across the grain. Fighting
  the will costs double and loses anyway — forced material fails
  later, at the scar of the fight.
THREAD: farming (plant with the season, not at it), martial arts
  (use the opponent's direction), people together (institutions
  have grain too — reformers who cut across it split them).
ASKED-AS: carving whittling sewing carpentry warp crack direction natural properties leather stone

ESSENCE: measure twice, cut once — checking repeats for free,
  cutting never repeats at all, so spend lavishly on the
  reversible step and once on the other kind.
ROOT: physics / disorder wins — one-way doors; the sawdust never
  rejoins the board.
CANNOT: no un-cutting, un-drilling, un-firing. A board cut short
  is short forever; no skill held in reserve argues material back on.
THREAD: medicine (the checklist before the knife), law (read
  before signing), cooking (salt in stages — adding is an open
  door, removing is a closed one).
ASKED-AS: mistake ruined careful irreversible permanent redo undo waste lumber haste regret

ESSENCE: a tool is the hand extended — harder, sharper, longer —
  and a sharp tool is a safe tool: it cuts where aimed under light
  force, while a dull one demands muscle and then skids.
ROOT: cooking / the knife's law — force onto a vanishing edge; and
  control shrinks as force grows.
CANNOT: a dull edge cannot be steered — by the time it is pushed
  hard enough to work, it is past the strength hands can stop.
  Workshop blood is mostly spilled by dull blades, not sharp ones.
THREAD: surgery (the scalpel's whole argument), martial arts (a
  technique that needs strength is the wrong technique), writing
  (the precise word needs no pounding).
ASKED-AS: honing whetstone accident slip injury kitchen chisel saw axe dangerous finger

ESSENCE: fit before force — parts made right join with a push and
  a click; if it needs hammering, something is wrong, and the
  hammer will make it wronger.
ROOT: physics / matter cannot share space — force does not correct
  a shape, it wounds one.
CANNOT: no beating a bad fit into a good one. The force goes
  somewhere: crushed threads, split wood, a crack that keeps the
  secret until the worst day to tell it.
THREAD: medicine (the body rejects the organ that does not match),
  persuasion (an argument that must be shoved is not yet right),
  mathematics (a proof that needs forcing has a gap).
ASKED-AS: stuck jammed stripped screw bolt assembly furniture alignment tight snug

ESSENCE: finish is protection wearing beauty — oil, paint, glaze,
  and varnish exist to keep water and air out of the work; the
  shine is the visible half of a seal.
ROOT: chemistry / air and water attack most materials given time —
  rust, rot, and tarnish are disorder arriving at the surface first.
CANNOT: no bare steel that stays bright, no bare wood that lives
  outdoors. Skipping the finish does not postpone the cost — it
  starts the clock.
THREAD: the body (skin is the original finish), cooking (the
  seasoning on cast iron), maintenance (the great bridges are
  painted end to end, then painted again — the seal is re-fought,
  never won).
ASKED-AS: stain sealant weatherproof deck fence peeling lacquer polish coating furniture

ESSENCE: a jig is remembered accuracy — build the guide once,
  carefully, and every piece after inherits its precision without
  inheriting its effort.
ROOT: keeping knowledge / a record outlives its maker — here it is
  accuracy stored in wood instead of concentration.
CANNOT: no jig better than the day it was made — its errors copy
  as faithfully as its accuracy, into every piece, forever. Right
  once or wrong always.
THREAD: printing (movable type is a jig for words), computing (a
  program is a jig for thought), factories (an assembly line is
  jigs all the way down).
ASKED-AS: template stencil pattern mold repeatable identical batch fixture consistent duplicates

ESSENCE: the last tenth takes half the time — rough work moves
  fast; fitting, smoothing, and truing devour the calendar.
  Done-looking is the halfway mark.
ROOT: physics / disorder wins — precision is order, and order is
  paid for at rising rates: each finer flaw needs finer tools and
  slower hands.
CANNOT: no scheduling the whole job at the rough pace. No skipping
  the last tenth either — it is where the piece stops being almost.
THREAD: software (builders there count ninety percent done, then
  the second ninety percent), music (learned in a week, polished
  for a year), building (the punch list outlives the roof).
ASKED-AS: nearly finished forever deadline behind schedule details final touches project overrun estimate

ESSENCE: mastery is economy — the master makes fewer motions, not
  faster ones; every stroke does work, nothing is done twice, and
  the piece seems to make itself.
ROOT: the mind / practiced skill sinks below deciding — what once
  took attention takes none, and the freed eye watches ahead.
CANNOT: no buying economy — it is distilled repetition. The wasted
  motions must be made, by the thousand, before they can be dropped.
THREAD: martial arts (the master is not fast, the master is
  early), sports (the easy swing outdrives the violent one),
  surgery (slow is smooth, smooth is fast), cooking (the practiced
  cook is never seen hurrying).
ASKED-AS: effortless graceful expert experience pro efficient years hours natural talent
