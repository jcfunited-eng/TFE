# 118 DEEP PHILOSOPHY OF KNOWING — what can be known and how
File 36 holds the method's working rules. This one holds the ground
under them: what knowing is, where it can reach, and the shape of
the walls it keeps running into. Every entry carries its cannot.

ESSENCE: three different things get called knowing. Believing is
  holding something as true. Believing for a good reason is more.
  Knowing is both of those plus the world actually being that way
  — and the last part is the one you cannot check from inside your
  own head.
ROOT: premise — a belief is a claim about the world, made in a
  head, and the head is not where the world is.
THREAD: evidence + measurement (the whole apparatus exists because
  the inside view is not enough), law (a witness sure and wrong
  looks exactly like a witness sure and right), the mind.
ASKED-AS: know think believe sure certain opinion fact difference guess convinced true

ESSENCE: you glance at the station clock, it reads three, and it is
  three. You believed correctly, for a reason that would normally
  be excellent. The clock stopped exactly a day ago. Right by
  accident is not knowing, and it feels no different.
ROOT: this file / knowledge needs belief, reason and world to line
  up — here the reason and the world lined up by coincidence
  instead of by connection.
THREAD: chance + risk (a right answer from a broken method pays
  once and teaches nothing), medicine (a correct diagnosis reached
  by wrong reasoning), navigation.
ASKED-AS: lucky guess right for wrong reason broken clock accident correct answer fluke

ESSENCE: ask why you believe something and you give a reason. Ask
  why you believe the reason and you give another. It cannot run
  forever, so every person's beliefs end somewhere — in a circle,
  in a stopping place taken as given, or in nobody having gone
  further.
ROOT: mathematics + logic / a chain is only as strong as its
  premises — walked backwards, that is a search for the bottom.
THREAD: law (a court eventually accepts the record, the oath and
  the calendar without proving them), building (every structure
  ends on ground nobody engineered), teaching (the child's fourth
  "but why" is the honest one).
ASKED-AS: why because reasons again proof basic assumption bottom circular endless kids asking

ESSENCE: belief is not a switch. You can hold a thing at
  nearly-certain, at leaning, at faint suspicion. Actions are
  switches — you take the umbrella or you don't — so living means
  converting a strength into a yes, and where you put that line is
  a separate decision from how sure you are.
ROOT: chance + risk / a gamble's price is each outcome weighed by
  its chance; premise — a graded quantity is being poured into an
  ungraded slot.
THREAD: medicine (a scan at eight in ten, and an operation that is
  all or nothing), law (beyond reasonable doubt against balance of
  probabilities — two lines drawn for two costs), money.
ASKED-AS: pretty sure maybe probably certain enough risk decide act umbrella doubt

ESSENCE: certainty is real and its territory is small. Inside rules
  somebody made up — arithmetic, chess, a legal definition — you
  can be certain, because you are unpacking what was put in. About
  the world outside, you get very strong and never finished. From
  the inside the two feel identical.
ROOT: mathematics + logic / a chain transports certainty and cannot
  mint it — and the world was never among the premises.
THREAD: physics (its firmest laws are the most heavily attacked
  ones, not the proven ones), law (no verdict claims certainty; the
  standard is a level of doubt), engineering.
ASKED-AS: hundred percent sure absolutely certain math proof world facts guarantee positive

ESSENCE: the sun has risen every morning anyone recorded. That is a
  fact about mornings that already happened. Getting from there to
  tomorrow takes an extra step — that the future keeps resembling
  the past — and that step cannot be earned by noting it has
  worked so far, because working so far is the past again.
ROOT: premise — no heap of particular cases contains a general law;
  the leap is supplied by the person, not found in the heap.
THREAD: farming (every planting date is this bet, made annually),
  engineering (a beam tested yesterday and trusted tomorrow),
  chance + risk (past frequency spent as forecast).
ASKED-AS: sun rise tomorrow always happened before predict future past pattern assume

ESSENCE: a thousand white swans do not establish that all swans are
  white. One black swan ends it on the spot. That lopsidedness is
  the engine of the whole business: a general claim can be killed
  by a single case and can never be completed by any number of
  them.
ROOT: this file / the gap that stops examples becoming a law does
  nothing to stop one counterexample being fatal.
THREAD: engineering (a bridge is disproved by one collapse and
  never proved by standing), law (one honest clock defeats a
  hundred character witnesses), cooking (one failed batch tells
  you more than fifty good ones).
ASKED-AS: all swans white exception one case proves disproves rule counterexample examples

ESSENCE: science has no "proved" drawer. What it has is a shooting
  range. Ideas are fired at, and the ones still standing are used
  until something knocks them down. A theory's standing is a record
  of attacks survived, and the best records belong to ideas that
  clever people wanted dead.
ROOT: this file / confirming is impossible and refuting is not — so
  the method aims itself entirely at the half that works.
THREAD: engineering (a design is trusted after the tests that tried
  to fail it), medicine (a treatment is kept while it keeps
  outperforming, never crowned), law (the account that survived
  cross-examination).
ASKED-AS: scientific proof proven fact just a theory disproved settled science evidence

ESSENCE: three different situations get flattened into "not
  proven". A claim can be refuted — a result went against it and
  it is dead. It can be unsupported — nothing yet, and it could
  still go either way. Or it can be untestable — no result could
  ever bear on it, so it can never stand at all.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / a law earns its keep by what it
  forbids; premise — the three states have different futures, so
  one word for all three loses the information.
THREAD: law (acquitted, unproven and never charged are three
  different things), medicine (no evidence of benefit against
  evidence of no benefit), money.
ASKED-AS: unproven disproved no evidence maybe true never tested impossible check claim

ESSENCE: nothing is ever tested by itself. Every experiment leans
  on the instrument working, the sample being what it says, the
  helper following instructions, and a dozen background beliefs.
  When the prediction fails, something in that bundle is wrong, and
  the result does not say which.
ROOT: this file / a refutation is decisive against a claim only if
  the claim was what got tested — and a bundle was what got tested.
THREAD: engineering (a failed part, a failed jig, or a failed
  drawing — the test looks the same), medicine (a trial that
  disappoints because the drug is inert or because the dose was
  wrong), computing.
ASKED-AS: experiment failed blame equipment excuse rescue theory setup wrong retest

ESSENCE: for any pile of facts there is more than one story that
  fits. The pile narrows the field and does not close it. This is
  not a shortage of cleverness; two accounts can agree on
  everything observed and disagree about everything underneath.
ROOT: this file / examples cannot complete a general claim —
  therefore the evidence never picks out exactly one.
THREAD: law (two accounts consistent with the same physical
  evidence — the whole reason trials exist), detective work,
  astronomy (the same sky read by two very different systems for
  centuries).
ASKED-AS: another explanation same evidence two stories fits both possible alternative decide

ESSENCE: when two explanations fit equally, take the one with fewer
  moving parts. Not because the world is simple, but because every
  extra part is another thing that can be wrong and another thing
  nobody checked, and a story with enough parts can be made to fit
  anything at all.
ROOT: this file / more than one story always fits, so a tiebreaker
  is needed and this is the one that has earned its place.
THREAD: engineering (fewer parts, fewer failures — the identical
  rule in metal), medicine (one condition explaining five symptoms
  is preferred to five conditions), computing.
ASKED-AS: simplest explanation occams razor fewer assumptions complicated straightforward likely tidy answer

ESSENCE: simple is not a synonym for true. Weather, bodies, markets
  and people are genuinely complicated, and the tidy account of
  them is usually the wrong one. The preference for few parts is a
  rule for choosing between equals, and it turns into a bad habit
  the moment it becomes a claim about how the world had to be.
ROOT: this file / parsimony as a tiebreaker — this entry is the
  edge of that tool, where its users mostly cut themselves.
THREAD: medicine (one cause is preferred and old people have six),
  farming (a field is soil, weather, seed, pest and history at
  once), people together (a single-cause history is nearly always
  a bad one).
ASKED-AS: simple answer complicated reality one cause many factors oversimplified messy

ESSENCE: the observation that does not fit is the most valuable
  object in the room, and it is the one most likely to be written
  off as an error. Everything that fits was already predicted and
  teaches nothing. Only the misfit has news in it.
ROOT: this file / a general claim can only be killed, never
  completed — so the killing candidates are the only informative
  events.
THREAD: engineering (the one odd reading before a failure),
  medicine (the patient who does not follow the pattern),
  astronomy (a wobble in an orbit that turned out to be a planet).
ASKED-AS: doesnt fit weird result outlier error ignore anomaly strange reading discard

ESSENCE: a field does not shift its mind steadily. Contrary facts
  pile up for years and are absorbed as odd cases, then the whole
  frame turns over quickly and the new arrivals cannot see what
  the fuss was. Slow, slow, sudden — and the suddenness is in the
  acceptance, never in the evidence.
ROOT: this file / anomalies accumulate without forcing anything,
  because a frame is what makes the evidence readable in the first
  place; people together (a shared frame is a shared possession and
  is defended like one).
THREAD: medicine (ulcers as stress until they were bacteria),
  geology (drifting continents laughed at, then obvious), power
  (institutions change by replacement, not conversion).
ASKED-AS: everyone believed suddenly changed old ideas overturned mainstream ridiculed accepted

ESSENCE: two people can use one word inside two different systems
  and neither is misspeaking. What "planet", "gene", "force" or
  "species" picks out shifts when the surrounding picture shifts,
  so the same sentence carries different cargo on each side and
  the argument runs without ever meeting.
ROOT: this file / a frame makes evidence readable — it also makes
  vocabulary mean; language (a word's sense comes from the system
  it sits in, not from a label on the thing).
THREAD: law (a term of art against its everyday twin — the source
  of a great many bad arguments), teaching, language.
ASKED-AS: talking past each other same word different meaning definitions argue clarify

ESSENCE: an argument that goes quiet has two possible causes.
  Either it was settled, or everybody got tired and moved on. From
  the outside, the silences look the same — and only one of them is
  knowledge.
ROOT: people together / a group's attention is finite and moves in
  fashions; this file (a settled question and an abandoned one
  leave the same record, which is nothing).
THREAD: keeping knowledge (what was not recorded cannot be
  re-examined), medicine (practices continued for decades on a
  trial nobody had reread), history.
ASKED-AS: nobody argues anymore settled question dropped subject moved on consensus quiet

ESSENCE: you cannot measure without already believing something. A
  thermometer measures temperature only if you accept that the
  liquid rises with heat in a regular way. The theory comes first
  and decides what the number even is — which is why a
  measurement can be perfectly executed and mean nothing.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / a number needs an agreed stick; this
  file (the stick is agreed on the strength of a belief about how
  the world works).
THREAD: astronomy (distance measured by brightness assumes what
  the star is), medicine (a blood test measures a proxy chosen
  because of a theory about the disease), money.
ASKED-AS: measure thermometer reading assumes theory instrument number means anything calibrate

ESSENCE: an instrument is an argument made out of metal and glass.
  Trusting the dial is trusting everything the maker believed
  about how the thing behaves. When you look through a microscope
  you are not seeing directly; you are accepting a long chain of
  reasoning that arrives as a picture.
ROOT: this file / measurement carries theory inside it — an
  instrument is that theory hardened into an object and sold as
  neutral.
THREAD: light + seeing (every optical trick has a matching false
  image), medicine (scan artefacts read as tumours), computing (a
  visualisation that shows the plotting choice, not the data).
ASKED-AS: microscope telescope screen seeing really there artifact instrument trust picture

ESSENCE: looking costs. Take a temperature and the thermometer
  drinks a little heat; ask a question and the person composes an
  answer; put a camera on a room and the room performs. There is
  no free look — the cost is sometimes tiny and it is never zero.
ROOT: physics / a reading is an interaction and interactions go
  both ways; this file (measurement is a doing, not a receiving).
THREAD: the mind (a watched worker works differently — the effect
  named after a factory), medicine (blood pressure raised by the
  cuff), evidence + measurement.
ASKED-AS: watching changes results observer effect camera behave differently disturb measure

ESSENCE: every observation is made from somewhere — a position, a
  moment, a species, a history, a set of interests. This is not
  the same as disturbing the thing. It is that the report always
  includes the standpoint, and there is no place to stand that has
  no standpoint.
ROOT: this file / instruments carry the beliefs of their makers,
  and a person is the instrument that carries the most.
THREAD: history (every source was written by somebody with a
  stake), image + design (a photograph is a decision about where
  to stand), evidence + measurement (many independent hands).
ASKED-AS: bias objective neutral point of view unbiased perspective everyone opinion fair

ESSENCE: "I saw it with my own eyes" reports a verdict, not a
  recording. What reaches awareness has already been sorted,
  filled in and named. So an eyewitness is not delivering raw
  material — they are delivering a conclusion their brain reached
  in a fraction of a second and did not show its work for.
ROOT: the mind / seeing is guessing — this file takes that fact and
  asks what weight the guess may carry as evidence.
THREAD: law (line-up procedure carrying more weight than the
  witness's confidence), light + seeing (illusions that persist
  after you know), medicine.
ASKED-AS: saw it myself eyewitness sure identified suspect trick eyes lying trust

ESSENCE: how sure someone is about a memory and how accurate it is
  are two separate things, and they come apart most where it
  matters most. A vivid, detailed, often-told memory has been
  rehearsed, and rehearsal builds confidence and edits content at
  the same time.
ROOT: the mind / recall rebuilds rather than replays — this file
  asks what that means for memory used as evidence by a court, a
  family or a person.
THREAD: law (statements taken early, in writing, for this reason
  alone), family (two siblings with incompatible childhoods, both
  honest), keeping knowledge (a record made at the time beats any
  recollection).
ASKED-AS: remember clearly vivid memory sure it happened childhood argue different versions

ESSENCE: nearly everything you know, you were told. The shape of
  continents, the year of a war, what a virus is, that your
  parents are your parents. First-hand knowledge is a thin strip
  around an enormous inherited pile, and this is not a weakness —
  it is the only way a person could know more than one life's worth.
ROOT: people together / knowledge accumulates because it is
  transmitted; premise — a single lifetime cannot verify a
  civilisation.
THREAD: keeping knowledge (libraries as the store of what nobody
  alive checked), teaching, money (credit works exactly this way,
  and fails the same way).
ASKED-AS: how do you know told me heard read school textbook trust source secondhand

ESSENCE: a report is worth what its chain is worth. Who saw it, who
  passed it on, how many hands, what each one had to gain, and
  whether anyone in the line could have checked. A claim repeated
  by fifty outlets that all read one wire story is one report, not
  fifty.
ROOT: this file / testimony carries almost everything, so the
  question of when it may be trusted is not optional.
THREAD: law (hearsay rules — an attempt to write this down), news,
  people together (a rumour that gains detail as it travels, which
  is the signature of a bad chain).
ASKED-AS: everyone saying heard from source rumor spread news repeated original independent outlets

ESSENCE: an expert's say-so is real evidence, and it is evidence of
  a particular kind: it stands in for reasoning you cannot check
  yourself, on the strength of a record you can. Treating it as
  proof overclaims; treating it as nothing throws away the best
  shortcut anybody has.
ROOT: this file / testimony is most of knowledge, and expertise is
  testimony with a track record attached.
THREAD: medicine (a second opinion, sought for exactly this
  reason), law (the expert witness, cross-examined like anyone),
  teaching.
ASKED-AS: experts say scientists doctors trust authority qualified degree listen professional

ESSENCE: authority fails in a small number of regular ways. It
  speaks past its range. It carries the prestige of one field into
  another. It has an interest nobody stated. It is a real
  consensus that got its early evidence wrong. Knowing the failure
  list is what makes deference usable rather than blind.
ROOT: this file / an expert's weight comes from a record inside a
  range — every failure mode is a way the record does not cover
  the claim being made.
THREAD: money (funded research and the direction its errors lean),
  medicine (guidelines written by people with a stake), power (a
  credential used as a fence around a question).
ASKED-AS: expert wrong outside field paid study famous scientist opinion celebrity credentials

ESSENCE: when many people who could have disagreed do not, that is
  evidence. Not because crowds are wise, but because independent
  attempts to break a claim that all failed are a lot of failed
  attempts. The value is in the independence and in the failed
  attempts, never in the headcount.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / independent repetition disinfects
  luck, tilt and wishful hands at once; this file (agreement is
  worth what the disagreement would have cost).
THREAD: law (twelve jurors who deliberated against twelve who
  copied one), science (replication by strangers), people together
  (a room that agrees because the boss spoke first).
ASKED-AS: everyone agrees scientists consensus majority believe most people wrong crowd

ESSENCE: consensus fails in its own regular ways. It counts the
  same source many times. It forms where testing is hardest and
  therefore cheapest to agree. It survives on the cost of
  dissenting rather than the strength of the case. And it is
  slowest to move exactly where it is most invested.
ROOT: this file / agreement is worth the risk it ran — each mode
  is a way the risk was absent while the appearance of agreement
  remained.
THREAD: medicine (a treatment standard held for a generation on
  weak trials), power (professions defending their own necessity),
  history (the once-unanimous positions nobody will now own).
ASKED-AS: mainstream science wrong before galileo laughed everyone believed dissent maverick

ESSENCE: real expertise only grows where the world answers back
  fast and clearly. A surgeon, a pilot and a chess player get told
  within minutes or moves. A pundit, a long-range forecaster and a
  hiring manager are told years later, vaguely, if at all — so
  their confidence rises with practice while their accuracy does
  not.
ROOT: this file / knowledge needs a check outside the head, and a
  field with no feedback supplies none; the mind (fluency builds
  confidence out of practice, regardless of results).
THREAD: medicine (surgery against long-term prognosis — one trains
  and one does not), money (traders and the scoreboard; advisers
  and none), teaching.
ASKED-AS: experienced years doing it good at judging feedback results confident actually accurate

ESSENCE: the same evidence gets two different bars depending on
  whether you liked the answer. What agrees with you is asked "can
  I believe this?" and what disagrees is asked "must I believe
  this?" — and almost anything survives the first question and
  almost nothing survives the second.
ROOT: the mind / beliefs defend themselves; this file (the defence
  operates on the standard of proof, which is why it is invisible
  to the person doing it — they really are examining the evidence).
THREAD: law (a standard of proof fixed in advance, for this
  reason), evidence + measurement (predictions registered before
  the result), persuasion.
ASKED-AS: double standard scrutiny picky when disagree accept easily fair examine evidence

ESSENCE: the map is not the land. Not a warning against sloppiness
  — a statement about what a map is. Every map leaves out almost
  everything, and it is useful because of what it left out. A
  perfect map would be the land again, and you could not fold it.
ROOT: premise — a representation stands in for a thing by being
  unlike it in most respects and like it in a chosen few.
THREAD: language (a word is a handle and has no crust), computing
  (a model of a system that runs faster than the system by
  discarding it), image + design.
ASKED-AS: map not territory model reality simplified leaves out useful picture chart

ESSENCE: abstraction is throwing things away on purpose. To call
  three stones and three days both "three" is to discard stones
  and days. Every concept, average, diagram and rule is a
  discarding, and its power is exactly proportional to how much it
  managed to drop while still working.
ROOT: this file / a map earns its use by omission — the same move
  running through every kind of thinking, not only maps.
THREAD: mathematics (number is what remains when the things are
  thrown away), money (a price discards everything about a good
  except one number), teaching (a rule taught by leaving out the
  exceptions).
ASKED-AS: simplify summary general rule leaves out details average abstract concept lose

ESSENCE: a model can be false and useful at the same time, and
  usually is. The flat map, the frictionless slope, the perfectly
  rational buyer, the ball-and-stick molecule: nobody thinks these
  are true, and they build bridges, price loans and cure people.
  Useful and true are two different tests.
ROOT: this file / abstraction drops things, so every model is
  literally false about what it dropped; premise — the question of
  a model is never whether it is true but where it holds.
THREAD: engineering (safety factors as an admission that the model
  is a sketch), money (a risk model that held until the day it
  mattered), physics (the ideal gas, wrong everywhere and useful
  everywhere).
ASKED-AS: model wrong but works assumptions unrealistic simplified prediction useful holds

ESSENCE: "it works, so it must be true" is a step too far, and it
  is the most tempting step there is. A wrong picture can produce
  right answers over the range where it was tuned. Sailors
  navigated for centuries on a sun that goes round the earth, and
  arrived.
ROOT: this file / models are useful and false — this entry names
  the mistake that fact invites, and how quietly it is made.
THREAD: medicine (bad theory with effective practice — bleeding was
  the exception, willow bark the case that worked), astronomy
  (circles upon circles predicted eclipses accurately for
  centuries), craft.
ASKED-AS: it works so its true results prove theory wrong reason right answer explanation

ESSENCE: a definition is a decision, not a discovery. Nobody found
  out what a planet is; astronomers voted, and a planet stopped
  being one. Nobody measures where childhood ends; a number is
  chosen. Definitions can be useful, awkward, or dishonest. They
  cannot be false.
ROOT: language / to define is to name the family and the
  difference — and both are choices about how to cut, made by
  people, for purposes.
THREAD: law (statutory definitions that decide who is a worker, a
  refugee, a child), medicine (a blood-pressure line moved, and
  millions become patients overnight), money.
ASKED-AS: definition means what counts as arbitrary decided vote word planet child

ESSENCE: draw any sharp line across a smooth change and you will
  misfile the middle. Where does a hill become a mountain, a
  stream a river, a puddle a pond, a child an adult? The line has
  to exist to be usable, and the cases nearest it will always be
  handled wrongly.
ROOT: this file / definitions are decisions — and a decision
  applied to a gradient produces neighbours on opposite sides who
  differ by nothing that matters.
THREAD: law (an age of majority — one day apart, two legal
  species), medicine (the diagnostic cut-off and the patients just
  under it), the living world (where one species ends).
ASKED-AS: where line drawn hill mountain adult eighteen borderline arbitrary cutoff exactly

ESSENCE: take one grain from a heap and it is still a heap. Do that
  again, and again. No single removal is the one that ends it, and
  the heap ends. So the word works perfectly in daily life and
  breaks under repeated small steps — and most words are like
  this.
ROOT: this file / a sharp line across a gradient misfiles the
  middle — this is what happens when the gradient is walked one
  step at a time and the line is asked to declare itself.
THREAD: law (the reasonable time, the substantial part —
  deliberately vague and litigated forever), farming, medicine
  (one drink, one more, and where impairment starts).
ASKED-AS: heap grains bald hair one more exactly when tall rich few many vague

ESSENCE: some words look like measurements and contain a verdict.
  Efficient, healthy, natural, primitive, innovative, fair,
  addicted. Each names something in the world and hands down a
  judgement in the same breath — so the argument is finished
  before anybody notices it started.
ROOT: right + wrong / no pile of facts adds up to an ought — this
  entry is where the ought gets in anyway, hidden inside a noun.
THREAD: medicine (healthy smuggles a chosen goal — the body has no
  preference), persuasion (choosing the word is most of winning),
  money (efficient means efficient at something, and the something
  is never in the word).
ASKED-AS: loaded word natural healthy efficient normal fair judgement built in describe

ESSENCE: before arguing, find out which of three arguments you are
  in. A fact dispute is settled by looking. A values dispute is not
  settled by looking at all. A word dispute dissolves the moment
  both sides state what they mean. Most long arguments are the
  third kind wearing the costume of the first.
ROOT: this file / definitions are decisions and thick words hide
  verdicts — so a single disagreement can be three different
  objects, needing three different tools.
THREAD: persuasion (find the kind before opening your mouth), law
  (a trial separates findings of fact from questions of law for
  precisely this reason), teaching.
ASKED-AS: argument going nowhere disagree facts opinion meaning definition circles settle

ESSENCE: if a tree falls with nobody there, does it make a sound?
  Two people can agree on every physical fact — the air moved, no
  ear was present — and still argue, because one uses "sound" for
  the air and one for the hearing. Nothing is unknown. Only the
  word is doing two jobs.
ROOT: this file / a word dispute dissolves on definition — this is
  the cleanest specimen of one, which is why it has survived as an
  example for centuries.
THREAD: law (arguments over what a contract's word covers, where
  the stakes are real and the dispute is still about a word),
  language, teaching.
ASKED-AS: tree falls forest sound argument words meaning same thing semantics pointless

ESSENCE: some questions are hard and some are broken. "What is
  north of the north pole?" is grammatical, urgent-sounding, and
  has no answer because the question assumes something false. A
  broken question does not get answered; it gets taken apart, and
  the taking-apart is the answer.
ROOT: this file / a question can smuggle a claim in its own
  wording; language (a question is a statement with a hole in it —
  and the statement can be wrong).
THREAD: mathematics (what is one divided by zero — the question
  needs dismantling, not computing), law (a question that assumes
  a fact not in evidence, which is why it can be objected to),
  language.
ASKED-AS: deep question no answer confused before universe north pole meaningless dissolve

ESSENCE: a thought experiment tells you what your mind does with a
  case, not what the world does. That is worth something: it
  exposes what your rule actually says when pushed. It is not
  worth what people spend it on, because intuitions about
  impossible situations are made from experience with possible
  ones.
ROOT: this file / a model holds only in the range it was fitted to
  — and a mind is a model fitted to ordinary life, then queried
  about a world with no friction and no death.
THREAD: right + wrong (the runaway-trolley family, endlessly
  productive and endlessly overread), physics (riding alongside a
  light beam — a thought experiment that paid), law (the extreme
  hypothetical put to a rule).
ASKED-AS: imagine if suppose hypothetical impossible situation intuition tells us thought experiment

ESSENCE: "how likely is this?" is not a complete question. Likely
  compared to what group of cases? A man of fifty. A smoker of
  fifty. A smoker of fifty with this test result and that family.
  Each is a different rate, all are correct, and the choice of
  crowd is doing the work.
ROOT: chance + risk / chance is lawful in bulk — but the bulk has
  to be named, and naming it is a judgement rather than a
  calculation.
THREAD: medicine (a survival figure that changes with every detail
  added), insurance (the whole industry is class-choosing), law
  (how rare is this evidence — and among whom).
ASKED-AS: odds people like me chances average rate compared group statistics apply

ESSENCE: some things happen once. The origin of life, a war, an
  extinction, a person's decision. You cannot rerun them, so
  knowledge there is built the way a detective builds it — from
  traces that would be different if the story were different, and
  from many independent lines that have to agree.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / an experiment isolates a cause by
  intervening — where intervention is impossible, the only
  substitute is a trace whose alternatives can be ruled out.
THREAD: the deep earth (a mountain read from its layers), law (a
  crime, reconstructed and never repeated), astronomy (one
  universe, no control group).
ASKED-AS: cant repeat history experiment past events evidence detective clues rerun once

ESSENCE: nothing about the past reaches you. What reaches you is
  present stuff — ink, rock, memory, isotopes, a photograph — that
  the past left behind. Every historical claim is an inference
  from a thing sitting in front of you now, and the reasoning is
