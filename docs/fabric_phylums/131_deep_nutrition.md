# 131 DEEP NUTRITION — what food does inside
File 86 holds what a cell burns and builds with. This one holds
what happens between the plate and that machinery: what food is
made of, what the body does with each part, and which of the
famous claims survive being looked at. Where the evidence is
genuinely unsettled the entry says UNSURE.
