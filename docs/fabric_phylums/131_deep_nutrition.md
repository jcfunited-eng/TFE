# 131 DEEP NUTRITION — what food does inside
File 86 holds what a cell burns and builds with. This one holds
what happens between the plate and that machinery: what food is
made of, what the body does with each part, and which of the
famous claims survive being looked at. Where the evidence is
genuinely unsettled the entry says UNSURE.

ESSENCE: energy in minus energy out equals energy stored is true the way
  a bank statement is true — it accounts, and it explains nothing. Both
  sides move together: eat less and the body spends less and wants more.
  The identity is not a lever, it is a ledger.
ROOT: physics / energy is conserved, so the books must balance;
  engineering (an accounting identity holds in every system and
  therefore names the cause in none).
CANNOT: no arguing with the arithmetic and no acting on it either — it
  is true of every person who ever gained or lost weight, including the
  ones for whom every method failed. And no reading one side while the
  other is free to move: a change to intake is answered on the output
  side within days.
THREAD: money (a balance sheet balances by definition and tells you
  nothing about why the firm is failing), engineering (conservation laws
  forbid, they do not steer), evidence + measurement (a statement that
  cannot be false is not a finding).
ASKED-AS: calories in out weight loss math simple eat less move more why not working

ESSENCE: most of your fuel is spent doing nothing you would call
  anything. Lying still, warm, breathing, thinking, replacing cells,
  takes roughly two thirds of the day's total. Deliberate exercise is a
  small slice, and the largest variable slice is all the fidgeting,
  standing and pacing nobody counts.
ROOT: this file / the output side moves on its own; living machinery
  (holding a difference costs continuously whether or not anything uses
  it).
CANNOT: no exercising your way out of a large intake — an hour of hard
  work buys back a modest snack, which is why training reliably improves
  a body and unreliably shrinks one. And no fixed number for anyone: the
  unconscious movement varies several hundred units between two people
  of the same size.
THREAD: engineering (a building's standing load dwarfing what the
  occupants add), money (fixed costs against discretionary spending),
  training (why training is bought for what it builds).
ASKED-AS: burn calories exercise gym treadmill how many workout weight fidgeting resting rate

ESSENCE: the numbers on the packet are averages of averages. They come
  from burning food in a machine and subtracting a standard guess for
  what the body fails to absorb — and the real figure moves with
  cooking, with grinding, with the food's structure and with whose gut
  it enters.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / a measurement inherits every assumption
  in its method, and these are old assumptions applied to every food
  alike.
CANNOT: no exact count from a label — the legal tolerance alone is wide,
  and whole nuts hand over noticeably less than the same nuts ground.
  And no correcting it by weighing more carefully: the error is in the
  conversion, not in the scale.
THREAD: evidence + measurement (precision reported past the accuracy
  that earned it), food + growing (cooking raising the energy a gut can
  actually take from the same food), money (a forecast quoted to the
  penny).

ESSENCE: a body defends a weight the way a thermostat defends a
  temperature — but the number it defends is not fixed at birth. It
  drifts upward over years of easy food and rarely drifts back, so the
  defended level is set partly by inheritance and largely by the world
  the body has been living in. UNSURE how the two divide.
ROOT: living machinery / the body treats losing weight as an emergency —
  this is the question of what number it is defending and where that
  number came from.
CANNOT: no single set point written in the genes — populations that
  changed nothing about their inheritance changed their average weight
  within one generation. And no defended level that moves down as
  readily as it moved up: the machinery is not symmetric, and neither is
  the history.
THREAD: engineering (a controller whose target has been quietly reset),
  food + growing (an appetite built for scarcity meeting constant
  supply), money (a spending level that becomes the new normal).

ESSENCE: hunger is not one signal but a committee. The stomach reports
  stretch and empties in hours. The gut reports what has arrived and how
  far along it is. Fat tissue reports the size of the whole store on a
  timescale of weeks. The last one is the quiet vote that keeps
  overriding the others.
ROOT: living machinery / a slow chemical message holds a body-wide state
  that no fast one can; engineering (a controller reading several
  sensors at different speeds).
CANNOT: no talking a body out of a store-level signal with a meal-level
  one — filling the stomach settles this hour and changes nothing about
  the week. And no simple fix by adding the store's own signal: in most
  heavy bodies that signal is already high and is being ignored, which
  is the failure rather than a shortage.
THREAD: engineering (a slow master control and a fast local one
  disagreeing), money (a monthly budget against a daily wallet), sleep +
  habits (short sleep raising hunger and lowering the fullness report).

ESSENCE: the appetite drugs settled an old argument by accident. Copy a
  gut hormone, give it a long life in the blood, and people who had
  fought hunger for decades simply stopped thinking about food. Whatever
  hunger is, it was never a measure of character.
ROOT: this file / hunger is a signal, and a signal can be supplied;
  evidence + measurement (an intervention that moves an outcome settles
  a causal question that argument could not).
CANNOT: no reading a person's discipline off their weight — the same
  person, same will, changes behaviour entirely when the signal changes.
  And no free ride: the effect lasts while the drug does, the weight
  returns when it stops, and muscle is lost alongside fat unless
  training and protein defend it.
THREAD: medicine (a condition reframed the day a treatment worked),
  right + wrong (moral judgments quietly resting on a factual mistake),
  training (why resistance training is part of the prescription).

ESSENCE: the same energy fills people to very different degrees. A plate
  of potatoes, a bowl of soup, a chicken breast and a handful of
  biscuits can carry equal fuel, and only one of them leaves a person
  looking for more. Bulk, water, protein and chewing all buy fullness
  that the fuel count cannot see.
ROOT: this file / hunger is signalled by stretch, arrival and
  composition — none of which is energy; living machinery (food does
  three unrelated jobs, and satisfaction tracks none of them cleanly).
CANNOT: no predicting how much someone eats from the energy density
  alone — dilute a food with water or air and intake falls without
  anyone deciding anything. And no fullness from drinks: liquid calories
  are barely registered by the machinery that counts arrivals.
THREAD: food + growing (why the cheapest energy is also the least
  filling), engineering (a signal measuring a proxy that usually tracks
  the thing and here does not), money (spending that feels smaller in
  small notes).

ESSENCE: you lose protein every day whether you eat any or not — the
  body is constantly pulling its own structures apart and rebuilding
  them, and some is lost in the process. So the requirement is a leak
  rate, not an appetite, and eating below it means the body quietly
  dismantles muscle to pay.
ROOT: living machinery / a living thing is never switched off, so its
  parts are replaced under load; the living world (an unused ability is
  dropped, and unfed structure is the first to go).
CANNOT: no storing surplus protein as protein — there is no warehouse
  for it, so an excess is burnt for fuel or converted, and the extra
  above the requirement buys nothing structural. And no protein damaging
  a healthy kidney: that claim has been looked for repeatedly and not
  found, though a failing kidney is a different case.
THREAD: living machinery (repair as roadwork with the traffic running),
  training (a training stimulus without material to answer it), building
  (a site with the crew paid and no steel delivered).

ESSENCE: of the twenty bricks proteins are built from, nine cannot be
  made by human chemistry at all. That is the whole meaning of
  "essential" — not most important, not most useful, simply not
  manufacturable here. The other eleven matter exactly as much and are
  built in-house.
ROOT: living machinery / a vitamin is a hole in our own factory — the
  same hole, in the parts list rather than the tool list.
CANNOT: no substitute for a cannot-make, so a diet short of one brick is
  short however much total protein it carries. But no requirement to
  combine at a single meal either: the body holds a free pool of bricks
  for hours, so the old rule about pairing foods at one sitting was
  never true — the day is the unit, not the plate.
THREAD: food + growing (the grain and the bean whose gaps differ),
  building (standard parts, some bought in and some made on site),
  living machinery (the short universal parts list everything alive is
  built from).

ESSENCE: protein on a label is not protein in a body. Some sources are
  digested almost completely and some resist; some are short of one

  the limiting brick arrives, and that can be half the number printed.
ROOT: living machinery / the gut wall is the border, and what never
  crosses it never reaches you; this file (a supply is set by its
  scarcest part, not its total).
CANNOT: no reading usable protein off a total — a food can be
  protein-rich and nearly useless for building. And no fixing a poor
  source with quantity alone in a small stomach, which is why the
  problem is severe in young children and mild in adults.
THREAD: food + growing (the traditional pairings that solved this
  without theory), engineering (rated capacity against delivered

ESSENCE: fats differ by shape. A chain with no kinks packs neatly and
  sets solid; one kink and it cannot stack, so it stays liquid at room
  temperature; more kinks and it is both runnier and far easier to
  attack. Hard or soft, stable or rancid, is one fact about shape.
ROOT: chemistry / molecules that stack closely hold together more
  strongly, and a bend prevents stacking; living machinery (the same
  eager chemistry that releases energy attacks the kinks).
CANNOT: no solid oil and no shelf-stable fish oil without changing the
  shape — the two properties are the same property. And no judging a fat
  by its source: within one seed or one animal there is a mixture, and
  "saturated fat" names a share, never a substance.
THREAD: cooking (why butter and oil behave differently in a pan), 97
  (fat going rancid as an attack on those same kinks), materials
  (crystal packing setting a melting point).

ESSENCE: to make a liquid oil behave like a hard fat, industry

  living thing makes. The body handled them badly. It is the clearest
  case in the whole field: a food ingredient identified, measured, and
  removed by law nearly everywhere.
ROOT: chemistry / a molecule's shape decides everything it does, and an
  unfamiliar shape meets machinery built for another; evidence +
  measurement (a harm large enough and specific enough to be seen
  through all the noise).
CANNOT: no gentle version — the harm ran in the same direction at every
  level studied, with no threshold anyone could defend. And no
  reassurance from a zero on a label: small amounts per serving round to
  zero legally in some places, so the ingredient list is the honest
  place to look.
THREAD: public health (a health gain delivered by changing the product
  rather than the shopper), law (a substance banned rather than

ESSENCE: carbohydrate is one job done at different speeds. Sugar arrives
  in minutes, cooked starch in tens of minutes, whole intact grains and
  beans over hours, and some starch never arrives at all and goes on to
  feed the gut instead. The chemistry at the end is identical; the
  timing is not.
ROOT: chemistry / a chain must be cut before its links can cross the

  storing much of the fast kind: the body's sugar store is a few hours'

ESSENCE: fibre is the part of a plant that human chemistry cannot open.
  Some of it soaks up water and turns the gut's contents to gel, slowing
  everything; some of it stays coarse and gives the contents bulk and
  speed. And the microbes in the far end ferment a great deal of it,
  which is how a food you cannot digest still feeds you.
ROOT: living machinery / the tube through you is outside you, and its
  far end is rented to tenants who can open what you cannot; cooking
  (fermentation is hired labour).
CANNOT: no fibre without a gut population — antibiotics or a long
  low-fibre stretch and the workforce thins, which is why sudden large
  increases produce gas and misery rather than benefit. And no
  interchanging the two kinds: gel and bulk do opposite things, so the
  supplement for one complaint makes the other worse.
THREAD: living machinery (the tenants who hold the ground), food +
  growing (grinding a grain removes the part the gut wanted),
  engineering (a filter medium and a bulking agent are different
  products).

ESSENCE: most vitamins and minerals are not fuel and not building

ESSENCE: every nutrient has a dose that kills as well as one that saves,
  and for some the two are not far apart. Enough vitamin A to fix night
  blindness is close to enough to damage a liver; selenium's useful and
  toxic doses sit within a few-fold of each other; iron kills small
  children who find the bottle.
ROOT: medicine / the dose makes the poison — applied to the substances
  most confidently believed to be exempt; this file (a tool in excess is
  not a better tool).
CANNOT: no safety by naturalness or by necessity — being required is
  exactly why the body has machinery that concentrates it, and that
  machinery is what makes an overdose possible. And no wide margin for
  the stored ones: the fat-soluble vitamins accumulate, so a daily
  excess that seems harmless is a rising balance.
THREAD: medicine (the same law stated for medicines), food + growing
  (fertiliser feeding by the spoon and burning by the sack), health
  practice (poisoning in a child who found the packet).

ESSENCE: the vitamins split cleanly by what they dissolve in, and the
  split explains two different facts at once. The water-soluble ones are
  not stored, so they must arrive regularly and an excess is flushed
  away. The fat-soluble ones are stored in fat and liver, so they last
  months and can be overdosed.
ROOT: chemistry / like dissolves like — a molecule lives wherever it is
  soluble, and the body's water is being flushed continuously while its
  fat is not; living machinery (the kidney's filter-then-buy-back, which
  keeps a short list of what to save).
CANNOT: no stockpiling the water-soluble ones, so no covering a week's
  requirement in one dose — the surplus is in the urine within hours,
  which is what expensive urine means. And no quickly correcting a
  fat-soluble deficiency or overdose: both are measured in months,
  because the store is what is being refilled or emptied.
THREAD: health practice (a drug's schedule set by how fast the body
  clears it), cooking (what dissolves in fat and what dissolves in
  water, deciding where flavour goes), living machinery (the kidney
  spending fuel to buy back what it wanted).

  channel, no urine channel, nothing but bleeding and shed gut lining.

ESSENCE: thirst is a late signal but a reliable one. The body detects
  concentration before volume, so the mouth goes dry at a loss that has
  barely begun to matter, and healthy people drinking to thirst stay in
  range for life. The famous daily target has no research behind it and
  never did.
ROOT: living machinery / the body keeps no water tank, so it defends
  concentration continuously and reads that rather than volume.
CANNOT: no benefit from drinking past thirst in a healthy person — the
  surplus is urine within the hour, and the claimed effects on skin,
  weight and concentration have not been found. And no safety at the top
  either: endurance athletes have died of drinking too much plain water,
  which dilutes the blood's salt faster than the kidney can dump it.
THREAD: living machinery (water follows salt, so neither moves alone),
  health practice (a rehydration mixture that works because it has
  both), training (the one situation where scheduled drinking beats
  thirst is prolonged heavy sweating).
