# 41 BEAUTY, PLAY AND HUMOR — the pleasures and their hidden work
The soft-looking field with hard bones: each pleasure here is
doing a job. Laws are tendencies; flagged where they wobble.

ESSENCE: beauty is order with a surprise in it — enough pattern to
  promise sense, enough deviation to demand attention; the live
  zone lies between wallpaper and static.
ROOT: the mind / perception hunts patterns and pays out pleasure
  when one is found barely.
THREAD: music (theme-and-variation is this law naked), gardens
  (between parking lot and jungle), fire and surf (order arriving
  as endless novelty — watchable forever).
ASKED-AS: pretty pleasing art paintings dull repetitive catchy songs landscapes sunsets staring waves

ESSENCE: symmetry pleases, and a slight deliberate break pleases
  more — the near-mirrored face, the pot with one flaw, the beat
  pushed just off center.
ROOT: the living world / symmetry signals health and clean growth
  (UNSURE on mechanism, iron on the preference); the pattern law
  above.
THREAD: pottery (the intentional flaw), jazz (swing is symmetry
  bent on purpose), lettering (the trained hand nudges the
  mathematical center).
ASKED-AS: attractive handmade imperfect charm wabi sabi crooked smile even features balance vase

ESSENCE: play is practice wearing a mask of pointlessness — the
  young of every clever species rehearse hunting, fighting, and
  losing where mistakes are free; fun is the wage that gets the
  repetitions done.
ROOT: the living world / costly universal behavior persists only
  if it pays; the free-mistake zone is the payment.
THREAD: kittens and chess (both rehearse wars never to be
  fought), fire drills and simulators (play formalized for
  adults), science (sanctioned "what if" with a budget).
ASKED-AS: kids pretend roughhousing puppies wrestling tag toys imagination recess games silly goofing

ESSENCE: a laugh marks a rule broken safely — something wrong
  (logic, dignity, expectation) inside a frame that is right (no
  harm, among friends); remove the wrong and it is dull, remove
  the safety and it is an attack.
ROOT: play's law above / humor is play with meanings; the mind —
  two readings held, then snapped.
THREAD: tickling (attack inside safety — and impossible by your
  own hand, since nothing is violated), puns (grammar's dignity
  slipping), the roast versus the insult (the frame is the whole
  difference).
ASKED-AS: funny falling banana peel slapstick offensive edgy dark mean teasing pranks fart

ESSENCE: timing is humor's physics — expectation built, held one
  beat past comfort, then snapped; the same words early or late
  land as nothing.
ROOT: music / tension needs time to build and resolution must
  land on the beat; the mind's expectations run on clocks.
THREAD: music (the rest before the final chord), suspense (dread
  is comic timing played slow), the auctioneer's held silence.
ASKED-AS: comedian delivery pause standup ruined funnier awkward anticipation moment setup waiting stage

ESSENCE: a joke explained is a joke dead — the laugh lives only
  in the instant of unassisted crossing. UNSURE on mechanism (the
  leap itself may be the reward: getting-it is a small private
  discovery, and discovery cannot be done on your behalf); the
  fact is iron.
ROOT: teaching+learning / the answer given is not the answer
  earned; owning the finding is part of the finding.
THREAD: magic (explained, it is a procedure), poetry (paraphrase
  carries the cargo and loses the poem), teaching (the struggled
  problem beats the solved example).
ASKED-AS: killed spoiled punchline meme inside reference translation flat unfunny groan dissected obvious

ESSENCE: laughter is social before it is funny — people laugh far
  more in company, catch laughs from strangers, and mostly laugh
  at remarks that are not jokes; it is a bonding sound wearing
  amusement's clothes.
ROOT: people together / synchrony binds — shared laughter is a
  two-second ritual.
THREAD: laugh tracks (engineering built on the contagion), cinema
  versus the same film alone on a couch, applause and yawns (the
  same contagion in other clothes).
ASKED-AS: giggling spreads crowd audience solo watching comedy friends nervous church inappropriate cracking

ESSENCE: play's deepest pull sits at almost-too-hard — a task
  just past comfortable skill with instant feedback; too easy
  bores, too hard frightens, and in the seam between, hours
  vanish.
ROOT: teaching+learning / the same seam is where learning runs
  fastest, which is likely the point (UNSURE on why it feels like
  joy, iron on where the seam sits).
THREAD: games (levels exist to chase the seam as skill grows),
  climbing and fishing, work at its best (the craftsman's lost
  afternoon).
ASKED-AS: video addictive hooked flow zone losing track time puzzles challenge engrossed hobbies
