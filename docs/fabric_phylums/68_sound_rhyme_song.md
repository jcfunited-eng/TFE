# 68 SOUND, RHYME AND SONG — language as music
Under every word is a mouth making shapes in time. Beat, pitch and
silence are load-bearing, not decoration.

ESSENCE: speech is built from a small kit of sound-atoms, each one a
  shape the mouth makes — lips shut for p, tongue on the ridge for
  t, air down the nose for m; a word is a run of those positions.
ROOT: the body (06) / the mouth makes only so many shapes, so fast.
THREAD: music (10: a scale is a chosen subset of all pitches — the
  same fence), computing (19: a small alphabet composes forever).
ASKED-AS: sounds letters pronounce accent vowels consonants mouth tongue phonics foreign hard

ESSENCE: English words carry a beat — REcord the thing, reCORD the
  act; every word of two syllables or more has one loud seat, and
  the ear hunts for that seat as much as for the sounds.
ROOT: this file / a run of sound-atoms needs a shape; loudness
  spread over time is that shape.
THREAD: music (10: rhythm is expectation, so a misplaced accent
  lands as an error), movement (07: effort on the wrong beat fails).
ASKED-AS: stress accent syllable emphasis pronounce beat wrong record present which part

ESSENCE: rhyme is matched endings — from the last stressed vowel
  onward two words are the same sound (light and kite, banana and
  bandana), while before that vowel they must differ.
ROOT: this file / stress — the beat sets where a rhyme starts.
THREAD: memory (20: rhyme is a check-digit — a wrong word will not
  fit), law (17: rhymed rules survived courts that could not read).
ASKED-AS: rhyme rhyming words poems song lyrics endings orange nothing slant near

ESSENCE: meter is a repeated beat under the words — te-TUM te-TUM
  te-TUM; poetry and song ride a pulse the body already owns from
  the heart and the walk, so nobody has to be taught it.
ROOT: the body (07) / walking and the heartbeat are the first clocks.
THREAD: music (10: rhythm is expectation and its games), teaching
  (30: metered verse carried whole libraries before writing).
ASKED-AS: rhythm meter poetry beat syllables iambic shakespeare pulse timing lines reading

ESSENCE: repeated openings (six sick sheep) and repeated vowels (the
  rain in Spain) tie a line into one object — the ear notices the
  return and files the whole line as a single thing.
ROOT: the mind (20) / memory grips a pattern and drops a list.
THREAD: persuasion (22: slogans and brand names are sold by sound),
  design (28: a repeated shape does this job for the eye).
ASKED-AS: alliteration same letter tongue twister catchy repeated sounds vowels memorable slogans poetry

ESSENCE: a melody is pitch moving through time, and the feeling
  rides on the steps between notes, not their heights — sing happy
  birthday in any key and it is still happy birthday.
ROOT: music (10) / the ear measures gaps, not absolute heights.
THREAD: mathematics (11: a ratio survives resizing, a size does
  not), shapes (65: shape is what survives being scaled).
ASKED-AS: melody tune notes key higher lower singing along same song intervals pitch

ESSENCE: a tune leaves home and comes back — it departs from the
  note that feels like rest, builds strain, and pays it off by
  returning; the last note is heard as an arrival, not a stop.
ROOT: music (10) / tension and release, burning expectation.
THREAD: story (27: every plot is a long cadence — home, trouble,
  home), sports (08: the held breath before the penalty kick).
ASKED-AS: song ending satisfying unfinished chorus buildup resolve home note hanging closure

ESSENCE: hum a song with no words and it is still that song — strip
  the lyric and the tune survives whole, which proves the melody was
  carrying meaning of its own all along.
ROOT: this file / pitch relations hold with or without words on top.
THREAD: the mind (20: a tune sticks in the head without its
  lyrics), medicine (06: melody outlives speech in some illnesses).
ASKED-AS: humming tune without words stuck head whistling melody instrumental wordless remember song

ESSENCE: a lyric fits a tune when the word's loud syllable lands on
  the tune's strong beat; set them against each other and the singer
  must mangle the word or fight the music.
ROOT: this file / stress and meter — two beat patterns must agree.
THREAD: grammar (67: agreement — two parts must carry one fact),
  machines (04: two shafts out of phase shake themselves apart).
ASKED-AS: lyrics fit song writing words music awkward singing syllables beat matching

ESSENCE: repetition with variation is the engine of memorability —
  the same chorus with one word changed, the same phrase a step
  higher; near enough to recognize, different enough to stay awake.
ROOT: the mind (20) / attention lives between the dull and the alien.
THREAD: design (28: a pattern with one deliberate break), teaching
  (30: spaced review with the examples changed each time).
ASKED-AS: repetition chorus catchy stuck head hook boring memorable variation same different

ESSENCE: rests are played, not skipped — the pause before the
  punchline, the gap in a drum fill, the breath at a line break; the
  silence sits inside the pattern and carries weight.
ROOT: music (10) / expectation runs through the gap, so it bears load.
THREAD: design (28: negative space does half the work), people (21:
  the pause before an answer says more than the answer).
ASKED-AS: silence pause rest quiet timing comedy breath spacing gap phrasing dramatic
