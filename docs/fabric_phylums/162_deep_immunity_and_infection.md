# 162 DEEP IMMUNITY AND INFECTION — the body at war
File 86 holds the defence force's basic economics and file 110 what
happens when it fails a patient. This one holds the war itself: how
the body tells friend from stranger, how germs get round it, and what
each side cannot do however hard it tries.

ESSENCE: the defence that does nearly all the work is the one nobody
  thinks of as defence — unbroken skin, a layer of slime, acid, tears,
  a cough, urine flowing outward, and hairs beating a film of mucus up
  and out of the lungs day and night. Almost every infection begins
  with one of those being breached, bypassed or stopped.

ESSENCE: there are two defence forces with opposite economics. One is
  fast, general and already on duty — it recognises the handful of

  The other takes days to build, is built to fit this particular
  invader exactly, and remembers. Neither could do the other's job.
