# 35 CHANCE AND RISK — laws for what no one can know
Single events are mute; crowds of events speak precisely. Every law
here is that one coin seen from another side.

ESSENCE: chance has laws — no one can call one fair coin flip, and
  anyone can call a million of them within a whisker (a fair
  million lands within a few thousand of half-and-half).
ROOT: premise — ignorance of the single case plus symmetry across
  cases; the crowd averages the ignorance away.
THREAD: insurance (no one knows whose house burns; the company
  knows how many), physics (disorder wins IS this counting),
  polling a nation from a fair thousand.

ESSENCE: the long run always arrives — averages walk toward the
  true odds as tries pile up, and every streak drowns in enough
  trials.
ROOT: the crowd-law above; each new try dilutes all history.
THREAD: casinos (the house needs no luck, only your continued
  presence), a full season settling who the good hitters really
  are, small samples lying everywhere (their long run has not
  arrived yet).
ASKED-AS: hot hand winning betting slots poker eventually even out gambler bankroll grind

ESSENCE: a coin has no memory — past flips leave nothing on the
  next one, so "due" is a fiction; five heads running make the
  sixth exactly even money.
ROOT: independence — each trial starts fresh; the long run works
  by dilution, never by correction.
THREAD: roulette halls posting past numbers (selling meaningless
  history), "due for a hit" in sports, a family of five girls
  awaiting the sixth child at the same old half.
ASKED-AS: gambler fallacy overdue slot machine jackpot baby boy odds tails lucky charm

ESSENCE: rare times many equals often — a one-in-a-million day
  happens daily in a city of millions; the lottery is hopeless per
  ticket and near-certain per nation.
ROOT: crowd-law — small chances sum across many independent tries.
THREAD: aviation (two separate one-in-a-thousand systems fail
  together at one-in-a-million — safety is multiplication),
  coincidence (a dream matching the news is the lottery paying out
  somewhere), evolution (rare copying slips times countless
  copies).
ASKED-AS: miracle amazing twins birthday paradox stranger same thinking someone called psychic freak

ESSENCE: a gamble's honest price is every outcome weighed by its
  chance and summed — expected value; a 10% shot at $100 is worth
  $10 no matter how the shot feels.
ROOT: crowd-law — the weighted sum is what repetition actually
  pays.
THREAD: insurance bought sensibly (paying a little above the
  arithmetic to move ruin off your own books — see the wall,
  below), medicine (treatments chosen by outcomes weighed by
  odds), every casino game priced a few percent against you.
ASKED-AS: raffle warranty scratch card powerball payout smart deal betting math cost winnings

ESSENCE: variance is the scatter around the average, and it is the
  toll the average charges — two bets with identical expected
  value can differ wildly in how rough the ride is.
ROOT: chance pays in spreads, not numbers; the average is a
  summary, not a promise.
THREAD: salary vs commission (same yearly average, different
  ride), farming (one harvest is a draw — hence granaries),
  diversifying (many draws average their own scatter down).
ASKED-AS: risky steady paycheck freelance income swings ups downs volatile safe job lumpy

ESSENCE: zero is an absorbing wall — touch it once and the game
  ends whatever the odds ahead; so bet size, not just edge,
  decides survival: a winning game bet too big is a losing game.
ROOT: variance meeting a floor no one passes back through; the
  long run pays only those still present when it arrives.
THREAD: trading (position size is the whole difference between
  investing and gambling), fit species erased by one bad decade
  (nature's ruin wall), the business that was right too early —
  out of cash before its long run arrived.
ASKED-AS: bankrupt all in savings emergency fund leverage wipeout drawdown overextended stake

ESSENCE: the extraordinary tends back toward the ordinary — a
  record year is part skill, part luck; the skill stays, the luck
  is redrawn, so the follow-up disappoints on schedule.
ROOT: any showing is true level plus draw (see variance); extremes
  are where the draw concentrated.
THREAD: the magazine-cover jinx (arithmetic, not curses), very
  tall fathers' sons (tall, but less so — where the law was first
  seen), patients trying anything at their worst and crowning
  whatever they tried.
ASKED-AS: sophomore slump sequel worse hyped rookie comeback cooled off second album flop

ESSENCE: chances multiply only when events are truly independent —
  two honest one-in-a-hundred surprises together make one in ten
  thousand, but if one drives the other the multiplication is a
  lie.
ROOT: independence is a claim about the world, not arithmetic; the
  math follows only where the events do not touch.
THREAD: passwords (each added character honestly multiplies the
  search), aviation again (spares count only if they share nothing
  — one shared fuel feed re-links them), lightning twice on the
  same golfer (tall, outdoors, often: linked, not cursed).
