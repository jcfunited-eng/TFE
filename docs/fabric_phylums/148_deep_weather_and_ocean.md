# 148 DEEP WEATHER AND OCEAN — the fluid planet
Two fluids share one ball of rock, are heated unevenly, and are made
to spin. Everything that follows — every wind, storm, current, wave
and tide — is those three facts working themselves out. These are the
laws, not the readings.

ESSENCE: the atmosphere is an engine. The tropics receive far more
  sunlight than they give back and the poles give back more than they
  receive, so heat must travel from one to the other — and every wind,
  storm and ocean current is part of the machinery doing that carrying.
ROOT: heat and electricity / an engine lets heat fall from hot to cold
  and takes work on the way down, and the planet has a permanent hot
  end and a permanent cold end.
THREAD: heat and electricity (work harvested from a flow, never from a
  still pool), money (profit from a difference), the body (blood
  carrying heat outward), energy (a temperature gradient as a resource).

ESSENCE: air rising at the equator cannot reach the poles in one

  circulation, not an accident of geography.

ESSENCE: wind is a difference in pressure being paid out. Air pushed
  harder on one side than the other accelerates toward the weaker
  side, and the closer together the pressures are packed, the harder
  the push — so a weather map's crowded lines are literally a picture
  of the wind's strength.

ESSENCE: on a spinning world the wind cannot go straight. Deflected
  steadily to one side, air aiming at a low pressure keeps missing it
  and ends up circling instead — which is why weather systems are
  spirals rather than simple inflows, and why they turn one way in the
  north and the other in the south.
ROOT: motion and force / a thing moving freely over a rotating surface

THREAD: motion and force (a moving thing curving on a spinning world),

ESSENCE: above the ground the wind ends up blowing along the pressure

ESSENCE: friction breaks the balance at the bottom. Near the ground

ESSENCE: a low is a machine for lifting air and an anticyclone is a

ESSENCE: sometimes the air gets warmer with height instead of cooler,

ESSENCE: hail is a measure of an updraught's strength. A stone can

ESSENCE: a storm makes lightning only if it has ice. The charge is
