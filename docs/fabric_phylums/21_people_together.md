# 21 PEOPLE TOGETHER — the mind's laws, compounded
Groups, status, trust, crowds, institutions. Softer still than the
mind's file: tendencies with exceptions, worn openly as UNSURE.

ESSENCE: people sort into us and them on any handle offered —
  shirt color will do — and warmth flows inward, suspicion
  outward, within minutes.
ROOT: the mind / pattern-finder plus fast verdicts, aimed at
  people. Why the trigger is so hairline is UNSURE; that it is,
  is measured.
CANNOT: no durable group without an edge — a club everyone belongs
  to binds no one. The sorting cannot be deleted, only redrawn.
THREAD: sports (a jersey manufactures an us in one afternoon), war
  (the first act of every war is redrawing "them"), meaning +
  ritual (congregation and outsider arrive together).
ASKED-AS: tribalism cliques teams rivalry fans prejudice belonging playground school strangers

ESSENCE: every lasting group grows a ladder, and members track
  their rung with an animal's vigilance; most social pain is
  rung-pain.
ROOT: UNSURE at depth — the hen yard had pecking orders before
  humans named them; the tracking looks inherited.
CANNOT: no group of equals stays flat for long — flatten the
  ladder and it regrows in new clothes (revolutions re-invent
  rank). No status for everyone: a rung's worth IS its scarcity.
THREAD: money (luxury goods are rungs made purchasable), language
  (honorifics and titles), war (armies write the ladder on the
  sleeve).
ASKED-AS: popularity hierarchy ranking office politics promotion respect social standing envy

ESSENCE: trust builds in drops and drains in buckets — one
  betrayal outweighs a hundred kept promises.
ROOT: chance + risk / misplaced trust costs ruin, extra vigilance
  costs little; the asymmetry is honest bookkeeping. UNSURE.
CANNOT: no buying back broken trust at the price it sold for. And
  past village scale, no verifying everyone yourself — trust must
  be borrowed (uniforms, brands, credentials); society IS that
  lending system.
THREAD: money (credit means "he believes"; a bank run is trust
  draining in buckets), law (a contract is trust written down for
  strangers), medicine (the white coat is borrowed trust).
ASKED-AS: cheating lying forgiveness reputation ruined apology relationships affair scandal liar

ESSENCE: a crowd is not a large person — packed and aroused,
  individuals shed self-monitoring and sync to neighbors, and the
  crowd does what none of its members would.
ROOT: the mind / attention is one beam, and the crowd captures it.
  Mechanism UNSURE; the observations are centuries deep.
CANNOT: no reasoning with a crowd mid-surge — argument needs a
  listener and the crowd has none. A panic cannot be recalled by
  whoever started it.
THREAD: money (a crash is a crowd-surge with tickers), physics
  (phase change is the rhyme: same molecules, different
  substance), music (the choir and the stadium wave — the same
  sync, worn kindly).
ASKED-AS: mob mentality riot concert stampede protest trampled madness chanting frenzy

ESSENCE: people match the room — most will doubt their own eyes
  rather than stand alone against a unanimous group; one ally
  breaks the spell.
ROOT: people together / rung-pain — dissent risks the ladder. How
  strong varies by culture and era: UNSURE.
CANNOT: no reading a unanimous room as independent confirmation —
  unanimity manufactures itself.
THREAD: evidence + measurement (secret ballots and separately
  polled juries exist for this), image + design (fashion is the
  room, matched), law (whistleblower protections pay for the
  spell-breaking).
ASKED-AS: peer pressure fitting in speaking up disagreeing meetings silence groupthink awkward

ESSENCE: favors demand return — every human society keeps
  gift-debts, and the ledger is felt in the body as obligation.
ROOT: right + wrong / reciprocity is ethics' oldest floor; the
  ledger may predate humanity — apes keep it too. UNSURE which
  root is deeper.
CANNOT: no accepting a gift and staying even — the ledger opens
  whether you like it or not (the salesman's free coffee is not
  free).
THREAD: persuasion (the sample sells), money (currency is the
  ledger made portable and exact), power (patronage runs states
  on it).
ASKED-AS: owing paying back strings attached presents treats guilt rounds drinks dinner

ESSENCE: gossip is the group's immune system — talk about absent
  third parties spreads reputation, prices betrayal, and polices
  what force cannot reach.
ROOT: people together / trust drains in buckets — the ledger needs
  couriers. That most conversation is this is measured, roughly;
  UNSURE on the fraction.
CANNOT: no group above village size without gossip or its
  descendants — news, reviews, credit scores; face-to-face
  verification cannot scale.
THREAD: money (a credit score is institutional gossip), computing
  (review sites), law (defamation law exists because gossip is
  power).
ASKED-AS: rumors behind backs word spreading small town whisper network cancel culture grapevine

ESSENCE: size changes substance — somewhere past a hundred-odd
  members, everyone-knows-everyone breaks, and rules must replace
  relationships; each growth trades warmth for structure.
ROOT: the mind / the workbench is small — tracked relationships
  have a ceiling. Exact number UNSURE; the transition is
  everywhere.
CANNOT: no running a nation on the trust that runs a family. No
  village intimacy at city scale — anonymity arrives with the
  crowd, whatever anyone wishes.
THREAD: power + governance (the state is the technology for
  cooperation past the ceiling), money (startups feel the snap
  when the founder no longer knows every name), war (units
  cluster near the ceiling, UNSURE).
ASKED-AS: company growing bureaucracy red tape team culture changing policies neighbors impersonal

ESSENCE: a lasting group hardens into an institution — roles,
  rituals, and rules that recruit new humans into old shapes; the
  people flow through, the shape stays.
ROOT: people together / the room matched, compounded over
  generations. UNSURE as law; iron as record.
CANNOT: no steering an institution like a boat — it turns the way
  a river changes course. And nothing survives on founders'
  intent: what is not re-taught each generation is gone in one.
THREAD: law (the court outlives every judge), language (grammar is
  an institution nobody runs), meaning + ritual (the oldest
  proofs still standing).
ASKED-AS: organizations tradition church school army corporation reform slow customs outlasting
