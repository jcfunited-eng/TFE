# 16 MONEY AND TRADE — agreement wearing a number
Nothing in this file is physical. Every law here is a law about
promises among strangers — the whole field stands on language and
trust, and falls with them.

ESSENCE: money is a token made valuable by agreement alone — the
  paper is worthless; it works exactly as long as everyone
  believes tomorrow's stranger will take it.
ROOT: language / words bind by agreement, not by nature — money
  is the same arbitrary-but-binding trick with numbers on it.
THREAD: language (the original worthless-token machine), maps
  (the agreed zero at Greenwich), law (titles and borders —
  fictions with teeth).
ASKED-AS: cash dollar spend gold backed bitcoin currency banknote coins trust

ESSENCE: a price is a discovered meeting point — where the most a
  buyer will pay crosses the least a seller will take; nobody
  decrees it, the haggling finds it.
ROOT: trade / both sides must win — price is where two private
  valuations overlap.
THREAD: physics (equilibrium — the resting point of opposing
  pushes), auctions, wages (a salary is a price wearing a collar).
ASKED-AS: supply demand market worth cost rent control shortage expensive cheap gouging

ESSENCE: value needs wanting AND rarity together; either alone is
  worthless — air is vital and free, a misprinted stamp is
  useless and priceless.
ROOT: premise — many wanters over few pieces; bidding does the
  rest.
THREAD: collecting (the error is the treasure), music (the
  unreleased tape), water and diamonds — the old riddle, solved
  by this line.
ASKED-AS: gold baseball cards collectibles antiques limited edition scarce expensive sneakers

ESSENCE: a voluntary trade happens only when each side values
  what it gets above what it gives — both walk away richer by
  their own measure, which is why trade is not war.
ROOT: premise — valuations differ by person and moment; the
  difference is the fuel.
THREAD: farming (the harvest swap that fed the first towns),
  chemistry (bonds form when the joined state suits both atoms —
  UNSURE as more than rhyme), friendship (favors ride the same
  surplus).
ASKED-AS: buying selling deal bargain fair mutual benefit ripped off zero sum

ESSENCE: interest is the price of time — money now beats the same
  money later (it can work meanwhile, and later is uncertain),
  so waiting is rented; compounding then grows the rent on the
  rent (at 7% a sum doubles in ~10 years).
ROOT: premise — the future is discounted for uncertainty and
  delay.
THREAD: fermentation (growth feeding on its own growth — the
  cooking file flagged it), herds (the oldest interest: flocks
  breed their increase), maintenance (the skipped repair
  compounds — file 18 runs on this law).
ASKED-AS: loan mortgage credit card percent bank account retirement investing payments early

ESSENCE: printing more tokens against the same goods thins every
  token — inflation is dilution: a tax on all held money, levied
  without a bill, paid by whoever holds the old paper.
ROOT: money-as-token — the whole pile of tokens prices the whole
  shelf of goods.
THREAD: language (a superlative spent on everything soon buys
  nothing — word inflation is real), cooking (watering the soup:
  more bowls, same soup).
ASKED-AS: rising groceries gas rent costlier living wages salary shrinking stimulus paycheck

ESSENCE: the ledger is trade's memory, and honest ledgers write
  everything twice — what arrived and where it came from; every
  asset is someone's liability, so the books balance because both
  columns describe one event.
ROOT: physics / conservation — bookkeeping is that law in a suit
  (the physics file says so); the two fabrics — every gain writes
  its debt beside it.
THREAD: chemistry (reaction equations balance both sides), the
  fabric itself (every minted CAN carries its CANNOT —
  double-entry done in ink).
ASKED-AS: accounting debit credit spreadsheet audit fraud embezzlement receipts income expenses

ESSENCE: trust is the real currency — every trade wider than
  hand-to-hand runs on believed promises (ship now, pay later),
  and trust is what makes strangers affordable to deal with.
ROOT: the deal as exchanged promises (law file); repetition —
  cheat once here, trade never again.
THREAD: law (enforcement is trust's backstop, priced in), banking
  (a bank IS stored trust — see the run, below), medicine (the
  placebo — belief doing real work, UNSURE as more than rhyme).
ASKED-AS: handshake credit deposit warranty escrow references vouching reviews online sellers

ESSENCE: a bubble is price detached from thing — when the reason
  to buy becomes "others will buy," value rides shared belief
  that needs new believers to live; when recruits run out it does
  not deflate, it collapses (tulips 1637, stocks 1929, houses
  2008).
ROOT: money-as-agreed-fiction — a bubble is agreement outrunning
  the thing agreed upon.
THREAD: fashion crazes, stampedes (each runs because the others
  run), applause dying in a hall — belief-structures share one
  failure mode.
ASKED-AS: crypto meme mania crash boom bust fomo speculation frenzy hype
