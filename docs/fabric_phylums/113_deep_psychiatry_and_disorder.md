# 113 DEEP DISORDER AND CARE — when minds stop working well
Where file 112 holds how people behave, this one holds what happens
when the behaving stops working, how the trouble gets named, what helps,
and what naming it costs.

ESSENCE: the line is not drawn at the trait. Plenty of people are
  anxious, sleepless, obsessive or odd and are getting on with their
  lives. What makes it a disorder is the cost — distress the person
  cannot carry, or a life they can no longer run — and that is judged
  against the life they are actually in.
ROOT: this file / a description of a mind is never a measurement
  against a standard, because there is no standard mind to measure
  against.
CANNOT: no disorder read off the behaviour alone, so no diagnosing
  somebody who is coping. And no line that stays still: the same
  restlessness that fails a classroom passes on a trawler, so moving
  the person can end the disorder without changing anything about
  them.
