# 99A DEEP RIGHT AND WRONG — the working parts of ethics
File 25 records the shapes every society keeps rediscovering. This
one opens the machine and names the moving parts people actually
argue with. It still preaches nothing.

ESSENCE: strip any moral system down and one item is always at the
  bottom: hurting someone requires a reason. Cultures disagree
  furiously about food, sex, purity, and gods, and agree that
  unprovoked injury has to be accounted for.
ROOT: right + wrong / reciprocity's floor; premise — a rule permitting
  free injury cannot be lived under by the injured, so no group
  carries it for long.
THREAD: law (assault is the oldest crime on every list), medicine
  (first, do no harm — the profession's opening line), people
  together.

ESSENCE: the same act is a gift, a trade, or a theft depending on one

THREAD: medicine (informed consent — the form is an attempt to prove
  all three parts happened), money (a contract signed under duress is

ASKED-AS: permission agreed said yes drunk pressured signed contract fine print tricked okay

ESSENCE: two people can both demand fairness and mean opposite things.
  One means the process was even — same rules, nobody cheated, the
  dice were not loaded. The other means the result was even — nobody
  ended with nothing. A perfectly fair race can end in a badly unfair
  pile.
ROOT: right + wrong / the fair-shares instinct, split into its two
  readings; premise — a procedure and its outcome are separate objects
  and can each be judged alone.
THREAD: sports (identical rules for both teams, and one team wins
  forty to nothing), money (markets are procedure-fair by design and
  say nothing about the pile), law (due process is procedural fairness
  written down).
ASKED-AS: fair unfair rules rigged equal chance winners losers taxes level playing field

ESSENCE: equality is giving everyone the same; equity is giving
  everyone what they need to arrive at the same place. Identical shoes
  for every child is equal; shoes that fit is equity. Which one is
  right turns on whether the starting difference was anybody's doing.
ROOT: this file / procedure against outcome, sharpened down to the
  actual handing-out.
THREAD: teaching (extra time on a test — this argument inside one
  classroom), medicine (triage gives most to the worst off, never
  equal shares to all), money (a flat tax against a graduated one,
  argued forever).
ASKED-AS: same treatment special help wheelchair ramp extra time disability handouts head start

ESSENCE: people feel they earned what they hold, and the largest

THREAD: chance + risk (a record year is true level plus draw), money

ESSENCE: every right is a bill sent to somebody else. A right to speak
  costs others the power to silence; a right to a lawyer costs a
  salary; a right to be left alone costs everyone else access. A right
  with no payer is a wish with good posture.
ROOT: law / rights and duties are two ends of one object; premise — a

THREAD: money (an asset on one book is a liability on another — double

ESSENCE: some obligations are hard-edged — do not lie, do not kill —
  and can be met completely, by everyone, forever. Others are open —
  help, give, show up — and have no ceiling at all. An obligation with
  no ceiling consumes whoever takes it seriously.
ROOT: this file / rights cost someone — a duty to provide has no
  natural stopping point; premise — an obligation must be performable
  or it stops working as one.
THREAD: medicine (the shift ends; the need does not), money (charity,
  and the permanent question of how much), law (bystanders are rarely
  required to rescue — the limit made legal).
ASKED-AS: obligation should help charity how much give guilt volunteering owe strangers enough

ESSENCE: people judge doing far more harshly than allowing, at

  tragic. Same body count, different verdicts, everywhere it has been
  asked.
ROOT: right + wrong / intent and agency drive blame — an act has an

THREAD: law (murder against failure to rescue), medicine (withdrawing
  treatment against never starting it — the same knot at every
  bedside), engineering (a designed failure against an unfixed one).
ASKED-AS: trolley problem push switch let die versus kill unplug machine doing nothing

ESSENCE: a child drowning in front of you commands everything you

ESSENCE: one named child down a well moves a nation; ten thousand dead

ESSENCE: a promise is one person spending another person's freedom —

ESSENCE: nearly everyone says lying is wrong and nearly everyone lies

ROOT: right + wrong / verdicts arrive as feelings with reasons drafted

ESSENCE: some lies genuinely protect — the hidden refugee, the

ESSENCE: loyalty says stand by your own; honesty says say what

ESSENCE: do unto others as you would have done to you works until the
  other is not you. The lover of surprise parties throws one for

ROOT: right + wrong / the golden rule's convergence; the mind

ESSENCE: there are three ways to judge one act, and they are three
  questions rather than three answers: what kind of person does this,

ESSENCE: two drunk drivers, identical choices, identical speed. A

ESSENCE: big harms are mostly done by many people each doing something

ESSENCE: the person who reports the wrongdoing usually loses — the

ESSENCE: forgiving is not saying it was fine, not forgetting, and not
