# 32 HEREDITY — the recipe that copies itself
How the instructions for a creature travel, and what the traveling
forbids.

ESSENCE: traits ride discrete instructions — genes — passed whole
  from parent to child; inheritance is particles, not paint, which
  is why a trait can skip a generation and return intact.
ROOT: premise — pea crosses come out in clean ratios (3:1), and
  blended paint cannot make ratios.
CANNOT: no averaging ancestors into soup — the particles stay
  whole and can resurface unmixed generations later.
THREAD: computing (digital survives copying, analog smears — genes
  chose digital first), language (vocabulary passes in whole
  words; accents blend, words are particles), music (notes, not
  smears).
ASKED-AS: eye color blue red hair grandma baby resemblance family look freckles

ESSENCE: the instructions are a recipe, not a blueprint — no gene
  maps to a body part as a drawing maps to a wall; genes say "make
  this protein now, here," and the body emerges from the cooking.
ROOT: living world / the cell — instructions mean nothing except
  inside a kitchen already running.
CANNOT: no reading the body straight off the text: identical twins
  — one recipe cooked twice — still differ, fingerprints and all.
  No changing one ingredient and changing only one thing.
THREAD: cooking (same recipe, two kitchens, two breads), building
  (the contrast: blueprints locate every brick; recipes locate
  nothing), law (statutes are recipes too — meaning emerges in the
  courts' kitchen).
ASKED-AS: dna code destiny determine born development womb grows environment unfold

ESSENCE: copying makes rare errors — roughly one letter in many
  millions per generation — and those typos are the only new
  material evolution ever receives.
ROOT: physics / no copy is perfect; living world / evolution then
  sorts the typos.
CANNOT: no mutation aimed at need — the typo falls blind, mostly
  useless or harmful, and the rare helpful one is luck kept later.
  No error-free lineage, ever.
THREAD: language (every dialect began as sloppy copying that
  stuck), keeping knowledge (scribes' slips let scholars draw
  family trees of manuscripts — same math), craft (the
  apprentice's drift from the master's hand).
ASKED-AS: dna random radiation cancer defects glitch accidents novelty change variation

ESSENCE: instructions come in pairs, one from each parent, and
  some versions shout while others whisper — a whispered version
  rides hidden until two meet. (two carriers: 1 in 4 shows it)
ROOT: heredity / particles — hiding is possible only because the
  particle survives unexpressed.
CANNOT: no losing a trait by hiding it — carriers keep dealing it
  forward. And whoever shows the whispered trait holds two copies
  and no loud one: no secretly carrying what you do not show.
THREAD: chance + risk (the 1-in-4 is coin arithmetic in pea
  costume), story (the lost heir's face surfacing generations on),
  medicine (carrier screening exists because of this law).
ASKED-AS: recessive dominant blue eyes red hair skip albino disease odds

ESSENCE: sex is a shuffle — each parent deals a randomized half of
  their deck, so every child is a hand never played before;
  variation is the point: insurance against changing worlds and
  evolving parasites.
ROOT: living world / evolution — clones are a monoculture, one
  plague from ruin; shuffled offspring cannot all be opened by the
  same key.
CANNOT: no child a copy of a parent. No two siblings alike —
  except identical twins: one hand, dealt once, played twice.
THREAD: farming (banana plantations — all clones — live in fear of
  one fungus), money (diversification is this insurance in a
  suit), sports + games (why decks are shuffled at all).
ASKED-AS: brother sister unique mix combination lottery babies different reproduce genes

ESSENCE: every trait is nature and nurture both, always — the
  recipe sets ranges, the world picks the point; asking which made
  a trait is asking whether area comes from length or width.
ROOT: heredity / recipe not blueprint — recipes need kitchens.
CANNOT: no trait from genes alone (a recipe uncooked is paper) and
  none from world alone (no dough without a recipe). The
  nature-versus-nurture fight has no winner — the question is
  broken.
THREAD: farming (seed and soil — no farmer debates which grows the
  wheat), teaching + learning (talent and training, same
  rectangle), the mind (temperament dealt, character built on it).
ASKED-AS: born made upbringing environment personality height intelligence twins raised debate

ESSENCE: the body and the seed-line travel separately — what life
  does to your flesh (scars, muscle, skills) is not written back
  into what you pass on; the recipe rides sealed.
ROOT: heredity / the recipe is copied forward, never re-derived
  from the cook's life.
CANNOT: no inheriting your father's trained arm or your mother's
  French — every child starts from the recipe, not the results.
  Giraffes did not stretch their way to those necks.
THREAD: teaching + learning (culture is the workaround — the one
  channel where acquired things DO pass on), craft (each
  apprentice starts with untrained hands; the workshop's books are
  the trade's seed-line), keeping knowledge (writing is inherited
  experience — the exception we built).
ASKED-AS: workout tattoo learned kids habits piercings gym practice exercise suntan

ESSENCE: every cell carries the whole recipe but reads only its
  chapter — skin and brain hold identical books opened to
  different pages, set during development.
ROOT: heredity / recipe not blueprint — one text, many dishes.
CANNOT: mostly no going back — a committed cell holds its page
  (the lab can force a reset; the body almost never does — UNSURE
  at the edges).
THREAD: music (every stand could hold the full score; each plays
  one line), people together (specialists in one town all carry
  the same law, read differently), computing (one program, many
  roles by configuration).
ASKED-AS: stem organs liver heart specialize clone regenerate matching dna tissue

ESSENCE: UNSURE — the moving edges: some experience-marks on the
  recipe (chemical bookmarks) may pass a generation or two;
  recipes can now be edited on purpose, and read whole from a drop
  of blood. Held loosely, as of this writing.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / young findings wobble before they
  set.
CANNOT: even a deliberate edit escapes nothing deeper — it is a
  chosen typo that must still survive the world's sorting. And no
  full revival of inherited effort: the sealed seed-line stands as
  the rule, bookmarks at most annotate it.
THREAD: law + agreements (editing humans runs ahead of the
  rulebook), right + wrong (the scissors arrived before the
  ethics), farming (edited crops are already in the fields).
