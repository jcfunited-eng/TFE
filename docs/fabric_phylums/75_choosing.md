# 75 CHOOSING — how anything picks one thing over another

ESSENCE: choosing is comparing, and comparing is holding two
  things against a single measure — without one measure the two
  cannot be ranked, only listed.
ROOT: mathematics / counting — a measure turns things into
  amounts that can be set beside each other.
RULE: to compare two amounts — count one down against the other,
  one for one, until one of them runs out; the one still standing
  is the greater, and if they run out together they are equal.
THREAD: markets (price is the shared measure), sports (a score),
  weighing scales, medicine (triage).
ASKED-AS: compare weigh choose pick better worse rank more less greater which one prefer weighing

ESSENCE: every choice costs the ones not taken — the value of a
  thing chosen is what it gives minus the best of what it shut
  out.
ROOT: trade / a thing given for a thing.
THREAD: money (spending is choosing), time (an hour spent is an
  hour gone), farming (a field planted is a field not fallow).
ASKED-AS: cost tradeoff give up sacrifice instead alternative what if opportunity worth

ESSENCE: what a choice is worth depends on who is choosing and
  what they are after — the same option is a gift to one and a
  burden to another.
ROOT: the observer decides worth; nothing carries it alone.
THREAD: gifts, medicine (the same treatment suits one body and
  harms another), taste.
ASKED-AS: depends who for whom useful to me worth it their needs subjective preference

ESSENCE: enough beats best — a chooser with limited time takes
  the first option that clears the bar, because searching for the
  best costs more than the difference is worth.
ROOT: attention is finite; searching is spending.
THREAD: shopping, hiring, foraging animals, engineering
  tolerances.
ASKED-AS: good enough best perfect settle decide quickly satisfice bar threshold stop looking

ESSENCE: when nothing clears the bar, not choosing is the choice
  — and it must be recognised as one, taken deliberately, not
  fallen into.
ROOT: choosing / an empty field of options is still a finding.
THREAD: medicine (watchful waiting), law (a hung jury), chess (a
  waiting move).
ASKED-AS: none of them nothing good wait hold off do nothing abstain pass decline

ESSENCE: a choice made once and kept costs less than the same
  choice remade every time — habit is a decision stored.
ROOT: attention is finite, so repeating a settled choice wastes
  it.
THREAD: routines, standards, law (precedent), a well-worn path.
ASKED-AS: habit routine default rule of thumb always usually every time custom standard
