# 156 DEEP COOKING TECHNIQUE — the working knowledge of the kitchen
Cooking holds the raw laws and the pantry's slow chemistry sits beside
them. This is the floor between: what a cook is actually deciding at the
stove, why the standard advice is right or wrong, and which failures are
one-way. Not recipes and not ingredients — the mechanisms under the
hands. Every rule here also forbids something, and where the evidence is
genuinely unsettled it says UNSURE.

ESSENCE: how the heat arrives matters more than how hot it is. The same
  chicken at the same internal temperature is a different food from a pan,
  an oven, a pot of water or a fryer, because each delivers heat at a
  different rate through a different surface condition.

  own speed; this file / the surface and the centre cook on different
  clocks.
THREAD: welding (the same metal joined by different heat sources), drying (a
  kiln against sunlight), teaching (identical content delivered at different
  rates).

ESSENCE: cooking splits in two. Dry heat lets the surface climb past
  boiling, so it browns and builds flavour that was not there before. Moist
  heat holds everything at boiling or below, so nothing browns and tough
  tissue slowly dissolves instead. Two chemistries, two jobs.

  browning needs about a hundred and forty; this file / connective tissue
  dissolves with time at a moderate temperature.
THREAD: chemistry (two reactions with different thresholds), laundry (a hot
  wash against a steam press), metalwork (annealing against forging).
