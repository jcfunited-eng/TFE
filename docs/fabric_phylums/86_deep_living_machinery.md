# 86 DEEP LIVING MACHINERY — how life runs itself
Middle floors under a body's daily work: what a cell is actually
doing every second, what it must spend, and what it cannot buy at
any price. Every entry carries its cannot-twin.

ESSENCE: a cell is not a container of stuff but a held difference —
  more of this in, less of that out, charged on one side, sour on
  the other. The difference is the whole asset, and pumps in the
  wall spend fuel every second keeping it from evening out.
ROOT: physics / disorder wins — everything mixes unless something
  is paying not to.
THREAD: money (a firm is a held difference between in and out),
  building (a warm house is one difference, billed monthly),
  engineering (a battery dies by becoming even).
ASKED-AS: cell inside outside salt water pump alive dead battery charge leaking balance

ESSENCE: the wall's default answer is no — a film of oil turns away
  everything that likes water, which is nearly all of life's cargo.
  Every salt, sugar and message that gets in comes through a
  doorway that was built and is paid for, one shape per door.
ROOT: chemistry / oil and water refuse each other — the refusal is
  hired as the wall, then must be drilled through on purpose.
THREAD: computing (every open port is an attackable port), law (a
  border is checkpoints, not a line), medicine (drugs are shaped to
  fit doors the body already has).
ASKED-AS: membrane absorb blocked barrier alcohol brain nicotine patch soaks salt sugar poison

ESSENCE: life runs on one small rechargeable token, spent and
  recharged thousands of times a second — food charges it, every
  job in the body pays in it, and you remake something near your
  own weight of the stuff in a day.
ROOT: engineering / one currency lets any source pay any job; a
  thousand kinds of work need one kind of money.
THREAD: money (dying rich in savings and broke in cash is exactly
  how some poisons kill), engineering (mains supply versus the
  socket you actually plug into).
ASKED-AS: energy calories tired fuel burn metabolism sugar boost seconds cyanide sudden collapse

ESSENCE: an enzyme is a shaped clamp that holds two pieces in the
  exact pose a reaction needs, so a change that would take a
  century at body warmth happens millions of times a second — and
  the clamp walks away unchanged, ready for the next pair.
ROOT: chemistry / a reaction needs an entry fee before it can roll
  downhill; the clamp does not push, it lowers the fee.
THREAD: cooking (heat is the brute alternative — same reactions,
  forced), craft (a jig holds the work so the cut comes easy).
ASKED-AS: enzymes digestion lactose intolerant tenderizer detergent fever protein spoil yeast rennet

ESSENCE: a held value is never held still. The sensor reads, the
  correction arrives late, and by the time it lands the value has
  already gone past the target — so every steady thing in a body is
  really a small hunt around a number, wobbling inside a band.
ROOT: engineering / feedback — measure, compare, correct; the lag
  between measuring and correcting is where the wobble is born.
THREAD: flight (a nervous hand flies worse than a patient one),
  money (a rate change bites a year after the fear did).
ASKED-AS: blood sugar crash shaky thermostat swings hormones balance steady wobble monitor overshoot

ESSENCE: nothing alive eats energy — it eats a difference. A
  waterwheel needs the drop, not the water. Sunlight feeds the
  world because it arrives from a small hot spot into a cold dark
  sky; a warm ocean holds vast energy and can drive nothing.
ROOT: physics / heat flows hot to cold, and work is taken from the
  flowing, never from the having.
THREAD: weather (wind is a pressure difference spending itself),
  money (a trade needs two people valuing the same thing
  differently), engineering (every engine is a hot side and a cold
  side, and dies without both).
ASKED-AS: energy heat engine solar power hot cold difference flow work useless lukewarm

ESSENCE: growth builds on open ground with the plan still loose;
  repair is roadwork with the traffic running. The heart cannot
  stop for maintenance, the gut cannot close for refitting, so
  every repair is a compromise made under load.
ROOT: the body / a living thing is never switched off, so it never
  gets the workshop conditions a factory gets.
THREAD: building (renovating an occupied house), computing
  (patching a server that must not go down), farming (rebuild the
  soil or take the full harvest, not both this year).
ASKED-AS: healing growing kids fracture recovery rebuild muscle injury teenager stunted illness weight

ESSENCE: every division copies three billion letters and splits the
  library evenly, and the machinery checks its own work — yet the
  error rate is deliberately not zero, because near-perfect copying
  costs more time and fuel than the errors cost.
ROOT: heredity / no copy is perfect; and every extra nine of
  accuracy is bought at a rising price.
THREAD: computing (checksums cost bandwidth; nobody verifies
  everything), craft (the hand copy drifts however careful the
  scribe), money (checking stops where it costs more than the
  fraud).
ASKED-AS: cells dividing mutation copy mistakes skin gut cancer risk renewal typo proofreading

ESSENCE: a cell buys skill by giving things up. The nerve, the
  heart muscle and the red blood cell each became superb at one job
  and lost the ability to make another of itself — the red cell
  threw out its instruction book entirely to carry more cargo.
ROOT: the living world / doing one thing superbly needs equipment
  that gets in the way of doing anything else.
THREAD: people together (the surgeon cannot also grow the wheat),
  engineering (a purpose-built machine does one job and rusts at
  every other), money (the one-industry town).
ASKED-AS: nerve cells regrow spinal cord damage permanent brain heart muscle stem specialized replace

ESSENCE: your cells carry a suicide switch, and the body's rule
  runs backwards from what you would guess: a cell keeps living
  only while its neighbours keep telling it to. Lose the signal and
  it folds itself up quietly. Your fingers are separate because the
  webbing between them was told to go.
ROOT: engineering / a safe default fails closed — trusting a kill
  order to arrive is more dangerous than demanding permission to
  stay.
THREAD: cleaning (a house is kept by removing, not adding), craft
  (carving is subtraction), computing (a process killed the moment
  its heartbeat signal goes quiet).
ASKED-AS: cells die tadpole tail fingers webbed shedding skin tumor turnover billions daily

ESSENCE: your body recognises germs that did not exist when you
  were born because it never held a list of enemies. It
  manufactures billions of random shapes, destroys the ones that
  fit you, and keeps the rest waiting. Recognition is a lucky match
  found by having tried everything first.
ROOT: chance + risk / with enough random draws something fits
  anything; the sorting comes after the draw, never before.
THREAD: keeping knowledge (a library that buys everything and weeds
  later), strategy (a militia trained for no particular enemy),
  money (buying a whole field for one winner).
ASKED-AS: vaccine immunity antibodies chickenpox booster memory recognize random new virus rehearsal

ESSENCE: the heat, redness, swelling and throb are not the injury —
  they are your answer to it. Vessels are ordered to leak so fluid
  and defenders flood the neighbourhood, and the tool works by
  wrecking the local area: a firebreak burnt on purpose. Most of
  feeling ill is the defense, not the germ.
ROOT: the body / a blunt weapon used close to home, because nothing
  fine can be aimed inside flesh.
THREAD: war (the shelled village that had to be taken), cleaning
  (bleach clears the mould and the finish), money (a rescue that
  saves the system and ruins the savers).
ASKED-AS: swelling redness sore throat ache steroids ibuprofen arthritis chronic puffy sprain heat

ESSENCE: an infection is two rising curves — the invader doubling,
  and the defense ramping up days behind it. Who wins is mostly
  settled in the quiet first hours, by how many arrived and how
  fast each side climbs, not by how hard anyone tries afterward.
ROOT: mathematics / compounding — a head start against a doubling
  number is nearly impossible to make up.
THREAD: money (a debt compounding against a fixed wage), computing
  (patch before the worm spreads, never after), farming (weeding
  early beats weeding hard).
ASKED-AS: infection early antibiotics wound wash hands sepsis spreading days exposure catch masks

ESSENCE: an antibiotic does not create resistance — it clears the
  field. Among billions of bacteria a rare one already survives it
  by accident, and the drug hands that one an empty world with no
  competitors left. Worse, survivors pass the trick sideways to
  strangers, not only down to their children.
ROOT: the living world / selection needs only variation and a cull,
  and the drug supplies the cull.
THREAD: farming (the sprayed field breeds its own resistant pest),
  strategy (an enemy shaped by the one weapon you keep using).
ASKED-AS: antibiotics resistant superbug finish course overuse doctor prescribe livestock germs stronger useless

ESSENCE: the tube through you is outside you. Food in the gut has
  not entered the body until it crosses the wall — and that wall is
  the body's largest border and, over most of its length, one cell
  thick, because absorbing needs thin and defending needs thick and
  it cannot have both.
ROOT: physics / crossing a barrier is slower the thicker it is, so
  the surface that must take everything in cannot be armoured.
THREAD: building (the loading door is where the wall is weakest),
  law (customs posts are thin places by design), farming (a root is
  the same trick, folded outward instead of in).
ASKED-AS: stomach intestines absorb digest bacteria fiber leaky swallowed poop microbes lining cramps

ESSENCE: food does three unrelated jobs — fuel to burn, bricks to
  build with, and a short list of things the body needs and cannot
  make. Calories measure only the first, so a diet can be swimming
  in fuel and starving for bricks.
ROOT: the living world / life eats life because other bodies keep
  their parts already in the shapes we need.
THREAD: building (a site needs both diesel and steel and neither
  substitutes), money (income against materials on a job), cooking
  (a meal is judged on three axes at once).
ASKED-AS: calories protein diet nutrients malnutrition fat carbs muscle empty hungry supplements bloated

ESSENCE: a vitamin is not a kind of chemistry — it is a hole in our
  own factory. An ancestor who always ate the stuff stopped paying
  to build it, the broken line never mattered, and it quietly
  became a requirement. The list differs by species: sailors died
  of scurvy while the ship's cat, which makes its own, did not.
ROOT: the living world / an expensive ability that stops earning is
  dropped, and losing a factory you never need is a saving.
THREAD: heredity (a lost gene is a shut factory), money
  (outsourcing until you can no longer make the part), farming (a
  crop bred hard for one trait loses others quietly).
ASKED-AS: vitamins scurvy oranges deficiency supplements sunlight rickets pills essential cure limes

ESSENCE: the body keeps no water tank. Every drop is already at
  work holding something dissolved, so losing water is not spending
  savings — it is concentrating everything at once. And water
  follows salt everywhere: neither moves alone, which is why
  drinking seawater dries you out.
ROOT: chemistry / water crosses toward wherever the dissolved stuff
  is more crowded, with no say in the matter.
THREAD: cleaning (rinsing costs water in proportion to the dirt),
  farming (irrigation salts a field for exactly this reason),
  survival (thirst is the shortest clock a person owns).
ASKED-AS: dehydration thirsty pee kidneys seawater sweat electrolytes drinking salt cramps hangover headache

ESSENCE: one pump makes one pressure for the whole body, so no
  organ can ask the heart for more — each takes its share by
  widening or narrowing its own pipes. Your vessels, opened all at
  once, would hold far more blood than you own, so supply is a
  permanent rationing.
ROOT: engineering / one line at one pressure serving many taps —
  whoever opens widest gets the flow.
THREAD: building (every shower in the block on one main), computing
  (bandwidth taken by whoever asks loudest), money (a fixed budget
  with departments bidding).
ASKED-AS: blood pressure dizzy standing fainting cramps exercise cold hands flushed circulation pale shunt

ESSENCE: the heart's rest lives inside each beat, not between days
  — the pause while it refills is its entire holiday. And it feeds
  itself only during that pause, because the vessels in its own
  wall fill in the gaps. Race it hard enough and it shortens the
  very window it eats through.
ROOT: engineering / a pump that may never stop must take its
  maintenance in the gaps of its own cycle.
THREAD: machines (a bearing oiled by the same rotation it serves),
  work (the shift with no cover — the gap is the only rest).
ASKED-AS: heart beat pulse rate resting racing chest pain angina exercise rhythm pacemaker

ESSENCE: nothing pumps oxygen anywhere. It falls, step by step,
  from crowded air to empty cell, and each step is a smaller drop.
  Blood cannot carry it dissolved — water holds almost none — so it
  rides clipped to a carrier that grabs where it is plentiful and
  lets go where it is scarce, all of it passive.
ROOT: physics / things spread from crowded to empty by themselves,
  and nothing else moves a gas.
THREAD: weather (altitude is this shortage), engineering (a chimney
  draws by difference, not by pumping), survival (why sealed rooms
  and grain silos kill without warning).
ASKED-AS: breathing oxygen altitude mountain sickness suffocate carbon monoxide lungs gasping hyperventilate drowning

ESSENCE: two message systems with opposite economics. Wires reach
  one address in a blink and stop the instant you let go. Chemicals
  in the blood reach everyone holding a matching lock, take seconds
  to hours, and keep working until they are destroyed. The slow one
  is not a failed version of the fast one.
ROOT: engineering / a phone call and a public notice are different
  tools, and neither does the other's job.
THREAD: people together (a shout to one person against a notice to
  the town), computing (a message queue against a global setting),
  money (a rate cut reaches everyone and cannot be un-sent).
ASKED-AS: hormones adrenaline nerves reflex shaky anxiety thyroid puberty signals slow fast messages

ESSENCE: the brain is a fiftieth of you and eats a fifth of your
  fuel, day and night, asleep or awake — and hard thinking barely
  moves the bill. The cost is readiness: every cell holding its
  charged difference primed to fire. It stores nothing and will not
  burn fat, so it needs sugar delivered without a break.
ROOT: the living world / holding a difference costs continuously
  whether or not anything uses it.
THREAD: computing (an idle server draws most of its full power),
  money (fixed costs dwarfing the variable ones), survival (the
  last thing a starving body gives up).
ASKED-AS: brain energy calories thinking tired glucose sugar faint starving concentration hungry dizzy

ESSENCE: sleep is not the absence of work — it is work that cannot
  be done with the lights on. Waste is flushed from between brain
  cells, the day's changes are filed into a keepable form, and
  connections that all crept upward while you were awake are turned
  back down. The same wiring does the day job and the filing.
ROOT: engineering / you cannot re-shelve a library while readers
  are in it, so some maintenance requires closing the doors.
THREAD: computing (defragmenting a disk taken offline), cleaning
  (the shop shut for stocktake), teaching (a lesson slept on is a
  lesson kept).
ASKED-AS: sleep tired insomnia naps deprivation memory dreams groggy hours rest catchup

ESSENCE: pain is not delivered to the brain, it is issued by it — a
  judgment about how much protection this situation needs, built
  from the signal plus what you expect, attend to, and believe it
  means. Same wound, different verdict: the soldier who felt
  nothing; the nail through the boot that missed the foot entirely
  and hurt anyway.
ROOT: the body / a brain reports its conclusion, never its raw
  input, and this is true of every sense.
THREAD: the mind (expectation shapes every sense), medicine (why a
  dummy pill measurably helps here of all places), sports (the
  injury not felt until the whistle).
ASKED-AS: pain chronic back placebo phantom limb hurts scans nothing wrong real imaginary

ESSENCE: repair runs in a fixed order and each stage gates the
  next: stop the bleeding, clear the mess, fill with cheap
  material, then spend months quietly re-organising the fill along
  the lines of pull. That last stage listens to load — a scar
  arranges itself around the forces you send through it.
ROOT: the body / repair is a supply job before it is a building
  job; nothing is rebuilt where blood does not reach.
THREAD: building (the pour cures on its own clock), craft (glue
  clamped is glue that sets true), sports (rehab is loading on
  purpose, not resting).
ASKED-AS: healing time scar cartilage knee tendon ankle surgery weeks physiotherapy immobilize slow

ESSENCE: two clocks run at once. One is wear — repair is very good
  and not perfect, and imperfect repair compounds. The other is a
  built-in stop, cells counting their divisions and quitting.
  Behind both stands the reason nothing ever fixed it: the process
  that shapes bodies barely sees a fault arriving after your last
  child.
ROOT: the living world / a trait's fate is decided by offspring
  counted, so harm that lands after breeding is almost invisible.
THREAD: maintenance (a fleet ages by a thousand small deferred
  jobs), building (an old house is never one problem), money
  (compounding runs downhill just as well as up).
ASKED-AS: aging old wrinkles lifespan die cells immortal repair damage slow reverse

ESSENCE: cancer is not an invader — it is your own cells that
  stopped answering, and it takes several separate failures in one
  lineage, not one: divide unbidden, refuse the suicide order,
  ignore the crowd, build your own blood supply, leave home. Each
  is a step, which is why it is a disease of time and of tissues
  that divide.
ROOT: the living world / selection runs anywhere copies vary and
  some leave more copies — including inside you.
THREAD: strategy (an enemy that adapts to your one weapon), farming
  (spraying breeds the pest that survives spraying), money (fraud
  inside the firm beats fraud at the gate).
ASKED-AS: cancer tumor spread cells mutations chemotherapy remission relapse smoking risk age

ESSENCE: there are far more numbers to hold than levers to hold
  them with, so the levers are shared and every correction drags
  something else — cooling by sweat spends salt, cooling by
  breathing spends water, warming by shivering spends sugar. And
  the targets move: a fever is a set point raised on purpose, which
  is why you shiver while burning.
ROOT: engineering / more dials than knobs means every knob is a
  compromise across several dials.
THREAD: money (one interest rate for jobs, prices and currency at
  once), engineering (one throttle governing speed, heat and fuel).
ASKED-AS: fever chills shivering sweating thermostat body temperature salt hot flashes regulate cold clammy

ESSENCE: the same tissue is asked for opposite properties. Bone
  stiff enough not to bend and light enough to carry. Blood thin
  enough to flow and quick enough to clot. A gut wall thin to
  absorb and tough to defend. A guard fierce enough to kill germs
  and gentle enough to spare you. One number must serve both jobs.
ROOT: mathematics / a single setting cannot sit at two optima, so
  the chosen value is a compromise and never a peak.
THREAD: engineering (a race car is a terrible tow truck), money
  (return and safety on one dial), sports (the sprinter's build
  loses the marathon).
ASKED-AS: immune boost supplements healthy optimize tradeoff clotting strokes allergies strongest balance perfect

ESSENCE: whenever a body needs more contact than its outside can
  give, the answer is always the same — fold. Lungs fold to the
  area of a tennis court, the gut's lining folds and then its folds
  fold, a kidney packs a million filters, the brain crumples to
  fit. Volume grows faster than surface, so surface must be bought
  by wrinkling.
ROOT: mathematics / squares grow slower than cubes, so a bigger
  body automatically gets less outside per inside.
THREAD: building (a radiator is fins for this exact reason), cooking
  (fine dicing cooks fast), engineering (filters and heat
  exchangers are one trick repeated).
ASKED-AS: lungs surface area alveoli intestine villi folds kidney wrinkled brain exchange breathing

ESSENCE: inside a cell nothing is delivered — molecules find each
  other by blundering about at enormous speed, and at cell size
  that is quick enough to run a chemistry plant. It also means the
  inside is not a soup but a crowd, packed nearly solid, where
  every step is a collision.
ROOT: physics / random motion crosses a hair in an instant and a
  fingernail in a day, so the size of a cell is the size where
  blundering still works.
THREAD: cities (a village walks, a city needs buses), engineering
  (stirring beats waiting at every scale), computing (keeping data
  near the work beats moving it).
ASKED-AS: cell size small microscopic diffusion inside crowded soup molecules random bumping transport

ESSENCE: the body runs three fuels with different rules — sugar is
  fast and there is barely any, fat is slow and nearly limitless,
  protein is the furniture being burnt. Switching between them is
  not instant, and every distance runner knows the lag by name: the
  wall is a changeover, not an empty tank.
ROOT: chemistry / burning fast and storing densely are opposite
  properties, so no single substance can do both.
THREAD: engineering (a petrol engine and a coal boiler in one
  chassis), money (cash, savings, and selling the furniture, in
  that order).
ASKED-AS: carbs fat burning wall marathon ketones fasting muscle loss energy switch bonk

ESSENCE: digestion is deliberate destruction. Food is torn down to
  identical bricks before anything crosses in, so that whose body
  it came from is erased — which is why you can eat a cow without
  becoming cow-shaped. It is also why a protein that gets across
  whole is trouble: an allergy is a brick that arrived still
  wearing its old address.
ROOT: engineering / standard parts — universal bricks let anything
  be built from anything, but only once the old shape is gone.
THREAD: craft (scrap steel is melted, never glued), building
  (demolition before rebuild), language (a quotation has to be
  understood, not merely carried).
ASKED-AS: digestion stomach acid protein amino acids allergy peanut gluten eating meat broken

ESSENCE: the body cannot destroy anything — it can only convert a
  troublesome molecule into a duller one and then spend water
  flushing it away. That is the liver's whole trade, running flat
  out every hour of your life, and everything absorbed from the gut
  passes through it first: a checkpoint built out of plumbing.
ROOT: physics / matter is never used up, only rearranged — so
  clearing a poison means changing it and carrying it out, never
  deleting it.
THREAD: cleaning (dirt is moved, never destroyed), law (a
  checkpoint on the only road in), money (all traffic routed
  through one clearing house, deliberately).
ASKED-AS: liver detox cleanse toxins alcohol hangover pills paracetamol damage flush juice

ESSENCE: bone is not scaffolding poured once. It is eaten and
  rebuilt continuously, and laid down along the lines the load
  actually travels — so a bone is a written record of the forces
  you have been putting through it, and a bone under no load is
  quietly dismantled and spent elsewhere.
ROOT: the living world / anything expensive that stops earning is
  dropped, and the body applies that rule to its own frame.
THREAD: engineering (material placed where the stress runs), craft
  (a tool's wear shows how it was held), farming (soil built and
  lost on the same slow clock).
ASKED-AS: bones strong osteoporosis calcium weight bearing exercise astronauts bed rest fracture density

ESSENCE: a muscle can only shorten. It pulls, and it resists being
  pulled, and it can never push — so every joint that must move
  both ways needs at least two muscles arranged against each other,
  and every movement is a negotiation between opposing teams.
ROOT: the body / the machinery inside a muscle ratchets one way,
  hauling filaments past each other, with no reverse gear.
THREAD: engineering (cables pull, struts push, and no cable ever
  pushes), craft (a rope is useless in compression), sports (why
  the neglected half of a pair is where the injury turns up).
ASKED-AS: muscles pull push biceps triceps opposite pairs tendon cut stuck cramp stretch

ESSENCE: the round trip to the brain is too slow for some
  decisions, so the spinal cord makes them without asking — your
  hand is off the stove before you feel the burn. The body pushes a
  decision down to whoever is near enough to make it in time, and
  accepts that a local decision will be crude.
ROOT: physics / signals travel at the speed of their wiring, and
  distance costs time nobody can borrow back.
THREAD: engineering (a cut-out wired at the machine, not the control
  room), computing (decisions cached at the edge), power (why the
  officer on the spot must be allowed to act).
ASKED-AS: reflex hot stove knee jerk blinking spinal cord fast automatic flinch catch

ESSENCE: every sense fades whatever stays the same. You stop
  smelling your own house within minutes, stop feeling your
  clothes, stop hearing the fridge. Sensing costs, and reporting an
  unchanged world buys nothing — so the body spends its attention
  on differences and lets the background disappear.
ROOT: engineering / a channel with limited room reports changes,
  because the steady part can be assumed instead of sent.
THREAD: money (a cost that rises slowly is never questioned),
  people together (you cannot hear your own accent), deep column 55
  (the inner ear that reads only changes).
ASKED-AS: smell own house nose blind clothes fridge noise adapt used to background gradual

ESSENCE: the body keeps its own day. Shut a person in a cave with
  no daylight and the rhythm keeps running, near enough to
  twenty-four hours — light does not make it, light only resets it.
  Nearly every organ carries a copy, so almost everything you do
  has an hour at which it is done best.
ROOT: astronomy / the planet's turn is the one perfectly repeatable
  fact, worth building a clock around.
THREAD: farming (planting by the calendar the sky keeps), money
  (markets that keep their own hours regardless of yours),
  engineering (clocks that free-run and are trimmed, never driven).
ASKED-AS: jet lag body clock circadian night shift melatonin waking same time cave schedule

ESSENCE: give the body a steady signal and it removes its own
  receivers until the reading looks normal again. That is why the
  second week of anything works less well than the first, why
  stopping leaves you below where you started, and why a drug can
  end up only holding off the trouble that stopping it would cause.
ROOT: engineering / feedback — a system correcting toward a set
  point pushes back against a helpful shove exactly as hard as
  against a harmful one.
THREAD: money (a subsidy that becomes the price), teaching (praise
  that stops meaning anything), persuasion (a threat repeated is a
  threat retired).
ASKED-AS: tolerance caffeine addiction rebound withdrawal nose spray sleeping pills painkillers dose worse

ESSENCE: the kidney does something that sounds insane — it dumps
  nearly everything out of the blood, then buys back the
  ninety-nine hundredths it wanted. Keeping a short list of what to
  save is cheaper and far safer than keeping a complete list of
  what to remove, because nobody can list the poisons they have not
  met yet.
ROOT: engineering / a permit list handles the unknown; a ban list
  only ever handles what someone already thought of.
THREAD: computing (allow-lists beat block-lists for exactly this
  reason), law (rules that grant beat rules that forbid), cleaning
  (empty the drawer and put back only what you use).
ASKED-AS: kidneys filter urine dialysis blood cleaning reabsorb waste sugar protein failure

ESSENCE: clotting is a controlled explosion. One small breach
  starts a chain in which each step wakes many of the next, so a
  faint signal becomes a plug within seconds — and anything that
  amplifies like that must carry brakes stronger than its trigger,
  or the first scratch would set the whole body solid.
ROOT: engineering / amplification and safety are one design
  problem; whoever builds the multiplier must build the limiter.
THREAD: engineering (a fuse sized to the current it must allow),
  money (a bank run is this cascade with the brake missing), law
  (emergency powers need a stronger off-switch than on).
ASKED-AS: blood clot bleeding hemophilia stroke bruise scab deep vein flying platelets

ESSENCE: you were never sterile and cannot be made so. Your skin
  and gut carry about as many bacterial cells as you have of your
  own, and they are not passengers — they make vitamins, school the
  immune system, and above all hold the ground so that worse things
  find nowhere to land.
ROOT: the living world / no surface stays empty, so the question is
  never whether it is colonised but by whom.
THREAD: farming (bare soil is the invitation, not the cure), people
  together (an empty market is filled by whoever turns up),
  cleaning (a surface scoured too hard is left worse defended).
ASKED-AS: gut bacteria microbiome probiotics antibiotics diarrhea yogurt hygiene germs skin overgrowth

ESSENCE: the body has one all-purpose emergency setting, and it
  buys a few minutes of performance by borrowing from every
  long-term account at once — repair, digestion, growth, defense
  and patience all suspended to fund the sprint. Brilliant for a
  chase. Ruinous held on for a year.
ROOT: the living world / a response is tuned to the length of the
  danger that shaped it, and nothing ever tuned this one for a
  danger that does not end.
THREAD: money (an overdraft funding today by taxing every
  tomorrow), engineering (an engine's overboost, rated in seconds),
  war (a unit held at readiness degrades where it stands).
ASKED-AS: stress cortisol chronic anxiety fight flight stomach ulcers sleep burnout adrenaline

ESSENCE: a body builds only what is demanded of it, and the demand
  signal is controlled damage. Training is a cycle — load hard
  enough to break something small, then rest while it is rebuilt
  slightly stronger than before. Both halves are the work; only one
  of them looks like it.
ROOT: the living world / an unused ability is dropped, so the only
  way to keep or grow one is to keep asking for it.
THREAD: teaching (practice at the edge of failure, then sleep),
  craft (a blade kept by being worn and dressed), maintenance (the
  machine run and serviced outlasts the one parked).
ASKED-AS: exercise muscle growth sore rest days overtraining injury gains stronger training recovery

ESSENCE: the gas that lets you burn fuel fast is corrosive, and it
  attacks you with the same chemistry it uses on your food. Every
  air-breathing creature runs a permanent repair budget against its
  own power supply — the price of a fast, warm, large body is being
  slowly rusted from the inside by the thing that runs it.
ROOT: chemistry / a reaction eager enough to release a lot of
  energy is eager enough to attack whatever else is nearby.
THREAD: cooking (fat going rancid is the same attack), materials
  (rust and rot are one process), engineering (a hotter engine
