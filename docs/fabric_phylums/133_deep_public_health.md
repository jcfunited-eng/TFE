# 133 DEEP PUBLIC HEALTH — keeping populations well
Health practice holds what one trained pair of hands does for one
patient. This file holds the other trade: the one whose patient is
a whole population, whose instruments are water, law, tax, roads
and trust, and whose successes are invisible by construction.
Where the evidence is genuinely unsettled the entry says UNSURE.

ESSENCE: a doctor asks what is wrong with this person. A population
  worker asks what is different about this crowd, and the two
  questions have different answers about the same disease. Why one
  man has lung cancer and why this country has a hundred thousand
  cases are separate problems with separate solutions.
ROOT: chance + risk / a rate is a property of a group and has no
  meaning for an individual; evidence + measurement (the unit of
  analysis decides what can be seen at all).
CANNOT: no explaining a population's rate from its individual cases
  — everyone in a high-smoking country has a personal reason for
  smoking, and none of those reasons explains the country. And no
  treating a population one patient at a time: the arithmetic does
  not close, however many clinics are built.
THREAD: evidence + measurement (a question that determines its own
  answer), money (a firm's accounts against an economy's), history
  (why a change in a whole society is never the sum of decisions).
ASKED-AS: why me population risk group individual cause disease rates country different question doctors

ESSENCE: most cases of most common diseases come from the enormous
  middle of the population, not from its dangerous edge. A small
  extra risk carried by millions produces more illness than a large
  risk carried by thousands — so the measure that helps a whole
  country barely helps any single person in it, and feels pointless
  to each of them.
ROOT: mathematics / a small number multiplied by a very large one
  beats a large number multiplied by a small one; this file (rates
  belong to crowds and are invisible from inside).
CANNOT: no population-scale prevention that any individual can feel
  working — which is why such programmes are chronically unpopular
  and chronically effective. And no fixing a common disease by
  finding its worst cases: treat every high-risk person perfectly
  and most of the cases still occur.
THREAD: chance + risk (rare times many equals often), money (a
  penny on every transaction against a fortune from a few), power
  (a policy whose beneficiaries never know who they were).
ASKED-AS: everyone told to cut down why me small risk majority cases prevention pointless population

ESSENCE: there are two ways to attack a disease and they are not
  alternatives. Find the highest-risk people and treat them
  intensively, or shift the whole population slightly. The first is
  efficient per person and misses most cases; the second catches
  most cases and treats mainly people who were never going to be
  ill.
ROOT: this file / the cases come from the middle, while the risk is
  concentrated at the edge — so each strategy hits what the other
  misses; strategy + conflict (two campaigns aimed at different
  parts of one problem).
CANNOT: no strategy that is both efficient and sufficient — the
  targeted one cannot reach the bulk of cases and the population
  one cannot avoid treating the well. And no honest comparison
  without saying which is being counted: cases prevented, or harm
  per person exposed.
THREAD: money (a few large accounts against a mass market), law (a
  rule aimed at offenders and one aimed at everybody), strategy +
  conflict.
ASKED-AS: statins everyone high risk patients screening targeted approach whole population strategy prevention which

ESSENCE: a risk factor is something that travels with a disease. A
  cause is something that, when moved, moves the disease. Most of
  what gets called a cause has only ever been shown to travel — and
  the way to find out is to move it and watch, which is expensive
  and often impossible.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / correlation identifies a companion,
  and only intervention identifies a cause; this file (populations
  cannot be randomised the way patients can).
CANNOT: no promoting a factor to a cause by finding it again in
  another dataset — the same confounding produces the same
  association everywhere it is looked for. And no waiting for
  certainty either: some causes will never be trialled, so the
  judgment has to be made on converging evidence of several
  different kinds.
THREAD: nutrition (a nutrient that marked a food and did nothing on
  its own), evidence + measurement (the untouched twin), money (a
  leading indicator mistaken for a lever).
ASKED-AS: risk factor cause linked to associated study proves causes correlation smoking sugar screens

ESSENCE: what is worth fixing in a population depends on how strong
  a factor is and on how many people carry it, multiplied
  together. A cause that triples the risk in one percent of people
  matters less than one that raises it a fifth in everybody. The
  question is never how dangerous, it is how much of this disease
  would disappear.
ROOT: mathematics / an effect's share of a total is its size times
  its frequency, so either can be the decisive term; this file (the
  population is the patient, so the total is what counts).
CANNOT: no ranking hazards by how frightening they are — the
  ranking by prevented cases looks entirely different, and it is
  the one that decides where money goes. And no reading it from a
  relative figure alone: the exposure's spread through the
  population is half the calculation and is usually left out of the
  story.
THREAD: chance + risk (a ratio that discards the size of what it
  divides), money (revenue as price times volume), power (a hazard
  that is common being politically harder than one that is
  dreadful).
ASKED-AS: attributable risk how many cases prevented population exposure common rare hazard priority ranking

ESSENCE: the honest unit for a preventive measure is how many
  people must take it for one to benefit. For a strong treatment in
  sick people it can be single figures. For a preventive drug in
  healthy ones it can be hundreds — hundreds of people taking a
  tablet daily for years so that one avoids an event.
ROOT: chance + risk / a benefit spread thinly across many is the
  same total as a benefit concentrated in few, and the two feel
  nothing alike; this file (a population intervention is bought by
  everybody and used by almost nobody).
CANNOT: no informed consent without this number — a person told a
  drug cuts their risk by a third and not told the risk was small
  has not been informed. And no free treatment of the many: the
  side effects land on all the hundreds, so the harms scale with
  the treated and the benefits with the few.
THREAD: chance + risk (relative against absolute), money (an
  insurance premium paid by everyone for the few who claim), right
  + wrong (a cost imposed on many for a good landing on one).
ASKED-AS: number needed to treat statins blood pressure pills worth taking benefit chance side effects

ESSENCE: the people who do the healthy thing differ from everyone
  else in a hundred other ways — richer, better educated, more
  likely to attend appointments, less likely to smoke. Any measured
  benefit of the healthy thing carries all of that inside it, and no
  statistical adjustment removes what was never measured.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / a comparison group assembled by
  choice carries the reasons for the choice; this file (the whole
  observational literature rests on people who selected
  themselves).
CANNOT: no adjusting away an unmeasured difference, so no
  observational estimate is safe from it. And no reassurance from a
  large sample: the bias does not shrink with numbers, it just gets
  measured more precisely, which is why huge studies confidently
  disagree with the trials that followed.
THREAD: nutrition (why the supplement trials keep reversing the
  observational findings), evidence + measurement (randomisation as
  the only device that balances what nobody thought of), chance +
  risk.
ASKED-AS: healthy user bias adjusted confounding observational studies vitamins exercise wrong reversed trials confounders

ESSENCE: when a trial is impossible, the next best thing is a
  change that arrived like a coin toss — a law passed in one region
  and not the next, a lottery for a programme, an inherited
  difference that shuffles at conception. Most of what is confidently
  known about causes in populations was learnt this way.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / the value of randomising is that it
  balances the unmeasured, and history sometimes randomises by
  accident; this file (the intervention that cannot be assigned can
  sometimes be found already assigned).
CANNOT: no natural experiment without a plausible claim that the
  assignment was unrelated to the outcome — and that claim is where
  every one of them lives or dies. And no substitute for looking:
  they cannot be commissioned, only found, which is why this work is
  half detective and half statistician.
THREAD: history (a border drawn by accident becoming a controlled
  comparison), evidence + measurement (the fair start as the
  instrument's zero), nutrition (inherited differences in alcohol
  handling settling a question argument could not).
ASKED-AS: natural experiment policy change one state law comparison evidence causal proof lottery borders

ESSENCE: a pattern true of countries need not be true of people.
  Nations that eat more fat have more heart disease, and within any
  nation the individuals who eat most fat may not. The two are
  different questions with different answers, and mixing them has
  produced some of the field's largest and longest mistakes.
ROOT: mathematics / a relationship between group averages is not
  the relationship inside the groups, and can even run the opposite
  way; this file (the unit of analysis decides the finding).
CANNOT: no inferring an individual's risk from a country
  comparison, which closes a whole genre of striking maps and
  charts. And no dismissing them either: cross-country patterns are
  often the first sign of something real, and are a hypothesis
  rather than a result.
THREAD: evidence + measurement (an average concealing its
  distribution), chance + risk (a rate belonging to a group),
  nutrition (a famous diet hypothesis built on exactly this shape
  of evidence).
ASKED-AS: countries compare rates map fat heart disease japan mediterranean individual people applies fallacy

ESSENCE: the considerations for judging whether an association is a
  cause — strength, consistency, dose-response, timing, plausible
  mechanism — are a way of organising an argument, not a test that
  can be passed. Their author said so explicitly and everyone has
  been treating them as a checklist ever since.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / judgment cannot be replaced by a
  procedure, and a procedure adopted in its place is worse than the
  judgment it replaced; this file (causes here are argued, not
  demonstrated).
CANNOT: no criterion that is necessary — real causes exist with no
  dose-response, with weak effects, and with no known mechanism.
  And no criterion that is sufficient: every one of them can be
  produced by a confounder, which is why the case must be built from
  several independent kinds of evidence pointing the same way.
THREAD: law (a body of evidence weighed rather than counted),
  evidence + measurement (a rule of thumb hardening into a rule),
  right + wrong (a judgment dressed as a calculation).
ASKED-AS: proof causation criteria checklist bradford hill smoking evidence strong mechanism dose response judgment

ESSENCE: survival after diagnosis is the wrong measure, and it is
  the one everybody quotes. Diagnose people earlier and they
  survive longer from diagnosis while dying on exactly the same
  day. The measure that cannot be fooled this way is deaths in the
  whole population, which is why it is the one that matters and the
  one nobody finds persuasive.
ROOT: mathematics / a duration measured from a moving start point
  moves when the start point does; evidence + measurement (a
  measurement whose zero is set by the intervention being tested).
CANNOT: no improvement in survival statistics that proves anyone
  lived longer — the number rises automatically the moment
  diagnosis moves earlier, with no treatment involved at all. And no
  comparing two countries' survival figures at all if they diagnose
  at different stages, which they always do.
THREAD: evidence + measurement (a number that measures the
  measuring), health practice (finding a disease sooner as a good
  in itself), money (a return quoted from a chosen start date).
ASKED-AS: survival rates five year cancer better earlier diagnosis statistics comparing countries mortality deaths

ESSENCE: screening catches slow disease preferentially, because
  slow disease sits around waiting to be found while fast disease
  appears and kills between appointments. So the cases a programme
  finds are, by construction, gentler than the cases it misses —
  and the programme is then credited with their gentleness.
ROOT: chance + risk / a sampling method that selects on duration
  over-represents the long-lasting; this file (a measure that
  creates the population it is measured on).
CANNOT: no screen-detected group that resembles the unscreened one
  — the comparison is rigged before anyone treats anybody. And no
  correcting it by analysis: the only honest test of a screening
  programme is whether deaths in the whole invited population fell,
  measured against a population that was not invited.
THREAD: health practice (a harvest of findings that would never
  have progressed), chance + risk (a sample selected by how long
  something lasts), evidence + measurement.
ASKED-AS: screening finds slow cancers aggressive missed between tests interval good outcomes bias comparison

ESSENCE: before a programme is worth running, several things must
  all be true: the disease must be common enough and serious
  enough, there must be an early stage, treating that stage must
  work better than treating the late one, the test must be tolerable
  and reasonably accurate, and the system must be able to handle
  everyone it flags. Fail any one and the programme does harm.
ROOT: engineering / a chain delivers nothing if any link is
  missing, and a screening programme is a long chain ending in
  treatment; health practice (a positive result is a beginning, and
  everything after it is where the harm lives).
CANNOT: no benefit from finding a disease with no better early
  treatment — the person simply spends more years knowing. And no
  programme without the follow-up capacity: a test rolled out
  without the clinics to investigate its positives converts a
  health measure into a queue and a fright.
THREAD: engineering (a process chain failing at its weakest stage),
  health practice (each false alarm buying its own procedure),
  money (a programme costed at the test and not at the pathway).
ASKED-AS: screening programme worth it criteria test everyone early detection treatment available capacity follow up

ESSENCE: a screening test does not diagnose anybody. It sorts a
  population into a small group worth investigating and a large
  group that can be left alone, and everything that matters happens
  in the investigating. The public hears a verdict; the programme
  issued a sorting.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / a cheap test tuned to miss little
  must therefore flag many, so its output is a shortlist rather
  than an answer; health practice (a result updates a chance, it
  does not replace it).
CANNOT: no test that both misses little and flags little, so no
  screening programme without a crowd of people who are called
  back and are fine. And no way to spare them the fright: the
  weeks between the letter and the all-clear are a real harm of the
  programme and are almost never counted in its ledger.
THREAD: chance + risk (a threshold chosen between two ways of being
  wrong), health practice (reading a positive against the
  before-chance), evidence + measurement.
ASKED-AS: recalled after mammogram scan further tests worried letter false alarm result screening positive fine

ESSENCE: the number that decides an outbreak's shape is how many
  people one case infects on average. Above one it grows, below one
  it dies out, and every control measure is an attempt to push it
  under. It is not a property of the germ alone — it depends on how
  people live, travel, crowd and behave, so it differs between
  cities and changes weekly.
ROOT: mathematics / a quantity multiplied by itself repeatedly
  either runs away or vanishes, and one is the dividing line; this
  file (a rate is a property of a population and its habits).
CANNOT: no single number for a disease, so no comparing an
  epidemic in two places by quoting it. And no comfort from a value
  just under one: it is an average over a population that has
  already changed its behaviour, so it climbs back the moment the
  behaviour relaxes.
THREAD: chance + risk (multiplication below one and above it),
  computing (a network's spread threshold, identical arithmetic),
  money (compounding that runs away or dies).
ASKED-AS: r number reproduction rate outbreak spreading growing below one lockdown measures average changes

ESSENCE: the coverage a population needs to stop a disease is set
  by how contagious it is, so it differs enormously by disease —
  measles needs almost everybody and several others need far less.
  And it is not a national average: a country at the threshold
  overall still burns wherever the unvaccinated cluster together.
ROOT: this file / the spread threshold depends on how many people
  each case reaches, which is a local quantity; health practice
  (protection that is shared cannot be opted out of privately).
CANNOT: no reading protection off a national figure — outbreaks
  occur in schools and neighbourhoods, so the relevant coverage is
  the local one and the average conceals it. And no single target
  for all vaccines: quoting one threshold across diseases is a
  category error that has caused real policy mistakes.
THREAD: chance + risk (an average hiding a distribution),
  computing (a network whose connections are clustered rather than
  even), people together (beliefs travelling in communities).
ASKED-AS: herd immunity percentage measles threshold coverage national average clusters school outbreak vaccinated enough

ESSENCE: most people infect nobody and a few infect very many. That
  lopsidedness is the rule rather than the exception, and it
  changes everything: an outbreak can die out repeatedly by chance
  before one gathering sets it running, and control that finds the
  events rather than the average does far more per unit of effort.
ROOT: chance + risk / an average says nothing about a spread, and
  where the spread is extreme the average describes almost nobody;
  this file (the same total transmission arranged two ways behaves
  differently).
CANNOT: no predicting an outbreak's course from its average alone —
  with heavy skew, the early history is dominated by luck. And no
  effective control that treats every case as equivalent: the
  measure that finds where the big events happen outperforms
  uniform restriction, which is the argument for tracing backwards
  to the source rather than only forwards.
THREAD: chance + risk (rare events dominating a total), money (a
  portfolio whose return comes from a handful of positions), people
  together (the gathering as the unit of transmission).
ASKED-AS: superspreader event most people infect nobody average variation gatherings indoor outbreak clusters control

ESSENCE: the gap between catching something and showing it decides
  what any control measure can do. A long gap gives tracers time to
  reach contacts before they infect anyone; a short one means the
  chain has already moved on. Quarantine lengths, tracing windows
  and travel rules are all this one number wearing different
  clothes.
ROOT: this file / control is a race between the spread and the
  response, and the incubation period is the head start the
  response is given; strategy + conflict (an operation's feasibility
  set by the enemy's cycle time).
CANNOT: no tracing faster than the disease's own clock — for
  something that transmits within a day or two of exposure, contact
  tracing cannot get ahead however well it is funded. And no
  quarantine period chosen for convenience: shorter than the tail
  of the distribution and it releases infectious people, longer and
  compliance collapses.
THREAD: strategy + conflict (getting inside an opponent's decision
  cycle), computing (patching before the worm arrives), health
  practice (a treatment window measured in hours).
ASKED-AS: incubation period quarantine days exposed symptoms tracing window testing after contact isolate long

ESSENCE: control built on symptoms fails against a disease that
  spreads before them or without them. Fever checks at borders,
  staying home when ill, isolating the visibly sick — all of it
  assumes the infectious and the unwell are the same people, and
  for several important diseases they are not.
ROOT: this file / a measure can only act on what it can detect, so
  an undetectable fraction sets a ceiling on what the measure can
  achieve; evidence + measurement (a test that misses a category
  entirely).
CANNOT: no symptom-based control of a disease with substantial
  silent transmission — no amount of diligence closes the gap,
  because the people transmitting are not identifiable as sick. And
  no knowing the silent share early: it is one of the hardest
  quantities to measure and one of the first that policy needs.
THREAD: health practice (a reserve masking a loss until it is
  gone), computing (an infection that spreads before any symptom
  appears in the logs), evidence + measurement.
ASKED-AS: asymptomatic spreading before symptoms fever checks airport screening silent carriers testing feeling fine

ESSENCE: contact tracing works when four things hold at once: cases
  are found fast, contacts are reached faster, people can afford to
  stay home, and the number of cases is small enough for the staff
  available. Take any one away and it becomes an expensive way to
  produce statistics.
ROOT: engineering / a system with several serial requirements
  succeeds only where all of them are met; this file (a race
  against the disease's own clock).
CANNOT: no tracing at scale — above a case threshold the workforce
  is overwhelmed and the whole activity stops contributing, which
  is why it belongs at the start and end of an epidemic and not the
  middle. And no compliance without support: asking a person to
  lose a fortnight's wages produces silence rather than
  cooperation, so sick pay is part of the epidemiology.
THREAD: money (an intervention whose binding constraint is a
  household's cash flow), engineering (a chain of serial
  dependencies), power (a measure that requires the public to
  cooperate and must therefore be worth cooperating with).
ASKED-AS: contact tracing app calls isolate work pay staff overwhelmed cases too many effective

ESSENCE: quarantine is for the exposed and isolation is for the
  infected, and the difference is the whole logic. Quarantine
  restricts people who have done nothing and may well be perfectly
  healthy, which is why it is the harder thing to justify and the
  more powerful thing to do — it acts before the infectiousness
  starts.
ROOT: this file / the value of a measure lies in acting before
  transmission, and before is precisely when you cannot know who is
  infected; right + wrong (a burden imposed on the innocent for a
  good that lands elsewhere).
CANNOT: no quarantine that does not restrict people who turn out
  never to have been infectious — that is not a failure of the
  policy, it is the policy. And no isolation that helps if it
  begins when symptoms do: for many diseases the infectious period
  starts earlier, so isolation alone always leaks.
THREAD: right + wrong (containment without blame in it), law (a
  restriction on liberty requiring a standard of evidence),
  history (the forty days at Venice that named the practice).
ASKED-AS: quarantine isolation difference exposed positive stay home days rules healthy contacts restrictions why

ESSENCE: how deadly a disease is depends entirely on the
  denominator. Deaths divided by known cases is one number; deaths
  divided by everyone actually infected is another and always
  smaller, because the mild and the silent were never counted. Most
  public arguments about lethality are arguments about which
  denominator.
ROOT: mathematics / a ratio is decided as much by what goes
  underneath as by what goes on top; evidence + measurement (the
  measured population is not the population of interest).
CANNOT: no honest lethality figure early in an outbreak — the
  denominator is unknown while testing is scarce, and deaths lag
  cases by weeks, so both parts of the fraction are wrong at once
  and in opposite directions. And no single figure for a disease:
  it varies with age, with treatment and with whether the hospitals
  are full.
THREAD: evidence + measurement (a sample that selects the severe),
  chance + risk (a rate quoted without its base), money (a return
  computed on the wrong capital).
ASKED-AS: death rate how deadly case fatality infection percentage dying testing denominator early estimates changed

ESSENCE: the numerator is usually easy and the denominator is
  usually the hard part. Counting the cases, the deaths, the events
  is laborious and doable; knowing how many people were exposed, in
  what age structure, over what period, is the work that decides
  whether the number means anything at all.
ROOT: mathematics / a count is not a rate until it is divided by
  the population it came from; this file (the whole discipline is
  the business of getting denominators right).
CANNOT: no rate without a population, so no comparing two places'
  counts — a city with twice the cases and three times the people
  has a lower rate. And no comparison across ages without
  standardising: an older population has more of nearly every
  disease, and half of all naive comparisons are measuring age.
THREAD: evidence + measurement (per-unit comparison as the whole
  discipline), money (a total that means nothing until divided by
  something), chance + risk.
ASKED-AS: cases per hundred thousand rate population compare cities age standardised counts more people bigger

ESSENCE: a case definition is a decision, and it moves the numbers.
  Widen it and the epidemic appears to grow; narrow it and it
  appears to shrink; change it mid-outbreak and the curve has a
  step in it that no germ caused. Every long time series of disease
  has these steps hidden in it.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / a measurement inherits its
  definition, so changing the definition changes the measurement
  and not the world; law (the words of a rule deciding what falls
  inside it).
CANNOT: no comparing counts across a definition change, so no
  reading a trend without knowing when the rules moved. And no
  neutral definition: every one is a trade between catching cases
  and including non-cases, made by people with a purpose, and it
  is a policy instrument whether or not anyone admits it.
THREAD: law (a category defined into or out of existence), evidence
  + measurement (a scale re-zeroed mid-experiment), history (a
  disease that appears to arrive when a name is invented for it).
ASKED-AS: case definition changed counting rules diagnosed criteria widened numbers jumped comparing years surveillance

ESSENCE: the count that survives every argument about definitions,
  testing and attribution is how many more people died than
  usually die. It needs no diagnosis, no test and no committee. It
  captures the deaths a crisis caused indirectly, and it is
  frequently much larger than the official toll.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / a comparison against a well-known
  baseline avoids every question about what was measured; this file
  (the population is the unit, and death is the one event that
  cannot be redefined).
CANNOT: no arguing about excess deaths on definitional grounds,
  which is precisely why it is the number that settles disputes.
  And no attribution from it either: it says how many more died,
  never why, so the missed cancer treatments and the delayed heart
  attacks are inside it along with the epidemic.
THREAD: evidence + measurement (a robust measure preferred to a
  precise one), money (cash movements against reported profit),
  history (populations counted through catastrophes by their
  gravestones).
ASKED-AS: excess deaths official toll counted with or from died anyway baseline mortality statistics arguments

ESSENCE: surveillance sees what it looks at. A rise in reported
  cases can be a rise in disease, in testing, in awareness, or in
  reporting rules — and the four look identical in the data.
  Whatever is not routinely counted is invisible to policy, which
  is why the diseases of poor and stigmatised people are
  systematically under-recorded.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / a measurement system produces the
  world it can see, and its gaps are structural rather than random;
  power (what is counted is what gets funded).
CANNOT: no distinguishing more disease from more looking without an
  independent measure — which is why deaths, hospital admissions
  and random population samples are used to anchor case counts. And
  no unbiased blind spot: the missing data is missing for reasons,
  and the reasons correlate with who suffers most.
THREAD: power (a budget following a statistic), evidence +
  measurement (a rise in cases as a rise in looking), people
  together (whose problems are recorded at all).
ASKED-AS: cases rising more testing surveillance data reporting numbers increase real awareness statistics blind

ESSENCE: acting early against an epidemic always looks like an
  overreaction, because if it works nothing happens. The measures
  that would have been proportionate are only visible in the
  disaster they prevented, so the successful public health official
  is indistinguishable from the one who panicked over nothing.
ROOT: health practice / prevention cannot point at who it saved;
  this file (a measure that changes the future removes the evidence
  for itself).
CANNOT: no proving an averted catastrophe, so no defence against
  the accusation of overreaction — the counterfactual is not
  available and never will be. And no waiting for the evidence
  either: with something growing by multiplication, the delay while
  certainty accumulates is the entire difference between a small
  outbreak and a large one.
THREAD: health practice (the invisible saved case), power (a
  decision judged by an outcome that the decision changed), money
  (insurance that looks wasteful in every year it is not needed).
ASKED-AS: overreaction lockdown too early panic unnecessary nothing happened worked prevented judged hindsight preparedness

ESSENCE: in an outbreak, speed usually beats precision. A measure
  taken a week early with imperfect information does more than a
  perfect one taken a week late, because the thing being fought
  multiplies while the analysis is done. The correct posture is to
  act on the plausible bad case and revise loudly.
ROOT: mathematics / a delay against a doubling quantity costs more
  the longer it runs, so the value of information falls faster than
  the cost of acquiring it; strategy + conflict (fight the nearest
  deadline).
CANNOT: no waiting for good data without paying for the wait in
  cases — the choice is never between acting and knowing, it is
  between acting now on less and acting later on more. And no
  acting early without being wrong sometimes: a policy of early
  action guarantees some unnecessary actions, and that cost has to
  be accepted openly rather than denied.
THREAD: strategy + conflict (a decision cycle that has to close
  faster than the opponent's), health practice (a clock treatment
  where waiting costs tissue), money (a position cut before the
  reason is fully understood).
ASKED-AS: act early wait for evidence precautionary uncertain data decision outbreak fast response cautious

ESSENCE: as countries get richer their diseases change hands. The
  infections that killed children retreat, life expectancy climbs,
  and the ground is taken by heart disease, cancer, diabetes and
  dementia — conditions of long life and long exposure. The
  institutions built for the first set are usually still there,
  aimed at the wrong target.
ROOT: history / a system built for one problem persists after the
  problem does; this file (a population's disease pattern is set by
  its age structure and its exposures).
CANNOT: no keeping the old disease pattern once child mortality
  falls, so no country escapes this transition. And no treating the
  new set with the old tools: the chronic diseases have no single
  moment of infection, no cure, and no end — they are managed for
  decades or prevented over decades, and both are unfamiliar work
  for a system built around cases.
THREAD: history (institutions outliving their purpose), health
  practice (prevention against cure at national scale), money (a
  cost structure that changes shape as a business matures).
ASKED-AS: diseases changed heart cancer diabetes infections used to kill children hospitals system transition

ESSENCE: the famous figure of a life expectancy in the thirties
  never meant most people died in their thirties. It was an average
  dragged down by enormous numbers of dead infants, and the adults
  who survived childhood often reached old age. Almost all of the
  historical rise came from stopping children dying.
ROOT: mathematics / an average over a distribution with a huge lump
  at zero says little about the rest of it; evidence + measurement
  (a summary statistic taken for a description).
