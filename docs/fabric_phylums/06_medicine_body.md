# 06 MEDICINE AND THE BODY — the chemistry plant that repairs itself
Cooking's chemistry running itself for eighty years, and its repair
trade. Half its knowledge is CANNOTs.

ESSENCE: a body is chemistry inside walls — trillions of sealed bags
  of salt water, each running the same reactions, kept apart so the
  reactions stay legible.
ROOT: cooking / oil and water refuse each other — the walls are thin
  oil films; the refusal is hired as the wall.
THREAD: building (a house is rooms, never one hall), money (accounts
  are walls around value — blur them and bookkeeping dies), farming
  (cell, field, fence: one idea at three sizes).
ASKED-AS: skin membrane organ tissue microscope blister bubble compartment barrier germ alive squishy

ESSENCE: blood is a delivery network — one pump, branching pipes,
  every living cell within a hair's width of the flow; the same
  loop hauls fuel in and garbage out.
ROOT: physics premise — molecules drift fast across a hair,
  hopeless across a hand; past that, life must pipe.
THREAD: farming (irrigation — the field dies from the far end
  first), engineering (a city is a body with the skin off),
  weather (currents are the planet hauling heat).
ASKED-AS: heart veins arteries circulation stroke clot attack oxygen numb tingling fingers toes

ESSENCE: the dose makes the poison — every substance has an amount
  that helps, one that does nothing, and one that kills; medicine
  and poison are one word at different sizes (even water kills by
  the gallon).
ROOT: chemistry / reactions run by amount — the body's machinery
  saturates, then drowns.
THREAD: cooking (salt: a pinch seasons, a cup ruins), farming
  (fertilizer feeds by the spoon, burns by the sack), money
  (leverage — the same instrument, ruin at size).
ASKED-AS: vitamins overdose pills toxic chemicals safe natural aspirin caffeine alcohol supplements sunshine

ESSENCE: the body's defense force runs on one question — me, or
  not-me? — checking every surface against a self-list drawn up
  early in life, destroying what fails.
ROOT: chemistry / molecules recognize by shape — shaped keys meet
  shaped locks; the list is a ring of keys.
THREAD: law (borders and passports — the same question at nation
  size), computing (every login is me-or-not-me), war (uniforms
  exist so the question can be asked at a distance).
ASKED-AS: allergy transplant rejection vaccine autoimmune arthritis antibodies fever swelling pollen peanuts lupus

ESSENCE: infection is fermentation with a hostile tenant — a tiny
  living thing finds the body's warmth and sugar, eats, doubles
  and doubles, and its wastes are the sickness.
ROOT: cooking / fermentation is hired labor — same workers,
  unhired; farming / living things eat and excrete by their kinds.
THREAD: farming (weeds are field infections — self-copying rivals
  for the same sun), computing (the virus is named honestly),
  money (fraud spreads on trust the same doubling way, UNSURE).
ASKED-AS: cold flu germs bacteria antibiotics contagious spreading fever sore epidemic catching quarantine

ESSENCE: the body heals fast and crooked — wounds close with cheap
  fibrous fill, and the patch (scar) does none of the old part's
  work; deep repair trades function for speed.
ROOT: physics / disorder wins — true rebuild is uphill; in a live
  body closed-now beats perfect-later, because leaks kill.
THREAD: building (the patched wall is never the poured wall), law
  (a broken agreement re-signed holds, but differently — the seam
  is the memory), materials (a mended pot is a mended pot).
ASKED-AS: stitches cut burn mark fading surgery stretch tissue knee blemish injury smooth

ESSENCE: the body is a thousand thermostats — warmth (37°C), salt,
  sugar, acid, each held at a set point by sensors pushing back
  against every drift; being alive is the holding.
ROOT: engineering / feedback — measure, compare, correct; the
  shiver and the furnace are one design.
THREAD: cooking (the boil stuck at 100° is a thermostat by physics
  alone), flight (autopilot holding altitude), money (central
  banks leaning on every drift, UNSURE how deep the likeness).
ASKED-AS: fever chills sweating thirst dizzy fainting dehydration goosebumps hunger cold hands clammy

ESSENCE: pain is the alarm wire, not the damage — a report that
  the harm line is crossed, sized to seize attention, not to
  inform politely.
ROOT: the body / nerves carry news as small electricity; premise —
  a machine that cannot feel damage destroys itself in use.
THREAD: engineering (tape over the oil light; the engine still
  seizes), money (hidden losses are the taped light), sports
  (playing through pain spends the alarm as fuel).
ASKED-AS: headache ibuprofen numbing aching sore chronic backache toothache injury nerve pinched stubbed

ESSENCE: every remedy ships with a do-not-give list — the pill
  that saves one body kills another (a blood thinner clears a
  clot, empties a bleeder); half of medicine's real knowledge is
  when NOT to act.
ROOT: the fabric itself — the white: what a treatment forbids is
  knowledge equal to what it allows. Beneath: a drug touches every
  lock it fits, not just the aimed one.
THREAD: cooking (the peanut that feeds the table and kills one
  guest), engineering (every tool's plate lists the load it must
  never carry), law (every right is fenced by where it stops).
ASKED-AS: prescription warning label interactions allergic reaction grapefruit pharmacist dosage mixing pregnant elderly

ESSENCE: the body was never designed — it is renovations stacked
  on a fish, and the seams show: the food pipe crosses the air
  pipe (choking), the eye is wired backward and keeps a blind
  spot, the tailbone remembers a tail.
ROOT: farming / selection shapes lineages — selection edits what
  exists, one living step at a time; it cannot go back to the
  drawing board.
THREAD: building (the old house rewired three times, never
  gutted), computing (ancient code load-bearing in every big
  system), language (silent letters are the tailbone of words).
ASKED-AS: hiccups wisdom teeth appendix back pain knees slipped disc snoring evolution leftovers goosebumps
