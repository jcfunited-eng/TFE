# 111 DEEP PHARMACOLOGY — how substances change a body
A drug is a molecule meeting machinery that was already there. This
file holds what follows from that: how much, how often, by which door,
what it costs, and why the same tablet saves one person and kills the
next.

ESSENCE: a drug's effect against its dose is not a straight line.
  Nothing happens at all below a threshold, then a narrow band where
  each small increase does a great deal, then a ceiling where the
  receptors are full and more of the drug adds only harm.
ROOT: chemistry / an effect built on molecules meeting fixed targets
  must run out when the targets are occupied.
CANNOT: no doubling an effect by doubling a dose that is already at
  the ceiling — past the flat part every extra milligram is pure side
  effect. And no useful effect below the threshold: a half dose of
  some drugs is not half a treatment, it is no treatment.
THREAD: cooking (salt below tasting and past ruin), farming
  (fertiliser that stops paying and starts burning), engineering.
ASKED-AS: double the dose stronger more tablets no effect small amount too much ceiling

ESSENCE: potent and strong are different words. Potency is only how
  few milligrams are needed; the height of the ceiling is what the
  drug can actually do. A tiny dose of a very potent painkiller may
  relieve less than a large dose of a weak one, and a strong drug in a
  small tablet is not a strong tablet.
ROOT: this file / the dose axis and the effect axis are independent —
  one says where the curve sits, the other says how high it goes.
CANNOT: no ranking two drugs by their milligram numbers, so no reading
  strength off the size of the pill. And no reaching one drug's
  ceiling by piling on another that has a lower one, however potent it
  is.
THREAD: money (a price per unit against total value), engineering (a
  fine adjustment against a large range), cooking.
ASKED-AS: micrograms milligrams stronger tablet small dose potent weaker painkiller compare drugs

ESSENCE: every drug has two doses: the one that helps and the one that
  harms. What matters is the gap between them. Most medicines have a
  wide gap and are forgiving; a handful have almost none, and those
  are the ones whose blood level is measured rather than assumed.
ROOT: medicine / the dose makes the poison — and the useful number is
  not either dose but the ratio of the two.
CANNOT: no safe drug and no dangerous drug, only wide gaps and narrow
  ones. And no narrow-gap drug dosed by the book alone: with the
  harmful dose that close, the only honest guide is measuring what is
  actually in the blood.
THREAD: engineering (a tolerance band on a machined part), flight (a
  speed window between stalling and breaking up), money.
ASKED-AS: blood levels monitoring lithium warfarin narrow range toxic dose test regularly safe

ESSENCE: a dose is really a spread. Give an identical amount to a
  hundred people and some feel nothing, some get the full effect, and
  one or two are poisoned — because clearance, weight, receptors and
  other drugs differ across a crowd. The number on the packet is aimed
  at the middle of a hill.
ROOT: chance + risk / any measured thing across many bodies arrives as
  a spread, and a single recommendation has to pick a point on it.
CANNOT: no dose that is right for everybody, so no packet instruction
  without a tail of people it under-treats and a tail it harms. And no
  finding out which tail somebody is in except by giving it and
  watching.
THREAD: chance + risk, farming (one sowing rate across an uneven
  field), teaching.
ASKED-AS: same dose everyone different reaction sensitive some people nothing worked strong tablet

ESSENCE: half-life is the drug's whole schedule. It takes about five
  of them for a repeated dose to build to its steady level, and
  another five to leave after the last one — so a drug started today
  may not be working properly until next week, and one stopped today
  is still acting for days.
ROOT: physics / a quantity leaving at a rate proportional to how much
  is there halves in a fixed time, whatever the starting amount.
CANNOT: no immediate steady level from starting a drug, so no judging
  one as useless after two doses. And no clean stop: the effect trails
  the last tablet by the same days it took to arrive, which is why a
  rash or a recovery days after stopping still belongs to the drug.
THREAD: money (an account settling toward a new balance), physics (a
  cooling body), engineering.
ASKED-AS: how long until it works stopped taking still in system days build up steady

ESSENCE: the way round that is: when a drug is needed now and its
  half-life is long, the first dose is deliberately much larger than
  the ones after it. The big first dose fills the space; the small
  ones only replace what leaves.
ROOT: this file / a level is the balance between what arrives and what
  departs, so filling a tank and keeping it full are two different
  jobs.
CANNOT: no fast arrival at a steady level without an oversized first
  dose — the waiting is arithmetic, not caution. And no continuing at
  the loading amount: what fills is what poisons if it is repeated.
THREAD: cooking (a hot start then a low simmer), engineering (priming
  a system before running it), farming.
ASKED-AS: loading dose first big tablet then smaller why two amounts starting antibiotic quickly

ESSENCE: most drugs leave at a rate set by how much is present, so the
  body copes with a bit more by clearing a bit faster. A few do not:
  alcohol and one or two medicines are removed at a flat rate, and
  once that path is full, a small extra drink or tablet raises the
  level enormously.
ROOT: chemistry / the clearing machinery is a fixed number of workers,
  and once every worker is busy the queue simply grows.
CANNOT: no proportional response once a clearance path is saturated —
  the last drink is not one more unit, it is a different curve. And no
  safe extrapolation from experience at low doses: the whole danger
  lies past the point where the machinery is full.
THREAD: movement of goods (a road at capacity, where one more car
  costs everybody an hour), money, engineering.
ASKED-AS: alcohol one more drink over the limit sudden drunk phenytoin small increase toxic

ESSENCE: a swallowed drug is taxed twice before it reaches the body.
  The gut wall works on it, and then everything absorbed goes through
  the liver before it joins the circulation. For some medicines nine
  tenths is gone that way — which is why the swallowed dose is far
  larger than the injected one, and why a few drugs cannot be given by
  mouth at all.
ROOT: living machinery / everything absorbed from the gut passes the
  liver first, by design, as a checkpoint.
CANNOT: no comparing an oral dose to an injected one, so no converting
  between routes by arithmetic. And no oral route for a drug the first
  pass destroys: for those the choice is under the tongue, through the
  skin, or a needle.
THREAD: law (a customs post on the only road in), money (a cut taken
  before the money reaches you), farming.
ASKED-AS: tablet injection different dose why not swallow patch under tongue liver destroys drug

ESSENCE: the route decides speed, peak and destination. The same
  molecule inhaled reaches the brain in seconds, injected in a minute,
  swallowed over an hour, and rubbed on skin over a day. The drug is
  the same chemical and a different drug in every practical sense.
ROOT: physics / the time to arrive is set by the distance and the
  barrier, not by what is travelling.
CANNOT: no medicine without a route attached — a dose with no route
  named is not an instruction. And no fast route without a high peak:
  the same total amount arriving quickly overshoots, which is where
  the harm of fast routes lives.
THREAD: cooking (the same ingredient raw, boiled or fried), movement
  of goods, engineering.
ASKED-AS: swallow inject inhale patch cream suppository how fast works peak nose drip

ESSENCE: the tablet is not the drug. A coating, a matrix or a pump
  releases the same amount slowly through the day, which is what makes
  a once-daily tablet possible. Crush it or chew it and the whole
  day's dose is delivered at once, which has killed people with drugs
  they took safely for years.
ROOT: this file / a level is set by the rate of arrival, so anything
  that changes the rate changes the drug.
CANNOT: no halving or crushing a slow-release tablet, ever — the
  formulation is load-bearing. And no assuming two products with the
  same name and dose behave alike: the release pattern is part of the
  medicine.
THREAD: engineering (a metering valve against an open tap), cooking,
  farming.
ASKED-AS: crush tablet cut in half slow release modified swallow whole feeding tube capsule

ESSENCE: some drugs are inert until the body builds them. They are
  swallowed as a blank and turned into the working molecule by a liver
  enzyme — which means the effect depends on a machine some people
  were born without. Roughly one person in ten gets little from
  codeine for that reason, while a few convert it so fast that an
  ordinary dose is an overdose.
ROOT: heredity / the machinery for handling substances is inherited
  and varies, so the same swallowed thing is not the same drug in two
  bodies.
CANNOT: no effect from a prodrug in somebody who cannot convert it, so
  no reading their lack of relief as exaggeration or drug-seeking. And
  no extra safety from the low starting dose in a fast converter — the
  risk is in the conversion, not the swallow.
THREAD: heredity, cooking (a recipe that needs an ingredient the
  kitchen lacks), engineering.
ASKED-AS: codeine does not work for me painkiller no effect converts liver fast slow gene

ESSENCE: blood level is not tissue level. A fat-soluble drug leaves
  the blood quickly by soaking into fat and brain, then seeps back out
  for days after the last dose — so a single anaesthetic wears off by
  moving, not by being destroyed, and a drug can be undetectable in
  blood while still acting.
ROOT: chemistry / oil and water refuse each other, so a molecule's
  fat-liking decides where in a body it will pile up.
CANNOT: no reading how much drug is in a person from a blood test
  alone. And no predicting how long a fat-loving drug lingers from its
  dose: the fatter the store it filled, the longer the tail, which is
  why some drugs behave completely differently after weeks of use.
THREAD: cooking (fat carrying flavour that water cannot), chemistry,
  farming.
ASKED-AS: drug stored in fat lingers weeks anaesthetic wears off quickly blood test negative

ESSENCE: the brain is walled off from the blood, and the wall is what
  decides which drugs can think. A molecule that cannot cross is
  useless for pain or mood and safe for everything else; one that
  crosses easily brings drowsiness, confusion and dependence along
  with whatever it was for.
ROOT: living machinery / a membrane's default answer is no, and the
  brain's is stricter than anywhere else.
CANNOT: no drug acting on the brain that does not cross, so no purely
  mental medicine without the whole set of mental side effects. And no
  keeping a crossing drug out of the brain by aiming it elsewhere: it
  goes wherever the blood goes.
THREAD: law (a border with its own separate customs), building,
  engineering.
ASKED-AS: drowsy antihistamine crosses brain barrier non sedating sleepy pain relief mood

ESSENCE: no drug adds a function. Every one of them turns a dial the
  body already has — filling a lock the body built, blocking one, or
  changing how fast something is made or destroyed. There is no
  molecule that gives a body a capability it did not possess.
ROOT: chemistry / molecules act by shape meeting shape, so a substance
  can only act where something already fits it.
CANNOT: no drug for a capability that does not exist, so no pill that
  adds memory, courage or willpower — only ones that turn up or down
  what is already running. And no effect on a tissue with no receptor
  for it, however much is given.
THREAD: engineering (a control panel with a fixed set of knobs), music
  (an instrument played, not rebuilt), computing.
ASKED-AS: pill make me smarter drug adds ability boost brain enhancer receptors blocks

ESSENCE: there is a ladder at every lock. A full agonist turns it all
  the way, a partial one turns it part way and then refuses to go
  further, a blocker sits in the lock and does nothing. Which explains
  the strange middle drug that both relieves a craving and caps a high
  — it is holding the lock at half.
ROOT: this file / a ceiling exists wherever the number of locks is
  fixed, so a molecule can occupy without fully activating.
CANNOT: no blocker doing anything in an empty room — an antagonist has
  no effect where there is nothing to block, which is why some drugs
  do nothing to a person and floor an addicted one. And no partial
  agonist that is only mild: in a body already loaded, the same drug
  acts as the brake.
THREAD: engineering (a valve at half travel), music, law.
ASKED-AS: blocker naloxone methadone buprenorphine partial ceiling craving high antidote nothing happens

ESSENCE: a blocker that lets go can be out-shouted: send enough of the
  natural signal and it wins back the lock. A blocker that binds
  permanently cannot, and its effect outlasts the drug entirely — a
  single small aspirin disables a platelet for its whole ten-day life,
  long after the aspirin itself has gone.
ROOT: chemistry / a bond that can be undone gives a contest of
  numbers, and a bond that cannot gives a fact.
CANNOT: no reading duration off half-life for a drug that binds
  permanently: the level and the effect part company. And no reversing
  that kind of block by waiting for clearance — only new machinery
  being built ends it.
THREAD: craft (a screw against a weld), law (a lease against a sale),
  engineering.
ASKED-AS: aspirin stop before surgery days platelets irreversible wears off new cells block

ESSENCE: selective is a statement about a dose, not about a molecule.
  A drug that touches one target at a small dose reaches its second
  and third at a larger one — so the beta blocker that spares the
  lungs stops sparing them when the dose climbs, and the same is true
  of nearly everything described as targeted.
ROOT: medicine / a drug touches every lock it fits, and the only thing
  keeping it off the others is that there is not enough of it yet.
CANNOT: no selectivity that survives dose escalation, so no safe
  raising of a targeted drug on the grounds that it is targeted. And
  no molecule with exactly one target: the claim only ever means the
  others need more.
THREAD: engineering (a filter that passes everything under enough
  pressure), cooking, farming.
ASKED-AS: selective beta blocker asthma higher dose side effects targeted therapy specific safe

ESSENCE: a side effect is usually the same action in another place.
  The drug that dries a chest also dries a mouth, blurs vision and
  stops a bladder, because one lock sits in all those tissues. It is
  not the drug going wrong — it is the drug working correctly
  everywhere it can reach.
ROOT: medicine / no remedy has exactly one effect, and the reason is
  that a target is a shape and shapes are reused all over a body.
CANNOT: no aiming a drug at an organ: swallowed medicine goes
  everywhere the blood goes, so no wanted effect without its family.
  And no separating a drug from its side effects unless the tissues
  really do use different locks, which is rare and is what a genuinely
  new medicine is.
THREAD: engineering (one switch wired to several circuits), language
  (a word that carries its other meanings), cooking.
ASKED-AS: dry mouth blurred vision constipation same drug why side effects everywhere unavoidable

ESSENCE: three different things get called a reaction. An allergy is
  the immune system attacking the drug — rash, swelling, airway, and
  it can kill on the next exposure. A side effect is the drug doing
  its job somewhere unwanted. An intolerance is simply the dose being
  too much for this body. Only the first bans the drug for life.
ROOT: medicine / the defence force runs on me-or-not-me — and it
  either listed the drug or it did not, which is a completely
  different fact from how the drug behaves.
CANNOT: no allergy on a first-ever exposure, so a reaction on day one
  is usually something else. And no lifetime ban from a side effect
  written in the notes as an allergy: the label removes a whole family
  of drugs from somebody who could have taken them.
THREAD: law (a criminal record against a complaint), keeping
  knowledge, computing.
ASKED-AS: penicillin allergy rash upset stomach sick label notes intolerance reaction avoid

ESSENCE: tolerance is not one thing. The body can remove receptors, or
  build more clearing machinery so the drug leaves faster, or grow the
  opposing system until it cancels the drug out. Three different
  mechanisms, three different rates, and only one of them is fixed by
  raising the dose.
ROOT: living machinery / a steady signal is answered by the body
  dismantling its own side of the loop — but the loop can be cut in
  several places.
CANNOT: no single explanation for a drug fading, so no reflex increase
  of the dose without asking which mechanism. And no cross-tolerance
  where the mechanism was clearance: two drugs sharing an exit fade
  together, and two sharing a receptor do not.
THREAD: money (a subsidy absorbed into prices), teaching (praise that
  stops meaning anything), engineering.
ASKED-AS: stopped working needs more tolerance built up higher dose same effect faded

ESSENCE: tolerance does not arrive evenly. With opioids the pleasure
  and the pain relief fade quickly while the effect on breathing fades
  far less — so the user who has climbed to a dose that still gets
  them high is sitting at a dose that comfortably stops their
  breathing. The gap between the two closes as use goes on.
ROOT: this file / different effects run through different machinery,
  so they adapt at different speeds and the safety ratio shifts.
CANNOT: no assuming the safe gap holds as tolerance grows: the harmful
  dose barely moves while the useful one climbs. And no safe estimate
  of a tolerant person's dose by anyone else — what is ordinary for
  them is lethal for a beginner.
THREAD: engineering (a safety margin eaten by wear), flight, money.
ASKED-AS: opioid tolerance overdose still breathing high dose heroin fentanyl safe amount respiratory

ESSENCE: tolerance is partly a learnt anticipation, not just a
  chemical one. The body prepares its counter-response to the cues of
  the drug — the room, the ritual, the needle — and in a new place
  those cues are missing, the counter-response does not fire, and the
  person's usual dose kills them.
ROOT: the mind / a habit is filed under a sight, a time or a place,
  and the body's preparation is filed the same way.
CANNOT: no carrying tolerance intact into a new setting, so no dose
  that is safe because it was safe yesterday somewhere else. And no
  way for the person to feel it: the missing part of the tolerance is
  invisible until the drug is already in.
THREAD: the mind, sport (a routine that stops working in a new
  stadium), teaching.
ASKED-AS: overdose new place usual amount different room tolerance cues ritual same dose died

ESSENCE: three separate things get muddled. Tolerance is needing more.
  Dependence is the body having rebuilt itself around the drug, so
  stopping makes you ill. Addiction is the compulsion — the seeking
  that continues against a person's own interests. A patient on
  morphine for cancer can have all the first two and none of the
  third.
ROOT: living machinery / a system correcting toward a set point pushes
  back against a steady push, whatever the reason for the push.
CANNOT: no reading addiction off a dose or a withdrawal, so no calling
  a dependent patient an addict. And no addiction cured by getting the
  drug out of somebody: detox handles the dependence and leaves the
  compulsion entirely untouched, which is why the relapse is expected.
THREAD: money (owing against gambling), law, teaching.
ASKED-AS: addicted dependent withdrawal needs it painkillers cancer patient junkie difference compulsion

ESSENCE: withdrawal is the drug's own action running backwards. Quiet
  a system for long enough and stopping releases it — so coming off a
  sedative brings agitation, tremor, and fits, and coming off a
  stimulant brings flatness and sleep. The danger of the withdrawal
  matches how hard the drug pushed and on which system.
ROOT: living machinery / dependence is the same machinery running with
  the push removed, so the withdrawal is the mirror of the effect.
CANNOT: no gentle withdrawal from a drug that quietened the brain:
  alcohol and sedative withdrawal kill, while opioid withdrawal is
  agony and rarely lethal — the suffering ranks them in the wrong
  order. And no drug whose stopping is a neutral act.
THREAD: money (a subsidy withdrawn), engineering (a spring released),
  farming.
ASKED-AS: withdrawal shaking sweating fits alcohol detox benzos cold turkey dangerous opioid sick

ESSENCE: addiction is a learning fault, not a pleasure fault. The
  machinery that marks things as worth pursuing is trained by the drug
  until wanting and liking come apart — so the person keeps seeking
  something that has stopped giving them anything, and the wanting is
  triggered by cues they never notice.
ROOT: the mind / a cue is cheaper than a decision, so anything that
  trains cues takes the job over from wanting.
CANNOT: no explaining addiction by the pleasure, since it outlives the
  pleasure entirely. And no unlearning it by stopping: the cues stay
  loaded for years, which is why a street, a smell or a payday still
  fires long after the drug is gone.
THREAD: the mind, teaching, farming.
ASKED-AS: craving wanting not enjoying anymore addiction relapse triggers cues years later still

ESSENCE: how fast a drug reaches the brain decides how strongly it
  teaches. The same molecule that is mildly useful swallowed becomes
  powerfully hooking smoked or injected, because a steep rise is what
  the learning machinery reads as important. Slow arrival is most of
  what makes a medicine a medicine.
ROOT: this file / the route sets the peak, and the mind's reward
  machinery responds to change rather than level.
CANNOT: no drug made safe by its molecule alone: the same substance is
  a treatment on a slow route and a trap on a fast one. And no safe
  substitute that arrives quickly — replacement treatments work by
  being deliberately slow.
THREAD: the mind, cooking, engineering.

ESSENCE: some drugs make the liver build more of its clearing
  machinery. Then everything else that uses the same machinery is
  destroyed faster and quietly stops working — a contraceptive
  failing, a transplant rejecting. Worse, when the first drug is
  stopped the extra machinery is still there, and the second drug's
  ordinary dose now overdoses.
ROOT: living machinery / a body builds what is demanded of it, and
  demand here means anything that keeps the enzyme busy.
CANNOT: no interaction that appears at once: induction takes a week or
  two to build and a week or two to fade, so the failure never lands
  beside the change that caused it. And no safe stopping either:
  adding and removing the inducer are both dosing events for every
  other drug.
THREAD: farming (a workforce hired for one harvest and still on the
  payroll), money, engineering.

ESSENCE: the opposite trick is commoner and faster. One substance jams
  the enzyme, so the next drug through is not broken down and its
  level climbs several-fold on the same dose. A glass of grapefruit
  juice does exactly this to a gut enzyme, and the drug that was
  ninety-five percent destroyed on the way in now arrives almost
  whole.
ROOT: chemistry / an enzyme is a shaped clamp, and anything else that
  fits the clamp occupies it and stops the work.
CANNOT: no grapefruit warning that is really about fruit — it is about
  a first pass being switched off, which is why a small glass can
  multiply a dose. And no delay to hide behind: inhibition takes
  effect with the next dose, not next week.
THREAD: cooking, chemistry, movement of goods (one blocked lane
  backing up a whole road).

ESSENCE: two drugs need never meet chemically to be dangerous
  together. Three things that each make a person slightly drowsy — a
  painkiller, a sleeping tablet and a glass of wine — add up in the
  same physiology and stop the breathing. Neither the liver nor the
  blood test shows anything unusual.
ROOT: engineering / effects that push the same output add, regardless
  of whether the causes have anything to do with each other.
CANNOT: no safety from checking a drug list for chemical clashes
  alone: the common fatal combination is three ordinary things acting
  on one system. And no warning from levels — every drug is within its
  range, and the patient is dead.
THREAD: engineering (three small loads on one beam), money (several
  small commitments against one income), flight.
ASKED-AS: sleeping tablets alcohol painkillers together drowsy stopped breathing combination safe separately

ESSENCE: most drugs travel stuck to a shuttle protein in the blood,
  and only the free portion works. A blood test measures the total, so
  a person whose shuttle proteins are low from illness or age has a
  normal-looking level and a dangerous amount of active drug.
ROOT: chemistry / a molecule bound to something large cannot reach a
  receptor, so being present and being active are different states.
CANNOT: no reading effect off a total level where binding varies, so
  no comparing a sick person's number with a healthy one's. And no
  displacement that stays harmless: another drug knocking the first
  off its shuttle raises the working dose without changing a single
  milligram.
THREAD: movement of goods (cargo on a ship is not cargo delivered),
  money, computing.

ESSENCE: the enzymes that handle drugs are inherited, and the
  population splits into fast, ordinary and slow processors. The same
  tablet is a sub-therapeutic dose in one person and an overdose in
  another, and nothing about the two people looks different.
ROOT: heredity / a lost or doubled gene is a shut or duplicated
  factory, and the drug-handling factories vary more than most.
CANNOT: no dose that fits a body whose clearance is unknown, so no
  explanation from the packet when a standard amount does nothing or
  floors somebody. And no telling from the outside: age, weight and
  appearance do not report which processor a person has.
THREAD: heredity, farming, money.

ESSENCE: two organs set nearly every dose. The liver changes drugs and
  the kidney throws them out, so anybody with less of either is being
  given more drug than the number says. That is most of why the old,
  the very ill and the small are harmed by ordinary prescriptions.
ROOT: this file / a level is arrivals minus departures, and these two
  organs are the departures.
CANNOT: no standard dose in failing kidneys or liver: the same tablet
  accumulates until it poisons, and it does so over days rather than
  at once. And no safety in a drug being old and familiar —
  familiarity is about the drug, not about this person's clearance.
THREAD: money (spending unchanged after income falls), engineering,
  farming.

ESSENCE: children are not small adults. The machinery that clears
  drugs matures on its own timetable, so a newborn handles some
  medicines many times more slowly than an adult while a five-year-old
  clears others faster than one. Scaling an adult dose by weight gets
  several of them badly wrong.
ROOT: living machinery / a body under construction has parts not yet
  finished, and drug handling is one of them.
CANNOT: no paediatric dose derived by arithmetic from an adult one —
  the relationship is not proportional and in some drugs it is
  inverted. And no drug licensed for children by default: the trials
  were done in adults, so much of children's medicine is careful
  borrowing.
THREAD: teaching, farming (a seedling is not a small tree),
  engineering.

ESSENCE: in pregnancy there are two patients and one dose, and almost
  no evidence — because the trials that would provide it are the
  trials nobody will run. So the field works from accident and
  registry, which is why advice changes and why untreated illness in
  the mother is often the larger risk of the two.
ROOT: evidence + measurement / knowledge comes from comparisons that
  were actually made, and here they mostly were not.
CANNOT: no safety established by absence of reports, so no reading
  silence as reassurance. And no free choice of doing nothing:
  stopping a mother's epilepsy or depression treatment is itself an
  intervention with its own harms to both of them.
THREAD: evidence + measurement, law, family + lives.

ESSENCE: the inert pill produces real side effects too. Told the list,
  people get the headaches, the nausea and the drowsiness from a sugar
  tablet — and a body that has met a drug several times will produce
  its response to the ritual alone, the way a meal's smell starts
  digestion.
ROOT: the mind / expectation is part of every perception, and the
  body's preparations are trained by whatever reliably precedes them.
CANNOT: no side effect proved to belong to a drug without the

  often on the dummy. And no separating the ritual from the drug in

  handing it over are part of the dose delivered.
THREAD: the mind, persuasion, evidence + measurement.

ESSENCE: a trial reports an average, and no patient is the average. A
  drug that lifts a group's outcome by a small amount often does a
  great deal for a few and nothing for most — and there is usually no
  way to know in advance which you are, so the honest description of a
  treatment is how many must take it for one to benefit.
ROOT: chance + risk / a mean is a property of a crowd and can describe
  none of its members.
CANNOT: no reading an individual's benefit off an average effect. And
  no judging a drug's worth from your own case either: one person, one
  course, and no control is exactly the comparison that cannot be
  made.
THREAD: chance + risk, money, evidence + measurement.

ESSENCE: a trial of a few thousand people cannot see a harm that
  strikes one in twenty thousand. Those are found after the drug is
  sold, by the millions who take it — which is why the safety of a new
  medicine is genuinely less established than that of an old one,
  whatever the trial said.
ROOT: chance + risk / a rare event needs a large enough sample to
  appear at all, and no care in a small study can substitute.
CANNOT: no rare harm ruled out by a licensing trial, so no new drug
  that is known to be safe in the sense people mean. And no detecting
  it later without somebody reporting: harms that nobody links to the
  drug are never counted.
THREAD: chance + risk, keeping knowledge, law.
