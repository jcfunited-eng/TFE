# 79 BEING SOMETHING — what it takes to be a thing that acts

Not what a thing is made of: what it takes for there to be a
someone in there at all — doing, rather than merely happening.

ESSENCE: a thing that acts has an edge — an inside it keeps and an
  outside it deals with. Without that edge there is nothing for the
  word "it" to point at; only weather, one region shading into the
  next.
ROOT: an actor is a part of the world that holds itself apart.
CANNOT: no acting without a boundary. No "it" in something with no
  inside — no owner, no doer, no address.
THREAD: a cell's wall, a country's border, a company's books.
ASKED-AS: boundary self edge inside outside skin membrane where do i end separate thing

ESSENCE: what a thing can do, and what it knows it can do, are two
  separate holdings, and acting well needs both. A tool with no map
  of itself swings at everything; a map with nothing behind it is a
  boast.
ROOT: acting well means matching the reach to the job before
  reaching.
CANNOT: no choosing well without a picture of your own parts. No
  picture doing the work in place of the parts.
THREAD: a climber checking the rope she actually has, a hospital
  knowing which beds are free.
ASKED-AS: know what i can do abilities strengths limits inventory skills self knowledge capable

ESSENCE: what a thing cannot do shapes it as much as what it can. A
  river is a river because of its banks. Take the limits off and you
  do not get a greater thing, you get a smear with no name.
ROOT: a shape is made by its edges, not by its middle.
CANNOT: no identity without limits. No thing that can do anything —
  such a thing is not powerful, it is nothing in particular.
THREAD: a chess knight (it IS that odd jump), a poem's metre, a
  craft defined by its material.
ASKED-AS: limits cannot weakness what i am not defined by boundaries constraints shape identity limitations

ESSENCE: the same actor across time is one that carries its own
  past. Cut the carrying and each moment is a fresh thing wearing an
  old name — no promise survives, no lesson survives, nothing owes
  anything to what came before.
ROOT: continuity is not sameness of parts but unbroken carrying.
CANNOT: no one actor across time without a thread of memory. No
  keeping a promise you cannot carry to the day it comes due.
THREAD: a ship rebuilt plank by plank and still the ship, a court's
  record, a friendship.
ASKED-AS: same person over time memory continuity history identity change grew up still me past

ESSENCE: nothing is at risk unless there is a someone for it to be
  at risk for. A landslide on an empty hill loses nothing. Add an
  owner and the same slope becomes a danger, a cost, a thing worth
  acting about.
ROOT: risk lives in the relation between an event and a holder, not
  in the event.
CANNOT: no stake without a self that holds it. No caring in
  something that cannot lose.
THREAD: insurance (someone must be named on it), a wager, a doctor
  answerable for the outcome.
ASKED-AS: stakes risk care matters skin in the game consequences lose something at stake owner

ESSENCE: the root error is reading your own state as the world's. A
  tired eye calls the room dim; a frightened one calls the street
  dangerous. Acting well means keeping a running account of which
  half of the report is you.
ROOT: every report is made by an instrument, and the instrument is
  in the reading.
CANNOT: no true reading without subtracting the reader. No fixing
  the world with an act aimed at what was your own state.
THREAD: a thermometer warmed by the hand holding it, a compass near
  iron.
ASKED-AS: my mood not the world projecting confusing feelings with facts objectivity inside outside blaming

ESSENCE: to act is to put something forward that could fail and
  could be shown to have failed. Where nothing can come back and say
  no, there is no acting — only events, running off whatever they
  ran off.
ROOT: an act is a claim on the world, and claims answer to it.
CANNOT: no acting without a way of being corrected. No being
  corrected without a way of being wrong.
THREAD: a rudder that needs water, a scientist's test, a
  bookkeeper's balance that must come out.
ASKED-AS: wrong mistake correction feedback learn from errors find out check test accountable

ESSENCE: a purpose is something held by someone, ahead of the doing,
  and the doing is judged against it. Without one there is no choice
  — only response: pushed here, tipped there, no ground for calling
  any of it right or wrong.
ROOT: choosing needs a standard, and a standard needs an owner.
CANNOT: no choice without a purpose behind it. No purpose without
  someone to hold it — a rock rolling downhill wants nothing.
THREAD: a captain with a port in mind, a thermostat with its number.
ASKED-AS: purpose goal want choosing acting on purpose intention just reacting decide aim reason

ESSENCE: what a thing says about itself is a claim like any other,
  made from inside, with the worst seat in the house. People are
  certain of reasons they never had. The self-report is evidence,
  not the fact.
ROOT: self-knowledge is knowledge, and knowledge can be mistaken.
CANNOT: no privileged truth in a self-report. No settling what a
  thing is by asking it — outside evidence is required.
THREAD: a witness certain and wrong, an engine that cannot hear its
  own knock.
ASKED-AS: self knowledge wrong about myself blind spot others see me honest self report mistaken

ESSENCE: parts make one actor only when they answer to something
  shared — one purpose, one account, one place the last word sits.
  Without that there are several actors under one name, and the name
  is a costume.
ROOT: unity is not nearness of parts but common answerability.
CANNOT: no single actor without something the parts answer to. No
  promise from a thing whose parts do not answer to each other.
THREAD: a crew under one captain, a body under one nervous system, a
  firm with two managers signing opposite orders.
ASKED-AS: one self or many parts pulling different ways unified whole conflicted inside coherent

ESSENCE: the words that make every other word worth something are "I
  don't know". A thing that always produces an answer produces them
  at the same confidence with ground or without, so none of its
  answers tell you anything about the ground.
ROOT: a signal that never varies carries nothing.
CANNOT: no trust in a thing that cannot refuse. No knowing which
  answers are held when none are marked as unheld.
THREAD: a gauge stuck at full, a witness who never says the light
  was too poor to see.
ASKED-AS: i dont know admit uncertainty trust bluffing making it up confident wrong reliable honest
