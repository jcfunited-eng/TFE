# 50 DEEP COLUMN — WATER, HEAT, AND FREEZING
The first DEPTH build: the middle floors between physics' roots and
one real phenomenon. Still essences, never data. Every entry carries
its cannot-twin. Purpose: give the walker real stories to climb so
it can derive what experiments found.

ESSENCE: cooling speed is rented by the gap — a thing sheds heat in
  proportion to how much hotter it is than its surroundings; the
  wider the gap, the faster the flow.
ROOT: physics / heat flows hot to cold.
CANNOT: no steady cooling — the rate must die down as the gap
  closes; the last few degrees always take the longest.
THREAD: cooking (searing's first minute does most), medicine
  (fever's fastest drop is the first), money (UNSURE — debts paid
  fastest when largest, by proportion).

ESSENCE: evaporation is the fastest escape and it accelerates with
  warmth — the hottest molecules leave first, carrying away far
  more than their share of heat, and the warmer the water the
  faster they go.
ROOT: chemistry / heat is a hurry; physics / disorder wins.
CANNOT: no evaporation without loss of substance — water that
  cools by evaporating is also water that shrinks. The two cannot
  be separated.
THREAD: the body (sweat), oceans (rain is distilled climbing),
  cooking (the uncovered pot reduces).
ASKED-AS: puddle drying laundry clothesline blowing towels damp dishes glass kettle steam mist

ESSENCE: hot water stirs itself — warm water is lighter, so heat
  below sends currents upward and the whole body circulates,
  carrying inner heat to the surface where it can leave.
ROOT: physics / a thing keeps doing what it is doing unless pushed;
  lighter rises through heavier.
CANNOT: no self-stirring in a uniform body — as the water cools
  toward evenness the currents die, and a still, layered body
  sheds heat only by slow touch.
THREAD: oceans (the planet's plumbing), weather (all wind), the
  fabric's own law that movement is life.
ASKED-AS: boiling pot rolling radiator room ceiling upstairs hotter lava lamp soup swirling

ESSENCE: cold water lies in layers — near freezing, water grows
  LIGHTER as it chills further (its one strange reversal), so the
  coldest water floats to the top and the pond freezes from the
  surface down.
ROOT: measured premise — water's density peaks a few degrees above
  freezing; UNSURE at plain-language depth, but the law is iron.
CANNOT: no pond freezing from the bottom up. No fish surviving
  winter if water behaved like almost every other liquid.
THREAD: the living world (why lakes stay livable), deep earth
  (ice wedging from the top), survival (thin ice over warmer
  water).
ASKED-AS: skating solid bottom deep swimming toes summer chilly beneath icebergs sink fishing

ESSENCE: freezing needs a starting corner — ice does not begin
  everywhere at once; it must start on something: a speck, a
  bubble, a scratch, a jolt.
ROOT: chemistry / reactions need their threshold; building /
  cracks start at corners (the same law reversed into birth).
CANNOT: no perfectly clean, perfectly still water freezing at its
  official freezing point — with nothing to start on, it slides
  below zero unfrozen.
THREAD: weather (rain needs dust to condense on), cooking (candy
  crystallizing on a seed crystal or a scratch in the pan),
  story/drama (UNSURE, but every avalanche of events needs its
  first pebble).
ASKED-AS: snowflakes clouds fog forming droplets trigger impurities hail nucleus soda pop

ESSENCE: water can sit below freezing unfrozen — supercooled —
  waiting for a starting corner, and the cleaner and stiller it
  is, the deeper it can slide before the wait ends in a sudden
  all-at-once freeze.
ROOT: this file / freezing needs a starting corner.
CANNOT: no gentle freeze from deep supercooling — when the start
  finally comes, it comes as a rush.
THREAD: weather (freezing rain is supercooled water waiting for
  the road), aviation (the same drops waiting for a wing — icing's
  menace), chemistry (a settled balance moved all at once).

ESSENCE: heating evicts the dissolved air — warm water holds less
  gas, so water that has been hot carries fewer trapped bubbles
  afterward than water that never was.
ROOT: chemistry / heat is a hurry — hurried molecules shake their
  passengers loose.
CANNOT: no re-boarding without time — cooled water does not
  instantly reclaim its lost air; the eviction outlives the heat.
THREAD: cooking (the flat taste of twice-boiled water), the living
  world (warm seas hold less breath for fish — UNSURE as exact
  parallel, the direction is iron), medicine (the bends — gas
  leaving solution when pressure drops).
ASKED-AS: kettle cloudy ice cubes clear fizzy soda stale aquarium tank oxygen pop

ESSENCE: a hot container recruits its surroundings — set warm on a
  frosted shelf it melts the frost beneath and settles into wet
  contact, and wet touch moves heat many times faster than dry.
ROOT: cooking / heat moves by touch, flow, and glow; survival /
  water strips heat many times faster than air.
CANNOT: no such recruitment for the cold container — it sits on
  its frost as on a dry cushion, insulated by the very cold it
  keeps.
THREAD: engineering (thermal paste under every computer chip),
  medicine (wet cold kills faster than dry cold), craft (the
  fit-before-force law — contact is everything).
ASKED-AS: freezer tray pan bowl sticking puddle underneath dish glued sweating condensation base

ESSENCE: the first skin of ice is a lid — ice conducts heat poorly
  and floats, so the moment a surface freezes over, the water
  below cools far more slowly.
ROOT: this file / cold water lies in layers.
CANNOT: no fast freeze under a formed lid — whoever freezes a
  skin first slows their own finish.
THREAD: survival (igloos — ice as blanket), oceans (sea ice
  insulating the deep), strategy (UNSURE — early small victories
  that slow the larger campaign).
ASKED-AS: pond crust shield cover lake fish alive under winter sealed cap sheet

ESSENCE: a race of coolings is decided by paths, not just starting
  points — mass lost, self-stirring, gas carried, contact made,
  skin formed: each racer's route differs, so the one that starts
  behind can finish ahead.
ROOT: this whole file, composed; evidence / every measurement
  wears error bars.
CANNOT: no single-cause verdict for such a race — any experiment
  that controls only temperature and calls the rest equal has
  not made the rest equal.
THREAD: sports (the fast starter is not the sure winner), money
  (compound paths beating head starts), teaching (the slower
  student with the better method).
