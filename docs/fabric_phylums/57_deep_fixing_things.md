# 57 DEEP COLUMN — WHY THINGS BREAK AND HOW FIXING WORKS
Middle floors for the junk drawer: why held things let go, and
why the old tricks — jiggle, tap, restart — are laws wearing
work gloves.

ESSENCE: a screw is a wound spring parked on a ramp and held by
  friction — every vibration is a split second of weightlessness
  for its threads, and each one lets the stored tension walk it
  a hair backward; a million small slips is a loose bolt.
ROOT: physics / the thread is a ramp wrapped in a circle, and
  only friction parks a load on a ramp.
THREAD: machines (washing machines eat their own bolts),
  aviation (critical fasteners are wired shut — slips forbidden
  by wire, not hope).
ASKED-AS: screws loose bolts rattle glasses hinge washing machine wobbly nut tighten again

ESSENCE: penetrating oil is a crowbar one molecule wide — an oil
  thin enough to be pulled in by narrow gaps themselves, creeping
  even uphill into a rusted joint until a slippery film lies
  between the stuck faces (WD stands for water displacement; the
  first job was shoving water off metal).
ROOT: physics / narrow spaces pull liquids in — the tighter the
  gap, the harder the pull (the candle-wick law).
THREAD: craft (solder wicking into a tight joint by the same
  pull), cooking (the lamp wick lifting its own fuel).
ASKED-AS: wd40 rusted bolt stuck squeaky hinge soak overnight spray loosen nut seized

ESSENCE: a lock is a row of spring-loaded pins that must all sit
  at exact heights at once, and a worn key leaves some a hair
  off — the jiggle is a blind search through nearby positions
  until one instant lines them all up.
ROOT: chance+risk / enough tries find a narrow target — the
  jiggle is sampling, not strength.
THREAD: engineering (adding small noise to un-stick a system is
  a real, named technique), this file (the whack that reseats
  an old radio — same family).
ASKED-AS: key stuck jiggle wiggle lock door won't turn worn deadbolt trick sticky

ESSENCE: off-and-on works because most machine sickness lives in
  short-term state, not in the bones — an accumulated tangle no
  designer foresaw — and a restart throws the tangle away and
  re-walks the one path rehearsed a million times: the path from
  the beginning.
ROOT: computing / a system knows its start better than any of
  its middles — beginnings are rehearsed, middles are wilderness.
THREAD: medicine (sleep looks like the nightly version — UNSURE
  at mechanism), teaching (starting the explanation over beats
  patching it).
ASKED-AS: restart reboot router wifi computer phone frozen unplug glitch works again why

ESSENCE: duct tape is a liquid handshake with a cloth spine —
  its glue is a near-liquid that flows into microscopic
  roughness and grips by sheer contact area, while the woven
  backing spreads any pull across the whole bond; mighty dragged
  flat, feeble when peeled — a peel attacks one thin line at a
  time.
ROOT: craft / contact is everything — glue is fit, poured.
THREAD: the living world (geckos climb on contact area alone, no
  glue at all), engineering (shear versus peel decides rivets
  and welds — plane skins are not taped).
ASKED-AS: duct tape sticky fix leak hose patch peeling won't stick cold dusty holds

ESSENCE: paint is a mask, not a cure — iron's hunger for oxygen
  never left — and a scratch is a door: rust born there takes
  more room than the metal it ate, so it heaves the paint at its
  own edges and opens ever more bare iron; the flaw recruits.
ROOT: physics / disorder wins — refined metal is uphill, and
  rust is the ore road home.
THREAD: medicine (infection through broken skin — the same
  barrier law), building (cracks start at corners; rust starts
  at scratches — one family).
ASKED-AS: rust car scratch chip spreading bubbles paint fender bike chain orange primer

ESSENCE: a cheap tool hides its true price — it dies sooner (buy
  twice) and damages the work before dying: soft steel rounds
  the screwhead, the sloppy jaw chews the bolt, and the rescue
  of a chewed bolt is never sold cheap.
ROOT: chance+risk / expected value — real cost is the price plus
  every consequence, weighted by its odds.
THREAD: money (the old boots parable — buying cheap serially
  costs more), craft (the master's few good tools against the
  drawer of junk).
ASKED-AS: cheap tools stripped screw rounded bolt broke again buy quality worth it screwdriver

ESSENCE: righty-tighty is an agreement hardened into hardware —
  a thread is a spiral with a handedness and the world
  standardized on one; exceptions sit exactly where a machine's
  own spin would unscrew the standard (the bike's left pedal is
  cut backward so riding tightens it).
ROOT: law+agreements / a coordination rule needs no truth, only
  unanimity — same species as driving on the right.
THREAD: money (value by unanimity is the same magic), machines
  (the turnbuckle runs both handednesses so one twist tightens
  two ends).
ASKED-AS: righty tighty lefty loosey which way jar lid unscrew clockwise bolt pedal backwards

ESSENCE: a wet finger finds the draft because evaporation is a
  cooler and moving air its accelerant — the draft's side dries
  faster and chills, and skin, which cannot feel air's motion as
  such, feels cold's direction perfectly.
ROOT: deep column 50 / evaporation is the fastest escape — read
  here as an instrument.
THREAD: sailing (the licked finger aloft — the oldest wind
  gauge), survival (windchill is the same theft at body scale).
ASKED-AS: draft window wet finger lick wind direction leaky door winter cold spot breeze
