# 85 DEEP HEAT, LIGHT AND ELECTRICITY — the flows that run everything
The floors between physics' roots and the flows a person actually
meets: warmth moving, light arriving, charge pushed round a loop.
Essences only, never data. Every entry carries its cannot-twin.

ESSENCE: temperature is how hard the average particle is jiggling;
  heat is the whole pile of jiggling added up. A spark is thousands
  of degrees and harmless; a lukewarm bath would scald nobody and
  still holds enough energy to warm a house for an hour.
ROOT: physics / matter comes in discrete pieces — heat is those
  pieces in motion, and temperature is only their average.
CANNOT: no reading danger from temperature alone — you must also ask
  how much of the thing there is. And no temperature for a single
  particle: an average needs a crowd to be an average of.
THREAD: cooking (you can reach into a hot oven; the same-temperature
  water would take your hand off), medicine (burn depth follows the
  pile, not the peak), evidence (a reading is never a state).
ASKED-AS: degrees hot sparkler firework oven reach steam burn boiling bathtub warm scald

ESSENCE: heat crosses a gap three ways, and each has its own defeat.
  By touch, handed particle to particle — beaten by putting a poor
  handler between. By flow, carried bodily in a moving fluid — beaten
  by penning the fluid. By glow, thrown across empty space as
  invisible light — beaten only by a shiny face that throws it back.
ROOT: physics / heat flows hot to cold; the three are vehicles for
  one journey, not three different journeys.
CANNOT: no single barrier stops all three. A vacuum flask must fight
  each separately — a thin glass neck against touch, a vacuum against
  flow, silvered walls against glow — and it still loses in the end.
THREAD: survival (a foil blanket answers glow alone and does nothing
  about wind), building (a wall, a cavity, and a bright membrane are
  three different arguments), cooking.
ASKED-AS: thermos insulation blanket foil space heater radiator draft oven mitt handle warm

ESSENCE: the good insulators are not the materials — they are the air
  the materials trap. Wool, down, foam and fur are one invention: a
  structure holding air in pockets too small to circulate. Squash the
  pockets and the coat becomes a rag.
ROOT: this file / the three routes — still air is a poor handler and
  a penned fluid at the same time.
CANNOT: no insulation from a wet or crushed layer — water fills the
  pockets and hands heat along many times faster than air. And no
  warmth from loft alone in wind: moving air empties the pockets.
THREAD: survival (you sleep on your mat more than under your bag —
  your own weight kills the loft beneath you), building (double
  glazing is a trapped pocket), the living world (birds fluff up).
ASKED-AS: coat down jacket wool sweater fluffy wet sleeping bag foam windproof crushed

ESSENCE: some things hold a great deal of heat per degree and some
  almost none, so the same warmth given to each moves them very
  differently. Water is the champion holder, air is nearly empty —
  and a thing that holds much changes temperature slowly both ways.
ROOT: this file / heat and temperature are different quantities;
  this is the exchange rate between them.
CANNOT: no material that is both quick to heat and steady once hot —
  the stone that takes an hour to warm takes an hour to cool. And no
  storing warmth in something light: storage is mass.
THREAD: cooking (a heavy pan does not drop its temperature when the
  steak lands; a thin one surrenders instantly), oceans (coasts have
  mild winters because water refuses to change), building.
ASKED-AS: cast iron pan heavy pot heats slowly stays hot brick oven stone coastal

ESSENCE: while a thing is changing state its temperature stops
  moving. Every bit of energy pouring in goes to breaking the
  arrangement rather than speeding it up — and it takes far more to
  boil water already at boiling than to warm it there from ice-cold.
ROOT: chemistry / bonds hold and breaking them costs; the bill is
  paid in full before the thermometer is allowed to move again.
CANNOT: no raising temperature through a change of state — heat
  harder and the pot boils faster, never hotter. And no free return
  trip: the same energy comes back out on condensing, which is why
  steam scalds far worse than the boiling water it came from.
THREAD: cooking (a rolling boil is not hotter than a bare simmer),
  weather (a storm is powered by water condensing high up), medicine
  (an ice pack works while melting and is useless afterwards).
ASKED-AS: ice melting stays cold boiling steam burn hotter lid sweating condensation stall

ESSENCE: evaporation is the only cooling that can push a thing below
  the air around it. The escaping molecules carry away more than
  their share, and no fan and no shade can manage that — a fan only
  clears away the full air so more molecules can leave.
ROOT: deep water and heat / evaporation is the fastest escape — this
  is what the escape buys for whatever stays behind.
CANNOT: no evaporative cooling into full air — when the air can hold
  no more, sweat simply sits and the whole trick fails. And no fan
  cooling the air itself: a fan helps a wet skin and nothing else.
THREAD: the body (sweat is why humans can run animals down in heat),
  weather (a desert night; a cooler that works only where it is dry),
  cooking (a wet cloth wrapped round a jug).
ASKED-AS: sweat cooling fan hot humid sticky wet towel misting swamp cooler pant

ESSENCE: warm air holds more water than cold air, so cooling any air
  far enough will eventually fill it. The temperature at which it
  fills is the dew point — and anything colder than that pulls water
  out of the air on sight.
ROOT: this file / phase change, plus chemistry / warmth is a hurry —
  hurried molecules stay airborne longer.
CANNOT: no dew, fog, cloud or condensation above the dew point, at
  any humidity whatever. And no dry glass around a cold drink in
  humid air — the water is not leaking out; it is being caught.
THREAD: weather (clouds form where rising air cools to its dew point,
  which is why cloud bottoms are flat and all at one height), the
  home (mould grows on cold walls where warm indoor air meets them).
ASKED-AS: sweating glass window fog condensation mirror bathroom dew grass morning humid mould

ESSENCE: your skin cannot measure temperature — it measures how fast
  heat is leaving it. A metal handle and a wooden one in the same
  room are the same temperature; the metal simply drains your finger
  many times faster, and the speed is what you feel as cold.
ROOT: this file / heat travels by touch at wildly different speeds
  through different materials.
CANNOT: no honest temperature reading by hand, ever — one reading
  feels like three through metal, wood, and air. And no metal that
  feels warm at room temperature, however long it has sat there.
THREAD: survival (why you sit on wood and never on bare metal or
  stone), medicine (a stethoscope warmed before it touches), the mind
  (perception reports change and rate, never state).
ASKED-AS: metal cold touch wood handle tile floor bare feet steering wheel doorknob

ESSENCE: nearly everything swells with warmth, but each material
  swells at its own rate. So the trouble is never expansion itself —
  it is the disagreement: any two materials fastened together fight
  each other every time the temperature moves.
ROOT: building / everything breathes with temperature; this is what
  happens when two different breathers are bolted to one another.
CANNOT: no permanent joint between materials that expand differently
  — enough cycles and it works loose, cracks, or curls. And no flat
  bimetal strip: bond two disagreeing metals and heat becomes bending,
  which is how the simplest thermostats have always worked.
THREAD: dentistry (a filling must match the tooth's rate or split
  it), engineering (glass-to-metal seals chosen for matched rates),
  building (rails, roads, and cladding all given room to disagree).
ASKED-AS: thermostat bimetal strip filling cracked tooth seal gap warping curling metal glass

ESSENCE: an engine does not consume heat — it lets heat fall from hot
  to cold and takes a cut on the way down. No cold place to fall to
  means no fall and so no work, which is why every engine has an
  exhaust, a radiator, or a cooling tower it cannot do without.
ROOT: physics / disorder wins — work can be harvested from a flow and
  never from a still, even pool of warmth.
CANNOT: no engine turning heat entirely into work — the best possible
  share is fixed by the two temperatures alone, and no cleverness of
  design has ever beaten it. And no engine at all in surroundings as
  hot as its own fire.
THREAD: engineering (power stations sited on rivers for the cold end,
  not the hot one), the body, money (profit comes from a difference,
  and dies when the difference closes).
ASKED-AS: engine radiator exhaust hot car cooling tower power plant steam waste heat

ESSENCE: a fridge is an engine run backwards. Pay it work and it will
  carry heat the wrong way, from cold to hot — but the carriage must
  be paid for, so the heat it dumps into the room is always more than
  the heat it took out of the box.
ROOT: this file / an engine harvests a fall; reverse the machine and
  you are buying the climb instead.
CANNOT: no cooling a room by opening its own fridge — the room gets
  warmer, always. And no free cold: the further uphill the heat must
  be carried, the more work each unit costs, so a freezer in a hot
  garage is a losing bargain that gets worse in summer.
THREAD: the home (an air conditioner is this machine with half of it
  hung outside), engineering (a heat pump is a fridge aimed at your
  house, which is why it delivers more warmth than it eats).
ASKED-AS: refrigerator air conditioner freezer coils heat pump garage door open cooling warm

ESSENCE: energy is never lost, but it is always devalued. The same
  joules concentrated in a fire can do work; spread evenly through a
  warm room they can do nothing at all. What every process actually
  consumes is not energy but its unevenness.
ROOT: physics / disorder wins — stated as a ledger of quality rather
  than of quantity.
CANNOT: no work from an evenly warm world, however much energy it
  holds. And no un-spreading without paying for it somewhere else —
  tidiness is always a local victory bought at a wider cost.
THREAD: cleaning (a clean room is paid for by a tired body and a full
  bin), life (eating is buying unevenness), money (an economy runs on
  differences in price and not on the total amount of money).
ASKED-AS: energy wasted heat death useless warm room fuel spent order mess cleanup

ESSENCE: everything with a temperature glows, and the hotter it is
  the brighter and bluer the glow. You are shining now, in waves too
  long for eyes; iron warms to red, then orange, then white; the sun
  is simply a very hot thing obeying the same one rule.
ROOT: physics / wiggled charges shed waves — heat is wiggling, so
  heat must shine.
CANNOT: nothing above absolute zero is truly dark — there is no
  hiding from a camera built for long waves. And no white-hot fire
  cooler than a red one: the colour reports temperature and cannot lie.
THREAD: astronomy (a star's colour IS its temperature, read across
  the galaxy), blacksmithing (the work judged by eye), photography
  (a bulb's "colour temperature" is a literal temperature).
ASKED-AS: red hot glowing iron night vision thermal camera infrared warm bulbs star

ESSENCE: every time light meets matter it is split three ways — some
  bounces, some passes through and bends, some is swallowed and
  becomes heat. The three shares must add up to everything that
  arrived, and how a thing splits them IS its appearance.
ROOT: physics / energy is never created — light arriving must be
  fully accounted for on departure.
CANNOT: no material that is black, mirror-bright, and clear at once —
  the shares are one budget spent once. And no absorption without
  warming: a black shirt is black because it kept what you cannot see
  coming off it, and it hands that back as heat.
THREAD: design (matte against gloss is a choice of shares), building
  (white roofs in hot countries), the living world (a leaf is green
  because green is the share it refuses to keep).
ASKED-AS: black shirt hot white roof mirror window tinted glass shiny matte glare

ESSENCE: the sky's blue and the sunset's red are the same light
  counted twice. Air scatters short waves sideways far more strongly
  than long ones, so the blue leaves the sunbeam and fills the whole
  dome — and what reaches you straight from a low sun is the remainder.
ROOT: light and seeing / scattering favours short strides steeply —
  read here as a conservation rather than as an effect.
CANNOT: no blue sky without a reddened sun somewhere — the two are
  one transaction, seen from two seats. And no long path through air
  leaving colour unchanged: the longer the path, the redder survives.
THREAD: astronomy (distant stars reddened by dust between), the ocean
  (deep water is blue because red is drunk first), photography.
ASKED-AS: sunset red sky blue sunrise orange moon horizon smoke haze distant mountains

ESSENCE: focus is not a setting, it is a marriage of two distances.
  A lens gathers every ray leaving one point and rebuilds them at
  exactly one other point — move the object and the meeting place
  moves too, so only one distance can ever be sharp at a time.
ROOT: light and seeing / a lens is bending made deliberate; focus is
  that bending arranged so rays reconverge.
CANNOT: no lens sharp at every distance at once — near and far cannot
  both land on one screen; a small opening only shrinks the blur
  until it is beneath notice. And no image without gathering: fewer
  rays admitted always means a dimmer picture.
THREAD: photography (the aperture trades brightness against how much
  is acceptably sharp), the eye (its lens changes shape instead of
  sliding), attention (the same trade, in a mind).
ASKED-AS: camera focus blurry background aperture depth field reading glasses zoom sharp near

ESSENCE: the eye works in ratios, not in amounts. It sets its gain to
  whatever is in front of it and then reports differences — which is
  how one organ covers starlight and noon. But it covers only a
  narrow slice at any moment, and it shifts down over long minutes.
ROOT: the mind / perception is construction — the eye's sensitivity
  is set by what it has just been looking at.
CANNOT: no seeing detail and colour together in the dark — the
  dim-light detectors are colour-blind and coarse, and the sharp
  central spot is nearly blind at night. And no seeing into a shadow
  that sits beside a bright light.
THREAD: photography (a camera records a range the eye can only visit
  one part of at a time), driving (oncoming headlights blind by
  resetting the gain), astronomy (looking aside to catch a faint star).
ASKED-AS: night vision dark adjust headlights blinding stars faint corner grey colours dim

ESSENCE: light has wavelengths; colour is a verdict. The eye samples
  the entire spectrum with only three kinds of detector, so different
  mixtures can return identical verdicts — which is how a screen with
  three lamps shows you colours it never actually emits.
ROOT: light and seeing / colour is the wave's stride length; this is
  the gap between the stride and the seeing of it.
CANNOT: no telling two spectra apart once they land the same on three
  detectors — the information is gone before the brain is consulted.
  And no brown, no pink and no magenta anywhere in a rainbow: those
  are judgements, not wavelengths.
THREAD: computing (why three numbers per pixel suffice), design (paint
  matching in the shop and clashing outdoors — the same trick
  failing), the living world (animals with two or four kinds).
ASKED-AS: rgb screen pixels magenta pink brown rainbow colourblind matching paint lights different

ESSENCE: a sharp shadow needs a small light. Every real source has
  width, so around the full shadow lies a soft rim where the object
  hides part of the light but not all — and the further the surface
  from the object, the more rim there is and the less core.
ROOT: light and seeing / a shadow is geometry cast by blockage; the
  width of the source becomes the width of the blur.
CANNOT: no crisp shadow from a broad source — an overcast sky is one
  enormous lamp and casts almost none. And no edge that stays sharp
  with distance: lift your hand off the page and it dissolves.
THREAD: photography (softboxes and umbrellas are sources deliberately
  widened), astronomy (a total eclipse is standing in the core and a
  partial one is standing in the rim), stagecraft.
ASKED-AS: shadow fuzzy edges soft light sunny sharp cloudy hand wall eclipse umbrella

ESSENCE: a light wave wiggles across its direction of travel, and it
  can wiggle at any angle around that line. A polarising filter passes
  one angle and stops the rest — and light bounced off a flat surface
  returns mostly wiggling one way, which is what glare is.
ROOT: physics / light is the charge-wave — the wiggle has a
  direction, and directions can be sorted.
CANNOT: no killing glare with a plain dark lens — darkening dims
  everything equally and only sorting is selective. And no seeing
  polarisation by eye: the information arrives and humans are almost
  entirely blind to it.
THREAD: photography (a turned filter deepens a sky and sees into
  water), screens (every LCD is two sorted filters with a controlled
  twist between), the living world (bees navigate by the sky's angle).
ASKED-AS: polarised sunglasses glare water fishing lcd screen rotating filter reflection sky dark

ESSENCE: some materials swallow a short-wave photon and give back a
  longer one — always longer, because part of the energy is kept as
  heat. So they answer invisible light with visible light and appear
  to glow out of nowhere.
ROOT: physics / the very small takes and gives energy in set steps;
  the size of the drop between steps chooses the colour returned.
CANNOT: no fluorescing upward — you cannot shine red in and get blue
  out, one photon at a time. And no glow without a live source: true
  fluorescence stops the instant the lamp does, and anything that
  fades slowly is a different, storing trick.
THREAD: the home (laundry brighteners are dyes returning more blue
  than they receive, so white shirts read whiter than white),
  lighting (a white LED is a blue chip under a fluorescing coat).
ASKED-AS: blacklight glowing white shirt neon highlighter tonic water uv party fluorescent bulb

ESSENCE: a wire is already full of loose charge, so switching on a
  lamp does not send charge from the switch to the bulb. The push
  travels at nearly the speed of light while the charges themselves
  crawl slower than an ant — a full pipe, pressed at one end.
ROOT: physics / like charges push — the shove is handed along a crowd
  that was standing there all along.
CANNOT: no waiting for charge to arrive from the switch. And no
  current without a complete loop back to the source — charge does
  not pile up somewhere and stop, so a cut wire ends it instantly.
THREAD: plumbing (a full pipe behaves in exactly this way), computing
  (signal speed is set by the push and never by the crawl), the body
  (a nerve is a push travelling down a line already loaded).
ASKED-AS: light switch instant electricity flow wire electrons speed circuit loop cut broken

ESSENCE: three words for one situation. Voltage is the push, current
  is how much flows, resistance is how hard the path fights. Fix any
  two and the third is already decided — you never get to choose all
  three. (V = I × R)
ROOT: physics / charge is pushed by charge, and every real path
  charges a toll for being crossed.
CANNOT: no current without a difference in push, and none at all
  through a broken path however high the voltage. And no truth in
  "the voltage kills" — it is the current through you that does the
  damage; voltage only decides whether any gets in.
THREAD: plumbing (pressure, flow, and narrowness — the same three),
  medicine (defibrillators and nerve studies dosed in current),
  engineering (every component rated in these terms).
ASKED-AS: volts amps ohms battery shock outlet wall power resistance wire fuse current

ESSENCE: a wire's own resistance turns part of what it carries into
  heat, and that loss grows with the SQUARE of the current. Double
  the amps and the wire wastes four times as much — so the cure is
  fewer amps: fatter copper, shorter runs, and high voltage for
  anything travelling far.
ROOT: this file / resistance costs, plus physics / energy is never
  created — the missing power is in the wire, warming it.
CANNOT: no long thin wire delivering full power. And no coiled
  extension cord under load: its heat has nowhere to leave, and
  coiled cords under load are how houses burn.
THREAD: engineering (the entire grid runs high and thin for this one
  reason, stepped down only at the last street), the home (lights
  dipping when the kettle starts), cars (thick battery cables).
ASKED-AS: extension cord hot thin wire gauge voltage drop long run power lines

ESSENCE: two ways to join things. In series everything shares one
  current and splits the push, so any single break stops all of it.
  In parallel everything shares one push and splits the current, so
  each is independent and the total demand simply adds up.
ROOT: physics / charge is conserved — whatever enters a junction must
  leave it, so a split of one quantity forces a sharing of the other.
CANNOT: no independent switching within a series string — one dead
  bulb darkens the whole chain, the old Christmas-light misery. And
  no free additions in parallel: each device draws its own current
  and the shared wire eventually gives way.
THREAD: the home (house wiring is parallel; a fuse is deliberately
  placed in series with everything it guards), the body (kidneys in
  parallel, the gut in series), engineering (redundancy is parallel).
ASKED-AS: christmas lights one out batteries stacked outlet strip overload breaker fuse wiring

ESSENCE: there is no separate magnetic stuff. Magnetism is what
  moving charge does — a current wraps a field around itself, and a
  permanent magnet is a material whose atoms carry their own tiny
  circulating charges, all lined up and agreeing.
ROOT: physics / a moving charge makes magnetism; physics / no
  magnetic pole exists alone.
CANNOT: no magnetism from charge at rest, and no magnet without
  circulation somewhere inside it. And no magnet surviving heat or
  hammering — shake the agreement apart and the magnetism is gone.
THREAD: the earth (its field is circulating molten iron, and it has
  reversed many times), medicine (an MRI is a coil strong enough to
  line up the body's own tiny magnets), electronics.
ASKED-AS: magnet fridge compass electromagnet coil nail wire iron filings demagnetise dropped heated

ESSENCE: a changing magnetic grip on a coil creates a push inside it;
  a current in a coil inside a field creates a force. These are one
  fact read forwards and backwards — so every motor is a generator
  and every generator is a motor, the same object either way round.
ROOT: physics / moving charge and magnetism are two faces of one
  thing, and the relation does not care which way you read it.
CANNOT: no induction from a steady field — something must be
  changing, so a magnet lying still on a coil makes nothing at all.
  And no free generation: drawing current out makes the shaft harder
  to turn, in exact proportion to what you draw.
THREAD: cars (regenerative braking is the motor read backwards, and
  the car's own weight pays for the charge), the home (an induction
  hob heats the pan itself), power stations (all of them spin).
ASKED-AS: generator motor alternator dynamo bike light crank regenerative braking induction stove turbine

ESSENCE: direct current flows one way; alternating current reverses
  many times a second. The reversal is not a flaw — only a changing
  current can be stepped up or down, and that single fact decided how
  every grid on the planet is built.
ROOT: this file / induction needs change; this file / high voltage is
  what makes long thin wires possible.
CANNOT: no transformer works on steady current — that alone settled
  the argument between the two. And no chemistry or chip running on
  alternating current: batteries, nerves, and every electronic device
  need one direction, so nearly every appliance quietly converts back.
THREAD: the home (every charger brick is a converter doing this), the
  body (nerves are strictly one-way pulses), engineering (long
  undersea links go back to direct current for other reasons).
ASKED-AS: ac dc outlet battery charger brick transformer hertz cycles power adapter converts

ESSENCE: two opposite temperaments. A capacitor is a bucket for
  charge: it fights a change of VOLTAGE, passing fast wiggles and
  blocking steady flow. A coil is a flywheel for current: it fights a
  change of CURRENT, passing the steady and resisting the sudden.
ROOT: this file / charge pushes back on charge (the bucket); this
  file / a changing current fights itself through its own field.
CANNOT: no instant change in either — a capacitor cannot jump its
  voltage and a coil cannot jump its current, whatever the switch
  does. And no safe opening of a loaded coil: the current's insistence
  on continuing appears as a spark across the contacts.
THREAD: music (tone controls are these two sorting fast from slow),
  the home (a camera flash is a bucket emptied at once), computing
  (memory holding bits as charge in unimaginably small buckets).
ASKED-AS: capacitor coil flash charging speaker crossover spark switch surge filter smoothing

ESSENCE: air is an insulator until it is not. Ice and water churning
  in a storm's updraft rub charge apart and stack it high and low,
  until the pull across the gap is fierce enough to tear electrons
  off the air itself — and torn air conducts, so everything crosses at once.
ROOT: physics / like charges push and opposites pull; every insulator
  has a strain past which it stops insulating.
CANNOT: no lightning without separated charge, and no separation
  without violent vertical churning — which is why calm weather never
  has any. And no gentle lightning: breakdown is all-or-nothing, so
  the discharge is always an avalanche.
THREAD: the home (a winter doorknob spark is the identical event,
  small), sound (thunder is air heated so fast it explodes), the
  living world (trees split because their sap boils in an instant).
ASKED-AS: lightning thunder storm strike static shock doorknob spark carpet winter zap flash

ESSENCE: put any two things in contact and they drift toward one
  temperature, always, and stop there. That plain fact is what makes
  a thermometer possible at all — the instrument does not read the
  thing; it joins the thing and then reports on itself.
ROOT: physics / heat flows hot to cold — so contact has exactly one
  resting state, and both parties must end at it.
CANNOT: no measuring temperature without changing it, however
  slightly — the thermometer must take or give heat to learn
  anything. And no instant reading: joining takes time, and a
  thermometer read too early reports its own past instead.
THREAD: cooking (a probe left in too briefly lies low), medicine
  (why a thermometer is held a full minute), evidence (every
  instrument disturbs what it inspects — this is the plainest case).
ASKED-AS: thermometer reading wait touch same temperature room settles equal warm cold contact

ESSENCE: cold has a floor and heat has no ceiling. Temperature counts
  jiggling, and jiggling can stop but cannot go negative — so there
  is a coldest possible state, roughly 273 degrees below the freezing
  of water, and nothing has ever been colder.
ROOT: physics / heat is motion of the pieces — the bottom of the
  scale is simply the pieces having no motion left to lose.
CANNOT: no temperature below that floor, anywhere, ever. And no
  reaching it either: every step of cooling removes a share of what
  remains, so the last step is always infinitely far away.
THREAD: astronomy (deep space sits a few degrees above the floor,
  warmed only by the universe's leftover glow), engineering
  (superconductors and quantum machines living just above it).
ASKED-AS: coldest possible absolute zero kelvin negative degrees limit bottom freezing space temperature

ESSENCE: a solid does not always have to melt first. Given low enough
  pressure or dry enough air, it hands molecules straight to the air
  and skips the liquid entirely — which is why dry ice smokes without
  puddling and why old snow shrinks on days it never thaws.
ROOT: this file / evaporation is molecules escaping a surface; a
  solid surface can be escaped from too, only more slowly.
CANNOT: no puddle from dry ice at ordinary pressure — the liquid form
  simply does not exist down here. And no protection for frozen food
  from freezing alone: ice leaves the surface anyway, which is what
  freezer burn is.
THREAD: cooking (freezer burn and ice cubes shrinking in the tray),
  the home (mothballs vanishing), weather (frost disappearing off a
  cold sunny roof with nothing running off it).
ASKED-AS: dry ice smoke freezer burn frost disappears snow shrinking mothballs vanishing cubes

ESSENCE: a tall column of warm air weighs less than the same column
  of cold, and that difference is a genuine pull. So a chimney does
  not merely let smoke out — it drags air in at the bottom, and the
  taller and hotter the column, the harder it drags.
ROOT: flight and fluids / fluid moves only where pressure differs;
  warmth makes the difference and height multiplies it.
CANNOT: no draw from a cold chimney — a fire lit in a cold flue backs
  its smoke into the room until the column warms. And no draw at all
  without an opening low down: a sealed room starves its own fire.
THREAD: building (stairwells and lift shafts drive winter draughts
  through whole towers), survival (a fire pit dug with an air channel
  burns hotter), cooking (a range hood working with warmth, not against).
ASKED-AS: chimney draw fireplace smoke rising stairwell draft attic vents hot air tall

ESSENCE: wind does not lower the temperature of anything — it raises
  the RATE. Still air clings to a warm surface as a thin insulating
  film; moving air keeps stripping that film away, so heat leaves as
  fast as the surface can make it.
ROOT: this file / insulation is trapped still air — wind is that
  insulation being removed continuously.
CANNOT: no wind chill for an object already at air temperature — a
  parked car cools no further than the thermometer says, however
  hard it blows, so exposed pipes freeze by cold and not by wind.
  And no beating it with loft alone: only a windproof layer holds.
THREAD: survival (a shell over insulation is not optional), the body
  (why a breeze feels wonderful in heat and murderous in cold),
  cooking (a fan oven cooks faster at the same setting).
ASKED-AS: wind chill cold feels colder breeze freezing pipes fan blowing exposed skin

ESSENCE: a clear night sky is effectively a very cold surface, and
  anything facing it glows heat away into that emptiness. So an
  exposed roof, windscreen or blade of grass can fall BELOW the air
  around it, and frost forms on nights the thermometer calls safe.
ROOT: this file / everything glows by its temperature — and glow, the
  third route, does not need anything in between to travel through.
CANNOT: no such cooling under cloud — a cloud is a warm lid that
  glows back, which is why frosts come on clear calm nights and not
  on overcast ones. And none under a tree or a carport, for the
  identical reason.
