# 15 BUILDING AND MATERIALS — the long argument with gravity
Physics called every structure an argument with gravity. The
rules: two jobs, a few shapes that win them, and water waiting to
win in the end.

ESSENCE: a structure hands out exactly two jobs — squeeze
  (compression) and stretch (tension); everything built carries
  one, the other, or both (a bent beam: squeezed above, stretched
  below).
ROOT: physics / no push without push-back — every pound aloft
  passes hand to hand to the ground.
THREAD: the body (bones take squeeze, tendons take stretch — the
  skeleton splits the jobs exactly), spider webs (all stretch), a
  sailboat's rig (mast squeezed, stays stretched).
ASKED-AS: hanging forces bridge column cable tower stand weight load holding up

ESSENCE: the triangle is the only pinned-stick shape that cannot
  change form without changing a stick's length; every other
  polygon folds.
ROOT: mathematics / three sides fully determine a triangle; a
  square's corners are free to lean.
THREAD: every truss bridge, crane, and roof, the bicycle (a
  diamond is two triangles), the wobbly bookshelf cured by one
  brace.
ASKED-AS: gate sagging fence diagonal crossbar geometry sturdy scaffolding pylon strong

ESSENCE: the arch turns a gap into a squeeze — each stone leans on
  the next so the load flows around the hole as pure squeeze and
  lands as outward-and-down thrust at the feet.
ROOT: building / two jobs — it needs only the job stone can do,
  asking no stretch of a material that has none.
THREAD: eggshells and skulls (domes are arches spun in a circle),
  the foot's arch carrying every step, Roman bridges standing two
  thousand years into their argument.
ASKED-AS: doorway curved opening tunnel cathedral igloo span brick vault aqueduct

ESSENCE: materials remember two ways — pulled a little, they
  spring back complete (elastic); pulled past the yield point,
  the change is permanent and the material keeps the scar.
  (elastic, then plastic)
ROOT: chemistry / bonds are electric springs — small stretch
  strains them, big stretch breaks and re-seats neighbors.
THREAD: the body (a sprain is a ligament past yield), cooking
  (dough kneaded past spring into set shape), cars (crumple zones
  spend the crash as permanent bend, on purpose).
ASKED-AS: paperclip rubber band snap wire hanger dent spoon twisting shape

ESSENCE: a crack is a force magnifier — load flowing around a
  sharp notch crowds at the tip, multiplying stress tens of
  times; each advance sharpens the tip, so cracks feed on
  themselves.
ROOT: physics / pressure = force ÷ area, taken to the limit — a
  crack tip is nearly zero area.
THREAD: cooking (the knife — the same concentration, employed on
  purpose), flight (windows rounded because square ones tore the
  first jetliners apart), engineering / fatigue (this law run a
  million gentle times).
ASKED-AS: chip windshield spreading bag rip scissors phone screen shatter split

ESSENCE: wood is a bundle of straws — mighty along the grain,
  feeble across it; it splits along, snaps across, and moves with
  moisture across the straws, hardly along them.
ROOT: farming+life / a tree is a pump — tubes built to lift water
  in life dictate the timber's character after.
THREAD: cooking (meat carves across the grain — muscle is straws
  too), firewood (ask along the grain, never across), plywood
  (the cure: grains crossed on purpose).
ASKED-AS: lumber sawing chopping axe kindling tabletop warping furniture swelling direction knots

ESSENCE: concrete and steel are a marriage of opposites —
  concrete takes squeeze and shrugs at stretch, steel takes
  stretch; laid as bars where the stretch runs, it lends concrete
  the missing job.
ROOT: building / two jobs; plus one piece of luck — both swell
  with heat at nearly the same rate, so the seasons don't divorce
  them.
THREAD: the body (bone is the original — stiff mineral wed to
  stretchy collagen), engineering (fiberglass and carbon fiber:
  same marriage, other couples), the rust-stained ruins where
  water won.
ASKED-AS: rebar cement rods sidewalk driveway crumbling bridge highway building cracking

ESSENCE: a foundation is a force spreader — walls concentrate
  weight and soil is weak, so footings widen the load until weak
  ground can carry it; every building's true base is dirt.
ROOT: physics / pressure = force ÷ area, run in reverse — grow
  the area, tame the pressure.
THREAD: snowshoes and camel feet (the same trick, portable),
  tank treads, farming (roots are the tree's own footing).
ASKED-AS: house settling sinking cracks basement mud sand tower slab deep digging

ESSENCE: water is the building's patient enemy — it rusts, rots,
  dissolves, freezes and wedges (ice swells ~9%, splitting
  stone), and it travels — up pores, through air as vapor, into
  every seam. Buildings die wet.
ROOT: chemistry / water's guest list is nearly everything;
  physics / freezing water must expand.
THREAD: mountains (frost wedging carves ranges with the same
  chisel), roads (potholes are freeze-thaw farming), ships
  (rust never sleeps — why bridges are painted forever).
ASKED-AS: leak roof gutter damp mold basement flooding waterproofing caulk siding drip

ESSENCE: everything breathes with temperature — warm swells, cold
  shrinks, and a long structure moves inches with the seasons;
  restrain the movement and it becomes force enough to buckle
  steel.
ROOT: physics / heat is motion — warmer atoms jostle wider, so
  the whole must widen.
THREAD: bridges (the toothed joints your tires thump are
  breathing room), cooking (the stuck jar lid freed under hot
  water — the lid swells first), power lines sagging on summer
  afternoons.
