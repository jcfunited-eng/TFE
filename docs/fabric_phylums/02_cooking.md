# 02 COOKING — physics and chemistry wearing an apron
Joe's own example of an "easy" field — and he's right: a dozen
essences carry nearly all of it.

ESSENCE: heat always moves from the hotter thing to the cooler
  thing, three ways — touch, flow, and glow (pan, oven air, broiler).
ROOT: physics / disorder wins.
CANNOT: a dish cannot cool the pan that is heating it. Nothing
  cooks by being near something colder than itself.
THREAD: weather (wind is the kitchen's convection at planet size),
  the body (fever, sweat), engines (all cooling systems).
ASKED-AS: stove burner frying grill toast warming radiator soup pot simmer campfire

ESSENCE: cooking proteins is one-way — heat unravels their folded
  shapes and the shapes never refold. (~60-70°C for eggs and meat)
ROOT: physics / disorder wins — unfolding is downhill, refolding
  is a staircase chance will not climb.
CANNOT: no un-cooking. No un-curdling. A tough steak cannot be
  made rare again.
THREAD: medicine (fevers past ~42°C kill by cooking the body's own
  proteins), sterilizing (boiling kills by the same one-way door),
  the fabric (a minted cannot-be, kept).
ASKED-AS: overcooked rubbery raw chicken fish scrambled hardboiled ruined dinner firm chewy

ESSENCE: browning is chemistry with a price of admission — the
  flavor-making reaction between proteins and sugars starts only
  above ~140°C and only where the surface is dry.
ROOT: chemistry / reactions need their threshold energy.
CANNOT: no browning in boiling water (capped at 100°C). No crust
  on a wet surface — the water must leave first, at 100°C, before
  the surface can climb to 140.
THREAD: engineering (activation thresholds everywhere), the
  discovery machine's lesson (some answers need a minimum heat of
  asking before they exist at all).
ASKED-AS: sear golden grill crispy soggy pale roast toast caramel color steak bread

ESSENCE: water at a boil is a thermostat stuck at 100°C — more
  flame makes faster bubbles, not hotter water.
ROOT: physics / changing form eats energy — the extra heat is spent
  turning water to steam, not raising temperature.
CANNOT: nothing boiled can pass 100°C (at sea level). No amount of
  impatience raises a simmer's ceiling.
THREAD: mountains (lower pressure, lower ceiling — recipes break at
  altitude), pressure cookers (raise the lid's pressure, buy a
  higher ceiling), steam burns (the stored energy comes back out on
  your hand).
ASKED-AS: pasta simmer kettle bubbles pot stove altitude eggs potatoes faster hotter waiting

ESSENCE: oil and water refuse each other; an emulsion is a forced
  truce needing a mediator whose molecule loves both sides (egg
  yolk, mustard).
ROOT: chemistry / like dissolves like.
CANNOT: no stable mayonnaise from oil and water alone. Every
  unmediated mixture separates — the refusal always wins in the end.
THREAD: soap and cleaning (soap IS the mediator between grease and
  water), medicine (cell walls run on the same oil-water refusal),
  diplomacy (UNSURE, but Joe will smile: truces need mediators).
ASKED-AS: salad dressing vinaigrette shaking separated split sauce butter whisk vinegar hollandaise layers

ESSENCE: salt moves water without touching it — water crosses
  toward the saltier side through any wall that passes water but
  not salt.
ROOT: chemistry / mixing wins where walls allow it.
CANNOT: a salted eggplant cannot keep its water. A brined bird
  cannot stay dry inside.
THREAD: medicine (IV drips must match blood's saltiness or cells
  burst or shrivel), oceans (why river fish die at sea), farming
  (over-fertilized roots burn by losing water backward).
ASKED-AS: pickles cucumber marinade juicy soggy curing jerky sweating vegetables turkey soak

ESSENCE: fermentation is hired labor — tiny living things eat
  sugar and pay rent in acid, gas, or alcohol (bread, beer, yogurt,
  kimchi).
ROOT: farming+life / living things eat and excrete by their kinds.
CANNOT: no fermentation in a sterilized jar. The workers must be
  alive; too much heat, salt, or alcohol kills the workforce — beer
  cannot ferment past the yeast's own tolerance.
THREAD: medicine (gut flora, antibiotics as targeted layoffs),
  farming (compost), money (compound interest is fermentation's
  arithmetic, UNSURE as essence — flagged as a maybe-thread).
ASKED-AS: sourdough rise wine vinegar pickles bubbling starter dough cheese sauerkraut brewing

ESSENCE: rising is trapped gas expanding when heated — yeast's
  exhalations, beaten-in air, or baking soda's chemical burp,
  caught in a stretchy net of dough.
ROOT: physics / warm gas takes more room; chemistry for the burp.
CANNOT: no rise without a net — overworked or netless batter lets
  the gas walk out. No rise after the net cooks stiff: the oven's
  first minutes are the whole ballgame.
THREAD: flight (hot-air balloons are bread that never sets),
  volcanoes (magma's dissolved gas is the burp at mountain scale).
ASKED-AS: cake flat dense fluffy muffin pancake souffle collapse bubbles airy loaf sank

ESSENCE: a knife works by concentration — modest force onto a
  vanishing edge becomes enormous pressure.
ROOT: physics / pressure = force ÷ area.
CANNOT: a dull edge cannot be pushed into cutting — widen the area
  and the same hand delivers a bruise, not a cut.
THREAD: martial arts (strikes land through small surfaces), nails
  and needles, ice skates (pressure melts the ice underfoot),
  engineering (why cracks spread from sharp corners).
ASKED-AS: sharp blade chopping tomato slicing sharpening scissors razor axe needle nail heel

ESSENCE: taste is a balance of five voices — salt, sweet, sour,
  bitter, savory — and correction works by opposition: acid cuts
  fat, salt mutes bitter, sweet rounds sour.
ROOT: the body / tongues report chemistry in five channels.
CANNOT: no removing a flavor once dissolved — only opposing it.
  An over-salted soup cannot be un-salted, only rebalanced or
  diluted.
THREAD: music (chords balance voices; correction by adding, not
  erasing), medicine (antidotes oppose, they rarely remove), the
  fabric's drain law (what cannot be removed can still be
  rebalanced into the possible).
ASKED-AS: seasoning lemon sugar vinegar bland fix dish sauce lime honey brighten mellow
