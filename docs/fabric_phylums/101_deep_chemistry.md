# 101 DEEP CHEMISTRY — what substances do
Middle floors under file 03: how fast a reaction goes, where it
stops, and how one substance is told from another. Still essences,
never data; every entry carries its cannot-twin.

ESSENCE: a buffer is a pair kept side by side — a weak acid and its

ESSENCE: oxidation is losing electrons and reduction is gaining

ESSENCE: burning, rusting and breathing drop the same fuel to the

ESSENCE: a catalyst works by holding both partners in one exact

ESSENCE: a settled reaction is busy, not stopped — traffic runs at

ESSENCE: push a settled reaction and it leans away from the push —

ESSENCE: reactions happen where particles meet, so the rate rides

THREAD: cleaning (the last stain costs more than all the rest),

ESSENCE: heat does not push every molecule over the threshold, it

ROOT: chemistry / every reaction has a price of admission (03) —

THREAD: the body (fever as a deliberate few degrees), crowds (a

ESSENCE: every solvent has a ceiling for every guest at every

ESSENCE: past the ceiling the extra must leave, and it leaves in

ESSENCE: a gas has no shape and no size of its own — it takes the
