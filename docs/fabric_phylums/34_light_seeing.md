# 34 LIGHT AND SEEING — the fastest thing, and the organ that edits it
Optics and photography: what light does, and what the brain does to
light's report before you are allowed to see it.

ESSENCE: light travels dead straight until something bends it; a lens
  is bending made deliberate — light slows in glass, slowing at an
  angle turns it, and a curved surface turns every ray toward one
  meeting point.
ROOT: physics / light is the charge-wave, and any wave turns where
  its speed changes.
CANNOT: no seeing around a corner without a bend or a bounce. No
  lens that focuses without slowing light somewhere — the slowing IS
  the lens.
THREAD: eyeglasses (a correction hung in front of the eye's own
  lens), telescopes and cameras (the same trick at both ends of
  scale), mirages (hot air bending light is a lens nobody built).
ASKED-AS: blurry vision nearsighted farsighted magnifying spectacles straw underwater crooked pool prism binoculars projector

ESSENCE: color is the wave's stride length — red long, violet short —
  and pigment owns no light: paint looks red by swallowing every
  other stride and refusing red back.
ROOT: physics / waves; a stride length is a wave's signature.
CANNOT: pigment cannot add light — mixing paints deepens the
  swallowing, which is why every paintbox stirred together makes
  mud, never white.
THREAD: stage lighting (mixed LIGHT sums toward white — the reverse
  arithmetic of paint), printing vs screens (ink subtracts, pixels
  add), autumn (green's pigment dies first and the refusals beneath
  show through).
ASKED-AS: rainbow crayons markers watercolor blending brown leaves fall foliage tv rgb dye

ESSENCE: a shadow is a report — the edge maps the blocker's outline,
  the length tells the light's height, the direction points away
  from the source; geometry you can read backward.
ROOT: light's straight travel — blockage casts geometry.
CANNOT: no shadow without a denied straight path; no reading one
  without knowing where the light stood.
THREAD: sundials (the day told by a stick), a Greek who sized the
  whole Earth from two towns' noon shadows, eclipses (the moon's
  shadow crossing us on schedule), craters measured from orbit by
  shadow length alone.
ASKED-AS: evening stretched tall short midday sun angle silhouette shade groundhog clock profile

ESSENCE: the eye is a camera — lens, pupil for aperture, a screen of
  detectors — but what you experience is the brain's edit: image
  flipped upright, blind spot painted over, every eye-jump smoothed
  away.
ROOT: the mind / perception is construction, not reception.
CANNOT: you cannot look AT your own blind spot — the editor fills
  it before you arrive. No unedited view is available to anyone,
  ever.
THREAD: optical illusions (the editor caught mid-guess), courtroom
  witnesses disagreeing in good faith (each saw their own edit),
  cameras trusted over memory for exactly this reason.
ASKED-AS: trick hallucination magic dress debate blue gold moon horizon bigger glimpse notice

ESSENCE: a wave cannot report detail much smaller than its own
  stride — visible light's stride is a few ten-thousandths of a
  millimeter, so no light microscope at any price shows an atom.
ROOT: physics / waves add — features finer than the wave blur into
  the wave itself.
CANNOT: no seeing atoms with light — the limit is the wave, not the
  glassmaker. No telescope out-resolving its own width.
THREAD: electron microscopes (hire a shorter-strided wave to see
  deeper), bats and sonar (sound's long stride blurs fine prey the
  same way), radio dishes built huge because long waves need wide
  eyes.
ASKED-AS: germs bacteria virus tiny zoom magnification fuzzy cells smallest lab squint

ESSENCE: a mirror does not swap left and right — it reverses front
  and back; you read the depth-flip as left-right because you
  imagine having walked around to face yourself.
ROOT: bounce law — each ray leaves at the angle it arrived, so each
  point maps straight back out.
CANNOT: no single mirror shows you what others see of you — that
  view is a walk-around, and the mirror only pushes depth through
  itself. Mirror writing un-flips only in a second mirror.
THREAD: gloves (a left cannot be worn right — a depth-flipped
  object), molecules with mirror twins the body treats as different
  keys, printers' plates carved backward on purpose.
ASKED-AS: reflection selfie weird photo ambulance lettering shirt logo reading letters turned around

ESSENCE: a photograph is light summed over a chosen slice of time —
  the exposure — and the slice is the style: a thin slice freezes
  the hummingbird, a fat one turns a waterfall to silk.
ROOT: light carries energy and collectors add it; the picture is
  the total.
CANNOT: no photograph of an instant — zero time collects zero
  light. Every photo is a duration wearing a moment's face.
THREAD: star photography (hours of patient adding shows what no eye
  can, because eyes cannot sum), long exposures of city streets
  where walking people vanish (too brief to deposit themselves).
ASKED-AS: shutter speed blurry motion night dark grainy sports crisp action trails headlights streaks

ESSENCE: air scatters short strides far more than long — blue
  bounces around the whole sky, so the sky is blue wherever you
  look; at sunset the path through air is long, the blue is spent
  en route, and the survivors are red.
ROOT: scattering law — obstacles much smaller than the wave favor
  short strides steeply (fourth power of the stride).
CANNOT: no blue sunset through clean air. No blue sky without air —
  the moon's daytime sky is black.
THREAD: far mountains turning blue (miles of scattering stacked
  up), fog lamps running red-orange (the strides that survive),
  thin smoke curling blue off an ember but grey from the exhale
  (droplets grew past the stride).
ASKED-AS: sunrise orange pink horizon dusk dawn haze pollution evening glow color overhead

ESSENCE: brightness thins with distance — twice as far means a
  quarter as bright, because the same light must paint a sphere
  four times as large. (falls as 1/r²)
ROOT: geometry — a sphere's skin grows as the square of its radius;
  gravity fades by the same law (physics).
CANNOT: no lantern lights a valley. No distance without dimming —
  and no hiding range: dimness betrays how far.
THREAD: astronomy (a star's distance read from its dimness once its
  true wattage is known), camera flash (the friend twice as far
  goes four times as dark), campfire warmth obeying the same
  square.
ASKED-AS: flashlight candle across headlights lamp faint room wifi signal weaker speaker volume
