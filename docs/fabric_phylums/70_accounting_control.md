# 70 ACCOUNTING AND CONTROL — the laws money must obey on paper

ESSENCE: every entry has two sides — what a thing cost and where
  the value went; the books balance or they are wrong.
ROOT: mathematics / the universe keeps its books.
THREAD: physics (conservation wearing a suit), law (contracts have
  two parties), farming (a harvest is a transfer, not a creation).
ASKED-AS: double entry debit credit journal ledger balance books posting bookkeeping

ESSENCE: a transaction belongs to the period in which it was
  earned or incurred, not the period the cash moved.
ROOT: law agreements / obligations bind when made.
THREAD: farming (the harvest belongs to the season it grew),
  contracts, taxes.
ASKED-AS: accrual cutoff period close month end matching deferred prepaid revenue expense timing

ESSENCE: revenue is earned when the promise is kept, not when the
  money arrives or the order is signed.
ROOT: law agreements / a promise binds on performance.
THREAD: trade (a sale is a completed exchange), shipping, law.
ASKED-AS: revenue recognition earned invoice shipped delivered contract booking sales when

ESSENCE: whoever approves a payment must not be whoever makes it,
  and neither may be whoever records it.
ROOT: people together / unwatched power drifts toward its holder.
THREAD: law (judge and prosecutor), sports (no player referees),
  security.
ASKED-AS: segregation duties approval payment fraud internal control authorization two people

ESSENCE: every figure must be traceable back to the document that
  caused it, through an unbroken chain nobody can quietly edit.
ROOT: evidence / a claim without provenance is a rumor.
THREAD: proof (a chain is only as sure as its weakest link),
  computing (append-only records), law (chain of custody).
ASKED-AS: audit trail support documentation journal entry adjustment who changed evidence traceability

ESSENCE: a balance is only believed when a second, independent
  record agrees with it.
ROOT: evidence / one witness is a story, two are a fact.
THREAD: navigation (two fixes make a position), measurement,
  science.
ASKED-AS: reconciliation bank statement match clearing outstanding checks variance tie out balance

ESSENCE: attention is spent where the risk is, not evenly — the
  accounts that can hurt most and are watched least get looked at
  first.
ROOT: chance risk / exposure is size times likelihood.
THREAD: medicine (triage), insurance, engineering (inspect the
  loaded members).
ASKED-AS: risk based materiality sampling audit scope exposure threshold significant accounts assessment

ESSENCE: an estimate is a claim about the future written in ink;
  it must state its method and be revisited when the future
  disagrees.
ROOT: evidence / a guess declared is honest, a guess hidden is not.
THREAD: weather (forecasts carry confidence), engineering
  (tolerances), medicine (prognosis).
ASKED-AS: allowance reserve accrual estimate bad debt depreciation useful life provision judgment

ESSENCE: money owed to a government is held in trust, not owned —
  it was never the company's to spend.
ROOT: law agreements / holding is not owning.
THREAD: law (fiduciary duty), trade, custody.
ASKED-AS: sales tax payroll withholding trust fund remittance filing deadline penalty state nexus

ESSENCE: a filing is a promise with a date on it, and the date is
  part of the promise.
ROOT: law agreements / a term is a term.
THREAD: farming (planting windows), law, deadlines everywhere.
ASKED-AS: filing deadline return state local quarterly annual extension late penalty compliance

ESSENCE: a system of record is one place — when two systems both
  claim to be true, the difference between them is the truth
  nobody has.
ROOT: evidence / two authorities are no authority.
THREAD: navigation (one compass), law (one original), computing
  (one writer per value).
ASKED-AS: integration erp sap interface sync source of truth master data duplicate systems feed

ESSENCE: access is a promise about who may act, and it must be
  provable after the fact, not merely claimed at the door.
ROOT: evidence / an unrecorded permission cannot be audited.
THREAD: law (signatures), security, the body (biometrics are
  identity worn).
ASKED-AS: access control login identity biometric authentication permission role who did it security
