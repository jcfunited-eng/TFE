# 81 STORIES AND GENRES — the kinds of made telling
A story is a machine for carrying change. A genre is the label on
the box telling you which machine is inside.

ESSENCE: a story is change with a cause — something is different
  at the end, and the difference was brought about by what came
  between. Events that merely follow in time are a list.
ROOT: story and drama (27) / attention tracks change, drops steady.
CANNOT: no story without a difference between first page and last.
  No story from after-this alone — something must make the next.
THREAD: evidence (36: after is not because), keeping knowledge (40:
  a chronicle lists, a history explains).
ASKED-AS: what makes a story plot boring nothing happens change ending beginning

ESSENCE: conflict is the engine — a want with something in its
  way. Take the obstacle out and events keep happening while the
  story stops; a wish granted on page one closes the book there.
ROOT: this file / change needs a cause, and a wall is the commonest.
CANNOT: no story without something opposed. No tension in a want
  that nothing resists.
THREAD: strategy (23: no contest without two sides), sports (08:
  the mismatch empties the seats), physics (01: a push needs a wall).
ASKED-AS: conflict tension stakes problem obstacle boring no drama villain goes wrong

ESSENCE: a genre is a promise about the kind of change coming.
  Open a mystery and you are owed an answer, a romance and you are
  owed the pair; a work is weighed against the promise it made.
ROOT: law and agreements (17) / the cover is an offer, taken up.
CANNOT: no judging a work outside the promise it made. No promise
  broken free of charge — pay for it, or pay off something better.
THREAD: money (16: a label on a package is a warranty), cooking
  (02: a menu sets what the plate will be measured against).
ASKED-AS: genre kind of book what to expect disappointed cover promise category rules

ESSENCE: comedy runs on a gap that closes safely — expectation and
  outcome pull apart, and nothing that truly matters is lost. The
  same fall is comedy in a clown suit and horror in a hospital.
ROOT: beauty, play and humor (41) / a laugh marks a safe breakage.
CANNOT: no comedy where real loss lands. No laugh from a gap that
  never closes — a mismatch left standing turns into unease.
THREAD: play (41: rough play works only inside a trusted frame),
  medicine (06: pain in a body under real threat is not funny).
ASKED-AS: comedy funny why we laugh joke too soon offensive humor safe silly

ESSENCE: horror runs on a threat that cannot be bargained with,
  and it works by withholding — the half-seen thing beats the shown
  thing, since a mind fills a gap from its own stock of fears.
ROOT: the mind (20) / a mind completes a picture from what it has.
CANNOT: no horror from a threat you can talk to. No fear survives
  full sight — show the monster whole and it becomes a costume.
THREAD: strategy (23: the enemy you cannot read frightens most),
  seeing (34: less light, more invention).
ASKED-AS: horror scary movies monster fear suspense dread not showing creepy frightening

ESSENCE: romance runs on the obstacle between two who belong
  together — pride, distance, family, timing. The story is the
  wall, not the pair; pull the wall down and the story is over.
ROOT: this file / a story is a want held against a wall.
CANNOT: no romance without an obstacle. No story left standing
  after the last wall falls — which is why the wedding ends it.
THREAD: people together (21: what is hard to reach is held dear),
  negotiation (22: a deal grips only while it can still fail).
ASKED-AS: romance love story will they wont they obstacle ending together boring

ESSENCE: tragedy runs on a flaw that cannot be cut out of the
  strength — the nerve that makes them worth watching is the one
  that ends them. Fix it and you have deleted them, not saved them.
ROOT: this file / a person is one shape, not separable parts.
CANNOT: no tragedy from bad luck alone — a falling tile is only
  misfortune. No tragic flaw that could be removed, the rest kept.
THREAD: medicine (06: a drug's cure and its harm share one path),
  engineering (04: the stiffness that carries the load cracks).
ASKED-AS: tragedy sad ending doomed flaw hero downfall fatal mistake why fail

ESSENCE: mystery withholds an answer that certainly exists and
  sends the reader hunting. The contract is strict: all that was
  needed sat on the page in time, findable by anyone who saw.
ROOT: story and drama (27) / who knows what runs the machine.
CANNOT: no mystery without an answer already in existence. No
  solution from evidence never shown — a late twin breaks it.
THREAD: evidence (36: a finding must be reachable from the record),
  games (08: a puzzle with no fair path is a cheat).
ASKED-AS: mystery whodunit detective clues twist unfair guessed ending solve puzzle

ESSENCE: poetry pays in compression — fewer words carrying more,
  with the sound doing part of the carrying. Put it into plain
  prose and you keep the report and lose the poem.
ROOT: sound, rhyme and song (68) / beat and silence are load-bearing.
CANNOT: no poem that survives a full paraphrase. No compression
  without work handed over to the reader.
THREAD: mathematics (11: a formula says a paragraph and says it
  once), music (10: the tune is not a summary of the tune).
ASKED-AS: poetry poem what makes it hard to read meaning rhyme sound words

ESSENCE: character is shown by what someone picks when picking
  costs. Description is free and buys nothing — a narrator's "she
  was brave" cannot earn what no paid choice has earned.
ROOT: meaning and ritual (26) / talk is free, choice is paid.
CANNOT: no character built out of description. No revealing choice
  that costs the chooser nothing.
THREAD: people together (21: the interview is a mask, the crisis a
  face), law (17: conduct outweighs testimony).
ASKED-AS: character development flat believable telling showing actions describe person real

ESSENCE: voice is the sense of one particular person speaking —
  what they notice, what they skip, the rhythm they fall into. It
  is why two honest tellings of one event are not the same telling.
ROOT: being something (79) / a self shows in its pattern of choices.
CANNOT: no voice without choices that could have gone another way.
  No two voices out of one set of preferences.
THREAD: music (10: two players, one score, two performances),
  craft (29: the maker's hand shows in the made thing).
ASKED-AS: voice style writing sounds like same story told differently narrator personality

ESSENCE: every telling is cut for a listener. The same day told to
  a child, to a court, and to a friend gives three tellings, and
  all three can be honest; what moves is the leaving out.
ROOT: language (09) / a saying is aimed, and aiming selects.
CANNOT: no telling without leaving things out. No single telling
  that serves every listener — aim at all and you reach none.
THREAD: teaching (30: one law explained two ways to two learners),
  law (17: testimony is answers to the questions asked).
ASKED-AS: telling same story different audience child court friend leaving out honest simplify
