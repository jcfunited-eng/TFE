# 134 DEEP SLEEP, STRESS AND HABITS — the daily machinery
Living machinery holds why a body must sleep and what its
emergency setting is for. This file holds the machinery a person
actually operates every day: when sleep is possible, what stress
does over a year rather than a minute, and why nearly every
attempt to change a behaviour fails in the same few ways. Where
the evidence is genuinely unsettled the entry says UNSURE.

ESSENCE: two separate systems decide whether you can sleep. One is a
  pressure that builds from the moment you wake and only discharges in
  sleep. The other is a clock that opens and closes a window regardless
  of that pressure. Sleep happens where high pressure meets an open
  window, and misery is what happens when they disagree.
