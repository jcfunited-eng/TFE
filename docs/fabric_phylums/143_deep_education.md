# 143 DEEP EDUCATION — teaching that works
File 30 holds the laws of knowledge moving between heads. This file
holds the trade built on top of them: what a lesson must do to land,
what reliably fails while feeling like it is working, and the standing
war between what is cheap to examine and what is worth knowing.

ESSENCE: how well somebody does during a lesson and how much they keep
  are two different quantities, and the arrangements that raise the
  first often lower the second. Smooth, blocked, well-supported practice
  looks like excellent teaching all week and is gone by the holidays.
ROOT: the mind / memory is strengthened by the work of getting at it, so
  removing the work removes the strengthening along with the difficulty.
THREAD: fitness (training load felt against fitness gained), farming (a
  crop forced early), medicine (a symptom eased while the disease runs).
ASKED-AS: went well in the lesson forgot everything later method looks good practice hard easy

ESSENCE: the feeling of knowing tracks how easily something came to
  mind, not whether it will come back tomorrow. Rereading, highlighting
  and watching a fluent explanation all raise that feeling and change
  the memory hardly at all — which is why learners abandon the methods
  that work and defend the ones that fail them.
ROOT: the mind / a judgement about your own memory is made from the ease
  of the moment, because nothing else is available to consult.
THREAD: evidence + measurement (a reading from the wrong instrument),
  medicine, sports.
ASKED-AS: felt like i knew it blanked exam reread notes highlighting confident revision studied wrong

ESSENCE: a test is not a measurement of learning, it is an act of
  learning. Pulling an answer out changes what is stored more than
  putting it in again does — and what is pulled out gets stronger while
  the near-misses that were pushed aside during the search get weaker.
ROOT: teaching / the travelled path widens; here the traffic is the
  retrieving, so it is the route to the answer that is being built.
THREAD: the mind, sports (drilling one move), music.
ASKED-AS: quizzes help remember testing improves memory practice questions forgot other parts exam revision

ESSENCE: the gap between visits should be set by how long the knowledge
  has to last. To hold something for a week, review it in a day; to hold
  it for a year, leave it a month. And the gap has to be long enough
  that getting it back takes real work, because the work is the whole
  mechanism.
ROOT: teaching / memory strengthens most when pulled back near the edge
  of loss, so the useful gap is the longest one that still succeeds.
THREAD: fitness (rest between sessions as part of the training),
  farming, medicine (a dose interval matched to the body's clearing).
ASKED-AS: how often review spacing schedule gap between study sessions weeks before exam forgetting

ESSENCE: practising one kind of problem twenty times teaches the method
  and hides the hardest part of the job, which is working out which
  method this is. Mix the kinds and every question begins with that
  decision — slower, more errors, and far better when the questions
  arrive unlabelled.
ROOT: this file / the training must contain the difficulty the real
  occasion will contain, and in blocked practice the choosing has been
  removed.
THREAD: sports (drills against open play), medicine (a diagnosis
  presented against a patient walking in), driving.
ASKED-AS: mixed practice one type at a time exercises chapter which method to use problems

ESSENCE: guessing before being told beats being told, even when the
  guess is wrong. The attempt opens the question, exposes exactly what
  is missing, and makes the answer land in a hole shaped to receive it —
  so a few wasted minutes before the explanation are worth more than the
  same minutes after.
ROOT: teaching / new knowledge holds where it hooks onto old, and an
  attempt is what finds the hooks and shows where none exist.
THREAD: evidence (a prediction made before the reading), craft (an
  attempt before the demonstration), science.
ASKED-AS: try first before teaching guess wrong answer then explain productive failure struggle learning

ESSENCE: feedback aimed at the work continues the learning and feedback
  aimed at the person stops it. "This step is where it went wrong" sends
  the learner back into the problem; "you are careless" and "you are
  brilliant" both send them out of it and into thinking about
  themselves.
ROOT: this file / attention has one beam, and any comment about the
  person moves the beam off the task and onto their standing.
THREAD: the other mind (a correction that costs face gets fought),
  sports coaching, work.
ASKED-AS: feedback criticism praise personal comments marking work not the student coaching corrections

ESSENCE: put a grade and a comment on the same page and only the grade
  is read. The number ends the question, so the reasoning underneath it
  is never opened — which is why the most useful correction a teacher
  writes is routinely the least looked at thing in the room.
ROOT: the mind / a summary that answers the question closes the search,
  and a mark is exactly such a summary.
THREAD: evidence, work (an appraisal score beside the advice), money.
ASKED-AS: grades comments ignored students only look at mark feedback wasted marking effort improve

ESSENCE: the part of the mind that holds a problem while you work on it
  is astonishingly small — a few items at once. A beginner spends all of
  it on things an expert does without noticing, so what looks like
  slowness or inattention is a person whose hands are already full.
ROOT: the mind / the effortful channel is narrow, and everything not yet
  automatic has to pass through it.
THREAD: the mind, computing (a machine that runs out of working space),
  driving (a learner who cannot talk and steer).
ASKED-AS: overwhelmed too much at once beginners confused steps working memory cannot follow lost

ESSENCE: whatever a presentation wastes is subtracted from the thinking
  the learner had available. A diagram whose labels are in a key on the
  next page, a slide read aloud word for word, a worked example split
  across two screens — each makes the audience spend their small budget
  on assembly instead of on the idea.
ROOT: this file / the working space is small and shared, so the cost of
  a bad layout is paid out of the same account as the cost of the
  problem.
THREAD: design (a form nobody can complete), engineering (an instrument
  that hides the reading), writing.
ASKED-AS: slides text read aloud diagram labels confusing layout materials clearer teaching design distraction

ESSENCE: a beginner learns more from studying a completed solution than
  from attempting the problem; a practised learner learns more from
  attempting it and is actively slowed by being walked through. The same
  support is a ladder at one stage and a weight at the next.
ROOT: this file / a beginner has no method to retrieve and an
  intermediate does, so the help either supplies what is missing or
  blocks what would have been practised.
THREAD: fitness (assistance that must be withdrawn), craft
  (apprenticeship), medicine (a dose that becomes harmful).
ASKED-AS: worked examples show me how beginners try it yourself too much help hand holding

ESSENCE: help has to be taken away on purpose and on a schedule, because
  nothing removes it by itself. The prompt, the formula sheet, the
  starter sentence and the teacher standing nearby all become part of
  the performance, and a skill that has only ever been done with them
  has not been learned yet.
ROOT: teaching / the product of teaching is independence, and support
  that never withdraws is measuring itself.
THREAD: craft, medicine (rehabilitation ending when the aid does),
  parenting.
ASKED-AS: prompts help removed independent work alone without support fading scaffolding gradually cannot manage

ESSENCE: a subject is a chain, and a missing link does not slow a
  learner down, it stops them. The child who never secured place value
  cannot be helped through long division by patience or by trying
  harder, and every lesson built on the gap widens it while looking like
  a lesson about something else.
ROOT: teaching / new knowledge holds only where it hooks onto old, so a
  missing hook is not a difficulty but an impossibility.
THREAD: building (foundations before floors), medicine (treating a
  symptom whose cause is elsewhere), computing.
ASKED-AS: falling behind maths gaps basics missing cannot do it stuck years earlier foundation

ESSENCE: a wrong model is not replaced by a correct one, it is joined by
  it. Students pass the physics exam and still expect the ball to keep
  moving because it has force in it — one set of answers for school and
  another for the world, kept in separate books and never compared.
ROOT: the mind / a belief that has always worked is not overwritten by a
  belief that has only ever been examined.
THREAD: history (a correct finding with no mechanism refused), the mind,
  medicine.
ASKED-AS: still thinks heavier falls faster passed the test misconceptions science wrong idea persists

ESSENCE: what is learned in one dress does not walk to another. Skill
  with percentages in a maths lesson stays there when the same sum
  appears on a wage slip, unless the lesson deliberately crosses the
  costume several times and says out loud what the two cases share.
ROOT: the mind / memory is retrieved by resemblance, so knowledge is
  found again only where the situation looks like the one it was stored
  in.
THREAD: teaching, craft, sports (gym strength that does not show up in
  the game).
ASKED-AS: cannot apply it real life same maths different context transfer school knowledge useless situations

ESSENCE: there is no general skill of thinking that can be trained apart
  from something to think about. Reasoning, problem-solving and critical
  judgement are made of knowing a great deal about the subject — which
  is why the brain-training course, the logic module and the puzzle club
  improve the puzzles and nothing else.
ROOT: this file / performance is retrieved from stored cases, so
  judgement in a field is mostly a large store of that field's cases.
THREAD: the mind, chess and medicine (expertise as stored patterns),
  evidence.
ASKED-AS: critical thinking skills taught brain training transferable general problem solving knowledge subject content

ESSENCE: effort is a product, not a sum. A learner works when they
  believe they could succeed and when the success is worth something to
  them — and because the two are multiplied, a zero in either gives
  zero. The confident student who sees no point and the eager one who
  expects to fail both do nothing.
ROOT: the mind / effort is spent where spending moves the odds of
  something wanted, so both terms have to be present at once.
THREAD: the mind, work, sports.
ASKED-AS: not motivated cannot be bothered pointless subject expects to fail why should i effort

ESSENCE: students learn whatever the marks are given for. Say the essay
  is about argument and grade it on neatness and length, and by
  Christmas the class writes neat long essays with no argument — not out
  of cynicism but because the mark is the only honest statement of what
  was wanted.
ROOT: money / behaviour follows incentives, and in a classroom the mark
  is the incentive that arrives.
THREAD: work (targets), sports, law (the rule with no enforcement).
ASKED-AS: marks reward what counts assessment criteria students game grades essay length teaching aims

ESSENCE: telling a child they are clever hands them something to
  protect. From then on a hard task is a threat to the label, so the
  safe move is the easy task and the plausible excuse — while praise for
  the strategy and the persistence points at things that can be repeated
  on demand.
ROOT: the mind / a self-story defends itself, so a compliment about a
  fixed quality makes any evidence against it dangerous.
THREAD: the mind, sports, work.
ASKED-AS: telling children clever praise effort avoid hard tasks giving up easily confidence smart

ESSENCE: two learners can work equally hard for opposite reasons. One is
  trying to get better, so a mistake is information; the other is trying
  to look able, so a mistake is a disaster to be hidden. The second
  chooses easier tasks, conceals confusion, and stops when watched.
ROOT: this file / behaviour follows what the situation makes
  threatening, and a room organised around comparison makes error
  threatening.
THREAD: the other mind (face), work, sports.
ASKED-AS: afraid to ask questions look stupid competing classmates ranking mistakes hiding trying to learn

ESSENCE: everything worth learning is hard at the start, so every
  learner meets a moment of struggling. What decides the outcome is
  which explanation they are handed: that this is what learning feels
  like, or that people suited to it find it easy. Both are taught, and
  usually without a word being said.
ROOT: the mind / an ambiguous experience takes whatever interpretation
  is available, and the available one is supplied by the room.
THREAD: sports, craft, the mind.
ASKED-AS: not a maths person struggling means bad at it gave up hard normal

ESSENCE: reading is two things multiplied: turning marks into words, and
  knowing enough about the subject to make sense of them. A fluent
  reader who knows nothing about cricket understands nothing of a match
  report — so what looks like a comprehension problem is very often a
  knowledge problem wearing its clothes.
ROOT: language / a sentence is instructions for building a meaning out
  of the reader's own materials, so a reader without the materials
  builds nothing.
THREAD: language, teaching, keeping knowledge.
ASKED-AS: reading comprehension cannot understand text knows the words background knowledge strategies practice topic

ESSENCE: words are learned mostly by meeting them in reading, and
  reading needs words — so a small early gap grows on its own. The
  child with more words reads more easily, therefore reads more,
  therefore has more words, and by adolescence the two have separated
  further than any lesson can close.
ROOT: money / a lead buys the means of keeping the lead, run here in
  vocabulary instead of in cash.
THREAD: money (compounding), language, teaching.
ASKED-AS: vocabulary gap reading more words children behind list learning volume early years catch up

ESSENCE: writing is not the recording of a finished thought. It is the
  operation that finds out whether there was one — because a sentence
  has to commit to an order, a claim and a connection, and the places
  where the thinking was vague are exactly the places where the writing
  will not go.
ROOT: language / speech tolerates gaps that both sides fill in silently,
  and writing to an absent reader has nobody available to fill them.
THREAD: language, craft (a drawing that forces the dimensions), law (a
  clause that must say who owes what).
ASKED-AS: writing essay hard organise thoughts explain clearly know it but cannot write thinking

ESSENCE: fear of a subject is not only avoidance, it takes the
  equipment. Dread occupies the same small working space the problem
  needs, so the anxious student is genuinely worse at that moment — and
  then avoids practice, loses fluency, and gets a further reason to
  dread it.
ROOT: this file / the working space is small and shared, so anything
  else running in it is subtracted from the problem.
THREAD: the mind, sports (choking), medicine.
ASKED-AS: maths anxiety panic in tests freeze numbers scared bad at it working memory blank

ESSENCE: hours spent are not practice. Doing what you can already do,
  slightly wrong, without knowing it is wrong, produces twenty years of
  the same year — which is why long experience so often stops improving
  people, in medicine, in driving and in music alike.
ROOT: teaching / an error teaches only when it is found, and unmonitored
  repetition fixes whatever was being repeated.
THREAD: sports, medicine, craft.
ASKED-AS: years of experience not better practice makes perfect repeating mistakes coach feedback improvement

ESSENCE: the practice that improves people has four parts: a task just
  past what you can currently do, immediate specific correction,
  repetition of the failing piece rather than the whole, and somebody
  who can choose the next task. Ordinary work supplies none of them,
  which is why work alone rarely makes anyone excellent.
ROOT: this file / learning happens at the edge with feedback, so
  anything that keeps you away from the edge stops it.
THREAD: sports, music, medicine.
ASKED-AS: deliberate practice hardest part slowly repeat coach chooses drills improve skill edge ability

ESSENCE: expertise is not more knowledge, it is knowledge arranged
  differently. The expert sees one meaningful pattern where the beginner
  sees nine separate features — so the steps that carry the work have
  become invisible to the person best placed to explain them, and their
  explanation starts three moves in.
ROOT: teaching / practice merges steps into single units, and once
  merged the pieces cannot be inspected separately.
THREAD: teaching, craft, computing (a manual by the machine's designer).
ASKED-AS: expert cannot explain skips steps obvious to them beginners lost chunking teaching experienced

ESSENCE: a single example teaches the example. The learner attaches the
  idea to the surface of the case — the triangle drawn on that
  particular slant, the story problem about trains — and the only cure
  is several examples that agree in structure and differ in everything
  else, with the shared part said out loud.
ROOT: the mind / a pattern is extracted by comparing cases, so with one
  case there is nothing to compare and the whole thing is the pattern.
THREAD: language (a child extracting a rule from many sentences),
  science, craft.
ASKED-AS: one example only worked that case different numbers confused varied practice similar problems abstract

ESSENCE: the second a teacher answers their own question, the thinking
  in the room stops. Most wait less than a second — and lengthening it
  to three or four changes who answers, how long the answers are, and
  how many questions the class asks back, without any other change of
  method.
ROOT: the mind / retrieval takes time, and a silence filled by the
  teacher is a retrieval interrupted.
THREAD: teaching, the other mind (the turn handed over), music (a rest
  as part of the phrase).
ASKED-AS: wait time silence after question teacher answers own questions class quiet thinking pause

ESSENCE: questions to the room are answered by the four people who
  already know. The rest are neither found nor helped, so the check that
  matters is the one every learner must answer at once — cards, boards,
  a written line — which turns the teacher's guess about the class into
  a reading.
ROOT: evidence / a sample of volunteers is a sample of the confident,
  and a confident sample cannot report on the others.
THREAD: evidence + measurement, medicine (checking the patient rather
  than asking if they follow), work.
ASKED-AS: same students answer hands up nobody says anything checking understanding whole class questions

ESSENCE: one adult with one learner beats one adult with thirty by an
  enormous margin, and the reason is not affection or attention. It is
  that the tutor sees each error the moment it happens and can change
  the next thing said — a loop that closes in seconds instead of in a
  week.
ROOT: engineering / a control loop's power comes from how quickly it
  senses and corrects, not from how strong its output is.
THREAD: engineering (feedback control), medicine, sports coaching.
ASKED-AS: tutor one to one better private tuition class size expensive individual attention feedback loop

ESSENCE: shrinking a class helps, and it helps far less than everyone
  assumes and costs more than almost anything else a school can buy.
  Nothing about the teaching changes automatically when eight chairs are
  removed, and if the extra classes are staffed by weaker teachers the
  gain can go the other way.
ROOT: this file / what teaches is what the learner is made to do, and
  the number of chairs does not change that by itself.
THREAD: money (a spend measured against alternatives), engineering,
  medicine.
ASKED-AS: class sizes smaller classes better funding teachers ratio evidence expensive improvement schools

ESSENCE: among the things a school controls, who is standing at the
  front matters most and by a distance. And nothing on a hiring form
  predicts it well — not the degree, not the certificate, not the years
  served — so the only reliable evidence about a teacher comes from
  watching them teach.
ROOT: evidence / a proxy is useful only where it correlates, and these
  proxies do not.
THREAD: work (hiring), sports (scouting), evidence.
ASKED-AS: good teacher matters most qualifications predict hiring observation experience degrees quality difference schools

ESSENCE: a syllabus that must be covered forces the class onward before
  half of them have it, and the debt compounds. Six weeks later the room
  contains a group who cannot do the current work for reasons three
  topics back, and the teacher who stops to fix it falls behind for
  everybody.
ROOT: this file / a subject is a chain, so moving on over a gap
  guarantees a larger gap later.
THREAD: money (compounding debt), building, engineering.
ASKED-AS: curriculum coverage moving on too fast behind syllabus finish course mastery pace catching up

ESSENCE: a class must be paced for somebody, and whoever it is paced
  for, it is wrong for almost everybody else. That is not a failure of
  teaching, it is arithmetic — and every remedy, from setting to
  differentiated work to letting learners move at their own rate, is a
  different way of paying for it.
ROOT: mathematics / one speed cannot match a spread, so a single pace
  produces boredom at one end and drowning at the other.
THREAD: engineering (one setting for a range of loads), medicine (a
  standard dose), transport (a timetable serving many trips).
ASKED-AS: mixed ability class bored struggling same lesson pace setting streaming differentiation one speed

ESSENCE: dividing children by attainment helps the top a little, harms
  the bottom more, and lasts because the labels stick. The clearest
  evidence that it is not measuring ability is the birthday: in every
  country, the youngest children in a year are placed lower, judged
  worse and diagnosed more, for years.
ROOT: evidence / a measurement taken at one date confuses development
  with attainment, and an administrative line then makes the confusion
  permanent.
THREAD: evidence, sports (age-group selection), work.
ASKED-AS: streaming sets top group bottom born in summer youngest year held back labels

ESSENCE: most of the difference between children is built outside the
  school, and it grows fastest when school is closed. Schools are then
  judged on the total — the part they made and the part they inherited —
  which makes the results of a school largely a report on its intake.
ROOT: measurement / a figure that combines two sources cannot be read as
  a statement about one of them.
THREAD: evidence + measurement, public health, money.
ASKED-AS: school results intake league tables good school area holidays gap summer parents home

ESSENCE: learners have preferences about how material is presented and
  those preferences do not predict how they learn. What decides the
  right medium is the material — a map for geography, sound for music,
  a demonstration for a knot — so matching the presentation to the
  subject helps everybody and matching it to the learner helps nobody.
ROOT: the mind / a memory is stored in the form the material demands,
  and a preference is about comfort rather than about that form.
THREAD: evidence, design, music.
ASKED-AS: learning styles visual auditory kinaesthetic prefer pictures matching teaching myth evidence style

ESSENCE: attention is one beam, so studying alongside a conversation, a
  feed or a running screen is not doing two things — it is switching,
  and paying a restart cost each time. The work takes far longer, the
  material is stored more shallowly, and the person doing it feels fine.
ROOT: the mind / the effortful channel handles one thing at a time, and
  switching costs both time and accuracy.
THREAD: the mind, driving, work.
ASKED-AS: studying with phone music background distraction multitasking homework television concentration switching attention time

ESSENCE: fluency in the small pieces and understanding of the whole are
  not rivals; each buys the other. A child who must reconstruct every
  multiplication has nothing left for the problem, and a child who can
  recite them with no idea what they mean is stuck in a different place.
  The argument between drills and understanding is a false one.
ROOT: this file / the working space is small, so automatic pieces free
  the space that understanding needs.
THREAD: music (scales and phrasing), sports, craft.
ASKED-AS: times tables memorising drill understanding maths rote learning versus concepts basics fluency both

ESSENCE: a failing learner repeated through the same course at the same
  speed fails again, because the problem was never the amount of
  exposure. Remediation works when it finds the specific missing piece
  and teaches that — which requires diagnosis, and diagnosis is the part
  almost never funded.
ROOT: this file / a chain is broken at one link, so treatment has to be
  aimed at the link rather than at the chain.
THREAD: medicine (diagnosis before treatment), engineering (fault
  finding), computing.
ASKED-AS: repeating the year extra lessons same again catch up diagnosis specific gap intervention failed

ESSENCE: nothing is learned in a room that cannot be settled, so the
  management of thirty people is not a separate matter from teaching —
  it is the precondition. And it is bought with routines established in
  the first weeks, not with volume or with punishment, which are what
  the loss of it looks like.
ROOT: the mind / attention is a shared budget, and a room in disorder
  spends all of it on the room.
THREAD: the mind, power, sports (a team's standards set early).
ASKED-AS: classroom behaviour discipline noisy class control routines shouting punishment new teacher order

ESSENCE: young people work for adults they believe are on their side.
  The same lesson from somebody they think has given up on them lands as
  nothing, which is why the relationship is not a nicety layered over
  teaching — it is the channel every part of the teaching arrives
  through.
ROOT: the other mind / a message is weighed by who sent it, so the same
  words carry different force from different mouths.
THREAD: the other mind, medicine (the alliance that carries treatment),
  sports coaching.
ASKED-AS: teacher believed in me hated the subject relationship students respect gave up on him

ESSENCE: exams exist to make claims about strangers comparable — that is
  the whole product, and it is genuinely valuable. The price is that
  everybody must be taught the same things in the same order and at the
  same time, so the instrument that makes fairness possible also fixes
  what may be taught.
ROOT: measurement / comparison requires a common scale, and a common
  scale requires a common thing being measured.
THREAD: measurement (a shared standard as a treaty), law, money.
ASKED-AS: national exams standardised same syllabus fair comparison universities choose freedom teach curriculum

ESSENCE: teaching is aimed at the shape of the test, not at its content.
  Multiple-choice papers produce teaching for recognition; essay papers
  produce teaching for structure; a practical exam produces practicals.
  So changing the paper is the single strongest lever anyone outside a
  classroom has, and it works whether or not it was meant to.
ROOT: this file / learners work for the marks, so teachers work for the
  marks, and the paper is where the marks are defined.
THREAD: work (targets reshaping the job), law, sports (rules changing
  how a game is played).
ASKED-AS: teaching to the test multiple choice exam format changed coursework practical washback reform

ESSENCE: as more people hold a qualification, the jobs that used to want
  it want the next one up. Nothing about the work has changed; the paper
  is a filter, and a filter that everyone passes has stopped filtering —
  so the years of study rise and the position bought with them does not.
ROOT: money / a positional good's value is its rarity, so producing more
  of it dilutes exactly what it was worth.
THREAD: money (dilution), work, sociology (a race for position).
ASKED-AS: degree needed for everything masters now inflation qualifications more school same jobs paper

ESSENCE: what is cheap to examine drives out what is worth learning.
  Facts, procedures and short answers can be marked in bulk and defended
  on appeal; judgement, taste, the framing of a question and the
  willingness to sit with a hard problem cannot — so they vanish from
  the record first and from the teaching soon after.
ROOT: measurement / what cannot be counted is absent from the accounts,
  and what is absent from the accounts stops being managed for.
THREAD: measurement, money, work (a job graded on what is countable).
ASKED-AS: exams test memory not understanding creativity judgement cannot be marked cheap assessment worth learning

ESSENCE: proving anything in education is unusually hard. The effect
  runs through a person over years, everything is tangled with
  everything, the volunteers are unusual schools with unusual staff, and
  the result is measured on a test that may not be the point — which is
  why promising findings shrink as the trials get bigger and better.
ROOT: evidence / an effect measured at the end of a long chain carries
  every disturbance along that chain, and the enthusiasts run the first
  trial.
THREAD: medicine (a drug trial and its later replications), evidence,
  money.
ASKED-AS: does it work research education studies evidence pilot worked scaled up disappointing trials

ESSENCE: a class of children is a one-shot experiment on people who only
  get that year once. Which is why the trade cannot follow the ordinary
  rule of trying things and seeing — the cost of a failed cohort is paid
  by the cohort, and it cannot be refunded.
ROOT: right + wrong / an experiment on people needs consent and a way to
  make good, and here the subjects are children who have neither.
THREAD: medicine (a treatment given while the evidence is thin), law,
  right + wrong.
ASKED-AS: experimenting on children new curriculum trying methods cohort lost year cannot repeat risk

ESSENCE: a smooth, confident, beautifully designed lecture is rated
  higher by the people in it and learned from less. The polish removes
  the effort, and the effort was the learning — so the ratings report
  how the hour felt rather than what was carried out of it.
ROOT: this file / ease is read as understanding, so a performance that
  removes difficulty raises the feeling and lowers the result.
THREAD: evidence + measurement, work (an appraisal built from what is
  easy to observe), theatre.
ASKED-AS: great lecturer students loved it learned nothing ratings feedback forms clear explanation entertaining

ESSENCE: work sent home is practice of something already understood, or
  it is nothing. Where the learner can already do it, the spacing and
  the retrieving are worth a great deal; where they cannot, the hour
  produces a wrong method practised alone, a parent's answers, or an
  argument.
ROOT: this file / repetition without correction fixes whatever was
  repeated, and at home there is nobody there to catch it.
THREAD: teaching, family + lives, sociology (a childhood address as an
  inherited asset).
ASKED-AS: homework useful pointless parents helping quiet place practice new topic set every night

ESSENCE: put four people on one task with one mark and one person does
  it. Group work teaches when each member owes something visible and
  separately checked — and it is not a way of covering more ground with
  fewer staff, because the coordination costs more than it saves.
ROOT: people and power / a duty owned by everybody is owned by nobody,
  so a shared mark removes the individual consequence completely.
THREAD: people and power, work, sports (a position that can hide a
  passenger).
ASKED-AS: group project one person did everything shared mark unfair teamwork classroom groups accountability

ESSENCE: the value of taking notes is almost all in the deciding — what
  matters, what is an aside, how this connects to that. Which is why a
  full transcript helps less than a rough summary you made yourself, and
  why handing out the complete slides removes the most useful thing the
  listener was doing.
ROOT: teaching / selecting and restating are retrieval and organisation,
  and a supplied record performs both of them for you.
THREAD: keeping knowledge, language (writing forcing a commitment),
  work.
ASKED-AS: taking notes copying slides handouts given out recording lecture summary own words useful

ESSENCE: a fact and a procedure are built by different work. A date, a
  word or a formula is laid down by being retrieved again and again at
  spreading intervals; a method is laid down by being carried out on
  varied cases with correction. Treat either as the other and the
  practice quietly does nothing.
ROOT: the mind / storing something and being able to do something are
  different changes, so the activity producing one does not produce the
