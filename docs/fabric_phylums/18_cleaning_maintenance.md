# 18 CLEANING AND MAINTENANCE — the standing argument with disorder
Joe's own example: order versus entropy. Physics ruled that mess
wins on its own; this whole field is the wage paid, forever, to
hold that verdict off. Nothing here is glamorous and all of it is
load-bearing.

ESSENCE: left alone, everything drifts toward mess — dust
  settles, edges dull, weeds return; order is not a state but an
  upkeep, bought and bought again.
ROOT: physics / disorder wins — this field is that law's bill.
THREAD: cooking (the kitchen dirties at the speed of its use),
  the body (living IS continuous self-repair), farming (an
  untended field walks back to wilderness), law (rules
  unenforced decay the same way — UNSURE as law, sure as
  pattern).
ASKED-AS: house chores endless clutter garage yard entropy laundry piles again

ESSENCE: dirt is matter out of place — soil in the field is
  wealth, on the carpet it is filth; cleaning never destroys
  matter, it re-sorts it to where it belongs.
ROOT: physics / matter is conserved — cleaning moves, never
  erases.
THREAD: pollution (the factory's "away" is someone's river),
  money (a cost cut is often a cost moved — UNSURE), tidying (a
  drawer is where out-of-place goes to be in place).
ASKED-AS: dust vacuum bag trash garbage dump recycling wiping sweeping mop bin

ESSENCE: grease and water refuse each other, and soap is the
  paid mediator — one end of its molecule holds water, the other
  holds fat, so grease that water cannot touch rides away inside
  soap's escorts.
ROOT: chemistry / like dissolves like; cooking / the emulsion
  truce — same treaty, different table.
THREAD: cooking (egg yolk is mayonnaise's soap), medicine (soap
  kills germs by dissolving their fatty coats — handwashing beat
  plagues before anyone knew why).
ASKED-AS: dishes detergent shampoo oily pans stains laundry suds bubbles scrubbing

ESSENCE: wear is slow disassembly — every use rubs a little off,
  and parts made to touch slowly eat each other; most machine
  death is surfaces meeting, and oil's entire job is to keep
  moving parts from ever quite touching.
ROOT: physics / friction taxes all motion; disorder wins.
THREAD: the body (cartilage is the body's bearing, and it
  wears), rivers (water filing mountains — a canyon is wear),
  stone stairs dished by centuries of feet, money (depreciation
  is wear written in the ledger).
ASKED-AS: brakes tires engine squeaking grinding lubricant replacing shoes soles hinges

ESSENCE: most failures announce themselves early and quietly —
  the hairline crack, the damp patch, the new noise — so
  scheduled looking buys the cheap repair instead of the dear
  one.
ROOT: wear is gradual, so warnings exist; the arithmetic of
  small-now against huge-later.
THREAD: medicine (the checkup — tumors and cracks obey one law),
  navigation (the position fix catching dead reckoning's drift),
  money (reconciliation is inspection of a ledger).
ASKED-AS: car service oil change smoke detector roof gutters little problems catching

ESSENCE: a skipped repair is a loan from the machine at ruinous
  interest — the leak rots the beam, the rotted beam shifts the
  wall; damage recruits damage, and "someday" pays for all of it
  at compound rates.
ROOT: money / interest — the same arithmetic, pointed at your
  house; wear feeding wear.
THREAD: medicine (the ignored symptom), the body (a limp
  reshapes the knee, the knee the hip), debt itself (file 16
  warned: compounding aims both ways).
ASKED-AS: procrastinating putting off fixing worse later expensive neglect delaying snowballs

ESSENCE: a tool is intention given a shape — the screwdriver's
  tip, the wrench's jaw deliver a hand's force exactly where and
  how the job requires; the wrong tool spends the same effort
  mostly on damage.
ROOT: physics / force concentrated (the knife's law,
  generalized); design as a decision made once, in metal, for
  all later hands.
THREAD: cooking (the knife — the kitchen's edition), surgery
  (the scalpel is intention at its sharpest), language (the
  precise word is the right tool; the almost-right one mangles
  what it grips).
ASKED-AS: hammer drill stripped screw diy toolbox makeshift improvising breaking gadget

ESSENCE: prevention beats cure by orders of magnitude — paint
  against rust, flossing against the root canal, the vaccine
  against the ward; keeping a thing whole costs a sliver of
  restoring it broken.
ROOT: the compounding law above — failure cascades, prevention
  does not.
THREAD: medicine (clean water saved more lives than all
  surgery), law (the careful contract against the lawsuit),
  farming (weeds pulled young).
ASKED-AS: checkup insurance sunscreen seatbelt cheaper dentist exercise habits backups maintenance

ESSENCE: refined metal is held above its resting state — ore is
  metal's home, and oxygen works patiently to marry it back;
  rust is burning without flame, and every bright blade or
  painted bridge exists only because renewal outpaces return.
ROOT: chemistry / oxygen bonds greedily; physics / downhill
  wins.
THREAD: the cut apple browning (its own slow oxygen fire), the
  body (the same oxygen that powers us ages us — UNSURE at
  depth), histories (iron empires survive as stains in the
  soil).
