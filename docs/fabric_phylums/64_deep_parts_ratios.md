# 64 DEEP PARTS AND RATIOS — the mathematics of pieces
Counting after the thing is cut, shared, compared, or scaled.
Every entry carries its cannot-twin.

ESSENCE: a fraction is a division paused — three-quarters IS three
  divided by four, held mid-air; the bar is a division sign in
  disguise.
ROOT: mathematics (11) / counting extended past the pebbles —
  three loaves among four needs a number no heap holds.
CANNOT: no zero below the bar — that is dividing by zero, and the
  contradiction fire (11) starts.
THREAD: cooking (half a recipe is the pause cashed in), money (a
  share is a division not yet performed), music (a quarter note).
ASKED-AS: fraction numerator denominator top bottom half quarter divide bar pieces pizza slices

ESSENCE: fractions add only when the pieces match — a half plus a
  third refuses until both become sixths: three and two, five in
  all; renaming changes the cut, never the amount.
ROOT: this file / a fraction is a paused division — only same-size
  pieces can be counted together plainly.
CANNOT: no adding tops across unlike bottoms — a half plus a third
  is never two fifths; cut a real pie and the recount law (11)
  convicts it.
THREAD: money (dimes and quarters become cents before adding),
  measuring (inches and centimeters, the same wall).
ASKED-AS: adding fractions common denominator different bottoms halves thirds sixths lcd same size

ESSENCE: "of" is times — a half OF a half is a quarter;
  multiplying by less-than-one shrinks: only part of a copy is
  laid down.
ROOT: mathematics (11) / multiplying is counting by copies — here
  the copies are partial.
CANNOT: no growth from a factor below one — times a fraction
  CANNOT give more than it started with.
THREAD: cooking (half of half the batch), chance (11: chances
  multiply because they are fractions), money (stacked discounts
  only deepen).
ASKED-AS: multiply fractions of half smaller shrink why times less quarter recipe chance

ESSENCE: a ratio compares without measuring — two parts flour to
  one part water stays true in a teacup or a truckload; the "part"
  has no size, only a partner.
ROOT: mathematics (11) / counting is the first abstraction —
  number drops the things; ratio drops the size too.
CANNOT: no size inside a bare ratio — 2:1 alone cannot say cup or
  tanker; any size read off it was smuggled in.
THREAD: cooking (recipes scale because they are ratios), building
  (one blueprint, any size of house).
ASKED-AS: ratio parts proportion recipe scale double batch mix two one concrete map

ESSENCE: percent is a ratio in uniform — 35% is 35 per hundred,
  the same fraction dressed in hundredths so any two shares
  compare at a glance.
ROOT: this file / same-size pieces only — cut everything into
  hundredths once, compare forever.
CANNOT: no percent without its whole — "35%" alone names no
  amount; 35% OF something, or the uniform is empty.
THREAD: money (interest, tax, tips), weather (rain chances, 11,
  worn as hundredths), school (grades).
ASKED-AS: percent percentage hundred convert fraction decimal tip tax interest score out of

ESSENCE: every percent stands on its own whole — 50% off then 50%
  back on does not restore the price: the off stood on the old
  price, the on stood on the smaller one.
ROOT: this file / no percent without its whole — change the whole
  mid-sentence and the numbers no longer match.
CANNOT: no undoing a percent fall with the same percent rise —
  down 50 needs up 100; forced by arithmetic, not fine print. The
  commonest money trap.
THREAD: money (a market down 20 must climb 25 to be even), news (a
  200% rise of nearly nothing is nearly nothing).
ASKED-AS: percent off then sale discount increase back same price stock drop recover

ESSENCE: a rate is a ratio with a clock in it — speed, wages,
  interest: an amount per unit of something else; the "per" is the
  whole machine.
ROOT: this file / a ratio compares without measuring — a rate's
  second partner is a unit: per hour, per pound, per year.
CANNOT: no mixing rates across unlike units — miles per hour plus
  meters per second is nonsense; a Mars probe died of this exact
  wreck.
THREAD: money (interest is money's speed), navigation (dead
  reckoning is rate times time, 11).
ASKED-AS: speed rate per hour wage interest unit convert mph how fast miles

ESSENCE: an average is the pile shared out even — total divided by
  count — and the sharing erases the shape: one millionaire in a
  poor village lifts the average past every villager.
ROOT: this file / a fraction is a paused division — performed on a
  whole crowd at once.
CANNOT: no reading members off the average — a village averaging
  comfortable can hold not one comfortable person; the middle
  member (median) tells the other story.
THREAD: money (average wage versus what most are paid), school
  (one zero drags a term).
ASKED-AS: average mean median middle typical salary misleading skew outlier income household

ESSENCE: double a shape's edges and its skin grows four times, its
  inside eight — length, area, volume climb different gears from
  one stretch; big is not small enlarged.
ROOT: shapes (65) / similar shapes scale by one factor — area
  takes it twice, volume three times.
CANNOT: no giant as drawn — legs carry by cross-section (times
  four) what the body loads by volume (times eight); the bones
  snap. And no big body cooling like a small one — a child chills
  faster than an adult.
THREAD: cooking (a doubled roast is not doubled time), life (ants
  wear skeletons outside; elephants could not).
ASKED-AS: double size area volume scale square cube giant ant surface heat cool

ESSENCE: a measurement carries only the sureness it was taken with
  — a scale reading 71.24853 lies in five of its digits; extra
  digits manufacture certainty out of ink.
ROOT: mathematics (11) / no proof stronger than its premises —
  arithmetic cannot mint digits the instrument never earned.
CANNOT: no calculation more precise than its worst input — rough
  times exact is rough; the calculator's ten digits do not vote.
THREAD: money (a budget to the penny built on guessed thousands),
  science (the doubt written beside the digit: 71.2 ± 0.1).
ASKED-AS: rounding significant figures decimal places accuracy precision estimate digits calculator measurement error
