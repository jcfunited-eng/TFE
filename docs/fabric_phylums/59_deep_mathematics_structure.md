# 59 DEEP — the shapes inside number and space
Middle floors between counting and structure; every entry carries
its cannot-twin.

ESSENCE: numbers wrap on a dial — keep only the remainder by
  twelve and 13 o'clock is 1; a number's place on the wheel is all
  that survives. The ones digit is this in street clothes: the
  remainder by ten.
ROOT: mathematics / counting; division as repeated taking-away.
CANNOT: no wheel disagrees with the line — add then wrap, or wrap
  then add: forced to match.
THREAD: time (clocks, weekdays), music (the octave wraps pitch),
  computing (counters overflow onto it).
ASKED-AS: clock hours remainder leftover division wraps around weekday cycle repeats

ESSENCE: every whole number has a heartbeat, even or odd, and
  addition adds heartbeats: odd plus odd is even — the two
  leftover ones pair off. Parity is the simplest survivor of every
  addition.
ROOT: mathematics / counting — pairing pebbles two by two.
CANNOT: no sum of two odds is odd; no chain of additions reaches a
  parity the starts forbid.
THREAD: computing (the check bit catching one flipped wire), games
  (who moves last is settled before play).
ASKED-AS: even odd numbers adding leftover pair split half team switches

ESSENCE: primes are the atoms of number — every whole number above
  one is a pile of prime factors, and exactly one pile; 12 is two
  twos and a three, always, in any order.
ROOT: mathematics / multiplying is counting by copies.
CANNOT: no second decomposition — no whole number breaks into
  primes two different ways, ever.
THREAD: money (card-guarding codes bank on how hard un-piling is),
  chemistry (elements are matter's primes).
ASKED-AS: prime numbers factors divisible breaking down building blocks smallest composite

ESSENCE: more pigeons than holes forces sharing — ten letters into
  nine boxes and some box holds two, however cleverly you sort;
  the pair is certain before it is found.
ROOT: mathematics / counting — certainty before arranging.
CANNOT: no fitting n+1 things into n slots one apiece; no
  cleverness or luck evades it.
THREAD: the body (two people in any big city share a hair count),
  computing (every file-shrinker must collide).
ASKED-AS: socks drawer matching guaranteed birthday same room sharing overlap duplicates

ESSENCE: a number and its digit sum leave the same remainder by
  nine — ten leaves one, so each bundle counts at face value on
  the nine-wheel; summing digits catches any error that moves the
  remainder.
ROOT: this file / numbers wrap on a dial; mathematics / numbers
  are bundles.
CANNOT: no catching an error of exactly nine — swapped digits
  slip through, the sum unmoved.
THREAD: money (the bookkeeper's check on long columns), computing
  (checksums industrialize the trick).
ASKED-AS: casting out nines digit sum add digits check trick shortcut

ESSENCE: an invariant is a quantity the moves cannot touch — every
  reachable state agrees with the start on it; differ there and no
  run of legal moves joins the two.
ROOT: mathematics / the domino law — survives-every-step, pointed
  at impossibility.
CANNOT: no move-sequence crosses an invariant gap — dead, not
  unlikely; no search needed.
THREAD: physics (conservation laws), money (double-entry's forced
  zero), puzzles (the slider prize no shuffle could win).
ASKED-AS: puzzle impossible stuck cant be done conserved unchanged moves trick

ESSENCE: the moves that leave a thing looking untouched form a
  closed family — do one, then another, still in the family. A
  square has exactly eight: four turns, four flips, no ninth.
ROOT: mathematics / symmetry is sameness under change — the moves
  themselves become the object.
CANNOT: no combining two symmetries into a non-symmetry — closure
  is a wall; no ninth move for the square.
THREAD: music (transposing a tune is such a move), weaving (every
  repeating flat pattern lives in one of seventeen).
ASKED-AS: rotate flip square looks same kaleidoscope turns mirror wallpaper repeats

ESSENCE: strip a map to dots and links — a walk crossing every
  bridge once must enter each middle landmass as often as it
  leaves, so at most two corners may carry an odd bridge count.
ROOT: mathematics / counting; parity (this file) decides the walk.
CANNOT: no once-per-bridge walk with three or more odd corners —
  Königsberg had four: no route, ever.
THREAD: navigation (delivery routes, subway maps), the body
  (vessels as a graph the heart serves).
ASKED-AS: dots lines network route drawing without lifting pen bridges stroke

ESSENCE: corners meeting at a point must count to a full turn —
  the whole law of tiling. Hexagons bring 120 degrees, three make
  the turn; the pentagon's 108 won't divide 360, so it never
  carpets a floor alone.
ROOT: mathematics / counting applied to angle — the full turn is a
  bundle to fill exactly.
CANNOT: no gap-free floor of regular pentagons — the deficit is
  arithmetic, not lack of ingenuity.
THREAD: the living world (honeycomb — most room for least wax),
  craft (quilts find the same three floors).
ASKED-AS: tiles fit together floor hexagon honeycomb pentagon gaps seamless bees

ESSENCE: some infinities are bigger — the fractions, dense as they
  are, list off against the whole numbers one for one; the points
  of a line refuse: any endless list of decimals misses one built
  to differ from every entry.
ROOT: mathematics / infinity is a process; same size means
  matching one-to-one.
CANNOT: no complete list of the decimals — not unfinished,
  impossible; the diagonal escape beats every candidate.
THREAD: computing (more problems than programs — most lack
  solvers), physics (UNSURE — is the continuum real or a smooth
  fiction).
ASKED-AS: infinity bigger smaller sizes endless more fractions decimals countable forever

ESSENCE: recursion is a rule fed its own output — each rung built
  from the rung below. Growth lives here: interest on interest,
  branch on branch. So does paradox: aimed at itself, it chases
  its tail forever.
ROOT: mathematics / the domino law — recursion builds what
  induction proves.
CANNOT: no recursion stands without a floor — no base case, no
  meaning; a machine running it never answers.
THREAD: money (compounding), the living world (ferns, lungs — one
  rule at every scale), language (sentences nested in sentences).
ASKED-AS: loop repeats itself fractal compound snowball circular definition nested inside

ESSENCE: a rulebook rich enough for numbers can be turned to talk
  about itself — and there its reach ends: it casts true shadows
  it can never prove. Recursion's trick aimed at proof.
ROOT: mathematics / Gödel; proof turned on itself.
CANNOT: no patch closes the gap — every repair rich enough to
  matter casts new shadows; no rulebook certifies itself.
THREAD: computing (no program decides all halting), this corpus
  (every essence carries a cannot; this is proof's own).
ASKED-AS: godel incompleteness unprovable true limits knowing gaps self reference certainty
