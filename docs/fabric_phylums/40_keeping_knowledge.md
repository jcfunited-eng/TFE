# 40 KEEPING KNOWLEDGE — memory's war with time
Records, archives, libraries: how minds hand their contents past
their own deaths, and what the handing costs.

ESSENCE: writing is the first time-travel — a thought fixed in
  marks crosses centuries and speaks into ears not yet born; the
  dead still talk.
ROOT: physics / matter holds arrangement — order pressed into clay
  or ink stays until something spends energy erasing it.
THREAD: heredity (DNA is writing older than writers — recipes
  crossing generations with no mind attached), music (a score lets
  the dead conduct), law (a constitution is the dead still voting).
ASKED-AS: books old authors voices past diary journals ancestors library preserved wisdom scrolls

ESSENCE: copies fight decay — every medium rots (paper burns, ink
  fades, stone weathers, files corrupt), so the only lasting
  defense is many copies in many places; what exists once is
  already dying.
ROOT: physics / disorder wins — no vault outlasts time. Decay
  cannot be out-built, only out-bred.
THREAD: heredity (life's whole method — individuals die, copies do
  not), computing (the backup rule: three copies, two kinds of
  medium, one far away), money (one vault is no vault).
ASKED-AS: photos hard drive crash cloud family albums fire flood save irreplaceable heirloom

ESSENCE: the index makes the library — a million books without a
  catalog is a warehouse of paper; unfindable equals nonexistent,
  and the map of the shelves is worth a wing of shelves.
ROOT: mathematics + computing / search without order grows with
  the pile; search with order barely grows at all.
THREAD: the mind (tip-of-the-tongue — the fact is in there, the
  handle is lost), cities (a city without addresses cannot be
  delivered to), computing (the web is unusable without search —
  the index outgrew the library).
ASKED-AS: organize folders labels misplaced sorting tags cabinet lookup google drawer stack retrieve

ESSENCE: formats die and take their contents with them — knowledge
  is stored in a code (a script, a language, a pattern of holes or
  magnetism), and when the last reader of the code goes, the
  storage becomes decoration.
ROOT: language / marks mean nothing alone — meaning lives in a
  pact between writer and reader, and pacts expire.
THREAD: computing (the tape is fine, the tape drive is extinct),
  heredity (a genome with no living cell to read it is the same
  dead tape), music (notations lost mid-history — UNSURE how much
  went with them).
ASKED-AS: floppy disk vhs cassette obsolete hieroglyphics translating ancient converting software player outdated

ESSENCE: the keeper's paradox — organizing costs now and pays
  later, so it is always tempting to skip; the archive is built in
  hours nobody demanded and consulted in the crisis everybody
  remembers.
ROOT: the mind + money / the future is discounted — present effort
  against later gain loses the vote daily. The same tax cleaning
  and maintenance pay.
THREAD: farming (no planting at harvest time), medicine (the
  allergy noted today saves the stranger ten years out), insurance
  (premiums are this paradox priced in money).
ASKED-AS: procrastinating paperwork receipts taxes backlog someday heap desk mess clutter labeling chores

ESSENCE: before writing, memory wore armor — rhyme, rhythm, and
  story bind spoken knowledge so errors show themselves: a wrong
  word breaks the meter, and the breaking is the alarm.
ROOT: music + language / pattern constrains choice — in a rhymed
  line most wrong words cannot fit, so the form filters errors at
  the door.
THREAD: computing (the checksum is a rhyme — extra structure that
  makes corruption visible), teaching (the alphabet is sung, the
  tables chanted), law + ritual (oaths keep fixed words so any
  tampering is heard).
ASKED-AS: reciting songs lyrics stuck head jingle catchy nursery poems telephone game verses

ESSENCE: every record chooses — someone decided what was worth
  keeping, and what was kept becomes what is believed; an archive
  is not a window on the past but an argument about it.
ROOT: the mind + people together / keepers keep what serves,
  flatters, or interests them; the rest was equally real and is
  equally gone.
THREAD: histories (the whole deferred field stands on this line),
  evidence (armor the bombers by the holes in the ones that
  returned and you armor the wrong places — the missing data is
  the message), news (every camera points somewhere, and pointing
  is a choice).
ASKED-AS: school written winners biased textbooks propaganda museums untold forgotten stories victors slant

ESSENCE: forgetting is a feature — keep everything and the useful
  drowns under the true; what to discard is a decision, and a
  memory that cannot release cannot serve.
ROOT: the index law inverted — every kept item taxes every future
  search; the fabric's drain law wearing an archivist's coat.
THREAD: the mind (the man who could not forget drowned in detail —
  normal forgetting is triage), law (sealed records and spent
  convictions — societies legislate forgetting so lives can
  restart), computing (caches evict, logs rotate — machines
  learned it too).
ASKED-AS: hoarding deleting old emails photos decluttering letting go perfect recall inbox overload
