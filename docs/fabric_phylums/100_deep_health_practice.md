# 100 DEEP HEALTH PRACTICE — keeping bodies working
File 06 holds the body's laws. This one holds the practice built on
them: what actually keeps people alive, in the order it matters.
Nothing here replaces a trained pair of hands.

ESSENCE: it is cheaper and better to stop a disease than to treat one,
  by a wide margin. The case that never happens costs nothing, needs
  no hospital, and leaves no damage behind. Almost all of the gain in
  human lifespan came from prevention, not from cures.
ROOT: medicine / the body heals fast and crooked — a disease survived
  still leaves its patch; money (a cost avoided beats a cost
  recovered).
THREAD: cleaning + maintenance (the service that stops the breakdown
  nobody then remembers), engineering (maintenance against repair),
  money.

ESSENCE: the largest health intervention in history was not a drug. It
  was separating drinking water from sewage. Clean water and a working
  drain have saved more lives than every medicine combined, and where
  they fail the old diseases return within days.
ROOT: medicine / infection compounds — a water supply is the fastest
  multiplier a germ can rent; building (the drain and the trap as
  medical equipment).
THREAD: building (drains, traps, sealed pipe), farming (irrigation and
  runoff carrying the same load), power (public works noticed only
  when they fail).

ESSENCE: a vaccine shows the body's defence force a harmless mugshot
  so it will recognise the real thing on sight. And the protection is

THREAD: chance + risk (multiplication below one), computing (a

ESSENCE: hands are the main vehicle. Twenty seconds with soap — which
  pries grease-coated germs off skin and floats them down the drain —
  cuts infection more than nearly anything else a person can do alone.
  It was resisted for decades because it accused the doctors.

ESSENCE: food poisoning is farming a crop you did not mean to grow.

ESSENCE: a fever, a cough, and a pain are the body's smoke, not its

ESSENCE: the same total amount of a drug does different things

ESSENCE: the survivors of a half-finished course are the toughest

ESSENCE: pain relief is bought, never granted. Strong painkillers slow

ESSENCE: of everything an untrained person can fix, the biggest is

  peeking, for ten unbroken minutes. For a limb that will not stop, a
  tourniquet high and tight — losing a limb is a distant second to
  losing the person.

ESSENCE: airway, then breathing, then circulation — a chain in which

  blocked. The order is not a preference; it is the plumbing's own

THREAD: survival, engineering (a process chain fails at its first
  broken stage and everything after it is wasted), computing (no point
  optimising downstream of a stall).

ESSENCE: shock is the delivery system failing — not enough blood
  reaching the tissues, from bleeding, a weak pump, or vessels gone
  slack. The signs run ahead of the collapse: pale cold clammy skin, a
  fast weak pulse, fast breathing, thirst, and confusion.

THREAD: engineering (a pump losing prime — the gauge reads fine until

ESSENCE: a burn keeps burning after the source is gone — heat held in
  the tissue drives the damage deeper for minutes. Cool running water
  for twenty minutes is the treatment. Ice, butter, and popped
  blisters each make it worse, in roughly that order of certainty.
ROOT: physics / heat flows down a gradient until the gradient is gone,

THREAD: cooking (the one-way door, applied to the wrong meat),
  building + materials, physics.

ESSENCE: a splint heals nothing. It stops the broken ends moving, and

ESSENCE: drowning is silent and fast. There is no waving and no
