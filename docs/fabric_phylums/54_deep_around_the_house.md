# 54 DEEP COLUMN — AROUND THE HOUSE
Middle floors under the house's small mysteries. Still essences,
never data; every entry carries its cannot-twin.

ESSENCE: air carries invisible water in proportion to its warmth, so
  warm damp air meeting cold glass is chilled past what it can hold
  and must set water down — window fog and mirror steam are the
  room's breath landing, coldest surface first.
ROOT: deep water (50) / heating evicts the dissolved air — the same
  law upside down: cooling evicts dissolved water.
THREAD: weather (dew, fog, every cloud), driving (the defroster
  answers with heat and dry air).
ASKED-AS: window fog mirror steam shower condensation glasses winter wipe drips bathroom

ESSENCE: doors stick in summer because wood never stops drinking —
  it swells through the damp months and shrinks as winter heating
  dries it back; door and frame both breathe, out of step.
ROOT: cooking / salt moves water without touching it — wood and damp
  air trade water toward evenness.
THREAD: craft (guitars cracking in winter rooms), building (decks
  laid with gaps between boards).
ASKED-AS: door stick summer swollen humid jam frame winter gap wood warped shave

ESSENCE: pipes bang because moving water is a ram — the column in a
  pipe carries real momentum, and a valve snapped shut stops it all
  at once, so pressure spikes and the pipe jumps; the bang is
  arrested hurry.
ROOT: physics / a thing keeps doing what it is doing unless pushed —
  and pays hard when stopped at once.
THREAD: engineering (dams keep surge tanks against this), driving
  (the seatbelt's gradual catch).
ASKED-AS: pipes bang knock washing machine valve thud plumbing wall clunk faucet shut

ESSENCE: the fridge's hum is a pump pushing heat uphill — from cold
  inside to the warm coils at its back, against heat's grain, paid
  in electricity; heat forever leaks back in, so the thermostat runs
  the pump in bursts — the cycling.
ROOT: physics / heat flows hot to cold — pushed backward only at a
  price.
THREAD: weather (an air conditioner is this with coils hung
  outside), the body (sweat buys the same uphill push with water).
ASKED-AS: fridge hum noise cycles clicks on off refrigerator warm back coils door open

ESSENCE: dust settles everywhere because air is a slow river of
  lint, skin, soil, and soot — grains too fine to fall fast ride
  every draft and land wherever air goes still; so the untouched
  shelf and the closed room gather it too.
ROOT: physics / disorder wins — everything sheds, and the shed
  spreads.
THREAD: weather (desert dust crossing oceans), astronomy (worlds
  condensed from settling dust).
ASKED-AS: dust everywhere where from shelf settle gray fuzz sunbeam vacuum closed room

ESSENCE: paint hardens from the surface inward, so a thick coat
  skins over and the skin becomes a lid — sealing its own wet middle
  in to sag, wrinkle, and stay soft; thin coats cure through, each
  dry layer gripping the next.
ROOT: deep water (50) / the first skin of ice is a lid — the same
  self-trap, in a can.
THREAD: cooking (the steak charred outside, raw within), craft
  (varnish, glaze, nail polish).
ASKED-AS: paint drips thick coat sticky tacky wrinkled second wait sag brush wall

ESSENCE: glue holds by touch and by teeth — flowed close enough to
  grip the surface molecule against molecule, and sunk into every
  pore so hardening leaves a thousand small anchors; clean, close,
  clamped is the whole recipe.
ROOT: craft / fit before force — glue is contact made permanent.
THREAD: medicine (the scab, and surgical glue copied from it),
  building (mortar — teeth first, chemistry after).
ASKED-AS: glue stick why holds tape superglue clamp wood dust grease bond fails peel

ESSENCE: zippers and velcro hold by many small grips sharing one
  load — hundreds of teeth or hooks, each weak, split a straight
  pull among all — while the slider and the peel visit the grips one
  at a time; opening is easy exactly where bursting is hard.
ROOT: building / cracks start at corners — unzipping and peeling are
  cracks run on purpose.
THREAD: craft (a seam fails stitch by stitch), the living world (the
  burdock burr, velcro's parent).
ASKED-AS: zipper stuck teeth velcro hooks loops rip open jacket why holds slider bag

ESSENCE: a battery is slow chemistry asked to hurry, and cold is a
  dawdle — chilled, its reactions thicken, so it can hold charge yet
  fail to serve it fast enough; the phone dead at forty percent in
  the snow wakes indoors with the charge still there.
ROOT: chemistry / heat is a hurry — read backward.
THREAD: driving (electric cars shedding winter range), the living
  world (cold-blooded animals slow at dawn).
ASKED-AS: battery cold dies fast phone winter car wont start percent drops warm pocket

ESSENCE: wifi is unseen light, and walls are its weather — every
  wall absorbs a share (concrete, brick, and watery things eat most;
  the faster high band dims quicker), while metal simply mirrors it
  away.
ROOT: cooking / heat moves by touch, flow, and glow — radio is
  glow's cold, long-waved kin.
THREAD: weather (rain fading the satellite dish), the sea (radio
  barely enters water; submarines run half-deaf).
ASKED-AS: wifi weak upstairs walls router signal bars drop basement microwave metal concrete slow
