# 13 OCEANS AND WEATHER — sunlight, redistributed
One engine (the sun), one substance in two costumes (air and
water), one spin beneath it all. Weather is the freight system
hauling heat from where it lands thick to where it lands thin.

ESSENCE: the sun heats the equator far more than the poles, and
  all weather is that imbalance being spent — every wind, storm,
  and current is heat in transit from surplus to shortage.
ROOT: physics / heat flows hot to cold; disorder wins.
THREAD: engines (every engine runs on a difference, not on heat
  itself), trade (goods flow surplus to shortage — UNSURE as law,
  sure as rhyme), cooking (a convecting pot is the sky in
  miniature).
ASKED-AS: tropics climate jet stream hurricane season air moving cold warm sky

ESSENCE: water resists changing temperature more than almost
  anything — the sea banks summer and pays it out in winter, so
  coasts stay mild while continental interiors swing wild.
ROOT: chemistry / water's molecules grip each other, and the grip
  eats energy before the temperature will move.
THREAD: cooking (the water bath that never scorches), the body
  (blood is the body's coastal climate — we are water-cooled),
  engineering (every radiator hires water's memory).
ASKED-AS: beach seaside inland desert lake breeze climate nights days warms slowly

ESSENCE: wind is pressure difference cashing out — air slides
  from where it is piled high toward where it is thin, harder
  when the difference is steeper.
ROOT: physics / pushed things move; disorder wins — differences
  level themselves.
THREAD: breathing (the chest makes a low and the world rushes
  in), drafts under doors (the house is a small weather map),
  crowds funneling through exits (UNSURE as law).
ASKED-AS: blowing breeze gust barometer front storm moving fast calm whistling flag

ESSENCE: the spinning earth bends every long journey — moving air
  and water curve right in the north, left in the south — so
  winds falling toward a low miss sideways forever: that spiral
  is a storm.
ROOT: physics / a thing keeps doing what it is doing — the ground
  turns beneath the traveler.
THREAD: gunnery (far shells must allow for the turning ground),
  navigation (long routes aim off), bathwater myths (too small,
  too brief — the bend needs distance).

ESSENCE: the moon pulls the near ocean harder than the earth, and
  the earth harder than the far ocean, so the sea bulges both
  ways — two high tides a day (~12.5 hours apart), doubled up or
  damped as the sun aligns or crosses.
ROOT: physics / gravity fades with distance — a tide is the
  DIFFERENCE in pull across a body, made visible.
THREAD: astronomy (the same difference-in-pull shreds moons that
  drift too close), farming (tide-flat harvests run on the moon's
  timetable, not the sun's).
ASKED-AS: beach tide moon high low sea level fishing boat harbor chart schedule

ESSENCE: a wave moves energy, not water — each parcel of sea
  rolls in a small circle and returns; the shape and the punch
  travel on, so a storm a thousand miles away delivers its blow
  to a sunlit beach days later.
ROOT: physics / a wave is a traveling disturbance, not a
  traveling substance.
THREAD: sound (air does not travel from mouth to ear; the shove
  does), stadium waves (every person keeps their seat),
  earthquakes (the ground's own version, same law).
ASKED-AS: surfing swell ripple shore crashing raft driftwood foam curl splash

ESSENCE: forecasts die of compound error — the laws are known,
  the starting point never fully is, and each small unknown
  roughly doubles every few days (UNSURE on the exact doubling
  time) until it owns the answer; past ~10 days weather is
  unforecastable in principle, not just in practice.
ROOT: mathematics / compounding — tiny causes get promoted, not
  averaged away.
THREAD: navigation (dead reckoning drifts by the same law), money
  (markets — same growth of small unknowns, UNSURE where the
  horizon sits), billiards (three bounces and aim is fiction).

ESSENCE: the ocean is the planet's plumbing — wind drives the
  surface loops, and cold salty water, being heavier, sinks and
  crawls the deeps for centuries; the Gulf Stream is a heat pipe
  keeping northwest Europe mild for its latitude.
ROOT: the sun-engine plus the spin plus water's density politics
  (cold sinks, salt sinks).
THREAD: the body (surface rivers and deep returns — blood again),
  heating systems (a radiator loop is a toy Gulf Stream).
ASKED-AS: england britain canada conveyor belt climate ice melting warm winters seas

ESSENCE: rain is distilled climbing — evaporation lifts pure
  water and leaves the salt behind (why the sea is salty and rain
  is fresh), and air made to climb cools (~1°C per 100 m) until
  it can no longer hold its water and lets go.
ROOT: physics / rising gas expands and cools; chemistry /
  evaporation abandons whatever was dissolved.
THREAD: cooking (a still is a kitchen-sized water cycle), farming
  (one side of the range green, the other brown, by this law
  alone), sweat (the body's evaporation engine, salt left on
  skin).
