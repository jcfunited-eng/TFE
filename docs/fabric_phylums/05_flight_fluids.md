# 05 FLIGHT AND FLUIDS — pushing on what pours
Air and water are the same physics at different thickness.
Everything that flies or floats deals with a partner that cannot
hold a shape — and always collects its toll.

ESSENCE: a fluid pushes on everything it touches, from all sides
  at once, and harder with depth — pressure is the weight of fluid
  above, shared as squeeze in all directions.
ROOT: physics / gravity, plus the fluid's inability to hold shape
  — it passes pushes along instead of resisting them.
CANNOT: a fluid cannot pull. Suction is a lie of grammar — the
  straw does not pull the drink; the atmosphere pushes it into the
  emptier pipe. Past ~10 m no pump on earth can "suck" water up.
THREAD: weather (a barometer weighs the sky), diving (every 10 m
  of sea adds an atmosphere), the body (blood pressure is pump
  plus depth).
ASKED-AS: drinking vacuum ears popping dam deep pool submarine siphon well squeeze crushed

ESSENCE: fluid moves only because pressure differs — every wind,
  current, breath, and heartbeat is fluid falling downhill from
  high squeeze to low.
ROOT: physics / a thing changes motion only when pushed — unequal
  squeeze IS the net push.
CANNOT: no flow between equal pressures, ever. No pump moves fluid
  except by first making a difference for it to fall down.
THREAD: weather (wind is the sky falling sideways toward low
  pressure), the body (breathing is the chest making a low; the
  heart makes the high), plumbing (a water tower makes the
  difference once; gravity keeps it).
ASKED-AS: vacuum cleaner straw fan draft hose syringe bellows blowing balloon deflating leak

ESSENCE: a fluid pushes up on anything in it with a force equal to
  the weight of fluid displaced — weigh less than the water you
  shove aside and you float. (Archimedes)
ROOT: fluids / pressure grows with depth — the push from below
  beats the push from above by exactly the displaced weight.
CANNOT: no shape saves a solid lump denser than the fluid. A steel
  ship floats only by wrapping air; holed, the wrapping fails and
  the law forecloses.
THREAD: cooking (ravioli rise when steam puffs them light), the
  body (fish trim a gas bag to hover; fat floats, muscle sinks),
  geology (continents float on hotter rock).
ASKED-AS: boat swimming ice cube helium balloon anchor lifejacket cork stone raft sinking

ESSENCE: in smooth flow, faster means lower pressure — honestly:
  the fluid sped up because pressure behind exceeded pressure
  ahead, and the low reading is that spent difference. (Bernoulli)
ROOT: physics / energy bookkeeping — pressure is stored push,
  speed is spent push; their sum along the stream holds.
CANNOT: no pressure drop appears from nowhere — something must
  first bend or squeeze the flow. And the schoolbook tale fails:
  parted streams owe no reunion — "longer path on top" explains
  nothing.
THREAD: weather (wind funneled between buildings speeds up and
  doors slam), harbors (two ships passing close pull together),
  the shower curtain leaning into the spray (UNSURE — still
  argued).
ASKED-AS: paper blowing atomizer perfume sprayer chimney whistling drafty windy alley gust breeze

ESSENCE: lift is deflection — a wing meets air at a slight tilt
  and bends the flow downward; the air, thrown down, throws the
  wing up, and the pressure difference across the wing IS that
  throw written in fluid. (one force, two descriptions)
ROOT: physics / no push without push-back; fluids / faster-lower
  along the bent flow — the same ledger read twice.
CANNOT: no lift without turning air downward. No wing works in
  vacuum. And nothing flies free — lift is bought with drag and
  paid in fuel, every minute aloft.
THREAD: sailing (a sail is a wing stood upright — beating upwind
  is lift), propellers and helicopters (wings on a carousel),
  baseball (spin bends the flow, the flow bends the flight).
ASKED-AS: airplane flying jet glider kite bird takeoff airfoil upside down paper dart

ESSENCE: tilt a wing too steep and the flow lets go — past a
  critical angle (~15°) air cannot follow the upper curve, tears
  away into churn, and lift collapses in a heartbeat. (stall)
ROOT: fluids / a flow can be bent only so fast — ask more turn
  than pressure affords and the flow divorces the surface.
CANNOT: no wing bullies air past the critical angle — pulling up
  harder past the stall gives LESS lift, the one place in the sky
  where instinct is backward. Recovery is nose down.
THREAD: sailing (the luffing sail is a stalled wing), swimming (a
  hand cupped too steep slips through), martial arts (yield to
  regain control — the same proverb).
ASKED-AS: airplane climbing steep drop plummet spin crash angle falling dive slow speed

ESSENCE: drag is the fluid's toll and it grows with the square of
  speed — double the speed: four times the push, eight times the
  engine power. (drag ∝ v²; power ∝ v³)
ROOT: physics / no push without push-back — shoving fluid aside
  means being shoved; the churned wake is the receipt.
CANNOT: no motion through fluid is free, and speed has no flat
  rate — every doubling costs eightfold, which is why cars have
  top speeds and raindrops land gently (terminal velocity is the
  toll's truce with gravity).
THREAD: cycling (the peloton is drag economics on wheels — hiding
  from the toll), swimming (records fall by shaving drag, not
  adding muscle), why fish and fast trains wear the same shape.
ASKED-AS: headwind gas mileage highway streamlined parachute skydiver wind resistance coasting bullet shape

ESSENCE: moving fluid is moving mass and hates sudden stops — slam
  a valve and the halted column spikes into a hammer blow; thick
  fluids resist even gentle stirring. (water hammer; viscosity)
ROOT: physics / moving mass keeps its punch — a pipe of water is a
  battering ram that happens to pour.
CANNOT: no moving column stops instantly without the spike — the
  momentum must cash out somewhere, and pipes burst to obey it.
  Honey cannot be stirred fast for free.
THREAD: the body (elastic artery walls swallow the heart's hammer;
  hardened ones pass it on), engineering (big pipelines close
  valves over minutes), the pipes banging when you shut a tap fast.
ASKED-AS: faucet clunk thud plumbing knocking ketchup syrup molasses pouring squeeze bottle radiator

ESSENCE: smooth flow is the exception on a leash — push fluid fast
  enough past a big enough thing and orderly layers shatter into
  self-feeding swirls no equation can track; only the averages
  stay lawful. (turbulence)
ROOT: fluids / momentum feeding back into the flow itself; UNSURE
  at depth — turbulence is an honest open wound in physics.
CANNOT: no keeping flow smooth at every speed — laminar has a
  ceiling, always. And no exact long forecast: swirls amplify
  differences below any instrument — the ignorance is the
  world's, not the forecaster's.
THREAD: weather (forecasts die near ten days because the sky is
  turbulence at planet size), rivers (rapids), smoke rising smooth
  then shattering, markets (calm churning into panic — UNSURE, but
  the shape rhymes).
ASKED-AS: bumpy flight seatbelt whitewater eddies chaotic swirling stream stirring coffee cream shaking

ESSENCE: everything that moves through fluid moves by throwing
  fluid the other way — propeller, jet, oar, fin, and wingbeat
  differ only in how much they throw and how fast. (momentum back
  = push forward)
ROOT: physics / no recoilless push.
CANNOT: no craft pulls itself along by pushing on itself — the
  throw must land on something outside. In vacuum only the rocket
  flies — it brings its own fluid to throw.
THREAD: squid and jellyfish (jet engines in flesh), rowing and
  swimming (every stroke hurls water backward), rockets (the same
  law with the river carried on board).
ASKED-AS: boat motor paddle kayak drone fan kicking treading helicopter blades outboard thrust
