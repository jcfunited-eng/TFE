# 88 DEEP MIND — how thinking actually goes
Not what a mind is made of, but how it runs: the guesses it ships as
sight, the store it rebuilds instead of replaying, and the small
dials that decide what actually gets done.

ESSENCE: the mind proposes the picture and the senses only answer
  where it was wrong. What you see is your own expectation, with the
  world's objections pencilled in.
ROOT: the world repeats itself, so most of the next moment can be
  written before it arrives.
CANNOT: no perceiving without a guess already running — a sense
  feeding a mind that expects nothing delivers noise. No inspecting
  the raw feed; the edit is finished before you get the picture.
THREAD: sport (the fielder runs before the bat connects), proofing
  (the word you meant is the word you read).
ASKED-AS: why i see what i expect brain fills in guess assume glance illusion

ESSENCE: the input is far poorer than the output. Sharp sight is a
  thumbnail wide at arm's length, there is a hole where the nerve
  leaves the eye, colour is a few crude bands. The seamless world is
  manufactured.
ROOT: senses are cheap samplers, and a sample is mostly gaps.
CANNOT: no gap left showing — the filling is automatic, so missing
  information never arrives labelled as missing. No telling which
  parts you sampled and which parts you made.
THREAD: film (still pictures and a fan), maps (the unsurveyed
  quarter drawn as confidently as the rest).
ASKED-AS: blind spot peripheral vision blurry edges colour how much do we actually see

ESSENCE: nothing is met fresh. A thing arrives, the store answers
  with the nearest match it holds, and that answer is what you
  experience — not the thing, the verdict on it.
ROOT: the store replies faster than the world can be examined, and
  the fast reply is the one that gets used.
CANNOT: no recognising what nothing in the store resembles — the
  genuinely new is not met as new; it is filed as the closest old
  thing, or missed entirely.
THREAD: medicine (a rare disease named as the common one it
  resembles), trades (another trade's tool looks like scrap).
ASKED-AS: recognise familiar looks like something else mistook it for never seen before

ESSENCE: membership is a slope, not a fence. A robin is a bird more
  than a penguin is; a chair without a back is nearly a stool. Every
  category has a solid middle and an argued edge, and the argument
  never ends because there is nothing there to find.
ROOT: the world varies smoothly and names come in whole units.
CANNOT: no true line on a slope — any line is drawn by someone for a
  purpose, so no dispute at the edge is settled by thinking harder
  about what the word really means.
THREAD: law (when a person counts as dead is legislated, not
  discovered), biology (one naturalist's species is another's five).
ASKED-AS: hot dog sandwich borderline edge case where do you draw the line counts

ESSENCE: to hold a concept is to hold a package of expectations.
  "Dog" is not a picture — it is: moves on its own, has an owner,
  may bite, will be hungry tomorrow. It earns its keep by what it
  saves you having to check.
ROOT: a mind keeps what pays forward, and only predictions pay.
CANNOT: no concept without expectations hanging off it — a label
  with nothing attached is a name on an empty box, and it cannot be
  wrong about anything, which is why it is worth nothing.
THREAD: engineering (a part number carries its whole spec), cooking
  ("batter" tells you what the pan will do).
ASKED-AS: what does it mean to understand a concept idea expect know about category

ESSENCE: there is no recording. A memory is rebuilt when asked, out
  of a few real fragments plus a lot of general knowledge about how
  such scenes go — and no join shows, because the machinery that
  builds is the machinery that inspects.
ROOT: keeping everything is unaffordable; keeping the gist and
  regenerating the rest is cheap.
CANNOT: no separating the kept part from the rebuilt part from
  inside. No remembering a scene without the era's furniture being
  quietly supplied by the builder.
THREAD: fossils (a whole animal drawn from four bones), law (two
  honest witnesses to one minute).
ASKED-AS: false memory remember wrong sure it happened details filled in argue happened

ESSENCE: every recall is a fresh write. The version you have just
  built is what goes back on the shelf, so a memory is dated at its
  last handling and not at the event — and the story told most often
  sits furthest from the day.
ROOT: what is rebuilt is what is saved; the shelf takes the copy.
CANNOT: no examining a memory without altering it. No returning to
  an untouched version — rehearsing a memory to protect it is the
  same act as editing it.
THREAD: photocopies (the copy of the copy of the copy), families
  (the Christmas tale improves annually).
ASKED-AS: memory changes over time retelling story got better every time i think

ESSENCE: the store has no front door. Nothing comes back when you
  ask for "everything you know" — things come only when something
  present resembles them. A cue is a key; without one you face a
  full cupboard with no handle.
ROOT: a store is reached by address, and resemblance is the only
  address a mind has.
CANNOT: no recall without a cue — not slow recall, none. No proving
  a thing is gone by failing to fetch it, since a better key
  tomorrow may open the same door.
THREAD: libraries (an unindexed archive is a warehouse), smells (one
  hallway hands back a whole childhood).
ASKED-AS: cannot remember it reminds me came back to me trigger jog my memory

ESSENCE: two stores, two kinds of failure. Facts have words and
  dates and go missing; skills have neither and stay. A man who
  cannot tell you his own age still rides the bike, and cannot tell
  you one thing about how.
ROOT: doing and describing are built by different work and kept in
  different places.
CANNOT: no handing a skill over in words — description moves facts
  only, so no skill crosses a gap without repetitions on the far
  side. No dating a skill; it holds no record of when it came.
THREAD: apprenticeship (the master shows because telling fails),
  swimming (the instructions are useless in the water).
ASKED-AS: never forget how to ride a bike cannot explain just know muscle memory

ESSENCE: a remembered answer and an understood one look identical
  until the question changes. Understanding is holding the machine
  that made the answer, so you can lose the answer and still produce
  it, and answer the version nobody asked.
ROOT: knowing a result is knowing one point; knowing the procedure
  is knowing all of them.
CANNOT: no testing understanding with the question that was taught —
  only with a new one. No understanding that keeps the answer after
  losing the machine; that is memory in a good coat.
THREAD: mathematics (a proof rebuilt versus a proof recited),
  cooking (a recipe followed versus a dish understood).
ASKED-AS: do i really understand or just memorised worked example new problem explain

ESSENCE: a learner who has learned holds less, not more. Twenty
  worked examples become one rule and the twenty are thrown away.
  The measure of learning is what you can now safely drop.
ROOT: a mind's room is fixed, so knowledge is paid for by squeezing
  and never by adding shelves.
CANNOT: no learning by accumulation — a store that only grows cannot
  be searched, so no pile of examples counts as learned until
  something has been discarded.
THREAD: writing (the rewrite that halves the length), pictures (an
  image kept as the rule for redrawing it).
ASKED-AS: too much to remember boil it down rule of thumb simplify get the gist

ESSENCE: what is learned comes welded to where it was learned.
  Traders who do lightning arithmetic at the market stall fail the
  same sums on paper; chess masters plan a holiday no better than
  anyone. The skill was never general.
ROOT: learning stores the surface along with the shape, because
  nothing at the time marks which is which.
CANNOT: no transfer without the same lesson met in two unlike
  settings — one setting, however long practised, yields a skill
  with the room still bolted to it.
THREAD: school (algebra passed and never used again), languages
  (classroom fluency struck mute at the airport).
ASKED-AS: good at it in class not in real life carry over apply skills elsewhere

ESSENCE: the master sees fewer things, not more, each one bigger. A
  board that is twenty-five pieces to a beginner is five shapes to
  him — and on a board of randomly scattered pieces his advantage
  vanishes. It was never a larger mind.
ROOT: the hands are small, so the only growth available is in the
  size of each grip.
CANNOT: no chunk without the years that built it — chunks cannot be
  issued by explanation. No expert advantage on material outside the
  ground the chunks were grown on.
THREAD: reading (a fluent eye takes words, not letters), music (a
  player reads a bar as one gesture).
ASKED-AS: how experts see it years of practice glance takes it in whole beginner

ESSENCE: an expert's map has walls drawn on it, and the walls were
  built from old failures under conditions that may since have
  changed. The beginner walks at one because nobody told him it was
  there, and now and then it has rotted through.
ROOT: the same store that supplies the fast right answer forbids the
  odd one.
CANNOT: no expertise without foreclosure — the speed and the
  blindness are one mechanism, so no keeping the quick answers while
  staying open to the strange one.
THREAD: industry (the outsider who did not know the price was
  fixed), sport (a technique no coach would have taught).
ASKED-AS: beginners luck outsider fresh eyes did not know it was impossible experts stuck

ESSENCE: most thinking is not rule-following. A doctor does not run
  the textbook; she is reminded of a patient from four years back
  and adjusts. Rules are the fallback when no case comes, and they
  are slower, coarser, and the only part that travels.
ROOT: a matched case still carries every detail the rule threw away.
CANNOT: no case reasoning without a stock of cases, so no shortcut
  past the years. No rule carrying the details it dropped, however
  carefully it is written.
THREAD: law (precedent outranks the statute in practice), cooking
  (adjusting by memory of a dish, not by the ratio).
ASKED-AS: this reminds me of when experience versus by the book rules similar case

ESSENCE: the hard half of an analogy is finding it, not using it.
  People are reminded by surface — a story about a war brings back
  other wars — while the match that would actually help lives in
  another field and is found only when pointed at.
ROOT: retrieval runs on resemblance, and resemblance is measured on
  the outside of things.
CANNOT: no retrieval by deep structure alone, so no reliable arrival
  of the right analogy from the field it lives in. No analogy safe
  to run without checking where the likeness stops.
THREAD: engineering (the burr and the hook), teaching (the example
  that helps only on same-looking problems).
ASKED-AS: like when analogy compare similar situation same as reminds me another field

ESSENCE: asked to test a rule, people go hunting for cases that fit
  it. Believing the new hire is good, you notice good work. The test
  that would settle it is the one never run, because running it
  means going looking for what would prove you wrong.
ROOT: a search must be pointed somewhere, and the belief is what
  points it.
CANNOT: no confirming a rule by collecting agreeing cases — a
  thousand that fit and one that does not leaves the rule dead, so
  no weight of evidence gathered this way is worth anything.
THREAD: science (the apparatus of controls exists for this), hiring
  (the candidate you liked interviews well).
ASKED-AS: looking for evidence that agrees prove myself right ignore what does not fit

ESSENCE: how often a thing happens gets judged by how easily an
  example comes to mind, and ease is bought with drama, recency and
  coverage. One shark outweighs the drownings; the crash outweighs
  the drive to the airport.
ROOT: counting is impossible inside a store that never kept a tally.
CANNOT: no frequency read off memory, so no true sense of a rare
  risk without an outside count. No unseeing a vivid case by
  learning the number — a number does not compete with a picture.
THREAD: insurance (premiums move after the hurricane, not before),
  news (chosen for rarity and read as commonness).
ASKED-AS: scared of flying shark attacks news makes it seem common how likely rare

ESSENCE: a number lands and every estimate afterwards is a walk away
  from it that stops too early. It works with numbers known to be
  meaningless — a spun wheel, a rolled die — which is the proof that
  no reasoning is involved.
ROOT: an estimate is made by adjusting from a starting value, and
  adjusting quits as soon as the answer looks plausible.
CANNOT: no estimate without a starting value. No discounting an
  anchor by knowing it is one, and no adjustment far enough — the
  walk halts on the anchor's side of the truth.
THREAD: shops (the crossed-out price is the product), courts (the
  sum asked for moves the sum awarded).
ASKED-AS: asking price first offer negotiation starting number guess estimate discount originally

ESSENCE: two separate questions — what is in there, and how much is
  in there. You can be certain a name is held without producing it,
  and certain you know a page you have only reread. The second
  question decides where the next hour goes.
ROOT: a mind must budget its own study, so it carries a rough gauge
  of its own contents.
CANNOT: no reading that gauge off the contents themselves, so no
  honest study plan built on a feeling of familiarity — which
  rereading manufactures for free.
THREAD: exams (the material that felt known and was not), shops (a
  business that does not know its own shelves).
ASKED-AS: tip of my tongue i thought i knew it felt familiar reread notes blanked

ESSENCE: confidence is built out of how smooth things are inside —
  how fast the answer came, how detailed the story is, how well the
  parts agree. None of that touches the world. A rehearsed wrong
  answer feels better than a fresh right one.
ROOT: a mind can only measure itself with itself, and what it
  measures is ease.
CANNOT: no accuracy read off confidence. No confidence built out of
  anything outside the head — which is exactly why practice raises
  it while the error rate stands still.
THREAD: courts (the certain witness convicts the innocent), medicine
  (the second opinion exists because of this).
ASKED-AS: how sure am i certain but wrong overconfident trust my judgement double check

ESSENCE: the click comes first and is not the thing. A clear
  lecture, a smooth page, a repair watched twice — all produce it,
  because the feeling is made of ease, and ease is what a good
  explainer manufactures. Almost everyone knows how a zip works,
  until asked to say.
ROOT: how easily a thing goes down is available at once; the machine
  itself is not.
CANNOT: no telling understood from watched-someone-understand from
  the inside. No verdict without producing the thing yourself, out
  loud or on paper, with the book shut.
THREAD: teaching (the nodding room fails the test), repairs (the job
  you have seen done and cannot do).
ASKED-AS: makes sense when he explains it cannot do it myself thought i got it

ESSENCE: a mind is a device for reducing surprise, and it feeds
  itself accordingly. Fully predictable material returns nothing and
  is felt as boredom; wholly unpredictable material returns nothing
  either and is felt as noise. Interest sits in the band between.
ROOT: attention goes where learning is still being made, because
  nowhere else pays.
CANNOT: no curiosity about a subject you know nothing whatever of —
  a gap must have edges before it can be felt. No holding interest
  in material that has stopped surprising.
THREAD: games (difficulty that tracks the player), music (a tune
  wholly new and one wholly known both slide past).
ASKED-AS: bored losing interest too easy too hard fascinating cannot put it down

ESSENCE: effort lives in the gap between what is wanted and what is
  expected. Close it from either end — stop wanting the thing, or
  become certain of it — and the effort stops. A prize you are sure
  of buys no work, and neither does one believed out of reach.
ROOT: effort is spent only where spending it moves the odds.
CANNOT: no motivation out of wanting alone — no drive without a
  believed link between your acting and the outcome. No lasting
  effort where the outcome arrives either way.
THREAD: work (a bonus everybody gets), sport (the game won by
  half-time is played badly by both sides).
ASKED-AS: motivation cannot be bothered why try hopeless pointless want it badly giving up

ESSENCE: a habit is a choice made once and filed under a sight, a
  time or a place. After filing it runs on the cue and not on the
  wanting — which is why the biscuit is in your hand at the same
  chair every evening, wanted or not.
ROOT: deciding is expensive and a cue is cheap, so the cheap thing
  takes the job over.
CANNOT: no habit without a cue that fires it, and no ending one by
  ending the wanting, since the wanting was never what ran it. Move
  the chair and the habit has lost its trigger.
THREAD: smoking (the cigarette belongs to the coffee), driving (the
  turn taken toward the old job on a Saturday).
ASKED-AS: bad habits breaking them same time every day automatic reaching for it triggers

ESSENCE: a feeling is a fast reading of what a situation does to
  what you want. One piece of news lands as anger, fear or relief
  depending on your stake in it — so the feeling reports the stake,
  not the event, and arrives before any reason for it.
ROOT: a slow judge dies before deciding, so the fast one runs first
  and the reasons catch up.
CANNOT: no emotion without something at stake — nothing is felt
  about a matter you have no interest in. No reading the world off a
  feeling; what you read off it is your own position.
THREAD: markets (one number, panic in one seat and joy in the next),
  sport (the same whistle heard from two stands).
ASKED-AS: why do i feel like this angry scared reaction before i thought about it

ESSENCE: a mood is a filter on the store. Low, you reach the low
  memories, and reaching them keeps you low; the past appears to
  have been bad all along. The evidence for the mood is produced by
  the mood.
ROOT: retrieval runs on resemblance, and your present state is part
  of the cue.
CANNOT: no fair survey of your own life from inside a mood — the
  view is filtered by the thing being judged. No mood-free rating of
  unrelated matters; a wet afternoon lowers the score of a life.
THREAD: medicine (a symptom diary kept during a bad week), money (a
  falling market makes every past decision look reckless).
ASKED-AS: everything feels bad today my whole life looks awful when down mood thinking

ESSENCE: the machinery that predicts the next second can be cut
  loose from the senses and run on invented input. That is what
  "what if" is — a rehearsal in the same equipment, at no cost and
  no risk, and fenced by the same model.
ROOT: a predictor that will accept made-up input has become a
  simulator.
CANNOT: no imagining outside your own model, so no free discovery
  from thought alone — a rehearsal returns only what the model
  already held. No surprise in the head of the kind the world gives.
THREAD: engineering (a simulation is worth its assumptions), chess
  (the moves seen ahead are the ones the player can hold).
ASKED-AS: imagine picture it in my head what if daydreaming rehearse plan ahead visualise

ESSENCE: at night the model runs with both doors shut — no senses
  coming in to correct it, no muscles to test anything against. So
  it drifts, and the story is stitched over the drift afterwards,
  which is why the absurd passes unremarked until waking.
ROOT: a predictor with no correcting input has nothing holding it to
  the world.
CANNOT: no checking inside a dream — the checker is offline, so the
  impossible cannot register as impossible. No dream faithful to the
  night's events, since nothing was measuring them.
THREAD: navigation (a ship steering by log alone walks off the
  chart), rumour (a story nobody can check).
ASKED-AS: dreams weird why did it make sense in the dream nightmares remember asleep

ESSENCE: hand a mind a scatter of events and it hands back a chain
  with causes, actors and a point. It will do this to random dots,
  to a run of coins, to a bad year — and the invented chain arrives
  with the same certainty as a found one.
ROOT: an explained world can be acted in and an unexplained one
  cannot, so an explanation is produced either way.
CANNOT: no leaving a set of events unexplained — "these things
  merely happened" is not a resting place a mind will hold. No
  telling a made story from a found one by feel, only by what it
  predicts next.
THREAD: history (a decade given its cause afterwards), illness (a
  diagnosis taken because the alternative is no story).
ASKED-AS: everything happens for a reason why did this happen to me making sense pattern

ESSENCE: once you know how it turned out you cannot get back to not
  knowing. The ending reaches into the memory of your own forecast
  and quietly rewrites it, so every outcome arrives looking like the
  one that was always likely.
ROOT: memory is rebuilt from the present, and the present now
  contains the answer.
CANNOT: no recovering a forecast after the result is in — not by
  honesty, not by effort. No fair grading of a decision from the far
  side of its outcome; only a record written before it can do that.
THREAD: sport (everyone saw the goal coming, afterwards), inquiries
  (the warning that was obvious only when read backwards).
ASKED-AS: knew it all along obvious in hindsight should have seen it coming looking back

ESSENCE: the part of you that explains your actions is not the part
  that chose them. Asked why, it answers at once and sincerely — and
  it answers just as fast when the real cause was a smell, a sound,
  or which shelf the thing sat on.
ROOT: deciding and explaining are separate jobs, and the explainer
  was not in the room.
CANNOT: no report of your own reasons that counts as evidence of
  your own reasons. No "I have no idea why I did that" from the
  explainer — it is not built to come back empty.
THREAD: shops (the right-hand pair chosen, the choice explained by
  the stitching), courts (a sincere account that is not what
  happened).
ASKED-AS: why did i do that my reasons explain my own choice made up afterwards

ESSENCE: nothing is felt as a total, only as a move from a mark. The
  same wage is a triumph or an insult depending on last year's, and
  a step down hurts about twice as much as the same step up pleases.
ROOT: a mind measures differences, and the mark it measures from is
  supplied by the situation rather than fixed.
CANNOT: no valuing an outcome without a reference point, so no
  choice that cannot be turned by moving the mark. No symmetry
  between a loss and a gain of one size.
THREAD: shops (a discount and a surcharge on one price), wages (a
  cut from the old rate versus a rise to the same figure).
ASKED-AS: pay cut hurts more than a raise compared to last year expected more losing

ESSENCE: "ninety survive" and "ten die" are one fact and two
  messages. People choose differently between them, and differently
  again when the same numbers are put as money kept instead of money
  lost.
ROOT: the mind works on the description it was handed, having no
  way to reach the fact underneath.
CANNOT: no neutral wording of a choice — every phrasing is a frame,
  so no way to lay out an option without steering. No checking your
  decision against the fact rather than the frame from inside.
THREAD: medicine (survival rates and death rates move consent), meat
  (ninety percent lean outsells ten percent fat).
ASKED-AS: same thing said differently survival rate wording changed my mind percentage phrasing

ESSENCE: what has already gone is counted as a reason to keep going
  — the half-eaten meal, the four years in the wrong course, the
  project everyone knows is dead. Stopping is the act that turns the
  spending into a loss, so stopping is what gets postponed.
ROOT: a loss becomes real only when it is booked, and the mind
  delays the booking.
CANNOT: no spent money buying anything except what it already
  bought. No decision improved by what cannot be recovered — the
  only live question is what happens next, and the past is not in
  it.
THREAD: business (the plant finished because so much went into it),
  cards (the money already in the pot is not yours).
ASKED-AS: already spent so much cannot quit now wasted the money too far in

ESSENCE: a plan is made by walking through the job in the head, and
  the walk is always the smooth one — no illness, no waiting, no rot
  behind the wall. So the estimate comes out short every time,
  including estimates by people who know this happens.
ROOT: imagination runs the model, and the model holds the steps but
  not the interruptions.
CANNOT: no honest schedule from inside the plan. No fixing it by
  care, or by a margin you also invented — only by the record of how
  long the last ten of these actually took.
THREAD: building (every large project late and over), study (the
  essay begun the night before, again).
ASKED-AS: how long will it take running late underestimated deadline slipped again plan schedule

ESSENCE: learning the right answer does not remove the wrong one. It
  lays a slower layer on top, and underneath the old one still fires
  first — which is why a trained physicist still feels that the
  heavy ball must fall faster.
ROOT: a mind adds and overrides; it has no way to delete what was
  built by experience.
CANNOT: no unlearning by being told — no correction that removes the
  intuition it corrects. No fluent right answer without the wrong
  one being held down underneath, at a cost.
THREAD: language (the first tongue's habits under the second),
  driving (the old car's gearstick reached for months later).
ASKED-AS: i know but it still feels wrong old habit thinking gut says otherwise learned

ESSENCE: nothing fires for a thing that did not happen. The missing
  tool, the guest who never wrote, the step left out of the
  procedure — all silent, because noticing runs on arrivals and an
  absence never arrives.
ROOT: the senses report events, and a non-event is not one.
CANNOT: no noticing an absence directly — it is caught only by
  running a list, so the list must exist beforehand. No learning
  from the letters nobody sent you.
THREAD: aviation (the checklist exists for this exact hole), history
  (the records that survived are the only ones read).
ASKED-AS: forgot something missing did not notice what was not there checklist left out

ESSENCE: knowing a thing when you meet it and being able to fetch it
  are two different powers, and the second is much weaker. The face
  is certain and the name is gone; the list is easy and the blank
  page is not.
ROOT: a match against something present is cheap; a search with
  nothing present to match against is not.
CANNOT: no reading your production off your recognition — the two
  are separately built, so a thing you would know instantly may be
  unreachable while nothing is there to match it. No blank page ever
  as easy as a menu.
THREAD: exams (multiple choice and short answer are two tests),
  shopping (the list you can tick and cannot recite).
ASKED-AS: i know the face not the name recognise but cannot recall multiple choice blank

ESSENCE: draw a line, name both sides, and the things nearest the
  line move apart in the seeing. Two shades one step apart look
  alike inside one colour word and plainly different across the
  border — and the same happens to people once the groups are named.
ROOT: a category exists for deciding, and a decider sharpens
  whatever it must decide between.
CANNOT: no name for a group without its members looking more alike
  than they measure. No comparison across a named border without the
  gap being read as wider than it is.
THREAD: politics (two sides agreeing on most of it and unable to see
  so), maps (a border that makes one valley two countries).
ASKED-AS: labels stereotypes them and us same but different once you name it groups

ESSENCE: a thought becomes inspectable only when it leaves the head.
  On paper, out loud, drawn badly on a napkin — the idea that felt
  whole inside shows its holes the moment it has to be produced. The
  builder cannot inspect its own build.
ROOT: holding a thing and checking it compete for the same small
  hands.
CANNOT: no full check of an idea kept inside. No listener required
  for the effect either — explaining it to someone who says nothing
  does most of the work.
THREAD: engineering (a drawing reveals a clash no meeting would),
  writing (the paragraph that will not go down because it was never
  a thought).
ASKED-AS: write it down explain out loud rubber duck talking it through notes clearer

ESSENCE: the answer arrives in the shower, on the walk, on waking —
  rarely at the desk. Being stuck is one approach held in place;
  stepping away loosens the grip, and something else finally gets a
  turn at the problem.
ROOT: the same search cannot be run harder — a store returns what
  the current cue points at, and the cue is the thing that is stuck.
CANNOT: no unsticking by pressing on. No incubation without the work
  done first — a walk gives nothing to a mind that never loaded the
  problem.
THREAD: sleep (the hard passage smoother in the morning), craft (the
  carver who puts the piece down for a week).
ASKED-AS: stuck on a problem came to me in the shower step away sleep break

ESSENCE: hours do not become skill. Only the hours at the edge — on
  the part you cannot yet do, failing and correcting — move
  anything. Twenty years of comfortable repetition maintains a level
  and never raises it.
ROOT: a system changes only where it is failing, so no error means
  no change.
CANNOT: no improvement out of practice that succeeds — comfort is
  the signature of a level already held. No staying at the edge
  without something showing you where the edge is.
THREAD: music (the hard bar taken alone and slowly, not the piece
  played through), sport (drills built out of the last defeat).
ASKED-AS: practice not improving plateau stuck at the same level drills hard parts coaching

ESSENCE: an order not to think of something is an instruction to
  keep checking whether you are thinking of it — and the check is
  the thought. So the secret surfaces at the worst moment, and the
  tune will not leave.
ROOT: a mind cannot watch for a thing without holding the thing.
CANNOT: no deleting a thought by deciding to. No control except by
  replacement — something else must occupy the place, because the
  place cannot be left empty on command.
THREAD: dieting (the food thought about all day), secrets (the word
  arriving just as you must not say it).
ASKED-AS: cannot stop thinking about it stuck song intrusive thoughts trying not to think

ESSENCE: how long a stretch felt depends on how much of it was kept.
  A week of new places is long to live and longer to look back on; a
  year of the same commute drags through and then vanishes, leaving
  nothing to measure with.
