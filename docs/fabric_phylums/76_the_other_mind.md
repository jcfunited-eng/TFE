# 76 THE OTHER MIND — reading what someone else is doing
Everyone you speak to runs a picture you cannot see. You get only
what they did and said — these are the laws of guessing from it.

ESSENCE: everyone carries their own picture of the world — what
  they know, want and fear is not yours, and the commonest
  mistake in any exchange is quietly assuming it is.
ROOT: the mind / seeing is guessing — each picture built from its
  own side of the room.
CANNOT: no reading another without granting them a view of their
  own. No message aimed at your picture landing in theirs.
THREAD: law (two honest witnesses saw different rooms), teaching
  (the beginner lacks what you know).
ASKED-AS: misunderstanding perspective point of view assume they know see it differently obvious

ESSENCE: you never see why someone did a thing, only the thing —
  the reason is a guess built from the act, the words and what
  came before, and a wrong guess feels exactly like sight.
ROOT: evidence / a cause is inferred from what it leaves behind.
CANNOT: no reading a motive without an act to read it from. No
  certainty in a reading one new fact could overturn.
THREAD: medicine (the symptom seen, the disease inferred), law
  (intent is proven, never observed), tracking.
ASKED-AS: why did they mean that on purpose motive rude intention assume malice guess reason

ESSENCE: nobody thinks themselves unreasonable — from inside
  their position, with what they knew and feared, the foolish
  act was the sensible one; finding that inside is the work.
ROOT: the other mind / everyone carries their own picture.
CANNOT: no explaining an act from outside the position that made
  it. No reading finished by calling it stupid.
THREAD: history (every side thought itself right), engineering (a
  bug is code obeying exactly), farming (the weed is thriving,
  not misbehaving).
ASKED-AS: stupid crazy why would anyone irrational makes no sense their reasons weird baffling

ESSENCE: the words of an ask are the top of it; under them sits
  the hunger that made it, and a perfectly right answer to the
  top can leave the person no better off.
ROOT: persuasion / positions are demands, interests are the
  reasons under them.
CANNOT: no settling a want by answering the words over it. No
  right answer to the stated ask closing an unstated one.
THREAD: medicine (asks for a pill, wants to stop being afraid),
  shops (the drill bought for the hole).
ASKED-AS: what they really want asked for behind question real need surface actually mean under

ESSENCE: everyone guards how they look while they talk, so a
  correction that costs someone face gets fought whether or not
  it is true — the truth was never what was being defended.
ROOT: people together / the ladder — being wrong in public costs a rung.
CANNOT: no correction landing while it costs face. No agreement
  bought by being right from someone charged for agreeing.
THREAD: diplomacy (leave them a way to say yes), teaching (a
  correction before the class teaches only shame).
ASKED-AS: embarrassed defensive admit wrong save face public correction pride stubborn apologize ego

ESSENCE: trust is made of small promises kept on time and unmade
  by one broken — the two directions run at wildly different
  speeds, and which one you are in sets what you can do.
ROOT: chance / betrayal costs ruin and caution costs little, so
  the guard rises faster than it falls.
CANNOT: no repair at the speed of the break. No large promise
  standing in for small ones not yet kept.
THREAD: money (a run empties in a day what took years to fill),
  building (the crack is quicker than the wall).
ASKED-AS: trust broken promise reliable let me down rebuild sorry depend keep word betrayed

ESSENCE: someone listening is spending something they cannot get
  back and can stop whenever they like; being listened to is a
  loan, not a right, and losing it is how most talking ends.
ROOT: the mind / attention is one narrow beam, elsewhere by default.
CANNOT: no holding attention without paying for it in worth. No
  length owed you because the point matters to you.
THREAD: shops (a queue is patience being spent), music (a long
  piece keeps earning its minutes), teaching.
ASKED-AS: boring lost interest rambling too long attention span listening tuned out get to point

ESSENCE: the feeling arrives first and decides which reasons get
  looked at, so by the time an argument is weighed the scale is
  already tilted — and arguing into a tilted scale moves little.
ROOT: the mind / emotions are fast verdicts, reasoning follows.
CANNOT: no argument heard before the feeling that would let it
  in. No reasoning reaching a mind a feeling has closed.
THREAD: medicine (a frightened patient hears none of the plan),
  teaching (a shamed student learns nothing that hour).
ASKED-AS: upset angry emotional wont listen calm down mood feelings first argue facts heated

ESSENCE: anything new travels only over ground both sides already
  stand on — shared words, shared facts, a shared sense of the
  subject; build the floor first or the new thing cannot cross.
ROOT: language / meaning is carried by what the listener holds.
CANNOT: no carrying a new thing across ground that is not shared.
  No explanation landing on someone lacking its pieces.
THREAD: bridges (a span needs both banks), teaching (start where
  they are), maps (agree the landmark first).
ASKED-AS: explain simply they dont get it jargon over their head common ground basics shared

ESSENCE: the same sentence does different work from different
  mouths — an instruction from below is impertinence, a joke
  from above is a threat; the words alone never decide which.
ROOT: people together / the ladder — a sentence is heard from a rung.
CANNOT: no sentence carrying its meaning without the speaker's
  standing attached. No stranger's advice weighing what a
  friend's does.
THREAD: law (who may bind whom), armies (rank turns a sentence
  into an order), family.
ASKED-AS: who said it rank boss authority talking down disrespect tone permission speak up junior

ESSENCE: what you send comes back — warmth returns warmth,
  suspicion returns suspicion, and the loop closes so fast that
  most quarrels are each side answering the other's answer.
ROOT: people together / favors demand return; manner is small change.
CANNOT: no cold opening reliably answered warm. No escape from
  the loop without one side paying to break it.
THREAD: music (call and response), animals (a growl answered by a
  growl), tennis (the ball returns as it was hit).
ASKED-AS: rude back attitude tone mirror argument escalate nice to them hostile spiral snapping

ESSENCE: being received is a want of its own, often the main one
  — someone may need their point held and said back more than
  solved, and a fast right answer can leave them worse off.
ROOT: the other mind / the want under an ask is sometimes hearing.
CANNOT: no solving a want to be heard. No answer, however right,
  doing the work of a hearing.
THREAD: medicine (the patient who needed telling), grief (nothing
  to fix, only to sit with).
ASKED-AS: just listen vent doesnt want advice heard understood fix it validate sympathy complain
