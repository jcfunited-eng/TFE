## How a stock gets picked
1. Decisions are made at the market close; purchases happen the next
   morning at real opening prices. Nothing is ever bought the day it
   is picked.
2. The pool is every operating company in the tracked universe whose
   last two weeks show structural damage in the kernel lanes (channel
   deaths, dead-channel readings, sagging stability). Funds and
   baskets — the zombie category — never enter the pool.
3. Every candidate gets a whole-life kernel reading naming the
   mechanism at work (fall in progress / based after old damage /
   bounce forming / still charging). Only a stock whose reading says
   the fall continues can be picked.
4. A rising stock is never entered (Joseph, 2026-08-21): no pick that
   closed up on its decision day, and no purchase above the picked
   price — ever.
5. A pick may carry one extra day at most, and dies if the next close
   runs more than 2% against it.

## Laws that refuse a pick regardless of its reading
- Zombie ban: operating companies only, identity from the reference
  registry; unknown identity is refused that day and never cached.
- Poison-pill ceiling: a company trading over $100 million on a
  normal day is never shorted — a spike in a giant is news repricing,
  not a crowd pump.
- Fillability: under $200,000 on a normal day is refused; every
  position is capped at 1% of the stock's normal-day traded money.
- Desk floor: thin freshly-pumped names with nothing to borrow,
  stocks under an uptick restriction (fell 10%+ intraday), and
  halt-prone tapes (two or more violent 15% reopens in a day) are
  refused.
- Destroyed-shell ban: lifetime peak 1,000x or more above today's
  price — never traded.
- Sound structure: a company two-plus years old still worth at least
  half its peak is never shorted.
- Anything unreadable is never traded. Every law fails closed.

## Execution
- $2,500 maximum per position (Joseph's cap), $2,000 minimum;
  $100,000 book; cash is checked before every purchase and the
  engine stops when the next slice isn't fully covered — no
  borrowing, no overdraft.
- Fills only at real in-session prices: the first timestamped trade
  at or after 9:30 AM Eastern, never a pre-market or stale print.

## Exits — all automatic, none discretionary
- A winner arms at +5% and banks when it gives back one point from
  its best mark.
- The day-end sweep at 3:55 PM Eastern banks anything at +2% or
  better (the fast-cash law).
- A 20% move against a position buys it back at the next available
  price, with a pre-market safety check at 9:00 AM before the open.
- The fifth completed session ends any position still open.
- Holdings are re-read daily against the shell and sound-structure
  bans; a law refusing a held name cuts it at the next mark.

## Records and honesty
- Every reading is filed before its outcome is knowable; every
  refusal names its law; every action carries a custody note.
- The book publishes to the live page as sealed snapshots; a write
  from an out-of-date copy of the book is refused and logged with
  the writer's identity.
- Simulated fills at real prices; borrow-style carry costs are
  deducted from every closed trade. No real orders.
