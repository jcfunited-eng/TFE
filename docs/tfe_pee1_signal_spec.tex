\section{Purpose and Scope}

This document specifies the complete signal detection, position sizing, execution, and
exit logic for the Personal Execution Engine (PEE-1) Chapter 1 and Chapter 2 signal
layers. Both chapters operate as \textbf{independent execution paths} within PEE-1.
Chapter 1 logic is never modified by Chapter 2 and vice versa.

The specifications herein are the authoritative record of what is deployed in the
TFE production environment. Any deviation from this document in code constitutes
a breaking change and requires a new version of this specification.

% ============================================================
\section{Unified Field (UF) Engine Prerequisites}

Both chapters consume output from the UF Engine Layer 4 decision vector. The relevant
fields from \texttt{runtime\_decisions\_latest} are defined as follows:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Definition} \\
\midrule
$D_k$          & $\{-1, 0, 1\}$ & Directional signal. $+1$ = structural expansion, $-1$ = contraction, $0$ = stillness \\
$S_{UF}$       & $[0, 1]$       & Stability score. Structural entropy order parameter \\
$\text{regime}$ & string        & \texttt{TRANSITIONAL} or \texttt{STABLE} \\
$\text{bar\_count}$ & integer   & Number of trading bars in history for the ticker \\
$R_{rev,k}$    & $\{0, 1\}$    & Reversal flag. $1$ iff $D_k \cdot D_{k-1} < 0$ \\
$B_k$          & $\mathbb{R}$  & Breath signal. Conviction accumulation state \\
$F_n$          & $\mathbb{R}$  & Cognitive scalar. Structural field intensity \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{Data source:} \texttt{runtime\_decisions\_latest} (Postgres). All fields read
from \texttt{snapshot\_row\_json} column. No fallbacks — missing field $\Rightarrow$
signal excluded.

\section{Chapter 1: Three-Wave Alignment (3WA)}

\subsection{Theoretical Basis}

The 3WA signal requires simultaneous confirmation of three structural waves:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Wave \arabic*.}]
  \item \textbf{New Listing Ground State} — The ticker is in its structural infancy
        ($\text{bar\_count} \leq 20$, approximately $\leq 1$ month of trading history).
        At this stage the UF field has not yet accumulated noise, and the initial
        structural attractor is cleanest.

  \item \textbf{Positive Field} — $S_{UF} > 0$. The structural entropy order parameter
        must be non-zero, confirming that the field is ordered rather than chaotic.

  \item \textbf{Market Structural Expansion (SPY)} — SPY $D_k = 1$. The macro structural
        environment is in directional expansion. SPY is used as the market-wide wave
        because it is the largest, most liquid US equity instrument and its $D_k$
        reliably captures macro regime transitions.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Entry Conditions}

All conditions are binary. Any failure $\Rightarrow$ signal excluded. No partial
qualification.

\begin{align}
  &\text{decision\_label} = \texttt{"Accumulate"} \label{eq:3wa-1}\\
  &\text{bar\_count} \in [1,\, 20] \label{eq:3wa-2}\\
  &S_{UF} > 0 \label{eq:3wa-3}\\
  &D_k^{(\text{SPY})} = 1 \label{eq:3wa-4}\\
  &\text{ticker} \neq \texttt{SPY} \label{eq:3wa-5}
\end{align}

\textbf{Signal class assigned:} \texttt{signal\_class = "3WA"}

\subsection{Position Sizing}

\begin{equation}
  \text{risk\_pct}_{3WA} = 1.5\%
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
  \text{dollar\_allocation} = \text{vault\_equity} \times \frac{\text{risk\_pct}}{100}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
  \text{shares} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{dollar\_allocation}}{\text{current\_price}} \right\rfloor
\end{equation}

Hard cap: $\text{risk\_pct} \leq 5.0\%$ (enforced at bridge level regardless of input).
Minimum viable position: $\text{shares} \geq 1$. Minimum share price: $\$5.00$.

\subsection{Bracket Order Parameters}

\begin{align}
  \text{entry\_price}       &= \text{round}(\text{current\_price},\, 2) \\
  \text{take\_profit\_price} &= \text{entry\_price} + 1.2 \times \text{ATR}_{14} \\
  \text{stop\_loss\_price}   &= \text{entry\_price} - 1.0 \times \text{ATR}_{14}
\end{align}

Order type: \texttt{limit}, time-in-force: \texttt{DAY}, order class: \texttt{bracket}.

\subsection{Exit Conditions}

Exits are evaluated by the Sentinel Monitor on each daily run. For 3WA positions
(\texttt{signal\_class = "3WA"} or null):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Exit Type} & \textbf{Trigger} & \textbf{Action} \\
\midrule
SPY Flip       & $D_k^{(\text{SPY})} \neq 1$           & Liquidate ALL open positions \\
Calamity       & $R_{rev,k} > 0$ on position ticker    & Market sell this position \\
Zombie         & $\text{bar\_count} > \tau_{\max}$ (default 10) & Market sell this position \\
Circuit Breaker & Portfolio drawdown $> \text{max\_dd\_pct}$ & Liquidate all, halt for $N$ hours \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{Empirical Performance (structural\_episodes.csv)}

Basis: 1,820 structural episodes. Chapter 1 proxy = sustained $D_k$ (entry $D=1$,
exit $D=1$, TRANSITIONAL regime, LONG):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrr@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Condition} & \textbf{Count} & \textbf{Win Rate} & \textbf{Avg Return} & \textbf{Edge Proxy} \\
\midrule
Entry $D_k=1$, Exit $D_k=1$ & 160 & 72.5\% & 22.0\% & 0.1595 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{E}_{3WA} = \text{win\_rate} \times \text{avg\_return} = 0.725 \times 0.220 = 0.1595
\end{equation}

\section{Chapter 2: Mid $S_{UF}$ Acceleration Layer}

\subsection{Theoretical Basis}

The mid-$S_{UF}$ band ($S_{UF} \in [0.50,\, 0.75)$) captures tickers in the
\textit{acceleration phase} of structural ordering. Unlike the high-$S_{UF}$ band
($S_{UF} \geq 0.75$), which represents mature structural states where the field has
already converged, mid-$S_{UF}$ positions are catching the field \textit{during its
rise} toward high conviction. The empirical data shows this phase outperforms the
high-$S_{UF}$ band on average return (15.0\% vs 13.6\%) with a higher win rate
(64.9\% vs 60.4\%), because entry occurs before the field is fully priced in.

Chapter 2 is \textbf{strictly independent} of Chapter 1. It does not require SPY
$D_k = 1$ (the ticker's own $D_k$ is sufficient), and it excludes new listings
($\text{bar\_count} \leq 20$) which are reserved for 3WA.

\subsection{Entry Conditions}

All conditions are binary. Any failure $\Rightarrow$ signal excluded.

\begin{align}
  &\text{decision\_label} = \texttt{"Accumulate"} \label{eq:ch2-1}\\
  &\text{regime} = \texttt{"TRANSITIONAL"} \label{eq:ch2-2}\\
  &S_{UF} \in [0.50,\, 0.75) \label{eq:ch2-3}\\
  &D_k = 1 \label{eq:ch2-4}\\
  &\text{bar\_count} \geq 21 \label{eq:ch2-5}\\
  &\text{ticker} \neq \texttt{SPY} \label{eq:ch2-6}
\end{align}

\textbf{Signal class assigned:} \texttt{signal\_class = "CH2"}

\subsection{Position Sizing — Dynamic Expectancy Multiplier}

Chapter 2 position size is derived from the ratio of its empirical edge to Chapter 1's
edge. This ensures position size is a function of demonstrated statistical advantage,
not an arbitrary heuristic.

\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{E}_{CH2} = \text{win\_rate}_{CH2} \times \text{avg\_return}_{CH2}
                    = 0.649 \times 0.150 = 0.0974
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
  \mu_{DEM} = \frac{\mathcal{E}_{CH2}}{\mathcal{E}_{3WA}} = \frac{0.0974}{0.1595} = 0.611
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
  \text{risk\_pct}_{CH2} = \text{risk\_pct}_{3WA} \times \mu_{DEM}
                         = 1.5\% \times 0.611 = 0.917\%
\end{equation}

Position size computation follows the same formula as Chapter 1:

\begin{equation}
  \text{dollar\_allocation} = \text{vault\_equity} \times \frac{0.917}{100}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
  \text{shares} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{dollar\_allocation}}{\text{current\_price}} \right\rfloor
\end{equation}

\subsection{Bracket Order Parameters}

\begin{align}
  \text{entry\_price}        &= \text{round}(\text{current\_price},\, 2) \\
  \text{take\_profit\_price}  &= \text{entry\_price} + 1.0 \times \text{ATR}_{14} \\
  \text{stop\_loss\_price}    &= \text{entry\_price} - 1.0 \times \text{ATR}_{14}
\end{align}

Take profit multiplier is tighter than 3WA (1.0× vs 1.2×), reflecting the shorter
median holding period (91 bars vs 223 bars for high-$S_{UF}$ positions).

\subsection{Exit Conditions}

Chapter 2 exits are evaluated by the Sentinel Monitor, routed by \texttt{signal\_class = "CH2"}:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Exit Type} & \textbf{Label} & \textbf{Trigger} & \textbf{Action} \\
\midrule
Exit A & Acceleration complete & $S_{UF} \geq 0.75$ & Market sell (profit take) \\
Exit B & Directional collapse  & $D_k \neq 1$       & Market sell (stop) \\
Circuit Breaker & Portfolio drawdown & $> \text{max\_dd\_pct}$ & Liquidate all, halt \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Exit A is the intended exit — the position is held until the structural field accelerates
into high conviction, at which point the edge rationale has been fully captured.
Exit B is the protective exit — directional collapse invalidates the entry thesis.

\textbf{Note:} Chapter 2 positions do NOT use the SPY Flip or Calamity/Zombie exits
from Chapter 1. Their own structural exit conditions (A and B above) are sufficient
and appropriate to the different holding horizon.

\section{Execution Flow}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Sentinel} (\texttt{sentinel\_monitor.mjs}) — audit all open positions.
        Apply circuit breaker, then route exits by \texttt{signal\_class}:
        Ch2 $\to$ exits A/B; 3WA/null $\to$ SPY flip, calamity, zombie.

  \item \textbf{Phase 2a} (\texttt{3wa\_strategist.mjs}) — detect 3WA signals from
        \texttt{runtime\_decisions\_latest}. Check SPY $D_k$ gate first; return
        \texttt{[]} if Wave 3 inactive.

  \item \textbf{Phase 2b} (\texttt{ch2\_strategist.mjs}) — detect Ch2 signals from
        \texttt{runtime\_decisions\_latest}. Independent of Phase 2a result.

  \item \textbf{Phase 3a} (\texttt{alpaca\_bridge.mjs::executeBracketOrder}) — place
        3WA bracket orders.

  \item \textbf{Phase 3b} (\texttt{alpaca\_bridge.mjs::executeCh2BracketOrder}) — place
        Ch2 bracket orders.

  \item \textbf{Audit} (\texttt{trade\_auditor.mjs}) — sync fills, print report.
\end{enumerate}

All phases share the same circuit breaker gate. If the breaker is active,
phases 2a, 2b, 3a, and 3b are skipped. Sentinel and Audit always run.

% ============================================================
\section{Invariants and Constraints}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Chapter 1 code (\texttt{3wa\_strategist.mjs}, \texttt{executeBracketOrder}) is
        \textbf{never modified} by Chapter 2 additions.

  \item A ticker may generate both a 3WA and a Ch2 signal in the same run only if it
        simultaneously satisfies both entry condition sets — which is impossible by
        construction ($\text{bar\_count} \leq 20$ for 3WA, $\geq 21$ for Ch2).

  \item The DEM multiplier $\mu = 0.611$ is computed from historical episode data and
        is fixed at deployment. It is revisited when a statistically significant number
        of new Ch2 episodes accumulate ($n \geq 200$ recommended review threshold).

  \item No signal is ever retried within the same run\_id. Deduplication is enforced
        via \texttt{client\_order\_id} (SHA-256 hash of \texttt{signal\_class:ticker:run\_id}).

  \item The \texttt{personal\_trade\_ledger} is the single source of truth for all
        open positions. Alpaca account state is cross-referenced but never overrides
        the ledger.
\end{enumerate}
