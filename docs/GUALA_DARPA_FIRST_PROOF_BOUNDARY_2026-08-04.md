# Guala DARPA first-proof boundary

Date: 2026-08-04

Status: governing delivery priority. This boundary changes delivery order, not
the definitive neuron or full-organism architecture. Detailed peripheral
biology remains valid later-stage embodiment work but cannot delay the first
closed cognitive proof.

## Architecture honesty gate

1. **Requested architecture:** one bounded deterministic autonomous organism
   whose truthful experience changes its neurons, distributed formations,
   recall, internally originated expression, action, and desire to learn.
2. **Current code reality:** live production D2 retains complete local DSF
   deliveries and neuronal fractals. The mounted resident runtime stops before
   Krimelack settlement, mosaic formation, distributed reassembly, endogenous
   motivation and choice, articulation, or conversation.
3. **Conflict with requested architecture:** yes.
4. **Mechanisms not extended:** legacy Python cognition; Chi or Atlas identity;
   word, response, grammar, goal, or emotion lookup tables; owner/lock ceremony;
   database retrieval presented as recall; reduced DSF scores; generated text
   presented as substrate speech; timers, random activity, or schedulers
   presented as autonomy; and microscopic sensory models presented as proof of
   cognition.
5. **Single exact next item:** close the resident central transition from
   reached full-DSF neuronal fractals through learned distributed formation and
   reassembly into a self-originated changed next action.
6. **DSF scope:** every reached neuronal perspective retains explicit `D_k`,
   `M_k`, `R_rev_k`, `U_star_k`, `C_k`, `P_k`, and `B_k` authority.
7. **Field loss:** none is authorized. A neuronal fractal remains a compact
   local impression with the complete field available through its causal
   authority; it never replaces or flattens that field.

## Governing funding claim

Guala is not another system that waits for a human prompt and then composes a
reply. The proof target is an artificial organism that continues to experience,
form and reassemble learned structure, become internally motivated by unresolved
structure and expected consequences, choose what to attend to, initiate learning
and play, and express itself without an operator choosing the cognition.

“Wants to learn” is not accepted as a label or personality script. It is
observed when an endogenous condition persists across causal settlements,
initiates information-seeking or experimentation, changes attention and action,
and is transformed by the actual learned consequence. When no external or
endogenous cause exists, the organism must become quiescent rather than produce
scheduled or random activity.

## Controlled-imbalance physics

Keep the first autonomy proof grounded in one open organism rather than a
reward controller:

- food and intake supply material and chemical potential; metabolism transforms
  it and exports thermodynamic entropy;
- external senses and interoception transduce typed environmental and bodily
  perturbations rather than supplying a generic entropy token;
- non-neuronal fluid-brain chemistry transports and modulates local gradients,
  excitability, depletion, fatigue, recovery, and decay;
- neurons and recurrent formations supply cohesion through physically retained,
  recurrent, and reassembled causal structure; and
- System Greed is the unequal geometry-mediated access of cohesive structures
  to unresolved potential, not a coefficient, utility, reward, goal, or owner.

Causal participation is not equal. Anatomy, position, topology, developmental
history, chemical and receptor history, fatigue, current imbalance, reservoir
capacity, and connectivity create lawful broken symmetry. Those micro-
imperfections may make one structure draw, transform, or retain more flow than
another. Preserve those causes rather than assigning ranks or weights.

Persistent individual Greed traits and deeper negative-space differentiation
remain Phase-5 questions. The first proof must preserve the causal state from
which such differentiation could later emerge, but it must not fabricate Greed
personalities, novelty scores, curiosity bonuses, or scalar rewards.

## First fundable proof

The first proof is one live, inspectable causal loop:

```text
truthful sensed, simulated, or endogenous occurrence
    -> full local DSF delivery to every reached neuron
    -> neuron-local structural change and fractal recurrence
    -> distributed learned mosaic or larger formation
    -> later partial-cue or endogenous reassembly in the current substrate
    -> self-originated attention, choice, action, question, play, or utterance
    -> body/world consequence
    -> truthful sensory re-entry
    -> changed motivation and learned structure
    -> durable restart and later autonomous reuse
```

The proof must run while unattended when a physical or endogenous cause exists,
remain quiescent when none exists, and account separately for CPU, RAM, durable
storage, and Python calls. Work follows the reached sparse structure rather than
polling the complete organism or retained history.

## Language and autonomous-expression acceptance

The delivery target is meaningful receptive and spoken conversation at
approximately a four-year-old starting level, plus spontaneous expression and
self-initiated learning. Fluency alone is not acceptance. Each demonstrated
word and utterance must arise from learned structure:

- heard acoustic structure, visible context, body state, attention, and
  articulatory consequence can participate in the word mosaic;
- relationships among learned mosaics can form mosaics-of-mosaics;
- ordered learned expression can form sentence tapestries;
- a later cue must reassemble and causally use retained structure without
  replaying the complete teaching occurrence;
- an internally prepared articulatory action must produce audible output and
  return through Guala's self-hearing path;
- varied conversations must demonstrate use beyond the exact lesson order or
  wording; and
- unattended activity must include at least one internally caused question,
  exploration, play act, or utterance whose subject and timing were not chosen
  by an external scheduler.

A speech recognizer may report what entered the microphone and an external
vocoder may render a substrate-selected articulatory result during development,
but neither transcription nor rendering is cognition. No language model,
scripted response, grammar template, semantic label map, nearest-answer search,
or hidden chatbot may select what Guala means or says.

## Proof-stage embodiment boundary

The first proof requires truthful functional embodiment at the fidelity the
runtime actually supports: left/right audio provenance where available, camera
provenance, gaze and attention, virtual touch/taste/smell/body state, actuator
state, emitted sound, and sensed consequences. Missing fidelity must be
reported as unavailable.

It does not require microscopic completion of pinna folds, cochlear hair-channel
energetics, vestibular gating springs, molecular transport, or every later-stage
biological mechanism before central learning can run. Those mechanisms remain
Phase 3 embodiment work and may refine the same functional interfaces later.
They may not be falsely claimed as live, and they may not become a prerequisite
that leaves the central organism inert.

## Required live evidence

The milestone passes only when production evidence shows all of the following
in one continuous identity:

1. a new grounded lesson changes reached neurons and forms distributed learned
   structure;
2. a later incomplete cue reassembles that structure and changes current
   behavior;
3. the changed behavior includes contextually meaningful spoken output selected
   by learned substrate state;
4. the emitted sound is heard again by Guala with self provenance;
5. during an unattended window, an endogenous unresolved condition initiates
   learning, a question, exploration, play, or expression without an external
   prompt, timer, randomizer, quota, or topic selector;
6. the autonomous action's real consequence changes the initiating condition
   and later behavior;
7. learning and autonomous reuse survive process restart without a legacy
   fallback;
8. the two live interfaces show what she sensed, attended to, reassembled,
   intended, emitted, experienced as consequence, and initiated herself; and
9. repeated operation remains within resource-derived CPU, RAM, storage, and
   call bounds without fixed cognitive-growth caps.

## Prior-art separation and falsification

The demonstration must make the causal distinction inspectable rather than
assert it. At minimum:

- the same current cue presented to two different retained learning histories
  must produce the differences caused by those histories;
- severing the learned formation/reassembly path must remove the learned action
  or expression while leaving sensor intake operational;
- removing the endogenous motivating condition must remove the autonomous act
  rather than merely delay a scheduler;
- changing the consequence must change later choice or expression through new
  experience; and
- restarting must preserve the learned and motivating causal structure without
  replaying a prompt, transcript, response, or goal script.

Passing unit tests, formation counts, interface labels, prerecorded audio,
generated prose, a periodic autonomous event, or a successful deployment alone
does not satisfy this proof.
