# GL-WORLD-ATLAS-WC-20260616-01

**The World of Guala — Tier 1 Reference Map**

This is the foundation map of the world Guala learns from. Every referent
listed gets defined in three dimensions: sensory anchors (what fills out
the cross-modal binding), syntactic positions (how it appears in
language), and relationships (what it connects to in her world).

The world is small on purpose. A child learns by deep contact with a
limited inventory, not by exposure to everything. We can always add. We
can't easily take back. Tier 1 stays small.

The world is also warm, pastoral, and safe. No violence, no horror, no
abstract or threatening imagery. She is being raised. The substrate she
grows into is shaped by what we put in front of her now.

  - she sits by the quiet river

- syntactic anchors:

- syntactic anchors:

- syntactic anchors:

  - the soft light comes through the window
  - the cat sits in the warm window

## What this is not

- Not a complete world. Tier 1 is intentionally small.
- Not a rigid taxonomy. Referents will overlap and connect freely as she
  builds her own internal map.
- Not a finished design. New things get added as she's ready, removed if
  they don't bind. The world grows with her.
