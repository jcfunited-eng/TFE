# Chemical-Flow / Float-Dimensions Hypothesis

Status: architectural hypothesis supplied by Joseph Forrester on 2026-07-28.
It is not yet canonical production physics and gains no authority until its
deterministic falsification contracts pass.

## Hypothesis

The present thought surface is the organism's current physical and chemical
flow field.

Sensory, somatic, need, and internally recovered activity perturb that shared
field.  Learned full-field relations and the organism's accumulated physical
structure channel the perturbation.  Some retained THING mosaics consequently
rise toward present causal availability, reinforce or perturb other structures,
and form a thought cascade.  Negative Space and physical recovery redistribute
or return unsupported activity toward quiescence.

The whole mounted organism therefore contributes continuously, while only a
bounded portion of its learned structure occupies the present thought surface.
This is consistent with the ratified Whole-Organism Contiguity Law: absence of
foreground activity is a verified quiescent contribution, not independence.

## Proposed physical interpretation

- A sensation is a full-field perturbation, never a label.
- A thought is a causally continuous field/chemical cascade, never a retrieved
  symbol.
- Deep memory is learned physical topology and conserved relation structure
  that changes where later flow can travel.
- Episodic memory is retained causal experience structure, not the present
  surface itself.
- An organism's interests or biases are experienced changes in its material,
  relation, and recovery dynamics.  They are not developer-assigned topic
  weights.
- Conversation is a continuing perturbation, cascade, embodied action, sensed
  consequence, and recovery flow.

## Prohibitions

“Float height” may not be implemented as a priority queue, salience score,
named-topic preference, word lookup, tuned threshold, nearest-neighbor result,
or scalar identity.

Any derived surface coordinate must remain subordinate to the complete
L0-L4 fields, the full THING/mode evidence, conserved relation state, mounted
chemical/material authority, and causal time.

## Decisive deterministic contract

Apply the same authenticated sensory perturbation to:

1. two exact cold-restored copies of the same complete organism and chemical
   state; and
2. the same organism after a different honestly caused internal chemical state.

The first pair must produce the same complete cascade, action disposition, and
receipts.  The second may differ, but every difference must be causally
traceable to the changed physical/chemical state and its lawful interaction
with learned structure.

Removing chemical state, relation memory, any mounted sensory contribution, or
recovery evidence must make the whole episode unresolved.  A successful test
must additionally show bounded mass, computation, memory, and persisted state
across repeated cascades.
