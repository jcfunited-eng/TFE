## Corrective speech specification and design

One causal chain governs every level:

```text
authentic acoustic + visual/body/context occurrence
  -> unchanged joint DSF and typed local neuronal transition
  -> retained lawful sensorimotor/relational change
  -> later partial-cue or internally caused recurrence
  -> ordered real motor discharges and body state
  -> newly generated physical pressure
  -> actual ear/world return and further retained consequence
```

The corrective design has one source clock and one persisted organism. Receptor
state, local conductance/phase, body configuration, paid work, pending pressure
and their exact successors must have declared meanings. The specified mechanism
must explain how heard experience changes future motor recruitment and how one
gesture's actual consequence participates in the next. Where that local law is
missing, it remains an explicit design gap; a score, prototype, target waveform,
phonetic token, table or invented sequence object cannot fill it.

An external tutor or voice-quality benchmark may supply acoustic experience only
under its disclosed authority. Its labels, expected answer, pronunciation program
and evaluation scores do not enter pupil decision state. A reference sound being
accepted by Joe does not authorize copying that sound's recipe into learned
pupil cognition. Functional acoustic emulation is assessed under its approved
scope; exact arithmetic, deterministic synthesis and native code alone do not
establish physically derived learning.
