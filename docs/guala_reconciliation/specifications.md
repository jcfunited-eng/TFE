Functional equivalence does not authorize ML, heuristic pupil policy, canned answers, fabricated memory or softened tests.

Use the smallest authorized mechanism that supplies the required causal function, specify it, and test that function. No additional physical model is ratified by this document.
Historical equations, component investigations and reported approvals are evidence at their dated scope. A clause requiring lawful state, conservation or timing must not be expanded into microscopic reconstruction. Original authority gaps remain identified; no kernel edit or new equation is authorized to make documents agree.
