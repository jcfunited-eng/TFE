# UI, sensory experience and observation: historical reconciliation

This chapter reconciles seventeen complete recovered documents dated June6–July28,2026 against the October10 source/configuration/browser audit. It adds29 source-bound obligations (HU01–HU29) to the selected SC crosswalk. **Document review is complete for these seventeen bytesets; transitive code/deployment history and system acceptance are not.** Overall April-to-current reconciliation remains OPEN in the [sole ledger](../../collaborative_todo.md).

The history does not support “everything was correct until64columns.” Earlier briefs explicitly prescribed semantic filters, chi lookups, hand-tuned activation multipliers, browser speech recognition and text-to-speech. Other older requirements correctly demanded raw multimodal experience, truthful empty displays and full hearing fields. Preserve those valid requirements; do not restore rejected mechanisms merely because they once produced convincing output. No allegation of author intent follows from these source findings.
