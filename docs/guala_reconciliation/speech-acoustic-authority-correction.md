Remez and colleagues demonstrated linguistic perception from time-varying sinewave replicas, including some two-tone conditions. Many uninstructed listeners did not hear speech; instructions and stimulus combination mattered. The stimuli followed analyzed human speech and included manual smoothing. The study supports a counterexample to universal human-glottis and three-mode necessities; it does not supply a lawful Guala learning mechanism, establish natural child-voice quality or prove ArcLoom's current capability. [Original primary study, pages947–950](https://people.ece.cornell.edu/land/courses/ece4760/Speech/remez_rubin_pisoni_carrell1981.pdf).
