The [current glossary](terminology.md) states the meaning to use. This companion
explains how meanings diverged and what must be reconciled in specifications,
schemas, source, tests and UI. A rename does not repair the wrong equation or
retained state. Source numbers identify [preserved versions](documentation-sources.md).

The distinction between physical approximation and prohibited shortcut is also
scope-dependent. A declared world rendering model is not automatically pupil
cognition, but it must state what it represents and must not claim unavailable
physical detail. Current strict cognitive/DSF requirements cannot be bypassed
by calling a score, projection or policy a functional equivalent.

## Speech and sensory execution: final Attempt54 distinctions

The [complete family review](speech-transition-reconciliation.md) separates sound from learned articulation, intelligibility, grounding and syntax; topology address from temporal physical cause; anatomical membership from experienced ownership; guided teaching from later recall; and archived topology from usable retained state. An expanded full-field32×24 grid is not automatically an organism-aimed fovea. Observation long-poll latency, sensory request age, native settlement and published cycle are different quantities. Exact call-local computation reuse is not semantic lookup; reduced-field or recipient thinning changes authority and needs its own law.


## Earlier first utterance is not a first word

[The L006 lineage](speech-organ-history-reconciliation.md) used utterance for physical vocal output with self-hearing and recurrent use, explicitly excluding word/meaning. Preserve that scope. Distinguish fixed effector anatomy from learned route, motor preparation from own discharge, respiratory work from glottal closure, emitted from in-flight from heard, and source continuity from learned content. Persisting an acoustic program does not by itself turn its prescribed timing into a qualified physical law. Later approved oscillator scope must be stated separately.


## Recognizing apple versus saying apple

The [L005 review](speech-organ-history-reconciliation.md) preserves the reported recognition of a changed human pronunciation and explicitly separates it from producing the word, arbitrary lexical generalization and relational syntax. A visual-pattern formation responding during an apple lesson does not become an apple-word memory by its lesson label. Input acceptance, reinforcement, retained structure and usable later memory are separate measurements. Human and externally synthesized tutor pressure need declared provenance; neither is organism-owned speech.


## Focal acquisition, hearing and speech are separate terms

The [D122334 contract](speech-transition-reconciliation.md) first corrected a whole-scene field called focal, then explicitly distinguished a derived sampling pitch from measured visual capability. Sparse coverage is acquisition, not compressed knowledge. Local metabolic afference is a real material feedback route, not by itself hunger, preference or foraging. The [task1401 contract](speech-organ-history-reconciliation.md) separates emission and hearing but still proposed overly broad speech credit; canonical usage reserves words, grounding and syntax for their own evidence. A pressure hash identifies content, not a unique lived event.


## Program, body act, choice and speech

[VO76–VO88](speech-organ-history-reconciliation.md) preserve the July record calling its neutral sound a program and expressly withholding vocabulary. Later persistent native implementation did not retroactively make it learned. Reached layer13 anatomy is not vocal ownership; a reflex is not internally reassembled action; one such act is not demonstrated choice among alternatives; bounded tissue movement is not a spoken word. Source-disjoint acoustic reuse and contrast rejection remain actual tests, not synonyms for large neuron counts.

Phase1 virtual activation units and conserved electrical work have different accounting roles. Endpoint displacement and total travel differ, and both differ from the time-resolved sensory experience. A valid coherent body/world pair cannot be assembled from two separately healthy moments.


## Seeded, retrieved, grounded and learned are different claims

[SH23–SH35](speech-history-reconciliation.md) show that authored lexical addresses and modality labels were called grounded experience, while a band familiarity hit was called a verified word. Canonical usage requires actual sources and exact claimed evidence: generated metadata is not mounted cognition; a dictionary pronunciation is not heard pressure; native/reflex sound is not learned speech; PRECONDITION_NOT_MET is not pass. A named L6 rank made from threshold counts is a reduced proxy, not a computed rank of physical constraints.


## Hearing, distinction and inference are different boundaries

[The July27 findings](speech-history-reconciliation.md) distinguish full-field custody
from full-field decision, repeated strength from changed receptive dynamics, context
familiarity from sound discrimination, and unique source reconstruction from learned
recognition. Sixteen cochlear channels and64 cortical columns are different structures.
Exact arithmetic, a canonical label and a distinct output vector do not establish the
claimed physical relation or semantic capability. Reported failures remain valid at
their tested scope; they do not prove permanent incapacity.


## Derived, calibrated, conserved and learned must retain separate meanings

[The speech-design reconciliation](speech-history-reconciliation.md) corrects four
conflations: a chosen gate-target gain is not independent calibration; reusing a
number is not physical derivation; proportional incident energy does not imply
proportional quantized delivery; and boundary ML transcription is not ordinary
codec decoding. A proposed profile, a single transaction, perceived voice identity,
screen text, external TTS and native learned articulation are distinct claims.


## Offered, sensed, grasped, chosen and understood are separate

[The complete L007–L009 review](media-history-reconciliation.md) records how no-book-
choice explanations were superseded by missing world grasp, anatomy and palmar input.
An innate grasp then worked while learned opening remained absent. A real media
experience, a new retained formation or audible response is not thereby reading,
preference, agreement or learned speech. Preserve the historical explicit limits as
well as the useful physical corrections.
