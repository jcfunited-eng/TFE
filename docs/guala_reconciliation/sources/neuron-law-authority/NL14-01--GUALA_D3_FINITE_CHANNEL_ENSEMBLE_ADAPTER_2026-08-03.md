# Guala D3 finite channel-ensemble adapter

Date: 2026-08-03

Status: isolated and unmounted. This is a minimal typed adapter over the
accepted bounded-quanta and localized-contact candidates. It does not claim a
biologically identified channel, membrane current, mounted neuron, D3

   transfer while preserving the complete channel population and every other
   declared conserved moiety.
2. **Current code reality:** the generic contact primitive already performs the
   transition. This adapter identifies which species are conformations of one
   population, requires a closed population-conserving relation, retains the

## Population and conformational law

One declared conserved moiety identifies the channel population. Every species
classified as one of its conformations must carry exactly one unit of that
moiety; all other species carry zero. At least two conformational species must
exist. The one local reaction lane must consume at least one conformation and
produce at least one successor conformation.

The underlying closed-reaction admission proves exact conservation of every
declared moiety and charge before a state can exist. The adapter additionally
requires the sum of all conformational populations to equal one nonzero fixed
population at creation and restart. Transition carries this immutable admitted
binding forward; it does not rescan the population as reaction dynamics.

This makes ligand binding representable as a relation such as free
conformation plus finite ligand becoming bound conformation. Ligand is a finite
species inventory, not an inexhaustible signal. When that reservoir is empty,
the contact settles no further material and the ensemble is quiescent.

## External contact and unresolved biology

An external observation supplies only exact occupied reactant quantities at
the predecessor's already-bound physical location, interval, and clock. It
never supplies reaction extent. The generic contact law derives the maximum
eligible extent from occupied reactants and finite destination capacity, then
applies the anatomy-bound exact fraction and retained remainder.

The adapter does not derive the external contact from diffusion, collision,
membrane geometry, voltage, or organism behavior. Test fractions and species
codes are synthetic falsification fixtures, not biological coefficients or
semantic authority.

There is exactly one directed conformational relation in this candidate's one
lane. Reverse transitions, multiple channel states, ligand competition, and
several reactions sharing one physical site remain unresolved. They require an
explicit phase/contact graph or another exact physical law; they must not be
improvised through scheduling or priority.

## Canonical restart and boundedness

The adapter codec records its population-moiety code and fixed population, then
embeds the complete canonical generic anatomy and state. Decode re-admits both
layers, recounts the population once at the restart boundary, and rejects
altered identity or total-population claims.

For fixed compile-time species and moiety counts, the resident state is an
inline fixed-width payload. Successful transition reports the generic
source-level contact work requirement, adapter payload width, and exact codec
length. These statements do not claim measured machine instructions, compiler
stack bounds, or boundedness of any future mounted organism.

## Current falsification coverage

The strict isolated suite proves:

- populations of 1, 3, and 9 conserve their fixed ensemble total;
- an exact one-third transfer retains its remainder and exhausts a finite
  ligand reservoir into material quiescence;
- absent external contact causes no conformational settlement;
- canonical restart preserves the next transition exactly;
- missing ligand or channel conservation is refused;
- an open population boundary is refused;
- altered population identity and fixed-total codec claims are refused;
- external contact is occupancy rather than caller extent; and
- 100,000 externally caused transitions retain constant state width and the
  same reported source-level work shape.

This does not prove a biological coefficient, sensory receptor, voltage-gated
channel, membrane current, neuron, synapse, fluid-brain mechanism, recall,
mosaic, cognition, autonomy, DSF execution, deployment, or production.
