## ArcLoom literature audit

The repository separates two structures that the candidate conflated.

### MathLoom positional arithmetic

Balanced ternary represents a word as:

```text
value = sum(trit[i] * 3**i)
```

The exact full-adder identity is positional carry:

```text
a[i] + b[i] + carry[i] = sum[i] + 3 * carry[i+1]
```

That law is established in `ArcLoom_MathLoom_Addendum_v1_4_d0_1.txt`
and the MathLoom HDL.  It is exact arithmetic within a typed word.  It does
not define physical oscillator circumference or neuronal fan-out.

### Krimelack phase and winding

The ArcLoom processor literature defines a closed phase ring with continuous
winding:

```text
w = (1 / 2*pi) * integral(dphi/dtheta dtheta)
```

and a triple-node trit with phase relations separated by `2*pi/3`.  Its
sensory-transduction equation accumulates physical phase relative to natural
frequency and emits a winding transition on a `2*pi` crossing.  Therefore
`3**width` is not a ratified Krimelack phase circumference.  A fixed-point
balanced-ternary angular discretization might be possible, but the repository
contains no canonical scale or mapping that authorizes one.

### Coupling, energy, and recovery

Historical ArcLoom documents propose pairwise cosine coupling energy,
Hermitian norm-preserving psi evolution, scalar DSF-to-`J` tables, score-driven
dead zones, and speculative material fatigue.  None supplies an exact current
Guala law for:

- conserving a transition across arbitrary sparse fan-out;
- preventing recurrent graph amplification;
- bounding cumulative winding;
- distinguishing constant evidence from new causal drive;
- depleting and recovering a neuron's physical response; or
- proving finite/quiescent settling or a bounded non-quiescent cadence.

The scalar tables also contain the rejected eighth local `S_UF` lane and
unspecified `J_base`/`J_max`.  Score/dead-zone familiarity is heuristic legacy
logic.  The dielectric-fatigue discussion explicitly lacks an executable
material law.  None can be resurrected as the D3 answer.

## Executed counterexamples

### Unchanged input produces unbounded winding

For a one-trit centered word, constant authenticated `+1` drive crosses once
every three generations.  The executable counterexample gives winding `100`
after 300 generations and `300` after 900.  The residue is bounded; the
cumulative winding is not.  `U_star=Observed` would be especially false as a
perpetual energetic drive because availability is state, not repeated new
evidence.

### Fan-out copies quantity

One source crossing with three outgoing routes produces three full unit
events.  Absolute downstream drive grows with outdegree even though the model
defines no energy source, split, or conservation law.

### A four-neuron complete graph never quiesces

After every neuron crosses once, each target receives three unit events from
the other three neurons.  Each target crosses again and emits three more.  The
model retains 12 pending events every generation and cumulative winding grows
forever without new local evidence.

### Width is an unratified physical coefficient

Changing `width` changes the crossing boundary from `3` to `9`, `27`, and so
on.  That is valid word arithmetic but silently changes oscillator sensitivity.
No anatomical, DSF, or ArcLoom authority currently derives this physical
choice.
