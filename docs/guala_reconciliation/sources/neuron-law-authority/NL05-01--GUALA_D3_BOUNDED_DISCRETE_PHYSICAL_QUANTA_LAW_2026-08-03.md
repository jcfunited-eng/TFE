## Bound anatomy

The state carries its complete immutable candidate anatomy rather than a
swappable reference. That anatomy contains:

- a canonical species code and exact rational unit scale;
- charge per quantum and an ordered conserved-moiety vector per species;
- one full physical location: region, compartment, side, control volume, lane,
  and site;
- one exact rational interval and phase code;
- finite inventory capacities and reactant contact limits;
- one integer stoichiometric relation;
- one reduced positive fraction no greater than unity;
- a closed conservation boundary or explicit per-extent open-boundary flux;
  and
- the maximum eligible extent, maximum fractional accumulator, and required
  fixed integer width derived before state admission.

Codes identify physical structure and serialization fields. They do not choose
a reaction or break competition.

## Fixed fractional settlement

For an admitted contact extent `N`, immutable fraction `p/q`, and the remainder
already bound inside that lane state:

```text
z        = p * N + r
transfer = z div q
r_next   = z mod q
```

`0 <= r < q` is checked when state is admitted. The anatomy constructor proves
that `p * N_max + q - 1` and every maximum stoichiometric product fit `u128`.
An anatomy that cannot prove this is refused before a state exists.

This is exact deterministic error retention at the declared population and
interval resolution. It is not a claim that biology literally stores an
integer remainder, nor that the declared `p/q` has been biologically derived.

## Conservation boundary

A closed relation is admitted only when the dot product of its stoichiometric
coefficients with every declared moiety vector and charge vector is zero.

An explicit open relation is admitted only when those dot products equal the
declared charge and moiety flux per whole reaction extent. This proves that the
local opening is explicit; it does not prove whole-organism conservation until
the opposite finite body or environment reservoir is mounted atomically.

ATP, ions, receptors, vesicles, and other biological material are conserved
only when they appear as named species with correct declared invariants. This
generic primitive cannot invent or certify a missing biological reaction law.

## Exact persistence boundary

The companion codec writes the complete schema, interval, lane anatomy,
conservation boundary, inventories, remainders, and interval index in a fixed
little-endian format. Decode reconstructs and re-admits that complete anatomy;
it does not trust a hash or reinterpret state using caller-supplied anatomy.

The codec proves byte-exact isolated restart. It is not durable storage,
authenticated custody, or mounted causal one-use proof.
