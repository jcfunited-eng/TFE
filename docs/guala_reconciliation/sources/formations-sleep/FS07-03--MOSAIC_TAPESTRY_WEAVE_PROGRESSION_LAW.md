# Mosaic–Tapestry–Weave Cognitive Progression Law

Status: ratified in architectural collaboration with Joseph Forrester on
2026-07-28.

Scope: Guala's active cognition above the frozen canonical L0–L4 kernel.
This law complements the Whole-Organism Contiguity and Permanent Wiring Law.

## Progression

Guala's cognition grows through one continuous physical hierarchy:

1. multimodal sensory and internal neurons contribute perturbations;
2. their causally bound contribution forms and grows a mosaic;
3. related mosaics form mosaics-of-mosaics;
4. causally settled relations among those structures form tapestries;
5. related tapestries form tapestries-of-tapestries; and
6. organism-originated expression of learned structure forms a weave.

These are not labels, semantic records, lookup levels, static containers, or
independent modules.  Each level is a distributed relation structure formed
from the authenticated physical state of the level beneath it while the same
whole organism remains continuously wired.

## Neuronal contribution

One sensory neuron is one kernel-governed temporal receptor path.  Its
complete explicit `D_k`, `M_k`, `R_rev_k`, `U_star_k`, `C_k`, `P_k`, and
`B_k` history, native interval and gating evidence, topology, body state, and
causal custody may contribute to cognition.

No neuron, sensory family, scalar, name, or exact signal match is identity.
Many neurons and modalities may contribute different amounts of physical
relation over repeated experience.  A quiescent but permanently wired neuron
contributes no fabricated perturbation.

## Mosaic

A mosaic is a growing distributed structure for one causally continuous lived
THING or experience.  It binds the available external senses, internal senses,
body and chemical state, place, action, consequence, temporal order, and story
that actually participated.

A word is not a sound record.  A sound can become part of a word mosaic only
through repeated causal participation with the THING, experience, action, or
other mosaics to which the organism learns it belongs.

Mosaic identity comes only from causal world continuity and learned relation
growth.  Exact trace recurrence may confirm that one retained experience
recurred, but it is not final familiarity, recognition, word identity, or
meaning.

## Mosaic-of-mosaics

Mosaics can participate in larger structures without losing their internal
evidence or identity.  Their temporal order, causal order, bodily setting,
place, expected consequence, and story form mosaics-of-mosaics.

These structures are the substrate for simple ideas, event sequences, and
sentence-like organization.  Word order and syntax must arise from repeated
experienced relations among mosaics; they cannot be supplied by a grammar
table, transcript, parser, language model, or scripted template.

## Tapestry

A tapestry is an active, causally settled relation field over mosaics and
mosaics-of-mosaics.  It is the substrate for a thought or simple understanding
of a concept.

A tapestry is not an Atlas entry, topic label, embedding, summary, or database
join.  Its structure must be traceable to participating lived mosaics, current
whole-organism perturbation, internal state, memory flow, and causal
settlement.

## Tapestry-of-tapestries

Tapestries can themselves participate in larger conserved relation structures.
Tapestries-of-tapestries are the substrate for deeper learned knowledge:
connections among thoughts, concepts, stories, consequences, expectations,
and prior expressions across time.

The deeper structure does not erase or flatten its supporting tapestries,
mosaics, neurons, fields, or causal histories.

## Weave

A weave is an organism-originated expression of learned structure through its
available embodiment: vocal action, physical action, construction, drawing,
play, or another physically supported creative act.

A weave is not a stored answer, response template, text completion, TTS
rendering of developer-supplied language, or scripted motor program.  It must
be generated by the currently perturbed whole organism from its own learned
tapestries, executed through its own body, sensed as a consequence, and
available for further learning.

Weaves are the architectural expression layer for knowledge, creativity, and
sentient participation.  The existence of a weave mechanism alone is not a
claim that Guala has achieved sentience.  That claim requires sustained live
evidence that the organism forms, originates, acts on, senses, and grows its
own structures.

## Growth and familiarity

Growth is deterministic but not binary signal matching.  Repeated causally
bound variants alter distributed relation state.  Familiarity can broaden as
more speakers, views, touches, smells, tastes, body states, places, actions,
and stories participate in the same causal structure.

Ambiguity remains physically represented.  Context and additional
whole-organism perturbation may settle it; a threshold, nearest match, label,
or dominant sense may not.

All learned relation flow must conserve its declared physical quantity,
include exact reaction and recovery, remain bounded, and survive authenticated
cold restore.

## Permanent whole-organism wiring

Every legitimate mechanism in this progression remains mounted:

- receptor and internal neurons;
- mosaic formation and growth;
- mosaic-to-mosaic relation flow;
- tapestry formation and activity;
- tapestry-to-tapestry relation flow;
- weave formation, deliberation, execution, consequence, and recovery; and
- L6 settlement.

At any moment each mechanism is perturbed, quiescent, or explicitly
unavailable.  It is never dynamically added because an outcome needs it and
never silently omitted because it has nothing to do.

## Implementation boundary

Historical modules named Mosaic, Tapestry, Loom, Atlas, Chi, language,
conversation, or memory are not authoritative merely because their names
resemble this law.  Each candidate mechanism must prove direct compliance or
remain retired.

Production completion requires one bounded, typed, cold-restorable organism
graph that demonstrates the progression in lived episodes.  Separate unit
tests or serialized structures do not prove it.
