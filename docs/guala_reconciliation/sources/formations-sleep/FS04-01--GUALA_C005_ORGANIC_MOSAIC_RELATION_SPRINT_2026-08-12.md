## Architecture honesty gate

1. **Requested architecture:** mosaics relate only when current neuron/contact
   physics reassembles at least one recurrent formation and the same reached
   frontier physically reaches another recurrent formation through shared or
   bridged retained topology.
2. **Current code reality:** `settle_organism_mosaic_boundary` can reassemble
   and alter each retained mosaic separately. It does not emit bounded evidence
   that one current frontier related two retained mosaics. A retired historical
   `mosaic_of_mosaics_relation_count` remains codec-only and is not physics.
3. **Conflict:** yes; C-005 is not yet live-proven.
4. **Not extended:** the retired relationship counter; hierarchy/parent/member
   objects; relation-kind enums; serial histories; generated groups; semantic
   labels; databases; owners; locks; full-population scans; or reduced DSF.
5. **Single exact item:** derive a transient connected relationship frontier
   only when one current physical transition reassembles a retained recognized
   mosaic and reaches another retained recognized mosaic through shared neuron
   lineage or an active sparse physical bond.
6. **DSF scope:** unchanged full joint seven-field DSF remains authoritative in
   each reached neuron; this operation does not evaluate DSF.
7. **Field loss:** none. No field is copied, averaged, projected, or replaced.

   changing state.

   its predecessor and successor organism ticks, in the complete admitted-
   experience observation. A final quiet hop cannot erase an earlier physical
   relation from that experience.

Expected successor:

- neuronal, contact, body, fluid, formation, identity, and complete DSF state
  change only through their already-authorized physics;
- no relationship object, counter, archive entry, parent, or semantic state is
  persisted;

  to durable bytes;

  their reached member/contact frontier, never all developmental neurons or
