glottal aperture, jaw opening, and lip width movement into three fixed resident
transducer impulses. Its four exported local body channels are respiratory
flow, glottis, oral aperture, and perioral skin, but respiratory flow is written

## Natural-flow causal impact

The native transition can settle multiple declared receptor sources in one
shared physical interval. The production wrapper nevertheless admits a whole
card lesson under one `_transition_lock` and serially advances its bounded hop
list. During this copied run, representative nominal 250 ms transport
intervals took about 2.3 seconds wall time. Other external or unattended lived
work cannot interleave while that lock is held. Together with the signed-16
overlap refusal, this means the present runtime supports simultaneous fields
inside one settlement but not continuous human-like concurrent experience.

Speech closure therefore includes, rather than postpones, these boundaries:
external speech plus self-pressure must coexist lawfully; one long lesson must
not monopolize lived time; and the organism must continue sensing, acting, and
self-hearing at its declared cadence with bounded CPU, RAM, state, and durable
storage. Broad unrelated cleanup remains outside this attempt.
