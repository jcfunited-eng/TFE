# 27 STORY AND DRAMA — the laws of told change
Every tribe found the same machinery. Soft field, but the oldest
tested one — ten thousand years of audiences are the laboratory.

ESSENCE: a story is change or it is nothing — someone crosses from
  one state to another (ignorance to knowledge, home to exile,
  coward to willing); events without a crossing are a list.
ROOT: the mind / attention tracks change and discards the steady.
CANNOT: no story of the static — "they stayed happy" holds no
  listener; a perfect world contains no stories.
THREAD: news (the plane that lands isn't news), music (a melody
  that never leaves home isn't one), sales (every pitch is a
  before-and-after).
ASKED-AS: boring plot movie book journey transformation arc beginning ending character

ESSENCE: conflict is the engine — a want meeting an obstacle; a
  story's size is the size of what blocks its people, not the
  size of its events.
ROOT: the change law above / nothing crosses without something to
  cross; physics — a push needs something pushed against.
CANNOT: no drama from an unopposed want — the wish granted on
  page one ends the book on page one.
THREAD: sports (the mismatch bores; the close spread sells),
  games (an opponent is a story machine), courtship tales (the
  obstacle made the old plots).
ASKED-AS: villain tension stakes struggle problem antagonist gripping page turner dull chase

ESSENCE: a story opens by making promises — the rifle shown in
  act one must fire by act three, the tone set must hold, the
  question raised must be answered — and the audience keeps this
  ledger without knowing it keeps one.
ROOT: law+agreements / an audience is a counterparty, paying
  attention forward on credit.
CANNOT: no unearned rescue satisfies — help nobody planted (the
  sudden inheritance, the god from the machine) breaks the
  contract even while it surprises.
THREAD: music (a cadence is promise-and-resolve; the ear sues
  over a dropped one), jokes (setup is promise, punchline is
  payment), architecture (the corridor that leads nowhere).
ASKED-AS: foreshadowing payoff twist earned plot hole cheated expectations loose ends satisfying

ESSENCE: character is revealed by choice under pressure — words
  show the mask; what someone picks when picking costs shows the
  face.
ROOT: strategy+conflict and meaning+ritual / cheap signals carry
  nothing; talk is free, choice is paid.
CANNOT: no telling makes character — a narrator's "she was brave"
  cannot buy belief that an unpriced choice has not earned.
THREAD: hiring (the interview is mask; the crisis is face),
  courts (conduct outweighs testimony), friendship (known at the
  hospital and the moving day, not at the party).
ASKED-AS: true colors actions deeds integrity test trust hard times sacrifice

ESSENCE: who knows what runs the machine — audience ahead of the
  characters makes suspense (the bomb under the table) or comedy
  (the man unaware); characters ahead of the audience makes
  mystery.
ROOT: the mind / tracking what another knows is one of the oldest
  pleasures and oldest tensions.
CANNOT: no suspense with every card face up, no caring with every
  card face down — the gap must be partial to pull.
THREAD: poker (managed knowledge gaps played for money), teaching
  (the placed question opens a gap the lesson closes), surprise
  parties (suspense run by the guests).
ASKED-AS: dramatic irony spoilers cliffhanger thriller secret reveal tension hooked whodunit

ESSENCE: an ending is the paying of the story's debts — questions
  answered or deliberately weighed, guns fired, changes finished;
  done right it lands surprising and inevitable at once.
ROOT: the promise law above; money / books must balance at close.
CANNOT: no ending by stopping — a story cannot end where it
  merely runs out; unpaid debts convert to anger on the last
  page.
THREAD: music (the final chord pays the piece), trials (a
  mistrial is a story denied its ending), meals (dessert as
  cadence).
ASKED-AS: finale resolution closure unresolved abrupt unsatisfying wrap up conclusion sequel payoff

ESSENCE: the villain is the hero of his own telling — durable
  evil in stories runs on justification, because people, real or
  written, cannot act long against their own account of
  themselves.
ROOT: the mind / self-image steers conduct; everyone narrates
  themselves the protagonist.
CANNOT: no believable villain from pure malice — "evil because
  evil" reads as cardboard because nothing like it walks around
  for the audience to recognize (iron as craft; UNSURE at the
  psychology's rare edges).
THREAD: negotiation (the other side always has a case — find it
  or fail), civil wars (both anthems are about defending home),
  history (each monster kept a story where he was the cure).
ASKED-AS: bad guy motivation sympathetic backstory realistic excuses self righteous worldview convinced

ESSENCE: a story lends eyes — the audience rides inside a chosen
  head and feels events as that person; the same battle is
  triumph or tragedy depending on whose shoulder carries the
  camera.
ROOT: the mind / imagination runs another's hours on one's own
  machinery.
CANNOT: no pointing at everyone at once — a story aimed at all
  its people equally aims at none.
THREAD: journalism (choosing whose war frames the war), law (two
  openings, same facts, two rides), history (written from the
  winning shoulder).
ASKED-AS: point of view narrator perspective first person bias empathy framing slant
