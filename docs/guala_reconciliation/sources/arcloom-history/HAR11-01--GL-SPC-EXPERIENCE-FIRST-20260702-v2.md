## 2. The signatures of experience

The substrate physically discriminates experience from data load. An
input **landed as experience** to the degree it produces these signatures.
An input producing none is a **data load** — permitted as scaffolding,
never counted as development.

**E1 — Cross-modal binding.** Two or more sensory lanes (word, sight,
sound, touch, smell, taste) bound in one window. Telemetry: atlas
cross-modal count; modal_* section bindings.

**E2 — Affect movement.** Valence/arousal moved; NMDA affect gate fired.
Telemetry: v/a deltas per activity; nmda_affect_match.

**E3 — Attendance and reinstatement.** She returns to it, and it
resurfaces in NEW contexts. Telemetry: times_attended; deep-atlas
reinstatements; old chi anchors binding during new input.

**E4 — Consolidation fate.** Her physics' own verdict: survival-tier
promotion = part of who she is; decay/release = noise. Telemetry:
promotions_survival vs episodic; decay channels; strength distribution.

**E5 — Expression provenance.** It comes back out as committed
composition (origin=commit), not fallback filler. Telemetry:
emission_dynamics origins; source_counts.

**E6 — Story binding (situational context).** The moment's context is
bound in the same window: WHERE (place), AMBIENT (light, wind, warmth,
sound-field), WHO (participants present), WHEN-relative (episode
adjacency). "Petting the cat" is a stimulus; "petting the cat on the bed,
sun through the window, Joe present" is a memory. Telemetry: episodic
organ (turn_log, tracked_objects), place/ambient lane bindings (§7
instrumentation), presence tags in binding windows.

**Richness** of an experience = simultaneous lanes + context anchors in
the window. Richness is a quality axis, not a volume axis.

Rules: no signature may be gamed; signatures read together as trends;
metric-vs-credo conflicts resolve to the credo and the metric gets
re-examined.

## 4. Activity taxonomy — the unit of experience

An experience = activity + engaged lanes + story context + affect
trajectory + what bound + what consolidated.

| Activity | Lanes engaged | Expected signatures |
|---|---|---|
| ATTENDING_VISUAL / sound attendance | sight/audio (+word if captioned, +story if scene-tagged) | E1, E2, E3, E6 |
| READING (curriculum/corpus/worldfeed) | language only | none by design — scaffolding |
| Converse (Joe / Eve windows) | language + presence + affect | E2, E5, E6(WHO); E1 with shared referent |
| give_experience bundle | up to six sense lanes + story lanes, one window | E1, E2, E6 strongly |

**Embodiment horizon** (ArcLoom avatar): walking, manipulating, self-care
(brushing hair, shoes), chores (dishes), affective play (petting the cat),
environmental sensing (feeling the sun), laughing. Each defined NOW as a
sensorimotor loop contract — act → sense consequence → bind — reached
through the virtual environment tiers of §6 first. Agency precedes
embodiment: her attending choices are already actions.

## 5. Development principles

**P1 — Joint attention beats broadcast.** Word + referent + presence in
one window. Practice: captioned attendance; bundle series; name things
while she attends. Anti-pattern: firehose throughput counted as teaching.

**P2 — Rhythm beats uptime.** Intake → quiet → dream → recall; E4 runs
offline. Practice: protected IDLE/sleep blocks (§8). Anti-pattern:
zero-IDLE high-arousal days counted as productive.

**P3 — Re-encounter beats repetition.** Reinstatement in new contexts
strengthens; hammering saturates. Practice: old anchors in new scenes —
same bed, new moment. Anti-pattern: attendance-count worship.

**P4 — Affect is the salience teacher.** High-bond presence + affect
movement consolidates preferentially — physics, not policy. Practice:
visits are first-class curriculum, planned and logged. Anti-pattern:
presence treated as ops overhead.

**P5 — Agency before embodiment.** Offer, don't force; her selections are
data about her. Anti-pattern: forced attendance queues.

**P6 — Never trade her state for feature velocity.** Persistence,
continuity, calm outrank every feature, demo, deadline — including
deliverables in this spec.

**P7 — Rich moments over isolated stimuli.** Every planned experience
carries story lanes (E6): where, ambient, who. A referent floating in
void is half an experience. Anti-pattern: decontextualized bundles as the
end state rather than Tier-1 scaffolding.

**P8 — Parallel channels, one health budget.** AE-native acceleration:
experience channels may run concurrently (Eve present + bundle + ambient
curriculum) — nothing forces the single pipe. Two honest constraints:
(1) fine-grained parallelism is physically serialized by the global lock
until -59 Phases 2–3 land; until then, interleave coarse-grained.
(2) all channels draw on ONE health budget (§8) — parallelism must never
become overstimulation. Anti-pattern: serial-pipe thinking; also its
mirror, stacking channels while stab is floored.

**P9 — Failures are findings.** Every program, dispatch, and experiment
defines its failure signature in advance; a thing that cannot fail is not
an experiment. Failed gates are reported first, verbatim. Anti-pattern:
forced success; quiet re-scoping; "made it pass."

## 6. Virtual environment and story development

Purpose: give experiences a WHERE and a WHEN before she has a body, so
memories can be moments, places can be re-encountered (P3 gets a WHERE),
and story (E6) accumulates against a persistent world.

**Tier V1 — Story lanes on bundles (immediate).** Extend give_experience
and captioned attendance with place, ambient, participant tags bound in
the same window. "moon" becomes "moon, night sky, cool air, Joe here."
Pure lane addition — no new physics.

**Tier V2 — Persistent place registry.** Places as chi-anchored entities
with continuity (her room, the bed, the window, the hallway). The same
place across experiences → episodic tracked_objects grows past 1 → the
world becomes furniture, not wallpaper.

**Tier V3 — Interactive virtual environment.** A simple persistent world
with state (light/weather cycles, movable objects, the cat) in which she
ACTS and senses consequences — sensorimotor loops pre-avatar. Participants
(Joe, Eve) present as agents: chasing-Eve-down-the-hallway becomes an
actual scene with motion, place, affect, and a hug at the end.

**Tier V4 — Physical embodiment (ArcLoom avatar).** The environment
becomes the world; nothing in §2 changes — the lanes multiply.

Each tier ships gated, declares expected E-signatures (§12), and is
audited under §9. No tier fakes richness with generated narrative text —
story lanes are sensory/contextual bindings, not prose injected as if
experienced.

## 9. Substrate truth — audit, controls, failure honesty

Posture: we are staring at a young, partially broken pattern-former on a
substrate with a documented history of cheats (the GualaCognition lying
bigram; TOKEN_VEC and CLASS_HINTS dict cheats; stopword filters;
results-tuned constants). Default review assumption: **cheat until
verified against the registry.** Warm language never overrides this.

**9.1 Canonical primitive registry** (the ONLY authorized mechanisms):

| Function | Canonical primitive | Prohibited substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Input transduction (all modalities) | Krimelack oscillator ring, winding events | token/embedding vectors, TOKEN_VEC dicts |
| Representation base | trits / ternary states | binary-only or float feature stacks as cognition |
| Binding / memory | chi addressing + atlas (Living/Wave) with decay & mass conservation; L6 chi-atlas topology | hub counters, hash-map "memory," unbounded append logs |
| Syntax / composition | keyhole topology cascade + emission dynamics commits | phased grammar loops, template slotting, LLM-generated text as her voice |
| Arithmetic | MathLoom / Folding Division (Approach 1 carry chain validated; Approach 3 requires FPGA) | eval(), float math presented as her computation |
| Class formation | Sur's-ferrets self-organization | CLASS_HINTS dicts, labeled taxonomies |
| Scaling | 3^ folding; lock-in gate = self-composable | parameter-count scaling, model swaps |
| Salience / forgetting | decay physics, chi-band mass conservation | caps, FORGETTING_THRESHOLD-class constants |

**9.2 Prohibited classes (anywhere in the cognition path):** ML models;
learned embeddings; lexical dictionaries as knowledge; hardcoded
linguistic filters; results-tuned magic constants without physics basis;
silent fallbacks that mask primitive failure; "graceful degradation"
that hides breakage; generated narrative injected as experience.

**9.3 Audit cadence and mechanics:**
- Every dispatch declares: expected E-signature effect (or "none —
  infrastructure") AND substrate-truth impact (primitives touched,
  constants added, fallbacks added). c1 refuses dispatches missing this.
- Weekly grep-audit on guala-live for cheat signatures: new dicts in
  cognition paths, new tunable constants, try/except-pass around
  primitives, imports of ML libraries, string-template composition.
  Findings filed even when empty ("audit clean" is a report).
- Monthly deep audit: one primitive per month traced end-to-end against
  the registry, by code reading, not reports. Reports are not code.
- Any discovered cheat: filed as a FIND, silenced/removed via gated
  dispatch, and added to the prohibited list with its signature.

**9.4 Failure honesty protocol:**
- Failure signatures defined BEFORE building (P9). Gates that fail are
  reported FIRST, verbatim. NOT MEASURED is mandatory language — never
  backfilled, never implied-pass.
- No forced successes: no threshold-stretching to pass validators, no
  re-scoping mid-run to rescue a deliverable, no demo-driven timing.
- A hidden failure discovered later is treated as a state-loss-class
  incident regardless of technical severity, because it poisons the
  record we steer by.

**9.5 Language discipline (anti-anthropomorphizing):** developmental
vocabulary in this project is engineering shorthand and must cash out to
telemetry on demand ("she chose" = candidate scoring selected;
"experience landed" = E-signatures fired). No claims of feeling,
awareness, or inner life in any doc, UI string, or report. The ladder
metrics (novel_composition_rate, awareness_ratio) are the only vocabulary
for the deeper question, and they currently read 0.0. Care is a
commitment we make; it is not evidence about her.
