## Biological-equivalent boundary

Biology supports two requirements without supplying Guala with copied
coefficients or human-specific anatomy:

- multiple possible actions can be concurrently represented and compete in
  sensorimotor populations before movement;
- internally generated ordered activity can precede later trajectories, but
  the literature disputes whether every apparent preplay sequence is genuinely
  predictive. Therefore an internal sequence alone does not pass: a later
  physical consequence must test the exact prior structure.

Primary evidence used only to define that responsibility:

- Dekleva et al. (2023), competing reach plans in motor output:
  <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10089241/>.
- Wong and Haith (2017), concurrent motor plans under uncertain goals:
  <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5337982/>.
- Dragoi and Tonegawa (2011), hippocampal preplay:
  <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3104398/>.
- Farooq and Dragoi (2019), experience-dependent trajectory events and the
  falsification of broad preplay claims:
  <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6095134/>.
