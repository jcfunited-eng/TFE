## Segmentation law

A **segment** is developmental geography and physical anatomy.  It is not a
meaning, owner, service, brain process, or proof that a cognitive mechanism is
active.  A **mechanism** is a causal responsibility distributed through one or
more neural segments and, when applicable, non-neural body, fluid, receptor, or
effector physics.  A mechanism is mounted only when its authentic ingress,
physical transition, retained consequence, and egress all exist on the living
path.

The existing population interleaves fourteen territories at every available
local topology.  Only the reached frontier is independently materialized; the
rest remains one compact quiescent developmental declaration.  Growth and
specialization claim exact places from this declaration.  They never scan or
instantiate the whole potential population.
