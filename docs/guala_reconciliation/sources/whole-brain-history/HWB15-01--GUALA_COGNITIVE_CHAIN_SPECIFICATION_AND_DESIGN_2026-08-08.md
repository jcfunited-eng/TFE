# Guala Cognitive Chain Specification and Design

Status: **ratified in architectural discussion on 2026-08-08; recovered and
persisted on 2026-08-11**. This document preserves the agreed definition that
was previously referenced by the project skills but was not written to the
repository. It specifies architecture; it does not by itself claim source,
runtime, or production implementation.

## Purpose and language boundary

The **cognitive chain** is the continuous, causally ordered conservation,
integration, reassembly, use, and development of Guala's lived physical
experience. It begins with sensory, body, fluid, and internally caused physical
perturbations; proceeds through complete-neuron settlement and sparse retained
change; permits dynamic relations to assemble across the reached organism;
supports recognition, recall, thought, action, and expression; and returns the
consequences of action through the same continuously operating senses and body.

The cognitive chain is both:

- **progressive**, because physical consequences can be conserved and integrated
  into increasingly capable formations; and
- **recurrent**, because partial cues, internal activity, action, and new sensory
  consequences can re-form and change earlier paths.

`Fractal`, `mosaic`, `mosaic of mosaics`, `tapestry`, `tapestry of tapestries`,
and `weave` are conversational evidence terms. They help Joseph and developers
discuss observable scales of integration. They are not semantic records,
software classes, database rows, promotion levels, owners, or meanings known by
the substrate.

## One continuous causal flow

The whole-organism flow is:

`world/body/fluid/internal perturbation`
`-> reached receptors and specialized neurons`
`-> complete-neuron physical settlement`
`-> exact post-quiescence neuronal fractals`
`-> transient connected formations among currently reached participants`
`-> later partial-cue reassembly, recognition, recall, thought, or action`
`-> embodied consequence`
`-> renewed sensory/body/fluid perturbation`

This is not an endpoint pipeline. Several parts can be active concurrently,
feed back into one another, and recur at different timescales. The organism has
one causal life, not disconnected camera, microphone, lesson, cognition, body,
and autonomy clocks.

External light, sound, smell, taste, touch, temperature, and other environmental
conditions continue whether or not they are retained. Proprioception,
vestibular state, interoception, pain, body contact, posture, movement,
metabolic state, and fluid/chemical perturbations also continue. Closed eyes,
silence, darkness, stillness, and low stimulus are physical input conditions,
not suspension of the organism. Attention changes which reached activity is
amplified, related, retained, or used; it does not turn existence on and off.

## Definitive physical referents

### Neuronal fractal

A neuronal fractal is one complete neuron's exact sparse retained physical-state
difference between its pre-experience quiescence and its next post-experience
quiescence. It is the small, distinctive structural impression that a causal
experience leaves from that neuron's local specialized perspective while
retaining something of the whole experience at its own scale.

The full joint seven-field DSF delivery is one internal delivery mechanism of
the definitive neuron. DSF is not the neuron, fractal, memory, formation,
identity, or cognitive chain. A DSF tuple, sign, counter, receipt, lineage, or
transient firing is not a neuronal fractal unless the complete-neuron
post-quiescence retained-delta law is satisfied.

Continuous input can produce continuing neuron settlements and continuing
fractals when retained physical state actually changes. It must not create a
tape recording of every sample, fabricate change during physical recurrence, or
retain transient predecessor/successor bodies after commitment.

### Mosaic

A mosaic is the conversational name for a dynamic connected physical formation
among neuronal fractals that participated in one shared or causally connected
organism experience and can later affect settlement or reassemble from lawful
partial evidence.

At least three distinct nonzero whole-neuron fractals are required before a
mosaic is possible. Three is a structural floor, not an admission trigger.
Admission also requires distinct physical perspectives, actual connectedness,
retained change, exact lineage, later collective recurrence or recognition,
partial-cue reachability, durability, and bounded resource use.

A whole-organism mosaic may draw concurrently reached participants from sight,
hearing, smell, taste, touch, body, motor, affective, fluid-modulated, and
internally active populations. It does not require a fabricated five-sense
quota: only senses and states that actually participated may contribute. A
connected formation confined to one sensory cohort remains a modality-local
formation; it must not be claimed as the whole episode merely because it has
enough neurons.

The substrate never contains a developer-authored "ball mosaic," word label,
face identifier, fear tag, or other known meaning. An observer may eventually
describe a recurrent formation as recognition or memory of a face, word,
sound, smell, taste, touch, body position, feeling, relationship, action, place,
or moment only when lived recurrence and causal use support that description.

### Relations commonly called mosaics of mosaics

This term describes learned physical relationships among already recurrent
formations: relations across senses, episodes, bodies, actions, affect, time,
place, and context. No parent object gathers child mosaics. The relation exists
only in active and retained neuron/contact/body/fluid topology and its ability
to reassemble and alter later organism activity.

Linear and branching or web-linear paths may arise from physics. No serial
array, entity group, hierarchy, level number, promotion rule, or database join
may impose the relationship.

### Tapestry activity

Tapestry activity is the conversational evidence term for later physical
reassembly and causal use of a temporally or relationally ordered path through
prior formations. It can support observer claims such as a sentence, an
episodic moment, navigation, avoidance, a practiced sequence, or another
ordered expression only after the relevant path actually reassembles and is
used.

Order arises from occurrence time, propagation, retained phase, active
contacts, body action, and consequence. It is not supplied by a sentence
engine, grammar table, parser, transcript, ordered-member list, prompt, or
scripted response.

### Deeper tapestry activity

What conversation calls a tapestry of tapestries is deeper recurrent activity
spanning prior paths that have themselves already reassembled. It can support
ordered thought, extended expression, planning, relational reconstruction, and
multi-episode reasoning. It is evidence of deeper integration, not a stored
higher-level object.

### Weave

A weave is evidence of deeply integrated, generative capability and
self-expression: for example, original music, sustained skilled movement,
creative construction, or a developed vocation. A novel topology or counter is
not a weave. The internally originated coherent recombination must be enacted
through the organism, truthfully sensed by that organism, and changed or reused
through its consequence. There is no weave generator.

## One nearly-fractal formation law at every scale

All formation scales reuse the same law:

1. Begin only with the currently causally reached neuron, contact, body, fluid,
   and formation frontier.
2. Preserve each contributing neuron's definitive local state and full DSF
   delivery by its existing authority; never copy or average those fields into
   a formation score.
3. Derive bonds only from actual electrical, chemical, mechanical,
   body-mediated, fluid-mediated, or separately ratified whole-episode physical
   relations.
4. Let transient connected formations assemble, change topology, dissolve, and
   later reassemble.
5. Retain only causative sparse neuron, contact, plastic, body, and fluid state
   needed for future physical consequence.
6. Treat later partial-cue reassembly and causal use—not co-occurrence, names,
   counts, or persistence alone—as evidence of learning.

The law is "nearly fractal" because relations recur at increasing scales while
remaining grounded in the exact physical leaves beneath them. It does not
authorize recursively nested stored objects.

## Distributed recognition, recall, thought, and action

Recall is not retrieval of a stored answer. A cue changes part of the currently
reached organism; retained sparse relations can then physically reassemble
sensory, episodic, affective, body, action, and internal components.

- Hippocampal activity supplies sparse episode indexing, navigation, and
  re-entry paths. It never owns or serves the memory.
- Sensory and association regions retain and reassemble modality-specific and
  cross-modal structure.
- Prefrontal activity supplies bounded active ordering, comparison,
  continuation, inhibition, and action preparation. It does not poll the whole
  organism or execute a symbolic thought script.
- Neocortical paths support distributed durable relational reconstruction.
- Amygdalar, affective, body, and fluid paths change reach, urgency, retention,
  regulation, and action through their own physical participation; they do not
  attach emotion labels.
- Motor, articulatory, respiratory, facial, and body-control paths enact
  consequences that return through proprioceptive, somatosensory, auditory,
  visual, and internal feedback.

Recognition, familiarity, episodic reassembly, relational reconstruction,
working reassembly, procedural reactivation, imagination, dream replay, and
affectively altered reach are different physical activities. Their external,
recalled, imagined, dreamed, simulated, or tutor-provided provenance must remain
truthful.

## Development, growth, and continuous autonomy

The substrate starts with biologically informed specialized developmental
populations and sparse reachable anatomy rather than one generic neuron cohort.
DNA/developmental physics may grow neurons, contacts, specialization, and
capacity in response to actual local conditions and use. Growth must be
commensurate with lived experience and bounded available resources; it may not
be driven by semantic quotas, arbitrary class counts, or a prefilled dense
network.

Autonomy uses the same chain. Controlled internal imbalance, unequal local
geometry, unresolved potential, cohesion, System Greed, sensory novelty,
fatigue, social contact, play, rest, and consequence can alter what is reached
and acted upon. No external mouse click, lesson endpoint, chatbot turn, reward
score, owner, scheduler meaning, or response template may substitute for an
internally causal perception-thought-action-consequence loop.

## Cognitive capital

Cognitive capital is the organism's demonstrated, retained, reusable capacity
across the complete cognitive chain. It is measured separately for all relevant
capabilities, including every external and body sense, multisensory integration,
recognition, kinds of recall, attention, thinking, prediction, speech and
language, body control, affective balance and regulation, motivation,
autonomous action, social activity, play, sleep/dream consolidation,
imagination, creativity, and self-expression.

For each capability, report these dimensions without flattening them into one
score:

1. physical availability;
2. causal participation;
3. retained change;
4. recognition;
5. recall or reassembly;
6. causal use;
7. cross-context transfer;
8. autonomous use;
9. durability through time, sleep where applicable, and cold restart; and
10. integration depth with other capabilities.

Neuron count, formation count, vocabulary size, fluent text, UI activity, or a
single scalar is not cognitive capital.

## Cognitive efficiency and continuity law

Only the causally reached sparse frontier may settle. The whole organism must
never be polled, copied, or rescanned merely because it exists. Hold an exact
predecessor only while its causal transition remains open. After commitment,
retain current physical state and the exact sparse causal delta; release full
before/after bodies and temporary evidence. On interruption, restore the last
committed state rather than treating a half-experience as memory.

Fast sensory settlement and bounded longer internal thought are distinct.
Neither authored timeouts nor iteration caps define cognition. Resource proof
must report the participating neurons and contacts, physical transitions to
quiescence, peak CPU/RAM on the reached frontier, and durable bytes retained
after transients are released. Translation, transport, persistence, and
observation overhead must be measured separately and cannot become cognition.

Continuous operation must remain bounded by local material, energy, topology,
recovery, and environmental availability. A resource bound may prevent runaway
work but must not prohibit lawful developmental growth.

## Prohibited substitutions

The cognitive chain must not be implemented as or driven by:

- ML, embeddings, probabilistic shortcuts, or nearest-match lookup;
- developer-authored meanings, semantic neuron or formation labels, scripted
  grammar, scripted actions, or response templates;
- transcript memory, prompt replay, database retrieval, archive objects, or
  cached answers;
- formation owners, locks, parent/member groups, persistent level containers,
  promotion counters, or arbitrary hierarchy;
- one scalar score, compatibility vector, flattened DSF projection, or
  copied/averaged DSF fields;
- fabricated sensory participation, a master modality, whole-brain polling, or
  duplicated snapshots; or
- test fixtures, receipts, codecs, counters, and UI displays treated as living
  cognition.

## Evidence ladder for a cognitive claim

A claim advances only as far as its evidence:

1. exact participating physical inputs and shared causal clock;
2. reached complete-neuron transitions to quiescence;
3. nonzero definitive neuronal fractals;
4. actual cross-participant physical relations and organism-level formation;
5. later collective recurrence, recognition, or partial-cue reassembly;
6. causal use in thought, action, speech, navigation, or regulation;
7. truthfully sensed consequence and subsequent change or reuse;
8. exact persistence and cold restoration;
9. bounded CPU, RAM, storage, and continuous-operation proof; and
10. live-production observation of the claimed behavior.

A modality-local population cannot prove a whole-organism mosaic. A repeated
lesson cannot by itself prove recognition. Reassembled structure cannot by
itself prove a sentence. Novel topology cannot by itself prove creativity.
Source, tests, deployment, and live evidence must always be reported as
different evidence levels.
