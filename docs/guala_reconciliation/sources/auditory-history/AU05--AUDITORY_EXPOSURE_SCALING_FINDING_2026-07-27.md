# Auditory lived-exposure scaling finding — 2026-07-27

## Scope

This is a read-only falsification result over the current exact recurrent
auditory peak neurons. It is not a hearing implementation, word-learning
claim, or production result.

The corpus authority was `/tmp/mini_speech_commands.zip`, SHA-256
`49650f2341b26d886b46b3f4fb8fed59e30300b17550f1ee4a768b3106cf93a0`.
The diagnostic selected 69 globally source-disjoint speakers: 23 for each of
`down`, `go`, and `left`. Twenty speakers per command supplied completed
physical W1 exposures and three previously unheard speakers per command were
fired without learning. Corpus command names grouped finished experiences
only; they did not enter PCM propagation, L0–L4, receptor construction,
neuron growth, or query firing.

Every exposure used the W1 physical acoustic authority and the unchanged
full-field kernel. The evaluated recurrence was the current mono temporal
owner's exact peak-relation neurons from one physically rendered ear. Full
D/M/R/U/C/P/B occurrence witnesses remained reachable from every activation.

## Question

Would increasing causally grouped experience alone make the current auditory
neurons form a selective word thread?

The same completed experiences were evaluated at 3, 5, 10, and 20 independent
speakers per command. For each command, canonical L6 selected neuron
relations whose recurrence locked across its lived exposures. A held-out
query was then tested in two exact directions:

1. context-gated familiarity: does the expected command's recurrent set lock?
2. auditory-only distinction: after removing recurrence shared with the other
   two command mosaics, does exactly one command lock?

No score, tolerance, alignment, classifier, ML, transcript, or scalar field
was used.

## Result

| Independent exposures per command | Expected context locks | Unique auditory-only resolutions |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 5/9 | 1/9 |
| 5 | 7/9 | 0/9 |
| 10 | 7/9 | 1/9 |
| 20 | 7/9 | 0/9 |

More exposure did not produce selective word identity. Context-gated
familiarity improved from three to five exposures and then stopped. For most
non-silent held-out recordings, all three accumulated command mosaics locked
because the current local peak relations are broadly shared. Removing shared
relations made the remaining sets small and unstable rather than selective.
Two held-out recordings produced almost no current q activation and therefore
correctly remained unresolved.

## Interpretation

The user's concern that five experiences might simply be too few was tested
directly. On the current local peak-neuron representation, increasing to
twenty independent speakers did not solve distinction and did not show a
monotonic path toward it.

This does **not** show that experience growth is unhelpful or that the
substrate cannot learn words. It shows that experience count alone cannot
repair the current missing organization:

- union-like familiarity becomes broad enough to lock unrelated vocal forms;
- common-core recurrence is too shallow to carry word order;
- exact contrast subtraction leaves unstable fragments; and
- the retained full-field witnesses are not yet organized into a
  sensorimotor THING sequence with causal prediction and fission.

The next implementation must therefore preserve accumulated lived examples
inside the THING mosaic while growing higher causal/temporal relations among
them. It must not promote the present q-neuron set, its union, its
intersection, or a match count into word identity.
