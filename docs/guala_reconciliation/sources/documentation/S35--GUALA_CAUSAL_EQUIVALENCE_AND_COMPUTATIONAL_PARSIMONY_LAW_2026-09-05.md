# Guala Causal Equivalence and Computational Parsimony Law

Status: ratified architectural law, 2026-09-05

## Governing law

Guala shall use the least computational representation that preserves every
causally relevant physical, developmental, cognitive, embodied, and lifecycle
invariant of the capability being implemented. Guala is an artificial organism,
not a molecule-for-molecule biological simulation. Biological detail is required
only when removing it changes a reachable successor, learned structure, action,
consequence, recovery path, or bounded-resource property.

This law rejects both expensive literalism and cheap behavioral imitation.
Microscopic detail with no distinct causal consequence is computational waste.
A label, lookup, script, score, canned output, or observer-side controller that
imitates the omitted consequence is not a reduction; it is false architecture.

For a biological transition `B`, a retained artificial state projection `pi`,
and a Guala transition `G`, a proposed reduction is causally equivalent only
when, over every admitted predecessor/input regime required by the capability,

```text
pi(B(predecessor, input)) = G(pi(predecessor), input)
```

for every observed and subsequently causative quantity in scope. When the
biological microstate is not available as an executable reference, equivalence
must instead be established from the governing physical invariants, declared
units and anatomy, known biological mechanism, and falsification across the
complete reachable regime. Uncertainty about an omitted causative variable
keeps the mechanism unavailable; it does not authorize a heuristic.

## Invariants that may not be compressed away

A reduction must preserve, where applicable:

- one continuous organism identity and exact predecessor/successor custody;
- locality and the complete cause-to-effect path;
- charge, carrier material, energy, work, heat, and matter conservation;
- unchanged full joint L0-L4 DSF delivery and every explicit field family;
- exact MathLoom positional ternary and typed Psi/Krimelack state whenever its
  position or phase can change a later transition;
- anatomy, topology, sparse learned contacts, plasticity, depletion, fatigue,
  recovery, developmental history, and lawful broken symmetry;
- physical timing, overlap, ordering, multirate interaction, and simultaneity
  when they change the successor;
- body motion, world consequence, same-organism sensory return, and learning;
- quiescence, severing, exhaustion, anatomical limits, and failure behavior;
- persistence, cold restart, next-interval continuity, and rollback safety; and
- bounded CPU, RAM, storage, allocations, evidence, calls, and I/O.

Full DSF fields, ternary positions, neurons, or learned sensory state may not be
flattened merely to save computation. Efficiency must come from executing the
same law more directly, not from weakening the organism.

## Lawful computational reductions

The following forms are preferred when their equivalence is proved:

- an exact analytical settlement instead of microscopic time stepping;
- a reduced-order physical organ whose state and output preserve the relevant
  work, geometry, timing, limits, and sensory consequences;
- sparse event-driven settlement of only the reached causal frontier;
- stable anatomy represented once and reused without copying;
- continuous tissue trajectories advanced on their own derived physical clock;
- one shared full-field evaluation per genuine joint occurrence;
- compact typed state sufficient to determine every later physical successor;
  and
- read-only incremental observation derived after settlement.

Examples include settling rigid-body motion without simulating its atoms and
using a bounded source-plus-lossy-tract vocal organ instead of simulating every
air molecule, provided the admitted controls, work, emitted pressure, and
self-hearing remain physical and organism-owned.

## Prohibited substitutes

No cost argument authorizes:

- ML, probabilistic approximation, semantic lookup, canned speech, phoneme or
  action tables, developer-authored meaning, or Chi as identity;
- scalar scores, selected trits, compressed DSF vectors, fixed output quanta,
  tuned sweet spots, or thresholds that replace causal field structure;
- polling all neurons, contacts, or world objects when only a sparse frontier
  changed;
- duplicated settlement, validation, serialization, hashing, evidence, or
  persistence work;
- caches, queues, locks, schedulers, managers, or observers that become a
  second cognitive authority; or
- omitted physics reconstructed afterward from a label or desired result.

## Admission and falsification gate

Before a reduced mechanism becomes production architecture, its sprint ledger
must name:

1. the biological capability and the causal invariants actually required;
2. the microscopic details omitted and why none can alter an in-scope successor;
3. the exact artificial state, law, units, anatomy, and conservation boundary;
4. every downstream consumer, persistence path, and observational projection;
5. dynamic regime tests over adjacent, extreme, mature, exhausted, severed,
   simultaneous, reordered, cold-restored, and recurrent states;
6. the copied-production-body end-to-end consequence and sensory return; and
7. measured CPU, RAM, storage, allocation, call, and I/O cost before and after.

If two implementations are causally equivalent, the lower-cost implementation
is mandatory. If the cheaper implementation loses a causative distinction, it
is rejected regardless of speed. If a detailed implementation adds no distinct
causal capability, it is waste and must not be built or retained.

## Current speech application

The accepted vocal body is a lawful reduced-order organ: bounded respiratory
work drives deterministic tissue/tract geometry, pressure enters the ordinary
world and cochlear path, and no semantic sound is installed. The open learned
motor repair must likewise be one sparse exact transduction law. Simulated
vesicles, neurotransmitters, ion species, atomistic tissue, or airflow grids are
not required unless a falsifier proves that their omission changes vocal motor
control, pressure, self-hearing, learning, or boundedness. Conversely, a fixed
carrier, phoneme selector, waveform table, or renderer-authored voice is never
an acceptable efficiency reduction.
