## Ratified law

(quantum = the lattice's 1/16 zJ step; no new constants). The number of
quanta delivered per interval is DERIVED from the unchanged ratified
energy law: the continuous 2·L·T energy becomes the exact expected
quantum count via accumulation — a per-site exact-rational accumulator
carries the sub-quantum remainder across intervals (same style as the
retained charge-carrier phase residue), so total delivered energy over
any window equals the ratified 2·L·T law exactly, with no rounding loss
and no tuned coefficients. Gates become reachable for any luminance
given honest dwell.
