# Guala Live Hearing Observation Authority — 2026-07-28

## Determination

The active recurrent-motif result does **not** satisfy the full-field
non-flattening requirement as a whole-organism sensory identity. It is a
useful presemantic perturbation alongside the full hearing field, but it
cannot replace that field and is not a word recognizer.

Before motif identity is formed, `AuditoryReceptorExperience` preserves:

- all seven exact `D_k`, `M_k`, `R_rev_k`, `U_star_k`, `C_k`, `P_k`, and
  `B_k` values as independent `Fraction` fields;
- separate pressure and phase trajectories for every cochlear channel;
- source order, exact source time, causal interval, winding direction,
  physical channel topology, and field-tuple authority receipts;
- raw exact peak witnesses and the complete admitted receptor occurrences
  supporting every live activation;
- bounded, authenticated recurrent state grown only after two source-disjoint
  physical experiences.

The first selection boundary is
`receptor_experience_from_full_field_event`: only physically admitted
upper-pressure-basin channels with settled, nonzero winding become receptor
occurrences. Unresolved frames are recorded, but non-admitted channel tuples
are not copied into the receptor experience. The complete upstream auditory
L5/full-field transaction remains authoritative.

The decisive identity-loss boundary is `_peak_atoms_from_experience` followed
by `_peak_neuron_id`. Each exact three-extrema trajectory is reduced to four
order relations: direction, relative span magnitude, same-polarity endpoint
order, and relative duration. The neuron identity contains only its physical
lane and those four relations. Absolute level, within-cell magnitude ratio,
and the joint cross-field trajectory are intentionally absent. Raw witnesses
retained beside a reduced identity do not make that identity full-field.

That quotient may participate as a presemantic perturbation. It is not a
learned word, meaning, speaker identity, whole-field identity, or L6 certainty
decision.

The existing `AuditoryKrimelackLiveAuthority` is not eligible for production
cutover. Its recognition identity converts every exact DSF magnitude and
causal change to `-1`, `0`, or `+1`, then decides learned-kind membership from
those sign paths. Retaining a separate copy of the full field does not make
that reduced path a full-field recognition authority.

## Exact live seam

The production seam is
`Guala._advance_continuous_auditory_terminal_inline` in
`dsf_ai_service/v4/guala_physical_runtime_core.py`.

At this seam the runtime already holds, and verifies together:

- the captured PCM bytes;
- physical auditory capture and cochlear state;
- the complete auditory L5 experience;
- transport continuity;
- causal settlement;
- joint stream settlement; and
- the verified receptor capability used to construct the recurrent-motif
  experience.

Whole-organism mosaic formation must therefore receive the authoritative
auditory L5/full-field experience. Recurrent-motif activations may accompany
it as derived presemantic perturbations. The serialized
`AuditoryLiveMotifResult` alone must not be used as sensory evidence: it
publishes occurrence roots and counts, not the actual `D/M/R/U/C/P/B` tuples.

The present live custody route does preserve this distinction. The verified
auditory transaction binds auditory L5 and its causal settlement. The
whole-organism owner receives that settlement directly, reconstructs every
observed substream root with all seven exact ordered fields, and passive mosaic
learning derives its retained roots from the same settled custody. Neither
recurrent-motif nor Krimelack identity is accepted by those functions as a
replacement sensory root.

No learned spoken-form owner should replace this observation path until a
non-flattening, multi-experience causal relation exists. That later owner must
be an additional whole-organism interpretation of the retained hearing
experience, not a transcript classifier and not a substitute for hearing.
