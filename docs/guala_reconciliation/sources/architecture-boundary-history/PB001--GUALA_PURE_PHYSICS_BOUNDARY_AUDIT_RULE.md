# Guala Pure-Physics Boundary Audit Rule

Status: canonical promotion constraint

This rule applies to every neuronal, Krimelack, synaptic, fluid, body, sensory,
motor, formation, growth-DNA, and autonomous physical transition proposed for
Guala. Passing ordinary behavioral tests does not waive it.

## Boundary law

```text
one authenticated predecessor boundary
    -> locality/topology-resolved typed physical inputs
    -> small pure physical transition
    -> one authenticated successor boundary
```

Authentication, custody, serialization, storage, recovery files, deployment,
and observation remain organism-boundary responsibilities. They may not be
called from the physical transition or from its transitive call path.

## Mandatory code-shape audit

Before a candidate may become architecture or production code, review its
source and executable call path and prove all of the following:

1. The transition contains only quantities that physically cause its successor.
2. No owner, lock, lease, database, query, digest, receipt, authority object,
   security check, serializer, network call, UI action, or deployment operation
   occurs inside the physics path.
3. An input authenticated and structurally admitted at the organism boundary is
   not recursively or repeatedly revalidated inside the physics path.
4. Exact topology and locality determine physical participation and orientation.
   A validator, semantic label, learned weight, score, static meaning table, or
   compatibility field may not substitute for that physical relationship.
5. Each reached local component and reached sparse edge transitions exactly
   once per applicable exact clock. No whole-population poll occurs when the
   causal frontier is already known.
6. Call work is measured. It must be linear in the reached physical frontier,
   not in total organism history, total possible neurons, or repeated authority
   chains. Recursion, re-entry, and hidden callback multiplication are forbidden.
7. Persistent state contains bounded causal physical state, not an append-only
   event history, duplicate field body, cumulative identity, or observation log.
8. Full DSF fields remain explicit and authoritative at their declared boundary.
   A compact local perturbation must declare its derivation and loss and may not
   be represented as the full field.

## Mandatory physical falsification

The code-shape audit is necessary but not sufficient. Each candidate must also
be falsified under quiescence, constant drive, opposing drive, depletion,
recovery, recurrence, cold restoration, and the smallest relevant one-, three-,
and four-element configurations. CPU, memory, and durable storage growth must
be measured or derived from the admitted physical frontier.

Any unexplained amplification, hidden energy source, arbitrary cap, unbounded
history, semantic injection, or nonlocal authority path rejects the candidate.
The defect is fixed before further architecture is built on it.

## Promotion consequence

A passing document or test is evidence only. Promotion requires explicit
review against this rule, an honest statement of what remains unresolved, and
production serving proof when production is claimed. A failed audit prevents
promotion and deployment; it may not be deferred as cleanup.
