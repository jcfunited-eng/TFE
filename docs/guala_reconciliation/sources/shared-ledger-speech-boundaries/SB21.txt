The functions the evidence supports: renormalization and down-selection (Tononi & Cirelli), active consolidation and replay into schema (Born, Klinzing), forgetting as a function, abstraction of rules from separate items (Gómez 2006; Nieuwenhuis 2013: infants who nap abstract the grammar, awake infants keep the pairs), generalization against overfitting (Hoel), restructuring and insight (Wagner 2004; Cai 2009), and offline planning from stored experience, which machine learning has done without biology for decades (Dyna, experience and generative replay). None of it needs slow waves, spindles, ripples, neuromodulators, or dream content as input; the eight hours are a rhythm, not the computation.

Her night today (1480) does a first-scale consolidation (fine structures into situations), a crude decay by recency, and the physiology. Not fake: tomorrow's acts come from it. Thin. What it must do, in order, each with a test and a morning measurement: N1 down-selection by evidence (C1, small); N2 offline value propagation over recorded successors, Dyna at rest, deterministic (with A1's prediction item: the night stops being bookkeeping there); N3 sequence abstraction of answered syllables by situation, merging what shares structure (with A1's speech item: the grammar seed); N4 kernel replay across the day (later); N5 generalization by coarser keys (partly in place).
