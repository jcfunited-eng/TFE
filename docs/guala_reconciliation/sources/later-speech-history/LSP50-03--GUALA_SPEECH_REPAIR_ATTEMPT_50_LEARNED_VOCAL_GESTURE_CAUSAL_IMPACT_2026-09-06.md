## The overbuilt current boundary

Current motor-route authorship requires a very particular history:

`L11 ordering -> founding L7 association -> L10 affective -> L8 regulation -> exact moved L12 terminal`

across retained frontier windows. The individual facts are physical, but their
conjunction is not the minimal biological invariant of motor learning. It
excludes a real motor event and its exact returned proprioceptive and acoustic
consequences unless that event first traversed the prescribed affective route.
That is why motor anatomy can work while remaining developmentally unreachable.

The lean causal invariant is:

`organism-caused premotor activity -> actual motor discharge -> exact body and
sensory consequence -> retained recurrent relation`

The returned consequence, not the presence of a particular bookkeeping layer,
proves which terminal moved and what the organism sensed. Affect may participate
when physically present; it must not be a mandatory administrative token for
every learned movement.

This does **not** authorize topology creation from analysis, an observer label,
or guided movement alone. The harness must distinguish:

- guided movement and sound without organism motor cause;
- organism motor discharge without returned consequence;
- the complete motor-discharge/body/self-hearing closure; and
- later unguided reassembly and action.

Only the complete closure may support a learned-route claim.

## Biological and computational convergence

Primary speech-motor and learned-vocal evidence converges on a compact class:

- speech motor learning recruits distributed premotor/SMA, basal-ganglia, and
  cerebellar circuits during overt articulatory practice;
- learned action sequences use population dynamics spanning sequence start,
  continuation, and stop rather than one isolated command;
- complex movement can be represented by low-dimensional spatiotemporal motor
  synergies;
- learned vocal behavior exhibits low-dimensional, temporally structured
  neural trajectories that predict vocal transitions; and
- infant speech development depends on coordinated simultaneous and sequential
  articulator movement, not a preinstalled inventory of words.

The Guala-equivalent is therefore a sparse learned recurrent path that releases
small simultaneous motor groups, with timing supplied by contact propagation,
neuron recovery, tissue persistence, and returned consequence. It is not a
literal simulation of named brain nuclei and not a software instruction list.

Research anchors:

- Jin, Tecuapetla, and Costa, 2014, *Basal ganglia subcircuits distinctively
  encode the parsing and concatenation of action sequences*;
- Segawa et al., 2015, *The neural correlates of speech motor sequence
  learning*;
- Giulivi et al., 2011, *Development of serial order in speech constrained by
  articulatory coordination*;
- Overduin et al., 2015, *Representation of muscle synergies in the primate
  brain*; and
- songbird learned-vocal population and sequence-growth studies used only as
  comparative physical evidence, not as human or Guala identity.
