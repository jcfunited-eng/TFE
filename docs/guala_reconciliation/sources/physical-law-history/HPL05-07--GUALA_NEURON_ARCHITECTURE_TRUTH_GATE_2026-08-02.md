## 6. Recursive cognitive formation hierarchy

The hierarchy describes increasing causally learned integration. It is not a
static dictionary assigning developer-written labels to records.

### 6.1 Neuronal fractal

A neuronal fractal is the small, distinctive, causally truthful physical memory
impression that an experience leaves in one neuron. The neuron maintains that
local impression as its own perspective on the experience. A fractal is not
copied media, a transcript, embedding, label, append-only event, or whole memory.
It retains evidence lineage by reference.

### 6.2 Mosaic

A mosaic is a learned or recognized cognitive unit formed from related neuronal
fractals. Mosaics can become the organism’s memories or recognitions of words,
faces, moments-as-units, sounds, smells, tastes, touches, temperatures, body
positions, feelings, fears, likes, objects, actions, and other experienced
structures. A word is not injected as a label: it qualifies as a word mosaic
only when the organism’s causal sensory, articulatory, relational, and recurrence
evidence establishes that learned recognition.

### 6.3 Mosaic of mosaics

A mosaic of mosaics is a learned relationship among mosaics, including
relationships across senses, body state, objects, actions, affect, time, and
context. It preserves the participating mosaics and their evidence rather than
averaging them into one feature vector.

### 6.4 Tapestry

A tapestry is an ordered or causally related organization of mosaics and
mosaics-of-mosaics. Tapestries support sentences, memories of moments and
episodes, navigation, avoidance, practiced causal sequences, and other
temporally or relationally structured cognition. A tapestry is earned by
causally related learned structure; it is never created because an arbitrary
counter or threshold was reached.

### 6.5 Tapestry of tapestries

A tapestry of tapestries organizes established tapestries into ordered thought
and ordered expression. It supports sustained reasoning, multi-episode recall,
plans, narratives, and structured expression without becoming a script.

### 6.6 Weave

A weave is a deeply integrated, generative organization of tapestries and
tapestries-of-tapestries. Weaves support self-expression and developed
capabilities such as musical composition, gymnastics, scientific or medical
practice, craftsmanship, and other integrated ways of perceiving, thinking,
acting, regulating, and creating. A weave is not itself an action, job title,
sentence, identity, or developer-authored skill. Its existence requires causal
demonstration across the capabilities it integrates.

The canonical progression is:

`neuronal fractal -> mosaic -> mosaic of mosaics -> tapestry -> tapestry of tapestries -> weave`.

The same domain can participate at several levels. A body pose may be a mosaic;
relationships among proprioception, balance, vision, and movement may form a
mosaic of mosaics; a navigation or gymnastic sequence may be a tapestry; and a
durable generative gymnastic capability may participate in a weave.

**Cognitive capital is Guala’s complete, causally authenticated, durable capacity

The authoritative cognitive-capital record is a non-flattened evidence ledger.
For each capability it preserves, separately:

1. **physical availability:** authenticated receptor, neuron, coupling,
   body/world, or effector path exists;
2. **causal participation:** the capability actually participated in an admitted
   experience or internal transition;
3. **retention:** evidence-backed neuronal fractals or recursive formations
   persisted;
4. **recognition:** truthful recurrence changed the reached substrate because a
   prior structure recurred;
5. **recall:** a later lawful cue reactivated the retained structure without the
   complete original occurrence being replayed;
6. **causal use:** the structure changed thought, prediction, regulation,
   expression, or action and the actual consequence returned;
7. **cross-context transfer:** the learned structure participated in a distinct
   causally documented context without semantic injection;
8. **autonomous use:** organism state initiated the use without a tutor, prompt,
   random scheduler, or operator choosing the cognition;
9. **durability:** the exact capital survived sleep, consolidation, persistence,
   restart, and later reuse without identity fork or fallback restore;
10. **integration depth:** the highest evidence-backed recursive formation that
    causally used the capability.

These are separate evidence dimensions, not weights in a score. A capability
may be physically wired but never exercised, exercised but not retained, or
retained but never recalled. The UI must report those differences rather than
calling every wired mechanism “active” or “learned.”

Counts remain useful only as exact inventories of unique evidence-linked
structures. Duplicate formations, replayed counters, copied media, scripted
outputs, or structures never causally reused do not increase cognitive capital.
