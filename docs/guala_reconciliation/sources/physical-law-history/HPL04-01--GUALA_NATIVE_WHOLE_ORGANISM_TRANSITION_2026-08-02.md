## Compact experience artifact

Every admitted causal occurrence appends one artifact. Repeated sensory bodies
reuse arena references, but no occurrence or temporal relation is discarded.
The artifact contains:

- schema revision and organism generation;
- occurrence identity;
- exact source start and end rationals;
- predecessor occurrence identity;
- ordered references to participating sensory and internal bodies;
- current world, place, body, carrier, need, and attention references;
- optional tutor provenance, organism intent, executed action, and sensed
  consequence references;
- ordered mosaic/tapestry/weave relation deltas produced by this occurrence;
- a receipt over the complete artifact and every referenced body.

This is the small per-experience object. It is a recursive causal artifact, not
a randomized or ternary lookup fingerprint. Its child references address exact
retained structures, and its receipt commits their order. Full bodies do not
appear again inside mosaics, tapestries, or weaves.

## Cognitive hierarchy

- A **mosaic** is an ordered distributed relation over occurrence and body
  references belonging to one causally continuous lived thing or experience.
- A **mosaic-of-mosaics** relates mosaics without flattening or copying them.
- A **tapestry** is an authenticated relation field over participating mosaics,
  body state, attention, actions, and consequences.
- A **tapestry-of-tapestries** relates intact tapestries.
- A **weave** is an organism-originated embodied action derived from active
  tapestries and closed by its sensed consequence.

Every node contains its own causal delta and ordered child references. Children
remain independently authenticated and available. A node is never a semantic
label, summary, score, database join, or stored answer.

## Derived growth boundary

There are no arbitrary cognitive record-count ceilings.

Before mutation, the transition derives its maximum possible byte growth from
the admitted physical input and current causal participants:

```text
new bytes <=
    exact bytes of distinct admitted full-field bodies
  + one occurrence artifact
  + exact relation deltas among structures participating in this occurrence
  + exact neuron/body/carrier deltas caused by this occurrence
```

One occurrence cannot scan and join every retained structure. Candidate
relations come only from structures physically re-entered into the current
field, current attention, current body/action context, or authenticated causal
predecessor. Therefore work and new edges are bounded by the current physical
topology and participating relation set rather than total lifetime memory.

The signed storage manifest supplies the available physical byte budget. If
the derived exact transition cannot fit, admission refuses before mutation and
reports the required and available bytes. It does not evict, overwrite,
compress semantically, truncate history, or pretend learning succeeded.

Raw camera frames and PCM remain transient after exact field settlement unless
a ratified sensory law requires particular physical samples for future causal
reentry. External books, pictures, and audio remain content-addressed world
materials; cognition retains physical encounter references, not duplicate
media libraries.
