one causal frame the joint field contains vertex-indexed facts and an edge-
indexed cohesion field. A neuron receives one typed local perspective:

- its declared field vertex and topology coordinate;

authority. A local perspective refers to its incident edges without replacing
or flattening the complete field. Three local perspectives must agree at
