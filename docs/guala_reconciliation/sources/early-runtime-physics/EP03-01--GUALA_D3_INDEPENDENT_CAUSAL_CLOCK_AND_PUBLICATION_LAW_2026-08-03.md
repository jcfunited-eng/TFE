# Guala D3 Independent Causal Clock and Publication Law

Status: ratified correction to the undeployed D3 candidate

## Purpose

Guala has one organism identity but does not have one numerical clock that
governs every physical and cognitive process.  A membrane interval, channel
interval, fluid interval, Krimelack settlement, mounted-state revision,
episodic height, and persistence publication are different dimensions.  Equal
numbers in two dimensions do not prove a causal relationship.

The production D2 artifact remains unchanged.  In particular, its outer
`GLMFAB03` generation `13` and inner `GLJNFT02` generation `2` are immutable
import provenance.  Neither value seeds, ages, authorizes, or synchronizes the
D3 organism.

## Independent local coordinates

Each process advances only from its own immediate predecessor:

1. A mounted revision binds one exact joint-source delivery to its successor
   neuron-fractal carrier.
2. A Krimelack interval settles the local and reached partner coordinates of
   one neuron.
3. A membrane interval settles charge transfer over one membrane-control
   interval.
4. A channel interval settles the finite channel ensemble at its declared
   physical location.
5. A fluid interval settles conserved material in its declared control
   volumes and boundary exchanges.
6. An episode height orders episodic indexing within the hippocampal causal
   chain.

Each local transition is exactly predecessor plus one in its own dimension.
There is no conversion between dimensions and no requirement that two local
coordinates have the same number.

## Cross-process causal binding

Processes participate in the same occurrence only when their exact evidence
binds through all applicable physical relations:

- the same neuron lineage;
- authenticated source and occurrence receipts;
- declared compartment, membrane, channel, and fluid locations;
- predecessor and successor state authorities for each participating process;
- the unflattened DSF tuple and its exact field/perspective/delivery receipts;
- Krimelack settlement and fractal continuity receipts;
- conserved charge and material transfer across declared boundaries; and
- content-addressed immutable transition bodies.

Numeric equality between local clocks is never causal evidence.  Missing
physical binding is not repaired by a counter, label, score, owner, lock,
lease, retry table, or validation ceremony.

## Publication

Publication is infrastructure beneath the organism, not organism physics.
The canonical head transition contains:

- the exact predecessor head address;
- one immutable candidate body;
- the occurrence identity;
- the content-addressed current-state root; and
- the immutable objects reachable from that root.

All referenced objects become durable before a conditional head replacement.
The replacement succeeds only while the head still equals the candidate's
declared predecessor.  A stale candidate is not canonical and must not be
reused against a different predecessor.

No stored global generation is an authority.  If an ordinal is useful for
observation, it is derived from predecessor causal height plus one and remains
diagnostic metadata.  It cannot select recovery state, authorize cognition,
identify the organism, or enter membrane, channel, fluid, Krimelack,
hippocampal, mosaic, or cognitive-capital physics.

A restore never moves the canonical head backward.  It creates a new
occurrence after the current head whose payload references the selected prior
content.

## Bounded restart and storage

Restart reads the one current head, verifies its addressed candidate and
current-state root, and resolves only reached compartments and objects.  It
does not scan or replay the whole predecessor chain.  Historical verification
is local and content-addressed on access.

A failed publication may leave an orphan immutable candidate.  Orphans carry
no causal authority.  Cleanup is confined to a bounded publication staging
area and cannot scan or delete the organism's reachable history.

## Implementation rejection conditions

The D3 candidate is rejected if any executable path:

- equates mounted revision with a membrane, channel, fluid, Krimelack, episode,
  mosaic, cognitive-capital, or publication coordinate;
- derives one local physical coordinate from another process's coordinate;
- uses a global counter as proof that components belong to one occurrence;
- selects current state by the numerically highest generation;
- restores through a whole-organism or whole-history scan;
- reduces the explicit DSF field to a score or compatibility vector; or
- introduces owners, locks, leases, schedulers, or database rows as organism
  authority.

## Required executable proof

Before D3 deployment, a cold test must use deliberately different coordinate
ranges for mounted, Krimelack, membrane, channel, fluid, and episodic state.
The test must prove:

1. every process advances exactly once in its own dimension;
2. the occurrence is admitted through physical receipts and topology, not
   counter equality;
3. a reached cross-boundary transfer conserves exact charge or material;
4. an unreached compartment is neither loaded nor scanned;
5. the complete DSF fields and learned neuron-fractal state survive; and
6. publication and restart select the content-addressed current head without a
   global generation authority.
