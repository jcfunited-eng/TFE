## Candidate transition

three phase nodes. For a locality-resolved displacement `d`, channel recovery
`r_c`, and energy supply `r_e`:

```text
c_available = c OR r_c
e_available = e OR r_e
x = q + d
```

If `-2 <= x <= 2`, the successor retains `x` and the available resources.

If `x` crosses `+3` or `-3` while both resources are available, the transition
emits the corresponding momentary winding, subtracts that winding's three
steps, and consumes both resources.

If a crossing arrives without both resources, no winding is emitted. The
sub-winding pressure remains at the approached boundary. Explicit local fluid
recovery is required before another winding can occur.

Recovery saturates availability; repeated recovery does not accumulate a count.
