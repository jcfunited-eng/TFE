# D3 Physical-Anatomy Genesis: Rejected Premature Design

Date: 2026-08-03

Status: no genesis schema, codec, admission gate, or production body exists. The premature implementation was deleted.

## Why there is no genesis code

The proposed schema repeated units, coefficients, quantities, conservation, and provenance already owned by physical bodies. The reduced replacement still wrapped missing anatomy in a composition codec and an always-failing software gate. Neither is organism physics.

Genesis can only be composed after the real typed physical anatomy exists. Until then, a schema would convert unknown biology into caller declarations and could falsely appear production-ready.

## Authoritative D2 facts and extraction seam

The latest read-only task-853 audit is the production authority currently
available in the repository. It records one live identity, tick 23,723,846,
two joint fields, ninety-six joint neurons, and the exact current and persisted
materialized-fabric hashes and byte counts. The production bytes themselves are
not present in this checkout.

The exact task-853 deployed commit uses inner `GLJNFT02`, not `GLJNFT03`.
Its receptor key contains only sense, topology index, sensor ID, and substream
ID. The later coordinates, physical quantity/unit, rational mapping, and group
receipt exist only in undeployed code and cannot be attributed to production.

The deployed D2 codecs provide the lawful extraction path:

- `decode_native_joint_source_episode` validates the exact six-sense domain, receptor topology and coordinates, canonical calibration rationals, reconstructed input-map receipt, sample equation, exact counts, and full body consumption.
- `resolve_exact_episode`, `ResidentTypedEpisode`, and `certify_exact_resolved_episode` resolve the exact cold episode and participant lineage roster with full resident-body equality.
- the deployed `GLJNFT02` body contains each actual lineage and legacy
  four-field physical port key; those values must be extracted from the
  authenticated body, never regenerated from the neuron count.

No existing safe production operation exports the raw materialized fabric,
inner `GLJNFT02`, or typed roster. The deployed parser is private and exposes
only summary data, so read-only extraction needs one dedicated canonical native
path; a heuristic second parser is forbidden.

Sparse-K, membrane, channel, fluid, and physical-binding decoders exist in the
development tree, but no authenticated production D3 bodies for them exist.
Their fixtures and candidate states are not D2 anatomy.

## Test and candidate values

Test fixtures, candidate anatomy, guessed coefficients, placeholder identities, synthetic provenance, and labels are forbidden as production genesis values. Passing a structural test does not establish biological anatomy or production authority.

## Missing biological decisions and typed decoders

Before genesis can exist, the project needs:

- authenticated resident organism identity;
- a read-only canonical `GLJNFT02` extraction path and exact
  lineage-to-legacy-port extraction;
- explicit separation of resolved receptor keys from historical empty keys,
  which cannot seed D3 anatomy;
- the reviewed choice of which extracted D2 lineages enter D3 genesis;
- the reviewed biological choice of initial receptor, membrane, channel, and fluid anatomy for every lineage;
- a canonical runtime-sized membrane-anatomy decoder binding topology, state, neuron index, lineage, and membrane identifier;
- a runtime-shaped channel inspector exposing the embedded decoded inventory and dimensions without compile-time guessing;
- reviewed initialization values and provenance owned by those physical codecs; and
- standalone exact-unit or provenance decoders only where the owning child codec does not already consume those facts.

Fluid anatomy must not be split into a second genesis object: it is already embedded in the canonical fluid-lane state. Genesis must not recreate child coefficient, quantity, conservation, unit, or provenance catalogs.
