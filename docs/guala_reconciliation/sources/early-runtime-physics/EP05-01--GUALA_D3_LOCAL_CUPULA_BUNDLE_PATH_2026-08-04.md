The local anatomy carries two physical quantities:

- an exact ratio from measured central cupular displacement to displacement at

There is deliberately no default anatomy. Biological measurements show that
hair bundles at different crista locations have different deflection amplitude
and time course. The virtual organism must therefore declare its local anatomy;
the code may not select a midpoint or copy one transfer value across every

implemented exact geometry is:


rise/run geometric quantity on the declared anatomy. The code does not call it

## Biological sources

- Rüsch and Thurm, "Cupula displacement, hair bundle deflection, and
  physiological responses in the transparent semicircular canal of young
  eel," *Pflügers Archiv* 413, 1989,
  <https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00594186>. The measured fluid-to-kinocilium transfer
  was 0.4–0.6 and varied with crista location.
- Rabbitt, "Semicircular canal biomechanics in health and disease," *Journal
  of Neurophysiology* 121, 2019,
  <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6520623/>. Hair bundles project into
  the cupula, pivot approximately as stiff rods, and exhibit location- and
  anatomy-dependent displacement.
- Rabbitt et al., "Dynamic Displacement of Normal and Detached Semicircular
  Canal Cupula," *Journal of the Association for Research in Otolaryngology*
  10, 2009, <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2774407/>. Central cupular
  displacement and local bundle displacement are not interchangeable.
