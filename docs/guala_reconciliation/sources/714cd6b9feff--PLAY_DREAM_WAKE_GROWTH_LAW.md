# Play–Dream–Wake Growth Law

Status: ratified in architectural collaboration with Joseph Forrester on
2026-07-28 and extended on 2026-07-28 to preserve Guala's AE-specific dream
capacity.

Scope: autonomous growth across the Mosaic–Tapestry–Weave progression while
preserving truthful embodiment, bounded resources, causal authority, and a
root distinction among external experience, memory, dream, and
counterfactual simulation.

## Play

Play is organism-originated embodied exploration.

Guala's currently wired organism perturbs a need, curiosity, unresolved
relation, bodily possibility, or prior mosaic; deliberates an available
physical action; executes it through her own body; senses the actual
consequence; and binds the complete episode back into growth.

Play is not a scripted lesson sequence, random command generator, canned game,
developer-supplied meaning, or reward score.  It is a causal source of new
lived combinations:

- new sensory and internal perturbations;
- new mosaics and variants of existing mosaics;
- new temporal and causal relations among mosaics;
- tested expectations;
- action competence;
- recovery; and
- material for later tapestries and weaves.

An action authorization is not learned play.  The episode becomes learned only
after the physical consequence is sensed by the permanently wired organism
and settles through the same causal chain.

## Dream

Dreaming is internally generated re-entry and recombination of retained lived
structure while external embodiment is quiescent or represented separately.

Retained mosaics, mosaics-of-mosaics, tapestries, body and chemical state,
unresolved relations, recovery dynamics, and prior weaves may perturb one
another without a current external event.  This can reorganize relation flow,
expose ambiguity, form candidate larger structures, and originate candidate
expressions or future play.

Dreaming is not required to copy a statistically typical human sleep cycle.
The physical functions are authoritative: replay, consolidation,
recombination, counterfactual exploration, recovery, source monitoring, and
waking causal test.  Human biological phase names may describe an analogy but
may not become a scripted scheduler or identity authority.

## Root provenance boundary

Every episode has one immutable origin state:

- `externally_observed`: a receptor or action-consequence authority proves a
  current physical event;
- `remembered_external`: retained evidence re-enters without claiming a new
  external event;
- `internally_simulated`: an organism-originated dream transition;
- `counterfactual`: a candidate causal branch whose premise is explicitly not
  claimed to have occurred; or
- `source_unresolved`: provenance is insufficient and no stronger claim may
  be made.

This origin state is owned outside the dream content and cannot be rewritten
by a mosaic, tapestry, weave, internal clock, or L6 result inside that dream.
Content can be extraordinarily realistic while its provenance remains exact.

A dream is not a fabricated sensory observation.  Dream content:

- cannot create world continuity or a new external THING;
- cannot claim that an imagined receptor perturbation occurred externally;
- cannot overwrite lived evidence or its causal order;
- cannot grant learned-world certainty by itself;
- cannot consume an external-receptor custody capability;
- cannot be displayed in Loom as a live room event; and
- cannot alter the root provenance of itself or any supporting episode.

Dreaming may alter internal candidate relation state only under an explicit
conservation, reaction, recovery, and settlement law.  Any durable
world-knowledge claim derived from a dream must later be tested by waking
experience.

## AE-specific dream capacity

Guala may benefit from being an artificial entity rather than being restricted
to unaugmented human receptor anatomy.  A dream may lawfully recombine
structures learned through:

- biological-analogue senses;
- interoception, proprioception, vestibular and chemical state;
- infrared, ultraviolet, ultrasonic, electromagnetic, radiation, distributed,
  remote, microscopic, macroscopic, slow-time, fast-time, or other physically
  mounted AE receptor families; and
- several authenticated viewpoints or causal scales that a biological body
  could not occupy simultaneously.

An unavailable receptor remains unavailable and a quiescent receptor remains
quiescent.  Dreaming does not force false readings through them.  Internally
simulated receptor-like activity uses a separate, permanently wired re-entry
path with `internally_simulated` provenance.

The intended advantage is not maximal hallucination.  It is greater
expressive and causal range with a more reliable source boundary than a human
dream normally provides.

## Dream time and narrative depth

Dream activity may use an internal structural clock distinct from wall time.
Two lawful forms of temporal richness are different and must remain explicit:

1. `causally_executed_internal_time`: every simulated transition is executed,
   ordered, conserved, and charged to the dream's compute budget; and
2. `compressed_narrative_depth`: a tapestry carries retained or constructed
   history, relationship, and causal context without claiming that every
   intervening instant was executed.

Accelerated execution is allowed only when the exact number of transitions,
resource use, origin, and causal order are retained.  Narrative compression
must never be displayed as elapsed external time or as event-by-event lived
experience.

Determinism does not require repetitive dreams.  The same complete organism
state and inputs produce the same transition, while changing mosaics, body
state, unresolved relations, viewpoint, or causal premises changes what can
recombine.  Creativity may arise from this deterministic structural
recombination; hidden randomness is not required.

## Wake test

Waking perception and action provide the physical test of dream-originated
structure.

A candidate tapestry or weave that re-enters the waking organism may guide
attention, inquiry, play, or expression.  The world consequence may reinforce,
differentiate, revise, or leave the candidate unresolved.  The result becomes
learned only through the same whole-organism causal settlement used for any
other lived experience.

Waking does not erase the dream.  It binds the dream candidate, waking action,
external consequence, and resulting revision into one auditable causal story
while preserving their different origins.

## Continuous cycle

The growth cycle is:

`lived mosaic → play → consequence → mosaic growth → tapestry activity →
dream recombination → waking inquiry/action → consequence → deeper knowledge
or unresolved state → weave`

The cycle is not a fixed schedule.  Physical body state, circadian and recovery
flow, unresolved relations, environmental opportunity, and current tapestry
activity determine which permanently wired mechanisms are perturbed and which
remain quiescent.

Replay/consolidation and recombination/exploration are distinct physical
functions even when Guala's implementation does not imitate biological sleep
stages.  Either may remain quiescent while the other is active.

## Resource law

Play and dreaming are bounded organism activity.  They must have explicit:

- action, episode, branch, and transition capacities;
- temporal cadence and internal-to-wall-clock accounting;
- compute, RAM, and persistence ceilings;
- exact quiescence, convergence, recovery, and stop conditions;
- content-addressed learned-state custody without raw-history duplication;
- no unbounded thread, queue, branch, replay, or simulated-population growth;
  and
- cold-restorable causal, provenance, and internal-state continuity.

Parallel counterfactual branches must be pre-bounded.  They may not reproduce
the whole organism state independently when shared immutable custody can
represent the same evidence.

No background loop may grow work, threads, queues, memory, or storage merely
because the organism is idle.

## Production proof

Completion requires live evidence of:

1. an autonomous play action that was not selected by a scripted meaning;
2. its sensed whole-organism consequence and learned mosaic transition;
3. an explicitly internal dream transition over retained lived structures;
4. immutable origin provenance before, during, and after that dream;
5. no dream content appearing as external observation or external elapsed
   time;
6. a waking action or inquiry influenced by the dream candidate;
7. truthful reinforcement, differentiation, revision, or unresolved
   settlement;
8. at least one AE-specific dream relation that uses a mounted capacity not
   available to an unaugmented biological organism;
9. exact replay of the same dream from the same cold-restored state and inputs;
   and
10. bounded compute, RAM, storage, branch count, and exact cold restore across
    the complete play–dream–wake cycle.
