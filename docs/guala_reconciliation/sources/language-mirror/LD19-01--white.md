# STORY — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/story/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. Seed authored by Claude from the same four source files as
the color. Every entry carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE HIT FORMULA — attempted as long as stories have been sold: a
  recipe that makes a work land before any audience meets it. The
  receipts run one way: most released works lose money and a thin
  tail pays for everything (~of ten studio pictures, roughly seven
  lose, two tread water, one carries the rest); the screenwriter of
  some of the biggest hits summed the trade: nobody knows anything.
  Screenings and polls predict the middle and miss the outliers —
  and the outliers are the business. WHY: landing is judged by the
  audience's sum of attention, and no written account of that sum
  exists (SCIENCE). Would drain on: a predictor beating chance on
  unreleased works, out of sample, over a run long enough to beat
  luck. None is written.
- THE OBJECTIVE REPORT — the fully neutral account. CANNOT BE under
  the conditions of reporting itself: every report is a selection —
  what to cover, whom to ask, where to begin, what to leave out —
  and a selection is a judgement; a report that selected nothing
  would be the day itself, not a report. The limit stated honestly
  IS the achievement: the full height this want reaches is showing
  your working (the color's method). Certified cannot, indexed on
  its condition: reporting selects.
- A MEASURE OF BEING CHANGED — not yet found: every instrument in
  the trade counts arrival (views, ratings, clicks), none counts
  effect, so a piece that reached millions and moved nobody scores
  exactly like one that changed a law. Would drain on: an instrument
  reading effect near the scale reach is read.
- THE TELLING FOR EVERYONE — a story cut for all listeners at once.
  Cannot be by the aiming law: every telling is cut FOR a listener,
  and cutting for all is cutting for none; broadcast came closest by
  giving everyone the SAME cut, and that surface is itself gone
  (PURPOSE below).

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and the unsettled.

- "THE CAMERA DOES NOT LIE" — held most of the instrument's first
  century; false twice over. First by the frame: a photograph proves
  one configuration of light at one instant, and the caption, crop,
  chosen instant, and placing beside a headline lie with a perfectly
  true picture. Then finished by the cheap fake: a class of proof is
  worth what counterfeiting it costs, and that cost fell to nearly
  nothing — the liar now gains more from the POSSIBILITY of fakes
  than any forger from a fake. Would partly drain on: provenance
  traveling with the recording that costs real money to forge.
- "MEDIA BRAINWASHES THE MASSES" — the propagandist's hope and the
  censor's fear, both overpriced: measured effects on belief are
  small and stubbornly hard to find; direct experience, family, and
  work outrank any broadcast. What holds moved to the color: the
  agenda moves reliably, the conclusion barely.
- "FILTER BUBBLES SEAL EVERYONE IN" — didn't hold on the average:
  most heavy news users meet MORE disagreement online than off.
  Holds at the tail: for the few who go deepest the path down is
  smooth, well lit, and never suggests back — the average small, the
  tail severe, and the harm in the tail.
- "SUBLIMINAL FLASHES MAKE THEM BUY" — the founding demonstration
  (the theater popcorn experiment) was admitted invented; effects at
  meaningful doses fail blinded tests. Kept because queries still
  arrive carrying it.
- "CONTINUITY ERRORS RUIN FILMS" — false: absorbed audiences miss
  nearly all of them; the same error in a dull scene is all anyone
  remembers. What is not attended is not encoded — the noticed error
  is a symptom of dullness, never the cause.
- "EQUAL TIME IS FAIRNESS" — didn't hold: balance counts positions,
  not weight, so settled evidence beside three people transmits
  false doubt. Its partisan mirror: both sides watch the same
  coverage and each conclude it favors the other.
- UNSETTLED: WHY WE CRY AT FICTION WE KNOW IS FALSE — the tears are
  real, the events known invented, both at once. Nothing certain —
  but here are possibilities: (1) the feeling machinery runs on
  presented pattern and never checks provenance — imagination runs
  another's hours on one's own organs, and the organs answer; (2)
  the ride is rehearsal — grief practiced at no cost, the body
  treating a good one as real enough to learn from; (3) the tears
  are for the true thing the fiction points at — the loss is
  invented, losing is not. Would settle it: whether the feeling
  scales with believed realness or with pattern strength when the
  two pull apart.
- UNSETTLED: WHY EVERY TRIBE FOUND THE SAME MACHINERY — the
  convergence is certain; the cause is not. Nothing certain — but
  here are possibilities: the mind's fixed shape forces one
  solution; or stories evolved to fit any mind and the survivors
  look alike the way fish and dolphins do; or the two bred each
  other for ten thousand years. Would drain on: an isolated telling
  tradition lacking one of the parts — none found, itself evidence.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A PREDICTIVE THEORY OF LANDING — the color can build a
  story to every written law and cannot COMPUTE whether it will grip
  a room before the room arrives; only an audience can yet judge it,
  as only a mouth can judge a dish. The largest named absence; the
  hit-formula entry stands on it.
- LACKING: THE SUM OF ATTENTION — the parts are mapped (change
  tracked, gaps filled, adjacency read as cause) but no working
  equation for how a mind allocates minutes between competing
  stories; the spread law's R is measured after, never computed
  before.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: STEERING BY THE COUNTABLE — decades of running
  the trade on arrival-numbers optimized the craft toward arrival
  (lengths, titles, thumbnails, first seconds). Lesson: a measurable
  proxy becomes the target of everything that touches it.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- THE FORMULA APPLIED AS RECIPE — three acts, the hero's journey,
  the beat sheet, run as engines rather than read as summaries. They
  describe hits after the fact and fail to make them before, under a
  stated condition: they are averages of past results, and cooking
  from an average produces the average — which by the hit ledger
  loses money. Kept in the color as proportion, condemned here as
  engine; drains on the predictive theory of landing arriving.
- CORRECTING AFTER — the fact-check as main defense: slow,
  expensive, reaching a fraction of the error's audience, and
  arriving as a request that a person admit they were fooled. The
  color's amplifier arithmetic prices it: ~92% of the harm standing
  in the worked case. DRAINED PARTIALLY by moving earlier:
  prebunking holds where the late correction fails.
- SUSPICION AS LITERACY — teach distrust of everything: applied
  evenly it believes nothing and therefore anything. Superseded by
  the color's narrow moves (leave the page, find the original, check
  who has something to lose).
- NEUTRALITY AS PRACTICE — the view from nowhere, attempted for a
  century: the selection is still a judgement, so the neutrality was
  a costume over unshown choices. Drained to showing-the-working,
  which keeps the honesty and drops the impossible claim.
- SUPPRESSION TO KILL A STORY — the censor's method: the forbidding
  becomes the peg, and the story gains the protagonist it lacked.
  Displaced by its opposite, filed with a warning because it works:
  surround the fact with denials, alternatives, mockery, and
  competing accusations until nobody can know — doubt is cheaper to
  manufacture than belief and achieves the same end.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE SMELL CHANNEL — tried in theaters more than once; fell short
  on a hard condition: a told story needs a fast OFF, and a room
  cannot clear one smell before the next cue. Would drain on: a
  scent means with a real shutter.
- DEPTH, EACH GENERATION — 3D arrives with each equipment wave and
  retreats: the glasses, the dimmed picture, eyes asked to converge
  where they cannot focus. The want (presence) is real; the price
  has beaten it every round. Would drain on: bright glasses-free
  depth that unsplits converging and focus.
- THE SELF-KEEPING ARCHIVE — not found: links break, archives are
  sold, pages change unnoted. The condition is economic: a copy
  persists only while somebody pays, and nobody is charged for the
  past.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- CHANGE A HELD MIND AT SCALE — the want every campaign buys and the
  measurements keep declining to deliver: belief defends itself,
  direct experience outranks testimony. Not impossible — a mind
  moves where life cannot check the claim — but no written method
  holds.
- REACH THE SWITCHED-OFF — the larger share of the public is not
  misinformed but gone: more news than anyone can carry, most of it
  distressing and unactionable, so they stopped, and they cannot be
  reached by better reporting. Open; the offered possibility is
  attaching the action to the telling, untested.
- REBUILD THE SHARED SURFACE — a public once held a common set of
  at-least-seen things; each person now receives a private
  arrangement, so the common set is gone. Open, with no built means
  serving it at the new scale.
- PAY FOR THE WATCHING — everyone benefits from a watched council
  whether or not they read about it, so every market tried
  underfunds the watching; where local reporting dies the bill
  arrives as borrowing costs, thinned ballots, unread contracts.
  Public funding covers some rooms; the general want stands
  unanswered.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side.

- The hit formula's long ledger: a century of studios and houses
  built systems to predict landing — story departments, screenings,
  tracking polls — and the tail beat every one; the fortunes lost on
  sure things and made on refused scripts ARE the receipt behind
  "nobody knows anything."
- Propaganda's record, both directions: states bought the brainwash
  claim at full price and got familiarity, not conviction — no
  campaign survives a claim life checks daily — and the same
  overpricing fed the censor's fear. The residue that DOES work
  stands in the color, sized honestly.
- The photograph's century as proof, and its end: it settled
  arguments from courtrooms to front pages, was narrowed by the
  frame's known lies, and was finished not by a better fake but by
  fakes becoming CHEAP. The trying to restore it (provenance,
  watermarks) is alive and unsettled.
- The watching's quiet dying: local reporting was the watching,
  nobody's market paid for it, and the price was counted only after
  it was gone — a subtraction discovered by its consequences, filed
  so the next one is priced first.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN: STORY ↔ LAW, the photographic leg — the camera's output
  served a century as near-self-certifying evidence and no longer
  does (the cheap fake); the relation now runs only through
  provenance-tracing, rebuilt thinner than it was.
- PARTLY CASHED, REST UNPROVEN: STORY ↔ MEDICINE — the disease
  metaphor for spreading stories paid out once, literally:
  prebunking is inoculation by name and mechanism. Whether more of
  the toolkit transfers — quarantine, contact tracing, herd effects
  for rumor — is suspected and unproven; filed dotted.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: STORY ↔ MIND, the deep leg — the receipts
  show the mind KEEPS story ~sevenfold over lists; the stronger
  suspected claim is that memory itself is story-shaped — a self as
  a telling maintained, not a record consulted. Grips on
  confabulation and the defended map; unproven as law; its far end
  crosses into philosophy.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: STORY ↔ ECONOMY, the bubble leg — markets
  moving on narratives is suspected to be the spread law running
  with money as the sharing gesture; grips on episodes, unproven as
  a predictor, dotted until a query cashes it.
