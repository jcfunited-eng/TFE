## THINGS
The units this subject is about; other homes named for the cascade.

- THE CHANGE — the crossing from one state to another (ignorance to
  knowledge, home to exile, coward to willing) that makes events a
  story instead of a list; also: mind (attention tracks change,
  drops the steady), teaching, music.
- THE WANT AND THE WALL — the fuel and the block; the engine is
  their meeting; also: mind, economy (demand), conflict, physics (a
  push needs something pushed against), play.
- THE PROMISE AND ITS SETTLEMENT — what an opening owes (the rifle
  shown must fire, the question must be answered) and the ending
  that pays the debts; also: law (a contract; a mistrial is a story
  denied its ending), music (cadence, final chord).
- THE CHARACTER — a person made visible by what they pick when
  picking costs; also: people, mind (self-image steers conduct).
- THE KNOWLEDGE GAP — who knows what, managed; also: play (poker is
  managed gaps played for money), teaching.
- THE VOICE — one particular person's telling: what they notice,
  what they skip, the rhythm they fall into; also: language, craft
  (the maker's hand shows in the made thing).
- THE AUDIENCE — a counterparty paying attention forward on credit,
  keeping the promise-ledger without knowing; also: people, economy.
- ATTENTION — the one material here that cannot be manufactured: a
  person's minutes; everything else can be increased; also: mind
  (one beam), economy (the scarce thing sets every price).
- THE FRAME — the edge of what is shown; it excludes by existing. In
  a still the edge is a wall; in a moving picture, a door things
  arrive and leave through; also: design, craft.
- THE CUT — the join between two shots: an instant jump the audience
  bridges unnoticed, and a claim that the two views are one event;
  also: mind (perception stitches gaps), language.
- SOUND AND SCORE — what reaches the audience without being looked
  at; some sound lives in the story's world, some only for the
  audience, and the line is a tool; also: music, body.
- THE CHANNEL — voice, page, stage, broadcast, feed: each machine
  between a happening and a knowing, each taking a cut; its headline
  is, for most readers, the article — written by someone else, to be
  clicked; also: computing, governance.
- THE SOURCE — who stakes a name on a claim; the unnamed source
  removes the stake, and whistleblower and briefer are
  indistinguishable from outside; also: law, people.
- THE RECORD — the kept telling; it does not keep itself: links
  break, archives are sold, pages change with no note; also: upkeep,
  computing, law.

## CLAIMS
What holds, written to be tested by a crossing query.

- A story is change with a cause: something differs at the end and
  the difference was brought about by what came between. Events that
  merely follow in time are a list — and lists are forgotten where
  stories are not (receipts in SCIENCE).
- Conflict is the engine — a want meeting an obstacle. Take the wall
  out and events keep happening while the story stops; a wish
  granted on page one closes the book there. A story's size is the
  size of what blocks its people, not of its events.
- A story opens by making promises, and the audience keeps the
  ledger without knowing. A genre is a promise about the KIND of
  change coming — a mystery owes an answer, a romance the pair — and
  a work is weighed against the promise it made.
- Character is revealed by choice under pressure: words show the
  mask, the paid pick shows the face. A narrator's "she was brave"
  buys nothing no costly choice has earned.
- Who knows what runs the machine: audience ahead of the characters
  makes suspense or comedy; characters ahead makes mystery — whose
  contract is strict: everything needed sat on the page in time,
  findable by anyone who saw.
- An ending is the paying of the story's debts — questions answered
  or deliberately weighed, guns fired, changes finished; done right
  it lands surprising and inevitable.
- The villain is the hero of his own telling: durable evil runs on
  justification — people, real or written, cannot act long against
  their own account of themselves.
- A story lends eyes, and the lending is the strongest choice: the
  same battle is triumph or tragedy by whose shoulder carries it,
  and the same events reported from a week earlier or through
  another person give opposite conclusions with no false statement
  in either.
- Every telling is cut for a listener: the same day told to a child,
  a court, and a friend gives three tellings, all honest; what moves
  is the leaving out.
- Each kind runs on one mechanism: comedy on a gap that closes
  safely (the same fall is comedy in a clown suit, horror in a
  hospital); horror on an unbargainable threat, working by
  withholding — the mind fills the gap from its own fears; romance
  on the wall between two who belong together — pull it down and the
  story is over; tragedy on a flaw that cannot be cut out of the
  strength — fix it and you deleted the person; poetry on
  compression, the sound load-bearing — plain prose keeps the report
  and loses the poem.
- A photograph proves a configuration of light existed before a lens
  at an instant — nothing before, after, or outside the edge. Its
  power as evidence and its unreliability come from the same
  narrowness.
- The assembled impression: two shots side by side make a third
  thing in neither (a face then soup reads hunger; then a coffin,
  grief); music tells the audience what they watch faster than any
  picture, and is believed; the edit invents — an hour spoken,
  ninety seconds shown, every sentence theirs, the impression
  manufactured. And recording changes the recorded: people differ
  under a lens; the only cure is time.
- Whoever pays decides what the thing is. Advertiser-funded: the
  reader is the goods, delivered in a buying mood. Reader-funded:
  the outlet flatters those already paying. Owner-funded: the
  owner's wants. Content follows the money with nobody deciding to
  be corrupt.
- Whatever a channel can measure becomes the target — minutes,
  clicks, shares, through all of which anger travels best; nobody
  chose outrage, it won the competition set up. And a thing spreads
  when passing it serves the passer: quick, fits a feeling, flatters
  the sharer. Truth is not on the list.
- The first report of anything is wrong in some particular — the
  first hours' facts are held by frightened, partial witnesses, and
  being first pays before checking is possible. The correction never
  travels as far as the error (SCIENCE).
- News is the unusual, never the important: a hundred thousand safe
  landings are unreportable and one crash is, so reporting is a
  catalogue of exceptions presented as a description. Slow
  catastrophes have no day on which they happened — no peg, no
  coverage — and diffuse harm has no protagonist, so no story,
  however large. Felt danger then tracks coverage, not frequency.
- Coverage reliably changes what people think ABOUT, rarely what
  they think: the list of important subjects is assembled by what
  was covered lately, while measured effects on belief are small —
  direct experience and the people nearby outrank any broadcast.
- The posts are not the public: comment sections and trending lists
  are a small, unusual, motivated minority, and a hundred accounts
  wear a crowd's costume — the channel transmits volume, not
  headcount.
- Balance is not fairness: equal time for a settled body of evidence
  and for three people counts positions, not weight, and the format
  itself transmits false doubt.
- Propaganda rarely sells a specific lie; it repeats until a claim
  feels familiar, supplies the words the argument must be had in,
  and makes one side feel normal. Belief follows familiarity more
  reliably than evidence.
- The business shapes the telling: everything published is a draft a
  clock stopped, the daily slot is filled whether or not anything
  happened, and what an owner blocks matters less than what never
  gets started — an editor learns which subjects end badly, and
  those quietly stop being proposed.

## SCIENCE
The math of telling and carrying, held — not mentioned: LAW to
compute with, RULE to follow, WORKED run to a real answer.

### THE MEMORY ARITHMETIC — why the mind keeps stories

LAW: unconnected items decay steeply — roughly half a meaningless
  list gone within the hour, ~two-thirds within a day. Chained items
  pull each other back: each recalled piece hooks the next, so a
  story is its own retrieval.
RULE: to make anything keepable, chain it — give each item a want, a
  use, or a consequence leading to the next.
WORKED (the classic receipt): people given 12 lists of 10 nouns and
  told to weave each list into a little story later recalled ~93%;
  people who only studied recalled ~13%. Of 120 nouns: 0.93 × 120 ≈
  112 kept against 0.13 × 120 ≈ 16, and 112 ÷ 16 = 7 — ~sevenfold
  retention from the same minutes. This is why laws, maps, and
  warnings traveled as tales in every tribe: the format IS the
  storage.

### THE SPREAD ARITHMETIC — how a telling travels

LAW: passing compounds. N = seeds × R^steps, R being the further
  passings each passing causes.
RULE: estimate R from what passing it does FOR the passer, count the
  steps, raise R to that power.
WORKED: R = 2 through 20 steps: 2^10 = 1,024; 1,024 × 1,024 =
  1,048,576 — one seed becomes ~a million. R = 0.8 through 10 steps:
  0.8^10 ≈ 0.107 — 1,000 seeds fade to ~107. The whole distance
  between a million and a fizzle is R crossing 1, which is why the
  passer's reasons rule the outcome and truth does not.

### THE AMPLIFIER ARITHMETIC — reach times trust

LAW: believers = reach × belief-rate; standing harm after a
  correction = first-story believers − (correction reach ×
  update-rate among them).
RULE: run both products and subtract; never assume the correction
  inherits the error's reach.
WORKED: a false story reaches 1,000,000; ~1 in 3 believes →
  ~333,000. The correction reaches 50,000 of them, ~half update →
  25,000 repaired. Standing: 333,000 − 25,000 = 308,000, and 308,000
  ÷ 333,000 ≈ 92% — the correction ran and nine-tenths of the harm
  stands. Why prevention (prebunking) beats repair.

### THE CAMERA'S PHYSICS — the claim of presence, counted

LAW: a photograph is a count of arriving light, bought three ways
  multiplied together: hole area × open time × amplification, hole
  area falling as the f-number squared (area ∝ 1/N²). Change one
  payment and another must repay it.
WORKED: f/2.8 to f/4: (4 ÷ 2.8)² = 2.04 ≈ half the light. Repay by
  doubling time, 1/200 s → 1/100 s (movement smears twice as far),
  or doubling gain and paying in noise. The exposure is one sum; the
  picture is the way the sum was reached — a camera choosing for you
  decides silently.

LAW: light arrives in countable events; roughness of the count is
  1/√count. Noise is thin light amplified, not a fault.
WORKED: 10,000 arrivals on a patch: 1/√10,000 = 1/100 = 1% wobble —
  clean. 100 arrivals: 1/10 = 10% — speckled. A hundred times less
  light is ten times rougher, always.

LAW: how two things sit against each other in the image is the ratio
  of their distances from the viewpoint, nothing else. A lens does
  not move the viewpoint; it crops. Move the feet.
WORKED: a person 2 m away, a doorway 12 m away: 12 ÷ 2 = 6 — the
  doorway draws at 1/6 the person's scale. Step back to 10 m
  (doorway 20 m): 20 ÷ 10 = 2 — it tripled against the person (6 ÷ 2
  = 3) and only the feet moved. The long-lens "compression" is where
  you stood, made visible.

LAW: a small source's light falls with distance squared; hard or
  soft is the size of the source as seen from the subject, not its
  brightness.
WORKED: a lamp 1 m from one face, 2 m from the next: (2 ÷ 1)² = 4×
  dimmer — two stops between friends. Moved back to 4 m and 5 m: (5
  ÷ 4)² = 1.56 — nearly even. Distance evens light, closeness
  steepens it; the sun lights the whole field flat.

LAW: film does not record motion — it samples stills the mind
  bridges, so anything repeating near the sampling rate records
  falsely.
WORKED: a wheel turning 23 times a second filmed at 24 frames a
  second advances 23/24 of a turn per frame; the eye reads the short
  road — 1/24 turn BACK per frame — and 24 × 1/24 = one turn per
  second, backwards. The wagon wheel is arithmetic.

### THE TELLER'S PROPORTIONS

LAW: speech runs ~150 words a minute; a telling is priced in the
  listener's minutes.
WORKED: a 20-minute telling holds 150 × 20 = 3,000 words. A
  ~100,000-word novel aloud: 100,000 ÷ 150 ≈ 667 minutes ≈ 11 hours
  — a film holds two, so the film of the book MUST cut ~4/5; "they
  left things out" is arithmetic, not betrayal.
LAW: a film's pulse is length over shot count; every join spends a
  moment of re-orientation.
WORKED: a 2-hour film (7,200 s) at 4 s a shot carries 7,200 ÷ 4 =
  1,800 joins to bridge; at 2 s, 3,600 — double the cuts, half the
  room to look around and think.
RULE, held honestly: the old shape — beginning ~¼, middle ~½, end
  ~¼; 120 minutes near 30/60/30 — is a summary of what worked, like
  the thirds grid: results, not a method (mirror holds the formula's
  record).

## METHODS
How telling is done here, with what governs and what breaks each.

- SHAPING (want, wall, turn): give someone a want, put a wall in its
  way, let the pushing change them; the turn is where the change
  becomes visible. Breaks when the wall is trivial (the mismatch
  bores; the close spread sells) or removed early.
- OPENING BY PROMISE: show the rifle, set the tone, raise the
  question — every one a debt. Breaks when a promise is dropped: the
  audience sues over loose ends it never knew it recorded.
- SHOWING BY PAID CHOICE: put the pick where picking costs — the
  crisis, the hospital, the moving day, never the party. Breaks when
  description asserts what no paid choice earned.
- RUNNING THE KNOWLEDGE GAP: audience ahead for suspense (show the
  bomb under the table) or comedy (the man unaware); audience behind
  for mystery, with fair play — every clue on the page in time.
- ENDING AS SETTLEMENT: answer or deliberately weigh each raised
  question, fire the shown guns, finish the changes; aim for
  surprising-and-inevitable. Breaks when debts are forgiven by fiat
  — an ending declared instead of paid.
- CUTTING FOR THE LISTENER: pick what this listener needs and can
  carry. The leaving-out is the tool AND the danger — it is how
  framing misleads without lying.
- STANDING IN THE RIGHT PLACE: the photographer cannot move the
  world, only themselves and the moment — where you stood, how high,
  how wide, when you pressed. Two frames a blink apart are two
  photographs, and one is the picture.
- BUYING LIGHT: choose which of the three payments the scene can
  afford; protect the highlights — a blown top recorded its maximum
  and nothing beyond, nothing exists to recover, while a dark floor
  lifts and drags noise up. Repair at the bottom.
- LIGHTING BY SIZE AND SIDE: a big source makes soft light, a small
  one hard shadows; side light carves form, front light deletes it;
  the ratio of a face's lit to shadow side sets the mood more than
  any brightness. A lamp in shot usually explains light it isn't
  making.
- KEEPING THE WORLD CONTINUOUS: show the geography once and every
  close-up files into the map the audience then defends — cross the
  line between two speakers and they suddenly face the same way.
  Hide joins where attention is busy: mid-motion, inside sound,
  sound arriving before picture. A naked cut on a still frame is
  seen.
- COVERAGE: shoot the scene from several sizes and angles, buying
  options for the edit, where the film is actually found. Editing is
  discovery, not transcription: scenes reorder, performances are
  built from takes thought wrong.
- LETTING THINGS TELL: the room, the clothes, the objects say who
  somebody is before a word — the one exposition nobody resents,
  because they think they worked it out themselves.
- VERIFYING A CARRIED STORY: leave the page and find who is
  speaking; find the original the retellings compressed (each
  retelling drops conditions and doubt first); check whether anyone
  with something to lose also carries it. Breaks when doubt is
  applied evenly — suspicion of everything believes nothing and
  therefore anything.
- PREBUNKING: show the shape of a manipulation BEFORE it is met;
  correcting after asks a person to admit they were fooled — a far
  larger request, and the amplifier arithmetic damns the late
  correction anyway.
- SHOWING THE WORKING: selection cannot be neutral because selection
  is a judgement, so publish the choices: what was covered, who was
  asked, where it starts, what was left out.

## MEANS
What the telling runs on, with the numbers that matter.

- THE VOICE AND THE MEMORY — the oldest channel; no equipment, no
  record, and the format is the storage (the ~7× receipt). Priced in
  listener-minutes at ~150 words each.
- WRITING AND THE PRESS — freezes the telling so the teller can die
  and the story not; the press multiplies it, and brings the
  question governing all later means: who pays.
- THE STAGE — telling with bodies in a room; staging is the primary
  craft the camera borrowed whole; the wings are real.
- THE CAMERA — a light-counting instrument (laws in SCIENCE); its
  raw file is measurements awaiting interpretation. Autofocus
  decides what the picture is ABOUT; the meter assumes every scene
  averages grey and will dim snow and brighten a black dog — an
  instrument answers its own encoded question.
- THE CUTTING BENCH — order and duration as raw materials; where the
  third meaning is made and the impression assembled.
- THE MICROPHONE — sound defines the space around the frame; bad
  picture with good sound is watchable, the reverse is not; a close
  voice on a wide shot reads wrong before anyone says why.
- BROADCAST — one shared surface for a whole public; agenda- setting
  at national scale, a generation's common references.
- THE FEED — an editor never appointed: rules written by a company
  for its own aims, selecting and burying without explaining; it
  pays makers in visibility, so a rule change remakes a craft in a
  fortnight; and it edits for one person at a time — a different job
  from editing for a public.
- THE ARCHIVE — a means re-bought every year: a copy persists only
  while somebody pays, and nobody is charged for the past.
- THE MEASURING INSTRUMENTS — ratings, views, shares: they count
  arrival, never effect (the mirror holds what is missing).

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject answers — the doors a query enters by.

- MAKE IT KEEPABLE: carry knowledge across minds and years in the
  format the mind actually retains — laws, maps, and warnings
  traveled as tales for exactly this reason.
- REHEARSE THE WORST SAFELY: grief, ruin, terror at full feeling and
  zero cost — tragedy and horror as practice for what life charges
  full price for.
- DELIGHT: comedy's safe breakage, poetry's compression, the
  pleasure of a gap well closed — play's door into this phylum.
- BIND THE ROOM: shared stories are shared references; a people is
  partly the set of tellings everyone has at least met.
- WATCH THE POWERFUL: reporting as the watching of councils and
  contracts — where it dies, borrowing costs rise, candidates and
  voters thin, and the bill arrives unsigned.
- MOVE PEOPLE: persuasion, honestly bounded — the phylum sets the
  agenda far more reliably than the conclusion; no campaign survives
  a product that fails twice.
- SELL: advertisement, sponsorship, the pitch (every pitch is a
  before-and-after) — named plainly, because who pays decides what
  the thing is.
- KEEP THE RECORD: the telling as a public's memory — with the
  archive's yearly bill as its condition.

## HISTORY
How the knowledge arose, in what order — the order carries why.

- Telling before writing, everywhere: ten thousand years of
  audiences are the laboratory, and every tribe converged on the
  same machinery — change, conflict, promise, paid choice, the
  managed gap. The convergence is the evidence: the laws were
  discovered, not decreed (why they converge is a mirror entry).
- Method outran science by millennia: tellers held the promise
  ledger and the knowledge gap long before anyone named them — like
  bread rising before chemistry, proof that HOW can outrun WHY and
  still work nightly.
- Writing froze the telling; the press multiplied it — and brought
  the payer, the deadline, the daily slot, and the peg. The carrying
  laws in CLAIMS are this era's scar tissue.
- The photograph arrived as proof: a telling with a mechanical
  witness. Its narrowness — one instant, one frame — was known early
  and priced in slowly.
- Film found its grammar inside a few decades: the cut (audiences
  bridged it unasked), the manufactured third meaning, the defended
  map, shot size as social distance — a language assembled by trying
  in front of paying rooms.
- Broadcast built the shared surface — one bulletin, a common set of
  seen things; the feed then unmade it: each person a private
  arrangement, the editor an unappointed rule, anger winning a
  competition nobody remembers designing.
- The cheap fake ended the photograph's century as proof — not
  because forgeries fooled everyone, but because their POSSIBILITY
  armed every liar: any genuine recording can now be waved away (the
  mirror holds the broken relation).

## RELATIONS
First-class connections; each names what runs along it.

- MIND: the machinery all of it runs on — attention tracking change,
  gaps filled from one's own stock, adjacency read as cause,
  familiarity mistaken for truth, the map defended. Story is mind's
  native file format.
- LANGUAGE: every telling is aimed, and aiming selects; meaning is
  completed by the situation, and a recording carries the words
  while leaving the room behind.
- MUSIC: promise-and-resolve as cadence, the score telling you what
  you watch, tempo as the cutting rate's cousin.
- PLAY: comedy's safe frame, games as story machines, the managed
  gap played for stakes.
- LAW: the audience contract, conduct outweighing testimony, the
  edited quote as evidence, the record's authority resting on its
  unalterability.
- GOVERNANCE: agenda-setting, the watched council and the news
  desert's bill, the public-broadcasting argument (a benefit nobody
  can be shut out of goes underfunded by every market), the leak as
  a move, the trial balloon.
- ECONOMY: attention as the scarce input pricing everything; who
  pays deciding what the thing is; aggregation moving money away
  from the reporting that feeds it.
- PEOPLE: shot size borrowing the body's code of distance; status
  read from objects; spread riding on what sharing does for the
  sharer.
- TEACHING: the placed question as a knowledge gap; the first
  explanation as a useful lie; prebunking as the shown trick.
- MEASUREMENT: instruments answering their own encoded question —
  the grey-assuming meter, the arrival-counting rating; what is
  measured becoming the target.
- PHYSICS: light counted in arrivals, falloff by distance squared,
  the sampling law — the camera is physics' reporting branch.
- MATHEMATICS: compounding spread, the ratio of distances, one
  product with three factors, the subtraction pricing a correction.
- COMPUTING: the feed's rules as an unappointed editor; automation
  guessing intention from a proxy.
- ETHICS: the pile-on (scrutiny aimed at someone with no press
  office and no next week), the disguised advertisement buying
  unearned trust, the aimed leak.
- CONFLICT: both anthems about defending home; every war's history
  written from the winning shoulder; doubt manufactured cheaper than
  belief.
- CULTURE: genres as a people's memory of what it feared and wanted;
  the shared telling as inheritance, like the recipe.
- ASTRONOMY: constellations are the sky storied; the cosmos got its names from tales.
- EARTH: flood myths and origin stories carry real geology dimly.
- FLIGHT: Icarus to moon landings — flight is story's oldest dream fulfilled.
- LIFE: creation tales and evolution's own epic — life demands narrative.
- MOVEMENT: the match is a story with an unwritten ending — sport narrates itself.
- PHILOSOPHY: the thought experiment is philosophy borrowing story's engine.
- PLANTS: the world tree and the forbidden fruit — plants root deep in myth.
- SURVIVAL: survival tales encode instruction; the cautionary story teaches cheap.
