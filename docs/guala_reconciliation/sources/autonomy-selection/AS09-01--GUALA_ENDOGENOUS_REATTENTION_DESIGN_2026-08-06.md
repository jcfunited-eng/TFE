## 4. Proposed law: the endogenous cue (RATIFICATION REQUIRED)

One sentence: **on a hop that delivered zero gate work anywhere in the
cohort, the perturbation available for cue formation is the settling charge
flow itself — the neurons that exchanged elementary charges this interval —
under the exact same proper-partial-subset and re-reaching admission law.**

Mechanism (no new constants, no new state, no schedulers):

1. On each recurrence-path hop whose cohort-wide gate work is entirely
   zero (the genuinely dark/endogenous regime — lit lessons are untouched),
   if no recurrence is pending, derive the settling-perturbed set from the
   interval settlement the substrate already computed: both endpoints of
   every contact that moved whole elementary charges this interval.
2. If that set is a PROPER non-empty subset of the retained formation's
   changed members (`is_proper_partial_cue`, unchanged), a pending
   recurrence opens with that set as its fixed cue. The cue is the causal
   origin set at opening; it does not grow afterwards (gate work is an
   external injection and accumulates in the exogenous regime; settling
   flow after opening is the re-reaching current itself and accumulates
   only into the contact mask, exactly as today).
3. Admission is `admit_physical_mosaic` UNCHANGED: currents must re-reach
   every member from the cue roots and every member must change.

Why this is the greed field and not a fabrication:

- The unresolved potential is real, decoded state: −1,705 separated
  elementary charges standing after 300 rest intervals, sub-carrier phases,
  retained charge asymmetries — all placed there by her own experiences.
- The direction of the trickle is decided only by the territory law and
  energy descent — unequal geometry-mediated access, verbatim.
- Quiescence stays honest: if settling stops, the perturbed set is empty,
  no cue forms, nothing opens — rest is rest.
- The proper-subset law self-excludes the immediate post-experience
  afterglow: while everything is still settling, the "cue" is the full
  member set and is refused exactly as full presentations are refused.
  Only when geometry has narrowed the flow to a strict subset does
  re-attention become lawful — recognition physics, not replay.
- Bit-determinism is preserved: identical state → identical outcome. No
  timer, randomizer, quota, or topic selector exists anywhere in the path.

Falsification tests that must pass before any deploy:

The missing ingredient is one physical fact: **re-attention is
re-excitation of a formation that has genuinely rested.** The original
tail is one contiguous non-quiescent settling run; only charge flow that

Amendment to §4 step 1: the endogenous cue may open only when the
retained experience has rested — a per-experience retained bit, false at
experience closure, set true by the first hop whose interval settlement
is electrically quiescent. Cost: one bool in the persisted experience
evidence (codec version bump; v2→v3 precedent exists in the same file;
pre-law bodies restore as rested=true, which is factually correct for the
live organism after hundreds of rest intervals).

Consequences: F1 passes structurally (the tail is one non-quiescent run,
so the gate stays closed through it); quiescence stays honest (a rested
body with no metabolic re-tipping never flows, never cues); determinism
is untouched. Whether the corrected law actually FIRES at real dynamics
(does the post-rest metabolic trickle ever confine itself to a proper
member subset and re-reach the whole formation?) is an open measurement —
it must be probed on the side branch before ratification is worth
Joe's time. If it never fires, the finding is that re-attention needs a
stronger motivation pressure (hunger-driven charge dynamics), and that
becomes the next design round — measured, not assumed.
