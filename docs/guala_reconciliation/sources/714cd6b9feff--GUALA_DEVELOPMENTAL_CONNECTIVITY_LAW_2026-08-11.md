# Guala Developmental Connectivity Law — 2026-08-11

Status: approved by Joseph Forrester, 2026-08-11.

Biological guide: Stiles and Jernigan, *The Basics of Brain Development*,
Neuropsychology Review 20 (2010), DOI `10.1007/s11065-010-9148-4`.
Biology informs this law; it does not impose literal embryology, human scale,
fixed schedules, or undefined chemistry on an artificial entity.

## Nine governing principles

1. **Progressive commitment without semantic labels.** Growth DNA declares
   physical potential. Local geometry, fluid/body state, reached activity,
   topology, timing, and prior physical state progressively specialize it. No
   developer-known meaning may enter substrate state or become causal
   authority before or after learning. Physical anatomy may be typed, and a
   read-only observer may describe evidence, but neither is semantic memory.
2. **Regional identity emerges from interacting gradients.** No master region
   label selects function. Opposing local physical conditions jointly shape
   receptor, relay, association, retention, affective, ordering, and motor
   anatomy.
3. **Development preserves causal order.** Reached need admits material;
   locality shapes anatomy; anatomy grows physical contacts; lived activity
   stabilizes, changes, or removes those contacts. Later stages cannot be
   authored in advance.
4. **Local integration uses sparse long-distance reach.** Each neuron settles
   only its local contacts. An axon-like route is sparse physical state with
   endpoints, geometry, conductance, delay/phase, attenuation, and material
   integrity. Activity propagates from an active endpoint; it never polls the
   whole organism. Multimodal episode paths provide convergence without a
   global matcher.
5. **Temporary scaffolds are physical and disposable.** A provisional route
   persists only while local causal work supplies the material and energy that
   maintain it. When stable routes carry the flow, the scaffold decays and its
   material becomes available. No manager, owner, lock, deletion service, or
   expiration counter performs this role.
6. **Development is lifelong, local, and asynchronous.** Different regions may
   simultaneously be nascent, exploratory, stabilizing, mature, decaying, or
   reorganizing. Bounded provisional contacts may grow along the reached
   frontier. Physical traffic may stabilize them; loss of maintenance may let
   them retract. There is no organism-wide development mode, random dense
   overgrowth, or importance score.
7. **Recall is distributed physical reassembly.** A partial cue first changes
   a local formation. Stabilized sparse paths may then re-form associated
   sensory, episodic, affective, body, action, and internally active
   components. Hippocampal anatomy supports episode routing, ordering, and
   reassembly; it does not retrieve a record from a database.
8. **Use changes transmission through physics, not counters.** Repeated causal
   flux can do conserved work on contact cross-section, conductance, sheath,
   delay/phase, and integrity. Physical structure therefore embodies its own
   history. No successful-use count, most-used index, reward score, or semantic
   judgment may change transmission. Lack of maintenance permits physical
   decay.
9. **Continuous experience is mandatory.** External senses, internal body
   senses, fluid state, and the persistent environment continue whether or not
   a person clicks, speaks, or observes. Darkness, relative silence, resting
   taste/smell, body position, temperature, and internal conditions are real
   input states. Attention changes participation and retention; it does not
   switch existence on or off.

## AE boundary

Do not import literal gestation, human regional ratios, human neuron counts,
undefined biochemical species, indiscriminate redundancy, or random pruning.
Compact unreached reserve is developmental potential, not cognition.
Functional tissue consists only of reached, differentiated, connected, and
physically participating cells.

Later enhancement may add sensors, anatomy, developmental potential,
accelerated simulated experience, replay, or surgery. It must preserve source
provenance. Directly writing a formation cannot be called learning, a lived
memory, or acquired meaning.

## Falsification boundary

For every implementation, prove the exact active frontier, local state and
energy/material changes, sparse contacts traversed, post-quiescence retained
state, persistence, resource cost, and live consequence. A label, counter,
topology declaration, reserve count, test fixture, or UI record is not proof
that development, recall, learning, or continuous existence occurred.
