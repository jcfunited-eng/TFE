16 kHz WAV format, Google collection, and CC BY release. This repository
retains twenty-six recordings from twenty-six distinct speaker hashes.
The bounded down and stop curricula each retain three independent external
tutor lessons and a fourth never-admitted fresh challenge. Each other command
retains two lessons and one fresh challenge. These cardinalities were selected
by exact full-q falsification, not a score or model. Folder words and
lesson/challenge roles are external tutor-plan metadata. They are never
supplied to the DSF substrate as perceptual identity or inferred meaning.
