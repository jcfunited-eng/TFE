## Selected minimum transducer class

The replacement is a **three-mode motor-operated acoustic transducer**, not a
human mouth and not a text synthesizer.

- Three bounded physical resonant modes supply audible carriers. Three is not
  an arbitrary feature count: primary listening experiments demonstrate that
  time-varying three-tone replicas can convey an utterance, and independent
  noise-band work demonstrates high speech recognition with three dynamic
  spectral bands.
- Native motor work changes the modes' physical frequency and coupling state.
  The substrate owns those changing controls. No input audio analyzer, word,
  phoneme, label, text, target waveform, or desired frequency trajectory enters
  the actuator.
- Each mode persists only current displacement, velocity, coupling and finite
  stored energy. Exact mass, stiffness, damping, coupling, and radiation load
  determine its next state. There is no phase clock and no duration counter.
- A layer-13 efferent event contributes no more energy than its own released
  work. With no work, damping reaches exact rest and allocates no output.
- The three radiated pressures add into one 16 kHz sample-domain world pressure
  field. The existing cochlea, not the actuator, determines the heard bands.
- The browser receives the exact same pressure bytes that self-hearing receives.
- The transducer is a developmental embodiment boundary. A future physical
  body may replace its mechanics while retaining the learned substrate and the
  organism-action -> pressure -> hearing contract.

The physical update must be written with checked exact/fixed-width integer
quantities derived from declared units and a stability proof. Coefficients are
not fitted to a word, voice, corpus, or desired output.
