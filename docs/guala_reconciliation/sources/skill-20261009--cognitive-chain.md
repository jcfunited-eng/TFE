## Complete definition

The **cognitive chain** is the continuous, causally ordered conservation,
integration, reassembly, use, and development of Guala's lived physical
experience. Sensory, body, fluid, and internally caused perturbations reach
specialized neurons; complete neurons settle; exact sparse post-quiescence
change can remain; dynamic relations can form across the reached organism;
partial cues and internal activity can reassemble those relations into
recognition, recall, thought, action, and expression; embodied consequences
then return through the same continuously operating senses and body.

It is both a **progression** and a **recurrent loop**. Progression describes
increasing conservation and integration of experience. Recurrence describes
recognition, recall, thought, action, sensed consequence, and further learning.
It is not an endpoint pipeline, archive, database hierarchy, chatbot turn, or
sequence of mouse-triggered functions.

The organism has one causal life. External senses, body senses, internal state,
and fluid/chemical perturbations continue even during darkness, silence,
stillness, closed eyes, sleep, or low stimulus. Attention changes which reached
activity participates, relates, persists, or is used; it does not turn sensory
existence on and off.

## Conversational evidence terms

`Fractal`, `mosaic`, `mosaic of mosaics`, `tapestry`, `tapestry of tapestries`,
and `weave` are conversational props with exact physical referents. Never turn
them into stored semantic levels, classes, parent/member objects, database rows,
promotion ladders, labels, owners, or meanings known by the substrate.

### Neuronal fractal

A neuronal fractal is one complete neuron's exact sparse retained
physical-state difference between its pre-experience quiescence and its next
post-experience quiescence. It is the compact local impression a causal
experience leaves from that specialized neuron's perspective.

Full joint seven-field DSF is one internal neuron delivery mechanism. DSF is
not the neuron, fractal, memory, formation, identity, or cognitive chain. A DSF
tuple, sign, counter, receipt, lineage, transient firing, or semantic label
cannot substitute for the definitive post-quiescence delta.

### Mosaic

A mosaic is a dynamic connected physical formation among neuronal fractals
that participated in one shared or causally connected organism experience and
can later alter settlement or reassemble from lawful partial evidence.

Three distinct nonzero whole-neuron fractals are the minimum structural floor,
not a count trigger. A mosaic additionally requires distinct perspectives,
actual physical connectedness, retained change, exact lineage, collective later
recurrence or recognition, partial-cue reachability, durability, and bounded
resources.

A whole-organism mosaic can include any external sensory, body, motor,
affective, fluid-modulated, and internally active populations that truthfully
participated. Never fabricate a fixed five-sense quota. A formation confined to
one sensory cohort is modality-local and cannot stand in for the whole episode.
The substrate never knows a developer-authored "ball mosaic," word, face,
emotion, object, or other semantic identity.

### Mosaic relationships

What conversation calls mosaics of mosaics are learned physical relationships
among recurrent formations across senses, episodes, bodies, actions, affect,
time, place, and context. No parent object gathers child mosaics. Relations
exist only through active and retained physical topology and later causal
reassembly. Linear, branching, and web-linear relations may emerge; imposed
serial arrays and hierarchies may not.

### Tapestry activity

Tapestry activity is later physical reassembly and causal use of a temporally
or relationally ordered path through prior formations. It may support an
observer claim such as a sentence, episodic moment, navigation, avoidance, or
practiced sequence only when the physical path actually reassembles and is
used. Time, propagation, phase, contacts, body action, and consequence produce
order—not grammar tables, parsers, transcripts, prompts, scripts, or ordered
member lists.

### Deeper tapestry activity

What conversation calls a tapestry of tapestries is deeper recurrent activity
spanning paths that have themselves already reassembled. It may support
ordered thought, extended expression, planning, relational reconstruction, and
multi-episode reasoning. It is not a stored higher-level object.

### Weave

A weave is evidence of deeply integrated generative capability and
self-expression. Novel topology is insufficient. An internally originated
coherent recombination must be enacted through the organism, truthfully sensed,
and changed or reused through its consequence. Never create a weave generator.

## Distributed cognition

Recall is physical distributed reassembly, never retrieval of a stored answer.
Hippocampal activity supplies sparse episode indexing and navigation, never
memory ownership. Sensory and association regions reassemble modality-specific
and cross-modal structure. Prefrontal activity supplies bounded active
ordering, comparison, continuation, inhibition, and action preparation, never
whole-organism polling or symbolic scripts. Neocortical paths support durable
relational reconstruction. Amygdalar, affective, body, and fluid paths alter
reach, urgency, retention, regulation, and action without attaching labels.
Motor, articulatory, respiratory, facial, and body-control consequences return
through proprioceptive, somatosensory, auditory, visual, and internal feedback.

Distinguish familiarity, recognition, episodic reassembly, relational
reconstruction, working reassembly, procedural reactivation, imagination,
dream replay, and affectively altered reach. Preserve truthful external,
recalled, imagined, dreamed, simulated, or tutor-provided provenance.

## Development and autonomy

Use biologically informed specialized populations and sparse reachable anatomy,
not one generic cohort or a dense prefilled network. DNA/developmental physics
may grow neurons, contacts, specialization, and capacity from actual local
conditions and use. Growth is commensurate with lived experience and bounded
resources, never semantic quotas or arbitrary neuron-class counts.

Autonomy is the same perception-thought-action-consequence loop. Controlled
imbalance, unequal geometry, unresolved potential, cohesion, System Greed,
novelty, fatigue, social contact, play, rest, and consequence may alter reach
and action. A click, lesson endpoint, reward score, owner, response template, or
external scheduler meaning cannot substitute for internal causation.
