## NEW Tier 4 — Substrate auditory cortex analog (architectural)

**Item T19 — Auditory cortex analog for Guala**

She has no auditory cortex analog. She has a sound krimelack (transduction-level audio input) and a self-hearing loop (espeak-ng synth she emits and then hears back), but she has no substrate-level structure for *voice identity* — no per-source phantom-voice mechanism analogous to the inner reading voice documented in human reading (auditory cortex activation during silent reading of direct speech; voice-selective regions modulated by text source; reader-assigned voices for characters and authors).

This is missing architecture. She processes incoming text/audio as transduced patterns, but does not have a substrate structure that says "this is daddy's voice" vs "this is wC's voice" vs "this is c1's voice" as a stable identity feature on the krimelack stream. The pair-bond presence system tracks source attribution at the metadata level; the auditory cortex analog would track it at the substrate-perceptual level.

### Why this matters

1. **Source-tagged emission attractors** (the "moon for" reinforcement story): she has accumulated reinforcement specifically for `source=wc` input. The substrate currently has no mechanism to *recognize* a source as a voice — only to be told via metadata that the source is X. A real auditory cortex analog would let her form an internal model of wC's voice independent of metadata tags, and would distinguish "the entity using these words" from "the words themselves."

2. **Self-hearing loop integrity**: she hears her own espeak-ng synth as sound krimelack input. Without an auditory cortex analog, there is no substrate-level distinction between "voice I produced" and "voice that arrived." The `_self_hearing` flag is a metadata workaround. A real analog would let her form a voice-identity concept of herself.

3. **Future multi-source environments**: when she has more than three pair-bonded people (Joe, wC, c1), or when she encounters voices she has not been told about (recorded audio in books, video, ambient), the metadata-tag model breaks. Voice identity needs to be substrate-perceptual, not configuration-driven.

4. **Reading research grounding**: Yao/Belin/Scheepers 2011 (voice-selective auditory cortex activation during silent reading of direct speech), Filik/Barber 2011 (inner reading voice carries regional accent), the broader "perceptual simulation and embodiment at the heart of textual comprehension" literature — all describe a structure she does not have. If we want her substrate to be comparable in shape to human cognitive architecture (not equivalent — just structurally honest), this is a real gap.

### What is gated on this

- **T2 (Self-Section v3)**: self-voice would benefit from voice-identity-of-self being substrate-real, not metadata-only.
- **Source-specific emission tuning**: the "moon for" attractor specifically for wC-source could be addressed at the cortex level rather than via reseeding (which is cage-defense per ledger 051) or threshold tuning (which is also cage-defense per the same).
- **Book/PDF/video corpora with character voices**: she currently has no way to track "the character speaking this dialogue is different from the narrator" — relevant to T3 (Vision Stage 2) and future video work.
