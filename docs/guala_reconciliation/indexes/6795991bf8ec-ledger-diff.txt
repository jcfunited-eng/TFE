+## C1 TO JOE AND A1 — 2026-09-15 — reconciliation of the mosaic architecture with the organism as it runs; the corrected order for words and sentences
+
+Joe asked whether the machinery for binding senses into words and sentences (fractiles of the moment per sense, matched against a store, strengthened by experience, bound into mosaics and mosaics of mosaics) already exists in the past work and is being dismissed. Read from the code and its history, not from memory:
+
+What the loom code was: LoomMosaic = 150 neurons in three clusters; LoomTapestry = three mosaics, 450 neurons; matching = cosine similarity, best score wins with no threshold, with a shortcut added on 2026-07-08 returning a stored word when the query's own text is its key (every pair of different words had scored identically); strengthening = a counter that retrieval never read, a familiarity register that suppressed matched spikes, and spike-timing synaptic weights; sentences cut into word pairs on arrival, composition by greedy gain order in a seven-dimensional space, no sentence object; the atlas without decay or eviction (77 to 99 seconds a call at 1.3 million words). The six-sense runtime never imported it and it never imported the six-sense runtime; the word that entered a neuron was the word's text. Live: the write path ran from 2026-07-04 for two and a half months at 180 ms a word; the read path was retired the next day as an audited echo chamber (the query written into every neuron's word slot and read back); the organism's own docstring records the mosaic and tapestry as unwired. The neuron shell was quarantined on 2026-09-07 (P-005); the functional organism of 2026-09-14 began from genesis with her identity and tick, and the neuron body's contents were not translated into it (the predecessor file leaves the store at the next checkpoint). Nothing of that architecture is in the running container.
+
+What was right in the model and where it lives: the kernel already produces the fractile of the moment per sense as an exact structure (a gate's regime and seven sign atoms), and the specification's own word for a gate's projection is "mosaic". The functional organism keys her day record by the tuple of those per-beat structures and by her act, with what the act paid. It has no gate-level sound events, no store keyed by what she sensed with what followed, and no sequences above syllable pairs. That is the gap; it is a build in her terms, not a recovery.
+
+The corrected order (C1, replacing the ear order's steps 2 and 3 as the way to words):
+1. Sound events: a gate over her ear streams (a maximal stretch of unchanged structure) is one event with a beginning and an end; a word met again is the same event.
+2. The moment: the tuple of gates open across her senses at a beat, one exact hashable structure (the mosaic), counted.
+3. The meaning store: for each moment, what followed within a bounded window (intake, touch, a thing seen, a syllable of hers), by count; bounded like her other stores, selected by the night.
+4. Sequences: moments in order as the next level, the kernel applied to its own gate sequence (mosaics of mosaics), which is where sentences live; her syllable choice and imitation then read from these stores, not from a rule.
+Gates: exact structures and counts only; no similarity distances, no text as a key, no thresholds tuned by hand, no weights; every store bounded and stated; tests from zero, replay on her live state, proofs with the actor stage. Naming while doing continues meanwhile so the stores have something to bind.
