    The functional organism: declared act laws over her measured state, the kernel over her sensed streams, bounded memory; no neurons, charge or muscles in the acting path (Joe, 2026-09-14)
    
    - guala_functional_organism.py: senses (world sight, camera, ear, touch, hunger) as measured streams; uf_core L0-L4 over each stream's trailing window every beat; familiarity/episode/sound memory with fixed bounds; acts: bite when hungry with food at the mouth (held or hand-fed), release an eaten core, grasp food in hand reach, turn and step toward seen food, keep one wander goal, attend to new structure, babble through her own airway when idle and imitate a heard sound with her nearest own syllable; metabolism in micrograms at the world's tastant masses; JSON body under 64 KB, restores byte-exact; the actor's runtime contract.
    - guala_functional_loop.py: one beat = senses in, one decision, the world validates her commands in order (sidesteps when a stride is blocked), passive interval otherwise; her voice rides the pressure feed and is heard by her next beat.
    - lean_production_app.py: restores a functional CURRENT body, or converts a native CURRENT body once at the same identity and tick (native envelope retained as predecessor); FunctionalPhysicalLoop replaces the native loop.
    - gualaloom.html: beat line shows her act and reason, kernel reading, what she ate, hunger, lifetime counts, her syllable.
    - manifest: native_resident_organism.py, lean_physical_loop.py, guala_motor_world.py leave the runtime closure; articulatory_self_vocal_mechanics.py joins it.
    - tests: 8 organism/loop tests on the real home world; app startup tests for restore and conversion; page literals; packaging closure.
