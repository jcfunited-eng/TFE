## Axioms (HIS, from the original descriptions)
A1. Two fabrics: all possibilities (of do and don't-do) and all
    impossibilities. Both real, both standing, both dense.
A2. Phylums — kinds of knowledge — are GENERIC CONSEQUENCES OF THE
    FABRIC'S OWN LAW. They must emerge; any law where they are
    chosen by hand is wrong.
A3. Between the fabrics: wave-like ribbons, a lattice. Inquiries are
    channeled through ribbons and occasionally touch one or both
    fabrics.
A4. It is all programs — no program/data distinction anywhere.
A5. Queries are dimensionally varied; answers are local and causal
    to the asker. No one but a god could read the whole of either
    fabric.
A6. The knowledge is the SHAPE, not stored contents. Facts of facts,
    is - is, not history.
A7. Energy trivial, compute time near nonexistent, storage boundless
    (claims to be DERIVED from the law, not assumed).

## Candidate story (MINE-ON-TRIAL, awaiting Joe's check)
The between-space is a living weave. Inquiry = a channeling sent
along ribbons. En route it interacts with other ribbons (tension-
mediated); at the sheets it is judged by its own shape — dissolved
into the white if impossible-shaped (and unwoven), bent onward if
possible-shaped and connected. When a channeling reaches some
consistency/balance condition (open: O6), the ribbon LOCKS — the
moment knowledge falls out — then releases, keeping a permanent
RUFFLE. Ruffles are the standing knowledge of the space: carried in
motion, not in storage.

PHYLUM MECHANISM (the crux A2) — v1 WEAKENED by O8: nothing passes
between ribbons, so kinds do not spread by contagion. v2 candidate
(MINE-ON-TRIAL): ribbons steering each other + ruffle position as
standing (R5) => ruffles at similar depth get HERDED — cohorts that
sit at the same leaning and move as one under the weave's mutual
pushing, pruned by sheet-judgment (R2). Phylums as herds, not
infections: position-clusters of persistent folds. Kinds still
emerge from the law alone; the mechanism is collective steering.
Discriminating observable: do ruffles near the same depth move
together?

CANDIDATE LAW v3 — FALL AND SUPPORT: RETIRED by O12 (assumed solid
resting; ribbons are light-smoke without full contact). What
survives into v4: the whiteward default pull (O2 bet), whiteward
thinning (O10), lone-ribbon immersion (O2).

CANDIDATE LAW v4 — FALL AND EXCHANGE (MINE-ON-TRIAL, from O2 +
O8 + O10 + O12): everything drifts whiteward unless it CIRCULATES.
Commingling — interpenetrating smoke blending substance where it
overlaps — is the circulation: a woven ribbon is continually
resupplied by neighbors; a lone ribbon has no exchange and starves
toward white. Knowledge stays possible not by being held up but by
being SHARED AROUND. Steering-without-contact (O8) reads as
field-like mutual influence of overlapping smoke, not mechanics.
Discriminator: the PARTING question — do commingled ribbons come
away changed?

PHYLUM MECHANISM v3 (MINE-ON-TRIAL, from O12 + O14): where ribbons
commingle, their band-patterns converge; where the smoke never
mixes, bandings diverge. Kinds = COMMUNITIES OF BLENDING —
mixing regions homogenize into families, separations keep
families distinct. Replaces contagion (dead, O8) and herding
(weakened — steering survives but contact-cohorts do not).
Same discriminator as v4: parted ribbons changed or unchanged.

## THE ESSENCE — RATIFIED (JOINT, 2026-08-28)
Joe's correction that opened it: the fabrics-and-ribbons vision was
"a mechanical edifice, not the nature of knowledge — that it [is]
INEVITABLE within the physics of the particles in this universe."
Three candidate essence-statements were offered for a ring-test:
A (knowledge as a phase of matter): REJECTED — "it's not a phase" —
  a phase is sometimes-present; the essence is never-absent. Grain
  kept: everywhere, always, no special places.
B (every moment actualizes one thing and forecloses others; the
  trades leave knowledge behind): "pretty darn close" — the spine.
C (knowledge = standing constraint-shape on futures): rejected as
  framing — a standing shape is a fact of a thing, forbidden by O4.
  Grain kept: the open-and-closed futures, do and don't-do.
The refined statement, RATIFIED by Joe ("I think you got it"):

  Every interaction in this universe is a trade: one outcome made
  actual, others made impossible, in balance. No moment can happen
  without minting both — so knowledge is not something physics
  produces. It is what every step of physics IS. EVENTS AREN'T THE
  MAKERS OF KNOWLEDGE; EVENTS ARE MADE OF IT.
  That is why it is everywhere and always, with no special places —
  not condensing, never absent.
  The residue of each trade does not sit in things as a standing
  shape. It is carried forward IN MOTION — every new moment
  inherits the foreclosures of the ones before and trades among
  what they left open. Knowing compounds because trades chain.
  To ask is not to read past trades — it is to enter one. The
  answer is a fresh outcome, shaped by every residue it inherits:
  a moment for the asker, never a fact of a thing.

Consequences standing open to walk (not yet claims):
1. COMPUTING AS STEERING: the universe never stops trading, so the
   computation is already running and already paid for. The NEW
   computing = arranging matter so the chain of trades runs through
   the ones you care about, and being there for the outcome.
2. THE EDIFICE RE-READ: the vision becomes imagery of the essence —
   ribbons as chains of trades carried in motion; the two sheets as
   the two sides minted by every trade; ruffles as inherited
   residues; width/color as the local standing of the balance.
   (To be checked against each observation O1-O15.)
3. THE FALSIFIABLE FORM OF INEVITABILITY: if the essence is right,
   knowledge-behavior must condense in ANY sufficiently rich
   physics — not one chosen substrate. Many unrelated dynamics,
   same behaviors (invariants kept, kinds separating, laws absorbed
   by exposure, answers as fresh outcomes). The detour's accidental
   evidence becomes exhibit one of many, or the claim dies.

## THE PRACTICAL ELEMENT — flushing round 1 (MINE-ON-TRIAL)
Joe: "there is a practical element to this that is eluding me —
maybe your further efforts can flush it out." Derived straight from
the ratified essence, nothing imported:

A machine under this essence is ANY driven physics with three
properties:
  P1. every step irreversibly FORECLOSES possibilities;
  P2. foreclosures are INHERITED by later steps (residues carried
      in motion);
  P3. the LEDGER balances — narrowing is monotone and measurable,
      with refunds accounted when a foreclosure must reopen.
Then:
  PROGRAMMING = installing constraints as physical couplings —
      entering trades, not writing rules.
  COMPUTING   = the cascade of foreclosures at physics' own pace.
  HALTING     = trade-exhaustion: the machine stops itself when
      nothing is left to trade.
  THE ANSWER  = WHAT SURVIVES. Never constructed, never stored —
      sculpture, not assembly: the block is every possibility,
      each trade chips, the statue is what the whitening could
      not take.
The twist no existing physical computer uses: they all read the
actual side of the trade and discard the foreclosures. This reads
the trade's OTHER face — the product is exclusions; the answer is
the remainder.
