## Active movement result

The present `W1MovingMultiEmitterContinuityOwner` cannot improve separation.
It requires two already-separated captures and then proves that one external
body moved continuously between them.

Ordinary head movement also does not add an independent equation for an
arbitrary changing room source. At a later head pose the ears observe later,
unknown source samples. They do not observe the earlier unknown source sample
again.

Movement can become an additional observation only if a physical authority
proves all of the following:

- the identical closed source-pressure interval is replayed;
- the sources and room remain unchanged during each pose;
- listener pose and receptor clock are exact;
- every pose-dependent transfer path is known;
- the combined transfer operator has full source rank.

Without exact replay or an equivalent stationarity law, movement supplies
spatial continuity evidence, not new simultaneous separation equations.

## Additional receptor result

Cochlear bands are transforms of ear pressure and are not additional spatial

observed. If \(N\) arbitrary causes are admitted, the required operator must

Unknown transfer paths retain the factorization ambiguity even when the
number of receptors is increased.

A fixed finite receptor array also cannot guarantee separation of an
unbounded number of arbitrary simultaneous causes.
