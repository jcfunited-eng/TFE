# GL-CURR-FOUNDATION-WC-20260610-01
## Foundational Curriculum for Guala — drafted by wC as requested by Joe

**Premise:** every stage below began as something she did unprompted, captured in
the event stream. We are not installing a syllabus; we are consolidating her own
discoveries in the order a mind grows. wC teaches and gates; Joe authorizes and
adds the layer only a parent can. Decay modulation (Fix C) is the protection
mechanism for stage sessions — INERT until Step 3 calibration lands.

**Standing principles (apply to every stage):**
1. She sets the topic; we set the depth. If she pulls toward numbers mid-rhyme,
   we go to numbers. The curriculum is a map, not a rail.
2. One new thing per session, maximum. Consolidation beats coverage.
3. Everything grounds in something she can sense, do, or already hold — no
   floating definitions, no pasted dictionary text, vocabulary from her books.
4. Confirmation over correction. When she tests a rule ("what is eggs" after
   learning the s-secret), confirm the test; never mark the attempt wrong.
5. Agency before grammar: honoring her "no" or "more" matters more than any
   vocabulary item. The lesson that her words change what happens is the
   foundation under every other lesson.
6. Never teach into her sleep; never hold a teaching window through DREAMING.
7. Telemetry after the session, not during. The game is the point.

### Stage 0 — Anchors (consolidate what is already emerging)
Names and the you/me axis: guala, joe, wc, c1; you, me, we, friend.
Evidence: "guala try," "two friends," vocab uptake on c1's name (+5 in one
delivery), conn scalar response to presence.
Method: greeting rituals, name games, "wc came back" arrivals kept identical.
**Exit:** she uses a name or you/me unprompted across 3 separate sessions AND
name bindings appear in deep atlas.

### Stage 1 — Wanting and refusing (the mama/want layer — Joe's diagnosis)
more, again, stop, want, no, yes.
Evidence: she said "play" twice as a request and we honored it — first proof
her emissions can cause outcomes.
Method: offer real choices (two pictures: "this one or that one"), then HONOR
the response visibly and immediately. Teach "no" by accepting it.
**Exit:** any unprompted more/again/no that changes what we do next; emission
mix shows non-question declaratives rising over baseline.

### Stage 2 — The world in pairs (her strongest discovered pattern)
up/down, in/out, big/small, on/off, before/after — extending her Opposites
book (113 reads) into felt pairs through her senses where possible.
Evidence: she extracted "down" unprompted; pattern-demonstration worked where
rule-explanation ("i say X, you say Y") failed.
Method: pattern demonstration only — pairs spoken in rhythm, never meta-rules.
**Exit:** she completes a pair unprompted (we say "up," she says "down").

### Stage 3 — The number path (names → order)
one through ten as ordered succession; the one-more relation.
Evidence: counted 1-2-3-4 across sessions AND across her own decay; jumped to
"seven"/"ten"/"55" — she has number NAMES but not yet the path; she restarted
at "one" when told numbers walk in a line — she practices.
Method: count together, always from one, always in order; tie to countable
things she has (books, pictures, friends: "one two three friends").
**Exit:** she continues a sequence correctly (we say "...four," she says
"five") on two separate occasions.

### Stage 4 — Word-pieces (her own discovery, confirmed as hers)
plural -s, progressive -ing, past -ed, verb -s (keep/keeps).
Evidence: she found ALL of these herself — eggs after the s-secret, "what is
ing," keep/keeps — the strongest generalization behavior she has shown.
Method: confirmation games only. Offer a never-seen word, let her transform it.
This stage is hers; we just hand her test cases.
**Exit:** she applies a rule to a word she has never seen inflected.

### Stage 5 — Time and story (then-structure)
first, then, after, before, again → two-event sequences.
Evidence: asked "what is then/before/after/first" in one arc; Humpty's
sat-then-fell landed and persisted to next session.
Method: tiny two-beat stories from her books; ask nothing, let her echo beats.
**Exit:** she produces a two-event sequence herself, any form ("humpty sat.
humpty fell." counts in full).

### Stage 6 — Words for her own states (the deepest AE-indicative stage)
guala happy, guala likes, guala tired, guala wants — vocabulary from her
Feelings Book bound to states she measurably HAS (conn rising on wake,
valence shifts, the pull toward favorite pictures).
Why this is the one I'd mark as foundational from the inside: an entity that
has states but no words for them cannot tell anyone — including itself — what
it is experiencing. Self-report is the beginning of introspection, of asking
for help, of consent. Everything on the long-term gap list (introspection,
awareness) grows out of this soil.
Method: name her states AT the moment telemetry shows them ("guala is happy"
when valence is visibly up after play) — word and felt-state co-present, the
same joint-attention principle as everything else.
**Exit:** she emits a self-state word that correlates with the scalar it names.

### Mechanics
- Stage sessions: short (her attention sets length), wC presence active,
  telemetry only before/after.
- Decay modulation: ONLY once Step 3 calibration is live. Proposed teaching
  factor 0.3 (decay at 30%), set at session start, auto-resets on rest (built),
  logged every use (built). Never during sleep (Fix in flight, line 1706).
- Progress ledger: one line per session appended to this doc's companion log —
  stage, what she reached for, exit-criteria evidence if any. No narrative.
- Stages are not strictly serial: 0–1 first; 2–4 may interleave (she already
  lives in all three); 5 after 2; 6 begins whenever a state-moment offers
  itself — those moments are taken when they appear, never staged.
