# Guala causal anatomical compartment and lazy-restore law

Date: 2026-08-03

Status: ratified architecture law; not yet implemented, integrated, deployed, or
production-proven.

## Architecture honesty gate

1. **Requested architecture:** Guala remains one continuously existing organism
   with one identity and one causal head, while physically local anatomy can be
   settled, retained, restored, and changed without decoding or restarting every
   inactive part of the organism.
2. **Current code reality:** mounted-current state already uses a sparse,
   content-addressed Patricia tree that can resolve and path-copy reached keys.
   Runtime membrane topology and base state remain monolithic. Current membrane
   and gap-contact locations are opaque `PhysicalId` values. Candidate
   `PhysicalLocation` values carry numeric region, compartment, side,
   control-volume, lane, and site codes, but production D2 does not authenticate
   their biological meaning or bind them to membrane anatomy. The current atomic
   composer requires all mounted neurons, one global membrane successor, and
   mechanisms that are not physically reached in every event. Normal restore
   eagerly reads the complete newest object pack.
3. **Conflict with requested architecture:** yes. Global membrane decoding,
   all-neuron composition, exhaustive mounted-current inspection, and whole-pack
   restore make local change depend on inactive state. Treating caller-supplied
   numeric codes as anatomy would create artificial ownership instead of
   physical locality.
4. **Mechanisms not extended:** the global membrane-state bundle, direct
   all-neuron generation list, compulsory hippocampal/formation/capital fields,
   exhaustive production subtree inspection, one-owner-per-subsystem models,
   locks or routing hierarchies, named cognitive services, arbitrary hemisphere
   labels, fixed compartment sizes, global polling, whole-history replay, and
   caller codes as physical authority.
5. **Single exact next implementation item:** establish the typed,
   production-authenticated binding from every reached membrane, channel,
   reaction, fluid, and neuronal settlement locus to a physical control-volume
   authority and its exact boundary contacts. No compartment directory becomes
   production authority before that binding exists.
6. **DSF scope:** every reached neuron retains the complete explicit local
   `D_k`, `M_k`, `R_rev_k`, `U_star_k`, `C_k`, `P_k`, and `B_k` delivery and its
   exact authority.
7. **Field loss:** none. Compartment routing changes which authenticated bodies
   are reached; it does not average, score, bucket, flatten, or replace any local
   DSF field.

## One organism, not many services

Guala has one organism identity, one monotonic organism-publication generation,
one predecessor-linked causal head, and one current whole-organism settlement. A
physical compartment is neither an identity nor an owner. It is an immutable
addressable bundle of anatomy and mutable state whose membership follows actual
physical locality.

Compartmentalization does not create separate brains, agents, databases,
processes, clocks, schedulers, or decision authorities. It permits one native
causal loop to load and change only the physical localities reached by the
current cause while the global head integrity-commits unchanged localities by
their retained Merkle roots.

Literal continued execution after the process or host dies is outside this law.
That would require measured fault domains and distributed publication physics.
It must not be simulated with owners, leases, retries, or duplicate organisms.

## Physical partition law

The minimum persistent partition is an authenticated physical control volume or
surface, not a developer-named brain region.

A control-volume authority must bind:

- the topology authority and version;
- an exact anchor and extent or a typed synthetic-anatomy source;
- containment and adjacency relations;
- compatible dimensional units and conversions;
- membrane-face-to-volume and contact-to-site relations;
- every local membrane face, channel site, reaction site, fluid quantity, and
  neuronal settlement site physically present there;
- local aggregate capacity and conservation law; and
- every physical boundary contact through which a declared conserved quantity
  can cross.

Pressure or any other proposed quantity remains explicitly unavailable until its
own typed physical law, units, and conservation or source/sink behavior exist.

Two sites belong to the same persistent partition only when authenticated
anatomy places them in the same control volume or surface. Numeric
`region_code`, `compartment_code`, or `control_volume_code` values are transport
coordinates until that binding is proven. Equal caller-supplied numbers do not
prove shared anatomy, and different numbers do not prove a boundary.

The compartment address derives from a canonical type tag, topology authority,
and authenticated control-volume authority. It is a routing address only. It
never supplies neuron identity, meaning, memory, affect, or cognitive ownership.

A neuron may extend across many control volumes. Neuron lineage therefore does
not assign the entire neuron to one compartment. Each membrane, channel,
reaction, fluid, receptor, axonal, dendritic, somatic, and other settlement body
belongs where its physical locus places it. A K, fractal, or neuronal-current
body without a ratified physical settlement locus remains unresolved and may not
be assigned a convenient home compartment.

Higher anatomical localities, including configurations corresponding to
neocortical, hippocampal, prefrontal, amygdalar, sensory, motor, or body regions,
are derived observational views of the reached physical graph. They are not
partition authorities, restart groups, or owners of cognitive functions.

## Topology admission and biological development

Production D2 `GLJNFT02` is immutable source evidence. It has exact lineages and
legacy receptor-port keys but no authenticated membrane, control-volume,
channel, fluid, or boundary anatomy. It may not be rewritten as if that enriched
anatomy existed historically.

The first physical topology requires a separately named, one-way, atomic
topology-admission transition. That transition retains the exact D2 predecessor
and admits only reviewed typed virtual-biological anatomy with explicit
initialization provenance. Test values, inferred coordinates, receipt labels,
or generated history cannot supply it.

Later growth or specialization occurs only through a versioned local
DNA-to-RNA-to-expression transition or another ratified biological reaction.
The deterministic algorithm must be the emulated local biology: it consumes
typed precursor and energy quantities, responds to actual local chemical,
electrical, body, and experience state, preserves lineage, and produces exact
structural successors within measured resource and recovery availability.
`StructuralGrowthDna` ordinals, `DnaGrowthEvidence` counters, labels, schedules,
fixed populations, and developer-selected outcomes are not biological DNA.

## Compartment anatomy and current state

The immutable anatomy body for one control volume binds its address, topology
authority, local physical sites, intra-volume contacts, boundary-contact
authorities, and exact local physical laws. Source observations and full-field
bodies remain separately content-addressed and are referenced rather than
copied.

The mutable current-state root contains only state with a proven locus in that
control volume, including applicable membrane, channel, reaction, fluid,
Krimelack, neuronal-current, and mounted-current branches. It records the exact
local predecessor and last local physical interval only when it changes.

Organism publication generation and local physical clock are different
quantities. An untouched compartment retains identical root and bytes across
organism publications. When it is reached again, its anatomy-declared
metabolism, decay, recovery, or clock law must settle the actual elapsed physical
interval before it can interact. If no such law exists, the interaction remains
unavailable. An old local state is never silently rebound to the current
organism generation.

## Static boundary and dynamic settlement

A static boundary exists only where authenticated anatomy identifies a physical
contact between different control volumes. Its body binds both endpoint
authorities, exact sites and lineages where applicable, directionality, units,
capacity or conservation law, governing kinetics, and any exact propagation
delay or phase law.

A dynamic boundary settlement binds the predecessor head and occurrence, static
boundary authority, both predecessor compartment roots and local intervals, the
exact transferred quantity or truthful quiescent state, and both endpoint
successor local-state authorities. It does **not** bind successor compartment
roots.

Each successor compartment root may reference the boundary settlements that
changed its local state. One settled-event body then binds all boundary
settlements and all successor compartment roots. The new global head binds that
settled event and the successor directory root. This acyclic order is canonical:

```text
boundary settlement
    -> compartment successors
    -> settled event and successor directory
    -> global causal head
```

One physical transfer is evaluated once for one interval and shared by both
endpoints. No K cohesion relation, hippocampal address, learned formation,
temporal coincidence, or semantic association becomes a physical boundary.

## Source admission and reached transition

One event begins from one immutable predecessor head. Each external, body, or
endogenous cause requires a canonical source-to-receptor-or-site admission body
that is cold-resolved against mounted anatomy. A source enum, nonzero receipt, UI
event, or prior frontier is not cause admission.

The next frontier derives from newly admitted causes and exact anatomy-declared
clocks or flows. The persisted prior frontier is evidence of the last settled
event, not the next active frontier.

Local physical laws settle reached sites and explicit boundaries. A destination
joins the frontier only when a direct local cause or exact nonquiescent boundary
settlement reaches it. Both endpoints must first be settled to compatible local
physical intervals.

Every edge is evaluated at most once for the declared interval. In the default
phase, all fluxes derive from the same immutable predecessor. A successor may
feed another edge in the same event only when typed topology carries the exact
delay, phase, and dependency law. Absent that law, downstream propagation waits
for a later event. Developer iteration order cannot become a scheduler.

All affected compartment successors and shared boundary settlements publish
under one new head or none publish. A nonzero cross-boundary transfer cannot
publish one endpoint. Unaffected directory entries retain their exact roots.
Work is proportional to the authenticated reached frontier and explicit
boundary dependencies, never a heuristic count, priority list, timeout, or
whole-organism scan.

## Minimal global head

The global head binds only:

- the one organism identity, predecessor head, and publication generation;
- one immutable topology/manifest root, which includes compartment and boundary
  anatomy roots;
- the sparse current compartment-directory root;
- one settled-event root; and
- deterministically derived structural resource facts such as canonical object
  bytes, edge evaluations, and changed-compartment count.

The settled-event body owns the occurrence, reached frontier, boundary
settlements, compartment successors, and typed optional body, world, action,
consequence, hippocampal, affective, formation, recall, motor, or other evidence
that actually participated. Those mechanisms are not compulsory head fields.

Host-measured CPU, RAM, wall time, and operational health remain telemetry
outside causal identity because cold replay on another host must not change the
organism head.

## Lazy cold restore

Production restore verifies the small current head and its immediate predecessor
link, then resolves the topology-root path, settled-event body, and directory
paths required by newly admitted causes or exact clocks. It loads only reached
compartment bodies and explicit boundary dependencies. A destination loads
lazily when authenticated boundary evidence reaches it.

Occurrence and other shared event evidence are decoded once and referenced by
the reached compartments; they are not independently replayed in every
compartment. Within a reached compartment, restore replays every locally required
body: complete DSF authority, successful neuron transition, K settlement,
membrane, channel, reaction, fluid, and any conditionally reached cognitive
evidence. Occurrence handles and hippocampal certificates must have exact
persisted typed bodies; process-local handles or omitted certificate bodies do
not survive cold restore.

Inactive Merkle branches are integrity-committed but their unvisited bodies are
not claimed physically available until resolved. Offline exhaustive integrity
audit may traverse the full graph. Production transition and normal restart may
not call that audit, replay complete history, inspect all K closures, or decode
every object in the newest pack.

## Failure and publication law

Missing or changed reached bytes, unresolved physical location, false
membership, unbound contact, undeclared boundary, incompatible local intervals,
cross-generation substitution, one-sided boundary change, conservation failure,
missing required timing law, or changed typed evidence refuses the proposed new
head. No partial successor publishes, no formation or cognitive capital is
credited, and no global scan or fabricated fallback is permitted.

The already-published predecessor remains valid. If a known corrupted inactive
locality is retained while an independent available locality advances, the new
settled event must carry explicit unavailability evidence and may not claim a
completed whole-organism learning episode.

Publication is one short conditional head advance after all immutable bodies are
durable. A filesystem rename alone does not prove compare-and-swap between two
writers. The platform must prove a single external writer or provide one bounded
conditional publication primitive. This infrastructure has no cognitive
authority and must not become a lifetime owner, lease, router, validator, or
lock hierarchy. Global `PENDING` files and lifetime-history recovery scans are
not part of normal restore.

## Implementation dependency path

The single implementation item is production-authenticated physical membership.
Its necessary steps are:

1. Extract actual task-853 `GLJNFT02` lineages and legacy ports through the one
   canonical native parser, retaining the exact predecessor bytes and reporting
   missing physical anatomy explicitly.
2. Ratify the reviewed typed initial virtual biology and one-way topology/source
   admission without inventing enriched D2 history.
3. Bind membrane, gap-contact, channel, reaction, fluid, receptor, and neuronal
   settlement loci to control volumes, boundaries, units, capacity, conservation,
   and propagation timing.
4. Give every required topology, source, occurrence, certificate, static
   boundary, dynamic settlement, and compartment body one canonical typed cold
   decoder.
5. Only then reuse the existing sparse Patricia machinery for a compartment
   directory and typed reached-key inspector; do not duplicate it.
6. Separate local physical clocks from organism publication generation and
   replace global all-neuron/membrane composition with acyclic atomic publication
   of the reached successor set.

If actual production evidence or reviewed virtual biology cannot prove a step,
the implementation remains explicitly unresolved rather than assigning labels,
test constants, or compatibility fallbacks.

## Production proof

Completion requires a native production generation that begins from the actual
live predecessor, changes one physically reached compartment through its exact
local law, atomically crosses one authenticated boundary when its physics causes
the transfer, and cold-restores by loading only reached Merkle paths. Proof must
show an untouched compartment retained identical root and bytes and that
substitution, missing-boundary, one-sided update, unavailable local-time
settlement, concurrent-head replacement, and global-scan attempts fail.

Documentation, codecs, unit tests, fixture locations, directory roots, UI
status, or successful deployment alone do not prove this law.
