## THINGS
The units this subject is about. Each sits in many phylums at once;
its other homes are named so a cascade can branch.

- THE WRITTEN RULE — an agreement written down so it outlives its
  writers: memory made public and durable, binding people who
  never met the authors, including the unborn. The durability
  machine; also: language (writing is speech that keeps), economy
  (the ledger outliving the trade), building (the blueprint).
- THE CONTRACT — claims with teeth: promises exchanged, each side
  giving to get, the law holding the far end of a trade stretched
  across time — private law two people write, enforced as if the
  state had passed it; also: economy (trust with a backstop),
  governance (treaties — contracts with no judge above, hence
  fragile).
- PRECEDENT — decided cases as accumulated rudder: like cases must
  fall alike, so every ruling steers all later rulings; also:
  language (grammar is usage hardened into rule), play (the
  referee's early calls set the real rules).
- THE CONSTITUTION — a law that ordinary law may not contradict,
  armed by a body that can strike down a validly passed act — the
  deepest rule is the one saying how rules change; also:
  governance, people (a will binding unwilling heirs).
- EVIDENCE — what a court may be told, filtered: some true things
  are excluded because their power to persuade exceeds their power
  to inform; also: measurement, mind (the vivid fact).
- THE STANDARD OF PROOF — a probability threshold in robes: how
  sure the court must be, chosen by which mistake is worse (math
  in SCIENCE); also: mathematics, medicine (screening),
  engineering (safety factors).
- PROPERTY — not a thing but a bundle of separate rights over a
  thing: use, exclude, sell, lend, alter, bequeath — sticks held
  by different people at once, so one field can have four owners
  of different kinds; also: economy, farming (mineral rights),
  music (writing, recording, performing held apart).
- THE REGISTER — a right good against the whole world must be
  findable by the whole world: it lets the stranger buying
  tomorrow discover what will bind him; also: measurement,
  computing (a directory turning names to addresses).
- THE LEGAL PERSON — a company that owns, sues, owes, and is
  punished as if a person, whose owners cannot be made to pay its
  debts: a useful fiction and a deliberate subsidy to risk; the
  unpaid losses land on creditors, workers, and the public. The
  boldest of the LEGAL FICTIONS — open pretences that get work
  done (a found item treated as never lost, a rule deemed known by
  all): everyone knows they are false; they survive because
  rewriting the structure costs more; also: economy, mathematics
  (imaginary numbers, useful before respectable), story.
- THE STATE'S FORCE — enforcement held as a monopoly precisely so
  citizens need not bring their own; a rule without consequence is
  a suggestion; also: governance, conflict, play (the whistle).
- RIGHT AND DUTY — one unit, two faces: every right is an
  instruction to somebody else — a right to trial is a duty to
  fund courts; an entitlement with no duty-bearer is a wish;
  also: ethics, economy (who pays).
- THE CAUSE OF ACTION — law's fixed vocabulary of hearable wrongs;
  a grievance fitting no shape on the list gets no remedy; the
  lawyer's real work is translating a life into that vocabulary;
  also: language, medicine (symptoms into a diagnosis).

## CLAIMS
What holds, written to be tested by a crossing query. Worth is
never absolute — it is applicability to the query at hand.

- Law's distinctive act is announcing IN ADVANCE what will be done
  to you and for what, so a person can read the rules and steer.
  Predictability is the product: people plan only under a law that
  repeats itself, and retroactive change destroys arrangements
  even when the new rule is better.
- Enforcement separates law from advice: an unenforced rule
  selects for the shameless and punishes the honest; a debt
  uncollectable is a gift misfiled.
- The accuser must show; the accused need not disprove: proving a
  negative is often impossible, and the two errors are unequal —
  caging the innocent costs more than freeing the guilty
  (threshold arithmetic in SCIENCE).
- Consent is the source of a contract's binding, so the binding
  cannot outrun the consent: only what was agreed, only who
  agreed — and where consent was not real (threat, childhood,
  terms nobody could read), the contract is not real either.
- Most of a contract is invisible: the law supplies default terms
  for everything the parties forgot, and the defaults govern until
  written over — no deal could be written from nothing every time.
- The law rarely forces you to keep a promise; it makes you pay
  what breaking it cost the other side. A contract is, quietly, an
  option — perform or compensate — and sometimes breaking and
  paying is efficient for everyone. Courts move money reliably and
  supervise conduct clumsily; they do what they do well.
- "I did not know it was forbidden" excuses no one — a free excuse
  nobody can disprove would make ignorance the thing to buy
  (strained when the code outgrows any reader; mirror entry).
- Every rule is written in words, and words never fully hold
  intent. In the gap between text and meaning live the loopholes:
  obeying the letter to beat the spirit. Judges must choose — read
  strictly and get absurd results the makers never wanted, or read
  purpose and hold the pen themselves; every system oscillates
  between the two, forever.
- Law and morality overlap and neither contains the other: much
  that is plainly wrong is legal. Law is the smaller, cruder set —
  it must be enforceable, provable, stated in advance, for
  strangers.
- One act can raise two questions — did you wrong the public (the
  state punishes) and did you wrong this person (you repay)?
  Different accusers, different thresholds; one lost, one won, no
  contradiction (worked in SCIENCE).
- Blame follows choice, graded on a ladder: meant it — knew it
  would happen — saw the risk and went on — fell below the
  standard of care. The rung is most of what separates murder
  from an accident.
- Tort asks nobody who is a bad person. A harm between strangers
  has already landed; the loss will not vanish, and leaving it
  where it fell is itself a decision. The useful question: who
  could have prevented it most cheaply — placed there, the rule
  works in advance.
- Trial protections shield the guilty most often, because the
  guilty are most of those charged — not a malfunction: a shield
  must be applied before the answer is known, so it cannot be
  conditioned on the answer.
- At some point an answer must stop being questionable, even if
  wrong: without finality nothing is settled and the strongest
  party litigates until the other runs out of money. And claims
  expire — evidence rots, witnesses die, memories rearrange; a
  late court cannot tell true claims from false ones.
- The rule of law is a claim about OFFICIALS: police, minister,
  and tax office bound by rules they cannot bend to suit a case. A
  place where everyone obeys the government is only obedience.
- Nearly every case ends without a verdict: both sides estimate
  the court, discount for cost and risk, and meet in between — the
  law most people experience is the shadow that decisions cast
  over bargaining. And a right you cannot afford to assert is a
  right in name: deeper pockets win by continuing (SCIENCE).
- A rulebook survives its own contradictions by quarantining them:
  strike the offending clause, the rest stands. A formal system
  cannot — one contradiction detonates the whole; the quarantine
  is the deep difference between human and formal rule systems
  (logic in SCIENCE).
- Law lags technology structurally: both engines run on the past —
  statute waits for the political moment after the harm, precedent
  for a harmed plaintiff and years of appeals — so the first years
  of anything new are governed by analogies stretched from the
  nearest old thing.
- A confession feels like the end of the argument and is the least
  reliable common evidence there is: pressure, isolation, and a
  promised exit produce admissions from the innocent — and once
  given, everyone stops looking (receipts in the mirror).

## SCIENCE
The mathematics and logic of law, held — not mentioned. Each piece
is a LAW a query can compute with, a RULE it can follow, and where
it earns it, a WORKED case proving the rule runs.

### THE ARITHMETIC OF SURENESS

LAW: a standard of proof is a threshold on the probability of
  guilt, set by the cost ratio of the two mistakes: convict when
  P(guilt) > C_wrong_convict ÷ (C_wrong_convict + C_wrong_free).
RULE: take how many times worse the tribunal holds one error than
  the other, R, and compute R ÷ (R + 1).
WORKED: criminal courts inherit Blackstone's ratio — better ten
  guilty free than one innocent caged, R = 10: 10 ÷ 11 = 0.909…
  — ~91% sure, and "beyond reasonable doubt" is that number
  wearing robes. Civil courts hold the errors equal (money is
  recoverable, years are not), R = 1: 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5 — "more likely
  than not." Two cost ratios, not two moods.

WORKED AGAIN, one act against both thresholds: the court believes
  the defendant did it with probability 0.7. Criminal: 0.7 < 0.909
  — acquit. Civil, same facts: 0.7 > 0.5 — liable, pay. Acquitted
  AND liable is one estimate read against two prices of error —
  this dissolves the whole "how could both happen" family.

LAW: evidence multiplies odds, never sets them: posterior odds =
  prior odds × strength, where strength = how much likelier the
  evidence is if guilty than if innocent.
RULE: start from how many could have done it, multiply by the
  evidence's strength, only then read off a probability.
WORKED: a pool of 100,001 possible culprits, one guilty: prior
  odds 1 to 100,000. A match with false-positive rate 1 in 1,000
  has strength 1,000. Posterior odds = 1,000 to 100,000 = 1 to
  100 — about 1% guilty ON THE MATCH ALONE. Check by counting
  heads: 100,000 innocents × 1/1,000 = 100 innocent matchers,
  plus the culprit = 101, of whom 1 is guilty: 1/101 = 0.0099 —
  the same ~1%. The prosecutor's fallacy reads "1 in 1,000 if
  innocent" as "1 in 1,000 innocent" — mistaking 1% for 99.9%;
  the mirror holds the wreckage this cost before it was learned.

### THE ARITHMETIC OF INCENTIVES

LAW: negligence has a price test (the Hand formula): precaution
  is owed when its burden is less than the odds of harm times the
  loss — B < P × L.
RULE: price the precaution, price the harm, estimate the odds;
  compare.
WORKED: a moored barge breaks loose with P = 0.001 per day, loss
  L = 100,000; a watchman costs B = 50 a day. Expected harm =
  0.001 × 100,000 = 100 per day; 50 < 100 — the watchman was
  owed, skipping him is negligence. Same barge, L = 30,000:
  0.001 × 30,000 = 30 < 50 — not negligent to skip. The
  reasonable person, made computable at the margin.

LAW: deterrence must price the act at its harm THROUGH the odds
  of being caught: required penalty F = harm ÷ P(detection).
RULE: when detection falls, divide again — never guess.
WORKED: harm 1,000, caught one time in ten: F = 1,000 ÷ 0.1 =
  10,000. Detection falls to one in a hundred: F = 1,000 ÷ 0.01 =
  100,000. CONDITION that caps the law: the penalty must be
  collectable — against an offender with nothing it fails at any
  size, so fines give way to other instruments at the bottom.

### THE ARITHMETIC OF BARGAINING

LAW: a case settles when a range exists — claimant's floor
  (believed win-chance × stakes − their trial cost) below
  defendant's ceiling (believed loss-chance × stakes + their
  cost); trial happens only when beliefs disagree by more than
  the combined costs.
RULE: compute the floor and the ceiling; overlap settles, and
  any point between splits the saved costs; a floor above the
  ceiling goes to trial.
WORKED: stakes 100,000, costs 20,000 each side, both believe 0.5:
  floor = 50,000 − 20,000 = 30,000; ceiling = 50,000 + 20,000 =
  70,000 — a range 40,000 wide (exactly the two costs); the case
  settles and the deal splits the savings. Optimism splits them —
  claimant 0.9, defendant 0.2: floor = 90,000 − 20,000 = 70,000;
  ceiling = 20,000 + 20,000 = 40,000; floor above ceiling —
  trial. Trials are made of DISAGREEMENT, not of stakes; that is
  why the law most people meet is the shadow, not the courtroom.

LAW: a plea beats trial for a calculating defendant whenever the
  offer < believed conviction chance × trial sentence.
RULE: divide the offer by the exposure; any believed conviction
  chance above that quotient takes the deal.
WORKED: offered 2 years against 10-year exposure: 2 ÷ 10 = 0.2 —
  any believed chance above 20% takes the deal. An innocent with
  a frightening case at a believed 30%: expected trial =
  0.3 × 10 = 3 years against a certain 2 — the deal wins. THE LAW
  ITSELF says a large enough discount makes innocents plead; the
  pressure is the gap, set by whoever writes the exposure.

LAW: a claim runs only when expected recovery exceeds the cost of
  running it — harms spread thinly have no plaintiff until
  bundled.
RULE: sum the scattered harms and set one shared running cost
  against the total; the bundle lives when the sum clears it.
WORKED: 2 overcharged on each of 1,000,000 bills = 2,000,000 of
  harm; an individual suit costs ~5,000 against a claim worth 2 —
  dead a million times over. Bundled into one action costing
  ~500,000: 500,000 < 2,000,000 — alive. At a ~30% fee (600,000)
  each member nets (2,000,000 − 600,000) ÷ 1,000,000 = 1.40 of
  their 2. The same subtraction turns no-case into a case AND
  hands the assembler power over a million strangers.

### THE ARITHMETIC OF REMEDY AND TIME

LAW: damages aim where PERFORMANCE would have left you — not
  where you started — and you must limit your own loss: harm you
  sat and watched grow is not a bigger claim, it is an
  uncollectable one.
RULE: price the substitute, subtract the contract price; freeze
  the claim at the moment mitigation was possible.
WORKED: goods promised at 10,000; the seller walks; a substitute
  costs 11,500 that week. Damages = 11,500 − 10,000 = 1,500.
  Waiting while the market climbs to 13,000 changes nothing:
  still 1,500 — the extra 1,500 was grown, not suffered.

LAW: money awarded late is less money — value today = award ÷
  (1 + rate)^years — so a judgment must carry the clock.
RULE: divide by (1 + rate) once for every year of delay; award
  interest by the same clock, or the delay becomes a discount
  the loser collects.
WORKED: 10,000 judged 5 years late at 4%: 1.04² = 1.0816;
  1.0816² = 1.1699; × 1.04 = 1.2167 — with interest the award is
  10,000 × 1.2167 = 12,167; without it the late 10,000 is worth
  10,000 ÷ 1.2167 = 8,219 in day-of-harm money. The delay
  quietly confiscated 18%; justice delayed is division.

LAW: precedent compounds. If each year's rulings settle a
  fraction f of a doctrine's open questions, the unruled residue
  after t years is (1 − f)^t — never zero, always shrinking.
RULE: count the years, raise (1 − f) to that power — that is
  what remains open.
WORKED: f = 10% a year, 20 years: 0.9² = 0.81; 0.81² = 0.6561;
  0.6561² = 0.4305; 0.4305² = 0.1853; × 0.6561 = 0.1216 — about
  12% still open. The shrinking residue is why old doctrines feel
  solid; its never reaching zero is why they still surprise; and
  new technology pours fresh open space in faster than compounding
  drains it — the lag claim, quantified.

### THE LOGIC OF RULEBOOKS

LAW: in formal logic one contradiction detonates everything.
  Grant both "the tenant may stay" and "the tenant may not stay":
  from "may stay" infer "may stay OR the moon is cheese"; from
  "may not stay" strike the first arm; conclude the moon is
  cheese. Four steps, all valid — from one contradiction, any
  sentence follows.
RULE (the severance clause): never let a contradiction propagate.
  Strike the offending clause; the remainder stands; the void
  piece binds nobody and infects nothing. Contradictions are
  quarantined locally — repealed, distinguished, severed — never
  fed forward as premises.
WORKED: a contract of ten clauses, one void. Under explosion the
  document proves anything and therefore nothing; under severance
  nine stand and one falls — the loss is 1/10 of the text, not
  10/10 of the meaning. This is why a code carrying thousands of
  latent inconsistencies runs for centuries while a formal system
  dies of one — and why "law as computer code" keeps failing
  (receipts in the mirror).

LAW: a general rule is written before its cases, in words, from a
  finite imagination — so every rulebook is incomplete against the
  world that will arrive. The mercy door is the designed answer to
  a provable shortfall (certified as a limit in the mirror).
RULE: when the arriving case fits no written rule and forcing the
  nearest one yields a result nobody can defend, route it out the
  mercy door — the exit is part of the design, not a breach of it.

## METHODS
How things are done and built here — each with what governs it and
what breaks it. The prepositioned how-to-build.

- WRITING RULES IN ADVANCE (the statute engine): a general rule
  drafted first, applied forward to whatever arrives — certain,
  cheap to obey, readable by strangers, blind to the case it never
  imagined. Breaks when drafted from the worst imaginable offender
  or grown past what a person could read.
- GROWING RULES FROM CASES (the precedent engine): decide one real
  dispute exactly; the rule emerges behind it and compounds (the
  (1−f)^t law). Exact, slow, written for specialists. Breaks when
  the first case in new territory is odd and steers all after it.
- DUE PROCESS (how the answer is reached IS part of the answer):
  tell the person the case, let them reply, judge without a stake
  — the three legs that make a verdict believable to everyone who
  was not there. Skip one and the same verdict is different.
- ADVERSARIAL TESTING: two sides build their strongest cases and a
  neutral decides — motivated attack surfaces more than neutral
  inquiry, and neither side is trying to find the truth. Breaks
  when one side can outspend the process.
- THE NEGLIGENCE WALK, four steps in order: a duty owed to THIS
  person; conduct below the reasonable standard; that conduct
  caused the harm (would it have happened anyway? is the
  consequence close enough to count? — every effect traces back
  forever, so the chain must be cut somewhere, and the cut is a
  judgement); the harm is real. The weight sits on step two,
  judged afterwards by people who now know what happened.
- STRICT LIABILITY (fault switched off): for chosen dangerous
  activities, harm pays regardless of care — the incentive lands
  on whoever chose the activity; they alone can choose not to.
- CHOOSING REGULATION OR LIABILITY: forbid the risky act in
  advance (certain, cheap, clumsy at odd cases) or price the harm
  after (exact, and late — someone is already hurt). Every working
  system runs both; the per-hazard choice follows the Hand
  arithmetic and who can bear the proof.
- DISCOVERY (compelled exchange): most of what proves a case is
  held by the party who did the wrong; force the documents out.
  Breaks into a weapon when volume is the point — a million pages
  is a wall, not a window.
- APPEALING: a second look at the LAW, rarely the facts — the
  first court saw the witnesses; the appeal court has a transcript,
  which preserves words and loses everything else. Correct
  reasoning; almost never re-decide who lied.
- SENTENCING FROM A RANGE: pick within limits written by lawmakers
  reacting to the worst case, graded by the ladder of blame.
  Breaks by ratchet — raising a range is cheap politics, lowering
  one dangerous, so ranges only climb.
- THE MERCY DOOR: one exit kept outside the machinery — pardon,
  clemency, the charge not brought — because rules faithfully
  applied sometimes yield a result nobody can defend, and no rule
  can be written to catch that. Breaks when used as favour; the
  mirror holds equity's cycle.

## MEANS
What the methods run on, with the numbers that matter.

- WRITING AND THE RECORD: the durability substrate — statutes,
  deeds, judgments, transcripts; the record is the finding that
  makes a rule owed. Stored two ways: the code (general, numbered,
  written for a stranger) and the case reports (exact, tethered to
  facts, written for a specialist) — opposite choices about who
  can retrieve the knowledge; whole legal families divide on it.
- THE COURTHOUSE AND ITS OFFICERS: judge (the law), jury (the
  facts — laypeople, because facts are about ordinary life, and
  because a body the state cannot appoint, promote, or dismiss is
  the only one structurally free of it; it may refuse to convict
  and nobody may ask why), clerk, bailiff — and outside, the
  police: the force monopoly's hands; where they stop, law
  becomes advice (borders — see RELATIONS).
- THE LAWYER: the translator — and the price of entry; the cost
  arithmetic decides who can afford the translation.
- MONEY AS THE REMEDY MEDIUM: courts move money well and supervise
  conduct badly, so most remedies are priced in it — the damages,
  interest, and mitigation arithmetic all run on this choice.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject answers — the doors a query enters by.

- LET STRANGERS RELY ON EACH OTHER AT SCALE: agreement outliving
  memory, backed by consequence — trade's handshake armed; the
  founding purpose.
- MAKE THE FUTURE PRICEABLE: a law that repeats itself lets people
  plan, build, lend, and marry on tomorrow's rules being today's.
- REPLACE PRIVATE FORCE WITH PROCESS: the state takes the feud and
  returns a procedure; possession is protected first and argued
  second, so nobody's claim begins with seizure.
- PLACE LOSSES THAT ALREADY HAPPENED: harm lands somewhere; place
  it where placing does work in advance (the cheapest preventer).
- BIND THE OFFICIALS: rules the enforcers themselves cannot bend —
  the rule of law aimed where aiming is hardest.
- SAY WHO WE ARE: naming an act a crime is itself an act — it
  teaches, comforts victims, and changes what people think
  ordinary; some laws are passed for the saying alone and barely
  enforced.
- KEEP PROMISES CHEAP: defaults, forms, registers — the millionth
  agreement costs almost nothing to make and to trust.

## HISTORY
How the knowledge arose, in what order, at what cost — the order
carries why.

- Custom before writing: rules enforced by memory and standing,
  binding only while the elders lived. Writing made the
  durability machine — law codes stand among the oldest long
  texts that survive, because outliving the writer was the point. Contract
  law then grew with trade: strangers at distance needed the far
  end of the deal held by something that did not know them.
- Verdicts before evidence: the ordeal and the combat — centuries
  of handing the answer to iron, water, and champions (the mirror
  holds the receipts). The jury and the law of evidence replaced
  them; the exclusionary rules still carry the scar — the court
  learned to fear the persuasive over the informative.
- The constitution trick: the dead constraining the living, on
  purpose — and with it the body that may strike down what the
  elected passed; powers split so that harming a citizen needs
  several bodies with different interests and timetables to agree.
- Equity's cycle, run in full: rules produced injustice in odd
  cases; a conscience court grew beside the law; its mercies
  became precedents, its precedents rules — and within generations
  two rulebooks needed a third thing to soften them. Standing
  proof of the completeness limit.
- Statistics entered the courtroom and hurt people before it
  helped (the mirror holds the squared-probability wreck).
- Each technology arrived before its law, on schedule: the press,
  the engine, the wire, the code that copies for nothing — each
  governed first by analogies from the nearest old thing, then by
  cases, then by statute. The lag is the pattern.

## RELATIONS
First-class connections — the cascade's paths outward. Each names
what runs along it.

- LANGUAGE: words as lossy vessels — the loophole, the
  interpretation war, the lawyer as translator, grammar as walked
  precedent. Law is language load-bearing at maximum stress.
- GOVERNANCE: law is its skeleton — constitutions, separated
  powers, the rule of law aimed at officials.
- ETHICS: overlap without containment; the ladder of blame is
  ethics made provable.
- ECONOMY: contract as trade stretched across time; property's
  bundle; deterrence and damages as prices; the limited company's
  subsidy; who bears the loss as a market question.
- MATHEMATICS: thresholds, odds, expected values, compounding
  precedent, explosion and severance — SCIENCE runs on it.
- MEASUREMENT: standards of proof as measures of sureness;
  registers as public measurement; safety factors from the cost
  of failure.
- COMPUTING: the program does what you wrote, never what you meant
  — the loophole machine perfected (the self-enforcing-contract
  attempt is in the mirror).
- MIND: why confessions mislead, why vivid facts are excluded, why
  hindsight contaminates the reasonable-person test.
- PEOPLE: the jury as the unappointable decider; bundled claims as
  organised numbers; the crowd's sense of fairness.
- CONFLICT: law as war's replacement inside the border; above
  states, law without a sheriff — working where reciprocity
  suffices, failing where a state can bear the cost of breaking.
- PLAY: rules fixed before the deal is dealt; the referee's early
  calls as living precedent; the whistle's reach as enforcement's
  edge — games are law's cheapest laboratory.
- STORY: the genie as literalist lawyer — folklore held the
  letter-versus-spirit lesson first; scripture wars as
  interpretation's oldest running case.
- BELIEF: the oath, the ordeal's ghost, dispensations hardening
  into canon — belief once supplied verdicts, still supplies
  commitment.
- TEACHING: the mark scheme handed out with the assignment — fair
  notice as pedagogy; discipline policies as small constitutions.
- MEDICINE: consent explained rather than signed; the professional
  standard; screening thresholds as the two standards of proof.
- BUILDING: codes against trade custom; permission relied on
  before foundations; the completion certificate as finality.
