# LAW — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/law/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. Seed authored by Claude from
docs/fabric_phylums/17_law_agreements.md and
docs/fabric_phylums/128_deep_law_and_justice.md. What didn't hold

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE COMPLETE CONTRACT — CANNOT BE: a contract listing every
  future it must survive. The unforeseen is unlistable — states of
  the world outrun any finite text written before them, and
  drafting cost grows with every clause while the deal's value
  does not; every dispute is a place the text ran out. What stands
  instead (color): defaults supplied by law for what the parties
  forgot. Would drain on: a drafter who can enumerate futures —
  none is written anywhere in the fabric.
- THE COMPLETE RULEBOOK — CERTIFIED LIMIT, same root: a general
  rule is written before its cases, in words, from a finite
  imagination, so every rulebook meets a case it never imagined.
  Conditions: rules finite and fixed in advance; cases arriving
  from an unbounded world. The mercy door and equity's whole
  history are the work-arounds — and they harden into rules and
  re-meet the limit (HISTORY). No drain while both conditions
  hold.
- A COURT ABOVE STATES WITH A SHERIFF — wanted for centuries, not
  real: enforcement separates law from advice, and above states
  nobody holds the force. Works where reciprocity suffices (post,
  flights, shipping, trade); fails where it matters, because a
  state strong enough to break it can bear the cost. Would drain
  on: a force-holder above states — which would itself become the
  thing law exists to bind; the want may be self-blocking.
- AN INSTRUMENT THAT READS TRUTH OFF A PERSON — attempted many
  times, the polygraph the famous one. Fell short: error rates
  far above the thresholds it would feed, plus trainable
  countermeasures — power to persuade past power to inform,
  exactly what the color's evidence rules exclude. Would drain
  on: an instrument whose measured error beats the standard of
  proof it serves — none written.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "THE ORDEAL REVEALS THE GUILTY" — held for centuries: hand the
  verdict to hot iron or deep water and the divine flags the liar.
  Failed as the claim it made: outcomes tracked flesh and
  steering, not guilt (receipts in METHODS). Would never drain
  under stated conditions — no instrument of the divine is
  written; the underlying want (a verdict independent of fallible
  humans) stands open and feeds every truth-machine attempt since.
- "PRESSURE PRODUCES TRUTH" — the confession claim: isolation,
  fear, and a promised exit produce the admission that stops the
  pressure, from guilty and innocent alike. Receipts: among
  convictions later undone by biology, a false confession sits in
  roughly a quarter (~25-30%) — detailed confessions to
  impossible crimes. WHY believed: a confession ends the work,
  and everyone stops looking. Drained INTO the color as an
  evidence rule, not out of existence.
- "SEVERITY DETERS IN PROPORTION" — partial failure: the color's
  arithmetic (harm ÷ catch-chance) assumes an offender who
  computes; measured behaviour tracks the CATCH-CHANCE far more
  than the penalty's size (~ — pickpockets worked the crowds at
  hangings of pickpockets); decades of measuring the harshest
  penalty against life caged found no stable margin. Would drain
  on offenders who calculate: the arithmetic assumed a calculator
  where there is a person.
- "EVERYONE KNOWS THE LAW" — held while a code was readable in a
  lifetime; strained past honesty when compliance became a paid
  profession. Kept as an open pretence because the alternative
  (ignorance as a free excuse) dissolves enforcement. Consequence:
  when the code outgrows the reader, prosecutors — not
  legislators — decide what conduct is criminal, by choosing what
  to charge. Would drain on: a code a person could again read in
  a life — and every year of drafting moves that condition the
  wrong way.
- UNSETTLED: WHETHER A BARE PROMISE SHOULD BIND — the color arms
  only bargains (given-to-get); lands differ, sealed gifts exist.
  Nothing certain — but a possibility: the bargain test is a
  cheap PROOF of seriousness, not a theory of promising, and
  lands with other cheap proofs (seals, notaries) need it less.
- UNSETTLED: WHETHER JURIES OR JUDGES JUDGE FACTS BETTER — no
  ground truth exists to score verdicts against. Possibility: the
  jury's worth was never accuracy but unappointability; measured
  on the wrong axis, the question dissolves.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A MEASURE OF SURENESS AT THE POINT OF USE — the color
  proves the thresholds are probabilities (10/11, 1/2), and no
  court can read a juror's number off the juror: words ("sure")
  stand in for the quantity, and nobody knows the spread between
  one juror's 0.7 and another's 0.95 on the same evidence. Would
  drain on: an elicitation that survives a courtroom — none
  written.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: PROBABILITY IN THE COURTROOM — the squared
  independent that wasn't. A mother lost two infants; an expert
  squared one death's odds (1 in 8,543) to 8,543 × 8,543 = 1 in
  ~73 million and called it the chance of innocence. Both moves
  false: the deaths shared genes and a household (not independent
  — the true joint odds are far shorter), and the right comparison
  was AGAINST the competing story, double murder — also
  vanishingly rare. Convicted; quashed years later. The reason
  the color's odds law says MULTIPLY ODDS, never square
  convenient numbers.
- LACKING, WITH A PROVEN WALL: A FAIR PREDICTION OF REOFFENDING —
  actuarial scores entered sentencing and hit arithmetic, not
  ethics: when two groups reoffend at different base rates, no
  score can be both calibrated (right at the same rate at every
  score level) and equal in false-alarm rates across the groups.
  Certified under its condition: base rates differ — the moment
  they don't, both fairnesses can hold, the shimmer exactly;
  until then every tool must choose which fairness to break, a
  value smuggled in as engineering.
- LACKING: A SCIENCE OF INTERPRETATION — no procedure decides
  letter against spirit. Possibility: a threshold problem like
  the standards of proof, unpriced because nobody has named the
  two error costs — absurd results against judicial rewriting.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- TRIAL BY ORDEAL — tried for CENTURIES: hot iron carried nine
  paces, boiling water, the sinking test. Receipts where a
  register survives: of ~208 recorded hot-iron ordeals in one
  13th-century book, ~130 ended unburned — five of every eight
  passed a test bare flesh should fail, consistent with priests
  steering outcomes toward those willing to take the iron. The
  half-working mechanism, the dangerous kind: those who BELIEVED
  confessed or settled rather than face it — believers sorted by
  conscience, the rest decided by rigging, a smuggled signal in a
  divine costume. Ended not by refutation but by staffing: clergy
  forbidden to bless it (1215), collapse within a generation.
- TRIAL BY COMBAT — outsource the verdict to force: selected for
  the better champion, not the truer claim — the exact inversion
  of what law is for; superseded by the same jury as the iron.
- FORBIDDING A MARKET BOTH SIDES WANT — the prohibition shape,
  run at full scale on drink for 13 years (1920-1933): with no
  complaining victim, no evidence flows; enforcement must
  manufacture its own witnesses; the trade moves underground and
  arms itself. CONDITION: both parties want the act and none
  complains. The shimmer: surveillance that needs no witness
  moves the condition — the entry recomputes, into a governance
  question rather than proof it now works.
- JAILING THE DEBTOR — collect a debt by caging the one person
  whose labour could pay it: self-defeating arithmetic (a caged
  debtor earns nothing; the creditor pays the jailer). Abolished
  into bankruptcy — wipe, redistribute, return the debtor to
  earning. A remedy that consumed its own object.
- REMOVING DISCRETION TO REMOVE DISPARITY — mandatory minimums:
  the judge's discretion did not vanish, it MOVED to the
  prosecutor's charging decision — earlier, invisible,
  unappealable. Discretion in a rule system is conserved;
  squeeze one joint and another bulges.
- LAW AS SELF-EXECUTING CODE — write the contract as a program so
  no court is needed: ran head-first into the color's oldest law
  (the text does what it says, never what was meant) with no
  judge left to close the gap. The famous receipt: a fund whose
  code permitted a drain its authors never intended lost roughly
  a third of its holdings to a move that was, by the only rulebook
  it recognised, legal — undone only by humans overruling the code
  from outside. The mercy door cannot be compiled away; would
  drain on a program that holds intent — none is written.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE MILLION-PAGE DISCLOSURE — meant as a window, weaponised as
  a wall: past some volume the search cost buries the signal and
  the means serves exhaustion, not truth. Would drain on: search
  whose cost does not grow with volume — a computing question.
- NOT YET FOUND: PROCESS CHEAP ENOUGH NOT TO GATE — where a claim
  worth 500 costs 5,000 to run, the right exists in name.
  Small-claims courts drain it partly; the general case stands.
- NOT YET FOUND: JUDGMENT COLLECTION ACROSS ALL BORDERS — winning
  is three questions (will this court hear it? whose law applies?
  can the judgment be collected where the money sits?) and a win
  on two is a piece of paper; treaties patch it land by land,
  with permanent holes. Blocked on THINGS' missing sheriff.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- PERFECT JUSTICE BY RULE ALONE — the headline cannot, certified:
  rules finite and written in advance, cases unbounded and
  arriving after. Every rulebook meets a case it never imagined;
  the mercy door exists because this is a limit, not a drafting
  failure. Recomputes only if rules could rewrite themselves per
  case — no longer rule; the want dissolves rather than drains.
- MAKE THE VICTIM WHOLE — punishment does nothing for the loss,
  compensation nothing about the public wrong; a system aimed at
  either leaves the other standing — why victims so often say the
  process was not for them. Open; repair-centered attempts drain
  it partly.
- BIND POWER AT THE VERY TOP — the rule of law is aimed at
  officials and thins as it climbs; above states it runs out of
  sheriff entirely.
- GIVE EVERYONE THEIR DAY — open while process costs gate entry;
  deeper pockets win by continuing, before anything is filed.
- ARRIVE BEFORE THE HARM — law lags technology structurally (both
  engines run on the past). Nothing certain — but a possibility:
  principles pre-positioned for classes of harm rather than named
  devices shorten the chase; the lag never reaches zero while
  rules must precede cases.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- The ordeal's centuries are this sheet's largest holding: a
  verdict machine believed across lands and generations, run by
  the best-educated officers of its day, half-working on a
  smuggled signal, ended by staffing rather than proof — cost
  uncountable, paid by whoever the iron was steered against.
- The wrongful-conviction ledger, still open: hundreds cleared by
  biology alone (~375+ in one land's count), each a verdict that
  passed every safeguard of its time — the receipts behind the
  color's fear of confessions and vivid facts, and the measure of
  how far ~91% sure runs from certain.
- The squared-probability case: one expert's arithmetic, one
  wrecked family — the courtroom is the worst room to do
  statistics in for the first time.
- Equity's cycle, filed here as trying: mercy grew beside rule,
  hardened into rule, and called for new mercy — generations
  proving the completeness limit by attempting to drain it.
- The plea bargain's quiet conquest: trial now decides only a
  sliver of outcomes (~19 in 20 convictions arrive by deal in its
  heaviest user) — certainty against a gamble, compounded case by
  case; no villain needed.
- Thirteen years of prohibition at full scale: the largest run of
  forbidding a wanted market, ended by repeal — the receipt the
  METHODS entry stands on.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN: LAW ↔ BELIEF AS A VERDICT CHANNEL — the ordeal's
  collapse when belief's officers withdrew; the relation now
  carries only commitment residue (the oath, the sworn text).
  Filed with its mechanism: a channel that broke by staffing, not
  refutation, could reopen anywhere belief again supplies the
  enforcement.
- BROKEN, PARTLY REBUILT: LAW ↔ COMPUTING AS SELF-ENFORCEMENT —
  the code-as-contract attempt (METHODS) broke on the lossy-words
  law wearing a compiler; rebuilt only in the modest form
  (registers, search, records), dotted for the ambitious one.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: LAW ↔ ECONOMY AS A TOTAL ACCOUNT — that
  every rule reduces to efficient loss-placement: grips hard on
  tort and contract (the Hand formula and efficient breach are
  its trophies), fails on rights nobody may sell. Filed dotted,
  working.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: LAW ↔ MEASUREMENT AT THE JUROR — whether
  sureness can ever be instrumented live (the SCIENCE lack);
  dotted until an elicitation survives contact with a jury box.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: LAW ↔ MIND ON DETERRENCE — the penalty
  arithmetic assumes an offender who computes expected cost;
  measured offenders track catch-chance and discount the future
  steeply. Filed dotted where the incentive laws borrow it.
