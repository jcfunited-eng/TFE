# ENGINEERING — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/engineering/color.md: same parts,
opposite sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting
for its drain. Seed authored by Claude and marked as seed.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE PERPETUAL MOTION MACHINE — the most-attempted thing in the
  subject's history: overbalanced wheels since the 12th century,
  self-filling flasks, magnet motors, each era's rediscovery in
  its era's parts. CERTIFIED CANNOT-BE under its conditions: a
  CLOSED loop in a world where energy is conserved returns at
  most what it was given, and friction taxes every real loop
  below that. The patent offices keep the ledger — the one class
  of application for which a working model is still demanded, and
  none has ever arrived. The certificate's edge: a machine
  drinking an OUTSIDE flow — sun, wind, tide, a temperature
  difference — runs "forever" honestly, because it is not closed;
  every working "perpetual" machine ever examined was this,
  misdescribed. Would drain on: a measured violation of energy
  conservation surviving audit; two centuries of candidates have
  all resolved into unmeasured inflows.
- THE UNSINKABLE SHIP, AND ALL FAILURE-PROOF DESIGN — tried,
  paid. The famous receipt (1912): compartments good for any two
  flooded, or the first four — the ice opened five, perhaps six,
  and the walls, carried only somewhat above the waterline, were
  overtopped one by one as the bow settled; lifeboat seats for
  barely half aboard, provisioned to the adjective rather than
  the count; ~1,500 dead. WHY filed: the design was sound UNDER
  ITS STATED CASES, and the adjective claimed all cases — no
  finite arithmetic covers every condition. What holds moved to
  the color: margins under NAMED conditions, failure designed
  rather than denied. This entry does not drain; it stands as
  the permanent price of adjectives.
- THE ELEVATOR TO ORBIT — open want, filed with its exact gap. A
  cable hung from beyond the stationary orbit must at least hold
  its own hanging weight; the measure is breaking length
  (strength ÷ weight-per-volume ÷ gravity): steel ~5 km, the
  best aramid ~255 km, the best manufactured carbon fiber
  ~400 km — and the job needs ~5,000 km, twelvefold past the
  best spool on earth. Taper eases it at exponential cost: at
  carbon fiber's numbers the top must be ~270,000× the bottom's
  area — absurd. Lab nanotubes pass the strength test (~4,000 km
  breaking length) in flawless fragments under a millimeter.
  Would drain on: a ~5,000 km-breaking-length fiber made
  flawless at 100,000 km lengths — the number to watch is the
  longest defect-free strand, nothing else.
- THE ROOM-TEMPERATURE SUPERCONDUCTING GRID — open want, and a
  true unknown, not a cannot: no written law caps
  zero-resistance below room temperature. Today every wire pays
  resistance — of each twenty units generated, roughly one dies
  as heat in the lines. Zero-loss wire exists, but cold (the
  practical best rides liquid nitrogen); room-temperature claims
  so far live only under millions of atmospheres or failed
  replication (the 2023 episode). Would drain on: zero
  resistance at ~300 K and one atmosphere, in a wire that winds
  and joints at grid prices.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "STRONGER IS BETTER" — didn't hold: strength and toughness are
  different properties, and hardening buys one by selling the
  other. The wartime receipt: ~2,700 welded cargo ships whose
  steel turned brittle near freezing — North Atlantic water —
  while the welds gave cracks a continuous highway; ~1,500
  serious fractures, about a dozen ships snapped in two, some
  quietly at dock. Condition filed: below its brittleness
  temperature the steel kept strength and lost forgiveness.
  Drained by cold-rated steel plus crack-arrestor seams — the
  claim recomputed into "tough enough AT SERVICE TEMPERATURE."
- "IT HELD IN THE TEST, SO IT WILL HOLD IN SERVICE" — the static
  test cannot see cyclic death. The receipt in the air (1954):
  the first jet airliners passed every load test, then two broke
  apart at altitude after roughly a thousand pressurization
  cycles each — cracks born at the sharp corners of nearly
  square openings, where stress crowds threefold and worse.
  Drained into the color as law: count cycles, round corners —
  every round jet window since is this entry, drained.
- "WIND IS A STEADY PUSH" — held a century of bridges, then
  didn't: the 1940 narrows bridge, sized soundly for wind as
  pressure, fell to wind as RHYTHM — moving air fed the deck's
  own twisting beat until the deck fed back, at 68 km/h, four
  months after opening. WHY: a load with a frequency is a
  partner, not a push. Drained by shapes tested in moving air;
  every slender structure now pays this entry's tunnel fee.
- "GOOD IN CRUSH IS GOOD ENOUGH IN PULL" — cast iron's century:
  it crushes at ~600 N/mm² and tears at ~150, and bridges that
  asked it to pull paid — girders down in 1847, a firth bridge
  down in an 1879 storm with ~75 aboard, none saved. The color's
  strength table lists both numbers because of this entry.
- "ADD METAL WHERE IT BROKE" — half-true and often backwards:
  the break is usually a SHAPE problem (corner, notch, sudden
  stiffness change), and meat added there moves the crowding
  next door. Holds only when the σ = F/A arithmetic truly
  condemned the section; otherwise the fix is a radius.
- UNSETTLED: WHEN EXACTLY A CRACK GROWS — measured growth fits
  interpolate well and extrapolate poorly; the inspection
  interval is the confession. Nothing certain — but here is a
  possibility: growth rides the stress swing at the tip so
  steeply that small unknowns in load history swamp any formula;
  continuous load RECORDS, not better fits, would drain it.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT THE DRAG WILL BE — turbulent flow past a real
  shape cannot be computed from first principles at real sizes;
  coefficients come from tunnels and tanks, and mathematics
  keeps its famous open problem under this entry's floorboards.
  Until it drains, the tunnel is the theory.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A PREDICTIVE THEORY OF FRICTION — every μ in the
  color's table is measured, none computed from the materials.
  The ignorance bites both ways: two truly clean metal faces in
  vacuum seize and weld cold; the same pair oiled slides at a
  twentieth of the grip. The subject's oldest daily quantity is
  bookkeeping, not law. Would drain on: computing μ from the two
  faces' measured roughness and chemistry alone — until then,
  the table is the theory.
- LACKING: FATIGUE LIFE TO BETTER THAN A FEW-FOLD — identical
  parts under identical lab loads scatter their lives by factors
  of two and three; hence calendars and counts where a formula
  is wished for. Would drain on: reading a part's internal
  defect census from outside.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: NAIVE SCALE MODELS — the model that holds
  proves the shape, never the size; weight grows a power faster
  than section strength (the color holds the law). The break
  built its repair: matching dimensionless ratios instead of
  sizes — knowledge about model-making, filed from the wreckage
  of trusting miniatures.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
wings-on-arms entries, waiting for their planet.

- THE OVERBALANCED WHEEL — weights swing outward on the falling
  side to "overweigh" the rising side; built in the same form
  for eight centuries; every one stops. WHY, one line: torque
  gained on the way down is repaid exactly in hauling the
  weights back up — around any closed loop in gravity the
  account balances to zero, minus friction. CONDITION: a closed
  loop. The wings-on-arms drain, already cashed: let an outside
  flow through the wheel and it turns forever, honestly — and
  is called a water wheel, or a turbine. The method was never
  wrong; the bookkeeping was.
- SAFETY BY ADJECTIVE — "unsinkable, fireproof, foolproof":
  tried each generation, paid each generation (THINGS holds the
  flagship receipt). Drains only into numbers under named
  conditions; the color's safety-factor table is this method's
  honest replacement.
- THE RIGID FIX — meet every disturbance with more stiffness and
  mass: wins against steady loads, loses against shaking ones,
  because shaking force = mass × acceleration and the added mass
  RAISES the earthquake's bill. Filed with its split: rigidity
  for the steady; deliberate flexibility — isolated bases, tuned
  counterweights — for the rhythmic.
- WELD EVERYTHING BECAUSE RIVETS ARE SLOW — the pure form failed
  at sea (CLAIMS): a hull became one crystal path and a crack,
  once born, ran the girth. Partially drained: welding returned
  with tougher steel and riveted arrestor strips — the method
  kept, its condition written on it. The general lesson stands:
  a joint is also a firewall; remove every joint and you remove
  every fire door.
- CALCULATE INSTEAD OF TEST — recurs in every era of good
  arithmetic, including this one: analysis covers the cases the
  analyst imagined; service supplies the others. The proof test
  (color, METHODS) is the standing repair, and this entry is why
  no confident calculation may argue it away.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE EMPTY CORNER OF THE MATERIALS TABLE — light AND strong AND
  cheap AND tough: pick at most three. Steel is strong, tough,
  cheap, heavy; aluminum light and affordable, soft; titanium
  strong and light at tens of times the price; carbon fiber
  strong and light, dear, and breaks without warning instead of
  yielding. Every new material is measured against this corner;
  the corner is still empty. Would drain on: a material near
  aluminum's weight and steel's strength and price that yields
  before it breaks — each recipe so far has bought its missing
  virtue by selling one it had.
- THE BATTERY THAT MATCHES THE FUEL TANK — gasoline carries
  ~12,800 watt-hours per kilogram, the best production cells
  ~300: roughly 40× apart as chemistry. The engine's waste
  (~28% kept) against the motor's thrift (~90%) closes the gap
  at the wheels to ~13× — still the number that sizes every
  electric machine's range. Would drain on: cells near
  1,500 Wh/kg, or a world where the tank refills everywhere the
  machine stops.
- THE BEARING THAT NEVER WEARS — every bearing in the color is a
  maintenance schedule; the body runs living joints at a
  friction coefficient (~0.005) an order below oiled steel,
  self-repairing, for eighty years. The mean exists in flesh and
  is not yet copyable in metal — standing proof the want is
  physical, not fanciful.
- THE ZERO-LOSS WIRE — cross-filed from THINGS: the grid's
  one-in-twenty toll stands until the superconductor drains.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- ENERGY FROM NOTHING — CANNOT, first law (the THINGS
  certificate). Its subtler sibling: WORK FROM ONE TEMPERATURE —
  CANNOT, second law: a ship cannot run on the ocean's warmth
  alone; an engine needs a cold side, and the color's ceiling
  formula prices exactly what that costs. Both certificates ride
  the widest conditions any entry carries: this universe's
  conservation laws.
- BUILD ONCE, MAINTAIN NEVER — open, never met: everything
  corrodes, fatigues, or wears. The only roads on record:
  enormous margins in inert stone (twenty-century arches, bought
  with hundredfold weight) or sacrifice schedules (zinc, paint,
  grease, calendars). Would drain on: self-repair — the mean the
  body demonstrates and metal lacks.
- LIFT TO ORBIT AT FREIGHT PRICES — the want behind the elevator
  entry: the climb to stationary orbit costs ~58 million joules
  a kilogram — 16 kilowatt-hours, about two dollars of
  electricity — while the rocket, carrying its own propellant,
  must be ~88% propellant merely to reach LOW orbit — climb plus
  losses ~9.4 km/s against ~4.4 km/s exhaust, e^(9.4/4.4) ≈ 8.5 —
  more again for the stationary height (~13 km/s from the ground:
  ~95%), and prices the same kilogram in thousands.
  The gap between two dollars and the going rate IS the want,
  measured; it drains with the cable, or any road that leaves
  the propellant on the ground.
- KNOW THE STRUCTURE'S HEALTH WITHOUT OPENING IT — cracks grow
  under paint and inside welds; today's answer is the calendar
  and the borescope. Partly drained (strain gauges at chosen
  points, fibers in new composites); open in general — most of
  the built world is mute.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- The patent ledger of perpetual motion: centuries deep, one
  rule-change deep — the demand for a working model — and still
  open at zero delivered. The subject's cleanest proof that a
  certificate BY CONDITIONS beats refusals one at a time: the
  office does not argue with each wheel; it holds the
  closed-loop condition and lets the wheels argue with that.
- The boiler century: steam power ran decades ahead of its
  arithmetic, and in the worst years a boiler burst every few
  days somewhere — the dead were the operating data. One
  factory's 58 (1905) bought the written code (1915). The
  color's HISTORY holds the compressed clause; this page holds
  what the compression is OF.
- The four tuition payments, each buying one law: the liner
  (state the conditions or the adjective sinks you), the jet
  (count the cycles, round the corners), the narrows bridge
  (respect the rhythm), the welded ships (toughness has a
  temperature). The color's shortest laws stand on this page's
  longest receipts — the mirror's ratio, nowhere plainer.
- The trying that never made the color at all: the uncounted
  collapsed roofs, burst dams, and folded bridges of anonymous
  builders before codes, whose only monument is the margins now
  carried. The safety factor's table of going rates is their
  compressed census.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, THEN REBUILT: ENGINEERING ↔ "JUST SCALE IT UP" — the
  intuitive relation to mathematics (similar shape, similar
  behavior) broke on the cube-square law and was rebuilt as the
  discipline of matched dimensionless ratios; the broken form is
  kept because every generation's first model-builder reaches
  for it again.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: LIFE AS AN UNREAD DESIGN LIBRARY — the
  copying relation grips in verified spots (the burr that became
  the hook-and-loop fastener, drag-shaped skins) and fails
  silently in most others: the living version's hidden
  conditions — self-repair, growth, wetness — do not transfer
  with the shape. Filed dotted: copy the principle with its
  conditions and the relation pays; copy the shape and it
  usually doesn't.
- BROKEN AND PATCHED, NEVER DRAINED: ECONOMY'S LOWEST BID ↔
  SAFETY'S MARGIN — the two pull opposite ways through every
  project, and the failure mode (margin quietly sold to price)
  recurs each generation; codes and liability are the standing
  patch, not a cure. Filed open: the next receipt is always in
  the mail.
