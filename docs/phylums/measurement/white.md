## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE PERFECTLY OBJECTIVE OBSERVER — wanted since measuring began;
  CANNOT BE under the condition that every reading routes through a
  chooser: someone picks the definition, the starting date, the
  threshold, the weights, the stopping point, and the eye votes for
  what it already believes. Blinding, controls, and pre-stated bets
  SHRINK the observer's hand, measurably — they never zero it. The
  honest substitute stands in the color: not one clean observer but
  many opposed dirty ones, replicating. Would drain on: an observer
  with no stake and no priors — none is written anywhere; the
  working drain stops at "adversaries who agree."
- THE DISTURBANCE-FREE READING — cannot at the small end, priced at
  the human end (the wall's numbers in SCIENCE); the want splits by
  scale and only the big half ever drains.
- THE SELF-CERTIFYING INSTRUMENT — cannot: a tool checked against
  itself reads its own drift as truth; the chain of trust must end
  OUTSIDE, and the end is an agreement, not a measurement — the
  shape mathematics knows as no-system-certifies-itself. Certified
  under the condition "certification from inside," no move of
  conditions on offer: one of the clean cannots.
- THE ONE TRUE NUMBER FOR UNLIKE THINGS — the composite that is not
  an opinion. Cannot: unlike quantities add only through a
  conversion rate, and the rate is a judgment that vanishes inside
  the total. Drains PARTIALLY, by honesty not arithmetic: publish
  the weights beside the score. An index that hides its weights
  stays white forever.
- THE FINAL LENGTH OF A ROUGH THING — the coastline's one true
  count. Cannot: length grows with every finer stick (~2,400 km at
  200 km, ~3,400 at 50 km, still climbing) and never settles — a
  property of the thing, not of rulers. Drains only into the
  color's rule: state the stick with the number.
- THE SOCIETY PREDICTOR — an instrument reading a people's next
  decade the way an almanac reads tides; attempted in spirit many
  times. The record and conditions are in SCIENCE — this subject's
  largest tried-and-broken want.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "THE BIGGEST SAMPLE EVER ASSEMBLED HAS SETTLED IT" — 1936: ten
  million straw ballots mailed, 2.4 million returned, landslide
  called for the loser by ~19 points; a rival's ~50,000, drawn
  even-handedly, called the winner. WHY: the frame was car and
  telephone lists in a depression — tilted before the first ballot,
  and 2.4 million copies of a tilt are a tilt with confidence. Size
  never repairs drawing; this receipt carries the color's
  blind-draw method. No drain: a tilted frame is tilted at every
  size — the fix is a different draw, and it already stands in the
  color.
- "THE MODEL MEASURED THE RISK" — a fund steered by prize-winning
  arithmetic, leveraged ~25-to-1, lost ~$4.6 billion in a few
  months of 1998. WHY, in this phylum's own laws: correlations
  measured in one range were bet outside it, and the co-movement
  was ACTED on — everyone holding the same numbers ran for the same
  door, and the act destroyed the pattern it bet on. Would drain
  on: exposures inside the measured range AND small enough not to
  move what they measure; the second condition is the hard one.
- "FUSION IN A JAR" — announced from one bench; no stranger's hands
  ever got it again. Replication's toll bridge held and the claim
  did not cross — the cleanest modern receipt that one result is a
  rumor, however loud. Would drain on: strangers' benches getting
  the heat again with instruments open; decades of trying have
  filed none.
- "SIGNIFICANT MEANT IMPORTANT" — the small word oversold: it means
  only bigger-than-luck's-wobble, and searching makes luck wobble
  on demand (1,000 rules at a 1-in-20 bar pass ~50 empty ones).
  Held white because queries keep arriving wearing it; the cure —
  pre-stated bets, strangers' hands — is in the color.
- UNSETTLED: HOW TO MEASURE WHAT HAS NO COUNTABLE HANDLE — pain,
  well-being, learning, effort, a company's health. Every current
  answer is a proxy, and every proxy detaches when leaned on (the
  target law). Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: a
  BUNDLE of weak unlike proxies with declared weights, rotated
  faster than any one can be gamed, judged by whether the bundle
  survives being made a target. Would drain on: one proxy anywhere
  shown to keep tracking its quantity AFTER pay was tied to it; no
  such receipt is filed.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT A BLANK MEANS AFTER THE FACT — nothing happened,
  nobody looked, and the instrument failed cannot be told apart
  from the blank alone, and archives are made of such blanks.
  Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: record the LOOKING
  itself — searches logged with their power, nulls filed as nulls —
  so future blanks arrive with causes attached. That practice is
  this fabric's own white; whether old archives can be retrofitted
  is the open half.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; where applied science
broke; and the certified wall.

- THE CERTIFIED WALL AT THE SMALL END: measurement without
  disturbance is forbidden by law, not clumsiness — the world
  refuses exact pairs: position tightened blurs motion, as
  Δx × Δp ≥ ħ/2 (physics holds the full form). WORKED at both ends:
  pin an electron (~9.1 × 10⁻³¹ kg) to one atom's width (10⁻¹⁰ m)
  and its speed is undefined by at least 1.055 × 10⁻³⁴ ÷
  (2 × 9.11 × 10⁻³¹ × 10⁻¹⁰) ≈ 580,000 m/s — the reading destroys
  the thing beside it. Pin a dust grain (10⁻⁹ kg) to a hair's width
  (10⁻⁶ m): the forced blur is ~5 × 10⁻²⁰ m/s — about a sixtieth of
  one atom's width of drift PER YEAR, nothing. CONDITIONS INDEXED:
  the wall binds near the electron's mass; at kitchen masses it is
  unmeasurably slack, and there the toll is ordinary and payable
  (the color's disturbance claim). This cannot does not shimmer at
  its own scale — one of the fabric's hardest certified
  impossibilities.
- LACKING: A SCIENCE OF THE SINGLE CASE — the machinery runs on
  repetition: bars from scatter, rates from crowds, verdicts from
  priors. The one-off — this patient, this year, this bridge —
  receives only its crowd's rate, and the gap between rate and case
  is real, felt, and unpriced. Nothing certain — but here is a
  possibility: cut the crowd closer to the case and pay the
  small-group scatter law for every cut; the trade between
  closeness and count has no settled optimum written anywhere.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: SOCIETY-SCALE PREDICTION — psychohistory
  tried in spirit, repeatedly. "Social physics" was coined for it;
  the confident laws of history predicted revolution first in the
  most industrial lands and got agrarian Russia; a whole economy
  was steered for decades by measured targets and produced
  nail-factory arithmetic at nation scale; the 1998 fund ran the
  same wager on markets. WHY, in conditions, all from this phylum's
  own color: (1) people READ the prediction and move — disturbance
  at full size, the watched behaving like the watched; (2) the
  definitions underneath (a job, a crime, a price) drift under
  political weight while the series pretends continuity; (3) error
  travels, and in a coupled system it compounds on the doubling
  clock. HONEST PARTIAL DRAIN, already cashed: aggregates that
  cannot hear the forecast hold — population projections stand for
  decades because next decade's mothers are already born and
  counted, and mortality tables carry the whole insurance trade.
  CONDITION FOR HOLDING: no feedback from forecast to forecasted,
  and pieces too many and uncoordinated to conspire. Societies fail
  both on purpose — that is rather what a society is. Would drain
  further on: a forecast of a self-aware crowd surviving its own
  publication; none is filed.
- BROKE: THE ARTIFACT STANDARD — the world's kilogram drifted ~50
  millionths of a gram against its own copies, with no way inside
  the system to say which had moved (the self-certification cannot,
  come due at the chain's top). Drained in 2019 onto a constant of
  nature; kept here because the century of quiet drift is the price
  tag on every chain ending at a thing instead of an invariance.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- STEERING BY THE MEASURE — tried by every institution that ever
  set a quota, fails on schedule: a four-hour ward target got waits
  renamed and patients parked in ambulances with the clock
  unstarted; an eight-accounts sales target got ~3.5 million
  accounts nobody asked for; and the told stories that carry the
  mechanism rather than receipts — weight quotas got few giant
  nails and count quotas got carpet tacks, the cobra bounty got
  cobra farms and more cobras after the bounty than before it. WHY: the measure was CORRELATED with the
  goal, and correlation licenses prediction, never action — pinning
  pay to the number is acting on it, and the unprotected
  correlation snaps while the number reports health. Drains only
  where the measured cannot respond (a thermostat is safely
  steered; a ward is not), or where targets rotate faster than they
  can be gamed — a possibility, with no receipt yet of one
  surviving long.
- RANKING AS IF IT MEASURED — league tables, top tens: order keeps
  sequence and discards distance, so first and second may be a hair
  apart while second and third are a canyon, and the list reads as
  equal steps. Recomputes the moment the gaps are printed beside
  the places; kept white because they almost never are.
- THE BET PLACED AFTER THE CARDS — patterns mined from a record
  then tested against the same record: trading systems fit to the
  past they were mined from, the horoscope's kept hits. Fails
  because the test could not have failed (the color's law: a test
  is real only where failure was possible). Drains one way only:
  new data, or a bet stated before it.
- THE QUESTION THAT PLANTS ITS ANSWER — surveys instructing their
  subjects: asking installs the opinion it then measures,
  disturbance in words. Fell short under every rewording tried; the
  residue is managed, never removed — the observer entry above is
  its ceiling.
- THE STRAIGHT LINE PAST THE LAST POINT — extrapolation as method:
  fertilizer curves carried past the turn, growth rates carried
  twenty years, materials trusted past the tested load until they
  suddenly are not. Fails by the range law; drains only by
  measuring further, never by drawing further.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE DRIFT-FREE INSTRUMENT — not found: heat, age, and use move
  every tool; the atom is the closest approach (~1 second per 100
  million years) and even it is read through machinery that drifts.
  The color's chain-and-schedule is the standing compensation, not
  a cure.
- A STICK FOR VALUE THAT HOLDS STILL — money measures worth while
  itself swelling and shrinking; the real-versus-nominal correction
  is a patch that must itself be measured, by a basket someone
  chose — the composite cannot, again. The economy phylum holds the
  live entry; filed here as the one treaty stick that moves by
  design.
- A NEUTRAL DEFINITION — not found: every countable line (a crime,
  a job, a death) was drawn by someone with something at stake, and
  redrawing it moves the number while the world stands still. The
  working substitute is the color's rule: publish the line WITH the
  number. A line with no drawer is not on offer.
- THE HONEST BLANK-FILLER — no instrument recovers what was never
  recorded; the unrecorded cannot testify, and imputation invents
  witnesses. Would drain per-case on an independent second record
  (the cross-checking-clocks pattern) — never in general.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- MEASURE THE IMPORTANT DIRECTLY — the countable-handle problem;
  every current road is a proxy and proxies rot when leaned on.
  Open.
- PREDICT THE SELF-AWARE — societies, markets, any crowd that reads
  its own forecast; blocked by the feedback condition in SCIENCE,
  open exactly where that condition fails to bind (aggregates that
  cannot hear).
- CERTIFY FROM INSIDE — closed. The chain ends outside or it is not
  a chain; the fabric's own trust in its written sources obeys the
  same law.
- READ WITHOUT TOUCHING, ALL THE WAY DOWN — closed at the bottom by
  the wall, open above it only as better-priced tolls.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- The straw-poll empire: the magazine that mailed ten million
  ballots was the most trusted forecaster of its age, and its 1936
  wreck retired it within two years. The blind draw's victory cost
  a famous name its life — the price at which sampling entered the
  color.
- The kilogram's quiet century: the drift was suspected for decades
  before the 2019 retirement; the world ran its commerce on a stick
  known to be moving because no better chain's end existed yet.
  Filed as patience, not scandal: a known white entry, worked
  around until the drain arrived.
- The probe and the two sticks: pound-seconds against
  newton-seconds, a silent 4.45, ~$330 million burned in another
  planet's sky. The color's unit-grammar law is written in that
  ink.
- The fund's autumn: the arithmetic was right inside its range, and
  the range was the past; priced at ~$4.6 billion and a near-miss
  for the whole credit system — the range law and the
  act-on-correlation law, invoiced together.
- The armor that almost went where the holes were: the first
  instinct — reinforce what the returning planes showed — was
  natural, and wrong; the unseen downed planes held the lesson.
  Survivorship is filed as the enemy that looks exactly like data.
- The jar that would not replicate: a fusion claim, weeks of
  headlines, then the silence of strangers' hands. The bridge held;
  the toll was two careers — replication's cost is real and the
  color charges it anyway.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- MEASUREMENT ↔ MIND, NEVER FULLY SEVERED: the dream of removing
  the observer broke everywhere it was tried; the standing link
  carries a toll schedule instead — blind what can be blinded,
  state bets in advance, book the residue into every bar. That the
  residue can reach zero is the suspected-and-unproven half, filed
  here.
- MEASUREMENT ↔ GOVERNANCE, LOAD-BEARING AND KNOWN-CORROSIVE:
  states cannot steer without numbers, and every number a state
  publishes becomes a target and begins to rot (receipts in
  METHODS). The relation cannot be cut — the census IS governance —
  so it stands with a maintenance duty: rotate, audit, publish
  definitions. A form of the link that does not corrode is not yet
  written.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: EVERY IMPORTANT QUANTITY HAS AN HONEST PROXY
  BUNDLE — the hope under all composite indexes, the possibility
  offered in CLAIMS. Stands dotted until one bundle survives being
  made a target.
- BROKEN AND PATCHED: MEASUREMENT ↔ ECONOMY ON VALUE'S STICK — the
  patch (deflation to "real" terms) works only as well as its
  chosen basket, which carries the composite's cannot inside it.
  First-class in both colors; filed here because the patch's own
  error has no settled bar.
- MEASUREMENT ↔ LIFE, SUSPECTED DOOR: senses are instruments —
  drifting, disturbing, self-calibrating against reference points —
  and whether this whole phylum formalizes what every organism
  already does is an open crossing with the life and body phylums.
  Nothing certain; filed as the interesting direction.
