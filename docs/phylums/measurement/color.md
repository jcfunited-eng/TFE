## THINGS
The units this subject is about. Measurement is not a field beside
the others: it is the toll road every claim in every phylum traveled
or must travel. Each thing names other homes so a cascade can branch.

- THE STICK — the agreed unit: meter, gram, second, dollar. Nature
  carries quantities but no units; the stick is the treaty that lets
  two strangers' numbers speak. Also: economy (currency is the stick
  for value), music (concert pitch, so orchestras can meet), law
  (the blood line, the deadline), cooking (grams vs cups).
- THE ERROR BAR — the give-or-take every honest number wears; a
  finding, not an apology. Also: physics (the floor where it becomes
  law), engineering (tolerance), people (the poll's small print).
- THE INSTRUMENT — whatever turns a quantity into a reading. Every
  one borrows a physical regularity, drifts, and takes a small toll
  from what it touches. Also: engineering, physics.
- THE CONTROL — the untouched twin showing what would have happened
  anyway; the only mirror a cause can be seen in. Also: medicine
  (the sugar-pill group), farming (the side-by-side plot).
- THE SAMPLE — the part made to speak for the whole; it speaks only
  for the crowd it was blindly drawn from, and size never repairs
  the drawing. Also: people, governance (the census), medicine.
- THE COUNT AND THE READING — two acts: a count is exact and jumps
  (four chairs, never 4.3); a reading is continuous and never exact
  (1.42 m, give or take). Confusing them births false precision.
  Also: computing (integers vs decimals), law (a legal age is a
  count imposed on something continuous).
- THE RATE — a count divided by its exposure; the division is where
  meaning enters. Also: economy (return on capital), medicine
  (deaths per procedure), play (per-game).
- THE DEFINITION — the line deciding what counts as a crime, a job,
  a death; it moves the number before counting starts. Also: law
  (statutes are definitions with consequences), governance.
- THE CLOCK AND THE CALENDAR — a counter of something regular we
  agree to call time (weight, crystal, atom's hum), and the
  bookkeeping for an orbit whose day, moon, and year share no common
  multiple. Also: physics, astronomy, music (the metronome),
  computing, farming.
- THE RECORD, IN TWO KINDS — the STEP (something happened: a sale, a
  tick — exact, dated) and the STATE (where things stand: a balance,
  a temperature — a level). The ledger law below governs them. Also:
  computing, economy, law (chain of custody).
- THE BLANK — a hole in the record; not a zero and not a fact until
  its cause is known. Also: story (the unrecorded cannot testify),
  medicine (the untested patient).
- THE RANKING AND THE INDEX — an order that hides distance, and a
  weighted sum that hides its weights; both used as if they
  measured. Also: economy, play, teaching.
- THE PROVENANCE — a number's maker, reason, funder, and stopping
  point; part of the value, like the unit. Also: law (chain of
  custody), language (a citation is a provenance record).

## CLAIMS
What holds, written to be tested by a crossing query.

- A measurement is a claim wearing an error bar. The honest form is
  "this much, give or take that much," and the give-or-take is
  itself measured: repeat the reading and the scatter you see IS the
  bar.
- Precision and accuracy fail separately: how tightly the shots
  cluster, and whether the cluster sits on the target. A scale can
  be wrong by the same two kilos every time — repetition measures
  scatter and is blind to the standing lean; only comparison against
  a standard sees the lean.
- A result is never firmer than its shakiest ingredient: bars travel
  through arithmetic with the numbers, so a long calculation can end
  vaguer than anything that went in. Dead reckoning is this law
  walking; stacked tolerances are why the last part won't fit.
- The control is the only mirror. "What would have happened anyway"
  cannot be reasoned out — time moves everything, so before-and-
  after is no mirror. Change one thing, hold the rest, keep the
  untouched twin.
- Moving together is not causing: a hidden third thing often moves
  both (ice cream and drownings — summer moves each), the arrow can
  point backward (roosters and sunrise), or the third thing is the
  trouble's own size (more firemen at bigger fires). Co-movement
  licenses prediction, never action: push one thing to move the
  other and you have assumed a cause — the assumption is the risk.
- Who you asked shapes what you heard. Convenient samples — who
  answered, who survived, who walked in the door — smuggle a tilt:
  the clinic sees the sick, the complaints desk the furious, old
  buildings look well made because the bad ones fell. The bombers
  that came home showed where a plane could be hit and LIVE; the
  armor belonged where their holes weren't.
- Seekers find what they seek: the eye votes for what it believes,
  keeps hits and forgets misses — superstition's whole recipe. The
  machinery (controls, blinding, bets stated in advance) exists
  first to defeat the measurer's own eye.
- One result is a rumor. A finding becomes knowledge when strangers
  with their own hands and no stake get it again; replication is
  truth's toll bridge, disinfecting luck, tilt, and hunger at once.
- A miss is worth exactly the search's power: a clear scan of the
  right organ means much, "I feel fine" means little, keys absent
  from the bowl are not absent from the house.
- The strongest knowledge forbids. A law earns its keep by what it
  rules out; a test is only real where failure was possible; a claim
  no outcome could embarrass measures nothing.
- Some quantities are decided by the ruler: a rugged coastline runs
  ~2,400 km under a 200 km stick and ~3,400 km under a 50 km stick —
  the small stick follows bays the large one skips, and the length
  never settles. State the stick or the number is not a claim.
- Counting has its own honesty rules: a blank is not a zero (nothing
  happened, nobody looked, and the instrument failed are three
  different facts); a count means nothing without its base (most
  accidents happen near home because most driving does — ask: out of
  how many?); and each thing counts once (one transfer becoming two
  admissions inflates quietly, because real categories overlap).
- Comparing across time means undoing what changed anyway —
  population grew, money thinned, December always sells more; skip
  the corrections and you measure the correction. And whoever picks
  the starting date picks the trend: begin at a peak and everything
  falls, at a trough and everything climbs, both charts honest.
- A measured relationship holds only across its measured range:
  fertilizer raises yield to a point and then poisons the field;
  past the last data point the straight line is a guess wearing the
  finding's authority.
- A measure that becomes a target stops measuring. Once a number
  decides pay, funding, or blame, effort flows to the number and it
  detaches from what it tracked while looking healthy. Receipts:
  ward waiting-time targets were met by renaming waits and parking
  patients in ambulances with the clock unstarted; a bank's
  eight-accounts target manufactured ~3.5 million accounts nobody
  asked for; drilling the test replaces the learning while scores
  rise. And the told story that carries the mechanism: quota by
  weight got a nail factory's few giant nails, quota by count got
  carpet tacks. Upstream of it: what is easy to
  measure gets measured instead of what matters — hours for effort,
  scores for learning, clicks for interest — and the gap between
  stand-in and thing is where the rot begins.
- Measuring disturbs: a thermometer takes heat, a tire gauge lets
  out air, a question plants the opinion it asks about, and the
  watched behave like the watched. At human scale the toll is priced
  and paid (SCIENCE below); at the bottom of the small it becomes a
  law the mirror certifies.
- Every number has a maker and a reason — someone chose to count
  this rather than that, funded it, defined it, and stopped. A
  figure with no provenance is an assertion in numeric costume, and
  most arguments about numbers are arguments about definitions in
  disguise.

## SCIENCE
The math of measuring, held — not mentioned. LAWs to compute with,
RULEs to follow, WORKED cases with the arithmetic run.

### THE ERROR ARITHMETIC

LAW: the bar means odds — ~2 chances in 3 that truth lies within one
  bar, ~19 in 20 within two.
RULE: to compare two measured numbers — subtract, combine bars by
  the square law, call the difference real only if bigger than its
  own combined bar.
WORKED: this month's poll reads 52 ± 3, last month's read 48 ± 3 —
  two independent samples. Lead 4; lead's bar √(3² + 3²) = √18 ≈
  4.2. A 4-point lead wearing a 4.2-point bar is not a lead — the
  small print unsays the headline. Inside ONE two-way poll the trap
  is worse: 48 is just 100 − 52, the same number worn twice, so the
  lead's errors are fully opposed and its bar is 2 × 3 = 6 — the
  aligned case of the law below, not the square-law case.

LAW: independent errors add by squares, aligned errors add straight:
  bar = √(bar₁² + bar₂²) when the errors don't know each other;
  bar₁ + bar₂ when one fault moves both.
WORKED: boards of 100 ± 3 cm and 50 ± 4 cm end to end: 150 cm, bar
  √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5, not ± 7 — independence bought 2 cm back. Same
  boards on one stretched tape: both errors lean one way, 3 + 4 = 7,
  a lean no averaging finds; that is the calibration chain's job.

LAW: averaging improves by the square root of the count —
  bar_of_mean = bar_of_one ÷ √N — so halving the bar costs FOUR
  times the observations.
WORKED: one weighing scatters ± 8 g; sixteen average to 8 ÷ √16 =
  2 g; reaching ± 1 g needs N = 64. Polls cluster near a thousand
  because 1 ÷ √1000 ≈ 0.032 — the familiar ± 3 points; ± 1 would
  cost ten thousand answers.

LAW: an answer cannot carry more digits than its inputs earned; no
  arithmetic adds information that was not measured.
WORKED: a field paced 43 × 87 paces, each pace ~1 m ± 10%: the
  calculator says 3,741 m², but the area's bar is √(10² + 10²) ≈
  14%, and 3,741 × 0.14 ≈ 520. Honest: ~3,700 ± ~500 m². The extra
  digits were decoration reading as authority.

LAW: rounding is a small lie repeated; direction decides whether the
  lies cancel (even-handed: grow as √N) or march (aligned: grow as
  N).
WORKED: a million entries rounded fairly to the cent drift about
  √1,000,000 × 0.29 cents ≈ $3 in total; the same million each
  shaved DOWN half a cent: 1,000,000 × $0.005 = $5,000 — the oldest
  theft there is. Auditors check the direction of roundings before
  their size.

### THE COMPARISON ARITHMETIC

LAW: a rate is a count divided by its exposure; never compare
  counts.
WORKED: the big hospital lost 100 of 10,000 treated — 1 in 100; the
  small one 30 of 1,000 — 3 in 100. The "deadliest" by counts is
  three times the safer by rate.

LAW: the mean weighs values by size, the median counts heads; in a
  lopsided pile they part company, and the gap measures the
  lopsidedness. An average alone is a rumor — one mean covers a
  tight lump, two separate crowds, and a long tail with giants.
WORKED: nine earners at 30,000 and one at 730,000: mean =
  (9 × 30,000 + 730,000) ÷ 10 = 1,000,000 ÷ 10 = 100,000; median =
  30,000. "Average income 100,000" is true of a street where the
  typical person earns 30,000.

LAW: a split can flip the answer — a combined ratio is not the
  average of its parts' ratios, because the parts carry weights.
WORKED: Doctor A — easy 95/100 live (95%), hard 540/900 (60%),
  total 635/1,000 = 63.5%. Doctor B — easy 810/900 (90%), hard
  50/100 (50%), total 860/1,000 = 86%. A wins BOTH groups and loses
  overall, because A takes the hard cases. The split is the true
  reading when difficulty differs.

LAW: relative and absolute change are two facts; only the pair means
  anything — a ratio discards the size of what it divides.
WORKED: "doubles your risk" on 1 in a million is one extra case per
  million; on 1 in 10, one extra per 10 — a different life. Same
  family: a rate moving 2% → 3% rose one POINT and also rose by
  HALF; both true, and mixing them silently resizes the claim.

LAW: how common beats how good the test is — the verdict combines
  evidence with prior frequency, and lopsided frequencies dominate.
WORKED: a condition strikes 1 in 1,000; the test is 99% right both
  ways. Of 1,000 tested: the 1 sick flags; of 999 healthy, 1% ≈ 10
  flag falsely. Eleven flags, one real — a positive is 1 ÷ 11 ≈ 9%
  likely true, from a "99% accurate" test.
LAW attached: no line through two overlapping crowds can miss less
  AND cry wolf less; every threshold is a chosen balance between two
  ways of being wrong, best chosen out loud (law's "better ten
  guilty go free" is such a threshold, picked in the open).

LAW: extremes live in small groups, and the pull back to the middle
  is a fact about measurement, not the world: any score is true
  level plus a draw; selecting the top selects both, and the draw
  cannot repeat.
WORKED: where ability and day-luck contribute equal scatter, the
  student 30 above the mean is best guessed 15 above on the retest —
  half the excellence was a draw, redrawn. The star fund, the hot
  week, and the rare-disease cluster in the smallest county are this
  law in three costumes.

LAW: "significant" means only bigger-than-luck's-usual-wobble — not
  large, not useful, not true in your case; searching makes luck
  clear the bar on schedule.
WORKED: screen 1,000 trading rules at the customary 1-in-20 bar:
  1,000 × 1/20 = 50 pass with nothing in them. The cures are above:
  the bet stated in advance, and strangers' hands.

### THE SCALES

LAW: get the power of ten before the digits — tenfold wrong is a
  different species of error from 3% wrong.
WORKED: a million seconds = 1,000,000 ÷ 86,400 ≈ 11.6 days; a
  billion ≈ 11,574 days ≈ 31.7 years. The million-billion gap is a
  vacation against a career — the whole argument, before polishing.

LAW: some of the world registers as multiples, not differences —
  sound, quake shaking, star brightness, acidity, money — so the
  matching scale spaces equal FACTORS equally.
WORKED: one Richter step ×10 shaking; +10 decibels ×10 power; one pH
  step ×10 acidity (cooking's canning line rides on it); one octave
  ×2 — A at 440 a second, its octave at 880. The ear is logarithmic
  by construction; a raise means the same as a percentage, not an
  amount.

LAW: seventy divided by the percent growth is the doubling time,
  near enough.
WORKED: 7% doubles in 70 ÷ 7 = 10 years (check: 1.07¹⁰ ≈ 1.97); 2%
  in 35 (1.02³⁵ ≈ 2.00). The mind extrapolates in straight lines and
  compounding does not — flat, then modest, then impossible in one
  stretch; the outbreak, the debt, and the herd past its pasture all
  arrive on this clock.

LAW: proportional changes multiply, making losses heavier than
  gains.
WORKED: down 50% then up 50% is 0.5 × 1.5 = 0.75 — down 25%; a 50%
  fall needs a 100% rise to get even. A sequence of swings ends
  below the average of its percentages; volatility itself costs.

LAW: a chain of rough factors lands closer than any link —
  independent errors partly cancel, the squares law working FOR you.
RULE: split the question into factors each guessable within 2-3×,
  multiply, trust the power of ten.
WORKED: piano tuners in a city of a million: 1,000,000 ÷ ~2.5 per
  household = 400,000 households; ~1 in 20 keeps a piano = 20,000
  pianos, tuned ~yearly; a tuner does ~4 a day × ~250 days = 1,000 a
  year; 20,000 ÷ 1,000 = ~20 tuners — within a factor of two of the
  listings, before any measuring.

### TIME — FROM THE SKY TO THE ATOM

LAW: a clock does not tell time; it counts something regular, and is
  only as true as the borrowed regularity.
WORKED, the second's whole history in arithmetic:
  · sky: one day ÷ 24 ÷ 60 ÷ 60 — 24 × 60 = 1,440; × 60 = 86,400 —
    the second was 1/86,400 of the spinning Earth;
  · pendulum: swing time T = 2π√(L ÷ g), so the length beating
    seconds is L = g × T² ÷ 4π² = 9.81 × 4 ÷ 39.48 ≈ 0.994 m — why
    the tall clock holds about a meter of pendulum, and why (g in
    the formula) it runs slow up a mountain;
  · crystal: quartz cut to vibrate 32,768 times a second — 2¹⁵, so
    fifteen halvings make one tick; drift ~15 s a month;
  · atom: 9,192,631,770 waves of one cesium hum — the definition
    itself since 1967, steady to ~1 s in 100 million years.
  The Earth was fired as the standard when the atom caught it
  wobbling (~2 ms a century slower, unevenly); the patch now runs
  backward — 27 leap seconds so far hold the sky to the atom.

LAW: "at the same moment" has to be shipped — no signal is instant,
  so distant clocks agree only through a sent signal plus an assumed
  delay; simultaneity is built, never observed.
WORKED: satellite clocks run ~38 millionths of a second a day off
  ground clocks (motion and gravity — physics' ledger); uncorrected,
  0.000038 s × 300,000 km/s ≈ 11 km of position error PER DAY. The
  correction is applied and your position holds; distributed
  computers, which cannot apply it perfectly, live without a true
  "now."

LAW: the calendar is remainder management — the year is ~365.2422
  days and days don't divide it.
WORKED: 0.2422 × 4 ≈ 0.97 — nearly a day per four years, hence the
  leap day; the Julian 365.25 average overshoots by 0.0078 day a
  year, × 400 ≈ 3.1 days — hence the Gregorian repair, dropping 3
  leap days per 400 years.

LAW: every clock in the rocks rests on two assumptions — starting
  amount known, nothing in or out since; a date is exactly as good
  as those two.
WORKED: carbon-14 halves every ~5,730 years; a sample holding a
  quarter of its start is two halvings old: 2 × 5,730 = 11,460 years
  — IF the start is calibrated (tree rings and ice layers, counted
  directly) and IF nothing seeped. Cross-checking unrelated clocks
  is replication in another form.

### THE LEDGER LAW — steps and states

LAW: state_now = state_known + sum of steps since. A complete step
  record rebuilds every state; no run of states rebuilds the steps —
  many histories share the same balances, and differencing two
  states recovers only the NET step.
RULE: record steps where you can; snapshot states as checkpoints;
  reconcile — a state recomputed from steps must meet the state read
  directly, and any gap is a missing step or a drifted instrument.
WORKED: start at 1,000; steps +250, −400, +75: 1,000 + 250 = 1,250;
  − 400 = 850; + 75 = 925. An auditor holding the steps sees the 400
  leave and can ask why; one holding only Monday's 1,000 and
  Friday's 925 sees a quiet −75 and can ask nothing — the traffic is
  gone. Hence banks keep transactions, not just balances; and the
  checkpoint still matters, because one correct snapshot repairs
  what a lost step would poison forever.

### THE CHAIN — calibration back to the standards

LAW: no instrument certifies itself; its truth is inherited down a
  chain of comparisons, and the chain must end at something agreed
  rather than measured. Today the ends are nature's constants: the
  cesium second; the meter as the distance light crosses in
  1/299,792,458 of it; the kilogram hung on Planck's constant
  (6.62607015 × 10⁻³⁴, exact by declaration since 2019). The
  agreement is the treaty; nature supplies the invariance.
LAW: calibration is maintenance, not an event — instruments drift
  with heat, age, and use; a tool checked once and trusted forever
  tells a story growing falser while looking the same.
RULE: compare against a reference on a schedule; when drift is
  found, every reading since the last good check is suspect, and the
  fix is a correction factor, not an apology.
WORKED: a kitchen scale reads 512 g on a true 500 g reference —
  everything since the last check ran 512 ÷ 500 = 2.4% heavy;
  multiplying the record by 500 ÷ 512 ≈ 0.977 repairs it. Books
  reconciled against the bank and the cuff checked yearly are the
  same law lower on one chain.

LAW: units are arithmetic's grammar — the unit is part of the value
  and travels with it; if the answer's units come out wrong, the
  method is wrong, whatever the number says.
WORKED: a Mars probe flew with one team writing thrust in
  pound-seconds and the other reading newton-seconds — a silent
  factor of 4.45 per burn. It crossed the atmosphere at ~57 km
  instead of the planned ~226 km and burned: ~$330 million spent
  certifying this law. Carrying units through the working would have
  caught it on paper.

## METHODS
How measuring is done — what governs each, and what breaks it.

- THE CONTROLLED EXPERIMENT: change one thing, hold the rest still,
  keep the untouched twin — causes hide in crowds of circumstance
  and answer only isolation. Breaks when two things changed at once,
  or the twin was not alike. Debugging any machine is this method.
- BLINDING AND THE PRE-STATED BET: decide what counts as success
  BEFORE looking; where people judge, let neither patient nor doctor
  know who got the real pill. Breaks when the bet is placed after
  the cards are seen — a pattern mined from the past and admired
  against the same past has passed no test.
- THE BLIND DRAW: lots, not judgment, pick the sample — convenience
  is never blind. Breaks when the frame itself tilts (who owns a
  phone, who answers, who is alive to be counted); the mirror holds
  the famous receipt.
- REPLICATION: hand the claim to strangers with their own
  instruments and no stake; what works only in the inventor's
  kitchen is not knowledge yet. One witness wants corroboration.
- CALIBRATION ON A SCHEDULE: reconcile against the reference, log
  the drift, correct the record back to the last good check.
- CARRYING UNITS THROUGH: write every unit into the working and let
  the grammar check the method; convert at the border and say so.
- ADJUSTING ACROSS TIME: divide by the base, deflate the money,
  remove the season before comparing Decembers to Junes. Breaks when
  the adjustment is itself the contested choice — then publish raw
  and adjusted both, with the method.
- RECONCILING STEPS AGAINST STATES: recompute the state from steps,
  compare to the state read directly, chase every gap to a missing
  step or a drifting instrument. Bookkeeping, inventory, and version
  control are one method in three trades.
- ESTIMATING BEFORE MEASURING: the rough-factor chain — catch
  tenfold errors before they cost, size a question before funding
  it. The fastest cure for a wrong answer is estimating it first.
- READING A NUMBER'S PROVENANCE: ask its maker, definition, base,
  starting date, bucket rules (anything counted twice?), and who
  paid. Most bad numbers fail this checklist before any arithmetic.
- SETTING THE THRESHOLD OUT LOUD: where a line must cut overlapping
  crowds, choose the miss-versus-false-alarm balance deliberately
  and state it. An alarm tuned to catch everything gets switched
  off, which catches nothing.

## MEANS
What the methods run on, with the numbers that matter.

- THE TREATY STANDARDS: the cesium second (9,192,631,770 waves), the
  light-second meter (1/299,792,458), the Planck kilogram — and
  their civil cousins: concert pitch at 440, the blood line at 0.08,
  the deadline at midnight. Sticks whose worth IS the agreement.
- THE REFERENCES: the checkweight, the standard resistor, freezing
  and boiling as free fixed points, tree rings and ice layers as
  nature's dated archive. An instrument is trusted only as far as
  its latest meeting with one of these.
- THE CLOCK LADDER: pendulum (~a meter of length, seconds by
  gravity), quartz (32,768 a second, ~15 s a month), cesium (~1 s
  per 100 million years). Pick by the job: the kitchen needs the
  crystal; the satellite needs the atom plus physics' correction.
- THE CONTROL AND THE PLACEBO: the twin as an instrument — the only
  device that reads "what would have happened anyway."
- RANDOMNESS: dice, lots, drawn numbers — the only known way to draw
  blind, and the yardstick luck is measured against.
- THE WRITTEN RECORD: the ledger of steps, the timestamp, the
  protocol stated in advance, the citation carrying provenance. What
  is not written cannot be reconciled, replicated, or inherited.
- THE SCALE OVER THE CUP: mass is truth; volume is approximation
  wearing confidence — flour by the cup swings 30% by packing
  (cooking holds the entry; this phylum holds the why).

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject answers — the doors a query enters by.

- LET STRANGERS' NUMBERS SPEAK: trade, treaties, recipes, and
  orchestras need sticks agreed before meeting; units are the
  handshake.
- SETTLE ARGUMENTS WITHOUT RANK OR VOLUME: the stick does not care
  who is louder; a measured claim can be lost by the powerful and
  won by the quiet — most of its civilizing work.
- KNOW BEFORE ACTING: the test before the medicine, the estimate
  before the design, the trial before the policy — with range limits
  and base rates carried honestly.
- CATCH DECEIT, ONE'S OWN FIRST: the machinery exists because the
  measurer's eye is the nearest crook; everyone else's tricks are
  the same tricks at a distance.
- MAKE KNOWLEDGE INHERITABLE: a quantity written with bar, unit, and
  provenance travels across generations; lore in thumbs dies with
  its holders (cooking's thermometer lesson, generalized).
- KEEP TIME TOGETHER: ships, trains, machines, and markets run on
  agreed clocks; longitude itself waited on one.
- PRICE RISK AND FAIRNESS: insurance, sentencing lines, safety
  margins, and pass marks are thresholds through overlapping crowds;
  this phylum's job is to make the choosing visible.

## HISTORY
How the knowledge arose, in what order, at what cost.

- Bodies first: cubit, foot, pace — sticks that traveled with a
  person and disagreed between persons; every market quarrel was a
  calibration failure. The want for a stranger-proof stick is as old
  as trade.
- The treaty sticks: the meter was born as one ten-millionth of the
  pole-to-equator arc — and the survey missed by ~0.2 mm. The world
  kept the BAR, not the arc: an early confession that the stick is
  the agreement, not the planet.
- The longitude clock: a ship's clock losing ~5 seconds in 81 days
  earned the £20,000 prize's money — paid grudgingly, in pieces,
  over decades — and turned "where are we" into "what time is it at
  home": measuring one thing by keeping another still.
- The pendulum's gift: clock error fell from ~15 minutes a day to
  ~15 seconds a day in one invention — the first time a borrowed
  natural regularity beat human attention.
- Errors averaged: astronomers learned to combine disagreeing
  observations instead of picking the best; squeezing the scatter
  recovered a lost asteroid from a handful of sightings, and the
  error bar became a tool instead of a shame.
- The state's counting and the gamblers' letters: "statistics" is
  named for the state's censuses; the arithmetic of chance began in
  letters about dice stakes. Governance and play jointly funded the
  science of scatter.
- The control's late arrival: a dozen scorbutic sailors split into
  pairs, one pair given citrus, made the case; farming's test plots
  and medicine's sugar pill made it method. The untouched twin took
  most of recorded history to be honored.
- The bombers: the returning planes' holes said "armor here"; the
  method said the downed planes, unseen, held the lesson.
  Survivorship entered the toolkit as a named enemy.
- The artifact fired: the platinum kilogram drifted ~50 millionths
  of a gram against its own copies, with no way to say which moved;
  in 2019 the last artifact standard was retired for a constant of
  nature. The chain now ends where nothing drifts.
- The sky fired: the atom caught the Earth's spin wobbling; the
  second moved from the day to the cesium hum (1967), and the leap
  second now patches the planet to the clock — standard and
  standardized trading places, the subject's arc in miniature.

## RELATIONS
First-class connections — the cascade's paths outward.

- MATHEMATICS: the whole error arithmetic, ratios and their traps,
  logs, compounding, the square-root law, chance itself; and the
  no-self-certification shape — why every chain ends at a treaty.
- PHYSICS: the regularities clocks borrow, the disturbance toll and
  its bottom wall (the mirror certifies it), the shipped "now."
- ASTRONOMY: the calendar's unmixable cycles, the old second, star
  brightness on the multiplying scale, the distant "now" that does
  not exist for us.
- ECONOMY: real versus nominal, the index as a weighting decision,
  profit needing its capital base, rounding theft, quarterly numbers
  shaped rather than earned — the target law's home turf.
- MEDICINE: the trial, the sugar pill, screening's base-rate
  arithmetic, dose ranges that turn, the cuff that raises what it
  reads.
- LAW: definitions with consequences, thresholds chosen out loud,
  chain of custody as provenance, timestamps, one witness versus
  corroboration.
- GOVERNANCE: the census, the state's own word in "statistics,"
  targets and their gaming, figures managed rather than earned.
- MIND: the seeker's eye the machinery exists to defeat;
  straight-line intuition beaten by compounding; confident memory as
  precision without accuracy.
- COMPUTING: counts versus readings as integers versus decimals,
  machines agreeing on time before agreeing on anything, rounding
  drift, file sizes that depend on how you count.
- ENGINEERING: parts made to tolerances never to sizes, stacked
  bars, the back-of-envelope check, the unit conversion worth a Mars
  probe.
- MUSIC: concert pitch as treaty, the metronome as a clock with a
