# CONFLICT — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/conflict/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. Seed authored by Claude from
docs/fabric_phylums/23_strategy_conflict.md and
docs/fabric_phylums/159_deep_war_and_conflict.md. What didn't hold
carries its WHY in quantities and conditions, so it recomputes when new
knowns move a condition. Nothing here is a tombstone.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot be
under stated conditions.

- THE WAR TO END WARS — attempted at the largest scale ever tried: one
  war so total the appetite for war itself dies. RECEIPTS: entered by
  both sides on short-war assumptions; run years past the point either
  wanted, on sunk cost's arithmetic; ended in a settlement harsh enough
  to enrage the loser and mild enough to leave it able; the same war
  reappeared wearing a later date, roughly a generation on, worse. WHY
  it cannot work as tried: force destroys armies, never disagreements —
  the ending removes the fighting and leaves the argument — and a huge
  war's sunk costs DEMAND harsh terms, which manufacture the
  resentment. Certified failed UNDER THE CONDITION that exhausted
  victors impose terms on a resentful, still-capable loser. Would drain
  on: a loser genuinely reconciled or genuinely unable — both exist in
  the record — made reliable rather than lucky.
- THE WONDER WEAPON THAT WINS ALONE — attempted with each new reach:
  the bomber fleet, the fleet-in-being, the precision arm. Each
  delivered its physics and failed its promise, because ground is
  governed by whoever stands on it and a lone arm is a solved problem
  (every weapon has a counter; only pairs cover each other). Would
  drain on: a weapon that can HOLD, not merely reach — none is written.
- THE IMPREGNABLE WALL — built repeatedly at ruinous cost. WHY it
  fails: a defense answers a named threat, and the threat gets renamed
  or walks around — the famous fixed line was not pierced, it was
  bypassed, the purer refutation. Conditions on the cannot: a wall with
  a flank, facing an enemy free to choose its point. Would drain on: a
  defense without a flank — the sea has sometimes been one, until it
  wasn't.
- THE PERFECTLY OBEDIENT ARMY — soldiers as instruments executing the
  script. FAILED because the script's situation is gone before the
  order arrives; the armies that beat it gave purpose and let the spot
  choose the method. Drill survived — as installed reflex under terror,
  never as a replacement for judgment. Certified: obedience cannot
  outrun events it must ask permission to answer.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is unsettled.

- "VICTORY GOES TO THE BRAVER" — killed by arithmetic, repeatedly: the
  color's production law shows a side losing every monthly exchange
  (250 lost against the enemy's 200) and still doubling past it within
  a year on output; the supply laws show courage moving the wagon's
  ~125 km radius not one kilometer. WHY believed anyway: fierceness is
  visible in the story and supply is not. Certified UNDER THE CONDITION
  of contests long enough for supply and replacement to bind (months,
  not hours); in a single afternoon at parity of means, will still
  decides — the residue the color keeps as morale.
- "THE WAR WILL BE SHORT" — the most-refuted forecast in the subject:
  each side counts its own advantages, discounts the other's, and
  assumes the first month goes to plan. WHY it recurs: the optimism is
  structural — each plan is authored by its believer — so the claim
  regrows every generation; filed as a standing bias to be priced in.
- "BOMBING WILL BREAK THEIR MORALE" — tried at scale more than once;
  measured: production rose under bombardment and the will hardened at
  least as often as it broke. It began to bind only aimed at supply —
  fuel and transport — and that correction is color. Certified failed
  UNDER THE CONDITION that the target is belief rather than logistics.
- "KILL COUNTS MEASURE WINNING" — failed wherever the enemy's victory
  condition was existence: the metric measured the wrong quantity, and
  each raid that killed a family recruited more than it removed, so the
  number rose while the war was lost. Drains only into better questions
  (who do the villages find it safer to help), never into the count.
- "HARSHER TERMS MAKE A SAFER PEACE" — refuted by the standard-recipe
  receipts: harsh enough to enrage AND mild enough to permit, on a
  loser still capable, produced the sequel. The condition matters:
  disabling harshness or genuine reconciliation alone has held; the
  middle has not.
- "THE INTELLIGENCE FAILED FOR LACK OF INFORMATION" — autopsies keep
  finding the warning already in the files, buried, contradicted, or
  refused for not fitting the standing belief. The failure is in the
  reading; buying more collection for a reading problem is the
  certified wrong repair.
- UNSETTLED: WHERE THE COLLAPSE THRESHOLD SITS — units switch off at a
  casualty fraction well under half, but nothing written predicts the
  fraction in advance. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: the
  threshold is a property of the small group's expectation web, not the
  count, so it should track replacements-per-veteran and
  days-without-rest better than percentage lost. Would drain on:
  records scored against both predictors.
- UNSETTLED: WHETHER ANYTHING RELIABLY PREVENTS WAR — trade bonds,
  shared institutions, democratic caution, deterrence: each grips in
  some records and fails in others. The subject's largest open claim,
  filed in full under SCIENCE.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied science
broke.

- LACKING: A RELIABLE SCIENCE OF PREVENTING WAR — the open want above
  all others. The color can price a war once chosen (matrix, supply,
  production, deterrence) and cannot compute the conditions under which
  the choosing itself reliably stops. Nothing certain — but here is a
  possibility, assembled from what grips: every mechanism that ever
  held moved a payoff in the matrix — inspection denying the lone
  builder's lead (the color's worked flip from −2/−2 to 0/0), assured
  answer removing the first blow's prize, trade making the quiet cell
  pay more than the lead is worth. If the science comes, it looks like
  verified payoff engineering, not persuasion. Would drain on:
  engineered payoffs holding through a real crisis where pride and sunk
  cost push the other way — tests so far are few and confounded.
- LACKING: A PREDICTIVE THEORY OF MORALE — belief is what breaks, and
  it breaks at a threshold; no written law computes the threshold in
  advance. The subject forecasts material honestly; about will it is
  still lore.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: MEASUREMENT IN WARTIME — imported counting broke
  on interested definitions (who is a combatant, whether hunger counts,
  which period). Every figure reported during a war is a move in it; a
  science built on such numbers inherits the player's aim.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: FORECASTING BY STRAIGHT LINES — the present
  stretched: the short-war claim, the army perfectly fitted to the war
  that is over. The breakage condition is exact: the opponent is a
  learner, so any curve fitted to his past is invalidated by his
  reading of it. The subject eats its own forecasts.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- VICTORY BY FIERCENESS ALONE — the parent entry of this page: spend
  courage where the bind is supply. Killed by the logistics arithmetic
  repeatedly: the dead radius (delivered = load − consumption ×
  round-trip; zero at 250 km for the worked wagon) caps reach whatever
  the heart says; the trips-per-day law quarters a fleet over 120
  successful kilometers; the production crossing hands the long war to
  the worse fighter with the better factories. Binds whenever the
  contest outlasts the first rush's stores. Would drain on: a fight
  genuinely decided inside that rush — the narrow residue the color
  keeps.
- PUNISHING THE POPULATION TO BREAK THE WILL — blockade and bombardment
  aimed at belief: scarcity rationed by power reaches the weakest first
  and the army last, and the will hardened as often as it broke.
  Certified UNDER THE CONDITION that the ruler is insulated from the
  suffering — the usual condition. Would drain on: a public that can
  and does move the ruler faster than the army starves; rare, not
  unwritten.
- KILLING PRISONERS TO TERRORIZE — backfires by its own arithmetic:
  surrender is a group decision resting on whether it looks survivable,
  so a reputation for killing the yielded makes every remaining enemy
  fight to the last. Certified under all conditions where word travels
  — and word travels.
- PAYING FOR PRESENCE (THE MERCENARY CONTRACT) — buying soldiers by the
  day buys days: the hired side's interest is to be paid and survive,
  neither requiring victory; the record holds avoided battles, extorted
  employers, switched sides, and wars prolonged because peace ends the
  contract. WHY: an agent acts on its incentives, and the contract
  cannot specify what is really wanted. Would drain on: payment truly
  tied to outcome — attempted forever, still leaking.
- BUILDING THE WALL AGAINST THE LAST WEAPON — stone against ladders met
  cannon; earth against cannon met bypass; the fixed line met an enemy
  who declined to visit. The method fails by timing, not engineering.
  Would drain on: a defense adapting faster than the threat is
  redesigned — a race, not a wall.
- THE KNOCKOUT PLAN WITH NO SECOND ACT — the scripted blow that needs
  surprise twice: certified against the color's law that surprise is
  spent in the using; the plan's early success teaches the enemy its
  shape. Condition: any plan whose later stages require the enemy not
  to have learned from its earlier ones.
- OCCUPATION ON THE CHEAP — taking the country and skipping the
  feeding, policing and courts: ruling must then be supplied without
  legitimacy, the one thing that makes it affordable; costs recur, the
  benefit does not, and every incident anywhere is the occupier's
  fault. Certified UNDER THE CONDITION of a population that did not
  invite the rule — the usual condition.
- FIGHTING THE GUERRILLA WITH THE ARMY'S SCORE — search-and-destroy
  scored by kill counts: the wrong quantity measured, the raids
  recruiting faster than they removed. The drained road is in the
  color: the population as the board, protection as the move.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE TREATY WITHOUT A CHECK — a promise with no way to be verified
  fell short exactly as the matrix said: the lone builder's lead stays
  winnable, so building stays dominant and the paper holds only what
  the payoffs already held. DRAINED IN PART, as the shimmer predicts:
  add inspection and the worked matrix flips its stable cell — the
  drained form lives in the color's MEANS as the word made checkable.
- THE ARMY TOO LARGE TO NEED CALLING UP — fell short on cost: keeping
  the full force standing is ruinous, so states keep a list, a store
  and a timetable — and bought a new danger, because running the
  timetable is visible and hard to reverse, turning crises into races.
  The compromise stands; readiness without the ratchet is open.
- A METRIC OF WINNING THAT CANNOT BE GAMED — not yet found: every
  scoreboard tried (ground, kills, sorties, quiet days) has been played
  to while the war was lost behind it. Would drain on: a measure tied
  to the actual object — the other side's decision — which so far only
  the ending reveals.
- A READER IMMUNE TO ITS OWN BELIEF — not yet found: the files keep
  containing the warning and the shared picture keeps filtering it out;
  organizations filter harder than any single person. Would drain on: a
  reading practice that scores its filters against withheld surprises
  as routinely as armies drill.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- PREVENT WAR RELIABLY — blocked on the lacking science above; what
  grips is payoff engineering, and the crisis-proof test is unpassed.
- END A WAR AS CHEAPLY AS IT STARTED — starting takes one decision,
  ending two, and paid costs harden both; no written method dissolves
  sunk cost at scale. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility:
  settlements designed BEFORE the costs mount, as planning walks
  terrain in advance, might stay acceptable while the original terms
  still are; untested at scale.
- PUNISH THE LOSER AND KEEP THE PEACE, BOTH — wanted after every war;
  structurally self-opposed under the certified condition (resentment
  plus remaining capability). The want stands; the pair does not.
- SPARE THE PEOPLE WHO NEVER FIGHT — most of the dead are not soldiers,
  and they die of second-order breakage for years after; every promise
  to confine war to armies has leaked. Open, and the bill is the
  largest number in the subject.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side.

- The war to end wars, the fullest receipt in the file: begun on two
  short-war claims, run past both sides' wish to stop on sunk cost,
  ended on the standard recipe, and refuted its own name within a
  generation. Every major entry on this page has a line item in that
  one ledger — which is why it anchors the page.
- Fierceness against arithmetic, the recurring tuition: each romantic
  generation re-tried valor against supply and re-paid in full — the
  offensive spirit against the fragment, the cavalry dash against the
  railhead, the garrison's honor against the larder. The color's supply
  laws are written in this ink; that is why they carry worked numbers
  and not adjectives.
- The walls' graveyard: stored defense meeting a renamed threat, paid
  at each age's costliest construction. Every wall was right about the
  last war; the sequence proves the error is in the timing.
- Disease's uncounted majority: the camp outkilled the enemy for most
  of the record, and the knowledge arrived from public health, outside
  the subject — the reminder that a phylum's biggest killer can be
  invisible to its own doctrine for centuries.
- The learners' trap: the armies that learned their last war best met
  the next one worst. Kept as the standing cost of learning itself, not
  of failing to learn.
