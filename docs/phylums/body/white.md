# THE BODY — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/body/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting
for its drain. Seed authored by Claude and marked as seed.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- SPONTANEOUS HUMAN COMBUSTION — never held. The arithmetic: a
  70 kg body is ~42 L of water; boiling that off alone costs
  42,000 g × 2,257 J ≈ 95 MJ, plus ~11 MJ to heat it from 37 to
  100 first. The fuel exists — 12 kg of body fat holds ~450 MJ —
  but living tissue stores no oxidizer, sits hundreds of degrees
  below ignition, and is water-quenched throughout: no internal
  path from 37°C to flame is written anywhere in chemistry. The
  found scenes reconcile WITHOUT the spontaneous step: external
  flame, fat as candle-wax, clothing as wick, hours of burning —
  exactly the ash patterns show. Would drain on: a body chemistry
  storing an oxidizer — none exists in any living thing on record.
- BLACK BILE — one of the four claimed humours; the other three
  named real fluids, this one was never once collected. A thing
  required by a theory rather than found in a body — the type
  specimen of that whole class of thing. Would drain on: the
  fluid itself, collected and shown — and after centuries of
  opened bodies, no candidate answers the description.
- THE VITAL FORCE — a special substance separating living matter
  from dead. Killed twice by measurement: a living chemical made
  from dead ones (urea, 1828), and the calorimeter's books
  balancing with no vital line item. The want it served — what
  makes living different — moved to an organization question.
  Would drain on: one living process whose energy books refuse
  to balance — two centuries of measuring have found none.
- THE MEMORY WAREHOUSE — a single organ holding memories as
  stored objects. Searched for; not found: damage anywhere
  degrades recall somewhat, nowhere deletes one memory cleanly,
  and remembering measurably re-edits what it recalls —
  re-assembly on demand, not a shelf. Would drain on: one memory
  located, read, and deleted at an address.
- THE SOUL'S ORGAN — an anatomical address for the self; every
  candidate (the heart, the pineal) turned out to have a mundane
  job. Not settled-no: the self plainly runs ON the body; WHERE
  has simply failed as a question shape. The live remainder is
  filed under SCIENCE.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "WE USE ONLY 10% OF THE BRAIN" — false by measurement: imaging
  shows nearly all of it active across a day, damage anywhere
  costs something, and the organ eats ~20% of the fuel — no
  budget that tight carries 90% idle. Believed because it
  flatters.
- "ULCERS COME FROM STRESS AND SPICE" — held for decades, false:
  a microbe survives the stomach's acid and farms the wall; a
  researcher drank the culture, sickened, and cured himself with
  antibiotics (Nobel, 2005). "Nothing lives at pH 2" was the
  wrong condition.
- "SUGAR MAKES CHILDREN WILD" — blinded tests: told-sugar parents
  saw wildness whether sugar was real or not; the children's
  measures didn't move. The dial that moved was the observer.
- "COLD AND WET GIVES YOU THE COLD" — chilling alone infects no
  one; a virus is required. Honest remainder: a chilled nose
  measurably weakens its local defense, and winter crowds people
  indoors — the season is real, the mechanism was misassigned.
- "CRACKING KNUCKLES BRINGS ARTHRITIS" — one doctor cracked one
  hand only for 50 years: no difference. The pop is a gas bubble
  in the joint fluid, not damage arriving.
- "WE HAVE EXACTLY FIVE SENSES" — an undercount: balance,
  body-position, heat, pain, hunger, thirst all report on
  separate wiring. The five-item list is culture's, not the
  body's.
- UNSETTLED: WHY WE YAWN — contagious across species, present in
  the womb (so not social alone), rises with warm brains and
  fades with cooled ones. Nothing certain — but here is a
  possibility: a brain-temperature and state-change reset LATER
  recruited as a group signal, which is why both accounts keep
  half-winning. Would drain on: a predictor of when a yawn comes
  that beats both single accounts.
- UNSETTLED: WHY PLACEBO MOVES REAL DIALS — belief that a
  treatment was given measurably moves pain, nausea, tremor; the
  pain relief is blocked by the same drug that blocks opium, so
  expectation reaches a real internal pharmacy. NOT shown:
  placebo shrinking tumors or clearing infections — it moves the
  dials the brain itself runs. Would drain on: the chain from
  expectation to pharmacy mapped and tested dial by dial.
- UNSETTLED: WHY WE SLEEP AT ALL — deprivation kills (weeks, in
  animals); the brain's rinse cycle and memory filing both
  demonstrably run in sleep, yet neither explains why death
  follows from none. Possibility: several maintenance jobs share
  one off-line window, so no single job IS the reason. Would
  drain on: the jobs separated and the lethal one named.
- UNSETTLED: WHY WE AGE AT THE RATE WE DO — wear-accumulating
  and program-running accounts each grip partly; other mammals
  run the same chemistry at tenfold different speeds, so the
  rate is SET somewhere, not merely suffered. Would drain on: a
  dial moved that reproducibly stretches a mammal's span.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A THEORY OF EXPERIENCE — how charged salt-gate signals
  become the taste of the apple rather than a report about it.
  The color traces every wire and names no point where feeling
  starts. The subject's largest absence; even the right
  question-shape is unsettled.
- LACKING: THE GENOME-TO-BODY COMPUTATION — the parts list is
  read end to end, but the body cannot be computed from it:
  sequence-to-folded-protein is barely won; protein-crowd-to-
  working-organ is not. Reading done; understanding absent.
- LACKING: AN ARITHMETIC OF PAIN — no thermometer exists;
  medicine steers one of its largest territories by asking "zero
  to ten?" An instrument-shaped hole, filed again under MEANS.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE HUMOURAL MODEL — 2,000 years of
  draining and purging, applied confidently to everything; broke
  on counting, not argument (HISTORY holds the ledger). Lesson:
  a model that explains every outcome forbids none, and what
  forbids nothing cannot be tested.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE CALORIE AS EXACT BOOKKEEPING — energy
  conservation holds absolutely, but the label's number is a
  furnace's yield, not a body's: cooking, food structure, and
  the gut's microbe workforce move actual absorption by real
  margins. The law is color; the claim that label arithmetic
  settles weight to the digit broke, by measured gaps.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
wings-on-arms entries, waiting for their planet.

- BLOODLETTING — the subject's biggest failed method: applied to
  fevers, wounds, madness for 2,000 years; counting found it
  killing the treated. AND THE SHIMMER, exactly as the model
  promises: under two narrow conditions found later — blood
  overloaded with iron, blood overcrowded with its own cells —
  removing blood IS the treatment, practiced today. Wrong as a
  general law, right under conditions nobody had named: the type
  specimen of the drained white entry.
- REGROWING A LOST LIMB — the standing want. The human wound
  program chooses scar: fast, infection-proof, cheap — and
  final. The newt holds the receipt that vertebrate flesh CAN
  re-run the building program; our species holds a small one (a
  young child's fingertip, lost beyond the last joint, regrows
  if the wound is left unclosed). The machinery is not absent — the
  re-entry switch is. Would drain on: the newt's switch found
  and thrown safely in a scar-choosing body.
- SUSPENDED ANIMATION ON DEMAND — the speed law cooperates (cold
  surgery already stops a chilled heart for tens of minutes, on
  purpose), but humans lack the hibernator's controlled descent:
  our thermostat fights the cooling, then fails rather than
  yields. The bear and the squirrel hold the receipt that a
  mammal can throttle to near-idle safely. Would drain on: their
  descent switch, found and made safe.
- CURING FEVER BY FORCE OF COLD — fighting the thermostat
  instead of resetting it: the cold bath at the climb's hour
  triggers violent shivering that burns fuel to RE-CLIMB.
  Drained correctly by resetting the set-point (what fever
  medicine does) — the cleanest proof that in this subject you
  treat the controller, not the reading.
- TRANSPLANTING WITHOUT SUPPRESSION — the army cannot yet be
  told "this stranger is home"; grafts hold only while the
  defense is drugged down, and the drugged body pays in
  infections. Would drain on: a new entry taught to the memory
  files instead of blinding the whole army.
- STRENGTH FROM THE OUTSIDE IN — corsets for posture, long bed
  rest for bad hearts: each removed load from tissue whose
  budget is set BY load, and the tissue obediently melted (~a
  few percent a week). Failed under the condition "support
  replaces use"; drained by its reversal — measured loading as
  the treatment — now standard.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- BLOOD SUBSTITUTES — tried repeatedly (freed hemoglobin,
  oxygen-carrying liquids): oxygen carried, harm delivered
  alongside; trials stopped. The want — transfusion without
  donors or refrigeration — stands. Would drain on: a carrier
  that loads and unloads like the cell-packed original.
- THE ARTIFICIAL HEART AS DESTINATION — pumps exist and work as
  bridges; clotting and wear keep every one inferior to the
  ~1-watt thirty-year original. The color's pump numbers are the
  specification the imitations still miss.
- A PAINKILLER WITHOUT THE TOLL — opium's family, re-announced
  as finally-safe once a generation; each time the tolerance and
  craving arrived on schedule. The pattern ITSELF is this
  entry's knowledge: "new" and "safe" are separate claims, and
  the second is only ever measured, never announced. Would drain
  on: relief split from the reward circuit — gripped, not held.
- NOT YET FOUND: AN INSTRUMENT FOR PAIN — the subject steers by
  self-report. Would drain on: a reading that tracks felt pain
  across people without asking.
- NOT YET FOUND: READING THE BODY WITHOUT WOUNDING IT — partly
  drained by imaging (bones, then soft tissue, then blood flow
  as a proxy), but the signal itself — a thought, a memory, a
  small tumor's first chemistry — still hides below the
  instruments' floor.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- LIVE PAST THE WARRANTY IN GOOD ORDER — the span stretches only
  by removing early deaths, never yet by slowing the clock;
  blocked on the aging unknown above.
- REGROW WHAT IS LOST — the limb, the tooth (the shark regrows
  forever; we get two sets and a bill), the worn joint; blocked
  on the re-entry switch above.
- TUNE THE DEFENSE — an army strong against invaders that never
  turns on home; the autoimmune diseases are this want stated as
  a price currently paid.
- KNOW THE OTHER'S PAIN — the instrument-shaped hole; every
  bedside guess is this unanswered want at work.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- The humours' 2,000-year ledger — the longest-held wrong model
  in the subject: bleeding, purging, starving applied with full
  confidence. WHY it held: it explained every outcome (recovery
  proved it, death proved the imbalance too great) and so
  forbade nothing. A dying president drained of ~half his blood
  in a day (1799) is the best-documented line. Killed by
  counting, ~1830s — not by a better argument.
- Galen's 1,300-year photocopy — animal anatomy applied to
  humans and recopied by generations disinclined to check;
  Vesalius's knife (1543) found the errors in an afternoon of
  looking. The cost of authority outranking the checkable.
- Semmelweis's counted, ignored answer — childbed fever: ~10% of
  mothers dying in the doctors' ward against ~4% with the
  midwives; hand-washing dropped it near 1%; the counts were
  mocked for a generation because they arrived before the
  mechanism. Filed twice: arithmetic can be right early — and
  holding a right count without its why is a position this
  subject had to learn.
- The vital force's quiet exit — no duel, just books that
  balanced without it; the cleanest case of a thing removed by
  bookkeeping.
- The new-equals-safe receipts — radium tonics sold as vigor,
  the lobotomy's Nobel, thalidomide's children: three eras, one
  lesson, already stated in MEANS: safety is measured in, never
  announced in.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN: BODY ↔ THE SKY — bleeding calendars set by zodiac
  signs governed treatment for centuries; counted, the sky
  carried nothing. Kept as this subject's proof that a relation
  must transmit something measurable to be a relation.
- BROKEN, REBUILT WITH A SPEED LIMIT: BODY ↔ NUTRITION — advice
  whipsawed for decades (fat condemned, then sugar, then
  reversals); the standing link carries the rule the cooking
  phylum filed from its side: adopt claims at the pace they stop
  reversing. One lesson, two mirrors, each in its own terms.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: GUT TENANTS ↔ MOOD — the microbe
  workforce signals the brain along real wiring and moves mouse
  behavior measurably; the human claim is dotted. Would firm on:
  a microbe intervention moving a human mind-dial, blinded.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: SCARCITY ↔ LONGER SPAN — eating less
  stretches life in worms, flies, and mice; the human receipt is
  absent (the trial is a lifetime long, and volunteers are
  hungry). Stands dotted until a marker short of a lifetime is
  agreed and moved.
