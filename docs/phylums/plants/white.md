# PLANTS — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/plants/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone. Seed authored by Claude from
docs/fabric_phylums/117_deep_plants_and_fungi.md.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE TALKING TREE OF THE STORIES — trees that warn, nurse,
  remember, converse. Held against the receipts, it splits clean.
  REAL: bitten leaves raise toxins in hours; untouched leaves on
  the same plant follow; in several species neighbors downwind
  pick up the drifting compounds and arm early; labeled carbon
  does move tree to tree through shared fungal threads. STRETCH:
  intent, address, and care. The drift is a leak, not a broadcast
  — no one has shown the SENDER profits, and eavesdropping
  explains every receipt; the "mother tree nursing her young
  through the web" version was re-audited and the measured flows
  are small, inconsistent, and often explainable without the
  fungal channel at all. Would drain on: field trials with mesh
  that passes molecules but not threads showing a survival
  difference at forest scale — and, for "talking," any showing
  that emitting pays the emitter. Until then: a real whisper, a
  fictional speech.
- A CEREAL THAT FIXES ITS OWN NITROGEN — the biggest open want on
  the sheet. The prize: wheat, rice, and maize paying their own
  nitrogen bill would retire much of a factory system eating
  ~1-2% of world energy. The exact gap, three parts: (1) the
  enzyme dies in oxygen and a working leaf is an oxygen fountain
  — legumes solved this with purpose-built housing and an
  oxygen-caging protein, which cereals entirely lack; (2) the
  bill is ~16 ATP per nitrogen molecule, ~6-12 g of sugar-carbon
  per gram of nitrogen — a yield tax the crop must survive
  commercially; (3) every route tried (the enzyme moved into
  low-oxygen compartments, cereals taught to nodulate, microbe
  seed-coatings) has given trace amounts or nothing at field
  scale. Would drain on: field-scale grain fixing a meaningful
  fraction of its nitrogen at a survivable yield cost. Nothing
  certain — but the nearest possibility is the partnership route:
  evolution already built that bridge ~ten separate times for
  other plant families.
- THE TREE THAT GROWS TO THE SKY — CANNOT under Earth's
  conditions: the color's arithmetic caps the hanging column at
  ~125-130 m (~2.5 MPa of tension at the top; past it, cells
  starve for pressure and bubbles snap the pipes). The shimmer,
  recomputed honestly: at a third of Earth's gravity the hanging
  cost drops to a third and the ceiling roughly triples, other
  limits permitting — wings-on-arms, filed with its numbers.
- PHOTOSYNTHESIS WITHOUT THE WATER PRICE — CANNOT while the pore
  law stands: the same hole passes both gases, so the price is
  structural. The color's two workarounds already claim the big
  cuts — halving, never abolishing. A dry-air photosynthesis with
  no open pore is written nowhere in the kingdom.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "PLANTS FEEL AND CHOOSE AS WE DO" — the stretch half of the
  talking tree. What holds moved to the color: summoned defenses,
  real electrical signals (a flytrap counts two touches before
  closing). What failed: "plant neurons" (no such tissue found);
  music improving growth (fails controlled tests — plants answer
  light, water, and wind, not the playlist); plant "memory"
  beyond the chill bank and simple habituation, whose flagship
  results have mixed replications. Would drain on: a replicated
  learning effect no clock or threshold explains.
- "THE VISIBLE MOLD IS THE WHOLE MOLD" — false on soft food: the
  fuzz is the fruiting stage of a net already grown through the
  loaf; cutting it removes the flag, not the army. Holds ONLY
  where threads cannot penetrate — hard cheese — and that
  boundary is the whole truth of the kitchen rule.
- "NATURAL MEANS SAFE" — fails on the color's own shelf: spice,
  medicine, and poison are one chemistry sorted by dose and
  target; none of it was made for us.
- "COAL PILED UP BECAUSE NOTHING COULD EAT WOOD YET" — the clean
  story, now contested: the digesters may predate the tale's
  need, and wet fast-sinking basins may explain the coal without
  any stalemate. Both grips are real; the color carries the
  stalemate with this audit attached. Would settle on: dated
  fungal fossils with a working lignin apparatus, placed before
  or after the coal peak.
- UNSETTLED: WHY FLOWERS BEFORE BEES — the famous timing question
  (an "abominable mystery" to the man who first asked): how does
  an advertisement evolve before its readers? PARTLY DRAINED:
  insects predate flowers by a long way, and the first flowers
  hired existing customers — beetles and flies already visiting
  seed plants for pollen and sweet droplets. No paradox: the
  readers were walking the street already. STILL WHITE: why
  flowers came so late and then took over so fast — the record
  shows a burst the fossil trail has not explained. Would drain
  on: earlier transitional fossils, or a mechanism for the speed.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT TRIGGERS A MAST YEAR — the synchronized flood
  is real (the color holds its arithmetic); the trigger is not:
  weather cues grip partly, shared pollination economics grip,
  stored reserves grip. Nothing certain — but here is a
  possibility: the AND of a weather cue with a filled reserve
  account, which would explain both the synchrony (shared
  weather) and the rarity (the AND).
- UNSETTLED: HOW A SNAPPED PIPE REFILLS — trees demonstrably
  recover broken columns, sometimes overnight, while neighboring
  pipes still hang at negative pressure; pushing water into a
  pipe embedded in tension has no settled mechanism. Possibility:
  local sugar-driven pumping at the pipe wall, isolated from the
  tension by valve pits — observed in pieces, composed nowhere.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A COMPOSABLE ACCOUNT FROM GENOME TO PLANT — breeding
  still runs on trying: nothing computes a variety's shape, chill
  number, or disease stance from its text. The color's clocks are
  read from outside; the counter itself is mapped in one weed's
  flowering delay and generalizes to little else yet. Would drain
  on: a variety's chill number or flowering week predicted from
  its text before the plant is ever grown.
- LACKING: THE SENDER'S LEDGER IN AIRBORNE SIGNALING — the
  drift's chemistry is cataloged; whether emitting ever pays the
  emitter is unmeasured, and on that one number hangs the whole
  difference between a language and a leak.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE MARKET METAPHOR PUSHED PAST ITS
  RECEIPTS — price-responsive trade is real in lab systems;
  "forest as cooperative economy" ran ahead of field evidence and
  was walked back. Lesson filed: a metaphor is a loan against
  future receipts, and this one was overdrawn.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: SINGLE-GENE RESISTANCE AS A PERMANENT FIX —
  deployed as a purchase, performed as a lease (~3-7 years
  against big fast-breeding populations); the arithmetic
  predicting the break was known and planted anyway, repeatedly.
  The color now carries stacking as the rule those failures
  bought.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- PAINTING TREE WOUNDS — a century of tar and dressings sealed
  moisture and rot IN against the tree's own walls; measured
  comparisons found painted wounds fare the same or worse.
  Drained to the color's wound care: cut at the collar, trust the
  compartment. The standing case of helping an organism by
  overriding the mechanism that already was the help.
- KILLING THE FUNGUS BY REMOVING MUSHROOMS — removes the apple,
  leaves the tree: the organism is the net in the substrate.
  Works never while the meal stays wet; the only drain is the
  color's — remove the moisture, remove the meal.
- SPRAYING THE VIRUS — aimed at nothing: a virus in a plant has
  no exposed life to poison; the courier does. Drained to vector
  control; kept because the instinct (attack the disease you can
  name) returns every season.
- STERILIZING SOIL FOR PLANT HEALTH — kills the market with the
  thieves: a brief clean-ground gain, then stunted crops missing
  their partners' reach. Conditional, not absolute: sterile media
  DO work where the grower pays the full mineral bill by hand —
  the failure is expecting free trade from an emptied
  marketplace.
- PLANTING BY THE MOON — no effect survives controls for season,
  soil, and rain. Filed with its why: the real clocks (day
  length, chill, damp) are strong enough that any calendar
  loosely tracking spring seems to work.
- THE CLONAL MONOCULTURE, RUN TWICE — one variety everywhere is
  one plant repeated: the potato famine wrote it in lives; the
  world's banana was then lost to one wilt (mid-1900s) and
  REPLACED WITH ANOTHER SINGLE CLONE, now cornered by the wilt's
  successor. A failure filed, read, and knowingly re-run — the
  clearest case that filing is not heeding.
- OVER-FEEDING NITROGEN — past the response curve's bend the
  surplus buys lodged stems, delayed ripening, and fouled water
  downstream; a fix that becomes a harm at the dose where the
  curve goes flat.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- A FUNGICIDE THAT SPARES THE PARTNERS — chemistry cannot yet
  tell the market-makers from the thieves; broad kills degrade
  the trade that feeds the crop. Would drain on: a compound or
  delivery keyed to the pathogens' side of the kingdom.
- AN INSTRUMENT THAT READS THE COLUMN WITHOUT WOUNDING — the
  standing tension gauge cuts the leaf it measures; flow gauges
  infer. The want: the tree's water ledger, read live, intact, at
  orchard prices. Would drain on: an uncut reading that matches
  the cutting gauge on the same branch, at a price a grower pays.
- THE PERENNIAL GRAIN — wants the perennial's roots and the
  annual's seed spend from one budget the color says pulls
  opposite ways. Real progress exists (a perennial wheatgrass
  crop is on sale) at roughly a quarter of wheat's yield (~) —
  the gap is the budget conflict in one number. Would drain on:
  breeding that shifts the trade instead of choosing a point on
  it.
- A SEED THAT WAITS FOREVER — the bank's spring runs down: most
  stored seed dies within decades even dry and cold; the
  ~2,000-year palm is the outlier, and why some lineages last is
  unwritten. The vaults bet on refreshing, not permanence.
  Possibility: the outliers dry to a glassy interior where
  chemistry nearly stops — measured in pieces, never across
  lineages; that comparison is what would settle it.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- RAISE THE PAYROLL'S RATE — fields run at ~half a percent
  against a ~5-6% ceiling, and the known waster is named: the
  capturing enzyme grabs oxygen by mistake roughly one try in
  four in warm weather, taxing ~20-30% of earnings. Engineered
  bypasses have shown ~+20-40% growth in trial plants (~), not
  yet in bread at scale. The fix already exists in maize's
  chemistry; moving it into rice and wheat is the open build.
- FEED THE WORLD WITHOUT THE FACTORY'S BILL — blocked on the
  nitrogen-fixing cereal above; until it drains, bread carries a
  fuel surcharge.
- FORECAST THE OUTBREAK A SEASON OUT — the disease AND is known,
  but its third leg is weather, so this forecast can never outrun
  weather's own horizon; the want waits on another phylum's
  ceiling.
- MAKE A FOREST FAST — time is the one unpurchasable input: the
  scaffolding kingdom compounds slowly and no method condenses
  centuries. Plantations buy wood, not woods; the difference is
  everything the color files under soil, market, and archive.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side.

- Domestication's uncounted testers: every safe crop from a
  poisonous wild ancestor was paid for in poisonings nobody wrote
  down; the detox steps in cooking's lore are the survivors'
  notes. The color's pantry stands on this page's dead.
- The talking-tree arc, complete in living memory: ridiculed,
  then proven small (the induced-defense receipts), then inflated
  (the web-of-care decade), then audited back down — a full
  shimmer cycle, filed as the model case of a true thing damaged
  by its enlargement.
- The famine ledger: monoculture's arithmetic was PAID before it
  was written — ~a million dead, ~a million fled — and the banana
  then ran the experiment again. Twice filed, once heeded.
- The wound-paint century: a harm sold as care for a hundred
  years because sealing LOOKS like helping; measured comparison,
  not theory, retired it.
- The resistance treadmill: each broken lease dated and archived
  by the breeders themselves — a white ledger kept ON PURPOSE by
  its own victims, because the next lease is priced off the
  record of the last.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- STORY ↔ PLANTS — the talking tree: real cargo moves both ways
  (the tales made the receipts famous; the receipts made the
  tales credible), but the story's version claims intent the
  receipts don't carry. The road stands, with the split posted on
  it.
- MIND ↔ PLANTS — "plant intelligence": suspected by some,
  disputed by most; every documented behavior so far reduces to
  clocks, thresholds, and chemistry without a nervous system.
  Dotted until the learning claim above drains either way.
- ECONOMY ↔ THE UNDERGROUND MARKET — first-class in the color for
  the audited trades; dotted HERE for everything past them:
  prices, strategy, and cooperation at forest scale are loans
  against receipts not yet collected.
- WEATHER ↔ MASTING — the suspected cue, unproven as the trigger;
  goes first-class the day the trigger is named.
- CHEMISTRY ↔ PERSISTENCE — lignin's no-repeat armor and the
  persistent plastics look like one law in two coats; suspected,
  useful, not yet written as a single statement with numbers on
  both sides.
