# CULTURE — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/culture/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting
for its drain. Seed authored by Claude and marked as seed.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE UNIVERSAL LANGUAGE — attempted a hundred times, most fully
  as Esperanto. The grammar worked; the adoption failed: a
  language lives as the mother tongue of a community, and no
  invented tongue has crossed the raised-in-it threshold (native
  speakers of the best attempt: ~a few thousand against a
  planet). WHY: a language's value is who ALREADY speaks it — a
  cold-start against every living tongue's compounding. Would
  drain on: a community large enough to make the compounding
  self-feeding; none is written.
- THE ETERNAL ARCHIVE MEDIUM — wanted since clay: durable AND
  capacious AND readable without its own machine. CANNOT BE so
  far: the color's media table shows lifespan and capacity
  running opposite down the whole record, and the densest media
  add the killer condition — readability dies with the reader
  device on a ~10-year format clock. Drained PARTIALLY by
  strategy instead of material: many copies, many places, many
  materials. A single-medium answer is still white.
- THE COMPLETE CHRONICLE — a record of what actually happened.
  Cannot under standing conditions: written by the literate few,
  surviving by chance of fire and budget, while most human days
  left no mark. Drained partially and steadily by the ground's
  audit (middens, layers, isotopes, old DNA testify unedited) —
  the drain is real and the gap stays vast.
- A PREDICTIVE SCIENCE OF HISTORY — attempted in every century,
  lately with real arithmetic (counted cycles, more trained
  claimants than seats to put them in).
  Grips backward on the record; the forward record is thin.
  Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: the parts of
  history that run on compounding and thresholds (the color's
  spread and surplus laws) may forecast the way weather does —
  bands, not events. Would drain on: dated forecasts published
  first and scored after.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "TECHNOLOGY NEVER GOES BACKWARD" — falsified three ways.
  Tasmania: a few thousand people, cut off ~10,000 years ago by
  rising seas, LOST arts they had held — bone tools and fishing
  fade from the middens — the holder law run against too few
  masters for too long (one holder's ten-generation odds: 0.9^10
  ≈ 35%, and ten thousand years is some four hundred
  generations). Roman concrete: standing
  today, unmakeable for ~1,300 years. Antikythera: geared
  computation ~2,100 years old, unmatched for ~1,400. WHY,
  common to all: knowledge is holders plus copies; drop either
  below threshold and the art goes, whatever the artifacts say.
- "THE VICTORS' CHRONICLE IS THE RECORD" — held while chronicles
  were the only instrument; broken when the ground learned to
  testify. The standing form is in the color: the midden audits
  the scribe.
- "EACH NEW MEDIUM WILL MAKE EVERYONE WISE" — claimed at print,
  radio, television, the network; failed on schedule each time.
  WHY: the spread law moves whatever copies well, and a rumor is
  lighter than a proof — it carries no conditions. Reach was
  delivered; judgment was not. Would drain on: a medium that
  prices provenance into spread; none is written.
- "THE YOUNG ARE RUINING THE LANGUAGE" — on file since antiquity
  (the complaints survive), false every time: each "ruined"
  generation's speech became the next standard; the drift clock
  measures change without decay. Kept because queries still
  arrive carrying it.
- "A GOLDEN AGE CAN BE RESTORED BY DECREE" — tried by empire
  after empire, receipts negative across the board. WHY in
  conditions: the admired age was a FLOW — trade, surplus,
  mixing, patronage — and decree copies the form without the
  flow. Form without flow yields museum pieces. The method entry
  below carries the drain.
- UNSETTLED: WHY PRACTICES PERSIST AGAINST ALL EVIDENCE — rites,
  remedies, and rules outliving every measurement against them.
  Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: the practice's
  real job is not its stated job; it badges belonging, proves
  costly loyalty, or anchors coordination, and evidence against
  the stated job never touches the real one. Would drain on:
  cases where the stated job was removed and persistence
  measured — if the practice stands, the hidden job is nameable.
- UNSETTLED: WHY SOME IDEAS SPREAD AND OTHERS DON'T — the spread
  law prices the compounding but not the conversion: no written
  predictor beats chance on which new idea catches. Would drain
  on: a predictor scored on ideas not yet released, never fitted
  to hits already known.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A MEASURE OF MEANING — translation loss is real,
  universally attested, and unquantified; without a unit for
  meaning there is no arithmetic of how much a rendering kept.
  This absence keeps the translation cannot (below) standing
  rather than priced. Would drain on: a score that rates a
  rendering against its original and agrees with human judges
  on texts it was never fitted to; none is written.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE CONSTANT-RATE DRIFT CLOCK — the
  color's language clock assumed one speed; measured speeds vary
  with contact and isolation (island tongues crawl, crossroads
  tongues sprint), and dates computed from the constant missed
  partings history already dates. The clock survives as a band;
  the constant is filed here. Would drain on: rate models
  conditioned on contact.
- LACKING: A PREDICTIVE THEORY OF SPREAD — the S-curve fits
  every adoption after the fact and picks none before it; the
  conversion term is the missing science. Would drain on: the
  same bar the unsettled claim sets — a conversion term written
  before an idea's release and scored after it.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: HISTORY AS SIMPLE PROGRESS — the ladder
  story failed against Tasmania, lost concrete, and every
  collapse in the ledger; the replacement that holds is the
  holder-and-copy arithmetic, which permits descent and predicts
  where.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
wings-on-arms entries, waiting for their planet.

- RESTORING THE GOLDEN AGE BY DECREE — classicizing revivals,
  purity laws, mandated canons: fails as restoration under the
  stated condition (form copied, flow absent). The conditional
  drain, honestly written: where the decree rebuilt the FLOW —
  funded the trade, paid the patronage, opened the schools —
  something genuinely NEW grew and took the old age's name. The
  method works exactly when it stops being restoration.
- ERASING AN IDEA BY BURNING — fails past the dispersion
  threshold (the color's erasure law: 10,000 scattered copies at
  99% seizure leave 100 seeds, and seeds recompound). SUCCEEDED,
  to permanent loss, where copies were few and central: of the
  Maya books, reachable in known temples, four survive on Earth.
  The condition IS the entry: erasure's power is copy count
  times centralization — so the defense is arithmetic: scatter
  early, copy often.
- TRANSLATION WITHOUT LOSS — THE STANDING CANNOT. A word's value
  is relational: it lives in its neighborhood of near-words,
  frequencies, and uses (the color's Zipf tail), and a
  translated word arrives in the wrong neighborhood. Certified
  under that condition — and outside it the shimmer works:
  fixed-referent vocabularies (numbers, elements, parts,
  procedures) translate nearly whole, and machine translation
  has drained the commerce-grade middle. Poetry, prayer, and
  joke stand undrained; the missing measure of meaning keeps
  anyone from even saying by how much.
- FORCING A LANGUAGE BY DECREE ALONE — fails on the standing
  record: the ruled majority's speech outlasts the ruler's. The
  drain is known and it is not decree: decree PLUS schools PLUS
  two-to-three generations shifts a language map (run to
  completion in living memory — regional tongues to national
  ones through the children). The condition is the children,
  never the law.
- SCALING ORAL STORAGE TO A CIVILIZATION — the trained-memory
  guilds (bards, reciters, genealogists) were the best form
  found, and hit the skull's ceiling: fidelity 0.9-grade per
  hand-off, a few epics per lifetime, the whole stock exposed to
  the holder law in every plague. Superseded by script, kept
  where script was absent — surviving honorably wherever never
  beaten.
- DECIMALIZING THE CALENDAR — tried at state scale once: the
  ten-day week. Rest fell from ~52 days a year to 36.5 (365÷7
  against 365÷10) — a third of the rest gone — and the method
  died in ~12 years against the body and the village it ignored.
  The cleanest case of a standard failing because the rhythm it
  broke was load-bearing.
- KEEPING A CRAFT BY SECRECY — hoard the method, hold the holder
  count low by design. The holder law prices it: k at a handful
  is a standing bet against fire, plague, and succession, and
  the ledger below shows the bet lost repeatedly. Works only
  while it works; fails permanently.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- ACID PAPER — the means that ate its cargo: wood-pulp paper
  (1850-1990) self-destructs in ~50-150 years, and the century
  that printed more than all before it printed on it. Drained
  going forward (acid-free standards); the brittle back-stock is
  a slow fire still burning in every library.
- THE LOST-KNOWLEDGE LEDGER — means destroyed, each indexed by
  what would drain it:
  · ALEXANDRIA — the loss itself is unrecorded: the library died
    of budget and neglect across centuries at least as much as
    of any one fire, and what it held is unknown — a named
    unknown about an unknown. Draining in progress: Herculaneum's
    carbonized scrolls, unreadable for ~2,000 years, are being
    read unopened by X-ray — every recovered roll subtracts from
    this entry.
  · DAMASCUS STEEL — the blade outlived the method: the smith
    chain broke when the wootz ore source changed, because part
    of the recipe lived unwritten in the ore's trace metals — a
    condition nobody knew was a condition. Drained partially:
    microscopy found the structures and named the impurities;
    working reproduction stays contested. The standing lesson:
    a method's conditions include ones its holders never knew
    they were using.
  · ROMAN CONCRETE — THE SHIMMER CASE, drained: standing two
    millennia, unmakeable for ~1,300 years, and the why found at
    last (seawater chemistry; hot-mixed lime clasts that
    self-heal cracks). Moved to color across engineering and
    chemistry; named here as proof the white's entries are
    waiting rooms, not graves.
  · GREEK FIRE — still lost, and lost BY DESIGN: secrecy held
    the holder count near zero on purpose, and the security
    model worked until it worked on its owners. Would drain on a
    find — a recipe text, a residue analyzed; nothing certain.
- THE UNREAD SCRIPTS — records kept, keys missing, each with its
  named drain: Linear A (a bilingual find, or a known relative),
  the Indus signs (longer texts — survivors average a few signs
  each), rongorongo (almost anything — about two dozen objects
  hold the whole corpus), Meroitic (the inverse: sounds read, meanings
  waiting on a relative). The color's Rosetta method says why
  they are kept: a locked room opens the day a key arrives, and
  hieroglyphs waited ~1,400 years for theirs.
- RECORDING BEFORE THE RECORDER — every voice and performance
  before 1877 is gone AS performance; the want (hear the dead,
  not just read them) stood unanswerable for the whole prior
  record. Not drainable backward; filed because it prices what
  the recorder bought.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- KNOW WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED — the past is underdetermined by
  its remains: chronicles biased, survival random, most days
  markless. Every method triangulates; none closes the gap.
  Permanently open, permanently narrowing.
- KEEP MEANING, NOT JUST WORDS — archives hold text; the context
  that made it mean evaporates on its own clock. A record can be
  perfectly preserved and perfectly opaque. Blocked on the
  missing measure of meaning.
- LOSE NOTHING THROUGH A COLLAPSE — unsolved on the whole
  record: every fallen civilization burned part of the ledger.
  The dispersion arithmetic says HOW (copies, places, materials,
  holders); no institution has yet outlived its own civilization
  to prove the recipe at full scale.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- The burnings ledger: books ordered burned by the first Chinese
  empire, the Baghdad libraries in 1258, the Maya codices in
  1562, down to shelled archives in living memory. Each ran the
  erasure arithmetic: where copies were scattered the ideas
  returned; where few and central, four books are what remain of
  a literature.
- The decreed revivals ledger: every empire's attempt to
  legislate its grandfather's brilliance back — form copied
  without flow. The trying was constant; restoration-as-such
  succeeded zero times.
- The failed calendars and invented tongues: each met a
  load-bearing rhythm or a cold-start threshold, and each is
  filed WITH its condition, because both recompute (a rhythm
  honestly replaced; a community that raises the children).
- Tasmania as the measured slow loss: the one case where the
  holder law can be watched running across millennia in the
  middens — arts fading from the record as the arithmetic says
  they must. The color's redundancy law is written in this ink.
- Secrecy's tax, paid in full: Greek fire gone, guild methods
  gone, court arts gone — every hoard that held its holder count
  down eventually paid the whole stock.
- The uncounted teachers: every method the color's HISTORY says
  arrived "from necessity" was found by people who left no
  names; their tries and failures were the tuition. This page
  acknowledges the payment; the color inherits the balance.

- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: THE HOLDER LAW ↔ ENDANGERED SPECIES

  chance mathematics that thins small populations in life's

  breed the holders) transfer with their numbers is untried on
  culture's side. The most promising dotted line on this page.
