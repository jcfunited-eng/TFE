## THINGS
The units this subject is about. Each sits in many phylums at once;
its other homes are named so a cascade can branch.

- BELIEF — a claim about the world held in a head, and the head is
  not where the world is; comes in strengths, not as a switch; also
  lives in: mind (how it forms and defends itself), belief (the
  organized kind), law (what a jury may hold).
- REASON — what a belief stands on; ask for it and you get another
  belief, so every chain of reasons ends somewhere; also:
  mathematics (proof), law (grounds), teaching (the child's fourth
  "but why", which is the honest one).
- TRUTH — the world actually being the way the claim says; the one
  part of knowing that cannot be checked from inside the head;
  also: mathematics, law, measurement.
- EVIDENCE — present stuff that would be different if the story
  were different: ink, rock, memory, a reading, a witness; also:
  measurement, law, medicine, earth (strata as testimony).
- THE CLAIM — a sentence that forbids something; what it forbids is
  its cargo, and a claim that forbids nothing carries nothing;
  also: language, law, measurement.
- THE WORD AND ITS DEFINITION — a handle cut by people, for
  purposes; a decision, not a discovery; also: language (its native
  home), law (terms of art), teaching.
- THE MAP AND THE MODEL — a stand-in useful because of what it
  leaves out; a perfect map would be the land again, and you could
  not fold it; also: mathematics, computing, design, measurement.
- THE QUESTION — a statement with a hole in it, and the statement
  can be wrong ("what is north of the north pole?"); broken
  questions get taken apart, not answered; also: language,
  teaching, law (objected to when it assumes facts not in
  evidence).
- CAUSE — a claim about what would have happened otherwise, which
  is a world not available for inspection; also: physics, law
  (but-for and proximate), medicine (the whole reason trials
  exist), engineering (root-cause).
- THE THING — a pattern that matter passes through; the stuff is
  traffic, the arrangement is what persists; also: physics, life (a
  body as a standing flame fed by food), music (a melody outliving
  every performance), earth (rivers).
- THE ARRANGEMENT — real without being made of anything: break a
  wall and you have the same bricks and no wall; nothing added,
  nothing lost, a relation destroyed; also: cooking (one dish, one
  mess, same ingredients), music, building, mathematics.
- THE SELF — an activity kept up continuously out of material that
  keeps arriving and leaving; a flame, not a stone; also: mind,
  body (rebuilt over years, the person unbroken), law (the one held
  liable), belief.
- EXPERIENCE — that there is something it is like to be you; the
  one place where explaining-by-what-it-does has not obviously
  worked; also: mind, medicine (what anaesthesia removes), music.
- POSSIBILITY — a fact about the world, not a mood: that the bridge
  could fail is true now, while it stands; also: engineering (safe
  loads), medicine (risk in the currently well), mathematics.
- TIME'S ARROW — the one-way direction that is not in the laws but
  in how the world started; traces run from earlier to later, which
  is why you have evidence about yesterday and none about tomorrow;
  also: physics, earth, computing (a log grows one way).
- ABSENCE — holes, shadows, the guard who did not come: items that
  are where something is not, countable and causal; also: physics
  (a gap that carries current), building (a doorway is what a wall
  lacks), mathematics (zero, which looked like cheating).
- NUMBER AND THE GENERAL — three apples rot and three does not;
  redness outlives every red thing burned; forced truths about
  things with no location; also: mathematics, language, economy
  (value: real, binding, and nowhere).

## CLAIMS
What holds, written to be tested by a crossing query. Worth is
never absolute — it is applicability to the query at hand.

- Knowing is three alignments at once: holding a claim, holding it
  for a good reason, and the world being that way. The last part
  cannot be checked from inside the head — the whole apparatus of
  evidence and measurement exists because of that.
- Right by accident is not knowing, and it feels no different. The
  station clock reads three, it is three, and the clock stopped a
  day ago: belief and world lined up by coincidence instead of by
  connection. A correct answer from a broken method pays once and
  teaches nothing.
- Every chain of reasons terminates — in a circle, in a stopping
  place taken as given, or in nobody having gone further. A court
  accepts the calendar without proving it; every structure ends on
  ground nobody engineered.
- Certainty is real and its territory is small: inside rules
  somebody made up — arithmetic, chess, a legal definition — you
  unpack what was put in. About the world you get very strong and
  never finished. From the inside the two feel identical, which is
  the danger.
- Tomorrow resembling today is a bet, not a proof. The sun's every
  recorded rise is a fact about mornings already over; the step to
  tomorrow needs "the future resembles the past", and that cannot
  be earned by noting it has worked so far — working so far is the
  past again. Every planting date makes this bet annually.
- Confirmation and refutation are lopsided: a thousand white swans
  complete nothing, one black swan ends it on the spot. Science
  runs on the half that works — no "proved" drawer, only a
  shooting range, and the best records belong to ideas clever
  people wanted dead.
- Nothing is tested alone. Every experiment leans on the
  instrument, the sample, the helper, and a dozen background
  beliefs; when the prediction fails, something in that bundle is
  wrong and the result does not say which.
- For any pile of facts, more than one story fits. The pile
  narrows the field and never closes it. When two fit equally,
  take the one with fewer moving parts — not because the world is
  simple, but because every extra part is unchecked and a story
  with enough parts fits anything. The edge of that tool: simple
  is not a synonym for true, and the tidy account of weather,
  bodies, and people is usually the wrong one.
- The misfit observation is the most valuable object in the room
  and the most likely to be written off as error. Everything that
  fits was already predicted and teaches nothing; only the misfit
  carries news. Fields absorb misfits for years, then turn over
  suddenly — the suddenness is in the acceptance, never in the
  evidence.
- The senses are instruments with error bars. "I saw it with my
  own eyes" reports a verdict, not a recording: what reaches
  awareness has been sorted, filled in, and named in a fraction of
  a second, with the work not shown. Illusions persist after you
  know better. Confidence in a memory and its accuracy are two
  separate quantities, and rehearsal raises the first while
  editing the second.
- Nearly everything anyone knows, they were told. First-hand
  knowledge is a thin strip around an enormous inherited pile —
  not a weakness, the only way a person can hold more than one
  life's worth. So the worth of a report is the worth of its
  chain, and agreement is worth the risk it ran: independent
  attempts to break a claim that all failed are evidence;
  headcount alone is not.
- Real expertise only grows where the world answers back fast and
  clearly. A surgeon and a pilot are told within minutes; a pundit
  is told years later, vaguely, if at all — so confidence rises
  with practice while accuracy does not.
- The same evidence gets two bars depending on whether you liked
  the answer: "can I believe this?" for the agreeable, "must I?"
  for the rest — and almost anything survives the first question,
  almost nothing the second. The defence operates on the standard
  of proof, which is why it is invisible to the one doing it.
- The map is never the territory. Every concept, average, diagram,
  and rule is a deliberate discarding, and its power is exactly
  proportional to how much it dropped while still working. A model
  can be false and useful at once, and usually is — but "it works,
  so it is true" is a step too far: sailors navigated for
  centuries on a sun that circles the earth, and arrived.
- A definition is a decision. Astronomers voted, and a planet
  stopped being one. Draw any sharp line across a smooth change
  and the middle is misfiled — the heap survives each single grain
  removed and does not survive all of them. And some words look
  like measurements and contain a verdict (natural, efficient,
  healthy): the argument is finished before anyone notices it
  started.
- Before arguing, find which of three arguments you are in: facts
  (settled by looking), values (not settled by looking), or words
  (dissolved the moment both sides state what they mean). Most
  long arguments are the third kind wearing the costume of the
  first — the falling tree "makes a sound" for one meaning of
  sound and not the other, with no fact unknown.
- A thing survives total replacement of its material. The ship
  with every plank renewed is the same ship because the
  arrangement carried on — and if the old planks are rebuilt
  alongside, two ships hold real claims and no further fact
  decides, because identity was our way of counting one across
  time, not a substance in the hull. This fabric faces the
  question about itself: its knowledge is restated file by file
  from older files, and what persists is the claims still
  gripping, not the wording — the ship, sailing.
- A whole is its parts plus their arrangement, and properties can
  live at the pattern's scale without living in any piece: water
  is wet and no molecule is; a traffic jam rolls backwards and no
  car does. Nothing changes upstairs without something changing
  downstairs — the higher level is carried, even where it cannot
  be predicted.
- Explaining a thing does not evict it. The rainbow survives being
  bent light; a decision made of neurons is still a decision.
  "Real" is not a prize for the smallest level — it belongs to
  whatever makes a difference, and tables stop coffee from
  falling.
- "The cause" is the unusual member of a set of necessary
  conditions: fire needs spark, dry wood, and air, and people name
  the spark because the other two were the ordinary background.
  Absences cause (the medicine not given); causes can be
  overplentiful (two shots, either fatal alone — the plain
  would-not-have-happened-otherwise test breaks); and every chain
  runs back forever, so wherever an explanation stops, a person
  stopped it.
- A system can be completely determined and completely
  unpredictable — being fixed is about the world, being
  predictable is about what a knower can do with it. And when we
  call a thing random we may mean nobody could know, or the world
  has not decided; the same arithmetic serves both, which is why
  that argument never closes.
- Purpose has two honest senses and only one needs a mind: a knife
  is for cutting because someone meant it; a heart is for pumping
  because hearts that pumped got copied. Meaning is not found
  lying in the world — it is made, by what you care about, tend,
  and are answerable to — and a made thing is not thereby a fake
  one: law, money, music, and promises are all real and all made.
- The examined life claim, weighed honestly: as an absolute
  ("worth nothing unexamined") it overclaims. What holds is
  sharper — unexamined beliefs steer you anyway; targeted doubt is
  the only working defence against a confident chain from a bad
  premise; and a person who cannot say what would change their
  mind is holding a flag, not a belief. Examination is not a
  luxury; it is the maintenance the steering gear needs.
- A description says what happens; an explanation says why it had
  to. "The tide comes in twice a day" predicts tomorrow perfectly
  and answers nothing; adding the moon turns the same facts into
  an explanation with nothing new observed. Prediction and
  understanding come apart in both directions, and neither
  delivers the other.

## SCIENCE
The logic and arithmetic of knowing and being, held — not
mentioned. Each piece is a LAW a query can compute with, a RULE it
can follow, and where it earns it, a WORKED case proving the rule
runs.

### LOGIC — the machinery of valid steps

LAW: a valid form never carries true premises to a false
  conclusion. The conditional "if P then Q" is false in exactly
  one of its four rows: P true, Q false. Denying the consequent
  is valid (if P then Q; not-Q; therefore not-P — no row breaks
  it). Affirming the consequent is not (if P then Q; Q; therefore
  P).
RULE: to test an argument's form — hunt for one row where every
  premise is true and the conclusion false; one such row kills the
  form regardless of subject matter.
WORKED: "if it rained, the street is wet; the street is wet; so it
  rained." Row: rain false, wet true (a sprinkler). Both premises
  stand, the conclusion fails — invalid, by one row. The valid
  twin runs the other way: street dry, therefore no rain. This
  pair IS the engine of testing: a theory predicts, the
  prediction fails, the theory falls — and a prediction that
  succeeds proves nothing, by the sprinkler row.

LAW: refutation lands on the bundle, not the claim. What is
  tested is (theory AND instrument AND sample AND helper); not-Q
  yields not-(all of them together), and logic alone cannot say
  which conjunct broke.
RULE: after a failed prediction — list the bundle, check the
  cheapest members first, and never let the favorite claim be the
  only suspect or the only one spared.

LAW: self-reference can build contradiction. Proof by cases on
  the barber who shaves exactly those who do not shave
  themselves: case 1, he shaves himself — then he shaves a
  self-shaver, breaking the rule; case 2, he does not — then the
  rule commands him to. Both cases contradict; therefore no such
  barber can exist. Two rows, airtight.
RULE: when a rule quantifies over all rules (a list of all lists,
  a sentence about its own truth), run the two cases on the rule
  itself before trusting the system; and keep the famous limit
  fenced — a fixed rulebook rich enough for arithmetic cannot
  prove all its own truths or certify its own consistency, and
  that narrow result says nothing about minds being magic or your
  argument being safe.

LAW: a valid chain with a false conclusion has a false premise —
  even when no premise looks false. The heap: 10,000 grains make
  a heap; removing one grain never unmakes a heap; apply that
  step 9,999 times and one grain is a heap. The end is false, so
  the step-premise fails somewhere — and every particular
  somewhere is wrong, because the word was built for daily use,
  not for repeated small steps.
RULE: when a sharp line must cross a smooth change, place it on
  purpose, state that it is placed, and expect the neighbours
  nearest it to be misfiled — that cost is the price of having a
  usable line at all.

### PROBABILITY — how sure to be, and when to act

LAW: evidence reweighs belief by how much better the claim
  predicts it than the alternatives do.
  P(claim | result) = P(result | claim) × P(claim) ÷ P(result),
  where P(result) sums over every way the result could come.
RULE: to weigh a positive test — multiply how common the
  condition is by how often the test catches it; divide by that
  plus how common the clean cases are times how often the test
  cries wolf.
WORKED: a condition carried by 1 in 1,000; a test that catches
  99 in 100 carriers and false-alarms on 5 in 100 clean.
  True alarms: 0.001 × 0.99 = 0.00099.
  False alarms: 0.999 × 0.05 = 0.04995.
  All alarms: 0.00099 + 0.04995 = 0.05094.
  Weight: 0.00099 ÷ 0.05094 ≈ 0.019 — about 2 in 100.
  A "99% accurate" positive leaves the odds at one in fifty,
  because the crowd of clean people is a thousand times larger
  than the crowd of carriers. The reference class did the work —
  which is why "how likely?" is incomplete until the crowd is
  named, and naming it is a judgement, not a calculation.

LAW: past repetition prices the next case and never reaches
  certainty. After n successes and no failures, the classical
  price of the next success is (n+1) ÷ (n+2).
RULE: to price a streak's continuation — add one to the count,
  divide by the count plus two; and read the honest small print:
  the formula assumes the trials resemble each other, which is
  the very bet it is pricing. The math prices induction; it does
  not ground it.
WORKED: 100 sunrises: 101 ÷ 102 ≈ 0.990. Ten thousand:
  10,001 ÷ 10,002 ≈ 0.9999. The price climbs toward 1 and never
  arrives — the gap that never closes IS the problem of
  induction, in one fraction.

LAW: acting converts a strength into a switch, and the honest
  threshold is a ratio of costs: act when
  chance of the bad outcome > cost of the precaution ÷ cost of
  the bad outcome.
RULE: to decide under uncertainty — price both errors, divide,
  and compare with your strength of belief; where you put the
  line is a separate decision from how sure you are, which is why
  two courts run two standards (beyond reasonable doubt against
  balance of probabilities) for two costs.
WORKED: carrying the umbrella costs 1 unit of bother; getting
  soaked costs 10. Threshold: 1 ÷ 10 = 0.1 — take it at any rain
  chance above one in ten, even while "probably dry" is true.
  Belief graded, action switched, no contradiction.

### THE ARITHMETIC OF TESTIMONY AND AGREEMENT

LAW: a chain's reliability is the product of its links. Through k
  hands each faithful with chance r, the message survives with
  chance r^k.
WORKED: five hands at 0.9 each: 0.9 × 0.9 = 0.81; × 0.9 = 0.729;
  × 0.9 = 0.6561; × 0.9 = 0.59049. Five good witnesses in a row
  and the story is a coin toss plus a sliver — which is why a
  record made at the time beats any recollection, and why a
  rumour that gains detail as it travels is announcing its own
  chain quality.

LAW: independent errors multiply; copied errors do not.
  Three independent witnesses each wrong 1 time in 10 are all
  wrong together with chance 0.1 × 0.1 × 0.1 = 0.001. Fifty
  outlets that all read one wire story are one witness: the
  chance stays 0.1.
RULE: before counting agreeing voices — count the independent
  chains behind them; the value of a consensus is in the failed
  attempts to break it, never in the headcount. A room that
  agrees because the boss spoke first has one chain.

### THE MATHEMATICS OF UNDERDETERMINATION

LAW: any finite data admits endless stories that fit it exactly
  and disagree about the next case.
WORKED: data (0,0), (1,1), (2,4). The story y = x² fits: 0, 1, 4.
  So does every member of y = x² + c·x(x−1)(x−2), for any c: at
  x = 0, 1, 2 the extra term multiplies by zero and vanishes. At
  x = 3 the family splits: x² says 9; the extra term is
  c × 3 × 2 × 1 = 6c, so c = 1 says 15 and c = −2 says −3. Every
  member agrees on all the evidence and disagrees about tomorrow.
RULE: this is why evidence narrows and never closes; the
  tiebreaker (take c = 0, the fewest moving parts) is a choosing
  rule between equals, not a fact about the world — and the next
  observation, not the elegance, is what kills the losers.

### THE ARITHMETIC OF BEING

LAW: disorder wins by count. Arrangements are countable, and the
  mixed ones vastly outnumber the ordered ones: ten coin flips
  give 1 way to be all heads and 10! ÷ (5! × 5!) ways to be
  five-and-five: 3,628,800 ÷ (120 × 120) = 3,628,800 ÷ 14,400 =
  252. Check: 14,400 × 252 = 3,628,800.
RULE: to see the arrow of time — count both directions'
  arrangements; the laws run fine backwards, the counts do not.
  Scale ten coins up to the particles in an egg and the ratio
  grows past naming: eggs break and never unbreak because the
  world started orderly and the count runs downhill. A tidy room
  is a purchased, temporary exception.

LAW: in a system whose small errors compound, the forecast
  horizon grows only as the logarithm of the measurement's
  precision. Error doubling each step, starting at one part in
  10^6: the error reaches full size in about 20 steps, because
  2^20 = 1,048,576 ≈ 10^6.
WORKED: improve the instrument a thousandfold, to one part in
  10^9: 2^30 = 1,073,741,824 ≈ 10^9 — about 30 steps. A
  thousand times the precision bought ten more steps. Determined
  and unpredictable, with the price list attached: this is why a
  weather forecast is good for days and never for months.

LAW: a person is a chain of overlapping stages, and overlap
  compounds away with distance. If each year keeps ~0.95 of the
  connections (memory, character, wants) to the year before, the
  connection across n years is 0.95^n.
WORKED: 0.95^10 ≈ 0.60; square it: 0.95^20 ≈ 0.36; square again:
  0.95^40 ≈ 0.13. To the self of next week you are nearly all
  there; to the self of forty years, about an eighth — which is
  why every pension, promise, and long sentence is partly a
  transaction between people who are not equally you, and why
  money's discount rate is this fact wearing arithmetic.

LAW: with endless collections, a part can match the whole
  one-to-one. Pair every number with its double: 1→2, 2→4, 3→6,
  onward forever. Every number is used, every even number is hit,
  nothing is left over — the evens are exactly as numerous as all
  the numbers.
RULE: "smaller than" is licensed by counting that ends; where the
  counting does not end, retire the license and judge size by
  matching alone. An infinite answer inside a physical formula is
  the formula announcing its own edge.

## METHODS
How the work is done here — each followable, with what governs it
and what breaks it. The prepositioned how-to-think.

- THE COUNTEREXAMPLE HUNT: to test a general claim, do not gather
  friendly cases — construct the hostile one. One black swan
  outweighs a thousand white; one row breaks a form. Breaks when:
  the counterexample is dismissed as an odd case (see the misfit
  claim — that dismissal is how fields sleep).
- THE DISTINCTION: when an argument will not resolve, split the
  word doing double duty. Sound-as-air against sound-as-hearing
  dissolves the falling tree; refuted, unsupported, and untestable
  un-flatten "not proven"; the two senses of "for" separate the
  knife from the heart. Breaks when: distinctions multiply past
  need — each must earn its keep by settling something.
- NAMING THE DISPUTE: before arguing, classify it — fact (go
  look), values (looking cannot settle it), words (state meanings
  and watch it dissolve). Most long arguments are word disputes in
  fact costumes; a trial separates findings of fact from questions
  of law for exactly this reason.
- DISSOLVING THE BROKEN QUESTION: when a question assumes
  something false, the taking-apart is the answer. "What is north
  of the north pole?" and "what is one divided by zero?" get
  dismantled, not computed. Governed by: locate the smuggled
  claim, test it separately. Breaks when: used to dodge — a hard
  question is not a broken one, and calling it broken must itself
  be argued.
- TARGETED DOUBT: applied to a specific claim, ask what would show
  it wrong and go look. This is what makes a person checkable
  rather than merely sincere. Breaks when: aimed at everything at
  once — you cannot doubt your senses using your senses; total
  scepticism consumes its tools and nobody has lived a day inside
  it.
- THE ADVANCE COMMITMENT: say, in advance and in detail, what
  would change your mind — an actual result, an actual number.
  Fix the standard of proof before the evidence arrives, as courts
  do, because afterward the two-bar machinery (can-I versus
  must-I) will be running and you will not feel it.
- THE CHAIN AUDIT: for any report — who saw it, how many hands,
  what each had to gain, whether anyone in the line could have
  checked, and whether fifty voices are fifty chains or one wire
  story. Expertise passes the audit as testimony with a track
  record attached, inside its range; the failure list (speaking
  past the range, borrowed prestige, unstated interest) is what
  makes deference usable rather than blind.
- THE REFERENCE-CLASS DECLARATION: before quoting any rate, name
  the crowd it belongs to, and say why that crowd — the same
  person carries a different number in every crowd that contains
  them, all correct.
- DECLARING THE DROP: abstraction is throwing things away on
  purpose, so say what was thrown. A model states where it holds;
  a rule is taught with its exceptions filed, not hidden; a safety
  factor is an engineer's written admission that the model is a
  sketch.
- THE THOUGHT EXPERIMENT, FENCED: push a rule to an extreme case
  to expose what it actually says — that is real yield. The fence:
  it reports what your mind does with the case, not what the world
  does, and intuitions about impossible situations were trained on
  possible ones. Riding a light beam paid; most trolley variants
  are overread.
- BURDEN PLACEMENT: whoever makes the claim owes the case — not a
  law of the universe, a working rule kept because the alternative
  is unlivable: disproof cannot be owed faster than invention is
  free. Breaks when: used as a cudgel to never engage a serious
  case once offered.
- THE STEELMAN SWAP: state the opposing case at its strongest,
  then apply YOUR side's bar to your own claim — ask "must I
  believe this?" of the agreeable answer. Cheap open-mindedness
  (moving with whatever arrived last) and cheap conviction (never
  moving) are the two failure modes; the expensive thing is moving
  in proportion to what arrived.
- TRACE READING: for what happened once and cannot rerun — a war,
  an origin, a decision — build the case as a detective does: from
  present traces that would differ if the story differed, and from
  many independent lines forced to agree. The past never reaches
  you; only its downstream stuff does, and the reasoning is the
  entire link.
- SEPARATING VERDICT FROM ARGUMENT: demolishing someone's
  reasoning does not make their claim false — it makes it
  unearned. Winning the argument and settling the question are
  different achievements; mark the correct answer reached by wrong
  working as wrong working still.

## MEANS
What the methods run on, with what matters about each.

- STATED DEFINITIONS: the cheapest instrument in the subject — two
  sides writing down what they mean by the contested word, which
  settles the word disputes (most of them) at the door.
- LOGIC'S SMALL TOOLKIT: the valid forms, the one-row kill, proof
  by cases, the bundle correction — portable into every phylum,
  subject-blind by design.
- THE RECORD MADE AT THE TIME: statements taken early and in
  writing; predictions registered before results; lab notebooks;
  the diary against the rebuilt memory. Rehearsal edits content
  while building confidence, so the instrument is the timestamp.
- THE ADVERSARIAL PAIR: cross-examination, the design review whose
  job is to attack, the differential diagnosis that deliberately
  entertains what nobody believes — doubt institutionalized so it
  does not depend on anyone's mood.
- INDEPENDENT WITNESSES: many hands, no shared source — the only
  disinfectant that works on luck, tilt, and wishful reading all
  at once. The arithmetic is in SCIENCE; the mean is the
  deliberate arranging of independence.
- THE BORDERLINE CASE: the probe instrument for definitions and
  lines — feed the rule the case nearest the edge and read what it
  actually says, not what it was hoped to say.
- THE FENCE: for famous results that leak (the incompleteness
  theorem above all) — a written statement of exactly what was
  proved and the standing list of what it does not license.
- THE LEDGER OF WHAT FAILED: this phylum's mirror — kept with the
  same care as the color, because in a subject where confirmation
  is impossible, the accumulated refutations and dissolved
  questions ARE most of the working knowledge.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject answers — the doors a query enters by.

- WEIGH WHAT YOU HOLD: sort knowing from lucky guessing, strong
  from certain, refuted from unsupported from untestable — so
  every other phylum's claims can carry honest weights.
- KEEP FROM BEING FOOLED: by chains, by crowds, by credentials, by
  the chooser of words — and above all by yourself, whose two-bar
  machinery runs silently on your own evidence.
- DECIDE UNDER UNCERTAINTY: convert graded belief into yes-or-no
  action with the cost ratio, and place burdens and standards of
  proof before the heat arrives.
- SAY WHAT COUNTS AS ONE THING: identity across change, the unit
  before the count, the line across the gradient — the questions
  law, trade, medicine, and this fabric itself must answer daily
  and cannot answer without this subject's tools.
- TAKE APART THE BROKEN QUESTION: distinguish the deep from the
  malformed, so effort lands where an answer can exist.
- FACE THE HARD FACTS WITHOUT LYING: freedom, death, meaning —
  hold what is genuinely unsettled as unsettled, use the workable
  parts (courts run on the workable meaning of "free" daily), and
  refuse both the comforting overclaim and the lazy despair.
- THE EXAMINED LIFE: run the maintenance on your own steering —
  because unexamined beliefs drive anyway, and the fee for never
  checking is paid in decades.

## HISTORY
How the knowledge arose, in what order, at what cost — the order
carries why.

- The examined-life claim opened the subject: a man in a market
  asking people to define the words they lived by, showing each
  definition failing its borderline cases. The city voted him
  dead for it. The method (the counterexample hunt, the stated
  definition) survived the sentence and built everything after.
- Certainty from pure thought was attempted at full seriousness —
  doubt everything, keep what survives, rebuild the world from it.
  One stone survived ("something is thinking"); the rebuild leaked
  at the second step and the world never followed from the
  armchair. What survived the failure was the tool: doubt as an
  instrument, and the discovery of exactly where its edge is (the
  mirror holds the receipts).
- The problem of induction was named plainly (~three centuries
  ago) and has never been solved — only rerouted: since no heap of
  cases completes a law, the method turned to the half that works,
  refutation, and the shooting range replaced the proof drawer.
  That reroute is the single most consequential repair in the
  subject.
- Definitions were learned to be decisions in public: a planet
  unmade by vote, an age of majority moving a person between legal
  species overnight, a blood-pressure line minting millions of
  patients. The lesson generalized backward through every old
  argument about what a thing "really is".
- The paradigm lesson was paid for in decades: ulcers were stress
  until they were bacteria; drifting continents were laughed at,
  then obvious. Slow, slow, sudden — and the record shows the
  suddenness lives in the acceptance, not the evidence.
- The incompleteness result arrived, was proved, and then was
  misquoted for a century; the fence around it became standing
  equipment because the misuse cost more arguments than the
  theorem settled.
- The turn to language: a generation found that many deep-sounding
  questions were word disputes and broken questions, and cleared
  them — then overclaimed that ALL of them were, and the survivors
  of the dissolving (why anything exists; what experience is)
  stood out the sharper for it. Both the clearing and the
  overreach are load-bearing history.
- Probability arrived late and rewrote "how sure": graded belief,
  priced bets, the reference class, the two error costs. The
  oldest questions of the subject gained arithmetic without losing
  their edges — the edges moved into the choosing of crowds and
  costs, where they remain.

## RELATIONS
First-class connections — the cascade's paths outward. Each names
what runs along it.

- MATHEMATICS: the valid forms, proof, the incompleteness fence,
  infinity's matching law, probability's whole machinery; and back
  the other way — whether numbers exist anywhere is this phylum's
  question about mathematics' subject matter.
- PHYSICS: the arrow of time from initial order, determinism and
  the chaos horizon, the quantum floor and the two randoms, the
  cost of every look (a reading is an interaction), simultaneity
  against the moving present.
- MIND: seeing as verdict, memory as rebuild, the two-bar defence
  of belief, tacit skill below the reach of words, the feeling of
  open futures, the constructed unity of the one occupant.
- LANGUAGE: definitions as decisions, handles and their carving,
  thick words with verdicts inside, the word dispute and its
  dissolution, what cannot be handed over in sentences.
- LAW: standards of proof fixed in advance, burden placement,
  but-for and proximate causation, terms of art, hearsay as the
  chain audit written into procedure, the person held liable
  across decades of change — the courtroom is this phylum's
  working model shop.
- MEASUREMENT: the agreed stick resting on theory, instruments as
  hardened arguments, error bars on the senses, the record made at
  the time.
- MEDICINE: the trial as counterfactual machinery, diagnosis by
  bundle, thresholds minting patients, evidence-of-absence against
  absence-of-evidence, degrees of awareness after injury.
- LIFE: species real in the middle and blurred at the edges, minds
  in degrees along the animal line, function without a designer,
  the body as standing pattern.
- ETHICS: no pile of facts adds to an ought; blame standing on
  choice; harm without felt pain (death as subtraction); promises
  binding a self who never voted; the capacity to suffer as the
  moral ticket.
- BELIEF: the why-questions and the author they presuppose,
  origin stories answering why rather than how, meaning made
  against meaning found.
- STORY: thought experiments as fiction put to work, the ending
  that pays the debts, counterfactuals as narrative's engine.
- COMPUTING: the halting wall as the incompleteness shape
  correctly applied, one program on many machines (pattern over
  stuff), the log that grows one way, randomness produced by rule.
- PEOPLE: testimony as most of knowledge, consensus and its
  failure modes, the paradigm defended as a possession, the
  fashion that ends arguments without settling them.
- TEACHING: the child's fourth why, the beginner's honest
  confidence, the rule taught by leaving out exceptions, marking
  right answers with wrong working.
- ECONOMY: value real and located nowhere, price as one-number
  abstraction, the discount rate as the overlap chain in
  arithmetic, positions with no exit rule as beliefs with no
  falsifier.
- MUSIC: the melody that survives every instrument (arrangement
  over stuff), the note that exists only while played (the self's
  model), the described chord against the heard one.
- ENGINEERING: safety factors as declared drops, failure modes
  nobody imagined, redundancy as overdetermined causation, the
  design review as institutional doubt.
- CULTURE: inherited knowledge as civilization's working capital,
  the once-unanimous positions nobody will own, practices built on
  the self-as-process observation.
- ASTRONOMY: one universe and no control group, the planet vote,
  circles upon circles predicting eclipses for centuries — the
  standing museum of useful-and-false.
- EARTH: the past read from present traces, strata as one-way
  records, continents drifting through a paradigm.
- WEATHER: the working exhibit of determined-and-unpredictable,
  with the forecast horizon priced in SCIENCE.
