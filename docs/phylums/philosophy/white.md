# PHILOSOPHY — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/philosophy/color.md: same parts,
opposite sign. Seed authored by Claude from docs/fabric_phylums/
118_deep_philosophy_of_knowing.md and docs/fabric_phylums/119_deep_
philosophy_of_being.md. What didn't hold carries its WHY in
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone; the deepest entries in the whole
fabric's white live on this page, filed with their possibilities.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE VIEW FROM NOWHERE — wanted since the subject began: a
  standpoint with no standpoint, the report that includes no
  reporter. CANNOT BE under the stated condition that every
  observation is made from somewhere — a position, a moment, a
  species, a set of interests — and the person is the instrument
  carrying the most theory of all. What stands instead (the
  color's MEANS): many independent standpoints forced to agree.
  Would drain on: a reporter shown to add nothing — no candidate
  written.
- THE PERFECT MAP — a representation losing nothing. CANNOT BE by
  the map's own working law: a map is useful because of what it
  leaves out, and the map that keeps everything is the territory
  again, unfoldable. Certified under the condition "stand-in AND
  complete"; drop either half and the want dissolves rather than
  drains.
- THE FREE LOOK — observation at zero cost to the observed. CANNOT
  BE while a reading is an interaction: the thermometer drinks
  heat, the question shapes the answer, the camera makes the room
  perform. The cost has no floor at zero; it is sometimes tiny and
  never absent. Would drain on: a look with no exchange in either
  direction — physics currently prices that at impossible too.
- THE CONSCIOUSNESS METER — an instrument reading whether, and how
  much, a creature experiences. NOT YET FOUND, and blocked deeper
  than instrument-making: behaviour and report are the only
  outputs, experience does not show up in either by itself, and
  anaesthesia removes it reliably while nobody can say what was
  removed. Would drain on: any measurable that tracks experience
  itself rather than its machinery — none is written.
- THE FINDABLE SELF — the thing inside that is really you, sought
  by looking inward. The search keeps returning an activity, not
  an object: a flame kept up out of arriving and leaving material,
  with no residue that was the real thing all along. Filed as a
  unit that dissolved under examination into a process — the want
  for a stone answered by a fire.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled, each carrying a possibility.

- CERTAINTY ABOUT THE WORLD FROM PURE THOUGHT — tried by the
  greats at full strength, and the receipts are kept. The
  method-doubter kept one stone ("something is thinking" survives
  its own doubt) and the rebuild leaked at the second step — every
  route from the stone back to the world borrowed a premise the
  doubt had already burned. The geometry-style systems deduced
  whole worlds from self-evident starting points — and the worlds
  disagreed with each other, which is the signature of certainty
  minted rather than transported: the world was never among the
  premises. WHY, in one condition: a chain transports certainty
  and cannot create it, and certainty is only available inside
  rules somebody made up. Certified under that condition. Would
  drain on: a premise about the world that is both contentful and
  undoubtable — three centuries of candidates have each failed one
  half of that test.
- THE COMPLETE FINAL PHILOSOPHY — each one tried; the ledger, kept
  because the pattern is the knowledge. The theory of perfect
  forms behind appearances. The system of natural places and
  purposes that held for ~2,000 years and fell to instruments, not
  arguments. The grand synthesis of faith and logic. The absolute
  system in which history itself completed. The school that ruled
  every unverifiable sentence meaningless — whose own ruling was
  unverifiable, the cleanest self-refutation on record. The claim
  that every philosophical question would dissolve into language —
  most did; the survivors stood out sharper. WHY the pattern
  repeats, two conditions deep: a final system must certify its
  own foundations, and no system rich enough to be interesting can
  (the fenced result in the color's SCIENCE); and any system's
  test is the counterexample, which no system can enumerate in
  advance. Certified under those conditions. Would drain on: a
  self-certifying rich system — the fence says not this way. What
  each collapse left behind stayed valuable (doubt, dialectic, the
  meaning test applied with its own edge): the ruins are quarries,
  which is why the ledger is filed as knowledge and not as scorn.
- INDUCTION, JUSTIFIED — the attempts and their receipts: justify
  the future-resembles-the-past step by its track record (the
  track record is the past again — circular); by the succession
  formula (the formula assumes the trials resemble each other —
  the bet smuggled into the price of the bet); by "it is the best
  we can do" (true, and a counsel, not a justification). Still
  unsolved after ~three centuries; the working reroute (test by
  refutation instead) is in the color. Would drain on: a
  demonstrated bridge from any heap of particulars to a general
  law — nothing certain here, and the honest possibility is that
  the want itself is malformed: proof was a standard built for
  made-up rules, applied to the world by hope.
- UNSETTLED: FREE WILL — both roads still run into something. If
  every act follows from what came before, the choosing has no
  entry point; if some act does not follow, that part is
  coincidence, and an uncaused twitch is less yours, not more —
  randomness was the first escape tried and it fails on
  authorship. What already works (filed in the color): the
  everyday meaning — acting from your own wants, unforced,
  undeceived — which courts and families use daily and get right.
  Nothing certain on the hard half — but here is a possibility:
  "did you choose your wants?" may be a broken question, since any
  chooser of wants would need wants to choose by, and the regress
  is the question's own smuggled premise, north of the north pole.
  Would settle it: an account of authorship that survives both
  horns, or a dissolution that survives the counterattack.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT EXPERIENCE IS — the machinery of mind yields to
  what-it-does explanations everywhere except the fact that there
  is something it is like to be the machinery. Complete knowledge
  of the colour system still seems to leave what red is like
  unlearned. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: the gap
  may be between two kinds of access (being a process against
  describing one) rather than two kinds of stuff, in which case no
  new ingredient is missing and the surprise was expecting a
  description to BE an undergoing. Would drain on: either a
  mechanism whose description entails the undergoing, or a
  demonstration that the entailment was never owed.
- UNSETTLED: WHICH CREATURES FEEL — one mind directly evidenced
  (yours), every other inferred by resemblance, and resemblance
  fades along the animal line with no step anyone can find.
  Possibility: there is no step — a slope with no line on it, and
  the line we need for practice (law, farming, medicine) will be a
  placed line, owned as placed. Would drain on: the consciousness
  meter above.
- UNSETTLED, THE DEEPEST STANDING UNKNOWN: WHY THERE IS SOMETHING
  RATHER THAN NOTHING. Every answer ever offered either names a
  something (a first cause, a law, a ground) that then stands in
  need of the same explanation, or declares the question
  inapplicable — and each move feels like a dodge to whoever
  wanted the other. The difficulty is structural: an explanation
  cites something, so an explanation of everything must cite
  something outside everything, or stop. Filed with honest
  humility: no phylum in this fabric reaches it, and none is
  expected to. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility,
  offered as such: "nothing" may not be a possible state at all —
  every attempt to picture it produces an empty room, a dark
  space, a silence, all of them somethings, and the one
  mathematics of absence we hold (zero) exists inside a system of
  somethings. If nothing is not a live alternative, there was
  never a fact needing explanation, only a question grammar built
  and the world never faced. Would move it: a demonstration that
  absolute absence is, or is not, coherent — either result
  re-files this entry, and no method for producing either is
  written anywhere in the color.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A LOGIC OF DISCOVERY — the color holds the full
  machinery of testing (the one-row kill, the bundle, the priced
  bet) and nothing on where hypotheses come from. The minting of
  the guess is unformalized; every written method starts after the
  candidate exists. Would drain on: a procedure generating
  candidates that are not recombinations of stored answers — the
  fabric's own assembler bet, still on trial.
- LACKING: A MEASURE OF SIMPLICITY — "fewer moving parts" wins
  ties, and no written rule counts parts without a chosen
  vocabulary doing the counting: a curve simple in one coordinate
  system is baroque in another. The tiebreaker works daily and
  cannot yet say what it measures. Would drain on: a
  vocabulary-independent count — candidates exist in computing's
  description-length work (not yet built here) and each smuggles
  its own alphabet.
- LACKING: A SETTLED TREATMENT OF VAGUENESS — the heap breaks
  two-valued logic on every gradient word, and each repair
  (degrees of truth, many stacked sharp lines, truth on all
  admissible sharpenings) grips part and none has won. The color
  files the working rule (place the line on purpose, own the
  misfiled middle); the theory under it is open.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: FORMAL LOGIC ON ORDINARY "IF" — the
  conditional of the truth table calls "if the moon is cheese,
  then two and two make four" true, and ordinary speech refuses.
  The tool is exact and the fit is loose; conclusions about
  everyday reasoning read off the formal conditional overreach.
  Condition on the break: it appears wherever the antecedent's
  falsity does the work.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: PROBABILITY ON THE SINGLE CASE — the
  arithmetic runs on crowds, and the single unrepeatable case (a
  war, an origin, this patient) belongs to many crowds at once
  with a different rate in each. The number is exact after the
  crowd is chosen, and the choosing is a judgement the arithmetic
  cannot audit. The break is permanent under the condition
  "unique case, no stated class"; the repair (declare the class,
  argue for it) is in the color's METHODS.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- DEDUCING THE WORLD FROM THE ARMCHAIR — the pure-thought program
  as method: doubt to bedrock, rebuild by chains alone. FAILS
  under the condition "conclusions must contain the world" —
  chains transport what premises hold, the surviving premises held
  no world, and the great attempts diverged from each other
  exactly as unanchored systems must. The tool that survived the
  wreck (targeted doubt) is in the color; the method entire stands
  here with its condition.
- UNIVERSAL DOUBT AS A STARTING POINT — doubt everything at once
  and see what stands. Consumes its own tools on contact: doubting
  the senses uses the senses' reports, doubting reason argues.
  Certified unlivable-and-unanswerable; kept because each new
  generation re-tries it, and the filed condition (doubt needs a
  target and an untouched toolkit) redirects the energy to the
  working form.
- MEANING-BY-VERIFICATION AS A UNIVERSAL TEST — rule every
  sentence meaningless unless observation could settle it. Broke
  on itself (that rule cannot be settled by observation) and broke
  on the subject matter: the untestable, the unsupported, and the
  refuted are three states, and the test flattened them to one.
  What survived: the demand that a claim say what it forbids —
  moved to the color as a tool, demoted from a law of meaning.
- ESSENCE-HUNTING DEFINITIONS — seek the necessary-and-sufficient
  core of justice, knowledge, game. Two millennia of tries; the
  borderline cases beat every candidate, and the words work anyway
  — most kind-words run on family resemblance, not cores. FAILS
  under the condition "the word must have a core to be usable";
  drop the condition and the practice (definitions as placed
  decisions) works fine and is in the color.
- INTROSPECTION AS THE LABORATORY — read the mind by looking
  inward and trust the view. The part that explains a decision is
  not the part that made it; rehearsal edits what it reports on;
  the feeling of open futures is missing information wearing a
  costume. FAILS as sole instrument under the condition
  "self-report taken as raw data"; stands as one witness among
  several, audited like any other.
- ETHICS AS GEOMETRY — axioms of good, theorems of duty, proved
  with a flourish. The proofs transported the axioms' standing,
  and the axioms were the whole dispute; opponents rejected the
  starting points, not the steps. The failure is induction's twin
  on the ought side: a chain cannot mint what its premises lack.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- A NEUTRAL OBSERVATION LANGUAGE — sought as the shared floor for
  all disputes: reports carrying no theory. Fell short: the words
  that carve the report carry the carver's frame, and the same
  dial read in two systems is two readings. The working substitute
  (independent standpoints, stated frames) is in the color.
- A SELF-CERTIFYING FOUNDATION — the regress of reasons ends
  somewhere, and every candidate ground (self-evidence, sense
  data, coherence of the whole) either needs its own ground or
  argues in a circle. NOT FOUND, and the fenced result says a rich
  system cannot notarize itself. Filed as the load-bearing
  absence under all of knowing: chains end on ground nobody
  engineered, and honest builders write that down.
- THE CRUCIAL EXPERIMENT — the test that isolates one claim and
  executes it alone. Fell short by the bundle law: the result
  indicts the conjunction, never the conjunct. The repair (check
  the cheap members first, spare no favorite) manages the
  shortfall without removing it.
- AN INSTRUMENT FOR OTHER MINDS — see THE CONSCIOUSNESS METER in
  THINGS; filed twice because the want enters as an instrument
  and as a unit, and both doors should brake a search here.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- DERIVE WHAT TO CARE ABOUT FROM FACTS ALONE — no pile of is adds
  to an ought; the thick words that seem to bridge (healthy,
  natural, efficient) carry the verdict in as luggage. The want
  stands open with ethics; this phylum's contribution is catching
  the luggage.
- DELIVER CERTAINTY ABOUT THE WORLD — the subject can grade,
  price, test, and fence belief; it cannot mint the certainty the
  askers keep coming for. The honest product is "very strong and
  never finished", plus the exact reasons no stronger product
  exists.
- SETTLE WHICH CREATURES FEEL, AND WHAT EXPERIENCE IS — blocked on
  the meter and on the gap; the practical lines get placed anyway
  (medicine, law, farming), and this phylum can only insist they
  be owned as placed.
- ANSWER WHY THERE IS ANYTHING — the standing unknown above; the
  subject's deepest door, and the one it can so far only hold
  open honestly.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- The ledger of final systems is this subject's portrait: each
  system certain, each buried by successors, each leaving tools
  the burial could not touch. The color's HISTORY keeps the tools;
  this page keeps the burials, and the count — every "complete"
  philosophy yet offered has joined the ledger, with no exception
  recorded.
- The two-thousand-year reign of one authority is the standing
  exhibit for the feedback law: a field where the world answers
  back slowly grew confident stagnation, and the instruments that
  finally answered (telescopes, balances) ended in years what
  argument had not ended in centuries. Filed as the cost of
  deference without a track-record check.
- The dissolving generation's overreach: having cleared real word
  disputes by the hundred, it declared the remainder empty — and
  the remainder (why anything; what experience is) outlived the
  school that dismissed it. Lesson filed: a method's victories do
  not license its universality; that inference is induction's
  error made by professionals about their own tool.
- The brain-timing overclaim: readings showing preparation
  ~half a second before a reported decision were announced as
  closing the free-will question. The measurement stood; the
  conclusion did not — what was timed was the report of deciding,
  itself a constructed thing, and the question's two horns stand
  exactly as before. Filed as the newest entry in an old genre:
  an instrument arriving with a philosophy smuggled in its
  readout.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN OPEN, NOT REPAIRED: PHILOSOPHY ↔ PHYSICS ON TIME — the
  moving present got no support from simultaneity's failure, and
  physics cannot yet rule between a real flow and a standing
  block; both fit every observation. The road stands, carrying an
  unsettled question in both directions.
- OVERCLAIMED, REBUILT NARROWER: PHILOSOPHY ↔ MIND ON THE WILL —
  the timing experiments (HISTORY above) broke the naive link
  "measurement settles metaphysics"; the rebuilt road carries a
  speed limit: findings about reports constrain reports, and
  claims beyond that must show the extra step.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: EXPERIENCE ↔ COMPUTING — whether running
  the right process is enough for there to be something it is
  like to run it. Both answers are asserted in the literature;
  neither has a test; the road is dotted until the consciousness
  meter or the entailment argument arrives. This fabric has a
  stake in the answer and files the stake as a stake.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: SIMPLICITY ↔ TRUTH — the tiebreaker earns
  its keep in practice, and no demonstrated law connects fewer
  parts to being right; the connection may be entirely about the
  checker (fewer places to hide an error) and not about the world.
  Dotted until someone shows which.
