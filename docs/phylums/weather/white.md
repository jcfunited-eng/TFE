# WEATHER — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/weather/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting
for its drain. Seed authored by Claude and marked as seed.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE TAMED HURRICANE — wanted for a century, attempted for two
  decades (METHODS holds the trial). CANNOT BE by force at present
  scale: the storm's engine runs at ~6×10¹⁴ W of latent heat (the
  color works this number); humanity's whole electric fleet is
  ~3×10¹² W — two hundred times too small before aiming starts.
  NOT certified against cleverness: steering a sensitive system
  with small, well-timed nudges is unproven, not disproven — the
  same sensitivity that kills the long forecast is, in principle,
  a lever. Would drain on: a demonstrated small push, early in a
  storm's life, with a measured, repeated effect.
- RAIN ON DEMAND — the machine that waters a chosen field. After a
  century of trying: seeding can nudge clouds ALREADY primed
  (~5-15% from wintry mountain clouds in the best measured
  programs, indistinguishable from zero in most others) and can do
  NOTHING with a dry sky — no vapor, no rain; the seed assists
  only the last step of a chain the sun must fund. Filed as the
  half-working kind the fabric is warned about: a small real
  signal keeps the big claim alive. Would drain on: a blinded
  trial moving rain where the air's water budget said none.
- THE PERPETUAL WEATHER ALMANAC — the daily future printed like a
  tide table. Cannot be past ~two weeks (the SCIENCE certificate).
  Tides ARE printable years out — two heavy bodies on clockwork;
  weather is a thin fluid with a temper, and the gap between those
  two futures is the certificate's whole content.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- RAIN DANCES AND WEATHER MAGIC — tried by every civilization, for
  millennia, in earnest (the want was survival). No receipts under
  any controlled look, ever. WHY it persisted: the ritual runs
  UNTIL rain comes, then stops — the last dance always "works";
  droughts end on their own schedule and collect the credit. Filed
  with respect, kept because queries still arrive carrying it.
  Would drain on: a blinded trial where danced-for districts
  out-rain matched undanced ones; none is recorded.
- THE YEAR-AHEAD DAILY ALMANAC (and the groundhog) — particular
  days sold months out verify at chance when scored honestly. WHY
  believed: hits remembered, misses forgiven, vague wording
  verifying itself. The seasonal ODDS of the color's flywheel
  method are this want's honest remnant.
- "LIGHTNING NEVER STRIKES TWICE" — false by counting: tall
  points collect strikes; one famous tower takes ~20-25 hits a
  year. The proverb inverted the truth — past strikes mark the
  places MOST likely to be struck again.
- "THE MOON AND PLANETS STEER THE WEATHER" — measured: the moon
  does raise a tide in the air, of ~0.1 hPa — against daily
  weather swings of 10-20 hPa, a hundred times too small to
  steer anything. The claim dies on the ratio, not the mechanism.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT LIGHTS THE FIRST SPARK — measured electric
  fields inside storms run ~10× SHORT of what laboratory air
  needs to break down, yet the bolt comes. Nothing certain — but
  here is a possibility: a cosmic-ray strike seeds a runaway
  avalanche of fast electrons that closes the gap locally. Would
  drain on: in-storm measurement catching the seed and the bolt
  together.
- UNSETTLED: WHICH ROTATING STORM DROPS THE TORNADO — most
  strongly rotating storms never make one; the twin of a killer
  storm often passes clean. The last-kilometer selection is not
  written; warnings therefore average ~13 minutes and false-alarm
  ~70% — those two numbers ARE the size of this unknown. Would
  drain on: the low-level trigger caught in the act (the MEANS
  gap below).
- UNSETTLED — THE BOUNDED MARQUEE: THE EXACT CLIMATE SENSITIVITY.
  The warming per doubling of CO₂. The direct part is settled
  physics: ~1.2°C from the gas alone. The feedbacks (water vapor
  amplifying, ice retreating, clouds doing something) stretch the
  honest range to ~2.5-4°C, best estimate near 3. WHY forty years
  of work narrowed it so little: clouds — the SCIENCE absence
  below — sit in the middle of the sum with either sign
  available. The bound is real knowledge (2.5-4 rules out both
  "trivial" and "runaway-tomorrow"); the missing digit is this
  white's most expensive open number. Would drain on: cloud
  behavior computed rather than approximated.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke — and one certificate.

- CERTIFIED CANNOT-BE: DAILY FORECASTS PAST ~TWO WEEKS.
  Conditions of the certificate, then the arithmetic.
  IT BINDS: particular days — this cloud, that shower, next
  month's Tuesday. IT DOES NOT BIND: averages (climate stays
  computable), seasonal odds (the ocean's slow memory carries
  months — the color's flywheel method), or the next few days
  (where the forecast is real and sharpening).
  THE ARITHMETIC: errors in the starting picture double every
  ~2-3 days as the flow stretches them. Start a model a mere
  0.1°C wrong — better than any real observing net — and by day
  14 that error has doubled ~7 times: 0.1 × 2⁷ = 12.8°C, as
  large as weather's own day-to-day swings. The forecast is now
  indistinguishable from a guess. And improvement is priced in
  the same coin, backwards: HALVING the starting error buys ONE
  doubling — about two days. A tenfold-better observing net buys
  log₂10 ≈ 3.3 doublings — about a week. A forecast for a day
  two months out would need the start known 2^((60−14)/2) = 2²³ ≈
  8 million times more finely — every cubic meter of sky to
  microdegrees, and the first unmeasured stirring re-runs the
  doubling anyway. The wall is arithmetic, not engineering: money
  buys days, never months. The color answers it honestly with
  ensembles — forecasting the fan instead of denying it.
- LACKING: THE CLOUD MATHEMATICS — one absence, two costs. Small
  end: how a cloud's droplets cross the million-to-one merger to
  rain (the color holds the ratio) — the middle of that crossing
  still leans on measured shortcuts, not computed law. Large end:
  clouds are smaller than any climate model's boxes, so every
  model APPROXIMATES them with tuned rules instead of computing
  them. This single absence is why the sensitivity number above
  has a spread and why rain amounts are the forecast's weakest
  column. Would drain on: computing clouds from droplet physics
  upward at affordable cost.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED, THEN DRAINED — RICHARDSON'S HAND FORECAST
  (1922): the first boxes-and-laws forecast, computed by hand —
  six weeks of arithmetic for six hours ahead — and spectacularly
  wrong (it predicted a pressure jump ~100× any real swing). WHY
  it broke: raw observations carry small imbalances, and his
  equations amplified them as shock waves — the noise computed
  faster than the weather. Drained decades later from both ends:
  smooth the starting field (initialization) and add machines.
  His method, so corrected, IS every modern forecast. Filed as
  this subject's cleanest proof that a failure can carry a whole
  method to the color intact.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
wings-on-arms entries, waiting for their planet.

- PROJECT STORMFURY (1962-1983) — the serious try at weakening
  hurricanes: seed the eyewall with silver iodide, freeze its
  supercooled water, release the heat wider, slacken the core.
  Early seedings looked like triumphs — one storm's winds fell 31%
  and 15% on two seeded days. Two findings killed it: mature
  hurricanes carry too LITTLE supercooled water for the mechanism
  to bite, and unseeded hurricanes did the same wind-drop on their
  own (natural eyewall replacement) — the triumph was the storm's
  normal breathing, credited to the airplane. Abandoned honestly.
  CONDITIONS filed: drains only for a mechanism whose lever the
  storm actually contains; the energy books (color: ~200×
  humanity's output) forbid force but not steering — the THINGS
  entry holds what would reopen it.
- HAIL CANNONS — loud blasts fired skyward to break hail before it
  forms; sold across a century (and still sold). Every controlled
  comparison: no effect. WHY it cannot bite: the sound's energy
  thins to nothing kilometers below the hail nursery (the color's
  updraft arithmetic puts it at storm-top heights the blast never
  reaches with meaningful pressure). Persisted because hail is
  patchy: most firings are followed by no hail — as is not firing.
- WEATHER AS A WEAPON — tried in earnest (monsoon-cloud seeding
  over supply roads, 1967-72). Result unmeasurable against the
  monsoon's own variance — the signal claimed was smaller than
  the noise it hid in. Ended by treaty (1977): the one relation
  in this phylum deliberately severed rather than disproven —
  filed under RELATIONS as such.
- SEEDING AS DROUGHT POLICY — the post-1946 enthusiasm: decades
  of state programs promising reservoirs from chemistry. The
  measured residue: ~5-15% in cold mountain clouds under narrow
  conditions, null elsewhere, zero in dry skies. The narrow
  residue lives in the color as a narrow method; the POLICY-sized
  claim is filed here, the believed-because-half-true kind.
- FORECASTING BY PROVERB OUTSIDE ITS CLIMATE — the lore that
  works (red sky, halo) encodes mid-latitude, west-to-east
  weather; carried to the tropics or the far south it fails on
  schedule because the steering reverses. Not a cannot — a
  conditions entry: a proverb is a claim ABOUT its latitude, and
  travels no better than a wet-method recipe travels uphill
  (cooking's mirror holds the twin).

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE OBSERVING NET'S THIN HALF — the southern oceans and the
  airless heights are sampled sparsely; the practical forecast
  dies near day 10 while the certificate allows ~14, and the gap
  is partly this hunger for starting data. Floats and satellites
  have drained much of it; the remainder is priced by the
  certificate's own arithmetic — each halving of starting error
  buys ~two days, no more.
- NOT YET FOUND: AN EYE ON THE TORNADO'S BIRTH MINUTE — radars
  sweep every few minutes from far away; the birth itself turns in
  seconds, near the ground, under rain. The selection unknown in
  CLAIMS stays unsettled largely for want of this instrument.
  Would drain on: dense, cheap, low-level wind sensing under
  storms — an engineering want, filed at that door.
- BROKEN AND NOT YET REPLACED: THE STILL-CLIMATE RAIN TABLE — the
  "hundred-year storm" tables engineering builds by assume the
  record's statistics stand still; the record has started moving
  (the color's long ledger). Bridges and drains are being sized
  from a book the sky no longer follows. Would drain on: tables
  rebuilt as moving statistics — mathematics and engineering
  jointly own the repair; weather owns the warning.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- MAKE RAIN WHERE NONE WOULD FALL — the oldest want at the sky;
  the color can nudge a primed cloud by a tenth and nothing else.
  Blocked on the water budget itself: the sun funds evaporation,
  and no ground-side trick has ever replaced the funding.
- STOP OR STEER THE STORM — see Stormfury and the energy books;
  force is certified out at present scale, steering stands open
  and unproven.
- PIN THE WARMING'S EXACT NUMBER — bounded (2.5-4°C per
  doubling), not pinned; blocked on the cloud mathematics; every
  economy-sized decision downstream carries the spread meanwhile.
- TELL EACH FARM ITS SEASON A YEAR AHEAD — delivered today as
  odds, in some regions, in flywheel years; near-nothing in
  continental interiors. The certificate forbids the daily
  version forever; the odds version is honest and still poor.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- The millennia of weather magic — dances, offerings, bells rung
  at storms, cannon fired at clouds: the longest-running failed
  experiment in this fabric, run by every people, because the
  stakes were the harvest and there was nothing else to try. The
  color's warning methods finally answered the want; the rituals
  are acknowledged here as its true cost.
- The storm-warner broken by his misses — the man who founded
  storm warnings (and the word "forecast") was mocked for every
  false alarm by a press that never counted his saves; he took his
  own life in 1865. Warnings were briefly discontinued — then
  restored by demand from the sailors' side of the ledger. Filed:
  the false-alarm price is old, human, and still the tornado
  warning's daily tax.
- Galveston, 1900 — the deadliest disaster in its country's
  history (~6,000-12,000): the storm was seen elsewhere and the
  seeing was dismissed. The satellite era's quiet record — no
  unseen hurricane since the mid-1960s — is this entry's drain,
  and why the color calls the shared picture a precondition.
- Richardson's six weeks of sums — the failure that carried the
  method (SCIENCE holds it). He estimated 64,000 people computing
  in a hall to keep pace with the weather; the hall was built of
  silicon forty years later.
- Stormfury's two decades — the honest arc: bold claim, apparent
  wins, self-audit, retraction. Kept as the subject's model of
  how to abandon a beloved program correctly: it was the
  project's OWN measurements that killed it.
- The almanac industry — still selling the year-ahead daily
  forecast the certificate forbids; scored at chance whenever
  scored. Kept open here because its persistence is itself a
  datum about how this subject's claims get believed.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- SEVERED BY CHOICE: WEATHER ↔ WAR — not disproven, outlawed
  (the 1977 treaty): the only relation in this phylum's record
  cut deliberately rather than by evidence. Filed because a
  severed relation is knowledge too — the cut carries WHY
  (unverifiable effects, unbounded harm).
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: SUNSPOTS ↔ SEASONS — a century of hunted
  correlations that appear and fade. The measured lever is real
  but small: the solar cycle swings the sun's delivery ~0.1%
  (~0.2 W/m² of the color's 238) — enough to seek in averages,
  a hundred times short of steering seasons. Stands dotted:
  regional pathways are claimed, none has held replication yet.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: PRESSURE ↔ ACHING JOINTS (body's door) —
  reported by patients for centuries, near-null in blinded
  trials. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: some
  joints may read the few-hPa swing, and memory does the rest
  (storms are remembered, still days are not — the rain dance's
  arithmetic in miniature). Would drain on: continuous pain
  diaries scored against pressure with the dates blinded.
- BROKEN, THEN REBUILT HONESTLY: FORECAST ↔ PUBLIC TRUST — the
  deterministic overpromise ("it WILL be sunny") broke it, one
  busted certainty at a time. Rebuilt as probability — the
  color's ensemble fan, spoken as odds. The standing lesson,
  filed as relation-knowledge: trust survives misses when the
  odds were stated, and does not when they weren't.
