## THINGS
The units this subject is about; other homes named so a cascade
can branch.

- THE ACT — the unit judged: author, intent, doing, outcome —
  four parts, separately weighable; also: law, story, mind.
- INTENT — the mind behind the act: the same broken body is
  accident, negligence, or murder by it alone; also: law, mind,
  story (the villain is made by motive, not body count).
- HARM — the base coin, the one thing every system agrees needs
  a reason; also: medicine, law, body, conflict.
- CONSENT — the switch turning one act into gift, trade, or
  theft; three parts: knowing, able to refuse, able to change
  your mind; also: law (contract), medicine, people.
- THE SHARE — the divided thing fairness argues over: cake,
  wage, burden, blame; also: economy, play, governance, people.
- THE PROMISE — the future self's freedom spent in advance,
  which is why it works: predictability sold to strangers;
  also: law, economy (a debt has a schedule), belief.
- BLAME AND CREDIT — one coin, two faces, minted only where
  choice was; also: law, teaching, mind.
- PUNISHMENT — one instrument asked to do four jobs (contain,
  deter, repair, settle); also: law, governance, teaching.
- FORGIVENESS — the debt put down, not denied: the account
  closed without being reversed; also: belief, economy.
- RIGHTS AND DUTIES — two ends of one object; every right a
  bill sent to somebody; also: law, governance, economy.
- THE CIRCLE — the roster of who counts as a somebody; a moving
  boundary with receipts; also: story, law, animals, culture.
- THE BRIGHT LINE — a verdict decided in advance, against the
  moment when judging is compromised; also: law, governance.
- THE ROLE — duty worn as a uniform (defense lawyer, soldier),
  justified one level up by the system it serves; also: people,
  law, conflict, medicine.
- GUILT AND SHAME — the inner enforcement pair: guilt aims at
  the act, shame at the self; also: mind, teaching, belief.
- REPUTATION — the public ledger everyone posts to, gossip its
  transport; also: people, economy (credit), language.
- THE INNOCENT — the fenced category: bystander, child, the one
  who did not do it; also: law, conflict (the civilian).
- LUCK — dealer of starting hands and outcomes; desert's quiet
  senior partner; also: play, economy, life.
- THE FUTURE — next Tuesday's self and the people not yet born:
  stakeholders with no voice; also: governance, economy, earth.

## CLAIMS
What holds, written to be tested by a crossing query. Worth is
applicability to the query at hand, never absolute.

- Every society examined lands on "return good for good; do not
  do what you would hate done to you" — found independently,
  continents apart, and older than the species: apes keep the
  grooming ledger; the monkey hurls back the cucumber when its
  neighbor gets grapes for the same task.
- Strip any system to the floor and one item remains: hurting
  someone requires a reason — a rule permitting free injury
  cannot be lived under by the injured, so no group keeps one.
- The innocent are fenced: punishing someone FOR another's act
  needs heavy disguise everywhere — collective punishment and
  hostage-taking are named atrocities even by their own doers.
- Intent changes the act, and two questions ride the word
  "wrong": was the ACT bad, and is this PERSON to blame — the
  correct drug that kills a rare patient is a bad thing done
  blamelessly; the failed poisoner is blameworthy with no bad
  result. Harm aimed at and harm merely foreseen divide the
  same way at identical doses: easing the dying patient's pain
  while shortening life is not the drug given to end it.
- Blame assumes choice: no one blames the avalanche; every
  system excuses what could not be otherwise (infancy, madness,
  coercion), arguing only the line. And not-looking is a choice
  — the deliberately unasked question is judged as knowledge.
- The feel for fair shares arrives early and runs below
  training: toddlers protest rigged splits; adults pay real
  money to punish a nine-to-one division (price in SCIENCE).
  Fair process and fair outcome are separate objects — a
  perfectly fair race can end in a badly unfair pile — and
  same-for-all versus each-what-they-need (identical shoes
  versus shoes that fit) turns on whether the starting gap was
  anybody's doing. Rigged systems persist, at rising cost.
- Desert is real and shallower than it feels: country, parents,
  era, talent, health were dealt; even the capacity for effort
  was partly issued at birth. Credit is blame's coin turned
  over: no crediting the unchosen.
- Every right is a bill sent to somebody else; a right with no
  payer is a wish with good posture.
- Hard-edged duties (do not lie, do not kill) can be met by all,
  forever; open duties (help, give, show up) have no ceiling and
  consume whoever takes them seriously alone. Above the duty
  line sits a real category — good and not required; erase it
  and heroes become clerks, ordinary lives failures.
- Doing is judged more harshly than allowing at identical
  outcomes, everywhere asked: pushing the man feels monstrous,
  the lever tragic, same count. Using a person as equipment is
  refused where the same death in-the-way is accepted — consent
  licenses the use of a person. Bias or truth: the mirror's.
- The moral pull tracks the senses, not the map: the child
  drowning before you commands everything; a thousand far away,
  a donation, maybe. Feeling flattens past a handful; giving
  FALLS as one named victim becomes two (SCIENCE).
- A promise is one person spending another's freedom — the
  future self, who never agreed; the foreclosure is the value.
- The lying rule was never "never lie" — a rule with unspoken
  exceptions kept daily; and the protective lie takes the
  other's decisions away, because choices run on information.
- Loyalty and honesty must eventually meet in one room — the
  friend who did it, the team that covered it — no move
  satisfies both, and the roster ships no tiebreaker. The
  reporter of rot usually pays: the organisation expels the
  reporter, not the rot — which is why most say nothing.
- The golden rule imports your preferences at the edges — the
  lover of surprise parties throws one for someone who dreads
  them; the repair is asking (METHODS).
- Three ways to judge one act — what it produces, what rule it
  breaks, what person does this — are three questions, not
  answers; where they diverge, most stubborn disagreement is
  two people answering different questions. Opened up, most
  moral fights are fact-fights or weight-fights between holders
  of one short list — care for kin, reciprocity, courage,
  fairness, loyalty, deference, purity — in local dress.
- A hypocrite does not refute the rule he breaks: the smoking
  doctor is still right about smoking.
- Bright lines beat judgment exactly where judgment is
  corruptible at the moment of use: "never take a gift from a
  supplier" beats "use your judgment about gifts."
- The slope argument is honest when the line is reasonless, a
  scare when the line has a reason — if nothing distinguishes
  step one from step two, the slide is real; else it is not.
- Sometimes every option is wrong: least-bad is correct and not
  clean — the chooser should feel the weight; feeling nothing
  is misreading, not rising above.
- Guilt says I did a bad thing and pushes toward repair; shame
  says I am a bad thing and pushes toward hiding and attack.
  Shaming mostly manufactures concealment.
- Responsibility divided by twenty does not become one
  twentieth — it evaporates, everyone sincerely innocent; big
  harms are many small paperwork-shaped acts. The more watchers
  the less likely any one acts (numbers in SCIENCE).
- Punishment's four jobs — stop this one, frighten the next,
  repair, settle — demand different sentences for one crime;
  every system picks a mixture and calls it justice. Wherever
  punishment exists, machinery for ENDING it exists: apology,
  restitution, forgiveness, debts that expire — societies that
  only escalate bleed out (SCIENCE). Forgiving is not excusing
  (debt put down versus never owed; a pardon does not declare
  innocence), and justification defends the act while excuse
  defends the person — different doors, different results.
- Yes means less as the power gap widens: the employee's to the
  boss, the patient's to the surgeon — each may be genuine, and
  none can be shown to be.
- An old wrong can outlive everyone involved and still be doing
  work: the arrangement it produced is what people stand on
  this morning, and withheld sums compound (SCIENCE).
- The ticket for counting is the capacity to have a bad time —
  not intelligence, language, or use: why the severely disabled
  count fully, and animals are hard to keep out once said aloud.
- The circle of who counts has widened across recorded history
  — kin to tribe to nation to strangers, now arguing at animals
  — unevenly, with reversals; each old edge later looks
  arbitrary from outside. The winning argument is nearly always
  "they are one of us too": old rule, new roster (HISTORY).
- No pile of facts about what IS adds up to what OUGHT to be;
  the bridge must be built of prescription (SCIENCE).
- Moral verdicts arrive as feelings, reasons drafted after;
  pressed past the reasons, the verdict often stays.
  Deliberation sometimes overrules; how often is the mirror's.
- Some goods are held off the market on purpose: offer a parent
  money for their child and the offence is the pricing itself;
  a relationship priced does not go back.
- Privacy is the room to be unfinished — foolish, ill,
  undecided — off the permanent record. Remove it and people
  become careful, not honest.
- Moral luck stands as a wound: two drunk drivers, identical
  choices, a child steps into one road — one killer, one drove
  home, judged worlds apart, and we cannot stop. Unresolved
  (mirror).

## SCIENCE
The math and working knowledge of right and wrong, held — not
mentioned: LAW to compute with, RULE to follow, WORKED to prove
the rule runs.

### LOGIC — what a moral argument can carry

LAW: no OUGHT from IS — a valid conclusion only unpacks its
  premises; if no premise prescribes, nothing below the line
  may.
RULE: audit any moral argument — list premises, find the ought;
  if invisible, it hides in a word ("natural," "healthy,"
  "efficient"); drag it out and argue about THAT.
WORKED: "this policy raises output 12%, so we must adopt it."
  Premise: +12% (an is). Missing: "more output is owed" (the
  ought). Stated, the argument stands honest or dies; either
  way the 12% stops doing work it never could do alone.
LAW: ought implies can — commanding the impossible commands
  nothing. Before a duty, check performability; before blame,
  check an alternative was open. This rule GENERATES the
  standard excuses rather than merely permitting them.

### GAME ARITHMETIC — why the reciprocity floor is load-bearing

LAW: each turn cooperate or cheat; payoffs order temptation >
  reward > punishment > sucker (take 5 > 3 > 1 > 0). Met once,
  cheating wins both branches (5 beats 3, 1 beats 0). Met again
  with continuation chance w, reciprocity holds when
  w ≥ (5−3)/(5−1).
RULE: to predict honesty in any arena, estimate the chance the
  parties meet again; above threshold the golden rule enforces
  itself, below it expect contracts, courts, and locks.
WORKED: threshold (5−3)/(5−1) = 2/4 = 0.5 — meet again with
  better than a coin flip's chance and cheating stops paying.
  At w = 0.9: two reciprocators bank 3/(1−0.9) = 30 each; a
  cheat takes 5 once then 1 forever: 5 + 0.9×1/0.1 = 14.
  Honesty out-earns better than two to one — why villages (w
  near 1) run politer than highways (w near 0), and why the
  rule recurs on every continent: it is the fixed point of an
  arithmetic every society runs. Guarding against an
  alternating cheat raises the bar to (5−3)/(3−0) = 2/3.

### KIN ARITHMETIC — where the care instinct's dial was set

LAW: aid fires (gene-deep) when relatedness × benefit > cost;
  child or sibling 1/2, grandchild 1/4, cousin 1/8, stranger ~0.
WORKED: rescue costing 1, worth 3 to a sibling: 0.5 × 3 = 1.5 >
  1 — fires. For a stranger: 0 × 3 = 0 < 1 — silent. Two
  siblings balance one self (2 × 1/2 = 1); so do eight cousins
  (8 × 1/8 = 1). The dial's short reach is why the circle
  needed OTHER engines — reciprocity, reputation, story — to
  pass the family fence at all.

### FAIRNESS ARITHMETIC — divide and choose

LAW: one cuts, the other picks: the cutter, cutting even by
  their own measure, guarantees exactly half by their own
  values; the picker takes the bigger by theirs. Both end at
  half or better by their OWN measure; neither can envy.
RULE: the one who divides does not pick; cut so you yourself
  are indifferent between the pieces.
WORKED: a cake, half chocolate, half vanilla. To Ana chocolate
  is worth 75, vanilla 25 (of 100); to Ben the sides are 50/50.
  Ana cuts piece X = all vanilla plus a third of the chocolate:
  25 + 75/3 = 50 to her; piece Y = two-thirds chocolate = 50 to
  her. To Ben, X = 50 + 50/3 ≈ 66.7, Y ≈ 33.3 — he picks X.
  Ana holds 50 of her 100, Ben 66.7 of his: both at half or
  better, no envy — and the felt total is 116.7, not 100.
  DISAGREEMENT about worth is a resource the procedure
  harvests.
RULE, extended: three can still split envy-free (a known
  procedure, more cuts); in turns the disadvantaged picks
  first; an indivisible thing is auctioned inside the group and
  the money divided.

### PROPORTION ARITHMETIC — the feud series

LAW: each side repays injuries at multiplier m; after n
  exchanges the blow is m^n times the first.
WORKED: repay at half again (m = 1.5), ten exchanges: 1.5² =
  2.25 · 1.5⁴ = 5.06 · 1.5⁸ = 25.63 · 1.5¹⁰ ≈ 57.7 — a small
  insult grown fifty-eight-fold; that is "bled out by feud" in
  numbers. At m = 1 the series stands still: an eye FOR an eye,
  read correctly, is a CAP, not an appetite. At m = 0 it ends —
  forgiveness is the only terminator. Third-party courts take
  the exponent out of the injured hands.

### THE PRICE OF FAIRNESS — measured

LAW: in the take-it-or-leave-it split, offers under ~30% of the
  pot are refused about half the time, across every culture
  tested — and refusal costs the refuser everything.
WORKED: $10 pot, $2 offered. Refusal burns the refuser's $2 to
  burn the offerer's $8 — four dollars of punishment per dollar
  paid — and people pay it. The fair-shares feeling has a
  measured price above zero; a split ignoring it buys
  enforcement trouble at that exchange rate.

### SCALE, DISTANCE, AND THE CROWD — feeling failing to count

LAW: felt concern flattens past a handful; giving FALLS as one
  identified victim becomes two. Help falls as watchers rise:
  ~85% act alone, ~62% with one other present, ~31% with four.
RULE: convert crowd duty into named duty — "you, in the red
  coat, call for help"; assign roles before the emergency; give
  every decision one accountable name.
WORKED: the cheapest verified saving of a life runs ~$3,000–
  5,500 (malaria nets). Ruining $200 shoes for the child
  drowning in front of you: instant yes. The same $200 sent
  far: 200 ÷ 4,000 = 1/20 of a life saved — same money, same
  arithmetic, opposite pull. The correction is an instrument
  (measurement), never a stronger feeling.

### THE PUBLIC ARITHMETIC OF LIVES

LAW: public bodies price a statistical life at ~$10M so safety
  purchases can be compared at all.
WORKED: a $50M program expected to save 8 lives: 8 × 10 = $80M
  of protection for $50M — build. The same $50M saving 2:
  2 × 10 = $20M — the money saves more lives elsewhere. The
  number is not a person's worth; it is the exchange rate
  between two ways of buying the same protection — refusing to
  compute it also chooses, blindly.

### THE COMPOUND LEDGER — old wrongs at current value

LAW: a withheld sum compounds: value = principal × (1+r)^years.
WORKED: $100 of stolen wages, 150 years at 5%: 1.05¹⁰ = 1.63 ·
  1.05⁴⁰ = 7.04 · 1.05⁸⁰ = 49.6 · 1.05¹⁵⁰ ≈ 1,508 — about
  $150,800 today. "Long ago" makes the claim LARGER, not
  smaller. The true difficulties are real too — evidence decays
  and parties die as the sum grows — and both go into the
  verdict; neither cancels the other.

## METHODS
How judging and repairing are done — what governs each, and
what breaks it.

- THE THREE-LENS PASS: run the act through outcomes, rules,
  character. Agreement: settled. Divergence: you have found
  WHERE the argument lives — most of resolving it. Breaks when
  one lens is silently treated as the whole.
- DIVIDE-AND-CHOOSE AND FAMILY: the cutter does not pick
  (SCIENCE); turns with the loser picking first; indivisibles
  auctioned inside the group.
- THE VEIL: design the split before knowing which share is
  yours. Breaks when the designer secretly knows the seat — use
  it on standing rules, not one-off grabs.
- THE UNIVERSALIZATION TEST: what if everyone did it — a move
  that works only while rare (the jumped queue, the lie told
  into general honesty) is spending a commons others maintain.
- THE PUBLICITY TEST: would the reason survive being said aloud
  to the face of the person affected? One that must stay secret
  from its subject is usually an excuse in a suit.
- ASKING (the golden rule's repair): treat others as THEY would
  be treated — your own mind as template is cheap, fast, wrong
  at the edges; the question costs less than the error.
- THE CONSENT CHECK: verify all three parts — knew, could
  refuse, can revoke — then check the power gradient. Any part
  failing, the yes does not license.
- FACTS-OR-WEIGHTS TRIAGE: find which fight this is — what will
  happen (bring evidence) or how much each value counts (bring
  trade-offs) — before opening your mouth.
- BRIGHT LINES SET IN ADVANCE: where the judging moment
  corrupts the judge (gifts, conflicts, the drink before
  driving), adopt the absolute rule beforehand — a promise made
  against the moment of temptation. Breaks when lines multiply
  until judgment atrophies (mirror).
- THE SLOPE AUDIT: name what distinguishes step one from step
  two. Named reason: the slope stops — say so. None: the slide
  is real, and the first step is the decision.
- BLAME ACCOUNTING: keep four verdicts separate — act wrong;
  agent blameworthy; harm aimed or foreseen; justification or
  excuse. Most miscarriages are two of these swapped.
- SIZING PUNISHMENT: name which of the four jobs the sentence
  serves before setting it. Quarantine is the clean case:
  containment with no blame in it.
- THE REPAIR SEQUENCE: name the act without "if"; size
  restitution to the harm; request — never demand — the debt be
  put down. Let some debts expire on purpose so the game
  restarts (bankruptcy, made law).
- POINTING AT ONE: convert diffuse duty to named duty — the
  first-aid rule and the single accountable signature are one
  repair for one evaporation.
- THE WILFUL-BLINDNESS CHECK: what would a reasonable search
  have found, and was not-searching chosen? A search not run is
  a result not obtained, and both parties know it.
- CARRYING DIRTY HANDS: choose the least bad and KEEP the
  weight — record what was traded away so the next chooser
  inherits the ledger, not a clean story.
- WIDENING ON PURPOSE: make the far present — trade, travel,
  story manufacture the meeting; the winning argument is "they
  are one of us too." Breaks when forced faster than the
  meeting can happen (mirror holds the receipts).

## MEANS
What the methods run on.

- THE PROMISE, CONTRACT, WITNESS: trust made portable to
  strangers; writing raises the cost of denial; the biggest
  promises get the most witnesses on purpose.
- REPUTATION AND GOSSIP: the ledger everyone posts to — the
  cheapest policing a group owns; how reciprocity's arithmetic
  reaches strangers met once.
- THE OATH AND RITUAL: commitment made expensive and public;
  the form survives where the watching gods have thinned.
- CODES AND LISTS: commandments, professional codes, statutes —
  rules made portable and heir-proof; what cannot be written
  into a claim cannot be inherited.
- COURTS AND THIRD PARTIES: verdict and sentence taken OUT of
  the injured hands — the feud exponent's removal, built as an
  institution.
- THE APOLOGY: cheap words made costly by status genuinely
  paid — the price is exactly what makes it information.
- STORY: the presence machine — strangers made feelable;
  fiction admits people to the roster before the law does.
- GUILT: enforcement installed inside the actor, cheaper than
  police; aim it at acts, never selves, or it turns to shame.
- BRIGHT LINES: the contract signed with your own tempted self,
  in advance, while you were still the judge.
- MEASURING INSTRUMENTS: statistics against the single face,
  cost-per-life against vividness — correcting an eye that
  flattens past a handful.
- MONEY AS RESTITUTION — and its fence: repairs the fungible,
  stops at the sacred, where pricing is itself the injury.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject answers — the doors a query enters by.

- LIVE TOGETHER WITHOUT BLEEDING OUT: cap the feud multiplier,
  build the exits — the oldest purpose, and the arithmetic's.
- LICENSE AND LIMIT HARM: keep "hurting needs a reason" running
  as everyone's floor.
- DIVIDE GAINS AND BURDENS ACCEPTABLY: shares that hold without
  constant enforcement.
- MAKE STRANGERS COOPERABLE: promises, reputation, contracts —
  trust past the kin instinct's reach.
- LAND BLAME ONLY WHERE CHOICE WAS: correction reaches the
  correctable; the avalanche gets engineering, not jail.
- PROTECT THE INNOCENT AND THE VOICELESS: bystander, child,
  animal, the not-yet-born.
- END WHAT PUNISHMENT STARTS: apology, restitution, expiry,
  pardon — the exit as designed-in as the penalty.
- ANSWER THE LONE CHOOSER: "what should I do," with lenses,
  tests, and lines prepositioned.
- KEEP THE CIRCLE HONESTLY DRAWN — holding the receipts of
  every past widening, because each old edge looked natural
  inside.

## HISTORY
How the knowledge arose, in what order, at what cost.

- Reciprocity before the species: the grooming ledger and the
  grape-and-cucumber protest live in other primates; the gift
  ledger predates writing everywhere writing arrived to record
  it. Cost unknown, paid before anyone could file it.
- Proportion written early: ~1750 BCE the eye-for-an-eye rule
  is carved as law — in its time a CEILING on revenge, the feud
  multiplier capped at one by statute.
- Intent graded early: the oldest codes and the refuge cities
  separate the accidental killer from the murderer, millennia
  before anyone wrote "guilty mind."
- The golden rule found independently: ~500 BCE in China (the
  do-NOT phrasing), in Leviticus, in Hillel, in the
  Mahabharata, in the Gospels; nearly every scripture carries
  both phrasings. The convergence is the receipt: it is what
  the repeated-game arithmetic looks like solved blind.
- The three lenses named late, each arriving as a claimed
  WHOLE: character ~350 BCE, duty 1785, outcomes 1789 — three
  total systems that fought for two centuries and settled into
  three questions asked of one act. The surpassed totalizers
  STAY: to some query each is still the best door in.
- The is-ought line drawn 1739: the audit rule every later
  argument runs under.
- The circle's receipts, unevenly paid: slave trade banned
  1807, slavery in that empire 1833, in the US 1865; the vote
  for women 1893 (NZ), 1920 (US), 1928 (UK, full), 1944
  (France), 1971 (Switzerland); first animal-cruelty statute
  1822; the universal declaration 1948. Direction observed,
  speed wildly uneven, reversals real (mirror).
- "Following orders" struck 1945–46: diffusion of
  responsibility met law built to re-assemble split authorship
  — command responsibility, conspiracy — and the 1947 consent
  code made "knowing, free to refuse, revocable" the standard.
- Fairness entered the laboratory: the take-it-or-leave-it
  split 1982; the capuchin refusal 2003; toddler protest
  measured — the instinct became data with prices (SCIENCE).
- Cooperation's math arrived 1980: in open tournament the
  simple strategy — open kind, repay in kind, forgive after
  repayment — beat every clever entry. The golden rule received
  its arithmetic twenty-five centuries after its wording.
- The veil formalized 1971; the trolley cases mapped
  doing/allowing and tool/in-the-way from 1967 on — verdicts
  charted, the function behind them still unwritten (mirror).
