## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or
cannot be under stated conditions.

- A PROOF OF ETHICS FROM PURE LOGIC — attempted by the greats,
  each announcing completion: duty derived from reason alone,
  outcomes declared self-evident, the contract derived from
  self-interest. Each stalls at one wall: the is-ought audit
  finds a prescriptive premise smuggled in, or a conclusion
  carrying no prescription out. OPEN, not cannot. Nothing
  certain — but here is a possibility: the ought may live in the
  preconditions of arguing at all — whoever demands a proof
  already treats truth and the other arguer as counting; tried
  in part, grips in part. Would drain on: a derivation whose
  every premise a consistent amoralist must accept.
- THE COMPLETE MORAL CALCULUS — feed in any act, read out the
  verdict. Attempted (the felicific arithmetic, 1789 on).
  FAILED at two joints: no unit adds one person's pain to
  another's gain (SCIENCE below), and the three lenses return
  different verdicts BY DESIGN. Would drain on: the unit being
  found — the lens divergence would still stand.
- THE MACHINE THAT BEARS DUTIES — newly open, filed honestly.
  Machines now act where blame used to land: they drive, advise
  sentences, fire. Blame assumes choice, and whether a trained
  system "could have done otherwise" is unsettled in BOTH
  directions — no test separates a chooser from a very fast
  mechanism, and the same missing test bedevils free will in
  humans. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: locate
  the duty one level up, as role morality already does — maker,
  deployer, checker — the way law gave corporations personhood
  without finding a soul in the filing cabinet. Would drain on:
  a working could-have-done-otherwise test, for machine or man.
- THE DIRECT CLAIM OF THE FEEL-LESS — a river, a species, a
  not-yet-possible person: stakes with no capacity for a bad
  time. The sentience ticket refuses them direct entry; law has
  begun admitting some anyway (rivers given standing).
  Unsettled: real claims, or guardianships in costume. Would
  drain on: a stated theory under the statutes.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "HARSHER PUNISHMENT DETERS PROPORTIONALLY MORE" — measured
  weak: certainty and swiftness of being caught move behavior;
  added severity moves it little. WHY believed: the feud
  instinct prices deterrence in pain; severity serves
  score-settling, then bills itself as deterrence.
- "CHARACTER HOLDS REGARDLESS OF SITUATION" — dented: ~65% of
  ordinary volunteers obeyed instructions to (believed)
  dangerous shocks; the situation moved more than character
  predicted. UNSURE at the edges — replications argued — so
  dented, not dead: predict from the room AND the person.
- "THE YOUNG ARE MORALLY WORSE" — claimed in writing for
  2,500+ years, each generation; the collapse never
  accumulates. WHY: each cohort mistakes the roster's shifting
  for decay of the floor. Kept because queries still carry it.
- UNSETTLED: DOING VERSUS ALLOWING — bias or truth? The verdict
  gap at identical outcomes is measured and universal; whether
  it marks a real moral joint or a bookkeeping error of the
  mind is the live argument. Would drain on: a case set where
  the gap survives every framing correction, or dissolves.
- UNSETTLED: MORAL LUCK — identical choices, verdicts worlds
  apart; named and left open 1976, closed by nobody. Nothing
  certain — but here is a possibility: two ledgers forced into
  one — the world's account of damage, the mind's account of
  choice — and the refusal to separate them may be load-bearing:
  sentencing intent alone would try only minds, and evidence of
  minds is the weakest there is. Would drain on: such a system
  shown livable.
- UNSETTLED: WHY MORAL PROGRESS IS UNEVEN — the circle widened
  at wildly different speeds, with real reversals; trade,
  story, prosperity, meeting-the-other all correlate, none
  predicts. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: the
  circle widens where admitting the newcomer got CHEAP —
  security and surplus lower the price of "one of us," and
  meeting presents the bill as payable; reversals then follow
  scarcity and fear on schedule. Would drain on: a predictor
  tested against the recorded reversals, not only the advances.
- UNSETTLED: WHICH IS SENIOR, FLASH OR DELIBERATION — verdicts
  arrive as feelings, reasons drafted after; slow thought
  sometimes overrules; how often, and which errs more, is
  unmeasured. Would drain on: error ledgers for both channels
  against later-settled cases.
- UNSETTLED: THE BYSTANDER NUMBERS' STRENGTH — direction
  reliable, size argued; the color carries ~85/62/31 marked
  rough. Would drain on: large preregistered counts.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A UNIT — no interpersonal unit of harm or good.
  Ethics can rank one person's options and cannot ADD two
  people's stakes without a chosen weight; every offered
  calculus smuggles its weights. The largest named absence: the
  arithmetic stops at the boundary of a skull.
- LACKING: THE CONSCIENCE FUNCTION — the trolley mapping
  charted the verdicts, not the function producing them; no
  written law predicts the felt verdict for a NEW case.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: BIOLOGY IMPORTED AS OUGHT — fitness read
  as worth, the is-ought gap ignored at the scale of states
  (the eugenic programs), paid in horror. Lesson: a fact-shaped
  ought is the most dangerous smuggle — the fact half is
  checkable and lends its credit to the half that never was.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE CRUDE SUM OVER PERSONS — run it and
  it licenses taking the healthy patient apart for five; nearly
  everyone refuses the answer. Filed as data: the refusal
  measures what the sum left out (consent licensing the use of
  a person); any future calculus must carry that term or break
  here again.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- DERIVING RIGHT FROM NATURE — "natural = good," tried forever;
  nature holds infanticide and parasitism with the same
  equanimity as care. Works ONLY where "natural" was pre-loaded
  with the chosen values — the smuggle in a gardener's coat.
  Certified wherever "nature" means what happens; no drain.
- VIRTUE BY TOTAL WATCHING — surveillance as character machine.
  CANNOT BE under its own condition: watched people behave for
  the watcher; remove the unfinished room and people become
  careful, not honest. The condition IS the mechanism, so no
  gentler camera drains it.
- SHAME AS THE CORRECTOR — stocks then, pile-ons now. Fails by
  the color's own law: shame aims at the self and manufactures
  hiding, denial, counterattack. Drained in the narrow:
  guilt-aimed correction (name the act, keep the person) is
  written in the color.
- THE FEUD AS JUSTICE — repayment above one-for-one, run by the
  injured; the geometric series damns it (color: 1.5× for ten
  rounds ≈ 58× the insult). Drained by three inventions now in
  the color — the cap at one, the third-party court, the
  forgiveness terminator. Filed as the mother of all three.
- BRIGHT LINES EVERYWHERE — zero tolerance generalized. The
  line works ONLY where the judging moment corrupts the judge;
  spread past that condition it convicts the schoolchild's
  aspirin and atrophies the judgment it was built to protect.
- GOODNESS BY DECREE, AT SPEED — remaking the value roster
  top-down faster than any circle widens; the attempts fill
  history's worst pages, each requiring terror in proportion to
  its hurry. Condition: value change outrunning its own
  meeting-machinery (trade, story, presence) reverts or is held
  by force. Would drain on: a documented counterexample; none
  is filed.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- NOT YET FOUND: A SUFFERING-READER — no instrument reads a bad
  time from outside. Newborns went unmedicated on this failure
  until the ticket was thought through; animal claims still
  argue in the dark. Would drain on: a measurable correlate of
  felt-bad accepted across species — the instrument this
  subject wants most.
- NOT YET FOUND: A CONSENT-VERIFIER FOR GRADIENTS — no
  procedure shows a yes was free when refusing had a price; the
  color says genuine and unprovable in one breath. Would drain
  on: a protocol whose yes and no cost the same.
- FELL SHORT: THE OATH — bound the inner ledger while the gods
  watched; secular replacements enforce the outside and reach
  the inside less. Kept where it works: the public promise
  before witnesses runs on reputation arithmetic, no gods
  needed.
- FELL SHORT: MONEY AS FULL REPAIR — repairs the fungible,
  insults the sacred; at the fence (child, verdict, vote) the
  pricing IS the injury. How to repair the unpriceable at all
  stands open.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- SETTLE THE LENSES' DIVERGENCE — the fabric can locate where
  outcome, rule, and character part ways; it cannot adjudicate
  which is senior. Blocked on the missing unit and the open
  proof above.
- GIVE THE FUTURE A WEIGHTED VOICE — every discount rate is an
  ethics smuggled as arithmetic: at 3% a year a life 200 years
  out counts 1.03²⁰⁰ ≈ 369 times less than one today — about a
  quarter of one percent — while 0% paralyzes the present under
  an infinite queue of heirs. No principled rate is written;
  choosing the rate IS the unanswered want.
- MAKE GOODNESS CHEAP AT SCALE — the drowning-child gap stands:
  feeling will not scale, arithmetic will not move hands. The
  want: the far child's presence at the near child's price;
  story does it retail, nothing does it wholesale.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side.

- The proof attempts of the greats: each total system announced
  the machine finished — duty alone, outcomes alone, character
  alone — and each settled into one lens of three. Not waste:
  the color's three-lens pass is built FROM the wreckage, each
  wreck contributing the question it answered best.
- The eugenic programs: the is-ought smuggle run by states, the
  gap's warning written in the century's worst ink; the color's
  audit rule carries this receipt behind it.
- Virtue by statute, the temperance case: a rule the governed
  did not carry bred contempt plus a black market, and was
  rolled back — law can lead the circle a step, not a mile.
- The obedience experiments: the finding dented fixed
  character; the COST to the subjects became the consent rules
  for experiments themselves — a white entry feeding the color
  directly, the trying improving the rules for trying.
- Moral luck, named 1976 and left open on purpose — the
  corpus's cleanest case of a wound filed honestly instead of
  bandaged with a theory that would not hold.

  The relation still carries ritual, witness, and the sacred
  fence; it no longer carries the foundation.
