# PEOPLE — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/people/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. Seed authored by Claude from the same six source files named
in the color. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE COMMUNITY BUILT BY DESIGN (the utopia) — attempted many
  times, with receipts (METHODS holds the ledger). The want: a
  society drawn on paper and lived in after, without ladders or
  the gift-ledger's grudges. What is real so far is the trying.
- THE PANICKING MOB — long believed real and planned for, found
  NOT to exist where looked: in disasters people mostly queue,
  share, and dig neighbors out. The believed unit did damage
  anyway — warnings withheld for fear of it (HISTORY holds the
  dead). It governed policy for a century without existing.
- THE AVERAGE PERSON — exists in the rates and never walks into a
  room; the group law is made OF averaged-away variation, so its
  subject cannot be met. CLAIMS holds the arithmetic.
- THE WHOLE CULTURE IN ONE HEAD — cannot be under the condition
  that makes it a culture: a shared practice is spread across
  many people; no member holds all of it, no line neatly encloses
  it. Every "the X people believe" is an edit. Drains only where
  the practice shrinks to one holder — and then the edit and the
  whole coincide.
- STANDING BOUGHT WITH MONEY — fails because standing is granted
  by an audience obeying its own rules, not the market's (the
  rich family nobody invites). Would drain on: an audience whose
  regard is actually for sale — and then what is bought is that
  regard, worth its price.

## CLAIMS
What didn't hold — with why — and what is still unsettled.

- "A CROWD IS ITS MEMBERS, SUMMED" — false: packed and aroused,
  individuals sync and do what none would alone. The opposite
  error fell too: "the disaster crowd is a selfish stampede" —
  measured false; helping is the rule.
- "GRIEVANCE EXPLAINS THE MOVEMENT" — false: grievances are
  everywhere, movements rare; the missing terms were supply
  (networks, money, safe rooms, divided authority) and the frame
  (name, culprit, meetable demand).
- "UNEQUAL ORDERS STAND ON FORCE" — false as stated: they stand
  on accounts of desert largely accepted even at the bottom;
  force is the expensive fallback.
- "ASK THEM AND YOU KNOW WHAT THEY DO" — broken: people describe
  the version they would defend; the say/do gap is where the
  exceptions live. Any method that only asks inherits it whole.
- "THE SIMPLE SOCIETY IS A WINDOW INTO THE PAST" — false: no
  living people are anybody's ancestors; every line alive has run
  the same length of time.
- "NO EVIDENCE OF IT" (from the ground) — nearly always "not dug
  there yet": the excavated fraction is pinpricks, and one new
  site regularly moves a date a thousand years.
- CERTIFIED CANNOT: PREDICTING THE PERSON FROM THE GROUP LAW —
  the insurance asymmetry. Conditions: the rate's steadiness is
  bought by 1/√n, and the gain runs only upward. 10,000 houses at
  1-in-10: the year's claims known to ~±3% before it starts;
  which house burns, not at all — and no bigger n helps, since n
  grows the crowd, not the person. Drains only by a DIFFERENT
  instrument: that person's own conditions, measured. A rate is a
  fact about everybody that is a fact about nobody.
- CERTIFIED CANNOT: CLOSENESS WHILE KEEPING THE WEAPON — being
  known requires handing over the material for the wound, and
  disclosure cannot be recalled. Privilege (lawyer, doctor,
  confessional) makes the exposure survivable, never avoidable.
- CERTIFIED CANNOT: A SIGNAL BOTH FREE AND TRUSTWORTHY — cheap
  talk is priced at its cost. Certified under "faking costs
  nothing"; drains locally wherever an institution makes lying
  expensive (perjury law, audited books) — which is what those
  institutions are for.
- CERTIFIED CANNOT: READING MEANING OFF USE — use leaves marks
  (wear, layers, receipts); meaning does not. "Ritual object" is
  the confession "we cannot see what this was for" in a finding's
  clothes. Belief leaves the thinnest trace of anything people do.
- UNSETTLED: WHY FASHION CYCLES — styles arrive, peak, fade
  steadily enough that pots are dated by the drift, one way,
  never doubling back — and no written law says WHY. Nothing
  certain — but here is a possibility: the chase of position runs
  it — a marker of standing dies by being copied, so the top must
  abandon it as fast as the middle arrives; copying error alone
  would drift but not RUN. Would drain on: a predictor of the
  next turn, tested ahead of it, beating chance.
- UNSETTLED: WHY THE US/THEM TRIGGER IS HAIRLINE — shirt color
  suffices, within minutes. That it fires is measured; why so
  cheap is not. Possibility: the sorting machinery predates the
  stakes and runs on any handle because handles were once always
  real. Would drain on: a condition found that fails to trip it.
- UNSETTLED: BLOOD VERSUS TEACHING IN WHAT PASSES DOWN — the
  temper, the trade, the distrust of banks: heredity carries
  some, the relay most; which carries what is centuries old and
  still live. Would drain on: lives where the channels split —
  the child raised off the bloodline shows what travels with the
  raising, the separated twins what travels without it.
- UNSETTLED: THE CEILING'S EXACT NUMBER — the break is
  everywhere; the number (~100-200) wobbles by group and era. The
  link square says a break MUST come; nothing says exactly where.
  Possibility: the spot is set by the mind's tracking budget
  (RELATIONS holds the dotted line). Would drain on: the break
  point moving with a measured tracking capacity.
- UNSETTLED: THE SIZE OF LONELINESS'S BODILY BILL — direction
  well replicated; mortality argued in the range of heavy
  smoking; size still argued. Would drain on: measures separating
  isolation from everything isolation travels with.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A LAW OF THE SINGLE PERSON — the phylum computes
  crowds and holds only claims about persons. The gap is
  structural (the certified cannot): what is lacking is not a
  bigger sample but a different instrument — the person's own
  conditions. Until then every individual prediction is a
  possibility offered, never a certainty.
- LACKING: THE FASHION ARROW'S LAW — the one-way drift is used
  daily without its why; the possibility in CLAIMS awaits test.
- LACKING: AN INSTRUMENT READING TRUST BEFORE IT IS TESTED — the
  balance shows only what it survives. Institutions substitute
  records for the missing gauge, at the price of every bought
  guarantee.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: CROWD DOCTRINE — a century of planning
  built on the panicking mob; warnings withheld to prevent a
  stampede that was never coming, deaths blamed on the crowd. Not
  too little science — a wrong claim held with confidence.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE RAW SURVEY — counts read as the country
  when they were the office: crime rising with easier reporting,
  answers aimed at the asker, wording half the result. Repaired
  only by carrying the procedure inside the number.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE TIMELESS PORTRAIT — one year's
  description read forever as how those people always were, while
  the community was mid-epidemic, mid-trade, mid-border. Every
  account stops the clock; the stopping must be declared.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- UTOPIAS BY DESIGN — the tried ledger, with receipts. Attempts:
  property abolished, family abolished, ladders legislated flat,
  duties assigned by plan. Record (~): of the counted designed
  communes of the last two centuries, most secular ones dissolved
  within about a decade; those lasting generations charged heavy
  entry costs — property signed over, dress, diet, celibacy — the
  costly-signal law arriving uninvited. The celibate
  best-survivors then ran the other arithmetic down: no children
  plus drying recruitment runs any membership to zero on schedule
  (several thousand at peak, near none now). WHY the failures
  repeat: the designs treated ladder and ledger as habits to
  delete, and both are load-bearing — the ladder reformed
  underground, and with the ledger outlawed the care work still
  fell to whoever could least bear it undone, now without even
  the debt being ownable. Would drain on: a design pricing the
  ladder and ledger IN — many rungs, debts nameable — instead of
  legislating them away.
- FEAR AS TEACHING — terror teaches, thoroughly, how not to be
  caught; the rule is not transmitted. Condition: total
  surveillance, which never is. Its cousin fell with it: the rare
  terrible penalty loses to the small certain one — long odds
  make heavy punishment a bet worth taking. Drains to: small
  consequences arriving every time.
- BUYING THE INNER RINGS — paying family for what the household
  ledger carried: the stated figure converts relation to account,
  permanently, whether or not the money returns. Money is the
  stranger-ring's instrument; carried inward it dissolves what it
  touches. Gifts survive where loans do not — a gift refuses to
  settle.
- AUTHORITY WITHOUT BOND — the stepparent or parachuted manager:
  rules from someone the child has no bond with read as trespass.
  The working order — friendly adult a long time, the bonded
  parent holding the rules — is slower than everybody wants;
  every faster route failed on one condition: legitimacy is not
  conferred by paperwork.
- SILENCE AS DAMAGE CONTROL — where an event matters and the
  account is thin, people build one from fragments: silence does
  not leave a blank, it commissions a story. Rumor fills by
  importance times ambiguity; the only drain is the account.
- WELCOMING EVERYONE, NEEDING NOBODY — produces attendance, not
  members: a group that misses nobody binds nobody. Drains on: a
  task in the newcomer's hands within the week.
- ARGUING THE TOPIC — in close pairs, where two thirds of
  conflicts are structural, winning the topic settles nothing;
  only the manner carries the future. Drains to: repair as the
  practiced skill.
- EXHORTING THE FIRST MOVER — asking for volunteers ignores that
  the sum points the wrong way at the front (cost certain and
  private, benefit shared and uncertain). Drains to: assurance —
  thresholds gathered where each can watch the count reach their
  number — and to rails already built.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- DESIGN A SOCIETY PEOPLE STAY IN — open behind its receipts; the
  offered possibility is in METHODS: price the laws in, don't
  delete them.
- ABOLISH THE LADDER — every attempt regrew one underground.
  Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: multiply ladders —
  many games, many rungs, so no single rung prices a person;
  villages with church, band, garden, and league run a rough
  draft.
- MAKE STRANGERS TRUST LIKE KIN, WITHOUT THE OVERHEAD — every
  known bridge (contract, license, audit, record) charges for the
  missing repeat-dealing; the bridge at kin prices stands open.
  Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: shrink the world
  instead of policing it — arrangements that make the same
  strangers meet again raise the odds-of-meeting-again itself,
  and the trust arithmetic does the rest.
- END LONELINESS AT CITY SCALE — the city's freedom and its
  indifference are one arrangement (partial relationships, by the
  link law); the want must beat arithmetic, not unkindness. Best
  standing possibility: the schedule-and-need machinery.
- PREDICT THE PERSON — filed under the certified cannot; wanted
  daily by every hirer, lender, parent, judge; answerable only as
  possibility-with-conditions.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side.

- The communes' two centuries: hundreds of designed societies,
  most gone in a decade, survivors selected by exactly the
  costly-commitment law their founders did not write. The color's
  ladder-and-ledger claims are cheap to state because these tries
  paid for them.
- The crowd doctrine's dead: warnings delayed and information
  withheld by officials applying a false claim in good faith, the
  harm billed to a crowd that had been helping. The bluntest
  lesson this phylum holds: a wrong claim, institutionally held,
  kills.
- The digs that destroyed their own pages: early excavation ran
  on treasure-rules — the object lifted, the ground that held the
  information discarded unread. The discipline (record
  everything, because reading destroys) is that loss, learned.
- Each generation dug up its own arguments: when empire was the
  question, prehistory filled with conquests; when trade was, the
  same finds became exchange networks. The ground never moved —
  the standing caution wherever evidence is thin.
- The say/do gap's long bill: rules collected in interviews and
  published as practice for decades, until long presence showed
  the two never match; paid again in every survey since read raw.
