## THINGS
The units this subject is about; other homes named so a cascade
can branch.

- THE TIE — one relationship: built by repeated exchange, callable
  in need; also: economy (savings held in people), life, mind.
- THE GROUP — people bound into an us; grows a ladder and a
  boundary within minutes; also: conflict, animals (flock, pack).
- TRUST — the working currency: builds in drops, drains in
  buckets, compounds where dealings repeat; also: economy (credit
  means "he believes"), law (a contract is trust written down for
  strangers), medicine (the white coat is borrowed trust).
- THE LEDGER — the felt account of favors owed; every society
  keeps it, apes keep it too. Its courier is REPUTATION, carried
  by talk about the absent; also: economy (money is the ledger
  made portable and exact; a credit score is institutional
  gossip), ethics (reciprocity's oldest floor), computing.
- THE LADDER — standing in a group, tracked with an animal's
  vigilance; also: animals (the hen yard had pecking orders
  first), economy (luxury goods are purchasable rungs), conflict
  (armies write the ladder on the sleeve).
- THE ROLE — a bundle of expectations attached to a position,
  held by everybody else; none exists alone (no boss without
  staff); also: law (rights and duties, two sides), story.
- THE INSTITUTION — a habit that got out of its holders: roles,
  rituals, rules recruiting new humans into old shapes while the
  people flow through. Its small change is the NORM, a rule
  nobody wrote, enforced by a look; also: law (the court outlives
  every judge), language (grammar, an institution nobody runs).
- THE FAMILY — the first economy: who must feed whom, who may
  marry, who inherits, who avenges — the oldest tax system, army
  roster, and will; the household is the unit that actually eats,
  works, saves, decides; also: economy, law, farming.
- THE CROWD — packed, aroused, synced; not a large person; also:
  physics (phase change is the rhyme: same molecules, different
  substance), economy (a crash is a crowd-surge with tickers).
- THE RITUAL — a visible shared act coordinating people without
  inspecting their minds; also: belief, music, teaching.
- THE NETWORK — thick bonds that carry you, thin ties that change
  your life, gaps whose bridgers hold quiet power; also:
  computing (choke points), economy (the middleman's function).
- THE BOUNDARY — the us/them line, drawn on any handle offered,
  maintained while everything inside drifts; also: conflict (the
  first act of every war is redrawing "them"), language.
- THE LEAVINGS — what people deposit without meaning to: layers,
  rubbish heaps, worn tools, graves, kin words, chanted lines —
  the readable record of unwritten lives; also: earth (layers as
  calendar), chemistry (decay sorts survival), craft.

## CLAIMS
What holds, written to be tested by a crossing query.

- People sort into us and them on any handle — shirt color will
  do — within minutes; warmth flows inward, suspicion outward.
  Why the trigger is hairline is unsettled (mirror).
- Every lasting group grows a ladder, and most social pain is
  rung-pain; contempt wounds beyond its words because it is a
  public demotion.
- Trust builds in drops and drains in buckets — one betrayal
  outweighs a hundred kept promises. Honest bookkeeping, not
  oversensitivity: SCIENCE holds the arithmetic.
- Favors demand return; the ledger is felt in the body. A gift is
  a trade with the clock taken out — the open, unpriced debt IS
  the relationship being made. Giving runs in three rings: in the
  household nobody counts, among neighbors the books balance over
  months, with strangers each takes what it can.
- Gossip is the group's immune system: talk about the absent
  spreads reputation, prices betrayal, polices what force cannot.
- A crowd is not a large person: individuals shed self-monitoring
  and sync. Yet in disasters people mostly help — queue, share,
  dig neighbors out; the panicking mob was largely the officials'
  picture (mirror holds that failed claim and its dead).
- People match the room — most will doubt their own eyes rather
  than stand alone against a unanimous group; one ally breaks the
  spell. Whether a room tips depends less on beliefs than on how
  thresholds line up (SCIENCE runs both).
- Size changes substance: past a hundred-odd, everyone-knows-
  everyone breaks and rules must replace relationships; each
  growth trades warmth for structure. The link law forces it.
- The pattern is in positions, not people: a school or an army
  keeps its shape while every member is replaced. A role is held
  by everybody else, so it can pull two ways at once (the nurse
  told to be efficient and kind) — structural strain, not
  personal. Every job keeps a front performance and a back room
  where it is dropped and repaired; both are necessary.
- Deviance is decided by the group, not the act — the same
  killing is murder, war, or duty. Being named is worse than
  caught: a label outranks its holder and closes the doors that
  would have disproven it.
- Nobody learns their society's rules as rules; they arrive as
  what food is, what work is for, and harden into preferences —
  durable exactly because invisible. Your own arrangements never
  feel like arrangements; another people's arrive pre-marked
  strange. Each custom has two honest accounts — what it means
  inside, what it does from outside — and neither replaces the
  other.
- Culture travels down the arm, not in the blood: everything must
  be re-handed each generation. A way of life is a relay.
- Standing and money are separate currencies running opposite
  ways at will: standing is granted by an audience and obeys the
  audience's rules, not the market's. Class is a line (own what
  makes the money, or sell hours to it) plus equipment — which
  fork, how to talk to a doctor — learned at home, read as
  competence at the door.
- Close friends carry you; distant acquaintances change your
  life — openings arrive down the thin ties, from news pools you
  don't stand in. Whoever bridges two silent groups holds quiet
  power, and it belongs to the gap, not the person.
- Grievance never explains a movement — grievances everywhere,
  movements rare. What explains one: supply (networks, money,
  safe rooms, divided authority) plus a frame — a name making it
  injustice, a culprit, a meetable demand. The first mover pays
  most and gains least; every strike clears or fails at that
  gate.
- Marriage, most of human history, was two groups signing a
  treaty with a person as the bond; the rule pushing marriage
  outward is what makes a society bigger than a household.
- A rite works by being performed, not by inner belief: people
  who agree on nothing can kneel at the same moment, and the
  kneeling binds them.
- Between strangers goodwill cannot be taken on words, so every
  culture builds displays that spend something real — the
  striking hand shown empty and held, the paid wait in a queue,
  shoes shed at the door. Only a costly signal is hard to fake.
- People decide by feeling and hire reasons after; lasting
  persuasion is self-persuasion. The frame half-decides ("nine in
  ten survive" and "one in ten die" move different choices from
  one number); the first number spoken bends all after it.
- Attachment is the first draft: a baby cries and learns, a
  thousand answers deep, whether anyone comes; every later love
  is read against the draft. The adult is the launch pad, not the
  opposite of adventure.
- A small consequence arriving every time beats a terrifying one
  arriving sometimes; the unpredictable adult teaches weather,
  not rules, and a child reading weather is gambling.
- Roughly two thirds of a durable couple's conflicts are
  permanent; lasting pairs hold them without contempt. Repair is
  the skill, harmony its by-product. Families break on long
  accumulation plus one small incident — never on the fight
  everyone names.
- Care concentrates on whoever can least bear it undone, then
  goes invisible; knowing a task exists is half the job and
  cannot be seen.
- Closeness is maintained, not stored: without a repeating
  occasion bonds fade by plain absence, each side feeling it as
  the other drifting. A childhood is made of the repeated small
  thing, not the big trip. Belonging is being missed when absent;
  groups that welcome everyone and need nobody produce
  attendance, not members.
- The most private acts have public rates: a city's totals barely
  move while individual reasons vary — something beyond persons
  sets the total (mirror holds the limit this implies reversed).
- A social number records a procedure as well as a world: crime
  rises when reporting eases; a survey answer is a social act
  aimed at the asker. Read it unexamined and you read the office.

## SCIENCE
The math of many people, held — not mentioned. LAW to compute
with, RULE to follow, WORKED run to a real answer.

### MATHEMATICS — the crowd arithmetic

LAW: ties grow by the square. links = n × (n − 1) ÷ 2.
RULE: headcount times one less, halved — the relationships that
  must be fed for all to know all.
WORKED: 5 people: 5 × 4 ÷ 2 = 10 — a dinner table holds it. A
  class of 12: 12 × 11 ÷ 2 = 66. A village of 150: 150 × 149 ÷ 2
  = 11,175. No head feeds eleven thousand ties: THE LAW ITSELF
  forces the break past a hundred-odd — rules must replace
  relationships because ties outran any maintenance.

LAW: reach multiplies per step: within k handshakes ≈ f^k, where
  f counts FRESH acquaintances only — friends' friends are mostly
  your own friends again, so raw counts overstate badly.
WORKED: know ~500, of whom ~50 lead somewhere new: 50² = 2,500;
  50³ = 125,000 — a town in three handshakes; 50⁴ = 6,250,000;
  50⁵ = 312,500,000 — a continent in five; 50⁶ = 15,625,000,000 —
  past everyone alive in six. The few-links small world:
  multiplication is merciless. Honest receipt: chains tried by
  mail averaged ~six hands WHEN they arrived — most died; the law
  proves the shortcut exists, not that anyone can find it.

LAW: spread doubles per round: N = 2^t — each knower finds one
  fresh ear per round, and every new knower is a teller. 2¹⁰ =
  1,024; 2²⁰ = 1,048,576.
WORKED: a secret told Monday passes a million ears inside three
  weeks of daily retelling — why reputation outruns the person,
  and why the only safe secret is the untold one.

LAW: the group steadies as 1/√n while the person stays loose:
  count ≈ n × p, wandering ± √(n × p × (1−p)).
WORKED: 10,000 households, each 1-in-10 to claim this year:
  expect 1,000 ± √(10,000 × 0.1 × 0.9) = √900 = 30 — the total
  known to ~±3% before the year starts, while NOTHING is known of
  any one house beyond its odds. Insurance lives in this gap: it
  prices the crowd exactly and cannot name the house that burns.
  The mirror certifies the reverse as a cannot.

LAW: cascades run on the line-up of thresholds, not on average
  belief.
WORKED: 100 people, thresholds 0, 1, 2, … 99: the 0 acts, the 1
  sees one and acts, the 2 sees two — all hundred go. Remove only
  the threshold-1 person: the instigator acts, nobody needs
  exactly one, the count stops at 1 forever. Same beliefs, one
  missing link: riot against lone shouter — why identical
  grievances tip one town and not the next.

LAW: mild preferences plus movement sort a map hard: give each
  household "a third of my neighbors like me," let the unhappy
  move, repeat — runs settle near-total separation (~two-thirds-
  plus like-neighbors, double what anyone asked). Never read the
  sorted map as the strength of the wish.

### THE TRUST ARITHMETIC

LAW: honesty pays when the future is long enough. Cheating grabs
  T once and ends the ledger; honesty pays R per round with
  chance w of another — a stream worth R ÷ (1 − w). Honesty wins
  above w = 1 − R ÷ T.
WORKED: cheat grabs 5, dealing pays 3: threshold w = 1 − 3/5 =
  0.4. Neighbors sure to meet again (w = 0.9): 3 ÷ 0.1 = 30
  against 5 — honesty wins six times over. A highway stall
  (w = 0.3): 3 ÷ 0.7 ≈ 4.3 against 5 — cheating pays. THE LAW
  ITSELF maps trust's geography: repeat dealing breeds honesty,
  passing trade breeds sharp practice, and contracts, reputations,
  and credit records are machines for raising w.

LAW: news moves belief by how much more often one kind of partner
  produces it. Kept promises come from the honest (~99%) AND from
  patient cheats grooming a mark (~90%) — weight barely 1.1 to 1.
  Betrayal comes almost only from cheats — weight ~10 to 1.
WORKED: start even. Twenty kept promises: 1.1²⁰ ≈ 6.7 to 1 in
  favor. ONE betrayal: 6.7 ÷ 10 = 0.67 — below even again. Twenty
  drops, emptied by one bucket: the asymmetry is what honest
  arithmetic does with evidence a cheat can cheaply fake.

LAW: a signal is worth what it costs the sender; talk is priced
  at its cost — zero. So the qualification certifies affording
  the years and finishing (why degrees are demanded for work no
  degree teaches), and the wrapped gift, the queue's wait, the
  shoes at the door spend real time or comfort where a faker's
  words spend nothing.

### THE PERSUASION LEVERS — with their receipts
Described, not endorsed; the limits are in the mirror.

- RECIPROCITY: a small gift opens a large door. Receipt (~): a
  sweet with the bill lifts tips ~3%; two, with a word, ~14%; two
  plus a turned-back "for you," over 20%.
- ANCHORING: the first number bends all after, even absurd ones,
  even for those who know the trick. Receipt (~): a wheel rigged
  to stop at 10 or 65, spun before guessing an unknown
  percentage — median guesses 25 versus 45. List prices exist to
  anchor, not to sell.
- CONFORMITY: against a unanimous room most deny their own eyes.
  Receipt (~): unanimously wrong confederates pulled subjects
  along on about a third of trials, three quarters yielding at
  least once — and ONE ally cut the yielding by three quarters.
  Secret ballots exist for this.
- AUTHORITY: calm instruction from worn legitimacy moves people
  past their own limits. Receipt (~): about two thirds of
  ordinary volunteers, told plainly to continue, went to the top
  of the shock board.
- SCARCITY AND SOCIAL PROOF: two borrowed compasses — what runs
  out must be valuable, what everyone picks must be right;
  installed because usually true, counterfeitable (countdowns,
  bought reviews). A crowd can be confidently wrong on no
  information: each follower copies the choice and drops the
  evidence — a queue outside the empty restaurant.
- COMMITMENT: a small public yes tilts the big yes later;
  self-contradiction hurts more than going along costs.

### THE NEGOTIATION ARITHMETIC

LAW: a deal lives only in the overlap of walk-away lines.
WORKED: seller's floor 800, buyer's ceiling 1,000 — 200 of space.
  First voice claims it: open at 1,100 and midpoint habits pull
  toward ~950; let the buyer open at 700, toward ~850. Same
  lines, ~100 moved by who spoke first — anchoring, cashed.
RULE: fix your floor before hearing any number.

LAW: positions are demands; interests are the hungers under them.
WORKED: two sisters, one orange. Split the position: half each —
  each hunger half fed. Split the interests — one wanted juice,
  one peel: whole juice to one, whole peel to the other. Value
  went ½ + ½ → 1 + 1 without adding an orange.

### THE HOUSEHOLD ARITHMETIC — family as the first economy

LAW: work that never crosses a market is invisible to every
  account by construction, yet is work at full weight.
WORKED: ~5 hours a day of unpaid cooking, washing, nursing,
  minding: 5 × 365 = 1,825 hours a year — a full-time job (a paid
  year runs ~1,800-2,000 hours). At 20 an hour: 36,500 a year,
  invisible; pay a stranger for the same tasks and the country's
  output rises with nothing real changed.
LAW: advantage compounds through two machines in series —
  schooling converts a head start into certificates that look
  earned; marriage pairs the certified with the certified. Two
  generations of sorting build a gap nobody had to intend.

## METHODS
How things are done here — with what governs each, and what
breaks it.

- BUILDING TRUST (small stakes, repeated, lengthening): start
  where betrayal is cheap, let the record accumulate, raise
  stakes as odds of meeting again rise. Breaks when: stakes
  outrun the record, or the future visibly shortens — the
  arithmetic reverses at a known exit.
- KEEPING THE LEDGER (give first, mark the coin): favors given
  before asking collect more; please marks a request refusable;
  thank you names the debt witnessed. Breaks when: a figure is
  stated between intimates — a priced favor converts ledger to
  account, permanently (the family loan at every dinner after).
- GREETING (spend a little, test the channel): the open hand
  shown and held; small talk as sound check — weather words carry
  nothing on purpose; their work is testing tone before anything
  heavy rides the channel. Breaks when: the cost is skipped.
- CHANGING STANDING (the three-move rite): take the person out of
  ordinary life, hold them in a marked in-between, hand them back
  renamed with new duties — witnessed and dated, because standing
  is held by everyone else and cannot change privately. Breaks
  when: unwitnessed — a private vow changes nobody's treatment.
- HOLDING A GROUP (schedule plus need): a repeating appointment
  people are missed at, and a task in the newcomer's hands within
  the week. Breaks when: everyone welcome, nobody needed.
- RUNNING PAST THE CEILING (write the roles): past a hundred-odd
  the link law forbids running on relationships — write roles,
  rules, records; state the warmth-for-structure trade. Breaks
  when: the founder pretends to know every name and the unwritten
  rules live only in the old guard.
- PERSUADING (arrange steps, let them walk): reach the feeling,
  supply defensible reasons, frame before numbers, prefer
  questions — a conclusion arriving as "mine" meets no wall.
  Breaks when: the lever is felt as a lever; an insulted listener
  has stopped listening.
- NEGOTIATING (floor first, then layers): fix the walk-away
  before hearing a number; probe for overlap; at deadlock go down
  to interests and look for the orange's peel. Breaks when: the
  deal humiliates — their ladder makes them break it.
- REPAIRING (fast, small, in the manner): the joke mid-fight, the
  hand on the arm, the apology naming the debt — since two thirds
  of close conflict never resolves, only the manner carries the
  future. "I was wrong" from power teaches that wrongness is
  survivable. Breaks when: contempt enters.
- RAISING (cheap editions of expensive lessons): play is practice
  with the price removed; then first job, first budget, first bad
  friend — each lesson cheaper now than later; small consequences
  every time; scaffolds off as skill arrives (a parent is fired
  from every stage by succeeding). Breaks when: protection
  cancels the editions, or fear teaches how not to be caught.
- READING THE LIVING (watch long, count carefully): ask and you
  get the rule; watch a year and you get the practice; the gap is
  where the exceptions live. Presence changes the watched —
  declare it, never subtract it. Breaks when: an afternoon of
  asking is mistaken for a year of watching.
- READING THE LEAVINGS (context is the information): lower went
  down earlier; a find's meaning is what lay beside it, how deep,
  which way up; the rubbish heap is honest because nobody poses a
  bin; the monument records what somebody wanted said. Reading a
  layer destroys it — the record made becomes the site. Breaks
  when: the object is lifted clean of its ground (a market for
  antiquities is a market for holes), or "no evidence" is read
  where nobody dug.
- STARTING A MOVEMENT (name, culprit, demand, rails): frame the
  hardship as injustice with a responsible party and a meetable
  demand; run it on networks that already exist; move when
  authority divides; beat the first-mover gate with assurance —
  gather thresholds where each can watch the count reach their
  number. Breaks when: grievance is trusted to organize itself.

## MEANS
What the methods run on.

- THE CALENDAR — the standing appointment: closeness is
  maintained not stored, so the repeating occasion is every
  lasting bond's maintenance schedule.
- THE TABLE — the shared meal, oldest synchronizer, the
  household's daily parliament; cooking holds its half.
- THE RITUAL KIT — handshake, toast, wrapping, queue, please and
  thank you: small coins, never zero-cost, spendable by anyone.
- THE WRITTEN LEDGER — money, contracts, credit records, minutes:
  the gift ledger made exact for strangers; each a machine for
  raising the odds-of-meeting-again that trust needs.
- THE ROSTER AND RULEBOOK — written roles for groups past the
  ceiling; the price is warmth, the purchase is scale.
- THE NETWORK AS ASSET — ties callable in emergencies, job hunts,
  harvests; savings held in people. Its two kinds pull against
  each other: bonds that hold you up also hold you in.
- THE ARCHIVE OF LEAVINGS — layers, middens, worn tools, graves,
  kin words, verse shaped for memory: instruments for reading
  people who cannot be asked.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject answers — the doors a query enters by.

- NOT BE ALONE: company is bodies in a room; intimacy is being
  known by at least one of them. Long isolation measurably
  damages the body (size argued — mirror holds the open part).
- BE SOMEBODY: a rung, a role, a name; the test of belonging is
  the noticed empty chair.
- COOPERATE PAST THE FAMILY: trust strangers enough to trade and
  build — the want every contract and institution answers.
- HAND LIFE ON: the relay — children raised, trades and accents
  and recipes passed down the arm; the estate is the visible part
  and often the smallest.
- KEEP THE PEACE INSIDE: norms, ladders, rites as fight-
  preventers; marriage as treaty; the feud ended by ritual.
- ACT TOGETHER: the strike, the barn-raising, the town that can
  organize — capacity built in dull settings, spent in crises.
- KNOW WHO TO TRUST: read costly signals, keep the books, consult
  the couriers.

## HISTORY
How the knowledge arose, in what order, at what cost.

- Kinship first, before offices: the family diagram was the tax
  system, army roster, and will; duties wore family words for
  most of human time.
- The gift before money: three rings of giving ran every economy
  for millennia; money arrived as the stranger's ring made exact
  — and the inner rings never adopted it.
- Surplus made chairs: leadership re-earned daily by giving all
  away (the feast as accounting in public) could not be
  inherited; the granary made the chair that outlives its sitter
  — storable surplus is the beginning of politics.
- Past the ceiling, the institution: settlements outgrew the link
  law and rules replaced relationships; the city traded warmth
  for freedom — nobody there has the standing to run your life.
- The nation, recently manufactured: a we of strangers assembled
  these last two centuries from picked dialects, schools, anthems
  — the assembling forgotten on purpose.
- The measured turn: crowd lore became instruments — line
  experiments, the shock board, threshold sums, the stable rates
  of private acts. Thumb-knowledge became claims with receipts,
  fileable and inheritable.
- The ground learned to talk: layers as calendars, middens as
  honest menus, bones keeping their own accounts — the ordinary
  people no scribe mentioned entered the record, at the price
  that reading a layer destroys it.
