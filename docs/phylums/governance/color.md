## THINGS
The units this subject is about; each names its other homes so a
cascade can branch.

- POWER — not a substance but a standing expectation, distributed
  through other people, that orders will be obeyed; one body can
  compel almost nothing alone. Also: people, conflict (armies
  dissolve by desertion before defeat), economy (money is orders
  sellers agree to honor), mind.
- THE STATE — a claim on every person and acre inside a line;
  membership imposed, not offered — nobody chooses their birth
  country, and leaving needs another state's permission to
  arrive. Also: law, conflict, earth.
- THE OFFICE AND THE CHAIN OF HANDS — the ruler passes, the
  office stays; every order runs on borrowed hands, each with a
  day that pays for something not quite the order. Also: people,
  economy, story.
- LEGITIMACY — the shared belief that this power is rightful; the
  discount that makes rule affordable (SCIENCE prices it). Four
  credits: always-been-so, properly-arrived-at, delivers,
  extraordinary person — each runs out its own way. Also: belief,
  mind, culture.
- PROCEDURE AND THE CONSTITUTION — the machine for removing
  persons from decisions; and its deepest piece, the rule about
  changing rules — procedure turned on itself, the one place it
  stands unleaning. Also: law, computing (code runs rules with no
  judge in the gap).
- THE VOTE, ITS COUNTING RULE, AND THE PARTY — same ballots,
  different arithmetic, different winners, in time different
  countries; the party is brand, slate, and filter for a choice
  no voter can research. Also: mathematics, play.
- THE TAX AND THE BUDGET — compulsion where volunteering fails,
  always buying money and changed behavior together; and the one
  statement that binds — the rest is speeches. Also: economy,
  farming (the countable harvest was the first base).
- THE REGISTER — census, map, income record: the state's eyes;
  it acts only on what its records contain. Also: measurement,
  computing.
- THE ARMED AGENTS — the force that protects can take; the
  account that undoes every control above it. Also: conflict,
  computing.
- THE CIVIL SERVICE AND THE COURT — permanent memory outlasting
  ministers, continuity and drag in one body; and the bench that
  owns no army — compliance in robes is belief. Also: teaching,
  law.
- THE CIVIC LAYER — clubs, unions, congregations: where strangers
  learn to run a meeting, keep accounts, organize. Also: people,
  culture, belief.
- THE COMMONS AND THE PUBLIC GOOD — what nobody can be fenced out
  of, and what private best moves empty or leave unbuilt. Also:
  economy, farming, earth.
- SYMBOL AND RITUAL — crown, oath, anthem, crest: machinery for
  making belief felt. Also: belief, culture, story, music.
- THE CROWD, ORGANIZED — union, march, movement: the many
  combined, the one thing rule by the few must prevent. Also:
  people, conflict.

## CLAIMS
What holds, written to be tested by a crossing query; worth is
applicability to the query, never absolute.

- No ruler rules alone: power is the standing expectation that
  the hands will obey. A coup is not a fight but a bet on that
  expectation — it succeeds by looking accomplished before it is.
- Legitimacy does the daily work; force is what gets spent when
  belief runs short. Belief is cheap; watching costs the base
  itself.
- Whoever controls what is known controls much of what can be
  done — seeing, hiding, telling; every power reaches for all
  three. But persuasion steers the words people argue in, not the
  week they live: the story loses to the empty shelf and the son
  who did not come home. And censorship's product is not silence
  but loneliness — each dissenter unsure what everyone else
  thinks, acting on believed belief.
- Power drifts toward concentration — office gathers the means of
  holding office. Dispersal is engineering against the current
  and needs upkeep like a dam; crisis removes the friction, and
  the emergency shortcut outlives the emergency, because whoever
  holds the new power would have to hand it back.
- Succession is the perennial crisis: between rulers, belief has
  no object, and only procedure can catch a crown. A handover
  moves authority (fast, public, whole) and knowledge (unwritten,
  slow) at different speeds; forcing either to the other's pace
  ruins it.
- Procedure makes outcomes predictable to strangers, and
  strangers build on the prediction — capital measurably flees
  unpredictable courts.
- Power poisons its own information: each rung reports what the
  rung above wants to hear, so the top rules a fiction that
  thickens the longer and harder the rule. Nobody lies —
  summarizing drops what shakes the summarizer's ladder.
- Rule endures by keeping the many divided; the many's mirror
  tool is combining anyway. The strongest player breeds the
  coalition against itself, which dissolves the day the common
  threat does.
- Every government runs on extraction and survives by returning
  enough — order, roads, justice, defense — that paying beats the
  alternatives. A state that need not ask for revenue need not
  answer: the export taxed at the port rots everything around it.
- Sovereignty is made from outside — recognized passports,
  received ambassadors; a claim becomes fact when enough others
  act as though it were one.
- Order and liberty are bought from one purse; the argument is
  never whether to trade, only where.
- Representation is delegation of judgment with a lossy brief:
  chosen on three questions, bound by a thousand.
- The counting rule shapes the party system more than opinion
  does, and where district lines fall decides winners on
  identical votes (SCIENCE runs both).
- Every workable democracy puts some things outside the vote —
  undemocratic by construction, on purpose: a majority can do
  something terrible to a minority quite democratically.
- A law is a wish until a desk and a budget carry it out: the
  front line's discretion, repeated a million times, IS the
  policy as experienced — and the state is met at a counter, so
  legitimacy is minted or melted there, not in theory.
- An intervention lands on people who rearrange around it: cap
  rents and landlords sell; pay by procedure and procedures
  multiply; ban a substance and a worse one appears. The behavior
  change is usually the main result.
- The regulator's facts come from the regulated — interested
  expertise is the only expertise available; lobbying is
  information and pressure in one envelope, useful, which is
  exactly why it works.
- What the state must own is set by metering technology, not
  ideology: lighthouses were public until ships could be charged
  by beacon. The boundary moves when the metering does.
- Capacity, not ideology, decides most outcomes; a crisis never
  builds capacity, only reveals what was built — and every piece
  looked like waste in the quiet years.
- A concentrated benefit beats a diffuse cost, so entitlements
  ratchet; and an institution's people depend on it, so its
  survival becomes an aim apart from its task — solved problems
  do not close their offices.
- Rules are written in the shape of the disaster that already
  happened, so protection points backwards and the next failure
  arrives through an unwritten door. Never delete an old fence
  before learning what it holds up.
- Trust is measured by what nobody has to do: where it is low,
  every deal carries a guard, a deposit, and a delay — a tax
  printed on no receipt. Betrayal is punished far past the loss
  because the group defends that discount.
- Easy exit drains exactly the members who would push for repair:
  leaving and complaining work against each other.
- The worst-off rebel least: revolt takes slack, organization,
  and an expectation — the rising comes first, and revolt arrives
  when the rise stops and the expectation stays.
- A group absorbs newcomers only as fast as it teaches what its
  arrangements are for; faster, and it keeps the rules, loses the
  reasons, and becomes a different group wearing the old name.

## SCIENCE
The mathematics of rule, held — not mentioned: LAW to compute
with, RULE to follow, WORKED with the arithmetic run to the end.

### THE ARITHMETIC OF OBEDIENCE — force priced against consent

LAW: cost of rule = watchers' lost production + (product of a
  free base − product of a watched base). Belief needs neither
  term; legitimacy is the discount.
RULE: to price a regime — count watchers per 1,000 and compare
  output per head against a consenting neighbor of the same
  people.
WORKED: a city of 1,000,000 workers at 50,000 each a year:
  product 50,000,000,000. Consent takes ~2.5 police per 1,000 =
  2,500 officers; lost production 2,500 × 50,000 = 125,000,000 —
  a quarter of one percent. Watching, at densities one real
  watched state ran (~1 full-timer per 180 citizens plus ~1
  informer per 90): ~5,600 full-timers + ~11,100 informers at
  quarter-time ≈ 8,400 worker-equivalents ≈ 420,000,000 — three
  times the consent bill. But the real bill is the base: the
  watched Germany ended forty years at roughly a third of the
  free Germany's output per head — same people, same language. A
  third of 50 billion is ~17 billion; the missing ~33 billion a
  year is ~80 times the whole watching payroll. And a guard for
  every citizen would cost more than the citizens produce — so
  obedience must be mostly free, or the regime is bankrupt before
  it is overthrown.

### THE ARITHMETIC OF EXTRACTION — what a tax can collect

LAW: revenue = rate × base, and the base flees the rate, because
  whatever is taxed is discouraged. Straight-line case:
  base(r) = B₀ × (1 − r).
WORKED: B₀ = 100. At 20%: base 80, revenue 16. At 40%: base 60,
  revenue 24 — rate doubled, revenue up only half. At 50%: 25,
  the hilltop. At 60%: 24. At 80%: base 20, revenue 16 — the
  same take as 20% with three quarters of the activity gone. The
  hilltop sits by flight speed: land cannot run, money runs
  fastest.
RULE: tax heaviest what cannot move; never read a doubled rate
  as doubled revenue.

LAW: the burden slides to the trapped side, whoever writes the
  check.
WORKED: a 10-per-unit tax on sellers. Buyers with rivals to walk
  to accept +3: the seller eats 7. Buyers trapped (the one
  bridge to work): the toll passes whole. The market's exits,
  not the law's wording, decide the split.

LAW: a good nobody can be fenced out of will not be funded
  voluntarily — each person's best move is to let others pay, so
  everyone reasoning carefully leaves it unbuilt.
WORKED: 100 households; a lit street worth 10 a year to each
  (1,000 total) costs 300. Voluntarily, no single 3 changes what
  its giver can see: collected ~0, unbuilt, 1,000 − 300 = 700 of
  yearly value lost. Compelled at 3 each: built; each pays 3,
  gets 10, keeps 7. The tax manufactured 700 a year that no
  volunteering could — that is what a tax IS.

### THE ENGINEERING OF DISTRUST — division of powers, priced

LAW: independent vetoes multiply escape odds: P(abuse passes) =
  p₁ × p₂ × … , when each body must separately agree and none
  answers to the others.
WORKED: one unchecked office goes rotten with odds ~1 in 10 per
  generation. Two truly independent: 1/10 × 1/10 = 1/100. Three:
  1/1,000. Independence is load-bearing: three bodies appointed
  by one hand are one body in three costumes — back to 1/10.
LAW, backwards: every required agreement also multiplies delay
  and divides blame — five bodies hold a fifth of responsibility
  each, and nobody can be removed for a fifth. Protection and
  unaccountability are bought with one coin.
RULE: audit any separation by three questions per body — who
  appoints, who pays, who removes; place vetoes only where a
  wrong act costs more than a slow one.

### THE LEAK IN THE CHAIN — delegation down, news up

LAW: transmission through a chain multiplies: fraction arriving
  = f per layer, raised to the layers. Words are lossy, and each
  agent's day pays for something not quite the order — nobody
  needs to lie.
WORKED, down: at f = 0.9, five layers deliver 0.9⁵ = 0.59049 —
  ~59% of the intention reaches the street, and the street's
  version is the policy people meet.
WORKED, up: each rung keeps 0.9 of the bad news (the dropped
  tenth shakes the summarizer's own ladder). Ten rungs: 0.9¹⁰ ≈
  0.35 — the top hears a third of what the street knows. Ten
  honest layers, one dishonest report; and fear shrinks f, so
  the harder the rule, the thicker the fiction.
RULE: to hear the street — cut rungs, or open a channel with no
  ladder in it (anonymous, unpunishable by design). To predict
  an agent — read what their day pays them for, not their
  loyalty.

### THE MATHEMATICS OF MANY — why hierarchy, why it grows

LAW: everyone-to-everyone grows as pairs, n(n−1) ÷ 2; a tree
  grows as n.
WORKED: 10 people = 45 pairs; 100 = 4,950; 1,000 = 499,500. The
  tree for 1,000 needs ~1,000 links. The ladder is a cost cut
  long before it is a power grab — the meeting that works at six
  dies at twenty by this law alone.
LAW: overseers follow the span of control.
WORKED: 1,000 workers, span 10: 100 + 10 + 1 = 111 overseers ≈
  10% of the whole. Span 5: 200 + 40 + 8 + 2 + 1 = 251 ≈ 20%.
  Halving the span doubles the overhead — closer watching,
  priced in headcount.
LAW: the ratchet — a benefit worth much to few beats a cost
  worth little to many, because the few can organize.
WORKED: a 100,000,000 subsidy to 10,000 producers is 10,000 each
  — a livelihood, a march, an office in the capital. Its cost to
  100,000,000 taxpayers is 1 each — not worth a stamp. Motive
  asymmetry 10,000 to 1: benefits enter one reasonable item at a
  time and almost never leave, while offices outlive their
  problems because their people depend on them. Span cost,
  ratchet, survival-as-aim: stack the three and bureaucracy's
  growth stops being a mystery and becomes an addition.

### THE MATHEMATICS OF COUNTING — what ballots can and cannot say

LAW: an average over groups depends on how the groups were drawn
  — and in districting, somebody chooses the groups.
WORKED: 50 voters, party P has 20 (40%), Q has 30. Five
  districts of ten. Pack Q: two districts lost 0–10 each. Spread
  the rest: P 7–3, P 7–3, P 6–4. Check: P seats 7+7+6 = 20 ✓,
  Q 10+10+3+3+4 = 30 ✓. P takes three seats of five — 60% of the
  power on 40% of the votes, no ballot altered.
LAW: majorities can loop — ranked preferences need not combine
  into a ranking at all.
WORKED: three voters rank A>B>C, B>C>A, C>A>B. A beats B 2–1;
  B beats C 2–1; C beats A 2–1. Let the chair pick the order:
  (A vs B) then winner-vs-C crowns C; (B vs C) then vs A crowns
  A; (A vs C) then vs B crowns B. Same votes, any winner — the
  agenda does work no honest count can remove, and it is proved
  that no counting rule past two options carries every
  reasonable fairness property at once.
LAW: one vote's leverage = personal stake × chance of deciding.
WORKED: stake 10,000 a term; chance one vote decides a large
  election ~1 in 1,000,000: expected value 10,000 ÷ 1,000,000 =
  0.01 — one cent, against ~40 hours of study. Staying
  uninformed is individually rational, so a public of rational
  individuals is collectively ignorant — an arithmetic failing,
  not a moral one. Parties exist to compress the choice this
  cent cannot fund.

### THE ARITHMETIC OF CORRUPTION AND DETERRENCE

LAW: a bribe is taken when bribe > penalty × catch odds — and
  the mind discounts the rare savage penalty to almost nothing,
  while a small certain one is felt every time.
WORKED: a stamp everyone needs; bribe on offer 500. Catch odds
  2%, fine 5,000: expected penalty 0.02 × 5,000 = 100 — each
  bribe nets ~400, and the honest clerk on a thin salary is the
  one behaving strangely. Fix by savagery — fine 50,000,
  expected 1,000: deters on paper, not in heads (pickpockets
  worked the crowds at the hangings of pickpockets). Fix by
  certainty — catch odds 20%, fine 5,000: the same expected
  1,000, but felt, because every fifth bribe ends the job.
  Mild-and-certain beats savage-and-rare at equal arithmetic,
  and costs less to run.
RULE: to clean a system — shrink discretion (fewer stamps),
  raise catch odds, raise the salary so the job is the hostage:
  an official earning three times the outside option risks a
  threefold job on a one-time bribe.
LAW, the trap: where everyone pays, refusing costs the refuser
  the service and buys nothing — honesty is punished
  individually even where all prefer clean. RULE: spring the
  trap whole — many-at-once enforcement plus the pay raise,
  never one martyr at a time.

### THE PRICES THE STATE PAYS — two standing bills

LAW: services made of a person's time do not get cheaper,
  because wages everywhere follow the parts of the economy that
  do.
WORKED: over three generations factory output per hour doubles
  three times: ×8; wages follow, ×8. A shirt that took an hour
  takes an eighth: 1/8 hour × 8× wage = the old price, flat. A
  teacher's class takes the hours it always took: same hours ×
  8× wage = ×8 in money while shirts held flat. The state buys
  person-hours — teacher, nurse, judge, officer — so its bill
  rises relative to everything else, forever, with nobody
  slacking. RULE: never read a rising service budget as rot
  before running this correction.
LAW: public debt is a claim by future bondholders on future
  taxpayers — a transfer inside the country, across time. Watch
  the gap between interest and growth, not the debt.
WORKED: debt of one year's product at 3% interest, growth 3%:
  the interest bill is 3% of product and the product grows to
  match — the ratio stands still forever, no coin repaid. That
  is why a state carries what would kill a household: it never
  dies, and it taxes heirs it need not consult. Interest 5%,
  growth 2%: the ratio compounds at 3% and doubles in ~24 years
  (72 ÷ 3) — the same law, now a countdown.

## METHODS
How governing is done — each with what governs it and what breaks
it. The prepositioned how-to-build.

- THE REGISTER FIRST: count the people, map the land, see the
  incomes — before taxing, conscripting, policing, or delivering
  can start. Breaks when the state governs its simplification:
  what the box misses, policy misses; collapsing states lose
  their information long before their territory.
- ELECTIONS AS SCHEDULED REMOVAL: the succession crisis defused
  by timetable; governed by the counting laws. Breaks when the
  loser will not walk away, or the map-drawing arithmetic is
  captured.
- DIVISION OF POWERS: engineered distrust — vetoes multiplied
  where a wrong act is dearer than a slow one. Breaks when one
  hand appoints, pays, or removes all the "rivals"; costs delay
  and diluted blame by the same law that grants protection.
- THE WRITTEN RULE ABOVE THE VOTE: a constitution forbids
  ordinary majorities some acts — undemocratic by design, which
  is the design. Its deepest clause is the amendment clause,
  settled in advance so arguments stay arguments instead of
  fights about who is in charge.
- MATCH AUTHORITY TO CONSEQUENCES: decide each thing at the
  smallest level containing its costs — bins local, rivers and
  epidemics not — because a decider who does not bear the
  consequences will not weigh them.
- EXTRACT AND RETURN: tax by flight speed; collect by voluntary
  filing plus an audited fraction (auditing everyone is
  impossible); return enough that paying beats the alternatives.
  Evasion is the first vote against.
- READ THE BUDGET AS THE POLICY: fund it or admit it is a
  speech; to know a government, divide two departments'
  allocations; to steer one, move money, not words.
- GUARD THE GUARDS: pay them well, split them into rival forces
  that watch each other, bind them with a belief, or keep them
  small and far. Every state runs some blend; running none is
  scheduling a coup.
- PROCEDURE PAST THE ACQUAINTANCE LINE: below roughly a hundred
  members judgment beats rules; above it, written rules win even
  though worse in every single case, because judgment does not
  copy and a rule copies perfectly. The price is paid at the
  counter: removing discretion removes kindness in the same
  motion.
- DELEGATE WITH THE LEAK PRICED IN: brief for the outcome;
  accept ~a tenth lost per layer or flatten the chain; inspect a
  sampled fraction; pay for what you actually want — any number
  rewarded stops measuring (receipts in the mirror).
- DETER MILD AND CERTAIN: spend enforcement on catch odds, not
  savagery. There is never enough enforcement for all the
  breaking, so the chosen fraction IS the law people learn —
  choose it as deliberately as the statute.
- PRICE THE UNPRICED: when an act costs somebody not party to it
  — charge the causer, cap the total and let quota trade, or
  hand the thing an owner. Each works, each fails differently;
  the whole job is making an invisible cost visible.
- RATION IN THE OPEN: when wanting outruns having, something
  decides who goes without — money, the queue, or an official's
  judgment; each currency favors a different sort of person.
  Expect the resale market under every rule ever written.
- LASH THE TEMPTED HAND: powers that reward today at tomorrow's
  expense — above all printing money — go to bodies the
  government cannot instruct. Not a competence claim: a promise
  put deliberately out of reach, fixed before anyone knows whom
  it will favor.
- PUBLISH, WITH BOTH LIMITS KNOWN: transparency is a real check;
  the published number becomes the target, and deliberation
  retreats to the corridor. Pair it with independent meters and
  rooms where talk is safe.
- ORGANIZE, AND PROTECT WHERE ORGANIZING IS LEARNED: the many
  combining is the counterweight to every drift in this file. A
  protest's message is not the placards but the count of people
  bearing a cost — talk is free, turning up is not. The civic
  layer is the school of it; guard it as infrastructure.
- ADMIT MEMBERS AT TEACHING SPEED: immigration and hiring alike
  are rate-and-terms questions — about people, irresolvable; as
  rate and terms, ordinary policy.

## MEANS
What the methods run on, with the numbers that matter.

- THE REGISTER AND THE STATISTICS OFFICE: the eyes — and an
  instrument controlled by an interested party measures the
  party's interest, so independence here is structural. Where
  the counters can be instructed, nobody — the government
  included — can find out what is happening.
- THE TREASURY: budget, audited fraction, and the debt book with
  its interest-minus-growth countdown.
- THE COURTS AND WRITTEN LAW: compressed conflict — every
  settled fight an answer the next generation need not fight
  for. No army; belief with a gavel.
- THE ARMED AGENTS: the spendable force, and the standing guard
  problem it creates, answered in METHODS.
- THE BALLOT AND ITS COUNTING RULE: a mean that shapes what it
  measures — winner-take-all districts squeeze toward two
  parties, proportional counting breeds coalitions. The rule is
  a decision made before anybody votes.
- THE PARTY: the voter's cent cannot fund research, so a brand
  vouches, bundles, and filters — a genuine service, and why
  politics arrives as package deals.
- THE CIVIL SERVICE: memory outlasting ministers — continuous
  and hard to steer, in one body.
- SYMBOL AND RITUAL: coronations seam the succession gap fast;
  oaths and anthems make belief felt. The welfare purse among
  strangers runs on this manufactured we — nobody funds a
  stranger indefinitely out of arithmetic alone.
- THE CIVIC LAYER: meeting-running, account-keeping,
  stranger-organizing — why anxious regimes dismantle it first.
- THE BORDER AND THE LIST: which list a person lands on at birth
  decides income, safety, schooling, and lifespan more than
  almost anything they will choose — the largest lottery a
  person is entered into.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject answers — the doors a query enters by.

- KEEP THE PEACE AMONG STRANGERS: predictability by procedure;
  fights settled by the group claiming punishment, so nobody
  funds a private feud.
- FUND WHAT NOBODY WILL VOLUNTEER FOR: the streetlight
  arithmetic — compulsion exactly where each person's best move
  leaves the wanted thing unbuilt.
- GUARD THE SHARED THING: quota, fee, or fence for the commons
  that private best moves would empty.
- DEFEND THE EDGE: the border held — the guard problem swallowed
  on purpose as the lesser danger.
- POOL RUIN: spread the rare disaster thin enough that no
  household is destroyed, at the scale where the pool cannot be
  refused.
- PASS POWER WITHOUT BLOOD: the timetabled handover; most of
  history's bloodshed crowds into the gap this closes.
- REMEMBER, COUNT, AND DELIVER: capacity — the slowly built
  ability to do what the speeches promise.

## HISTORY
How the knowledge arose, in what order, at what cost.

- States first grew where grain could be counted and carted (~):
  a harvest visible at the threshing floor could be taxed; the
  register was born as a tax tool.
- Rule began attached to persons, and the succession gaps filled
  with wars; procedure caught the crown slowly — coronation,
  charter, council — paid in exactly the blood it now prevents.
- The many learned combining against divide-and-rule: franchises
  and labor rights were organized for, not granted (~); the
  count of people bearing a cost was the argument that carried.
- War built most of the machine: income tax, conscription,
  universal registration, railways, mass schooling — each pushed
  hardest by the need to raise armies, each kept after. Capacity
  arrives under threat and stays.
- Elections' real discovery was the loser leaving: removal on
  schedule, accepted in advance — a crisis turned into a
  calendar entry.
- Money's printing press was lashed away from rulers only after
  war-funded inflations taught the temptation's price: the hand
  tied to the wheel before the storm.
- The regulatory state is written in disaster ink: aviation's
  rulebook reads as a list of crashes, finance's as collapses —
  each clause the shape of its catastrophe, its strength and its
  blindness forward.
- The welfare purse grew widest after shared dangers, and
  universal schemes outlived means-tested ones because everyone
  was a claimant — the ratchet law working, for once, in the
  open.

## RELATIONS
First-class connections — the cascade's paths outward.

- LAW: procedure's whole field — precedent as compressed
  conflict, courts owning no army, the contract riding a larger
  unwritten expectation, selective enforcement as the law
  actually learned.
- ECONOMY: extraction and its hill, incidence and flight, debt's
  interest-minus-growth clock, the person-time cost disease,
  inflation as the unvoted tax, the resource curse — and money
  itself as orders sellers agree to honor.
- PEOPLE: the group laws this subject arms with a budget — free
  riders, the emptying commons, pairs versus trees, trust as the
  invisible tax, exit draining voice, absorption at teaching
  speed.
- CONFLICT: war as the state's builder, the coup as a bet,
  armies dissolving by desertion, the security spiral (arming
  for defense reads as attack), sanctions reaching everyone but
  the decider.
- MIND: belief doing the daily work; losses felt sharper than
  equal gains (the ratchet's engine); the near beating the far
  (the election horizon); the lonely dissenter acting on
  believed belief.
- MATHEMATICS: multiplied vetoes, pair counts, looping
  majorities, compounding advantage, counting rules that shape
  party systems before anybody votes.
- MEASUREMENT: the register before every function; the
  independent statistics office; the meter law — a number
  rewarded stops measuring (receipts in the mirror).
- BELIEF: legitimacy's four credits; ritual making belief felt;
  guards bound by ideology as one answer to the guard problem.
- CULTURE: the manufactured we that lets strangers fund
  strangers' children and old age — flag, story, language doing
  load-bearing work under the welfare purse.
- STORY: the telling as power's third instrument; the child who
  says the emperor is naked — common knowledge snapping into
  place; the succession crisis carried down centuries in verse.
- TEACHING: institutions re-taught into each arrival; rules
  traveling better than their reasons; the civil service as the
  school that never graduates its pupils.
- ENGINEERING: checks as redundancy; dispersal as a dam needing
  maintenance; safety factors and inspection schedules — the
  analogy the mirror leans on for the incorruptible-institution
  want.

- ETHICS: what may never be put to a vote; duty crowded out by a

  identical treatment at the counter.
- UPKEEP: every dispersal of power decays unserviced — term
  limits, audits, fences deleted by inheritors who never learned
  their why. The dam needs walking.
- BUILDING: public works legitimize states; the capital city is governance built.
- EARTH: borders follow rivers and ranges; resources underground make law above.
- MOVEMENT: stadium spectacle serves states; sport is soft power.
- PLANTS: grain stores fed the first bureaucracies; famine topples governments.
- TRANSPORT: roads are the state's arteries; who moves freely is policy.
