# GOVERNANCE — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/governance/color.md: same parts,
opposite sign. Seed authored by Claude from
docs/fabric_phylums/24_power_governance.md,
docs/fabric_phylums/129_deep_government_and_state.md, and

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or
cannot be under stated conditions.

- THE PHILOSOPHER KING — the wise ruler above procedure, wanted
  and tried in every century. The receipts are uniform: the
  wisest head still rules through the chain, and the chain
  poisons upward — ten rungs deliver a third of the truth, and
  the leak law spares nobody for being wise; and whatever wisdom
  builds dies at succession, because belief attached to a person
  has no procedure to catch it. The philosopher who coined the
  want tried it on a real prince, twice, and failed twice — the
  first adventure ending, by the old accounts, with the
  philosopher sold as a slave. A cannot UNDER THE CONDITIONS of
  chains-of-hands and mortal rulers — so far, all conditions.
  Would drain on: unpoisoned information plus non-crisis
  succession; no mechanism is written. The partial drain ran the
  other way: procedure did not perfect the king, it removed him.
- THE INCORRUPTIBLE INSTITUTION — a court, a police, a treasury
  that cannot rot. Open, and honestly restated by the
  engineering analogy: no builder orders the unbreakable beam —
  they order a stated margin and an inspection schedule. The
  buildable form is corruption-SLOWED: independence multiplied
  (1/10 × 1/10), meters held by outsiders, parts replaceable on
  a calendar, catch odds funded. The want as worded names no
  material; until one exists, the honest answer is a maintenance
  schedule, not a substance.
- THE STATE THAT WITHERS AWAY — promised: rule would dissolve
  once its causes were cured. Tried at scale; the states that
  promised withering grew the densest watching apparatus in the
  record (~1 watcher per 180 plus ~1 informer per 90 — the very
  numbers the color prices force with). WHY: the emergency never
  declares itself over, because whoever holds the new power
  would have to hand it back. Would drain on: one receipt of a
  large power dissolving itself unforced; none is filed.
- WORLD GOVERNMENT — wondered about as long as wars; blocked by
  three of the color's own laws: sovereignty is made from
  outside, and a world state has no outside; members could not
  exit, removing the check that drains bad rule; and the biggest
  player breeds the coalition against it — here, the government
  itself. The treaty layer is the standing partial form: rules
  without a single ruler. Would drain on: planet-scale
  enforcement that is not itself the danger; none is written.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "THE PEOPLE RULE THEMSELVES" — fails as transmission: one
  ballot against a thousand questions, and the voter's cent
  prices study at five orders of magnitude above its return.
  What holds moved to the color: the people cheaply REMOVE
  rulers on a schedule.
- "HARSHER PUNISHMENT DETERS MORE" — false at the margin that
  matters: at 2% catch odds a tenfold fine barely moves the felt
  arithmetic, while tenfold catch odds moves everything.
  Pickpockets worked the hangings of pickpockets. Re-bought by
  legislatures every crime wave; kept here with its numbers.
- "SANCTIONS BEND RULERS" — falsified in the broad form: the
  pressure reaches everyone except the decider, and the shortage
  often tightens the ruler's grip as sole source of the scarce.
  Narrow form that holds: pressure landing on the deciders' own
  assets. The broad tool persists because it is visible and
  cheap to the sender — both true, neither about working.
- "THE STRONGMAN GETS THINGS DONE" — the speed is real and
  borrowed: the checks' delay refunded at the price of their
  protection. The bill arrives twice — at the first big mistake
  nobody dared report (the leak law compounding under fear), and
  at succession, which he by construction cannot timetable. The
  watched-state receipts (a third of the neighbor's output per
  head) are this claim's long-run ledger.
- "TRANSPARENCY FIXES IT" — half held: the published number
  became the target (schools taught the test, waits were
  renamed, surgeons declined hard cases), and deliberation moved
  to the corridor. Holds only paired with independent meters and
  rooms where talk is safe.
- "BAN IT AND IT STOPS" — falsified across substances, prices,
  and windows: people rearrange — the capped rent sold the
  housing, the banned substance bred a worse one, the taxed
  windows were bricked up (the walls still show it). Compute the
  rearrangement first, or discover it after.
- "PROPAGANDA CAN MAKE A PEOPLE BELIEVE ANYTHING" — false where
  the audience can check: the story loses to the empty shelf and
  the son who did not come home. What the teller rules is the
  argument's vocabulary and each dissenter's loneliness — real
  power, narrower than the claim.
- UNSETTLED: HOW LONG CAN NEAR-PURE FEAR RULE? — decades are in
  the record, always expensively. Nothing certain — but here is
  a possibility: fear rules while the guards' pay clears and the
  borders hold the workers in, so the clock is the guard bill
  against the shrinking base (color SCIENCE), and the collapse
  date is the treasury's, not the people's. Would settle on: the
  watched-state ledgers read as solvency records.
- UNSETTLED: WHY DID SOME STATES BUILD CAPACITY AND OTHERS NOT?
  — war pressure grips part, revenue-bargaining part (a state
  that must ask must answer), time part. Nothing certain — but
  here is a possibility: capacity compounds like the color's
  other ratchets, so small early differences in register and
  bargain widen on their own. Would settle on: capacity built
  quickly without threat — candidates exist (~), none cleanly
  separated.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A METER FOR LEGITIMACY — the color prices what belief
  saves but cannot read the balance remaining. Polls measure
  saying, not the standing expectation, which shows itself only
  when tested — why coups are bets, and revolutions surprise the
  police files that watched for them. The one directional law
  held (risen expectations halted precede revolt) predicts
  direction, never date.
- LACKING, AND PROVED MISSING: A WORKING SUM FOR GROUP
  PREFERENCE — past two options no counting rule carries every
  reasonable fairness property at once; the looping majority is
  the standing exhibit. All voting design is choosing which
  failure to keep — certified negative knowledge.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: MEASUREMENT UNDER REWARD — every meter
  wired to consequence stopped measuring: tonnage quotas made
  few heavy nails, count quotas many useless ones, league tables
  taught the test, clearance rates renamed the crimes. Survives
  by inversion: measure many things, reward none directly, keep
  the meter-holders outside instruction.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: WHOLE-ECONOMY CALCULATION — the missing
  science under the planning entry below: the facts a plan needs
  are local, unwritten, and stale by tomorrow, and no instrument
  reads them without a price. The arithmetic half improved a
  millionfold and moved the failure not at all — certifying
  WHERE the gap is: not in the adding, in the knowing.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — waiting
for their planet.

- CENTRAL PLANNING OF A WHOLE ECONOMY — tried at the largest
  scale anything in this phylum was ever tried: roughly a third
  of the world, most of a century, and the want was real (no
  waste, no idle hands, production by need). The receipts:
  millions of distinct goods against a price bureau that could
  reset only a fraction of its prices a year, so most ran years
  stale (~); meters gamed by the measurement law; reports
  poisoned by the leak law; shortage and glut as standing
  weather, the queue replacing the price; a grain exporter
  turned grain importer; and the paired-country experiments —
  same people, split by a line — ending near 1:3 output per head
  in one pair, ~20:1 in the other, half the night photograph
  dark. CERTIFIED UNDER CONDITIONS: fails at whole-economy
  scale, human planners, reported information, peacetime. The
  shimmer, honestly: it ran total-war economies passably for a
  few years (one known output, waste tolerated, an end date),
  and every firm is a small planned island that dies when too
  big to compute itself — the firm's boundary is this entry's
  live edge. Would drain on: an instrument reading local,
  unwritten, perishable knowledge without a price; none is
  written.
- RULE BY TERROR — runs, and ran for decades; never cheaply. The
  color's obedience arithmetic is this entry's bill: three times
  the watching payroll and a base shrunk to a third, the missing
  product ~80 times the payroll. Collapses at the guard ledger
  or the succession, whichever fails first. Would drain on:
  watching made free AND believed — the second term no
  technology has touched.
- PRICE CONTROLS AGAINST SHORTAGE — the capped rent sold off the
  housing; the capped bread emptied the bakery; the shortage
  moved from money into queues and favors and kept its size.
  WHY: a price is compressed news, and silencing the messenger
  keeps the drought. Narrow drain: brief, for staples, WITH
  rationing attached — the coupon admits what the cap denies.
- BUYING VIRTUE WITH FINES — the nursery that fined late parents
  got more lateness: the fee reclassified a wrong into a
  purchase and dismissed the shame doing the work; paid blood
  drives drew less, and worse. CONDITION: where duty works, a
  price fires it. Drains only where no duty was working.
- THE PERMANENT EMERGENCY — powers taken for the crisis, kept
  after: wartime measures on the books generations later, the
  old republic's six-month dictatorship stretched to a lifetime.
  WHY, in one line: whoever holds the new power would have to
  hand it back. Partial drains are engineering, not virtue:
  sunset clauses with the calendar outside the holder's reach.
- DISMANTLING THE CIVIC LAYER — anxious regimes' first move; it
  works as intended, then bills twice: the society can no longer
  organize against the regime, or FOR anything — nobody left to
  run a meeting or deliver the regime's own plans. Receipts: the
  congregations and cooperatives that outlasted the regimes that
  banned them.
- THE INQUIRY AS THE ANSWER — rage converted into a document on
  a schedule chosen to outlast the attention. The honest form
  exists (facts compelled that nothing else could — aviation's
  rare genuine rewrites); the failure form is the recommendation
  list nobody funds. Distinguishable only afterward, by the
  budget line: funded findings were meant.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE PARCHMENT BARRIER — a fine constitution nobody is bound to
  enforce is a suggestion with a seal. Texts copied word-for-word
  between countries produced opposite results, locating the
  working part outside the text: in who appoints, who pays, who
  removes. Filed because the failure keeps being re-bought as
  "better wording".
- SURVEILLANCE AS A SUBSTITUTE FOR CONSENT — fell short twice:
  watching is not believing (the discount stays unbought), and
  the more watched the reporters, the worse the reports — the
  leak law tightening under fear until the top rules a fiction.
  The full price is worked in the color; this is its receipt.
- NOT YET FOUND: AN UPWARD CHANNEL THAT CANNOT PUNISH — the want
  behind the leak law. Partial drains, real and bounded:
  anonymity, and reports made unpunishable on purpose — much of
  why flying became safe. Unproven where power needs it most,
  because the power that would install it is the power it would
  embarrass.
- NOT YET FOUND: AN INSTRUMENT READING LOCAL KNOWLEDGE WITHOUT A
  PRICE — the missing means under the planning entry; named here
  so the cascade finds it from both doors.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- MAKE PEOPLE GOOD BY LAW — law compels acts, not hearts, and a
  fine reclassifies where duty was working. The reachable form
  is narrower and real: make the honest choice the cheap one
  (the corruption arithmetic run backward).
- END SCARCITY BY DECREE — a decree only chooses which currency
  the shortage is paid in (money, queue, judgment); it has never
  removed one. The want belongs to economy and farming; this
  subject can only pick the landing spot honestly.
- RULE WITHOUT EXTRACTION — governing costs, and someone always
  pays. The tried form — revenue without asking — delivered rule
  without answering: the want fulfilled and made worse.
- PERFECT FAIRNESS AND PERFECT KINDNESS AT ONE COUNTER — every
  step that removes discretion removes kindness in the same
  motion; identical treatment of unidentical people is
  fairness's own price tag. The trade moves; it does not
  dissolve.
- THE PERMANENT SETTLEMENT — every constitution ages into rules
  whose disasters nobody remembers, aimed at the last
  catastrophe while the next arrives through an unwritten door.
  The reachable form is the amendment clause: not a settlement
  that lasts, but a settled way to unsettle.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the
story.

- The succession gaps hold most of this phylum's dead: wars
  between rulers, crowded into the moments belief had no object,
  paid century after century before procedure learned to catch
  the crown. The color's calm election line is written over this
  ledger.
- The planning century's famines are the leak and meter laws'
  joint receipt at full scale: harvests reported upward as
  fiction while the countryside starved — somewhere in the tens
  of millions across the two great cases (~; counted only
  afterward, because the registers themselves were part of the
  fiction). Filed with its why, to recompute as a warning
  wherever reports replace prices.
- The philosopher-king ledger runs unbroken from the Syracuse
  adventures to the present tense: each century's wise strongman
  arriving as the exception, leaving as the receipt.
- The emergency ratchet's trail: temporary taxes still
  collected, wartime powers still on the books, six months
  stretched to a lifetime — each kept by the same one-line why.
- The legibility disasters: the counted forest of one species
  that died of its own tidiness; villages redrawn to be
  countable, and starving by design — the state governing its
  simplification at the simplification's price.
- The deleted fences: rules written after deaths nobody now
  remembers, repealed by inheritors as pointless, followed by
  the return of what the rule was holding back — finance keeps
  the cleanest recent receipts.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, REBUILT AT A PRICE: GOVERNANCE ↔ ITS OWN REPORTS — a
  state cannot learn the truth from instruments it can instruct
  (the leak and meter laws). Rebuilt only by placing the meters
  outside instruction — statistics offices, audits, courts: the
  color's engineering of distrust wearing an eyeshade.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: THE VILLAGE SCALED — the recurring dream
  that acquaintance-line trust can run a nation without
  procedure's cold machinery. Every large try re-taught the
  color's threshold law instead. Dotted: no drain written, and
  the want returns every generation.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: COMPUTING ↔ PLANNING REVIVED — "the
  calculation failed for lack of machines" returns with each new
  machine. The certified condition was never the arithmetic but
  the local, unwritten, stale-by-tomorrow knowledge. Dotted
  until an instrument reads that without a price; a millionfold
  of compute has so far moved it not at all.
- BROKEN, SPLIT ON PURPOSE, TENSION STANDING: GOVERNANCE ↔
  BELIEF — rule fused to one faith burned at that faith's
  schisms; the split (belief made private) was itself an
  engineering fix. The tension stands because the welfare purse
  still runs on manufactured solidarity — the state dismissed
  belief's authority and still borrows its glue.
