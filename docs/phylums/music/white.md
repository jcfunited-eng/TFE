# MUSIC — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/music/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting
for its drain. Seed authored by Claude, mined from
docs/fabric_phylums/10_music.md, 153_deep_music_theory.md, and
68_sound_rhyme_song.md.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- AN INSTRUMENT PURE IN EVERY KEY — CANNOT BE with twelve fixed
  pitches per octave: twelve pure fifths overshoot seven octaves
  by 129.746 ÷ 128 ≈ 1.0136, a quarter semitone that can be moved
  but never deleted (the color's comma). DRAINED PARTIALLY,
  exactly as the shimmer predicts, by removing the fixed-pitch
  condition: split keys (historical keyboards ran 19+ notes per
  octave — playable purity, unplayable hands), unfretted strings
  and the voice (intervals bent pure in flight), electronic
  retuning that re-prices notes as the harmony moves. The fixed
  acoustic twelve stays closed.
- A NOTATION THAT KEEPS THE PERFORMANCE — the score fixes pitch
  and rough duration and drops timbre, groove, and all timing
  below its smallest symbol; the record keeps the SOUND perfectly
  and drops the instructions (nothing in it says how to make it
  again, or what may vary). Each keeper holds the half the other
  drops. Would drain on: a written form carrying measured
  deviation and timbre at the resolution grooves live at (~tens
  of milliseconds, per voice).
- THE MUSIC OF THE SPHERES — planets sounding the ratios. BROKE
  as astronomy: space carries no sound and the orbits fit no
  fixed chord. The partial return, filed honestly: orbital
  resonance is REAL small-whole-number physics — neighboring
  moons lock into 1:2:4 and 2:3 period ratios by the same
  push-in-time law as the bridge. The claim died as sound and
  survived as ratio; the shimmer at its cleanest.
- AN EQUAL-STEP SCALE THAT CARRIES A HOME — attempted (whole-tone
  and kin). CANNOT under its own terms: a location is recognized
  by its neighborhood, and a neighborhood identical everywhere
  carries no location — a coastline of identical bays cannot be
  fixed on. Drains only by importing help the scale refuses (a
  drone, a rhythmic anchor). Kept as a color ON PURPOSE by
  composers who wanted floating — the failure as home is the
  success as weather.
- SEEING SOUND AS COLOR (the color organ, the note-to-hue chart)
  — FAILS as physics: hearing spans ten octaves and folds at
  every doubling; sight spans barely one octave and never folds —
  light's highest visible frequency is less than double its
  lowest, so there is no seam to map the octave onto. Every chart
  is a private convention, and the charts disagree. UNSURE at one
  edge: some nervous systems do wire the senses together — stable
  for the person, arbitrary between persons — a fact about
  brains, not a mapping waiting to be found.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "MUSIC IS THE UNIVERSAL LANGUAGE" — partly false, with
  receipts: the dissonance line is drawn per culture and era (the
  ladder, not a fence); a rainforest people with little exposure
  to Western harmony heard the roughness of grinding intervals
  and simply did not dislike it — the perception traveled, the
  preference did not; gamelan tuners deliberately install the
  few-per-second beating Western tuners spend lifetimes removing;
  major-means-happy fails outside the tradition that taught it.
  What DOES travel: the octave's kinship (nearly everywhere,
  stretched at some edges), the pulse and its grouping, the
  lullaby's register and slowness. Verdict: universal MATERIALS,
  local grammar — like language itself, which is exactly what the
  claim forgot.
- "KEYS HAVE CHARACTERS" UNDER EQUAL TEMPERAMENT — mostly didn't
  hold: equal temperament makes every key one shape shifted,
  removing the only mechanism that could make keys structurally
  different. The honest causes of the strong shared impression:
  instrument resonance, open strings ringing in some keys,
  singers' ranges, centuries of association. Anything left over
  is UNSURE — small and probably ordinary.
- THE MOZART EFFECT — the popular claim (music makes babies
  smarter) was never in the paper and failed when tested; the
  original was a small ~10-15 minute bump on one spatial task.
  What survived: mood and arousal from ANY liked music briefly
  help. Kept because queries still arrive carrying the inflated
  version.
- "THE RULES OF HARMONY ARE LAWS OF NATURE" — didn't hold: theory
  is a description drawn from a sample of admired pieces, and a
  description forbids nothing outside its sample; the rules
  changed each time the music did. The color keeps them as
  GRAMMAR; this entry keeps them from promotion to physics.
- "PERFECT PITCH IS THE MUSICAL EAR" — didn't hold: absolute
  naming is rare and not what music runs on; the paying ear is
  relative — the melody is its gaps, not its heights, which is
  why the transposed tune is still the tune.
- UNSETTLED: WHY SMALL RATIOS PLEASE — half settled: aligned
  overtones make no beating roughness and the summed wave repeats
  quickly — the SMOOTHNESS is physics. Open: whether the PLEASURE
  in smoothness is wired or taught — the rainforest receipt says
  taught does more work than assumed; infant studies pull the
  other way and are contested. Nothing certain — but here is a
  possibility: the roughness detector is universal hardware, and
  the liking is a learned coat hung on it, thicker or thinner by
  culture. Would drain on: cross-culture, cross-age preference
  tests separating detection from liking, exposure controlled.
- UNSETTLED: THE OCTAVE'S EDGES — some traditions stretch the
  octave past 2:1. The piano's stretch is SETTLED (stiff strings
  run overtones sharp; tuners follow the strings — the color
  holds it); the cultural stretch, where no stiff string demands
  it, is not. Possibility: ears calibrated on stretched
  instruments carry the stretch as the learned octave. Would
  drain on: octave-matching tests on listeners raised on
  unstretched sources.
- UNSETTLED: WHY THE KNOWN PIECE STILL MOVES — the guessing goes
  on after the answer is known (the color's one mechanism), but
  WHY prediction keeps paying at total certainty is not written.
  Possibility: the pleasure attaches to the handling — the body
  rides the tension curve in real time whatever the mind knows,
  as a punchline lands on the twentieth telling. Would drain on:
  a mechanism separating knowing-that from feeling-when, tested
  on one listener, one piece, many passes.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A PREDICTIVE THEORY OF THE MOVING PIECE — the color
  can build a CORRECT piece (grammar, voice leading, form) but
  cannot COMPUTE whether it will move a given listener before it
  plays. The subject's largest named absence; every industry that
  bets on songs holds a portfolio because no one can hold a
  prediction. Twin of cooking's missing theory of taste — expect
  the two to drain together if either does.
- LACKING: THE WRITTEN SUM OF CONSONANCE — the parts are mapped
  (ratio simplicity, beating roughness, learned exposure) but no
  working formula weighs them into where a NEW listener's line
  will fall. Until it is written, the ratio claim above cannot
  close.
- LACKING: THE ISOLATED MECHANISM OF MINOR-MEANS-SAD — tempo does
  much of the work credited to mode (halve the speed, keep every
  note, get a lament — the color's own claim); what mode alone
  contributes, tempo and register held still, is small and
  part-learned. The honest answer is the decomposition.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE SPHERES — ratio arithmetic exported to
  the heavens as sound; wrong medium, wrong claim (THINGS holds
  the wreck and the salvage).
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: STRUCTURAL KEY-CHARACTER — pre-temperament
  acoustics rightly said keys differed (under uneven tunings they
  did); the claim was carried into equal temperament after the
  mechanism was removed. A true law outliving its conditions is
  how a superstition is made from a fact.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
wings-on-arms entries, waiting for their planet.

- CLOSING THE CIRCLE WITH PURE FIFTHS — tune twelve perfect 3:2
  fifths and arrive home. The arithmetic refuses: 3¹²/2¹² ≈
  129.746 against 2⁷ = 128 — overshot by a quarter semitone,
  always, for everyone. Not a skill failure; a remainder. Every
  tuning system since is a negotiation with this one number.
- PILING THE REMAINDER INTO FAR KEYS — near keys almost pure and
  lovely, the whole error heaped into one interval so wrong they
  named it the WOLF — a fifth that howled. Worked UNDER THE
  CONDITION that music stayed in the near keys; broke when
  composers wanted all twelve. Superseded by spread-evenly and
  KEPT in the color's tempering menu: music written inside the
  old system still answers best inside it.
- THE EQUAL-STEP HOME — a key built on a uniform ladder: no
  unequal neighborhood, no address, no home (THINGS holds the
  law). Tried as a tonal system; delivers only weather.
- PARALLEL COUNTERPOINT — two voices moving the same way, called
  two: the ear fuses what moves together and the second voice is
  GONE — not bad, absent. The prohibition on parallel fifths is
  this measurement wearing a rule's clothes. Drains, and did:
  doubling ON PURPOSE became orchestration's thickening tool once
  the two were understood as one bigger voice.
- QUANTIZING THE GROOVE — snapping every note to the grid:
  correct and dead, because the deviations WERE the information
  (measured, consistent, per player). The condition is exact:
  keep the grid AND the deviations. Drains partially where
  machines apply measured deviation profiles — the copy lives
  exactly as far as the measurement reaches.
- THE LOUDNESS WAR — mastering every record to the ceiling:
  louder won the first comparison and lost the listening, because
  senses report change and a held maximum stops being heard;
  dynamics is a curve, and this was the flat line industrialized.
  Second run of pitch inflation's arms race (HISTORY).
- SETTING WORDS AGAINST THEIR STRESS — the loud syllable on a
  weak beat: the singer mangles the word or fights the music,
  every time; two beat patterns must agree or the seam shows.
  Not taste — a collision law, filed because it keeps being
  re-tried.
- ALL-SURPRISE AND ALL-REPETITION — both poles fail the same way:
  constant syncopation with nothing holding the beat re-teaches
  the ear until the offbeats ARE the beat (surprise spent); exact
  repetition without variation loses attention (promise, no
  game). Attention lives between the dull and the alien; both
  edges are cliffs with measurement behind them.
- A WHOLE PIECE AT THE RANGE'S EDGE — strain as a diet: muscles
  at the end of range make force badly and tire fast, so the
  performance degrades on schedule. Strain pays only against a
  livable baseline; the reserve is the method.
- TEACHING THE GRAMMAR AS PHYSICS — theory first, as law, to
  students who haven't heard what it describes: produces
  rule-followers who build the correct and not the moving. The
  order that works is the historical one — music, then the
  noticing. Filed for the teaching phylum as much as this one.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE CASTRATO — a means that WORKED (a grown chest driving an
  unbroken register) and is condemned: the range was bought with
  a child's body and the price ruled unpayable. A certified
  cannot BY DECISION, not physics — ethics' line through this
  subject, and the record that not every drain is wanted.
- EARLY RECORDING'S NARROW DOOR — the first horns caught a slice
  of the spectrum and the quiet died in the wax: basses dropped,
  drums banished, ensembles rebuilt to fit the funnel. Fell short
  as a keeper and RESHAPED what it kept — a means with a narrow
  door edits the art passing through it, and the edit outlives
  the door.
- A MACHINE THAT COMPOSES WHAT MOVES — arrived at competence:
  grammar followed, styles imitated, pieces wholesale. Unsettled
  at depth, honestly: what it must master is the listener's
  prediction handled skillfully, and the written sum that would
  let anyone compute that is the SCIENCE absence above. The
  machine's ceiling and the missing theory are one unknown in
  two coats.
- NOT YET FOUND: A WRITTEN FORM FOR TIMBRE AND GROOVE — no symbol
  system at the resolution performance lives at; the color's
  scores stay seeds until it arrives.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- HEAR ANTIQUITY — bring back the sound the old world actually
  made (HISTORY holds what stands and what would drain it).
- GUARANTEE A FEELING ACROSS A CULTURE LINE — move a stranger
  reliably without sharing their grammar first. Blocked by the
  universal-language receipts: materials travel, grammar doesn't,
  and the grammar carries most of the feeling.
- WRITE THE SURE HIT — compute what will move before it plays.
  Blocked on the missing predictive theory; the industry's
  portfolio is its confession.
- KEEP A PERFORMANCE WHOLE — the score keeps instructions and
  drops the sound; the record keeps sound and drops the
  instructions; the want is both at once, and it stands open.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- THE LOST MUSIC OF ANTIQUITY — what survives: instruments (bone
  flutes ~40,000 years old preserve their SCALES — the holes sit
  where they sit — but not one tune), a few dozen notated Greek
  fragments, one complete short song cut into a tombstone
  (~2,000 years old), an older hymn (~3,400 years) whose reading
  is still argued. What is gone: the rhythm's feel, the voices'
  production, the tunings' living detail — entire musical
  civilizations survive by their theory treatises and not one
  audible bar. Nothing certain — but here are possibilities: play
  the instruments' copies (the scale constrains the possible
  tunes), cross-check fragments against the treatises, mine
  unbroken oral traditions as living constraints. Would drain on:
  a find pairing notation with performing instructions, a longer
  corpus, or a proven unbroken transmission. Until then this
  entry is the subject's sharpest proof that what cannot be
  written into a symbol cannot be inherited.
- THE TEMPERAMENT WARS — centuries of skilled people arguing
  where a quarter of a semitone should land, closed by a bargain,
  not a victory: every key playable, every key slightly wrong,
  thirds paying most. Kept because the arithmetic never changed
  and the question REOPENED the moment the fixed-pitch condition
  fell — electronic instruments retune freely, so the war's
  ground dissolved rather than the war being won. The shimmer,
  run at full length in one subject.
- PITCH INFLATION — "A" crept upward for brilliance, hall by
  hall, until orchestras a semitone apart could not share a
  soloist; surviving forks disagree by more than a semitone. A
  commons drifting without an anchor until a number was agreed —
  the record of why the arbitrary must still be fixed.
- THE LOUDNESS WAR — the same arms race re-run in mastering,
  within living memory, against a known law (senses report
  change). Filed twice on purpose: as a failed method above, and
  here as evidence that a recorded lesson unread is a lesson
  unlearned.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, PARTIAL RETURN: MUSIC ↔ ASTRONOMY — the spheres broke
  as sound and returned as orbital resonance's genuine
  small-ratio physics (THINGS holds it). Rebuilt narrower and
  true: the ratios travel; the song was never there.
- WHIPSAWED, REBUILT WITH A SPEED LIMIT: MUSIC ↔ MEDICINE — the
  Mozart effect inflated and burst; what held under test: rhythm
  steadying a broken gait (the pulse as an external clock for a
  failing internal one), melody carrying words past a lesion that
  stops plain speech. The link now carries the speed limit
  cooking↔nutrition earned: adopt claims at the pace they stop
  reversing.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: MUSIC ↔ ECONOMY (markets) — crowds taught
  a pattern, then paid or broken on its violation; the anomaly
  that stops paying once everyone trades it is syncopation's law
  in another coat. The analogy grips; whether a query can USE the
  relation beyond the analogy is unproven. Filed dotted, awaiting
  a query that cashes it.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: SONG BEFORE SPEECH (music ↔ language) —
  the hints: melody outlives speech in some broken brains,
  infants read contour before words, every language keeps a sung
  register for what matters. Whether song is speech's parent,
  child, or twin is not settled. Would drain on: converging
  developmental and comparative lines no one has yet welded.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: MUSIC ↔ ANIMALS — birdsong runs on the
  same materials (pitch, phrase, dialect, learned transmission);
  whether the bird's listener runs the expectation game — the
  thing that makes sound MUSIC in this phylum — is unknown. Would
  drain on: prediction-violation tests in the animal (surprise at
  broken song grammar), some beginning to exist, none settling
  it.
