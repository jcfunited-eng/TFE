# LANGUAGE — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/language/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and conditions,
so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition. Nothing here is a
tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting for its drain. Seed
authored by Claude and marked as seed, from the same source files
the color names; the measured receipts trace to
docs/fabric_phylums/99_the_white.md and 174, counted 2026-08-29/30.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE FULL SENSE-LIST OF A COMMON WORD — certified unlistable
  UNDER THE CONDITION that the word stays in use: the extension is
  live, and speakers mint senses on need — the short common words
  take the most jobs because they are reached for first. Any
  finished list is out of date the moment it is written. The color
  keeps sense-lists as KNOWLEDGE of the known crowds; they can
  never be the MECHANISM (WALL 4). Would drain on: a dead word in
  a dead tongue — there the list closes, which is exactly why the
  impossibility is conditional and not absolute.
- A LANGUAGE WITHOUT AMBIGUITY — attempted many times: invented
  tongues, codes. Works as code, fails as language: handles are
  few and things many, so reuse is forced, and banning it prices
  every word long where the shortening law demands short. Drained
  PARTIALLY and locally: law's definitions clause and mathematics
  buy precision for one bounded room by paying in length and
  stiffness — the compromise is the answer so far; the global
  version stands white.
- THE EXACT SYNONYM — two handles gripping one spot at one amount:
  never found. Every near-pair splits somewhere — on amount (cold
  against chilly), on verdict (sluggish blames, steady praises),
  on register, or on crowd. Why: two tokens kept identical do no
  extra work, and a distinction nobody uses is not paid for; one
  of the pair drifts. Would drain on: a community caught paying to
  keep two handles identical, and none is written.
- A NEUTRAL WORDING — a sentence carrying zero claim about the
  relation between speakers — cannot be, in tongues that force the
  choice of "you" or of title, and is rare everywhere: register is
  read off every dress, including the plain one, and arriving
  without a tie is also a statement. Would drain on: a room whose
  occupants hold no standings toward each other at all.
- A WRITTEN MARK FOR THE VOICE — attempted for centuries: the
  marks bought back pauses and grouping, and capitals, italics and
  faces are the later bolt-ons. Stress and irony are still not
  recovered, which is why plain notes start fights. Would drain
  on: a mark-set carrying weight and flip that readers actually
  adopt — each century buys back a different fraction, never the
  whole.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- WORD ORDER ALONE AS STRUCTURE — killed, with receipts from the
  benches. The same words nest two ways and mean two things
  (WALL 1). And the count that tried to read a sentence's turning
  word off position — what sits straight after the pointers —
  ranked the THINGS first and the doing last, because a pointer
  arrives FOR a thing: 5 of 15 sentences read right, and the same
  fifteen read 15 of 15 the moment the count was turned around
  into a contrast. Counting after the whole frame instead read 3,
  worse than the error it replaced. Order keeps one lawful job
  (naming roles, in this language), and nothing more. Do not spend
  a night re-proving any of this.
- "LANGUAGES DECAY" — false: the forms complained about are old,
  regular, or both, and every construction now "correct" was once
  a novelty deplored in writing. Change enters through learners at
  every generation; a still language would need a generation that
  learned nothing on its own.
- "SOME TONGUES ARE PRIMITIVE" — false: complexity moves between
  endings, order and particles, and the expressive power does not
  differ. Claims about what languages cannot do rest on a sample
  assembled by wealth and empire, and every few years a
  well-described newcomer overturns a supposed universal.
- "CHILDREN LEARN LANGUAGE BY COPYING" — false: goed and mouses
  were never said in front of them, and a makeshift handed to
  children comes back a full language with tenses nobody in the
  room had used. The learner builds a structure behind the output
  — and when it builds a different one, the language has changed
  and nobody notices for a generation.
- "A WORD'S CLASS FOLLOWS ITS MEANING" — fails on half the
  vocabulary: a word for an event can sit like a thing-word, a
  word for a state like a doing-word. Class is distribution
  (WALL 5); the school definitions stay filed as history.
- "THE DICTIONARY DECIDES" — false as authority: it is a chart of
  a moving coastline, dated when printed. Use moves the words, and
  only use does.
- UNSETTLED: THE MEANING OF MEANING — what the joining of sound
  and sense IS, underneath all the agreements. Nothing certain
  here — but a possibility: meaning as the difference a signal
  makes to what the receiver builds and does next, so that sense
  lives in the change it causes and nowhere else. What would
  settle it: a measure of understanding that needs no second
  speaker to judge it. Until then the color works entirely on
  agreements, contrasts and crowds — which function without ever
  saying what meaning is.
- UNSETTLED: THE SOUND-SYMBOL BIAS — small things take high thin
  vowels, round shapes round sounds, nose-words carry an n past
  chance: real across unrelated tongues, and far too weak to tell
  you any single word. Would drain on: a predictor beating chance
  on words never seen.
- UNSETTLED: HOW FAR THE TONGUE SHAPES THE THOUGHT — the strong
  claim (no word, no thought) broke: speakers notice what their
  language does not name. Weak measured effects stand. Would drain
  on: an effect that survives translating the task itself.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke. Receipts counted 2026-08-29/30 against this fabric's
own writing.

- LACKING: WHAT SEPARATES A CONTENTFUL WORD FROM A MERELY RARE
  ONE — three measures failed. Spread per use: onion 0.60, knife
  0.78, connect 0.79, exclude 0.80 — the contentful and the empty
  sit on top of each other. Concentration in one subject: onion
  0.33, knife 0.28 against connect 0.14 — then wheel 0.13 and
  friction 0.12 fail it, because a word can be contentful AND
  general. Company-averaged reinforcement: exclude came out
  HIGHEST of everything at 0.448, and what separation appeared was
  frequency smuggled into the method. Would drain on: whatever
  tells "general" from "empty" — it is not width of use, which has
  now been counted twice.
- LACKING: WHAT MARKS A POINTER AMONG WORDS IN GENERAL — which
  frame words behave as pointers HERE is counted and works; what a
  pointer IS is not known. The before-and-after lean was counted
  and runs the wrong way: "a" precedes 21,774 times and follows 19,837
  — no asymmetry — while "knife" has 17 distinct followers to 5
  preceders, so the signal is stronger in content words and a test
  built on it breaks groups at the nouns. Would drain on: a
  measurable property separating group-openers from group-fillers
  — and it will not be found in before-and-after counts, because
  those have been counted.
- LACKING: A MODIFIER TOLD FROM A DOER — the located cause of the
  dominant reading error: a phrase carrying two content words
  splits in half, the bare second word arrives "alone", and a
  standing fakes a doing — 91 of 96 misread standings had exactly
  that shape. Four routes closed with numbers: lowering the chunk
  floor (53 and 58 against 62); widening the frame (55, 55, 59
  against 62 — the hundred is already best); a second merging pass
  (no change: the wrongly chosen doing sits inside the run and
  protects itself); merging after non-pointer frame words (wins
  statements five to two and DESTROYS questions — the bench falls
  62 to 29, because the fabric's own writing is all statements and
  a rule measured only on it cannot show what it does to a
  question). Would drain on: something that tells a modifier pair
  from a doer-doing pair without meaning and without hand lists —
  the part-of-speech problem the ribbon exists to avoid, now
  cornered exactly.
- LACKING: AN ORDER TOLD FROM A STATEMENT OF BARE WORDS — the two
  can be the same shape, and this corpus holds no orders to learn
  command words from. The loose marks were measured on 400
  statement-sentences, where every firing is wrong: a pointer
  opening a later group fires on 70 in 100 orders and 28 in 100
  statements; any frame word opening one, 83 and 40; an asking
  word not first, 10 and 4. All three retracted — they bought
  29 of 30 on an all-order bench by misreading two statements in
  five. What stands fires on no statements: the constraint (33 and
  0) plus the learned class on the first word, catching ~37 in 100
  of orders; the honest bench is 17 of 30 against the false 29.
  Would drain on: knowledge in the fabric of what a command word
  is — the gap is in the knowledge, not the reading, and a third
  is the ceiling until something holds it.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: WORD CLASSES TAUGHT FROM THE RIBBON'S OWN
  READINGS — the classes are real (doing-doing 0.91, thing-thing
  1.00, doing-thing 0.25; seeds from the 625 readings the contrast
  settles alone, no hand lists) and they fail in use five ways: as
  a filter, 60 in 100 against 63; sighting-gated at five floors,
  59 to 62; as a tiebreak, noise; on carried groups, level; on
  bare groups, one better while the doing bench falls 62 to 25.
  The shape of the failure is constant: right about common words,
  answers "thing" for rare ones — and rare is where the doings
  live. Would drain on: a class judgement that degrades gracefully
  on thin evidence instead of defaulting. The most promising thing
  standing, and five failures make the promise itself worth
  doubting.
- LACKING: A KIND-RELATION SIGNAL (whether one word names a kind
  of another) — three routes closed. Company containment reads
  frequency: a true kind-pair and an unrelated pair both score
  0.57 one way, because a narrow word's company sits inside a wide
  word's company whatever the two are. Overlap against chance is
  worse: it ranks a one-story coincidence highest of all pairs at
  9.33. The corpus's declared root-edges carry nothing
  word-to-word: dog to animal 0 of 15, copper to metal 0 of 14,
  gravity to physics 0 of 13 — while a false pair scores 4.
  Likeliest reading: this writing HOLDS no taxonomy — it says how
  things work and never says a dog is an animal — so the honest
  crossing answers that nothing written says so. That reading is
  itself reasoning: test before believing it, which is how the
  last false rule got in. Would drain on either of two things: a
  kind-signal that is neither company overlap nor the declared
  hierarchy — both counted, both reading something else — or
  kind-lines written into the fabric as knowledge, a sentence that
  SAYS a dog is an animal, at which point no signal is needed.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- THE SENSE TABLE AS MECHANISM — fetch-the-meaning from a stored
  list: killed by the live extension (the THINGS entry above). The
  lists stay as knowledge of the known crowds, and nothing more.
- HAND LABELLING AS A BENCH — scoring the reading against the
  builder's judgement of English measures agreement with the
  builder, not understanding: against a yardstick not hand-made
  (each entry's own declared subject line), the reading scored 73
  in 100 and a control picking RANDOM words scored 74. Standing,
  and forbidden. Drains to nothing: benches must find yardsticks
  that are nobody's opinion — the arithmetic bench in the color's
  MEANS is the shape that cannot be gamed.
- SCORING ON SENTENCES THE BUILDER CHOSE — 63 of 70 on
  constructed sentences against 3 of 20 on sentences drawn by seed
  from the real writing; every chosen sentence had the one shape
  the reading gets right. Holding a set back does not fix it — the
  same hand wrote the held-back set and reached for the same
  shape. Drains to: cases generated by rule, drawn by seed, with
  THAT number the one reported.
- MEASURING ON ONE DISTRIBUTION, SHIPPING AGAINST ANOTHER — made
  three times in one day: a statement-rule that wrecked questions,
  order-marks that wrecked statements, a bench built only for the
  new case. Condition to stand down, permanent: both arms on the
  same corpus, and every bench run through the exact path that
  ships.
- CARRYING CHUNKS TO SPEAK WITH — nothing to carry: this writing
  almost never says the same phrase twice, three-word runs at the
  habit floor exist for almost no content word, and the chunks
  that do exist are field names. Chunk-carrying works for READING
  here and fails for SAYING. Drained differently — the build order
  run forwards speaks bare parts in role order — and three bare
  words are not prose: not solved.
- THE BEARING RULE — one word bears the other when its share of
  the shared company runs twice the share the other way: reasoned
  instead of measured, written unmarked as though the fabric held
  it, falsified the same day (numbers in SCIENCE). The lasting
  lesson is provenance: a construct carries its author's name
  until it earns another. Would drain on the kind-signal SCIENCE
  names and still lacks — company overlap is not it, counted two
  ways.
- TRANSLATION WITHOUT LOSS — cannot, under conditions worth
  indexing: five things ride every expression — sense, sound,
  level, association, length — and no crossing carries all five;
  each keeps some by spending the rest, and every translation is
  an argument about which mattered here. DRAINS exactly where
  fewer than five matter: a torque table crosses whole (sense
  only), a contract crosses with sense and level, a poem never
  crosses. The condition-index is the useful knowledge;
  "untranslatable", said bare, is not.
- EXAMPLE SENTENCES WRITTEN INTO READING-KNOWLEDGE — an
  illustration becomes a sighting: one phrase written twice as an
  example reached the chunk floor, and the very sentence it
  explained stopped being readable. Worked around — write the
  rule, never the sentence it applies to — not solved. Would drain
  on: a mark the counting skips, which is a change to how every
  file is written.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- LETTER-MATCHING AS MEANING-MATCHING — fell short in every way
  the color's guard-list names: letters are a cheap way to find
  candidates and a terrible way to settle anything. Kept strictly
  as the finder; one shared word remains a coincidence.
- SPELLING AS A SOUND MAP — the deep spellings record the history
  of the words instead, and cost years; they pay it back by
  keeping kin words visibly kin and staying readable across
  accents. The two aims cannot both be served fully; every system
  pays somewhere, and reform stands white against the installed
  base — a script is adopted once and inherited forever.
- DENIAL LINES AS A SECOND COPY OF CLAIMS — 5,427 hand-written
  negatives removed deliberately: what a thing holds is written
  once, as the claim, and the negative is read off it. The related
  receipt: of 2,938 denials once written, everyday words dragged
  1,629 of them into gripping anything at all — a wall made of
  common words is not a wall, and what a denial forbids must be
  named in uncommon words. Do not put the second copy back.
- NOT YET FOUND: A MARK TELLING ILLUSTRATION FROM FACT in written
  knowledge — see METHODS; the counting cannot tell an example
  from a habit of the language.
- NOT YET FOUND: AN INSTRUMENT READING INTENT — no means reads a
  speaker's meant sense directly; company infers it, and thinly
  where the writing is thin. A possible next means, untried: the
  sense produced from the company OF the company, so a word two
  steps out supplies the line the sentence's own words do not.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- GUARANTEE THE SAME SENSE BUILT AT BOTH ENDS — cannot: nothing
  passes between two heads but the signal, and the sense is made
  twice from different materials. The usual failure is not
  confusion but smooth agreement inside two different frames, both
  sides nodding, found only where the work lands. Narrowable —
  shared ground named out loud, one question fetched before the
  answer, the repair habit — and never closable; the standing
  possibility is to price the checking into the talk.
- READ A MIND OFF ITS WORDS — a named person's present thought is
  the unanswerable class: the question's shape looks ordinary and
  the wall is total; even reported reasons are reconstructions.
- A LANGUAGE ONE CANNOT LIE IN — form and sense are separate
  machines, so a well-built sentence in a confident mouth passes
  as a thought, and no grammar inspects cargo. What CAN be had is
  a room where claims carry their hedges and their grounds —
  bought by convention, never by the tongue itself.
- SPEAK FLUENT PROSE FROM THIS FABRIC — blocked on the
  carried-chunk gap in METHODS: the parts exist, the joints are
  named, the small words do not come out; three bare words in role
  order is the present ceiling.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- The benches that lied, in order: fifteen of fifteen by
  selection; twenty-nine of thirty on orders while two statements
  in five were being misread as orders; a bench quietly pruned of
  its failing case reading perfect — each found, each written back
  with the reason beside it. A bench not run through the shipping
  path is not a bench, and a test pruned until it passes is hand
  labelling in different clothes.
- The digits that vanished: every reading pattern kept letters
  only, so a request to add two numbers arrived with no numbers in
  it — the sentence's only precise cargo discarded before reading
  began. Found late, fixed, and filed so no future reader trusts a
  pattern by its looks.
- The stemmer that made strangers of one word's forms — rise and
  rises never met, so the plainest sentence in the fabric found no
  company, and the defect had been waved off as cosmetic that same
  day. The lesson holds past stemming: a defect is cosmetic only
  until it is measured against the thing it blocks.
- The is-blindness: the reading was built for sentences where
  something acts on something, and 1,411 of this fabric's 3,212
  sentences — near half — say what something IS. The blindness was
  invisible in every hand-picked bench because the picking hand
  liked doers. The color's standing method was bought against this
  receipt, 44 against 40 on seeded sentences.
- Language death runs a generation ahead of its last speaker: the
  handing-over stops in households wherever the work, the
  schooling and the future live in the other tongue, and by the
  time only the old speak it the outcome was settled decades
  before. Classrooms produce people who know ABOUT a language — a
  different thing from a community that speaks it; revival works
  exactly where occasions are restored, a market, a playground,
  and nowhere else so far.
- What dies with a tongue is the system of distinctions, not the
  labels: a dictionary keeps the words and drops the web that gave
  them their edges — this subject's clearest proof that meaning is
  relational (WALL 10, wearing mourning).
- The invented rules: centuries of "proper" forms reasoned from
  Latin by individuals and enforced by schooling and hiring,
  banning constructions older and more regular than the
  corrections — the standing proof that a correctness claim needs
  its source named.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN AT FULL STRENGTH: LANGUAGE ↔ MIND as determination — the
  claim that the tongue fixes what can be thought failed; the
  weak, measurable residue stands and is filed under CLAIMS with
  its drain.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: SOUND ↔ SENSE — the symbolism bias is real
  and unusably weak; dotted until a predictor beats chance on
  unseen words.
- SUSPECTED ABSENT: KIND-RELATIONS ↔ THIS WRITING — three closed
  routes point at a corpus with no taxonomy in it (SCIENCE); the
  relation everything assumed may not be there to find, and the
  honest crossing says so instead of manufacturing one. Test
  before believing: that verdict is reasoning, and reasoning is
  how the last false rule shipped.
- BROKEN AS A METHOD, STANDING AS A WARNING: LANGUAGE ↔ COMPUTING
  by best-hit weighting — a reader that returns the best stored
  match can only return what was stored; five of five answers
  were already in the corpus before the question was asked.
  Ranking is the tell. The relation the first ribbon needs runs
  the other way: knowledge constraining a reading that is BUILT,
  never fetched.
