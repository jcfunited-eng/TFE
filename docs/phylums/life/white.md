# LIFE — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/life/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting
for its drain. Seed authored by Claude and marked as seed.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- A LIVING THING WITHOUT A PARENT — believed real for two
  thousand years (maggots from meat, mice from grain). CANNOT BE
  under the certified conditions: sterilized matter, sealed
  against incoming cells and dust, stays lifeless indefinitely.
  THE SHIMMER: outside those conditions — the early Earth,
  unsterile, unsealed, ages to spend — it happened at least once,
  because we are here. One entry read at two conditions; the
  origin half is in SCIENCE below.
- THE IMMORTAL ORGANISM — the old want, with partial receipts: a
  jellyfish (Turritopsis) that runs its development BACKWARD to
  the polyp and starts over; hydra aging unmeasurably; a pine
  ~5,000 years old; a clam past 500; the naked mole-rat, whose
  yearly odds of death do NOT rise with age. The receipts prove
  aging is not a law of living matter. They do not give it in a
  large warm animal, nor any shield against being eaten, sick, or
  crushed — reversal is not invulnerability. Would drain on: a
  mechanism from the ageless kinds shown to run in a mammal.
- A LIVING THING WITHOUT CELLS — the virus stands at the door:
  recipe in a coat, no eating, no excreting, no engine; it runs
  only by borrowing a cell's machinery. Whether "alive" includes
  it is definitional and unsettled — the cell claim's edge is
  drawn by a word, not by nature. Settles by ruling or by find:
  fix the fence (own engine required, or not) and the virus lands
  accordingly; a found virus running any engine of its own would
  move it bodily into the color.
- LIFE ON ANOTHER CHEMISTRY — no water, or no carbon: wondered
  long, found never. Not certified impossible — no law forbids
  it; the search has simply returned nothing. Would drain on one
  example (the dotted relation below).
- THE UNCULTURED MAJORITY — most microbes on Earth are known ONLY
  as recipe-text read from soil and sea, never grown, never seen
  working: units real but unheld. Draining slowly (see MEANS).

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- SPONTANEOUS GENERATION — held from antiquity because it always
  APPEARED to happen (meat visibly "makes" maggots in days).
  Killed in stages: Redi's screen (1668) — no flies in, no
  maggots out; Pasteur's swan-neck (1859-61) — boiled broth, air
  freely admitted, dust and cells trapped in the bend: clear for
  years; snap the neck, teeming in days. WHY believed: the
  arriving cells were invisible, so the effect seemed causeless.
  Certified cannot UNDER: sterile start + barrier to incoming
  life. Alive outside those conditions as the origin question.
- VITALISM — "the chemicals of life carry a force no bench can
  make." Killed by one synthesis: urea, a kidney's own product,
  made from mineral stock (1828), no life anywhere in the path —
  the whole catalogue followed. WHY believed: every prior attempt
  had failed, and the failure of tries was mistaken for a law of
  nature. The warning label: absence of a success is not a wall.
- ACQUIRED TRAITS PASS TO CHILDREN — the blacksmith's arms, the
  stretched neck. Tested directly: tails cut off mice generation
  after generation, ~900 young — not one born short-tailed. WHY
  it fails: the body's changes do not write back into the recipe
  the child is dealt. LIVE SHIMMER, narrow: chemical tags ON the
  recipe (not in its letters) can carry a generation or two; the
  broad claim stays dead, the narrow form drains at its own size.
- BLENDING INHERITANCE — children as a paint-mix of parents.
  Fails its own arithmetic: blending halves the spread of
  variation each generation — (1/2)^10 leaves ~1/1,000 of it in
  ten — so selection would starve for material, yet variation
  visibly persists. Killed by the counted 3:1: particles deal
  whole, hide, and return. Its corpse points at what inheritance
  must be: particulate, or evolution stops.
- EVOLUTION AS A LADDER — "toward perfection, toward us." Didn't
  hold: selection fits kinds to PRESENT conditions only; the
  bacterium is not on its way to becoming anyone. WHY believed:
  we stood at the top of our own diagram. What replaced it is in
  the color: no foresight, no direction, only fit-now.
- UNSETTLED: WHY EVERY ANIMAL SLEEPS — universal across animals
  with brains, at savage cost (hours defenseless, unfed), and
  never deleted: dolphins, which must surface to breathe, sleep
  one brain half at a time rather than skip it — the workaround
  is the receipt that the need is absolute. Candidates, each
  gripping part: the brain's night washing (waste clears faster
  in sleep), memory filing, energy budget. Nothing certain — but
  here is a possibility: sleep is maintenance that cannot run
  during use, as a road cannot be paved under traffic. Would
  drain on: a mechanism shown necessary, or an animal that fully
  lives without it.
- UNSETTLED: WHY SEX IS WORTH DOUBLE — a cloner hands EVERY child
  the whole recipe; a sexual parent hands half. Twofold cost per
  generation, paid by nearly every large kind — the benefit must
  be huge and steady. Candidates: shuffled offspring as moving
  targets for fast-compounding parasites (receipts: snail
  populations go clonal where parasites are rare, sexual where
  they swarm); purging typo loads. Would drain on: measured
  parasite pressure flipping the advantage in the wild.
- UNSETTLED: WHY AGING RUNS AT SUCH DIFFERENT SPEEDS — a mouse
  gets ~3 years; a bat of the same size ~40; the mole-rat's odds
  never worsen. The fourth-root law (color) ties burn rate to
  lifespan and the bat breaks it by an order of magnitude. Would
  drain on: the bat's and mole-rat's mechanisms named and moved
  into another kind.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: RECIPE TO BODY — the letters can all be read, and the
  organism still cannot be computed from them. Half a drain has
  arrived as the shimmer predicts: one protein's SHAPE from its
  letters is now computable — the fold problem, open fifty years,
  largely fell. The development problem — recipe plus egg plus
  conditions to a whole body — stands. The subject's largest
  named absence.
- LACKING: THE ORIGIN ACCOUNT — from chemistry to the first cell,
  no path runs end to end. The parts come easily: sparked early-
  atmosphere gases yield amino acids in days (1953); fatty films
  self-close into cell-sized bags unaided; one molecule (RNA) can
  be both recipe and tool, and pieces of such machinery still run
  in every living cell. Three candidate roads (recipe-first,
  metabolism-first at seafloor vents, container-first), each
  gripping a segment, none joined. Nothing certain — but here is
  a possibility: the roads are not rivals but stretches of one
  road. Would drain on: a self-copying chemistry demonstrated
  from plausible early-Earth stock without hand-feeding.
- LACKING: A LAW FOR EXPERIENCE IN LIVING MATTER — some
  arrangements of cells feel like something from inside; no
  written law says which, why, or how to measure it from outside.
  Nothing is certain here — but here is a possibility: feeling
  tracks what an arrangement DOES, not what it is made of — how
  much of itself it binds into one traffic at once — so a measure
  of that inner traffic could rank arrangements from outside.
  Would drain on: an outside measure that calls, in advance,
  which systems behave as feeling ones; anesthesia, where such
  measures already track the lights going out, is the one
  gripping edge.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: HEREDITY NUMBERS TAKEN PAST THEIR
  CONDITIONS — inheritance measures computed on populations were
  applied to individuals and to law (the eugenics decades): the
  numbers do not transfer across environments and do not rank
  persons. The guard, filed as knowledge: a population statistic
  states its population and conditions or states nothing; harm
  followed wherever that line was ignored.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
wings-on-arms entries, waiting for their planet.

- FIGHTING DISEASE BY FIGHTING BAD AIR (miasma) — half-worked,
  which made it dangerous: draining stinking water often removed
  the germs ALONG WITH the smell, so the method banked credit for
  a mechanism it didn't have — the smuggled-signal pattern. It
  failed exactly where smell and germ parted: a clean-smelling
  well spread cholera until the pump handle came off (1854).
  Drained INTO germ theory: keep the cleaning, replace the reason.
- BLANKET ANTIBIOTICS — the drug everywhere, always, in feed and
  for every sniffle. Works at first, then fails ON SCHEDULE by
  the color's resistance arithmetic: every exposure is a selection
  round, and ~10 pre-written resistant cells per billion need
  hours to inherit the field. CONDITION: works only while
  resistant variants are absent or cost-burdened — a condition
  use itself destroys. A method whose own success removes its
  working condition.
- FREEZING A WHOLE BODY FOR LATER — works for cells in vials
  (glycerol against the spears, −196°C, decades); fails at organ
  and body scale: ice spears and cracking wreck fine structure.
  Partial receipt for the drain: a rabbit kidney vitrified
  glass-hard, thawed, and it worked. Would drain on: vitrifying
  large organs without poisoning doses of the anti-ice agents.
- BREEDING BY DECREE (the Lysenko program) — crops improved on
  the dead acquired-traits claim, enforced by the state: hardening
  treatments claimed to inherit; harvests said no, season after
  season; famine counted the cost, and the counting itself was
  punished. The method dies with its claim; the deeper failure —
  striking a true claim by decree — is HISTORY's below.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- LIGHT BELOW 0.2 MICRONS — the light microscope's floor is
  light's own wavelength; the small half of biology stayed
  invisible for two centuries. CANNOT BE with visible light — and
  it drained the day the illumination stopped being light:
  electron beams, then tricks that make single glowing molecules
  betray positions finer than the light that shows them. The wall
  stood; the condition moved; wings found their thinner air.
- A GENERAL VIRUS KILLER — antibiotics hit machinery bacteria
  have and we lack; a virus USES OUR machinery, so broad poisons
  aimed at it land on us. Fell short as a general mean; stands as
  precision means: vaccines ahead of time, drugs shaped to one
  virus's own few tools. Would drain on: a target common to
  viruses and absent from hosts; none is written.
- GROWING THE UNCULTURED MAJORITY — the dish that grew the famous
  microbes refuses ~99% of wild kinds: it lacks their neighbors,
  their trades, their chemistry. Draining at the edges: weak
  broths, long patience, chambers buried back in the soil they
  came from. Each new grower moves a unit to the color's sheet.
- LIFE PAUSED WARM — cold pauses life; nothing yet pauses it at
  room temperature ON DEMAND. The receipts say it is not
  forbidden: dried tardigrades have returned after ~10 years
  (~30 frozen); a ~2,000-year-old
  seed germinated; both dry into a sugar-glass. Not found: doing
  that to a human cell line, organ, or person and returning them.
  Would drain on: controlled glassing and waking of large wet
  tissue.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- MAKE A LIVING CELL FROM PARTS — blocked on the origin absence.
  Partial receipt: a full recipe, chemically written, was
  installed in an existing emptied cell and ran it (2010) — the
  text can be authored; the CELL, the running system, has never
  been built from stock. The gap between those two sentences is
  the exact size of the want.
- STOP OR REVERSE AGING — the jellyfish and the mole-rat prove no
  law forbids it; no means exists for us. Everything known moves
  the edges (years, not ages); nothing yet moves the center.
- BRING BACK THE LOST KINDS — recipe fragments read well from old
  bones (mammoth); but a kind is recipe PLUS conditions — womb,
  mother, herd, world — and conditions do not fossilize. The
  honest near-term drain is a proxy: an edited living cousin, a
  new kind wearing an old coat, and filed as such.
- PREDICT EVOLUTION FORWARD — the arithmetic is exact in
  hindsight, contingent forward: which typo arrives first is
  chance. Receipt of the ceiling: the yearly flu pick, made by
  the best-informed process there is, buys ~40-60% protection in
  good years — a forecast, never a computation. Would drain on:
  constrained cases (few paths open) forecast repeatedly and
  verified — early receipts exist in resistance-path studies.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- Two thousand years of spontaneous generation: the belief was
  not lazy — it had daily evidence and published recipes (wheat
  plus a sweaty shirt, 21 days, mice), each "working" by leaving
  the door open to what walked in. It took the screen, then the
  flask, to kill, because the true cause was invisible. The
  color's sterile technique is this record, inverted to practice.
- Semmelweis: measured that hand-washing dropped childbed deaths
  from ~18% to ~2%, said so, and was mocked, ruined, and dead
  before germ theory vindicated him. A TRUE claim failing
  socially for want of a mechanism — cost counted in mothers;
  lesson filed: a measured effect outranks a missing explanation.
- Mendel's 34 unread years: the inheritance arithmetic sat
  published and unconnected while the field argued blending. The
  fabric lesson, plainly: knowledge without relations is
  knowledge lost; the entry was true and still did not exist
  until CONNECTED.
- Lysenko's decades: a true claim deleted by decree — the
  selection arithmetic banned as ideology, its defenders jailed,
  the harvests arbitrating anyway. Price paid in famine.
  Arithmetic does not negotiate.
- Fleming's 1945 warning: resistance predicted publicly, from the
  arithmetic, before it was widespread — and use ran ahead
  anyway. A filed closure only saves the search of those who
  read it.

  flask, replaced by a single dotted line at the origin only,
  carried in SCIENCE above. Everyday traffic closed; ancient
  traffic is the open question.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: SLEEP ↔ THE BRAIN'S NIGHT MAINTENANCE —
  the washing observation grips (body, medicine); whether it is
  THE reason or one of several is unsettled; dotted until the
  sleep entry drains.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: LIFE ↔ OTHER WORLDS — the chemistry says
  possible, physics and weather name the candidate seas, and the
  count of found examples is zero. One find drains it and
  rewrites the THINGS list; dotted until then.
- BROKEN, REBUILT WITH A GUARD: LIFE ↔ LAW AND ECONOMY — twice
  this relation carried more than the science could hold (the
  eugenics decades outbound, Lysenko inbound); both directions
  paid in lives. The rebuilt link carries its guard as cargo:
  population numbers state their conditions or state nothing,
  and no decree moves the arithmetic.
