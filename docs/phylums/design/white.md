# DESIGN — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/design/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. Seed authored by Claude from
docs/fabric_phylums/28_image_design.md,
docs/fabric_phylums/167_deep_design_and_products.md, and
docs/fabric_phylums/96_deep_arts.md. What didn't hold carries its
WHY in quantities and conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns
move a condition. Nothing here is a tombstone.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE AVERAGE USER — designed for everywhere, does not exist. The
  receipt: ~4,000 pilots measured on ten body dimensions, with
  "average" generous (the middle 30% band on each); pilots average
  on all ten: ZERO. Were the ten independent, 0.3¹⁰ ≈ 0.000006 —
  about 0.02 expected people in four thousand; real, correlated
  bodies still delivered none. CANNOT BE under "one fixed shape
  fits the middle"; drained not by a better average but by
  adjustability — design for the range, let the seat move.
- A FORMULA FOR BEAUTY — tried in earnest by a real mathematician:
  beauty = order ÷ complexity, computed for polygons and vases.
  Receipts failed twice: judged rankings disagreed with the
  formula's beyond the simplest cases, and the quotient crowns the
  barest square forever — order and complexity behave as a
  tension, not a fraction. WHY any such try struggles: one number
  ranks all works on one axis, and force, truth, strangeness, and
  beauty are separate currencies. Would drain on: a measure stated
  IN ADVANCE that predicts judgments across cultures on works
  never yet seen.
- THE UNIVERSAL ICON LANGUAGE — attempted repeatedly. A genuine
  handful travel everywhere; the rest mean what a community agreed
  and nothing outside it — a screen of unlabeled symbols is a
  memory test dressed as a clean design. Partially drained where
  the meaning rides in the shape's own promise (the arrow that
  points, the handle that asks); the rest waits.
- THE NEUTRAL DEFAULT — cannot be, wherever a default exists:
  whatever is preselected is kept by most and reads as a
  recommendation. The only choice is which behavior to decide for
  the majority, never whether to.
- THE SELF-EXPLAINING MACHINE — the design that needs no manual:
  the want, open. Partially real: the door that needs no sign, the
  spoon, the one-way plug — achieved wherever the WHOLE use fits
  in the shape. Open in general, with the why in quantities: a
  shape can say a handful of things at once, and a machine that
  does a hundred cannot say them all with form — the channel is
  narrower than the cargo. Standing partial drains: show few and
  keep the rest one step away; borrow conventions already taught;
  explain at the moment of use. Would drain further on richer
  channels — form that changes to show only what is currently
  possible, motion and sound as added sayings.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "THE GOLDEN RATIO IS BEAUTY'S LAW" — the algebra is real (the
  color holds it); the beauty claim fails its receipts: preference
  tests rerun for a century spread across ~1.4-1.8 with the peak
  moving by method and decade — no spike at 1.618; facades fit
  only when the lines are chosen after the fact; the bank card
  (1.586) misses by 2% and is cited anyway. WHY believed: a number
  that explains beauty is a beautiful idea. Would drain on a
  preregistered test finding the sharp peak; none has.
- "USERS CAN TELL YOU WHAT THEY WANT" — asked, they describe a
  faster horse: people can name what they have seen and cannot
  name what does not exist. The fix is in the color: ask what they
  were doing when it went wrong, and watch.
- "MORE CHOICE SERVES THE CHOOSER" — holds to a point, fails past
  it: decision cost grows with the options considered, so a wide
  field is answered by not considering — the sixty-item list is
  abandoned or answered from the top (the arithmetic is in the
  color's clock).
- "A WARNING WILL PREVENT IT" — the dialog that appears every time
  stops being read; habit clears the button, so the safeguard
  fails precisely on the occasion it existed for — silently,
  having appeared to work. Certified under "same prompt, every
  time, no consequence"; the rare, consequence-bearing
  interruption still works — that is the drain.
- "FORM FOLLOWS FUNCTION" AS A COMPLETE ACCOUNT — half held, half
  fell. Held: form must not lie about use. Fell: function
  underdetermines form — many shapes serve one job, so convention,
  meaning, and the material's refusals always finish the choice;
  pretending otherwise only hides that second law from argument.
- "BUILD IT BETTER AND THEY WILL SWITCH" — fails against the
  arithmetic of conventions: the improvement is charged to the
  visitor as relearning they never asked for. Drains only when the
  gain exceeds the relearning — rare, computable case by case.
- CONSISTENCY'S DARK HALF — the same law that lets a pattern be
  learned once also preserves mistakes: once a wrong thing is
  everywhere, correcting it in one place makes the product feel
  broken, so the error hardens into convention and is copied into
  the next thing — the keyboard layout arranged for a machine that
  no longer exists. Certified under "the pattern is already learned
  by everyone"; drains only at a platform break, when everything
  must be relearned anyway.
- UNSETTLED: WHY TASTE CHANGES — the pendulum is real (ornament to
  plainness and back); the mechanism contested. Nothing certain —
  but here are possibilities that each grip a part: (1) contrast
  against the field — when every surface is ornamented, plainness
  is the biggest difference on the page, so the eye-grammar itself
  pushes taste toward what the field lacks (a pendulum, never a
  direction); (2) wear-through — what works is copied until it is
  processed as one unit and stops reaching the senses, and taste
  flees the numbness; (3) the status chase — as a look spreads
  down, its top abandons it to stay distinguishable. Would drain
  on: a model stated in advance that beats chance on the next
  turn.
- UNSETTLED: WHETHER BEAUTY IS ONE THING AT ALL — strong works
  trade beauty away for force, truth, or strangeness, arguing for
  separate currencies; yet judges agree across cultures more than
  chance allows, arguing for a shared core. Both receipts stand.
  Would drain on: finding what the cross-culture agreement tracks.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A PREDICTIVE THEORY OF DELIGHT — the color computes
  legibility, reach, contrast, and read order, and cannot compute
  whether the assembled thing will please before a person meets
  it. The stranger remains the only instrument; five of them is
  the working substitute for the missing law.
- LACKING: A MEASURE OF "ONE WORLD" — viewers instantly file an
  element from another world (one bad effect shot, a plastic
  window in a stone wall) as error before judging it at all; no
  written measure computes that unity ahead of the viewer. Named
  because so much craft serves a law nobody can state.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: DESIGNING TO THE AVERAGE — the zero-of-4,000
  arithmetic (THINGS) broke every fit-the-middle design that met
  real bodies; replaced by design-for-the-range.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: MEASUREMENT AS THE DESIGNER — the comparison
  test finds only the better of the two things put in front of it;
  steered by it alone, a design climbs the nearest small hill and
  is never offered the mountain. Filed as a speed limit: measure
  to refine, never to originate (the rebuilt relation is below).

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- THE OPENING TOUR — teach everything first, then let them in.
  Remembered by nobody: given before anything means anything, and
  a lesson without a use is stored badly and retrieved worse.
  Drains to its inversion — findable at the moment of want.
- DESIGN BY COMMITTEE — every taste in the room removes what the
  others find strange; what remains is the part nobody objected
  to, which is the part nobody remembers. Certified under "many
  hands, no single authority"; drains the moment one person owns
  the refusals.
- THE SECOND SYSTEM — the successor built by someone who has
  succeeded once, carrying every idea cut from the first; arrives
  late, huge, and worse. WHY: the first's constraints were doing
  much of the design, and success taught they were the enemy.
  Drains only through kept constraints — a hard budget written
  before the confidence arrives.
- DESIGNING FOR YOURSELF AS PROXY FOR THE STRANGER — structurally
  broken, not morally: knowledge cannot be un-had, so the maker
  cannot simulate not-knowing. Only the actual stranger drains it.
- ASKING HYPOTHETICALS — "would you use this?" measures the
  respondent's imagination of a calm, interested person with time
  — who will not be the one who turns up. Drains to watching real
  behavior on real tasks.
- THE SETTINGS SCREEN AS PEACE TREATY — every switch an argument
  nobody settled, shipped to a user with less information than
  either side and no interest in the question. Drains to defaults
  chosen on purpose, the surviving switches earning their place.
- GROWTH BY REASONABLE REQUESTS — each feature small, requested,
  defensible; the sum unexplainable, the original purpose one item
  on a crowded surface. WHY it recurs: the asker is present and
  the harmed user is not; refusing is invisible work nobody is
  thanked for. Drains only where someone's job is to refuse.
- BEAUTY SPENT ON THE WRONG PURPOSE — the beautiful building that
  leaks, the handsome screen nobody can use. Condition: appearance
  is read as evidence of care everywhere else, so unearned beauty
  is not neutral — it hides the fault, including from the makers.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE ICON WITHOUT A WORD — a guess outside the community that
  learned it; stands only where the meaning rides in the shape
  itself. The pairing with a word is the drain.
- COLOR AS THE ONLY CARRIER — fails a share of every audience (a
  large minority cannot separate certain pairs), fails screens
  that shift it, fails entirely at the photocopy: whatever is said
  in color alone is said to some of the audience and not the rest.
  Drains by redundancy — shape, label, or position carrying the
  same meaning — never by better color.
- THE FLATTERING PROGRESS BAR — lies to look better and destroys
  the only thing it was for: a signal is worth its accuracy
  record, and a person misled once reads every later bar as
  decoration.
- THE MANUAL — the document a design writes when it has failed to
  explain itself. Sometimes unavoidable (a genuinely deep machine
  must be taught); mostly a paragraph was cheaper than fixing the
  shape, and it arrives before the need and is forgotten by it.
  Fell short as a repair; stands as a confession the next version
  should read.
- NOT YET FOUND: AN INSTRUMENT THAT PROPOSES — every measuring
  tool compares what exists; nothing measures the design not yet
  built. The working substitute is in the color: generate the
  candidates by hand, then let the instruments compare them.
- NOT YET FOUND: A TEST FOR TEN-YEARS-LATER — no cheap instrument
  reads how a design wears: what charms at first meeting and
  grates by the hundredth. Repeat encounters are the missing
  dimension of every quick test.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- THE DESIGN THAT NEEDS NO MANUAL, IN GENERAL — open; the why in
  quantities and the partial drains are filed under THINGS.
- SERVE THE FIRST DAY AND THE THOUSANDTH HOUR WITH ONE FACE — the
  two want opposites: guidance against speed, prompts against
  shortcuts; practice turns deliberate steps automatic, and what
  supports the deliberate stage obstructs the automatic one.
  Partial drains exist (few-shown-rest-near, shortcuts); the full
  want stands open, and most products quietly choose the beginner
  and lose their best users.
- PLEASE EVERYONE — forbidden by committee arithmetic run on the
  audience itself: distinctiveness lives in the parts somebody
  objects to, so the design everyone accepts is the one nobody
  remembers. A property of averaging, not a failure of skill.
- PREDICT TASTE'S NEXT TURN — blocked on the unsettled mechanism
  in CLAIMS; until a possibility there drains, position for the
  pendulum without pretending to time it.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- THE PURGE LEDGER — every generation that stripped ornament
  believed it final: the moral purges, the crime essay, the flat
  screens. Each shipped real gains (the lying ornament died) and
  each was partly reversed, because the purged decoration had been
  DOING jobs nobody inventoried — marking joints, taking wear,
  showing rank, saying "pressable." A warning shaped like a law:
  before stripping a thing, list its jobs; skip the inventory and
  repay it with interest inside a decade.
- THE CANON AS A FILTERED SAMPLE — what survives is what was
  durable, lucky, and fashionable in the intervening centuries;
  survival is not quality. Comparing yourself with the old masters
  is comparing with a filter that misleads in one direction — the
  past looks better than it was. Filed with the filter named.
- THE DECADES OF BLAMING PEOPLE — wheels-up landings charged to
  pilot carelessness, drug swaps to nurse carelessness, year upon
  year, while the identical levers and identical vials sat
  unindicted. The error rate was always a property of the object,
  measurable before anyone was hurt; the mischarging bought
  nothing but repetition. What drained it — counting errors per
  design instead of per person — is now the color's law.
- THE DARK LEDGER — the color's laws read backwards and aimed at
  the user: habit farmed by the dialog, defaults set against the
  majority that keeps them, cancellation routed through the
  labyrinth. Filed as history and not method because it works —
  that is the warning — and because law has begun pricing it.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, THEN REBUILT WITH A LIMIT: DESIGN ↔ MEASUREMENT — the
  comparison test steered designs up small hills and away from
  mountains until the limit was learned; the standing link now
  carries its rule (refine by measurement, originate by making),
  which is itself knowledge about the relation.
- CARRIES A SIGN: DESIGN ↔ ETHICS — identical laws serve or prey
  depending on whom the effort is aimed at; every crossing should
  read the sign before trusting the destination.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: HARMONY-BY-SHARED-CAUSE ↔ MUSIC'S ONE KEY —
  one light contaminating every color and one key relating every
  chord look like the same law in two coats; whether a query can
  cash the identity (carry a working method across, not an
  analogy) is unproven. Filed dotted, awaiting the crossing that
  cashes it.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: TASTE'S PENDULUM ↔ ECONOMY'S FASHION CYCLE
  — the contrast-against-the-field possibility (CLAIMS) and the
  economy's crowd-and-flee arithmetic rhyme; if one mechanism runs
  both, a predictor found in either phylum drains the other's
  unsettled entry. Dotted until one side produces it.
- BROKEN AS A ONE-WAY STREET: DESIGN ↔ TEACHING — the old link ran
  "design, then teach the users"; the manual's record (MEANS)
  broke it. Rebuilt reversed: teaching happens inside the design
  at the moment of use; the separate lesson is the fallback.
