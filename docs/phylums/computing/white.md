# COMPUTING — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/computing/color.md: same parts,
opposite sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
This subject is unusual: several of its cannots are minted by
proof, not physics — they bind every machine that could ever be
built, on any substrate. Even those are indexed by conditions,
because the conditions are exact and stepping outside them is the
drain. Seed authored by Claude from the same source files as the
color, and marked as seed.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE UNIVERSAL FORETELLER — the program that reads any program
  and truly answers "finishes or loops forever." CANNOT BE, by the
  construction held in the color's SCIENCE: feed it a program
  built to do the opposite of whatever is foretold about itself.
  CONDITIONS: the foreteller is itself a program, and the judged
  language can express unbounded repetition. The drain is real and
  written: languages that cannot loop (queries, layouts, forms)
  are fully checkable — surrender generality and the wall falls.
  The general oracle stays white forever; the fenced ones are
  color.
- A MACHINE THAT DOES WHAT WAS MEANT — the want beneath half the
  subject's failures. Every machine built does what was SAID, with
  perfect obedience and no common sense; the gap between said and
  meant is where every bug lives. Attempted as rule pyramids (see
  METHODS: expert systems); the want stands open. Would drain on:
  an instrument for intent — none is written.
- TRUE RANDOMNESS FROM ARITHMETIC ALONE — cannot: a machine is a
  device for doing the same thing every time, so a rule-made
  sequence is at best hard to predict, never impossible, and a
  predictable draw shrinks a key space to one. DRAINED PARTIALLY,
  as the shimmer expects: harvest physical noise and feed it in —
  the arithmetic-alone entry stands; the harvested-noise drain is
  written and is how real keys are made.
- THE PERFECT COPY OF A QUANTUM STATE — nature's single ban on the
  otherwise-free perfect copy; the one place physics overrules the
  subject's founding claim, and the suspected seed of unforgeable
  keys (see RELATIONS).
- THE ROBOT SKIN — touch reporting pressure everywhere, surviving
  impact (it is by definition the part being hit), not drowning
  its computer in data. Promised for decades, still not real;
  machines act on vision and position, blind at the only moment
  that matters. Would drain on: a material and a data-diet found
  together; neither is written.
- THE GENERAL HOUSEHOLD ROBOT — the factory robot is real because
  the world was made simple for it: trays, jigs, marked floors.
  The household refuses simplification: the light changed, the
  part is bent, the box is wet, a child stands still. CONDITION
  indexed honestly: capability and narrowness of world are direct
  substitutes — the entry drains exactly as fast as machines
  handle spread instead of having it removed. Grasping is the
  sharpest point: an unfamiliar object of unknown weight,
  friction, and stiffness, lifted without crushing or dropping —
  a small child still beats every machine.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "A CLEVERER TEST WILL PROVE THE PROGRAM CORRECT" — cannot as
  stated: a test proves the presence of one behavior, never the
  absence of all faults; searching finds what is there and cannot
  certify that nothing is anywhere. The honest form moved to the
  color: lanterns, scars, shapes that cannot say the wrong thing.
  Proof CAN certify — see SCIENCE for why it has not scaled.
- "MORE HARDWARE WILL SOLVE IT" — false against the exponential
  shape, by arithmetic: a thousandfold faster machine moves a 2^n
  search on by exactly ten items (2^10 = 1,024). Feasibility
  belongs to the shape; the machine only rents. Believed by every
  generation of buyers; falsified by every generation of walls.
- "THE AVERAGE CASE PROTECTS US" — false wherever a chooser
  exists: the rare terrible case (the quick sort's disaster, the
  hash table's pile-up) is reachable on purpose, and a system
  serving strangers is judged on its worst case because a
  stranger will find it. Drain written in the color: randomize
  the choice so the bad input cannot be aimed.
- "ADDING PEOPLE WILL SPEED THE LATE PROJECT" — false by the
  pairs law: lines of talk grow as n(n−1)/2 (five heads hold 10,
  ten hold 45), and the newcomers are taught by exactly the
  people already behind.
- "THE REWRITE WILL FIX EVERYTHING" — it inherits none of what
  the old system learned: the ugliness IS the record of real
  cases met, and the new one must meet them all again, from zero,
  while the old one keeps running. Fails on schedule; filed
  because every generation re-proposes it.
- "ENCRYPTED MEANS UNREADABLE" — every working lock is a budget
  and a clock: more than the secret is worth, longer than it
  matters. Forever-claims are claims about an attacker's future,
  and the prime-product door is undefeated, not proven.
- "DELETED IS GONE" — removal takes the name, not the contents,
  already copied into backups, caches, indexes, and screenshots
  nobody tracked. A cannot under all ordinary conditions: this
  subject cannot build the recall of information. The narrow
  drain: destroy the KEY of encrypted data and every copy goes
  dark at once — the closest written thing to unsaying.
- "IT WORKED IN THE DEMO" — 19 in 20, one attempt every three
  minutes, is one failure every hour with a person standing
  there. Fails by per-hour arithmetic, not dishonesty — which is
  why it keeps being believed.
- UNSETTLED — P VERSUS NP, the subject's largest open question:
  whether every problem quick to CHECK (a filled puzzle, a
  seating meeting all constraints, the factors of a product) is
  also quick to FIND. Thousands of hard problems convert into one
  another and stand or fall together. Nothing certain — but here
  is the possibility nearly every builder bets on: finding is
  truly harder, and the locks lean on that bet. Would settle on:
  one fast method for any member (everything falls — locks break,
  search collapses to arithmetic), or a proof none exists (the
  locks rest on rock). Neither is written.
- UNSETTLED — WHETHER THE MIND IS COMPUTATION: the wall's
  self-reference echoes in thought, and the universal imitator
  wears any body — but no written result cashes the claim either
  way. Nothing certain — here is a possibility: the question as
  posed is a boundary dispute over the word "computation," and
  moves only when a claim is stated that some experiment could
  refuse. Filed dotted; see RELATIONS.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A SCIENCE OF LARGE CORRECT SOFTWARE — programs proved
  right the way bridges are computed to stand. What exists: full
  proofs of small, hard kernels at roughly twenty lines of
  checked proof per line of program (~) — a price that kept
  whole-system proof out of reach while systems grew a
  millionfold. The gap has a name older than most of its tools
  (the "software crisis," named at the field's first
  self-examination, never closed). Would drain on: proof cost
  falling toward code cost — machine-checked proof assistants are
  the offered possibility; the proved kernels are the down
  payment.
- LACKING: A LAW OF ESTIMATION — software has no thickness-
  squared rule for time. The bias is structural, not moral:
  imagining the work is exactly the act that omits the unimagined
  parts, so estimates err one way and never average out. The
  honest substitute, filed as method: measure your own past
  misses and multiply by them.
- LACKING: THE MISSING THEOREM — P vs NP (held in CLAIMS): its
  absence leaves the locks resting on a bet and the search costs
  on a conjecture.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: METRICS AS TARGETS — the loop law (the
  sensor decides what the machine is good at; the unmeasured
  drifts) applied to people broke the measuring: what is counted
  gets optimised, what is not counted pays. The failure is the
  loop law working correctly on the wrong plant.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: AVERAGE-CASE ANALYSIS UNDER ADVERSARIES —
  sound mathematics about populations, applied where an
  intelligence picks the member. The repair (worst-case
  judgement, randomized choice) is color; the wreckage
  (predictable seeds, aimable collisions) is real and recurring.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
wings-on-arms entries, waiting for their planet.

- THE UNIVERSAL MALWARE SCANNER / PROGRAM JUDGE — impossible in
  full: "will this program ever behave badly" is a question about
  what all programs DO, and every such question shares the
  halting wall. DRAINED PARTIALLY three ways, all written:
  restrict the question (known-bad patterns, not all bad),
  restrict the language (no loops, full checkability), or bound
  the wait (decide in advance how long you will not know). The
  pure form stands white forever, by proof.
- EXPERT SYSTEMS — the grand promise, paid for twice: general
  judgement from hand-stacked pyramids of rules. WHY it failed,
  in conditions: a hand-written rule carries no statement of its
  own conditions, so it fires confidently outside them; the world
  outruns any hand-kept book; the pyramids grew brittler as they
  grew taller, each rule patching the last. It HALF-WORKED in
  narrow wells — which made it believed about the whole world,
  the most dangerous kind of failure. Faith and funding
  collapsed, twice, hard enough that the field renamed itself to
  keep working (HISTORY holds the record). Would drain on: rules
  that carry their own conditions and recompute when conditions
  move — the entry describes the shimmer itself, which is why it
  is filed with care instead of contempt.
- PROVING WHOLE SYSTEMS BY HAND — fell short at scale: the proof
  grows faster than the program, and a hand-proof of a moving
  target is out of date at the next edit. Drains only through the
  machine-checked road named in SCIENCE.
- THE SINGLE GREAT PLAN — everything planned before anything
  built. A conditions entry, not a cannot: planning pays exactly
  where the ground is known; past that uncertainty, every extra
  hour of plan is fiction. Drained by the short loop (try, look,
  adjust — the loop's length, not the plan's depth, sets the
  learning rate); kept conditional, because on known ground the
  plan is still the cheaper tool.
- ORDERING EVENTS BY COMPARING MACHINE CLOCKS — tried daily,
  fails on schedule: clocks drift, jump, and are corrected
  backwards, so "earlier timestamp" is not "happened first." No
  drain inside the clocks: order must be carried by the messages.
  Filed because the mistake is rebuilt in every distributed
  system's first year.
- AVERAGING SENSORS THAT DISAGREE — the average of a right answer
  and a wrong one is wrong. Superseded by belief-by-strength
  (fuse each where strong) and explicit deciders — and the
  decider is a single thing that can be fooled, which is where
  the interesting failures moved.
- SECURITY BY HIDDEN DESIGN — hide how the lock works and hope.
  Fails at scale by its own success: a design in a million
  devices is published whether you meant it or not, and cannot be
  rotated; a key can. Paid for in broken wartime traffic; drained
  into the color as its opposite.
- THE HOME-ROLLED CIPHER — trust in a lock comes from one source:
  many skilled strangers attacking in public for years and
  failing. A private design has zero such years by construction.
  Not a cannot — a condition nobody meets alone.
- OPTIMIZE FIRST — speed bought before measurement: the
  complexity is paid at once and forever, and the guess about
  which part matters is nearly always wrong (the time hides in a
  tiny part, and nobody guesses which). Drains only through the
  profiler's condemned line.
- COUNTING TEXT BY BYTES — splits letters in half and makes
  rubbish of names: most alphabets need more than one byte per
  character, and some signs are several characters wearing one
  face. Fell short everywhere off the narrow home alphabet it was
  tried on; the conditions were the method.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE ONE-TIME PAD — perfect secrecy EXISTS: a key as long as the
  message, truly random, used once, defeats any budget, proven.
  And it cannot be general: the key must travel as secretly as
  the message it protects — the mean solves the delivery problem
  by requiring it already solved. The standing proof that the
  want is not impossible, only self-defeating; drains only where
  couriers already run.
- QUANTUM MACHINES AGAINST THE PRIME DOOR — the condition-mover
  filed before it moves, as the shimmer instructs: the
  prime-product lock is undefeated, not proven, and a large
  enough quantum machine runs a known fast method against it.
  None at working scale is written; replacement locks (on
  problems the quantum method does not touch) are already being
  laid. The entry is the white working correctly: an old
  certainty re-indexed as conditional the day its condition
  became buildable.
- A MEDIUM THAT OUTLIVES ITS READERS — every medium so far dies
  with its drives: the tape is perfectly good and the machine,
  cable, and skills that read it are gone. A format's life
  expectancy is the count of independent people who can build a
  reader — not its cleverness, not its owner's size. Elegant
  proprietary formats died with their companies; the partial
  drain is a discipline, not a device: plain, documented, widely
  implemented, migrated forward on schedule.
- WATTS FOR AUTONOMY — the mobile machine carries its own energy
  and computer, and both are weight: every sensor costs battery,
  battery costs mass, mass costs motors, motors cost battery. The
  spiral is priced, not escaped; a tethered robot is a different
  and much easier machine. Would drain on: energy density moving
  — a condition owned by chemistry and physics, not this subject.
- NOT YET FOUND: AN INSTRUMENT FOR INTENT — the machine reads the
  said; nothing reads the meant. Every shipping method in the
  color (show something criticisable, react, adjust) is a
  workaround for this missing instrument, and the requirements
  discipline is its scar tissue.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- ANSWER EVERY QUESTION ABOUT PROGRAMS — barred by the wall, by
  proof, forever, under the stated conditions. The purpose
  retreats to fenced languages and bounded waits, and is served
  well there.
- BUILD LARGE SOFTWARE KNOWN TO BE RIGHT — open; blocked on the
  SCIENCE absence (proof cost). The field ships by lanterns,
  scars, and staged exposure instead, and says so honestly.
- KEEP A SECRET FOREVER — cannot, with one self-defeating
  exception (the pad): all working locks are budgets against a
  clock. The honest purpose, kept in the color: longer than the
  secret matters.
- UNSAY A COPY — cannot: copying is free and perfect, and recall
  is not buildable. Served only in advance: the record never
  made, the retention schedule, the key destroyed.
- WORK BESIDE PEOPLE IN A WORLD NOBODY SIMPLIFIED — open: the
  factory's trick (narrow the world) does not transfer to the
  kitchen, the street, the ward. Waits on the THINGS entries —
  skin, grasp, spread-handling.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the
story.

- The winters, in order: a grand promise of machine judgement;
  narrow successes taken as down payments on the general case;
  the general case failing at the edges of its rules; faith and
  funding collapsing; the field renaming itself and continuing
  quietly — then the cycle again. The lesson filed at full
  strength: a method that half-works on a narrowed world is more
  dangerous than one that fails outright, because it gets
  believed about the whole world. This page is where the cost of
  that belief is acknowledged as the method's true price.
- The null century: a convenience adopted into every language
  because it fit every claim — which was precisely the flaw, a
  value answering every claim falsely. Its own inventor's
  accounting: the billion-pound mistake. The repair (absence as
  its own case) was known and available for most of that century;
  why it waited — familiarity, momentum, the safer shape's daily
  cost on every writer — is the honest part of the entry.
- The software crisis, named and never closed: the field
  convened, compared itself to bridge-building, named the gap —
  systems late, over budget, wrong, unprovable — and the gap
  outlived everyone at the meeting. Its remainder is filed in
  SCIENCE as the standing absence.
- The graveyard of better languages: finest-on-paper lost to
  known-by-people, repeatedly — a language lives on answers,
  libraries, hirable speakers, and employers, and most language
  deaths were tooling deaths. The lesson (a coordination rule is
  worth exactly its followers) is color; the corpses are this
  entry.
- The lost spacecraft: one team's pound-force met another's
  newton, and a number with no unit flew as a rumour until it
  wasn't. The cleanest receipt for quantity-is-number-AND-unit,
  purchased at full price.
- The counter that rolled: fields sized in a fat decade wrapped
  in a lean one — two digits of year, thirty-two bits of seconds.
  Each wrap was arithmetic visible decades ahead and priced at
  zero until the decade arrived. The standing proof that
  "eventually" is a date.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, REBUILT: COMPUTING ↔ PEOPLE — the field assumed the
  operator stood outside the system; attackers proved the
  operator was the cheapest door (nobody breaks the cipher; they
  ask the person, and the person tells them). Rebuilt with the
  human priced in: false alarms train the ears that ignore the
  true one; security that obstructs the work is routed around and
  lowers safety. The rebuilt relation carries this speed limit
  and is color; the naive original is filed here.
- STRAINED, LIVE: COMPUTING ↔ LAW — property built for loaves
  meets the free perfect copy; the right to be forgotten meets
  the fact that deletion removes only names. The relation stands
  under visible strain, and the strain itself is the knowledge:
  law moves at drafting speed, copies at wire speed.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: MIND ↔ COMPUTING — whether thought is
  computation. The echoes are loud (self-reference, universality,
  recall-versus-lookup as the memory ladder's twin); no written
  result cashes them. Dotted until a refusable claim exists.
- SUSPECTED, UNSURE (the source itself marks it so): ECONOMY ↔
  INFORMATION — prices said to move only on surprise, the
  expected already priced in. Grips as analogy; unproven as a law
  a query can compute with. Dotted, awaiting a query that cashes
  it.
- SUSPECTED, HALF-CASHED: PHYSICS ↔ THE LOCKS — nature's cloning
  ban run as a mean: keys that show evidence of having been read.
  Working at short range, unproven as general infrastructure;
  filed dotted with its direction of drain marked.
