# MOVEMENT — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/movement/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. Seed authored by Claude from
docs/fabric_phylums/07_martial_arts_movement.md, 08_sports_games.md,

What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and conditions, so it
recomputes when new knowns move a condition. Nothing here is a
tombstone.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE FAT-MELTING EXERCISE FOR ONE SPOT — tried forever, killed by
  measurement: tennis players' playing forearms carry more muscle
  and the SAME fat layer as the idle arm; abdominal-exercise trials
  strengthen the wall and move the fat everywhere or nowhere. WHY:
  fat is released to the blood by whole-body signals — the working
  muscle burns fuel from circulation, not the pad lying over it.
  The honest crack: release over working muscle IS measurably
  elevated, by too little to matter at any written dose. Would drain
  on: a means of raising local release beyond a token; none written.
- THE UNIVERSAL TRANSFER MACHINE — one training that improves
  everything: forbidden by the subject's first law (the body answers
  the literal question; one question leaves the rest unasked). The
  survivor is smaller and real: general capacities raise the ceiling
  many skills draw on. Would drain on: a demand shown to transfer
  across speed, load, AND position at once; the tested ones do not.
- BOTTLED RECOVERY — a purchasable substitute for sleep and food:
  massage, compression, boots, rollers feel good and change
  performance little; one popular item runs backwards (CLAIMS, cold
  water). WHY: the building happens in sleep and food, and nothing
  sold reaches that machinery — the two things with large effects
  cost nothing and cannot be sold, which is why the shelf stays
  full. Would drain on: any product beating sleep-and-food controls
  by a large margin in a blinded trial; none has.
- THE CHILD CHAMPION DETECTOR — tests at twelve measure maturity
  plus practice-so-far, both unstable; most identified talents do
  not make it, most who make it were never identified — and it
  MANUFACTURES what it claims to find: children born just after the
  cut-off are up to a year older, get selected, get the coaching,
  and the head start becomes real. Would drain on: a childhood trait
  stable enough to predict the adult; none is written.
- A LIVE THROTTLE DIAL FOR EFFORT — one number to steer a session
  by. Heart rate auditioned and failed (drifts, lags, lies on heat
  and caffeine — the color holds the list); the own-baseline jump
  test is the nearest working part. The want stands open.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "NO PAIN, NO GAIN" — HALF-killed, and the halves matter. DEAD:
  soreness as merit — the two-day ache measures unfamiliarity, and a
  productive session done fifty times leaves nothing. DEAD: playing
  through warning pain — the receipts are the injury record:
  injuries cluster where load jumped above recent weeks; the best
  predictor of an injury is the same injury earlier, partly because
  people trained through the warnings; strength built in weeks tears
  the tendon that adapts in months. ALIVE: effort near failure IS
  part of the demand that builds — the DISCOMFORT of effort was the
  signal; the PAIN of harm never was. The working rules stand in the
  color: below moderate, not climbing, gone by morning.
- "LACTIC ACID CAUSES THE BURN AND THE SORENESS" — killed twice: it
  is a fuel other muscles and the heart burn, a marker of hard work
  outrunning the slow supply, and long gone when the two-day ache
  arrives. A correlate promoted into a cause for a century.
- "STRETCH BEFORE EXERCISE TO PREVENT INJURY" — killed at the
  outcomes: no prevention in the trials that looked, no soreness
  relief, brief power LOSS just before efforts; the added range
  mostly tolerance, not longer tissue — UNSURE at mechanism, firm at
  outcomes. Would drain on: a population where range itself is the
  failing quantity (dance and gymnastics keep their own case).
- "DRINK AHEAD OF THIRST, ON A SCHEDULE" — killed with deaths on the
  record: over-drinking dilutes blood salt, rarer than dehydration
  and worse; thirst regulates well for almost everybody. A plausible
  prescription that outran its testing and hardened into doctrine.
  The certified condition: thirst blunts with age — the old on long
  hot efforts are the measured exception, not a reason to revive the
  schedule.
- "TEN THOUSAND HOURS MAKES ANYONE AN EXPERT" — killed as stated:
  the finding was an AVERAGE among top violinists, one domain, wide
  spread; practice explains only part of the gap. The kernel kept in
  the color: deliberate practice is specific, effortful, feedback-
  rich — required, not decisive.
- "WEIGHTS STUNT CHILDREN'S GROWTH" — killed: born from injuries in
  unsupervised young workers, looked for repeatedly in supervised
  training, not found. What harms children is year-round single-
  sport competition (METHODS).
- "ICE BATHS PROTECT YOUR GAINS" — backwards: cold immersion after
  strength training blunts the growth signal it was bought to
  protect — inflammation is the building crew as well as the damage.
  Conditions matter: for comfort or repeated same-day performance it
  may buy something; as a builder it costs.
- "GOOD POSTURE PREVENTS PAIN" — mostly didn't hold: curved spines
  and forward heads appear at similar rates in the pained and
  painless, and correcting them does not reliably help; load and
  duration matter, shape less. Kept because queries still carry it.
  Would drain on: a trial where changing shape alone moves pain.
- "MOVEMENT SCREENS PREDICT INJURY" — keeps failing when tested;
  what predicts is mostly unchangeable (age, sport, prior injury);
  the modifiable list is short: load progression, strength in the
  failing tissue, sleep. Would drain on: a screen beating that short
  list in a fair prospective test.
- "THE RECORDS PROVE HUMANS KEEP IMPROVING" — partly costume:
  surfaces, swimsuits, shoe foams. A record jumping in one season
  across many athletes was not the athletes changing. Era comparison
  carries the asterisk permanently.
- UNSETTLED: THE LIMIT OF HUMAN SPEED — bounded, not open. The
  slices shrink: 100 m fell 10.6 (1912) → 9.95 (1968) → 9.58 (2009)
  — two-thirds of a second in the first half-century, a third in the
  four decades after, cut in hundredth-sized slices, nothing since.
  The binding arithmetic: at ~12 m/s the foot touches ground
  ~0.09 s of every ~0.21 s step, so support force must AVERAGE
  686 × (0.21 ÷ 0.09) ≈ 1,600 N — 2.3× body weight, peaks near 4-5×;
  going faster shortens the contact, demanding more force in less
  time from muscle whose force falls as speed rises and tendon
  already near its strength. Nothing certain — but a possibility:
  the wall sits within a few tenths of the standing record (~9.2-9.5
  s), set by tissue strength against contact time, not by will.
  Would drain on: measured tissue limits under sprint loads, a gait
  that lengthens contact without braking — or another planet: at a
  third of Earth's gravity the arithmetic recomputes entirely, the
  wings-on-arms shape exactly.
- UNSETTLED: HOW HOME ADVANTAGE DIVIDES — the total (roughly 54-60%
  everywhere measured) is iron; the split among crowd, travel, and
  familiarity is argued. One clean partial: the crowd's lean on the
  referee showed itself when games ran in empty stadiums. Would
  drain on: decompositions across such experiments.
- UNSETTLED: CONCUSSION'S LONG-TERM SIZE — a real harm genuinely
  hard to measure: long latency, confounded populations, an organ
  reporting its own damage with the damaged function. The working
  rules (if in doubt, sit out; staged return) stand in the color
  BECAUSE the size is unsettled.
- UNSETTLED: MENSTRUAL-CYCLE-PHASED TRAINING — sold with far more
  confidence than the small, inconsistent research supports; best
  reviews find trivial average effects and enormous individual
  variation. A possibility: the individual's own tracked record is
  the usable instrument; any real programming lives there, not in a
  group template.
- UNSETTLED: SITTING'S INDEPENDENT HARM — the association shrinks
  once exercise is accounted for; what remains is contested (it
  travels with income, work, illness). The supported mechanism:
  harm attaches to the UNBROKEN stretch — the lever is standing up
  regularly, not standing all day.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: AN EQUATION FROM PRACTICE TO MASTERY — the retention laws
  are directional (varied beats blocked, sparse feedback beats
  constant, tested-later is the only honest measure) but nothing
  written computes hours-and-structure into skill the way the color
  computes fuel into kilometers. The largest named absence.
- LACKING: THE RESPONDER MAP — identical training returns
  improvement from enormous to none, the response itself partly
  inherited, and no written test says WHICH before the training is
  spent. Until it exists, the own record is the only instrument.
- LACKING: THE GOVERNOR'S MACHINERY — effort regulated against an
  expected finish is proven as behavior (lie about the distance and
  exhaustion moves) and unmeasured as mechanism; its cousin: how
  much of fatigue is command withdrawn versus tissue spent.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE SMALL-STUDY DECADES — underpowered trials,
  unblinded designs (fatal where belief moves the measured thing),
  and averages hiding spreads wide enough to include zero produced
  doctrines that reversed: hydration schedules, stretching, cycle
  programs. Lesson filed: adopt at the speed claims stop reversing.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- TRAINING TO THE SHEET — the program followed regardless of the
  day's body: beaten by autoregulation, by more the longer it runs;
  a plan is a forecast made last month. Kept only where no judgment
  exists yet (the novice's first months).
- BLOCKED DRILLING AS THE ROAD TO MASTERY — twenty in a row looks
  like learning because the last beat the first; tested a week later
  the jumbled group is far ahead and looked worse throughout. Its
  failure's condition is its survival's: the coach watches the
  session, not the week after.
- FEEDBACK EVERY REP — speeds the session, slows the learning:
  constant correction becomes part of the task, and the skill falls
  apart without the corrector.
- THE WEIGHTED BAT AND ITS FAMILY — exercises that LOOK like the
  sport change the speed and load of a finely tuned movement and
  train something adjacent, sometimes worsening the real one.
  Transfer follows match in speed, load, position; resemblance is
  not that match.
- YEAR-ROUND SINGLE-SPORT CHILDHOOD — one set of tissues, one
  pattern, thousands of times: strong in a narrow band, fragile
  beside it; the overuse receipts are the record. Variety is part of
  the tolerance.
- WEIGHT CUTTING BY DEHYDRATION — works only because everyone does
  it, so refusing costs a size class: a rule-created trap that
  degrades the systems the sport tests and occasionally kills.
  Drains only by rule change (same-day weigh-ins, hydration tests),
  never by individual decision.
- COPYING THE CHAMPION'S PROGRAM — the champion is a selected
  sample: rowers are tall because tall rowers won. The program came
  AFTER the selection; copying it copies neither the fibres, the
  responder's luck, nor the decade of base.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE AGE FORMULA FOR MAXIMUM HEART RATE (220 − age) — misses real
  people by ~±10-12 beats; a population line worn as a personal
  dial. Superseded by the athlete's own measured values.
- THE TEN-THOUSAND-STEP TARGET — a pedometer's brand name, not
  research; the health curve flattens well below it. Kept only as a
  memorable nudge, which is what it always was.
- THE SUPPLEMENT SHELF — nearly everything sold does nothing
  measurable; the short surviving list is in the color. A large
  market at a low burden of proof; contamination has ended careers.
- DOPING TESTS THAT SAMPLE THE WRONG MOMENT — the drugs that matter
  raise recovery, so the athlete TRAINS more; the substance goes,
  the training it bought stays. A contest-day test measures nothing
  about the preparation; the passport (the athlete's own
  longitudinal baseline) is the partial drain.
- THE CALENDAR AS A RETURN GATE — six weeks describes how long
  tissue usually takes; it does not test that it did. Superseded by
  measured capacity; kept only where no measurement exists.
- NOT YET FOUND: AN INSTRUMENT THAT READS THE REPAIR — the session
  is visible, the building afterwards is not, so coaches manage the
  smaller half. The jump-test-against-own-baseline is the working
  fragment; the full want (today's repair state, cheaply) stands
  open.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- PICK THE CHAMPION FROM THE CHILD — blocked twice: childhood
  performance is maturity plus practice, unstable; and the selection
  machinery manufactures its own evidence (relative age).
- PREVENT THE INJURY BY PREDICTION — the predictive list is mostly
  unchangeable; the answer so far is management (progress load
  gradually, strengthen the failing tissue, sleep), which reduces
  and does not prevent.
- BUY BACK RECOVERY — nothing yet substitutes for sleep, food, and
  time; every sold shortcut has tested small or backwards.
- MAKE ONE BODY BEST AT EVERYTHING AT ONCE — endurance interferes
  with strength more than the reverse; one recovery budget, two
  directions; the want is closed by the body's first law.
- SETTLE THE ERAS — who was fastest, across shoes, surfaces, and
  rulebooks: unanswerable as asked; every cross-era ranking smuggles
  an equipment claim.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side.

- The doctrines that killed: drinking schedules diluted athletes to
  death; weight-cutting saunas stopped hearts; play-through-it built
  the tendon-rupture and stress-fracture wards. Each ran on a
  plausible mechanism, hardened before testing, and is filed here so
  its shape is recognizable under the next name.
- The lactic-acid reign: a century of coaches burning a fuel as a
  villain — the longest-lived correlate-for-cause error, and why
  "marker versus mechanism" is a standing check here.
- The stretching century: universal, ancient, obligatory — then
  measured. Proof a practice can be old, everywhere, and wrong at
  its stated purpose.
- Blocked practice's long disguise: it LOOKED better every session,
  as long as anyone watched sessions; only delayed testing caught
  it. The warning: the measurement's timing is part of it.
- The talent-machine audit: academies built on tests that predicted
  maturity, harvesting birthdays and calling it destiny — the
  relative-age receipts run through every youth league measured.
- The technology asterisk: records leaping in single seasons —
  suits, then bans; foams, then rules — the argument between what
  the body did and what it wore.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN: POSTURE ↔ PAIN — the striking visible feature drew the
  blame; the measurement kept coming back weak. The load-duration
  relation stands in the color in its place.
- BROKEN: STRETCHING ↔ SAFETY — ritual and protection ran on
  different roads; loading the range took over the job.
- BROKEN, DIRECTION REVERSED: COLD ↔ ADAPTATION — the recovery aid
  suppresses the building signal after strength work; survives only
  under changed conditions (comfort, repeated same-day efforts).
- BROKEN: CHILDHOOD TESTS ↔ ADULT CHAMPIONS — prediction failed both
  ways; the relation stands only as selection's artifact.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: CYCLE PHASE ↔ PROGRAMMING — mechanism real,
  prescription untested at strength; dotted until the individual-
  record instrument (this page's possibility) is built and run.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: UNBROKEN SITTING ↔ INDEPENDENT HARM — the
  exposure travels with a dozen others; the stand-up-regularly lever
  is the defensible fragment.
- CORRECTED, CARRIED WITH AN ASTERISK: RECORDS ↔ HUMAN LIMITS — real
  and permanently entangled with equipment; every crossing that
  cites a record owes the entanglement a sentence.
