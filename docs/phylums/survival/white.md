# SURVIVAL — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/survival/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and conditions,
so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition. Seed authored by
Claude from docs/fabric_phylums/38_survival_wilderness.md.

This white is unusually well stocked: its failed tries ran at full
stakes for the species' whole history, and the receipts are graves.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE OCEAN AS CANTEEN — every thirsty sailor's want: CANNOT BE by
  drinking (the osmosis ledger in CLAIMS). Drained PARTIALLY by
  energy: distillation and pressure-desalting trade fuel or muscle
  for fresh water. The bare-handed version stays white: no written
  way to unsalt water for free.
- A COMPASS IN THE BODY — some animals carry one; humans do not,
  usably: no inner sense holds a heading (the circling receipts in
  CLAIMS). Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: the sense
  exists weakly, drowned by noise; a training that raised it above
  the veer would drain this entry.
- A UNIVERSAL EDIBLE-PLANT TEST — the taught screen (skin, lip,
  tongue, swallow, wait — hours per plant) is a slow risk-reducer,
  not a law: it cannot catch killers whose damage arrives days
  late (some mushrooms). Would drain on: a field-readable marker
  separating edible from deadly across families — none is written.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "DRINK SEAWATER WHEN DESPERATE — SOME WATER BEATS NONE" —
  certified harmful, the osmosis why: seawater carries ~35 g of
  salt per liter, and the kidney's most concentrated possible
  urine is about that same strength — excreting one liter's salt
  costs roughly the liter back AT BEST, and the day's other
  wastes still bill their own water on top. The ledger closes
  negative: every liter drunk removes more than a liter. Its
  magnesium and sulfate also purge the gut — further loss.
  RECEIPT: the world-war lifeboat ledgers — ~39% of seawater
  drinkers died against ~3% of abstainers. CONDITIONS: whole
  seawater, drunk as water; distilled, or thinned to a small
  fraction of fresh, it serves — those drains stand in THINGS.
- "DRINK YOUR URINE" — a losing trade past the first desperate
  round: urine is the outbound stream, carrying salts and urea
  the kidney already PAID water to refuse. A hydrated first pass
  is dilute and nearly harmless once; a dehydrated pass is
  already at the concentration ceiling, so drinking it hands the
  load back at full strength and each cycle concentrates. The
  armies' manuals struck it. What holds: the liquid still cools —
  wet a cloth and let evaporation's ~2,400 J/g work on the
  OUTSIDE of the body.
- "MOSS GROWS ON THE NORTH SIDE" — fails as a compass: moss grows
  where damp lingers — the shaded side in an open stand, every
  side in a wet wood. A weak lean, not a law; kept only as a
  corroborator where many open-stand trees agree.
- "RUNNING WATER IS CLEAN WATER" — false: clarity advertises
  nothing about what swims there, and the sickness it buys spends
  the water account faster than thirst. The boil costs a double
  handful of wood; the shortcut costs days.
- "ALCOHOL WARMS YOU" — backwards: it opens the skin's vessels,
  which FEELS warm precisely because core heat is being shipped
  out and spent faster.
- "RUB FROSTBITE" — harmful: frozen tissue is laced with ice
  crystals; rubbing grinds them through cells. Rewarm gently,
  once, only where refreezing cannot happen — medicine's entry.
- "MOST HEAT LEAVES THROUGH THE HEAD" — false as stated: the head
  is ~10% of body surface and loses about its share. The origin:
  cold tests on subjects clothed everywhere BUT the head. Cover
  it — because it is bare, not because it is special.
- UNSETTLED: WHY UNGUIDED WALKERS CIRCLE — measured fact (without
  sun or landmarks, walkers loop, sometimes inside ~20-100 m);
  mechanism contested — leg asymmetry grips less than expected,
  drift in the felt "straight" grips more. Nothing certain — but
  here is a possibility: with no external reference, small random
  heading errors accumulate as a drunkard's walk and any brief
  bias curls the path. Would drain on: a predictor of WHICH way
  a given walker curls.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A PREDICTOR OF WHO FREEZES AND WHO ACTS — the counts
  are stable (in sudden disasters roughly ~10-15% act well,
  ~70-80% stall stunned, ~10-15% panic) but no written law says
  which a given person will be, and self-prediction is worthless.
  Training moves people between bins (RELATIONS holds the dotted
  line); the pre-training predictor is this subject's largest
  named absence.
- LACKING: DEAD RECKONING WITHOUT INSTRUMENTS — position from
  one's own steps drifts without bound (the circling entry is
  its receipt). This lack is WHY the color's navigation is all
  handrails: external references standing in for a sense the
  body does not hold.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE FIRST WIND-CHILL INDEX — calibrated on
  water freezing in cans on a pole, trusted on faces; it
  overstated the theft badly and was rebuilt on skin models (the
  color's ~ −6°C is the rebuilt scale's). Filed as this phylum's
  copy of a general law: ask what a borrowed number was
  calibrated ON.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- CUT-AND-SUCK SNAKEBITE TREATMENT — a century of kits sold, then
  measured: suction recovers on the order of ~2% of venom or
  less (mock-venom trials), because venom leaves the puncture for
  the lymph within moments; the cut adds a wound, the mouth adds
  bacteria, and the minutes are stolen from the walk toward help.
  RECEIPTS: extraction trials; outcome records showing no benefit
  plus extra tissue damage. What holds moved to color and
  medicine: still, splinted, limb low, carried out — the real
  drain is antivenom, at medicine's door. The phylum's cleanest
  case of a method that FELT like fighting and measured as harm.
- THE FEAR-FREEZE AS STRATEGY — mostly killed, and the receipts
  are the move-or-die records: cabin fires and sinkings where
  passengers who stalled within reach of an exit died seated
  while neighbors who moved at the first command lived. WHY it
  exists: freeze is a prey reflex tuned for predators that key
  on motion — against THAT threat it works (and its twin,
  running from a large predator, fails for the same reason: no
  human outruns the animal, and motion marks prey). THE
  CONDITION LINE, exact: stillness pays when the threat hunts by
  motion; stillness kills when the threat is a clock — cold,
  fire, water, smoke — because the clock runs regardless. DRAIN,
  partial and real: drilled procedure converts stalling to
  action; the untrained reflex stays filed here.
- EATING SNOW FOR WATER — the want is honest, the arithmetic
  refuses: melting costs 334 J/g and warming the melt to body
  heat ~155 J/g more — ~489 J/g, so a liter's worth (1 kg) bills
  489,000 J: at the body's ~100 W, ~82 minutes of the ENTIRE
  resting furnace — the three-hour account billed to serve the
  three-day one, triage inverted. Drains completely by fire: let
  wood's joules pay, one more reason fire ranks first.
- THE DUG SOLAR STILL AS A DESERT PLAN — real physics, wrong
  ledger where most wanted: a still yields ~100-800 mL/day;
  digging its pit in sun costs on the order of a liter of sweat.
  Net negative. CONDITIONS: stands where digging is cheap (soft
  damp ground, dug at dusk) or as a passive extra; as the desert
  plan, filed here with its ledger.
- SWIMMING AGAINST A RIP CURRENT — cannot be won at the speeds
  involved: a rip runs up to ~2 m/s, a strong swimmer ~1 m/s;
  the race is lost by construction and the swimmer drowns tired.
  Drains sideways: swim ACROSS the narrow river of current, or
  float with it past the bar — the sea beside it is calm.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE WATCH-HANDS COMPASS — degrades exactly where needed: fails
  near the equator, skews with shifted clocks, reads opposite
  south of the equator (point 12 at the sun there), and goes
  ambiguous near six o'clock when hand and 12 sit opposite.
  Stands as a rough mid-latitude aid; filed so its conditions
  travel with it.
- NOT YET FOUND: A BARE-HANDS WAY TO HOLD A LINE BLIND — fog,
  whiteout, starless night: no instrument-free method keeps a
  heading (the dead-reckoning lack is the why). Every working
  answer adds a means — rope to a handrail, or wait it out.
- NOT YET FOUND: FIRE FROM TRULY SODDEN COUNTRY AT NEED — every
  written method presumes SOMETHING dry exists (a standing dead
  heart, carried tinder); after days of rain in flat country the
  presumption can fail. Offered possibility: tinder dried next
  to skin by body heat, hours ahead of the need.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- MAKE THE LOST FINDABLE AT ONCE — bare skills make the lost only
  VISIBLE and PATIENT; instant findability arrived as beacons —
  electronics carrying this phylum's want. Inside these skills it
  stands open by construction: no fire signals over the horizon.
- ABOLISH THE COLD NIGHT — fire and shelter halve the theft; they
  do not repeal the law. The body's ~100 W against winter is a
  losing ledger managed, never won.
- CHOOSE STAY-OR-WALK WITHOUT DOUBT — the color holds conditions
  (searchers likely? stay; none coming, water failing, weather
  closing? walk while strength holds), but no law closes every
  case. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: let the
  accounts decide — walk only when the water account will empty
  before any search plausibly arrives, and then downhill.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side.

- The uncounted first tuition: every color claim was bought before
  writing, and the buyers of the failed tries are the silence in
  the record. The color is short precisely because this side is a
  graveyard of full depth.
- The seawater ledger: proverb said don't; one famous voyage
  claimed otherwise (a doctor sailed the Atlantic professing to
  live on the sea itself); the war records' ~39% versus ~3%
  settled it against him. Filed as proof that one vivid survivor
  cannot outvote a ledger.
- The suction-kit century, and the manuals' purged pages: a
  treatment that measured as harm was SOLD for a hundred years
  because it felt like fighting back, and armies printed
  urine-drinking, seawater rations, and cut-and-suck before
  striking each as records came in — a corpus correcting itself
  against graves, this white built the hard way.
- The freeze counts: only when disasters were studied as behavior
  (exits mapped, who moved when) did the stunned ~70-80% become a
  number instead of a private shame — and training built on the
  number moved it, the drain-in-progress recorded in METHODS.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, THEN REBUILT: SURVIVAL ↔ MEDICINE ON SNAKEBITE — the
  relation shipped harm for a century (cut, suck, tourniquet,
  ice — each struck); it now runs the other way: field skill's
  whole job is stillness and transport, the cure at medicine's
  door. The reversal is itself knowledge about the relation.
- BROKEN AS CALIBRATED: SURVIVAL ↔ WEATHER ON WIND CHILL — the
  proxy-calibrated index overstated; rebuilt, it holds. Kept as
  the standing caution on every number this phylum imports.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: TEACHING ↔ THE FREEZE — that drills
  convert stunned bystanders to actors is strongly evidenced;
  WHICH drill features do the converting (repetition? rehearsed
  first move? assigned role?) is unwritten. Effect cashed,
  mechanism white — the line stands half-dotted.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: ANIMALS AS FORECASTERS — birds gone quiet
  and stock lying down before storms: an old lean, a partial
  correlation, no written rule that beats the sky's own signs.
  Dotted to animals and weather until a tested predictor files.
