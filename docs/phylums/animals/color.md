Every animal is a solution to one short list: get fuel, avoid
becoming fuel, hold a workable temperature, leave copies. The
bodies differ because the solutions do. Every KEPT animal adds a
second list: the jobs it can no longer do for itself, transferred
to whoever penned it.

## THINGS
The units this subject is about; other homes named so a cascade
can branch.

- THE BODY PLAN — symmetry announces the living: built round a
  center (jellyfish, starfish) means food from every direction and
  no front; built to go somewhere means a front, and the front
  collects the senses, and the brain follows the senses; also:
  physics, engineering (controls at the leading end).
- THE SEGMENT — one unit repeated: worm rings, insect sections,
  your vertebrae; cheap to grow, cheap to alter singly — a leg
  here, a jaw there, wings on two; also: life, building, language.
- THE THROUGH-GUT — mouth to exit, a production line grinding,
  dissolving, absorbing at once on different mouthfuls; a
  one-opening sac works in turn and cannot eat while it empties;
  also: engineering (stations in series), cooking.
- THE SKELETON — a frame you hang meat on or a box you live in:
  the box protects, seals water in, anchors muscle; the frame
  grows continuously, never abandoned. Nothing does both — and the
  box charges the MOULT: split it, drag out (breathing-tube
  linings included), then hours to days pale, soft, unable to run
  — where most predation lands; also: building, engineering.
- THE EGG WITH ITS OWN POND — shell holding water in, letting air
  through, food store and waste dump inside: what made land
  breeding possible (not lungs or legs — amphibians have both and
  still go back); also: chemistry, engineering.
- THE WING — dearest travel per minute, cheapest per mile; also:
  flight, physics, economy (fixed cost paying at volume).
- THE WARM ENGINE AND THE COLD — burn fuel continuously, or wait
  for the sun; and a third answer, bulk, holding the day's heat
  for nothing; also: physics, cooking (the big joint), body.
- THE HERD — more eyes, diluted odds, spoiled aim; bought with
  easier detection, competing neighbors, disease unhindered; also:
  people (crowds), economy (risk pools).
- THE COLONY — an ant nest is one animal in thousands of bodies:
  queen an ovary, not a ruler; workers its hands and gut; success
  counted at colony level only, a worker's death a scratch; also:
  people (institutions outliving members), body.
- VENOM AND POISON — delivered versus eaten: venom needs a needle,
  costs to make, is metered and advertised; poison needs only
  swallowing and works after the bite it should have prevented.
  Hunting venom paralyzes and pre-digests, often painless;
  defensive venom is built for instant agony, often harmless —
  sting severity is a poor guide to danger; also: chemistry,
  medicine, law (deterrence timing).
- THE SENSES BEYOND OURS — electric fields of buried fish, the
  planet's magnetism, ultraviolet marks on flowers, sky
  polarization, a mouse's warmth seen as shape in the dark:
  information lives in any varying quantity a nerve can be built
  to read; also: physics, measurement.
- THE KEPT ANIMAL — the wild one finds water, picks its bed, walks
  from its dung, moves when grass is gone; the penned one can do
  none of it, so each need became somebody's job — husbandry IS
  that list; also: farming, governance (duty of care).
- THE RENTED VAT — no animal makes the enzyme that cuts plant
  fiber; every grass-eater keeps microbes warm for days and lives
  on what the tenants leave: a herbivore's real skill is farming,
  not digestion; also: farming, cooking (fermentation).
- COLOSTRUM — first milk carrying the immunity a farm newborn was
  born without, usable only while the gut passes whole antibodies
  — a door shut within a day; also: body, medicine.
- THE RECORD AND THE TAG — which animal, from whom, born when,
  treated with what: a herd turned from impression into a thing
  measurable; also: measurement, economy, law.

## CLAIMS
What holds, written to be tested by a crossing query.

- Front, senses, brain arrive together where a traveling body
  meets its world. Read a skull: eyes forward = overlap = depth =
  striker; eyes sideways = near-full circle, no depth = watcher.
  Depth is bought with field of view, exactly.
- Strength and surface follow the square; weight and need follow
  the cube (SCIENCE holds the ladder). One law caps insects,
  prices flight, starves shrews nightly, cools elephants badly.
- Most named animal species are insects because four edges
  MULTIPLY: a crumb is a meal and a crack is a house; waxed
  against drying; flight; several generations per summer. Remove
  one and the group is unremarkable. Two of three named animal
  species are insects, one in four a beetle; vertebrates ~5%.
- Splitting a life ends the war between jobs: the caterpillar only
  eats; the butterfly drinks, travels, mates; young never compete
  with adults. Groups paying for the full rebuild in the box
  (fly, beetle, bee, moth) took over the world; those hatching as
  miniatures (grasshopper, dragonfly) enlarge stepwise and never
  get a wholly different adult body.
- Flight is kept only where it pays — escape upward, a hundredfold
  search area, scattered patches; where those stop (no predators,
  food underfoot, an island), wings are dropped, repeatedly.
- A hunter's budget picks its method: waiting costs nothing and
  needs one strike (stillness, camouflage, venom); chasing costs
  enormously and needs stamina, cooling, and the sense to abandon
  a failing run.
- The colony runs on arithmetic, no commander: workers change jobs
  with age — clean, nurse, build, guard, then forage outside — the
  dangerous job last, to those with least life left to lose. A
  sterile worker's whole value is the nest, so the sting that
  kills the stinger is straight profit — why social insects fight
  above their weight. A swarm picks a home with no chooser:
  scouts advertise to their finding, others inspect, and at a
  quorum standing on one site the swarm goes — a vote counted by
  attendance, and it picks well.
- A flower pays two currencies for two purchases: nectar (sugar,
  flight fuel) buys a visit; pollen (protein, food of the young)
  buys a returning household. It works through a habit in the
  bee — one species per trip, better flowers standing open beside
  — loyalty bought, not visits: wrong-address delivery is worth
  nothing.
- Safety is paid in mouthfuls: every head lifted is a bite not
  taken. Hungry animals risk more; a grazer in company looks up
  less; a predator's main bill is the prey's lost feeding.
- Two ways to be missed: not detected (match the background), or
  seen and dismissed (be a twig, a dropping, a dead leaf) — the
  second beats recognition in plain view. Warning colors are the
  opposite wager: they work only on predators that learned, the
  lesson paid by the pattern's own dead wearers — collapsing
  where predators are short-lived or new.
- A heading is not a position: sun compass against internal
  clock, star pattern learned young, magnetic sense; smells,
  coastlines, remembered ground. Break one instrument and the
  animal travels confidently the wrong way.
- Signals go where the habitat lets them: sound bends round
  obstacles, works in the dark (forests, nights); color needs
  line-of-sight and daylight; scent lasts days with the sender
  absent (borders, trails). In a full chorus, calls push apart in
  pitch, hour, rhythm — a pond divided like a radio dial.
- Territory holds only where the patch beats the patrolling: rich
  staying food is fenced; thin-spread and unpredictable gluts are
  not — one species defends here, wanders there. Mating follows
  the same geometry: females gathered at something defensible =
  one male holds the lot; spread thin, young needing two workers
  = pairs. Geography, not temperament.
- Care falls on whoever is left holding the eggs — who cannot
  leave without abandoning the investment stays. Internal
  fertilization leaves the female holding; fish fertilized on a
  male's patch often leave the MALE guarding.
- An egg left behind is cheap and exposed — many laid, parent
  mobile, package guarded by something; young carried are safe
  wherever the mother goes, at the cost of her speed, feeding,
  and numbers. Cold and danger push toward carrying.
- A clutch settles below the physical maximum — past a point each
  extra chick lowers the odds for all (SCIENCE runs it). And a
  fish never stops growing: a few old, large fish out-breed a
  shoal of young — output climbs with volume, not length.
- Honest evidence for animal thinking is behavior needing an
  ABSENT thing held in mind: a jay re-hiding food only when a
  known thief watched; a crow bending a wire it never saw bent;
  preparing for a state not felt. The bar: the simplest account
  with no mind in it — much impressive behavior is a rule running
  blind (the digger wasp restarting its inspection forever, the
  goose rolling a missing egg): precise, appropriate, uncoupled
  from any grasp.
- Domestication is a conjunction: of ~150 large candidates, ~14
  passed. Failures fail on stated items — will not breed watched,
  grows too slowly to pay its feed, panics into fences, eats what
  we cannot supply, no ranking a person can enter — ONE failure
  ends it. THE ZEBRA, settled: individuals were tamed (carriages
  driven behind them); no population was domesticated — it
  panics, bites without releasing, ducks a rope, and no
  multi-generation program was sustained. Taming is a biography;
  domestication is control of matings across generations. The
  open remainder is in the mirror.
- Domestic means who chooses the mates: breeding controlled, a
  population reshapes in dozens of generations — why every dog
  breed is recent, why breeds differ more than wild species, why
  the extremes carry defects nobody selected and everybody got.
- The animals thriving beside people share one list: eat
  anything, breed fast, tolerate crowding, fit inside structures,
  sleep anywhere. Rat, pigeon, cockroach, fox, gull share nothing
  else; the list is the whole explanation.
- A kept animal's five needs: right food and water; a fit place;
  health or treatment; freedom from fear; and the missed fifth —
  room and company to behave as its kind behaves (root, perch,
  graze, lie properly, be among its own). An evolved behavior is
  a need, not a preference: prevented, the animal is under strain
  even fat and disease-free.
- Disease is dose against resistance, and crowding pushes both
  wrong at once: more shed pathogen, more dung, damper air, less
  escape — while the crowding itself lowers resistance.
- Cold alone kills few; cold WITH wet or wind kills many —
  insulation is trapped still air, and soaking or blowing it away
  removes the insulation, not the comfort (number in SCIENCE).
- Water decides the other feeds: short of it an animal eats less
  within hours, and growth, milk, condition fall with intake.
  Animals refuse foul water long before danger — the refusal IS
  the injury; any change to the trough is a diet change.
- Feed is commonly two-thirds of the cost of keeping any animal —
  so lameness, bad air, a slow trough, a bully at the feed face
  are financial matters: they all cut intake.
- Prey animals hide pain — the limper is the one taken — so real
  pain may stand quiet and eat a little: absent signs are NOT
  comfort. Pain shows as small specific change (lying
  differently, shorter feeding bouts, grooming stopped, hanging
  back) visible only against a known baseline — the daily walker
  of the shed is the instrument.
- Calm handling is a number: rough handling cuts eating, gain,
  fertility, adds bruising, and is harder next time — fear taxes
  growth, digestion, breeding through the same hormones. Welfare
  and production are the same argument here.
- The visibly sick are the small loss; the larger never looks ill
  — grows slower, milks less, breeds later: a scarred lung, mild
  lameness, worms below the scour line — invisible, most of the
  bill. And carriers shed at stress for life: the healthy-looking
  one in the corner is where a herd problem keeps returning from.
- Stress before slaughter is written into the meat: exhaustion
  spends the muscle sugar — dark, firm, dry, pH stuck above ~6.0
  instead of ~5.5; last-minute fright the opposite — pale,
  watery. Both visible in the chiller: a record no one witnessed.
- Diseases cross to people at closest contact — birthing, dung,
  raw milk, bites, markets — and the protection is as mundane as
  the route: gloves, washed hands, food out of animal areas,
  pregnant women away from lambing.

## SCIENCE
The math, physics, and chemistry of animals, held — not
mentioned. LAW to compute with, RULE to follow, WORKED with real
numbers run to a real answer.

### MATHEMATICS — the square-cube law, the master key

LAW: scale a body by s in every direction — weight and fuel need
  grow as s³; bone strength, muscle force, wing area, and
  heat-shedding surface grow as s². The penalty per step is
  s³ ÷ s² = s.
WORKED, legs: scale a 4 kg cat up 10×: weight 4 × 1,000 =
  4,000 kg; bone cross-section 10² = 100×; load per unit of bone
  1,000 ÷ 100 = 10× worse — why the elephant stands on columns,
  the cat on springs, and the largest land animals thicken out of
  all proportion.
WORKED, the ant's "strength": shrink a person 100× in length:
  weight falls 100³ = 1,000,000×, force 100² = 10,000×,
  strength-to-weight RISES 1,000,000 ÷ 10,000 = 100×. Lifting
  half your weight becomes 0.5 × 100 = 50× your weight at ant
  size. The ant is not strong; it is small.
WORKED, wings: double every dimension: weight 2³ = 8×, wing area
  2² = 4×, load per area 8 ÷ 4 = 2×; lift rises with speed
  squared, so needed airspeed rises √2 ≈ 1.4×. Every doubling
  makes flight faster, harder, dearer — the flying-bird ceiling
  sits near ~15 kg; the heaviest fliers, near ~18, barely get
  off the ground.
WORKED, the exoskeleton cap: box weight climbs s³ against
  strength s², AND breathing is passive tubes working only over
  short distances, AND each growth step is a day of being soft:
  any one caps size, three cap it hard — no dog-sized spiders at
  today's air (the condition that once moved is in the mirror).

LAW: heat lost follows surface (s²); heat made and stored follows
  volume (s³) — small runs cold, big runs hot.
WORKED, small: a hummingbird's night heating bill exceeds a day's
  storage — it drops to near air temperature every evening and
  restarts at dawn; the smaller the warm animal, the closer to
  that edge, every day of its life.
WORKED, big: a crocodile's bulk barely cools overnight — size
  buys steady temperature with no fuel bill (why the biggest
  cold-blooded live where it is warm). Above that, SHEDDING heat
  is the large animal's problem: the elephant's ears are
  vessel-laced radiators; the dog pants because it cannot sweat
  through fur; overheating, not cold, kills a working animal.

### PHYSICS — the running cost of warm and cold

LAW: holding a body above its surroundings costs continuously,
  paid whether anything is done or not; warm-blooded eats ~10× a
  same-size cold-blooded. The tenfold buys night work, cold-
  country work, and no mornings waiting on a rock.
WORKED: a lion needs ~5 kg of meat a day; a same-mass crocodile
  ~1/10 — 5 ÷ 10 = 0.5 kg/day, one ~3.5 kg meal a week
  (0.5 × 7 = 3.5). That ratio decides who hunts daily and who
  waits by the river for a month.

LAW: per-gram burn rises as bodies shrink — roughly the fourth
  root of the mass ratio.
WORKED: elephant 5,000 kg vs mouse 30 g: 5,000,000 ÷ 30 ≈
  166,667; fourth root: √166,667 ≈ 408, √408 ≈ 20. A gram of
  mouse burns ~20× a gram of elephant — why the mouse eats about
  a fifth of its weight daily and the elephant about a
  twenty-fifth; the rest of the 20× hides in the food (grain
  dense and digestible, forage neither).

LAW: torpor is the heating switched off — a hibernating ground
  squirrel falls to a few degrees above freezing and cuts fuel to
  ~1/50 (chemistry slows steeply as temperature drops).
WORKED: 180 winter days at 1/50 = 180 ÷ 50 = 3.6 warm-days of
  fuel — a small animal cannot store 180 days of fat but CAN
  store four: that arithmetic is why hibernation exists. The bear
  runs the other book: big, slow to cool, months of fat aboard —
  drops a few degrees, stays rousable, births in the den:
  costly, shallow, reversible any afternoon.

LAW: insulation is trapped still air — air conducts ~0.026 W per
  meter-degree, water ~0.6.
WORKED: 0.6 ÷ 0.026 ≈ 23 — a soaked coat leaks heat ~23× faster.
  A dry animal out of the wind survives weather that kills it wet
  in a draught; this number is husbandry's whole shelter rule.

LAW: evaporating water carries ~2,400 J per gram at body heat
  (cooking's kettle law, run in reverse on a tongue).
WORKED: a dog shedding 100 W by panting evaporates 100 ÷ 2,400 ≈
  0.042 g/s = 0.042 × 3,600 ≈ 150 g of water an hour. Water is
  the coolant; a dog without it overheats by the arithmetic a
  kettle boils dry by.

### PHYSICS — the senses, priced

LAW: a wave resolves objects down to about its own wavelength;
  wavelength = wave speed ÷ frequency.
WORKED: a bat at 50,000 Hz (sound 340 m/s): 340 ÷ 50,000 =
  0.0068 m = 6.8 mm — moth-sized detail. The price of shouting
  to see: high notes fade fast (short range), the call must be
  deafening, so the bat mutes its own ears and reopens them
  hundreds of times a second, once per shout.
WORKED, the dog's nose: a person ~5 million smell receptors, a
  dog ~250 million — 250 ÷ 5 = 50×, on ~30× the surface, read by
  a far larger brain share. Scale: a teaspoon of sugar (~4 g) in
  two Olympic pools (2 × 2,500,000 L = 5 × 10⁹ g) is 4 ÷ 5×10⁹ =
  0.8 parts per billion — and trained dogs resolve some odors
  near a part per TRILLION, nearly a thousandfold finer. A dog does not
  smell a stew; it smells every ingredient separately.

### CHEMISTRY AND LIFE — fuel, vats, and guts

LAW: fat carries ~9 kcal/g; sugar-store (glycogen) ~4, stored
  wet at ~3 g water per gram.
RULE: count glycogen at 4 ÷ (1+3) = 1 kcal per gram CARRIED, fat
  at 9 — fat gives ~9× the range per gram of takeoff weight,
  which is why migration fuel is fat.
WORKED: a ~12 g songbird doubles to ~24 g on fat, first
  shrinking its own gut and liver so as not to carry what it will
  not need. ~12 g fat = 12 × 9 = 108 kcal; over a ~3,000 km
  ocean crossing, 108 ÷ 3,000 = 0.036 kcal/km — the right order
  for a small bird. It lands with organs to rebuild: stopovers
  are repairs, not rests.

LAW: the rented vat needs days of warmth, steady acidity, and a
  microbe population that shifts only slowly. A cow's vat runs
  above pH ~6; a sudden grain load feeds the acid-makers, pH
  crashes below ~5.5, the fiber digesters die — the animal
  poisoned by good feed.
RULE: feed the bugs, not the cow — change rations over one to
  two weeks; keep long fiber in: cud-chewing makes ~150 L/day of
  bicarbonate saliva that holds the vat steady. Fiber is
  machinery, not filler.
LAW: vat placement writes the life — ferment FIRST (cow):
  extract nearly all, slowly, intake capped; ferment LAST (horse,
  rabbit): less per mouthful, far more throughput. Gut length
  predicts diet better than teeth: meat is near what a body is
  (short gut); leaves must be held and worked (enormous). A pig
  or chicken has no vat: its ration is a formulation balanced to
  specific building blocks — pork and chicken prices track the
  grain markets within months.

### MATHEMATICS — populations, herds, and the farm calendar

LAW: living things compound — N = N₀ × 2^(t ÷ doubling time) —
  and a population knocked below its ceiling grows back:
  survivors inherit the food and room.
WORKED, pest rebound: poison 90% of 1,000 rats → 100 left with
  the food of 1,000; doubling monthly: 100 → 200 → 400 → 800 →
  1,600 — past the start in ~4 months. Control that skips food,
  water, and entry holes is a subscription, not a solution.
WORKED, resistance (the drench lottery): a flock carries
  ~1,000,000 worms inside and ~20,000,000 on the pasture as eggs
  and larvae — the great majority OUTSIDE. Dose all with a drug
  killing 99%: 1,000,000 × 0.01 = 10,000 survivors, all
  resistant. Onto CLEAN ground they are the only parents: next
  generation 100% resistant-descended. On the dirty field they
  breed among 20,000,000 ordinary: 10,000 ÷ 20,010,000 ≈ 0.05%.
  The untreated reservoir keeps the drug working — why some
  animals are left undosed on purpose, and why parasite control
  is grazing management with a drug as a tool, not the reverse.
RULE: burdens are uneven — a few animals carry most worms: treat
  the few that need it (growth, condition, pale eye membrane,
  dung count); one decision serves animal, flock, and drug.

LAW: risk shared falls per head; detection and infection rise
  with the crowd.
WORKED: a predator takes one per attack — alone, odds 1/1; among
  50, 1 ÷ 50 = 2%. The same 50 are easier to FIND, eat each
  other's grass, pass each other's parasites: the herd's
  three-for-three trade, priced.

LAW: when quality falls as quantity rises, the best total sits
  in the middle.
WORKED (model ladder, declared as such): survival per chick
  falling as clutch grows — 2 × 0.9 = 1.8 fledged; 4 × 0.8 =
  3.2; 6 × 0.6 = 3.6; 8 × 0.3 = 2.4. The peak (6) sits BELOW the
  physical maximum — why real clutches settle under what the
  parent could lay.

LAW: the farm year counts forward from the breeding date, and a
  birth moves only by skipping a cycle — the calendar is the
  animal's. Day length, not feeding, opens many seasons (sheep
  as days shorten, horses as they lengthen): the one signal
  exactly repeatable every year.
WORKED: a cow carries ~283 days; the same calving date next year
  needs conception within 365 − 283 = 82 days — at a 21-day
  cycle, 82 ÷ 21 ≈ 4 heats, minus ~50 days recovery: about two
  honest tries. A herd slipped six weeks has every job of the
  year in the wrong month; the road back costs a season.
WORKED, colostrum: the newborn gut passes whole antibodies for
  hours — closing from ~6, shut by ~a day, never reopening.
  Rule: ~10% of body weight in the first half-day — a 40 kg calf
  needs 40 × 0.10 = 4 kg ≈ 4 L; missed, nothing corrects it.
  The mother's antibodies also BLOCK vaccines while they last:
  protected but unteachable, then briefly neither — the gap when
  the young get ill, so vaccination is timed to the fade.
WORKED, feed conversion: to add 1 kg of body — chicken ~1.7 kg
  feed, pig ~3, steer ~7; feed-for-feed the beef kilogram costs
  7 ÷ 1.7 ≈ 4× the chicken kilogram. The young convert best
  everywhere — maintenance is paid before growth and the body to
  maintain keeps growing — so the last month of finishing is the
  dearest food on the farm.
WORKED, the withdrawal clock: a treated cow's milk is dumped for
  the drug's stated period — 4 days × 2 milkings = 8 milkings
  poured away; one missed clock spoils the bulk tank. The period
  belongs to the drug's own chemistry, not to judgement.

## METHODS
How things are done and kept here — the prepositioned how-to.

- READ THE BODY PLAN FIRST: symmetry tells feeding, eyes tell
  hunter from hunted, gut length tells diet, teeth confirm —
  unrelated animals doing one job converge on the same toolkit,
  so the job is readable off the tools.
- AIR BEFORE HEAT: animals throw off warmth, water vapor,
  ammonia, dust; seal the building for warmth and all four stay.
  Cold dry moving air grows healthy stock; warm damp stillness
  grows pneumonia. Check the air at the animal's head — near the
  floor, where ammonia off wet bedding pools and burns the airway
  open for every virus, while the inspector stands five feet up
  in good air.
- BEDDING'S FOUR JOBS, all mechanical: dry, warm off the floor,
  cushioned, gripped; wet bedding reverses all four and cultures
  the udder and foot diseases. The udder is an opening into the
  body, held over the bed, opened twice daily by a machine going
  cow to cow — mastitis is treated in the parlour and shed before
  the tube.
- TRUST THE FLOOR: on slippery concrete a cow will not show heat,
  stride, or turn — missed breeding, less eating, one day the
  splits. Lameness is nearly always MADE: hours on concrete, bad
  cubicles, wet underfoot, sharp stones, too little rest. A hoof
  grows and wears continuously; comfort is those rates matching,
  and trimming only corrects a mismatch the ground caused.
- FEED THE VAT SLOWLY: rations changed over weeks, long fiber
  kept, grain gradual — the pH law governs.
- WATER FIRST: a shed doing poorly on good feed is asked about
  the trough before the ration — how much, how fast, how clean,
  how many can drink at once.
- CONDITION-SCORE BY HAND: fat felt over back and tail head
  reports WEEKS of balance between fed and demanded — two cows of
  one weight can be one too thin, one too fat, and the difference
  decides who gets in calf. Integrating measurements read
  management; snapshots read today.
- MIND THE GROUND: copper, selenium, cobalt, iodine run soil →
  plant → animal; shortages are regional, the same fields yearly
  — local knowledge beats a general supplement. The margin is
  narrow: lick + bolus + injection + fortified feed stack to a
  poisoning, each correct alone; sheep die of the copper cattle
  need.
- PLAN THE BIRTH MONTHS EARLY: dam's condition, sire's size, the
  mother's own finished growth decide the calf-to-pelvis fit
  before they meet. Feed the last third of pregnancy hardest —
  nearly all growth is there, and a weak newborn, poor colostrum,
  and no milk reserve cannot be corrected after.
- AT THE BIRTH, WATCH PROGRESS, NOT THE CLOCK: pulling before the
  passage opens tears the mother; waiting past stalled progress
  kills both. Know each stage's normal length; ask "is it
  advancing," not "how long has it been."
- SPREAD THE WEANING STRESSES: diet change, loss of the mother,
  mixing, transport — manageable alone, together they make the
  famous pneumonia and scour. Separate by a week or two apiece.
- VACCINATE BEFORE THE RISK, NEVER AT THE OUTBREAK: two to four
  weeks and often a second dose to work, to healthy animals, on
  a plan — a vaccine reduces severity and shedding; it is not a
  fence (the mirror holds that want).
- KEEP THE GATE: new disease arrives by a countable list — bought
  animals, shared kit, vehicles, boots, the fence line, water,
  feed, vermin — each route closed by an ordinary measure.
  Quarantine arrivals for WEEKS: incubation is silent; appearance
  at arrival carries no information.
- ALL IN, ALL OUT: fill, empty completely, clean, DRY, refill —
  an interruption the organism cannot cross. A never-empty
  building hands the youngest whatever the oldest carry, load
  climbing year on year.
- HANDLE BY GEOMETRY, NOT WILL: every animal has a distance at
  which it moves off and a point at the shoulder — ahead backs it
  up, behind sends it forward. A race curving out of sight of its
  end, solid-sided, evenly lit, without shadows, puddles, or
  flapping coats moves stock faster than shouting — high-pitched
  noise frightens more than movement. What is blamed on
  stubbornness is design.
- LOAD LESS, MIX LESS: transport's harm is at the ends —
  gathering, fasting, mixing, the ramp, the strange pen — not the
  smooth-road middle. Count loadings and mixings before hours.
- AT SLAUGHTER, THE STUN IS THE QUESTION: instantly unconscious,
  never recovering before death — restraint, trained operator,
  checked signs, backup device. Where death is accepted, the
  moral weight falls on whether there was experience of it.
- CULL AS A HEALTH TOOL: removing the chronic mastitis cow, the
  repeat-lame, the carrier, the never-in-calf removes infection
  source, treating cost, and the genetics that produced them —
  the strongest, cheapest lever, used least because it feels like
  failure. The individual's value caps its medicine, so the
  patient is the herd: prevent, vaccinate, adjust, cull.
- BREED AGAINST THE LINKED DRAG: one production trait pushed
  drags fitness traits (the highest-yield cow breeds worse, walks
  worse, lasts less; the fastest broiler's legs fail). Not a
  moral failure — a correlation, bred against deliberately.
- TIME OUTSIDE PARASITES BY CALENDAR: fly, tick, louse, mite run
  on warmth and season — treat against emergence, not visible
  bother, by which point the season is half over.
- END A LIFE ON TIME: the commonest failure is too late — hope,
  sunk cost, nobody wanting to say it. A day too early is a
  small wrong; a week too late is a large one.
- WALK THE SHED DAILY, KNOWING THE INSTRUMENT'S CAPACITY: the
  primary diagnostic is a person who knows what these animals
  usually do; past a number per person the walk becomes a count
  and the quiet early signs vanish.
- TREAT THE BUILDING, NOT ONLY THE ANIMAL: almost everything a
  farm vet treats was caused by air, floor, bed, feed change,
  mixing, or water — the useful visit walks the buildings and
  reads the records; the drug is done while the cause is put
  right.

## HISTORY
How the knowledge arose, in what order, at what cost.

- The dog first, before farming (~15,000-30,000 years): wolves at
  the fire's edge, the bargain struck on scraps and warning
  barks. The receipts of who changed sit in both parties — the
  dog: brain ~30% smaller than a same-size wolf's, floppy ears,
  starch digestion multiplied (many extra amylase gene copies
  against the wolf's two), an eyebrow muscle wolves lack, the
  reading of a human point that hand-raised wolves fail; the
  human: in herding peoples, adult milk digestion rose from
  nearly none to the majority within a few thousand years. The
  bargain ran both ways; both parties carry the paperwork.
- The barnyard next (~10,000 years): sheep, goat, pig, cow — the
  ~14 of ~150 candidates that passed the conjunction. The
  failures are knowledge too, filed with their reasons.
- The fox experiment, the receipt read in one lifetime: caged
  foxes selected ONLY for tameness gave floppy ears, curled
  tails, piebald coats within ~10 generations, an eagerly tame
  line within ~40 — temperament is breedable and drags a whole
  syndrome, exactly as the dog's body testifies.
- Husbandry by eye for millennia: shed rules, birth timing, the
  scoring hand all predate their explanations — method outrunning
  why for centuries and feeding everyone meanwhile.
- Germ theory reached the barn late and rewrote it: dose,
  crowding, carrier, quarantine — superstition became arithmetic;
  the gate replaced the charm.
- The wormer and the routine antibiotic, then their shadow: the
  drench that stopped working, resistance bred in the gut and
  shipped off the farm in meat and slurry — the stewardship rules
  above are that lesson, written in lost drugs (the mirror holds
  the record).
- Welfare codified late: the five freedoms reached law-shaped
  language only in the 1960s, after a report on what confinement
  had become — the fifth need entering the books last, a century
  after the trades knew it.
- The fisheries lesson, paid in cod: culling the biggest breeders
  spent capital while reporting it as income — a whole fishery
  closed in one year, not recovered decades on.
