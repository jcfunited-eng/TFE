# ANIMALS — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/animals/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. Seed authored by Claude from
docs/fabric_phylums/116_deep_animals_and_insects.md and

What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and conditions, so

tombstone.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE TALKING ANIMAL — the limit stated exactly, because the real
  part must not be buried with the false part. REAL: reference
  (vervet monkeys give distinct alarms for eagle, snake, leopard,
  and hearers act differently on each); displacement in one closed
  case (the bee's dance reports a place not present — angle from
  vertical = angle from sun, ~1 second of waggle ≈ ~1 km — but its
  grammar is exactly two variables and can say nothing else);
  vocabulary (a border collie held ~1,000 object names; a parrot
  used ~100 words with categories). NEVER DEMONSTRATED: syntax —
  combining known signs by rule so the ARRANGEMENT carries new
  meaning. Conditions of the certification: decades of ape sign
  projects; the best-recorded chimp's utterances averaged under
  two signs, word order never carried meaning, and his longest
  string — "give orange me give eat orange me eat orange give me
  eat orange give me you" — is sixteen words with no structure.
  Would drain on: any animal producing novel rule-governed
  combinations understood by another. Communication is real;
  language, so far, is ours.
- THE GIANT INSECT — cannot at today's air, for three stacked
  reasons (the color holds them: s³ weight against s² strength,
  passive breathing tubes, the naked moult). THE DRAIN ALREADY
  HAPPENED ONCE, which is why this entry shimmers: when the air
  held roughly half again today's oxygen (~30% against ~21%),
  dragonflies with ~70 cm wingspans flew — one condition moved
  and the ceiling moved with it. File beside wings-on-arms: a cannot UNDER CONDITIONS, with
  the receipt that the conditions once differed.
- THE DOMESTICATED ZEBRA, the open remainder — the settled half
  is filed in the color (temperament conjunction; tamed
  individuals, never a domesticated population). Open: whether a
  sustained, fox-style, generations-long program selecting only
  tameness would crack it. The fox receipt says temperament IS
  breedable; nobody has ever paid for the zebra version. Nothing
  certain — but here is a possibility: the failure was economic
  (the horse already existed, so the program never earned its
  cost), not biological. Would settle it: the program itself, run
  for ~40 generations.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "THE QUEEN RULES THE HIVE" — false: she issues no orders and
  decides nothing; she is the colony's ovary, and the observed
  order comes from thousands of workers running local rules (age
  decides the job, attendance counts the vote). Kept because
  queries still arrive carrying a royal court that is not there.
- "THE ALPHA WOLF WINS ITS RANK BY COMBAT" — didn't hold: the
  model came from unrelated captive wolves forced together; wild
  packs are a breeding pair and their own young — a family, not a
  fight ladder. The author of the popular version spent years
  asking for his own book to be withdrawn. Conditions filed:
  captivity manufactured the behavior the theory then explained.
- "NO SIGNS, NO PAIN" — the century's costliest husbandry error:
  prey animals SUPPRESS the display precisely when the weakness
  is greatest, because the limper is the one taken. The claim
  read silence as comfort; the color holds the correction (the
  baseline method) and the history holds the bill.
- "TREAT THE SICK ANIMAL AND THE HERD PROBLEM IS SOLVED" — failed
  wherever the cause was the building: air, floor, bed, water,
  mixing. The treated recover into the same cause; the problem
  returns on schedule. Drained only by treating the place.
- "BATS ARE BLIND"; "THE GOLDFISH FORGETS IN SECONDS"; "THE
  OSTRICH BURIES ITS HEAD" — all false by plain observation (all
  bats see; trained fish remember for months; no ostrich does).
  Kept as a bundle: folk claims that queries still carry.
- UNSETTLED: THE MAP SENSE — a first-year bird crosses an ocean
  alone with an inherited heading, and the compass instruments
  are known (sun, stars, magnetism) — but WHERE the magnetic
  sense lives, no one has found: no organ located after decades.
  Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: the receptor is
  molecular (a light-driven reaction in the eye whose outcome
  tilts with the field), which would explain why no dissectible
  organ exists. Would drain on: locating and disabling the
  receptor, and losing the heading with it.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT, IF ANYTHING, IT IS LIKE — behavior evidence
  (the jay, the crow) certifies planning and memory of absent
  things; it cannot reach whether experience rides along. The
  simplest-account bar that guards against over-crediting minds
  cannot certify their absence either — the instrument is missing
  in both directions (named in SCIENCE). Nothing certain — but
  here is a possibility: experience is graded, not on-off, and
  tracks integration rather than kinship to us — which is how the
  law that recently added octopus and crab to the protected list
  actually bet.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: AN INSTRUMENT FOR EXPERIENCE — the subject can measure
  behavior, hormones, and healing, and cannot measure feeling
  directly in anything that does not talk. Every welfare number
  is a proxy read through behavior against a baseline. This is
  the subject's largest named absence; the pain entries in both
  sheets lean on it. Nothing certain — but here is a
  possibility: calibrate a measure in the one animal that can
  report (us), then carry it across the shared machinery. It
  would start to drain the day that measure out-predicts the
  baseline walker.
- LACKING: A PREDICTOR OF DOMESTICABILITY — the conjunction list
  (breeds watched, grows fast enough, calm at fences, feedable,
  rankable) is real but written AFTER the fact; no test run on a
  wild population predicts, before the decades are spent, whether
  the fox result generalizes. Would drain on: a trialed predictor
  scoring candidate species before the program buys them.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: ROUTINE ANTIBIOTICS AS GROWTH TOOL — the
  chemistry worked (animals grew), and the heredity answered:
  gut-bred resistance left the farm in meat, slurry, and boots,
  and the genes moved sideways into human pathogens. Banned in
  whole jurisdictions; the cost fell on people outside the
  transaction. The color's stewardship rules are the rebuild.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: SINGLE-TRAIT BREEDING ARITHMETIC — indexes
  that priced only yield dragged fertility, feet, and lifespan
  backward through shared physiology; the broiler's legs and the
  dairy cow's short career are the measured wreckage. The fix
  (balanced indexes) is the same math with the linked traits
  priced in — the science didn't fail; the objective did.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- TEACHING APES SENTENCES — signs were learned (real), requests
  were made (real), grammar never came (the receipts are in
  THINGS). Conditions: cross-fostered rearing, years of training,
  the most human-ready species available. The strong form is
  certified failed; the entry stands so the next try must name
  what it will do differently.
- RIDING THE ZEBRA — tried by colonists who needed African horses
  (harnessed teams were driven as stunts): individuals broke to
  harness; populations never held. Failure conditions: panic
  reflex, the bite that does not release, the rope-ducking head,
  and no funder staying ~40 generations. Paired with its drain
  path in THINGS.
- KILLING PESTS WITHOUT TOUCHING THEIR SUPPORT — a century of
  poison campaigns rebuilt every population they knocked down
  (the color runs the arithmetic: 90% killed, back in ~4
  months). Works only fused with sealing food, water, entry.
  Filed because the failing form is still the commonest form.
- BLANKET-DOSING THE WHOLE FLOCK ONTO CLEAN PASTURE — the
  best-practice of one generation became the resistance engine of
  the next: it hands the next generation only resistant parents
  (arithmetic in the color). Drained by refugia — deliberately
  leaving some untreated — which is the old method inverted.
- HELPING THE BIRTH EARLY, SEALING THE SHED WARM, FEEDING THE
  PREGNANCY FLAT, WEANING ALL AT ONCE — four husbandry tries that
  each failed the same way: acting on the schedule of the human
  instead of the biology's own stages, and paying in torn
  mothers, pneumonia, weak newborns, and scour. Each drained by
  its color method (watch progress; air before heat; feed the
  last third; spread the stresses).
- CAGE BANS THAT MOVED HARM INSTEAD OF REMOVING IT — freeing hens
  to floors and ranges traded one injury list (confinement) for
  another (pecking, piling, predators); measured mortality did
  not simply fall. Not a cannot — a conditions entry: the fifth
  need and the safety needs pull against each other, and a ban
  answers only one. The open want is in PURPOSE.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE VACCINE AS FENCE — fell short of the wish everywhere: it
  reduces severity and shedding, needs weeks and boosters, and
  covers the strains it was built from. Kept as the honest
  version of a great tool; the wish for a wall stands open.
- THE GENERAL MINERAL SUPPLEMENT — fell short because shortages
  are written in the local rock (soil → plant → animal), and the
  narrow-margin elements make a blind supplement a slow poison;
  superseded by testing the ground and the herd.
- THE SENSOR IN PLACE OF THE STOCKPERSON — collars and cameras
  count steps and chewing, and still miss what the daily walker
  catches: the small specific change against a known baseline.
  Not yet found: an instrument that knows THIS animal's normal.
  Would drain on: monitoring that beats the walker's early-catch
  rate on trial, not on brochure.
- A PAIN READOUT WITHOUT WORDS — wanted by every vet and every
  keeper; blocked on the SCIENCE absence above. The workaround
  (baseline behavior, read by the person who knows the animal) is
  the best mean written, and it does not scale past the walker's
  stated capacity.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- ASK THE ANIMAL — put the question in ITS terms and get its
  answer back. Communication is real and used daily (the flight
  zone, the alarm call, preference tests that let animals vote
  with their feet); the full want waits on the language limit in
  THINGS and the instrument gap in SCIENCE.
- THE HUMANE PERFECT FARM — all five needs met, the fifth
  included, at food prices people pay. The tensions, stated so
  the want stays honest: behavior-room costs land; outdoors
  trades confinement's harms for weather, predator, and parasite;
  safety pulls against freedom; and feed at two-thirds of cost
  presses every margin toward crowding. Nothing certain — but
  here is a possibility: the calm-handling result (welfare and
  production the same argument) is the pattern to hunt — find the
  designs where the two curves run together instead of crossing,
  and build only those.
- KEEP THE DRUGS WORKING FOREVER — stewardship slows resistance;
  nothing written stops it. Every dose is still a selection
  event; the want is a treatment that selects for nothing, and
  none is written.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side.

- The uncounted failed domestications: for every barnyard species,
  many tries on species that panicked, sickened, or starved —
  the ~14 winners are survivorship's shortlist from ~150
  candidates, and the losers' reasons became the conjunction
  list. The color's checklist is this graveyard, compressed.
- The century of withheld pain relief: silence read as comfort,
  in exactly the animals built to be silent. Paid by the animals;
  corrected only when baseline-reading entered the trades.
- The ape-language decade: raised hopes, learned signs, and a
  collapse under its own recordings — the project that certified
  the limit now filed exactly in THINGS. Its cost bought the
  clean statement of what IS real in animal communication.
- The growth-promoter era: the cheap gain taken for decades, the
  resistance bill arriving later, off the farm, in other bodies —
  the clearest case in the subject of a cost that walked away
  from its cause.
- The cod ledger: the biggest breeders culled first for decades
  under the belief the sea was bottomless; the fishery closed in
  a single year and has not come back in a working lifetime. The
  indeterminate-growth law was in the color all along; the white
  records that it was ignored at industrial scale.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- ANIMALS ↔ LANGUAGE — the suspected full relation never held:
  the crossing carries signals, referents, and vocabulary, and
  stops exactly at syntax. Stands dotted at the limit stated in
  THINGS; would go first-class on the drain named there.
- ANIMALS ↔ MIND — partly proven (planning, memory of absent
  things, blind scripts mapped), unproven at experience itself;
  the missing instrument is named in SCIENCE. The most consequent
  dotted line in the phylum: welfare law is already betting on it
  ahead of proof.
- ANIMALS ↔ MEDICINE — broke at routine antibiotics (the cost
  crossed species and left the transaction); rebuilt carrying a
  speed limit, exactly as the model says a repaired relation
  should: stewardship IS knowledge about the relation.
- ANIMALS ↔ ETHICS — alive and unsettled at its boundary: the
  protected circle has widened (mammals, then birds, lately
  octopus and crab), and where it stops — the insect, the colony
  whose members are spendable to itself — is not written. Nothing
  certain — but here is a possibility: the boundary will follow
  the experience instrument when SCIENCE builds one, as it has
  followed every proxy so far.
