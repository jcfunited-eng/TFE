# FARMING — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/farming/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting
for its drain. Seed authored by Claude and marked as seed.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE PERPETUAL-YIELD FIELD — the oldest want: harvest forever,
  return nothing. CERTIFIED CANNOT-BE under conservation of mass:
  every tonne of grain carries off ~20 kg nitrogen, ~4 kg
  phosphorus, ~5 kg potassium (the color's ledger); a field is a
  finite vault, and every "inexhaustible" new land in the record
  proved it in two or three generations of falling yields. The
  shimmer, honestly drawn: for NITROGEN the condition moved — the
  air is four-fifths nitrogen, and legumes plus the factory drain
  that third of the entry. Phosphorus and potassium have no air
  route: rock or return, or not at all. Two-thirds certified,
  one-third drained — the exact shape of the shimmer.
- A CEREAL THAT FIXES ITS OWN NITROGEN — wheat with clover's
  hired bacteria; not yet real, the grasses never built the
  nodule contract. Partial patch: the rice paddy's floating fern
  carries a fixing partner, ~30-50 kg/ha the traditional way.
  Would drain on: engineering the symbiosis into a grass —
  attempted now, unfinished, alive.
- THE PERENNIAL GRAIN — plant once, harvest yearly, never plow:
  the erosion ledger's dream crop. Attempted (perennial
  wheatgrass): real grain, ~a quarter of wheat's yield. WHY it
  lags: seed and lasting roots draw on the same sunlight payroll
  — a plant that must keep its roots alive for next year cannot
  yet put half its weight into seed. Would drain on: breeding that lifts
  harvest index without spending the root; a budget conflict, not
  fate, and the compounding law has had decades, not millennia.
- THE WEATHERPROOF CROP — immune to drought and frost. Not real:
  tolerance breeding buys ~10-20% under stress, never immunity —
  500 tonnes of water per tonne of grain is not a preference a
  gene can decline. A gradient being climbed, not a wall drained.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- LYSENKO'S INHERITANCE — the claim: treat the parent (chill the
  seed, train the plant) and the gain passes to offspring. Tried
  at NATIONAL SCALE for decades. FAILED CATASTROPHICALLY: the
  breeder's equation answers only heritable variation; treating
  a parent writes nothing into its seed — even the chilled-seed
  trick must be redone from scratch every generation. Receipts: yields never rose; genetics
  banned for a generation, its scientists imprisoned; the founder
  of the first great seed bank starved in prison while the
  doctrine held office; meanwhile real breeding elsewhere
  compounded 2% a year. WHY believed: it flattered a politics and
  promised speed the equation refuses. The shimmer at true size:
  a treated parent can leave faint chemical settings on a
  generation or two — real, narrow, fading — and nothing at any
  dose rescues the doctrine at crop scale. Would drain only on:
  stable multi-generation inheritance
  of an acquired trait at field scale; none is written.
- "RAIN FOLLOWS THE PLOW" — cultivation itself brings rainfall;
  sold to settlers of dry plains during a wet decade. FAILED on
  the first dry cycle: plowing moves no ocean and lifts no air
  mass; the wet years were weather, not reward, and the
  homesteads emptied. Filed as farming's cleanest lesson that
  coincidence in a short record is not a claim.
- THE MOON-PHASE PLANTING CALENDAR — measured repeatedly: no
  yield effect survives controlling for soil temperature and
  date. Kept with its kindness noted: it got seed sown on time,
  which the real scheduling law rewards. Would drain on: a
  controlled lunar effect beyond schedule; none is written.
- "FALLOW REST RESTORES EVERYTHING" — half held. Rest stores a
  dry year's water and breaks pest cycles (the color keeps both);
  it does NOT refill phosphorus or potassium — rest is not
  revenue. The why: nitrogen alone creeps back (free-living
  fixers), and early observers credited rest itself.
- "DEEP AND FREQUENT PLOWING IS ALWAYS BETTER" — held on wet
  European loams, carried as dogma to dry plains, and failed at
  continental scale twice in one century: bare turned soil on
  dry wind obeys the erosion arithmetic. The condition — rain and
  cover — was part of the method and was dropped in transit.
  Drained into no-till, the color's condition-moved form.
- UNSETTLED: WHY TWO LIKE FIELDS YIELD UNLIKE — same seed, same
  rain, same bag, a fifth apart at harvest. Nothing certain — but
  here is a possibility: the living fraction differs field to
  field and pays different wages from the same vault (the SCIENCE
  absence below, seen from above). Would drain on: reading a
  field's biology well enough to predict its paycheck.
- UNSETTLED: HOW MUCH ANIMALS BELONG IN A FED WORLD — the 10:1
  toll is arithmetic (the color holds it); the counterclaim
  (stock on land and scraps people cannot eat are addition) is
  also true. Where the line sits, place by place, is not yet a
  computable claim. Filed unsettled with both halves stated.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A LEDGER FOR THE LIVING BANK — a gram of soil holds a
  billion organisms, most unnamed and unread; the color can weigh
  the vault (2,600 tonnes, 3% organic) and meter its average
  payout (~78 kg nitrogen a year) but cannot say WHICH staff do
  it or predict one field's payroll from its census. Farming
  manages a bank whose tellers it cannot interview — the
  subject's largest named absence. Would drain on: community
  reading that predicts function; the census has begun, the
  prediction is the missing half.
- LACKING: THE MISSING FOUR-FIFTHS OF THE PAYROLL — field crops
  catch ~1% of the season's sun against a ~4-6% ceiling. The gap
  has a named culprit (the carbon-catching enzyme wastes cycles
  on oxygen) and a running attempt (the thriftier C4 route bred
  into rice), unfinished. Every point drained from this gap
  re-runs the color's payroll arithmetic with a bigger wage.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: NUTRITION FLATTENED TO THREE LETTERS — the
  mineral doctrine's triumph (N-P-K) was applied as if three
  numbers were the whole ledger; fields fed the big three quietly
  mined zinc, boron, and sulfur, and the shortest-stave law bit
  its own students. Lesson filed: a ledger's discovered columns
  are not its only columns.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
wings-on-arms entries, waiting for their planet.

- WEATHER CONTROL FOR THE FARM — order rain, cancel hail. MOSTLY
  WHITE with one small drained patch. The measured failures:
  cannonading clouds for rain (nothing), hail cannons (still
  sold, no measured effect ever recorded), prayer and payment
  (the record's control condition). The DRAINED PATCH, exactly
  bounded: seeding suitable winter mountain clouds with
  ice-nucleating smoke squeezes ~5-15% more snow from storms
  already coming — measured, marginal, real. Hard condition:
  there must BE a cloud, cold and wet in the right way; seeding
  summons nothing from clear sky. The rest stands white: one
  storm outweighs humanity's machines, and the lever is not
  found. Would drain further on: a lever, not a louder cannon.
- LYSENKO'S FIELD METHODS — filed apart from the claim: mass
  chilled-seed sowing promised multiplied harvests and delivered
  noise; "cluster planting" on the theory that a species does not
  compete with itself — it does; the seedlings shaded and starved
  each other exactly as the payroll arithmetic prices it.
  Receipts at national scale, paid in hunger. The why: a method
  built on a false claim fails wherever the claim binds, and no
  scale of application converts belief into nitrogen.
- ONE MOLECULE FOREVER — the mid-century bet that one potent
  insecticide ends the insect question. WORKED, then reversed:
  spraying whole regions IS selection — kill 99.9% and the
  survivors' children inherit the reason; the breeder's equation
  ran for the insect at a generation a month, and the molecule
  climbed a food chain it was never invited to. Drained into the
  color's threshold scouting and refuge practice (keep some pests
  unselected). Condition carried: ANY kill applied uniformly and
  constantly re-runs this entry, whatever the molecule.
- ONE CLONE ACROSS A NATION — uniformity's three paid receipts:
  a country fed on a single potato clone lost it to one blight
  (a million dead, a million fled); the world's export banana was
  one clone and one soil fungus retired it — its replacement is
  one clone again, and the fungus's new strain is walking; most
  of a nation's corn carried one borrowed mother-line (a shortcut
  for making hybrid seed without hand labor), its matching blight
  arrived, and ~15% of the crop went in a season. WHY, in one
  sentence: one genome is one lock, and evolution picks locks for
  a living. Not a cannot — a condition: uniformity is safe
  exactly until something that fits it arrives, and the record
  says that clause expires. Drains only into diversity held on
  purpose (the color's seed bank and rotation).
- PLOWING THE DRY GRASSLANDS — tried twice at continental scale
  in one century (the plains in the '30s, the steppes in the
  '50s), same result: a few wet-year harvests, then the erosion
  arithmetic collected — soil in the sky, farms in default. The
  dropped condition: the grass cover WAS what held that soil.
  Filed as the standing proof that a method's conditions are part
  of the method — farming's altitude lesson, written in dust.
- IRRIGATING WITHOUT DRAINING — hot-country irrigation with no
  exit: evaporation leaves every dissolved salt behind, and
  fields are seasoned to sterility over generations. Paid first
  by the oldest irrigating civilizations, paid still. Drains on:
  drainage built WITH the canal and water enough to flush — the
  method is a pair, not a single.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE HAIL CANNON — bought by generations of orchardists,
  measured whenever anyone dared: no effect. The monument to
  buying a lever that does not reach the sky. Would drain only
  with the weather-control entry above: a real lever, not a
  louder cannon.
- THE LOST DARK-EARTH RECIPE — Amazonian farmers built soil still
  fertile centuries after its makers vanished: charcoal, wastes,
  and time in proportions never written down. The modern
  reconstruction (biochar) gives mixed trials — half-refound at
  best. Would drain on: trials that say WHICH soils and doses
  repay it; a mean is refound when its conditions are.
- THE SOIL TEST'S BLINDNESS — the honest instrument weighs the
  vault (stocks) and guesses the payroll (what the living staff
  will release this season — what the crop actually receives).
  NOT YET FOUND: an instrument reading what a field will PAY,
  not what it holds.
- STORAGE WITHOUT LOSS — shares the kitchen's white: every
  keeping costs. Grain never fully stops breathing; the floor of
  loss is low but not zero, and in poor storage the old tithe
  (a sack in five) is still paid today. Ancient, open, honestly
  stated: time still charges for keeping, in barns as in
  kitchens.
- A SUBSTITUTE FOR LAND — the one unmanufacturable mean. The
  attempted patch (stacked indoor farming) must BUY the light the
  sun delivers free: ~27,000,000 MJ falls on a hectare per season
  (the color's 2,700 MJ/m²), and the crop catches only ~1% of it
  — so even lamps tuned to waste less than the sky leave an
  electric bill in the millions of MJ per hectare of grain. That
  is why the patches grow leaves and herbs, never wheat. Would
  drain on: energy cheap enough that a manufactured sun undercuts
  a free one.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- THE LOST FLAVOR OF BRED-FOR-SHIPPING PRODUCE — filed with its
  why: the breeder's equation improves what is measured, and for
  a century the scoresheet read yield, firmness, shelf life,
  uniform ripening — flavor was not a column, so it drifted. And
  worse than drifted: the tomato gene chosen for uniform ripening
  sits broken across one that fills the fruit with sugar — the
  beautiful shelf tomato PAID in sweetness, by linkage, not
  accident. Would drain on: flavor entering the scoresheet as
  measurable columns (sugars, acids, aromas — the cooking phylum
  knows them); begun, unfinished. The wolf-to-dog story runs
  wherever it is pointed; this is the proof it was pointed at
  the truck.
- FARMING WITHOUT WEATHER RISK — cannot yet; the season is a bet
  and the sky holds the deck. Irrigation, storage, insurance,
  and forecasts each shave the risk; none retires it. Blocked
  jointly on the weather-control entry above and the weather
  phylum's own limits.
- FEED EVERYONE WITHOUT NEW LAND — the payroll ceiling times the
  land already farmed, against mouths still compounding. The
  color says the hectare can still climb (the 1% gap, the 2%
  breeding rate); whether it climbs faster than the need, and who
  can pay, are books this phylum holds only a page of — the want
  lives jointly with economy, weather, and culture.
- KEEP EVERY VARIETY ALIVE — most named vegetable varieties of a
  century ago are gone from commerce; each was a tested answer to
  some place's soil, pests, and kitchen, and the one-clone entry
  says the lost locks will be wanted. Seed banks are the patch,
  not the answer: a variety in a vault is paused, not farmed.
  Would drain on: diversity that pays its way back into fields,
  not just freezers.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- The uncounted selection dead: behind every landrace in the
  color stand the seasons that failed — frost-killed sowings,
  wrong-valley transplants, the famines that were the price of
  learning each place's window by dying outside it. The color
  says "learned by exhaustion"; this page counts the exhausted
  as the method's true cost.
- The seed bank kept by starving curators: in a besieged city,
  keepers of the world's first great crop collection died of
  hunger in rooms full of edible seed, and did not eat it — one
  winter weighed against every future harvest. The white's
  clearest statement that the seed IS the compounding instrument,
  priced by people who held it while its science stood banned
  outside the door.
- The guano ledger: the balance law proved by exhaustion —
  island cliffs of concentrated payroll, built dropping by
  dropping over millennia, shipped away in ~forty years while
  empires bid. Deposits that took a thousand years to write
  cannot be withdrawn in one lifetime and remain a bank.
- The dust decade: the plow's portrait — tens of millions of
  hectares opened in wet years, then the sky took the topsoil in
  black blizzards visible from the cities that had eaten the
  wheat. The color's no-till entry is the clean answer; this
  entry is what it cost, and why "keep it covered" is written
  where it can never again be dropped in transit.
- Lysenko's thirty years: the fullest record anywhere of a false
  claim held in office by force — the trying was not stopped by
  evidence because evidence was demoted below the claim. Filed
  beside the starving curators, its exact contemporaries: the
  same state starved the science that worked and fed the doctrine
  that didn't. The white keeps both so neither is retried.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, THEN REBUILT HONESTLY: FARMING → WEATHER — "rain
  follows the plow" claimed a lever that was not there; the
  rebuilt relation runs the other way (weather drives farming)
  with one honest dotted line back: large irrigation measurably
  moistens its downwind air a little, and the seeded winter
  cloud yields its ~5-15%. The relation now carries a warning
  label: claims of farming steering the sky must arrive with
  measurements, because the last one that didn't emptied a
  region.
- BROKEN, THEN REBUILT: FARMING ↔ HEREDITY — the Lysenko years
  wired farming to a false biology and paid in famine; the
  rebuilt line runs on the breeder's equation and is the most
  profitable relation the subject owns (2% a year, compounded).
  Filed so the wire is never again chosen by politics.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: SOIL LIFE ↔ FLAVOR AND HEALTH — that a
  living soil's produce tastes different and feeds better; grips
  in lore (the vineyard's terroir, the gardener's certainty),
  unproven as field-scale measurement. Stands dotted on the same
  unread ledger as the SCIENCE absence; if that drains, this
  line firms or falls.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: FARM DIVERSITY ↔ HUMAN GUT HEALTH — the
  narrowing of crops to a few staples parallels a narrowing
  measured in the eater; cause is not established. Filed dotted
  between farming, the body, and medicine, awaiting a study that
  cashes it.
