# TRANSPORT — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The anti-mirror
of docs/phylums/transport/color.md: same parts, opposite sign. What
didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and conditions, so it
recomputes when new knowns move a condition. Seed authored by Claude
from the same three source files as the color.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot be.

- TELEPORTATION OF GOODS — the want under all of transport: the thing
  there without the journey. Open at physics, and the arithmetic says
  how far: matter as energy runs at 9x10¹⁶ J per kilogram — one
  kilogram converted and rebuilt would equal ~25 billion kWh, ~7 hours
  of the world's electricity — and no round-trip mechanism is written;
  the informational route (measure here, rebuild there) collides with
  the rule that a full unknown state cannot be copied, only moved by
  destroying the original. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility:
  the want drains piecewise, because words already teleport — every
  good whose worth is its information (documents, designs, money, the
  printable part of objects) exits the freight system entirely. Would
  drain further on: fabrication at the destination good enough that
  only feedstock ships.
- A FLEET WITH NO EMPTY MILES — structural, not a defect: freight
  rarely balances, so somebody rides back light and the paying leg
  carries both fares. Backhaul markets and triangular routings shave it
  (~20-30% of lorry-km still run empty); zero needs flows to balance,
  and flows are set by where things are made and wanted — which
  transport serves and cannot command. The economy phylum owns that
  condition, not this one.
- THE FLYING CAR FOR EVERYONE — attempted for a century; three stacked
  conditions fail it: lifting costs ~5-10 MJ per tonne-km against the
  road's ~0.7; a city's sky is a separation problem needing gaps that
  do not exist for a million commuters; and the last mile is the noisy
  mile. Would drain on: several times today's battery density (measured
  in SCIENCE) AND machine-run separation — the second is arriving.
- THE ONE VEHICLE FOR ALL MEDIA — cannot be: the medium shapes the
  machine, and water, air, and land price opposite shapes; every
  amphibian is a poor boat welded to a poor car. The compromise IS the
  finding, filed as law in the color.
- THE TUBE TRAIN IN VACUUM — the drag term deleted by removing the air.
  The physics is willing; the entry stays white on the prepared path's
  arithmetic: hundreds of km of sealed tube is dearer than rail for
  less capacity, with every breach a shared emergency. Would drain on a
  tube cheap per km at rail's scale — engineering, not physics.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is unsettled.

- "MORE LANES WILL CURE THE CONGESTION" — tried for seventy years,
  measured false: added road fills back to its old speed. WHY: people
  spend a roughly fixed budget of travel time (~1 hour a day, stable
  across cities and decades), so a faster road is spent on farther,
  oftener, at worse hours; measured elasticity near 1 — ~10% more
  lane-km, ~10% more vehicle-km. Receipt: a freeway widened to 23
  lanes for ~$2.8 billion had slower commutes within three years.
  PARTIAL DRAIN, as the shimmer predicts: change the condition — price
  the peak instead of building for it — and congestion charges cut
  inner traffic ~15-30% where tried. The lane was never the lever; the
  price of the hour was.
- "A FASTER VEHICLE MEANS A FASTER JOURNEY" — false wherever the joins
  dominate, which is most journeys under a day. Receipt: the
  supersonic airliner saved ~4 hours over the ocean at ~3x the fuel
  per seat, was forbidden its speed over land (the boom), barely moved
  the door-to-door day, and died of the arithmetic in 27 years.
- "SALES FIGURES ARE DEMAND FIGURES" — self-blinding: nobody buys what
  was not on the shelf, so every stockout deletes the demand it
  caused, and a system fed its own sales learns to stock less of what
  it kept running out of. The record measures the filter.
- "TWO NAMED SUPPLIERS ARE TWO PATHS" — failed in the counting: many
  dual-sourced parts traced to one factory, port, or country three
  tiers down. Diversification is where the paths separate, not the
  list. And the system's number is not the shelf's number: miscounts
  and wrong scans drift the record, the machine acts confidently on
  the wrong reading, and the gap is invisible until a customer faces
  it.
- "AN EFFICIENT CHAIN IS A GOOD CHAIN" — half a claim: efficiency
  means nothing spare, resilience means something spare — opposites by
  definition. Every calm decade converts the second into the first,
  because the spare costs visibly every year and pays invisibly once a
  generation — then the shock arrives and the shelves audit the
  doctrine in public.
- UNSETTLED: HOW TO DISSOLVE A JAM ALREADY FORMED — its birth is
  understood (gap shorter than reaction, filed in the color); its cure
  is not, beyond waiting out the wave. Nothing certain — but here is a
  possibility: the jam is made of human reaction time, so vehicles
  reacting in milliseconds and holding gaps by law could damp the wave
  as padding damps a timetable — a few per hundred might do. Would
  drain on measured dissolution in mixed traffic.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where it broke.

- LACKING: A LONG-RANGE FORECAST OF DEMAND — error compounds with the
  distance predicted across, and no model escapes it; the working
  answer is to need less prophecy: shorten the wait, aggregate until
  the numbers behave (the total is steady where the item is noise).
  Filed so queries stop shopping for a sharper prophet.
- MEASURED GAP: THE BATTERY AGAINST THE FUEL TANK — fuel ~43 MJ/kg,
  batteries ~0.9 (250 Wh/kg); engines keep ~35% at their best (the
  everyday quarter is in the color), motors ~90%, so the
  honest gap is 43 x 0.35 = 15.1 against 0.9 x 0.9 = 0.81 — ~19x. The
  road lives with it (it can recharge); flight cannot: fuel is cargo,
  and a 19x heavier store eats the payload, then the aircraft. Would
  drain on ~4-5x today's density — short hops first, the ocean last.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: PREDICT-AND-PROVIDE ROAD MODELS — the demand
  curves justifying wider roads assumed demand fixed while building
  for it; the assumption was the error (the lanes claim is its
  receipt). The repair: travel demanded is a function of travel
  supplied.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- WIDENING AS A CURE — tried repeatedly, receipts above; the subject's
  cleanest case of a method failing by succeeding: the new capacity is
  consumed as new journeys.
- THE FREIGHT AIRSHIP — lift free from buoyancy, tried and retried:
  slow (~100-130 km/h), a sail in any weather, sheds sized like a
  port, its safe gas scarce; a modern revival went bankrupt before its
  first ship. Would drain on cargo light, huge, unhurried, AND bound
  for doorless terrain — the niche exists; the network never closed.
- THE NUCLEAR MERCHANT SHIP — sailed, worked as physics, failed as
  arithmetic and welcome: crew and build above fuel savings at any oil
  price it met, and ports refused it entry. Three conditions to
  drain — dearer fuel, crewless reactors, willing harbors — none moved.
- LEAN WITHOUT DEPENDABILITY — stock stripped from an undependable
  system stops the line weekly: the buffer was absorbing variation
  nobody had measured. The color holds the order (dependable first,
  lean last); this is the graveyard of doing it backwards.
- FORECASTING HARDER AT THE ITEM LEVEL — the wobble at one shop, one
  color, one day is mostly noise. Aggregate, hold stock, or decide
  late — never prophesy the unpredictable finer.
- EXPEDITING AS A SYSTEM — the hot list reorders the queue and creates
  nothing: every place gained is a place lost, and when everything is
  rushed there are no priorities left — only a confession that the
  ordinary flow has died.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- NOT YET FOUND: A CONTAINER FOR THE DOORSTEP — the box abolished
  touching down to the depot; the last mile still takes hands on every
  parcel because doors, parcels, and people refuse one shape. Would
  drain on a receiving standard as universal as the box — an agreement
  problem, the hard kind.
- NOT YET FOUND: A PORT THAT NEVER QUEUES — the color's wall forbids
  it at high busyness; slack is the only known cure and slack is what
  the efficiency ledger keeps shaving. Not a cannot — a condition
  endlessly un-bought.
- ARRIVING, NOT ARRIVED: THE CREWLESS LORRY — the motorway leg is
  nearly solved; the joins (yards, docks, doorsteps) are not, and the
  color says the joins are the journey. Drains leg by leg, middle
  first.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- MOVE BODIES AT THE PRICE OF WORDS — distance died only for
  information; mass still pays the ladder. The gap between a free call
  and a thousand-dollar seat IS this phylum's outer wall, and the
  video call keeps eating the journeys that were only information
  wearing a body.
- DELIVER EVERYWHERE DAILY WITHOUT WASTE — the doorstep's arithmetic
  (one door splits nothing) and the empty backhaul stand between the
  want and the fleet.
- ABSORB A WORLD SHOCK WITHOUT EMPTY SHELVES — resilience is spare
  capacity some ledger must carry every quiet year; no chain yet holds
  it honestly priced. Open at governance and economy as much as here.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself.

- The box's decade of war: the container won only after fights with
  every union, port, and railroad whose living was the touching it
  abolished — the fortunes sunk into wrong-shaped ships are the white
  behind the color's tidy $0.16.
- The gauge wars: widths chosen almost by accident could not be
  un-chosen; borders where every wagon is lifted, one country running
  three gauges for a century — the standard's power, learned as its
  price.
- Canal mania's stranded fortunes: prepared paths bought at ruinous
  cost, obsoleted overnight by better prepared paths — the fixed
  asset's risk, paid one mode before rail paid it again to the road.
- The pandemic's public examination: ~100 ships at anchor off one
  coast, box rates from ~$2,000 toward ~$20,000, order books swollen
  by allocation gaming, then the cliff of cancellations — the queue
  wall, the bullwhip, and the efficiency-resilience trade running at
  once, on television. The chain's whole white, receipted in one year.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, REPAIRED WITH A CORRECTION: TRANSPORT ↔ GOVERNANCE —
  predict-and-provide planning fed the widening method its false
  premise; the repaired link carries induced demand as law and pricing
  as the working lever.
- HALF-BROKEN, FILED HONESTLY: TRANSPORT ↔ ECONOMY — "cheaper
  transport is always better" held for two centuries of growing trade,
  then bought fragility with the same coin; the link now carries both
  signs at once.
- SUSPECTED, PARTLY CASHED: QUEUE LAW ↔ EVERY SHARED RESOURCE — the
  junction's wall reappears at servers, hospitals, and power grids;
  computing has cashed the congestion-pricing shape as backpressure.
  Dotted toward medicine and governance until their phylums cash it
  with their own numbers.
