# TEACHING — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/teaching/color.md: same parts,
opposite sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone. Seed authored by Claude from
docs/fabric_phylums/30_teaching_learning.md and
docs/fabric_phylums/143_deep_education.md.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE GENERAL THINKING SKILL — reasoning trained once, applied
  everywhere. CANNOT BE while judgement is retrieved from a
  field's stored cases: brain training, the logic module, and the
  puzzle club improve the puzzles and nothing else — measured
  repeatedly. Would drain on: a training whose gains appear on
  tasks sharing no surface or structure with it; none is written.
- EFFORTLESS LEARNING — the sleep tape, passive absorption,
  material "so clear it teaches itself." CANNOT BE under the
  color's own mechanism: memory is strengthened by the work of
  getting at it, so removing the work removes the strengthening
  WITH the difficulty. Indexed by that condition: any known
  decoupling storage from effortful retrieval reopens this;
  nothing in the color does.
- THE TEACHER-PROOF MATERIAL — a package teaching the same
  regardless of who runs it. Tried at scale many times; every try
  foundered on one condition: everything works only through what
  the learner is made to do, and the person in the room is what
  makes them do it. Open, not cannot — the tutor-loop drain below
  is this want's honest form.
- THE HIRING FORM THAT FINDS GOOD TEACHERS — not found: degree,
  certificate, and years served fail to correlate past the first
  few years. Would drain on: a proxy validated against watched
  teaching; until then the only instrument is observation.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "MATCH THE TEACHING TO THE LEARNER'S STYLE" — tried for
  decades, killed by measurement. The fair test is the crossover:
  sort learners by preferred style, teach each group both ways;
  matching must beat mismatching. Almost the whole literature
  never ran it; the studies that did found no matching benefit
  (~ at least one found the reverse). The preference is real but
  about comfort; memory is stored in the form the MATERIAL
  demands — a map for geography, sound for music, a demonstration
  for a knot. Matching medium to subject helps everybody; to the
  learner, nobody. Would drain on: a replicated crossover with a
  matching gain; none is written, and an industry ran on its
  absence.
- "THE LECTURE TRANSFERS THE LECTURE" — mostly killed. Across
  hundreds of comparisons (~): under pure lecture ~34% fail the
  course; the same content worked actively, ~22% — exams ~half a
  spread-step higher. Worse, polish inverts the readings: the
  smooth, confident lecture is RATED higher by the people in it
  and learned from less — ease is read as understanding, and the
  effort removed was the learning. Survives in the color only as
  orientation, demonstration, and the scarce expert's reach.
- "PRACTICE MAKES PERFECT" — false as stated: unmonitored
  repetition fixes whatever was repeated, flaw included; twenty
  years of experience so often equals one year twenty times that
  the color rebuilt the claim as deliberate practice's four parts.
- "IT WENT WELL, SO THEY LEARNED IT" — the trade's standing
  instrument error: the five-minute reading crowns rereading
  (~81% to ~75%) while the week crowns retrieval (~56% to ~42%).
  Any method judged by the lesson's smoothness is judged by the
  wrong instrument. Drains per use, never in general: read the
  method at the holidays' distance, and the error is gone for
  that reading only.
- "REPEAT THE YEAR" — fails as practised: the same course at the
  same speed re-runs the exposure that already failed; the
  problem was a specific broken link. Drains only through
  diagnosis — the part almost never funded.
- "SMALLER CLASSES WILL FIX IT" — holds weakly, believed
  strongly: ~+0.2σ for a third fewer chairs (~), at nearly a
  second teacher's price, reversible if the extra rooms get
  weaker teachers. The chairs never taught; the claim survives
  because it is the remedy a parent can see.
- "STREAMING SORTS BY ABILITY" — it measurably sorts partly by
  birthday: in every country the youngest in a school year are
  placed lower, judged worse, diagnosed more, for years — a
  one-date measurement confusing development with attainment,
  made permanent by an administrative line. Helps the top a
  little, harms the bottom more, and the labels stick. Would
  drain on: placement scored against the child's own age in
  months, not the year's single line — computable now, rarely
  done.
- "GOOD SCHOOL" READ FROM RESULTS — unsound: most of the
  difference between children is built outside school and grows
  fastest in the holidays, so a league table reports mostly on
  intake. A figure combining two sources cannot be read as a
  statement about one. Would drain on: progress measured from
  each child's own starting point — partly built, noisy for
  small schools, and still gamed at the margins; the raw-results
  reading stays white.
- "THE YOUNG CAN MULTITASK" — false at the bench at every age:
  the feed beside the homework is switching with a restart cost —
  longer, shallower — and the person feels fine, which is why the
  claim survives its refutations. Would drain on: a measured
  group whose switching carries no restart cost on tasks that
  both need the bench; none is written at any age.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT EXACTLY THE GOOD TEACHER DOES — the largest
  effect a school controls, predicted by no proxy; no written
  composition of the color's practices equals the measured spread
  between teachers. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility:
  the difference lives mostly in loop speed and task choice,
  which paper cannot see and observation partly can. Would drain
  on: measured loop speed predicting teacher effect across rooms.
- UNSETTLED: HOW SLEEP FILES — that it files is measured; the
  mechanism is partly gripped (replayed patterns, moved storage),
  not composed. Would drain on: a mechanism predicting WHICH
  material gains most from which sleep; the body phylum holds the
  open end.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A PREDICTIVE THEORY OF THE SINGLE LESSON — the color
  prices schedules, chains, and class sizes, but cannot COMPUTE
  what one named learner keeps from tomorrow's hour; too many
  terms of the product (prior, dread, worth, sleep, room) are
  unread at lesson time. The trade steers by receipts and loops,
  not forecasts — weather before instruments.
- LACKING: THE COMPOSITION OF THE TWO SIGMA — the famous gain is
  measured as a lump (tutoring + mastery); no written account
  splits it into parts (loop speed, task choice, spacing,
  relationship, dread removed) with each part's own number. Until
  decomposed, nobody knows which parts scale.
- WHY PROVING ANYTHING HERE IS UNUSUALLY HARD, held as knowledge:
  the effect runs through a person over years; everything tangles
  with everything; volunteer schools are unusual schools; the
  outcome is a test that may not be the point. So findings SHRINK
  as trials grow bigger and better — the enthusiasts run the
  first trial. RULE the white enforces: believe an education
  effect at the size of its largest disinterested trial, not its
  first.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: FOLK PSYCHOLOGY AS CURRICULUM — styles,
  brain-halves, brain gym: each imported a mind-shaped story
  without receipts and spent a generation of teacher hours before
  the measurement arrived. Lesson filed: adopt claims about the
  mind at the speed they are certified, not the speed they
  flatter the classroom.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- MATCHING PRESENTATION TO LEARNER PREFERENCE — killed by the
  crossover receipts above; kept because queries still arrive
  carrying it: the cleanest case of a method that felt right, was
  cheap to believe, and measured as nothing.
- PRESSURE AS THE ENGINE — compliance bought with fear: produces
  work and burns the wish. The motivation product explains the
  wreckage: pressure raises neither term, and a zeroed worth term
  zeroes the product the day the pressure lifts — borrowing, not
  learning, by the color's own definition.
- PURE DISCOVERY FOR NOVICES — the beginner left to find the
  method unaided: fails on the bench budget (no chunks; the
  search fills the space the learning needed) and on guess-
  first's fine print — the attempt must be short, then corrected.
  The pendulum between tell-everything and tell-nothing swung a
  century; both ends are white.
- BLOCKED, MASSED, SMOOTH PRACTICE — twenty of one kind, today
  only, well supported: raises the week's performance, lowers the
  keeping, hides the choosing. Feels like teaching, measures like
  its absence — the trade's most defended error.
- HANDING OUT THE COMPLETE SLIDES — the transcript removes the
  deciding, and the deciding was the value of the notes. Serves
  only AFTER the learner's own summary exists, as a check; as a
  replacement it deletes the work.
- GROUP WORK ON ONE SHARED MARK — four people, one grade: one
  person does it, because a duty owned by everybody is owned by
  nobody. Drains only through visible, separately checked shares
  — and coordination costs more than the staffing it saves.
- PRAISE FOR THE PERSON — "you're so clever" hands over a label
  to protect; the hard task becomes its threat. Drained in the
  color by praise for strategy and persistence — repeatable on
  demand.
- COVERAGE OVER MASTERY — the syllabus that must be finished
  moves the class over gaps the chain arithmetic charges compound
  interest on. Honest condition: the teacher who stops to fix a
  gap falls behind for everybody — the failure belongs to a
  system that grades coverage, filed with it.
- TEACHING MACHINES, EACH GENERATION — film, radio, television,
  the computer lab, the recorded lecture library: each arrived
  claiming to replace the teacher, each settled into a means or a
  cupboard. The missing part every time was the loop — sensing
  THIS learner's error and changing the next thing said. Stands
  as the test any new machine must pass.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE PAPER PROXY FOR TEACHER QUALITY — fell short (no
  correlation past the early years); the want stands in THINGS.
- THE LECTURE HALL AS TRANSFER MACHINE — fell short by the
  receipts in CLAIMS; stands as orientation and reach.
- THE CLASS-SIZE PURCHASE — real, small, among the most expensive
  gains per spread-step a school can buy.
- THE RATINGS FORM — reads ease, and ease is the inverse of the
  mechanism: it measures the performance, not the keeping. Not
  yet found: a cheap in-course reading tracking what survives to
  the holidays.
- NOT YET FOUND: THE SCALABLE TUTOR LOOP — the mirror's largest
  open entry. The want: the two-sigma loop (error sensed in
  seconds, the next task chosen for THIS learner) at
  one-to-thirty prices. WHY it resists: the loop's power is
  sensing speed, and sensing has been priced at one adult's full
  attention per learner — the wage bill scales with heads.
  Candidate drains, each partial (~): peer and cross-age tutoring
  (~+0.3-0.4σ, cheap, amateur loop); high-dosage small-group
  tutoring in school time (~+0.3-0.5σ, the strongest replicated
  program result, still costly); mastery pacing (~+0.5-1σ
  claimed, loop weekly not instant); software tutors in narrow
  drillable domains (~+0.5σ where errors can be enumerated);
  machine tutors reading free work and choosing tasks (suspected,
  unproven — whether the sensing is real or a scripted guess is
  exactly the open question). Would drain on: any of these
  measuring ~+2σ in a disinterested trial at scale. The entry
  that would change schooling most, still white.
- NOT YET FOUND: A BULK INSTRUMENT FOR JUDGEMENT — facts and
  procedures mark cheaply and defensibly on appeal; judgement,
  taste, the framing of a question, the will to sit with a hard
  problem do not — so they vanish from the record first and the
  teaching soon after. Until this drains, the cheap-to-examine
  drives out the worth-learning, structurally.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- CLOSE THE GAP BUILT OUTSIDE — the difference grows fastest when
  school is closed, and schooling is judged on a total it only
  partly made. Teaching holds its corner (early interruption of
  compounding); the rest lives with economy, governance, and
  people — served, not answered, here.
- ONE PACE THAT FITS EVERYONE — CANNOT BE while one adult sets
  one speed for a spread (the color's arithmetic: ~38% in reach,
  ~31% bored, ~31% drowning). Every remedy is a payment; the full
  escape is the tutor-loop drain above.
- EXCELLENCE FROM ORDINARY WORK — work supplies none of
  deliberate practice's four parts; the want (a working life that
  trains as it pays) stands open across teaching, economy, and
  craft.
- PROOF AT THE SPEED OF THE SCHOOL YEAR — the cohort gets its
  year once, cannot consent, cannot be refunded; the trade cannot
  ethically try-and-see at will, so it learns slower than its
  questions arrive. An open tension, filed with ethics.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the
story.

- The styles industry: a generation of teacher hours and
  laminated inventories spent on a matching effect never measured
  to exist — a field adopting a flattering claim ahead of its
  receipt, and the reason the white now demands the crossover
  before belief.
- The pendulum's century: tell-them-everything and
  tell-them-nothing each held power for decades and each failed a
  different half of the bench law; the color's settlement (brief
  attempt, then telling; examples faded on schedule) was paid for
  by both swings.
- The teaching machines' parade: each new medium arrived with the
  same claim and retired into the same cupboard, having shipped
  the telling without the loop. Filed so the next arrival is
  asked first: where is the sensing?
- The two-sigma chase: published as a problem, pursued for
  decades, still open — the trying produced the partial drains in
  MEANS and the shrinkage rule in SCIENCE. A success that opened
  a larger question than it closed.
- Pressure's long reign: the cane, the dunce cap, the ranking
  board — centuries of compliance mistaken for learning, ended
  (where it ended) by the motivation product's arithmetic
  becoming undeniable in the wreckage.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, THEN REBUILT HONESTLY: TEACHING ↔ MIND — once ran on
  folk psychology (styles, brain myths) and broke on measurement;
  it now carries a speed limit (adopt at the pace the mind phylum
  certifies), which is itself knowledge about the relation.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: TEACHING ↔ COMPUTING — the machine tutor
  as the two-sigma drain: the loop closes in seconds and scales
  for free IF the sensing is real. Whether it reads this
  learner's actual error or pattern-matches a script is unproven
  either way; dotted until a disinterested trial at scale moves
  it.
- SUSPECTED, PARTLY GRIPPED: TEACHING ↔ BODY — sleep's filing is
  measured, its mechanism half-held; which sleep, for which
  material, how soon after study — open on the body's side.
- STANDING TENSION, FILED: TEACHING ↔ MEASUREMENT — the exam
  makes strangers comparable AND fixes what may be taught AND
  crowds out the uncountable; load-bearing and wounded at once.
  The bulk-judgement instrument in MEANS would heal it.
- STANDING TENSION, FILED: TEACHING ↔ ETHICS — the one-shot
  cohort forbids the ordinary experimental road; every method in
  the color was proven slower and later than it would have been
  on consenting adults, and the white is larger for it.
