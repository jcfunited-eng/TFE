# THE MIND — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/mind/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and conditions,
so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition. Nothing here is a
tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting for its drain. Seed
authored by Claude from the same named source files.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- PERFECT MEMORY — the oldest want in the phylum: keep everything,
  lose nothing. Near-exists, and the receipts are a curse: the
  famous mnemonist who could not forget found every page of his life
  crowding every present moment and could barely hold a gist or
  recognise a face — the details never stepped aside; the woman who
  recalls each day of her life describes it as a burden running
  unasked. The color's law says why: forgetting is the sweeping that
  keeps the useful reachable, and a store searched past everything
  finds nothing. The want CANNOT BE granted inside the head without
  paying that bill. Drained PARTIALLY, outside the head: writing and
  the machine store keep everything and let the head keep the gist —
  the shimmer's answer so far. Would drain further on: an external
  store consulted as fast and as cueably as the inner one.
- TELEPATHY — mind read directly, no behaviour between. Tried for
  over a century: card-guessing runs, sender-receiver rooms, whole
  institutes. The record is one shape throughout — effects near
  chance that shrink toward zero as controls tighten, and no result
  that replicates on demand. And the physics closes the pure form:
  the head's fields measure millionths of a volt at the scalp and
  fall away within centimetres; there is no carrier for the claim to
  ride. CANNOT BE under the condition "unaided across a room."
  Drained twice at the edges, exactly as the shimmer predicts:
  language IS working telepathy by other means, and electrodes laid
  on or in the head now move cursors and speech for the paralysed —
  add a physical channel and the impossibility recomputes. The
  unaided form stands closed, indexed by its conditions.
- A LIE DETECTOR — wanted by every court. The polygraph measures
  arousal, and arousal is not lying: the calm liar passes, the
  frightened truth-teller fails, and the base-rate arithmetic (the
  color holds it) drowns the rare liar in false alarms. Brain-scan
  versions inherit the same fault at higher cost — the scan sees
  blood, in cubes of millimetres and seconds. No instrument reads a
  lie; instruments read bodies. Would drain on: a measured signal
  that tracks the lie itself and not the fear of telling it —
  nothing certain, and no candidate currently holds.
- A COPY OF A MIND — the upload want. CANNOT BE under present
  conditions, and the reason is structural, not technical courage:
  in this machine the wiring is the memory and running is changing
  it — no separation of program from machine, nothing to read off
  without the reading being a rebuild, no pause. Recomputes honestly
  on its stated condition: a substrate whose knowledge sat in
  readable, writable structure would be copyable — that substrate is
  not the head (outward pointer past the phylum set: not yet built).
- A THERMOMETER FOR EXPERIENCE — pain, mood, effort, tiredness: all
  read by self-report, and the self doing the reporting is the
  impaired instrument (the short-slept insist they are fine and test
  otherwise). The want: a reading of experience that does not pass
  through the experiencer. Not yet found; the trackers measure
  bodies and guess at the rest. Would drain on: any measured
  quantity shown to track a felt intensity across people without
  asking them.
- A PILL FOR UNDERSTANDING — wanted at every exam and every grief.
  Every drug in the color turns a dial the body already has; none
  installs structure. Understanding is holding the machine that
  makes answers, and the machine is built only by the slow physical
  strengthening of junctions under practice and sleep. CANNOT BE by
  dial-turning; would drain only on a means of writing structure
  directly — none is written.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "MEMORY IS A RECORDING" — held for centuries, paid for in
  convictions: confident, sincere, detailed eyewitnesses were wrong
  in court after court. Measured false: recall is rebuilding, each
  visit a re-write, the version saved dated at its last handling.
  The claim's replacement now runs the courtroom warning and the
  trauma treatment both.
- "WE USE 10% OF THE BRAIN" — false at every level measured: the
  scan finds no idle nine-tenths, damage nowhere is free, and a
  19-watt organ billing a fifth of the body's energy is not keeping
  90% of itself dark. WHY believed: the wish for headroom, and the
  honest fact that most cells are support crew was misread as spare
  capacity.
- "LEFT-BRAINED PEOPLE, RIGHT-BRAINED PEOPLE" — the real narrow
  asymmetry (language machinery usually left; whole-scene attention
  and tone usually right) was inflated into a theory of personality
  that imaging flatly contradicts: both sides equally busy in
  everybody, on everything.
- "THE BLANK SLATE" — didn't hold: settings visible in the first
  weeks, fears prepared ahead of any experience, equipment arriving
  on a build order no upbringing chooses. What survives of it is
  real and smaller: the content of categories is learnt, the
  machinery is not.
- "A CHEMICAL IMBALANCE" — no mental illness is a shortage of one
  chemical; the same transmitter excites here, inhibits there, and
  sets gain elsewhere, so raising it everywhere cannot correct
  anything specifically. The story survived because it is easy to
  tell and lifts blame — the drugs sometimes working never proved
  it, any more than aspirin proves a headache is aspirin deficiency.
  Would drain on: a measured shortage that tracks one illness and
  whose correction alone cures it — decades of looking, none found.
- "WILLPOWER IS A FUEL TANK" — one of the field's most repeated
  findings (choices draining a reservoir spent by earlier choices)
  largely failed its large pre-registered retests. Something real
  may remain — attention and motivation do shift with fatigue — but
  plans that budget willpower are built on sand; the reliable levers
  measured are structural (cue, friction, arrangement). UNSURE at
  the remainder's size.
- "21 DAYS TO A HABIT" — folklore traced to a surgeon's remark about
  amputees adjusting; measured formation runs ~18 to ~254 days,
  median ~66. Filed because plans built on the folk number abandon
  themselves at week four as failures that were on schedule.
- "LEARNING STYLES" — the claim that matching teaching to a
  declared style (visual, auditory...) improves learning fails its
  controlled tests repeatedly. What survives: content has a best
  modality; learners have preferences; preferences do not predict
  gains. Would drain on: matched-versus-mismatched teaching showing
  a gain in a controlled test — the design is simple, the result has
  been sought for decades and not found.
- "BRAIN TRAINING" — games improve the game. The color's welded-to-
  context law forbids the transfer the adverts promise, and the
  measured record obeys the law: gains on the trained task, nothing
  on the life. Would drain on: transfer measured on tasks sharing no
  surface with the training — repeatedly sought, not found.
- "SUBLIMINAL PERSUASION SELLS" — the famous cinema experiment (buy
  popcorn, flashed unseen) was admitted a fabrication; measured
  subliminal effects are real, tiny, and gone in moments. The strong
  claim didn't hold; the fear of it outlived the evidence.
- "THE FULL MOON MADDENS" — counted against admissions, calls, and
  crimes: no signal. Believed by the availability law — the wild
  night under a full moon is filed, the wild night without one is
  not. Would drain on: the signal appearing in an honest count —
  counted many times, never there.
- "TRUTH SERUM" — the drugs loosen talk, not truth: suggestibility
  rises with the flow, and the flow includes confabulation. Fails
  exactly where it was wanted. Would drain on: a drug measured to
  raise accuracy, not just volume — none does; every candidate
  raises both the telling and the making-up together.
- "HYPNOSIS RECOVERS BURIED MEMORIES" — the catastrophe receipts:
  confident, vivid, detailed accounts assembled under suggestion,
  families and verdicts broken on them. The rebuilding law says why
  it MUST fail this use: the machinery that builds is the machinery
  that inspects, and hypnosis lowers the inspection. (Whether
  hypnosis is a distinct state at all is still argued; its measured
  usefulness against pain is real. UNSURE at the mechanism.)
- "SPEED READING" — past roughly doubling ordinary pace,
  comprehension collapses on test after test. The eye's thumbnail of
  sharpness and the fetch rate of meaning set the gate; the courses
  sell the feeling of speed, which is ease, which is not
  understanding. The certified condition is "same eyes, same page":
  what does honestly speed a reader is knowing the subject already —
  bigger grips, fewer fixations — which is expertise, not technique,
  and cannot be sold in a weekend.
- UNSETTLED: WHY PLACEBO WORKS — the verdict reaching the body is
  measured fact: pain falls, nausea falls, the tremor disease
  improves with the reward chemical's release observed; and the
  effect part-survives even told-it's-a-placebo. Not settled: the
  mechanism's shares. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility:
  expectation is prediction, and the color holds real machinery by
  which predictions run downward (the pain gate at the cord's door,
  the alarm's dials); a body told "help has arrived" may simply run
  those levers. Conditioning grips another share (the learnt bell),
  the relationship another. Would drain on: the shares measured
  separately with the downward pathways blocked one at a time — the
  pain-gate share already part-measured (blocking the body's own
  opiates blocks part of the relief). What placebo does NOT do is
  also part of the entry: it moves reports and dials, not tumours.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT DREAMS ARE FOR — a century of confident answers,
  none holding the whole. Rehearsing threats, filing emotion,
  keeping the visual system exercised, or nothing beyond the
  machinery running unfenced — each explains part. The observation
  base is itself compromised: the only witness reports after waking,
  through the rebuilding law. Would drain on: a manipulation of
  dream content with a measured next-day consequence that the rival
  accounts predict differently.
- UNSETTLED: ADULT NEW NEURONS — reported in one memory structure
  for twenty years, then contested by studies that could not find
  the cells in adult humans; the animal evidence strong, the human
  evidence hanging on chemical markers that may be shared. UNSURE,
  honestly.
- UNSETTLED: THE BINDING — colour, motion, shape, and sound of one
  object processed in different places at different speeds, arriving
  at no single destination, experienced as one thing. The leading
  suggestion — the cells of one object firing in step — is UNSURE.
  There is no place where it all comes together, so unity must be
  made some other way, and the way is not written.
- UNSETTLED: WHY SLEEP AT ALL, AT DEPTH — the filing is measured,
  the repair is measured, and neither is proven the whole rent for a
  state that leaves every animal defenceless a third of its life.
  Would drain on: a creature engineered around either function and
  its sleep bill recomputed.
- UNSETTLED: PSYCHOSIS OUTCOMES BETTER IN POORER COUNTRIES — found
  repeatedly, explained never. Possibility offered: the life waiting
  outside the illness (work, family, a place) is the treatment the
  richer setting withholds. Unproven.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING, THE LARGEST: WHAT CONSCIOUSNESS IS. The measured part is
  real and the honest edge is sharp. Known: awareness tracks
  widespread, sustained, back-and-forth traffic between regions, not
  strong local activity; an unconscious signal can travel far
  through the senses without producing it; the anaesthetics —
  chemically unlike one another — share the breaking of long-range
  exchange while the regions keep working. Candidates on the table:
  awareness as broadcast (a thing becomes conscious when made
  available to the whole workspace), awareness as integration (a
  quantity of how much a system's parts inform each other),
  awareness as the system modelling its own attending. Each grips
  part; none says why any of it is FELT — why there is something it
  is like to be the traffic. Filed by the answer rule: nothing is
  certain here — but here is a possibility: the feeling is what the
  integration is, from inside, the way heat is what the motion is;
  and what would settle any candidate is a theory that predicts
  presence or absence of experience in hard cases (anaesthesia
  depths, absence seizures, the split brain, a machine) and survives
  the checks. Until then the fabric answers questions ABOUT
  experience with the correlates and marks the why as open.
- LACKING: UNITS FOR THE FELT — no scale of pain, mood, or effort
  that crosses two people; every trial in the whole field leans on
  the impaired instrument's self-report. Would drain on the same
  condition as the thermometer in THINGS: a measured quantity shown
  to track a felt intensity across people without asking them — and
  the whole field's trials would need re-reading the day it landed.
- LACKING: WHY FOUR — the workbench's size is measured everywhere
  and explained nowhere. Candidates (interference between held
  items; rhythms nesting a few slots inside a slower wave) grip
  partly. A real answer would say what would make it five.
- LACKING: MECHANISMS UNDER THE MANUAL — the categories predict
  course and treatment (their earned place, the color holds it) and
  name no causes; the drugs ignoring the diagnoses is standing
  evidence the joints are drawn wrong. Would drain on: mechanisms
  found that regroup the symptoms — the redrawing already begun.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE LOBOTOMY — a mechanism-free cut sold as
  cure, honoured with the field's highest prize, paid for in
  thousands of flattened persons. Filed as the permanent warning:
  a treatment that quiets the ward is not a treatment that helped
  the patient, and confidence without mechanism cuts.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE SCAN'S CAPTIONS — the pictures honest, the
  captions not: cubes of millimetres and seconds averaging hundreds
  of thousands of cells were sold as thoughts located, love found,
  lies detected. The instrument's claims cannot exceed its
  resolution.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: RISK SCORES AS GATES — the base-rate
  arithmetic (color holds it worked: ~0.9% true among the flagged at
  1-in-1,000 rarity) was ignored in the field for decades of
  checklist confidence; people scored "low risk" died and "high
  risk" crowds mostly did not. The arithmetic was always available;
  the want overrode it.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
wings-on-arms entries, waiting for their planet.

- RAISING SELF-ESTEEM DIRECTLY — programs of praise and affirmation:
  the readout was mistaken for the cause. Moves almost nothing;
  doing a hard thing and succeeding moves it as a side effect. The
  entry stands as the general warning: attempts to change a feeling
  first are working from the wrong end.
- INSIGHT AS CURE — tracing the habit to its childhood origin and
  expecting the trace to stop it. Fails because the origin is not
  what runs the loop now; the cycle is cut at the behaviour or not
  at all. Insight remains worth having — as a map, not a mechanism.
- PUNISHMENT AS TEACHER — suppresses in the punisher's sight,
  teaches concealment everywhere else, names no alternative.
  Conditions where a consequence does teach: immediate, certain,
  attached to the act not the catching — and even then it only says
  stop, never what instead.
- SCARED STRAIGHT — fear-tours for the young measured to INCREASE
  later offending. The arousal was mistaken for the lesson; what was
  learnt was the visit.
- DEBRIEFING FORCED RIGHT AFTER HORROR — sitting the survivors down
  to relive it at once, measured to worsen outcomes for some. The
  rebuilding law says why: the memory re-written while the alarm
  still runs is filed hotter. Drained to: watchful support, and
  going back through it later, under safety, at the person's pace.
- THOUGHT SUPPRESSION — the order not to think of it is an
  instruction to keep checking, and the check is the thought. Fails
  by its own mechanics; drained to naming, writing out, and letting
  the thought stand unanswered.
- SLEEP HYGIENE ALONE FOR CHRONIC INSOMNIA — necessary, nowhere near
  sufficient: it fixes the sleep of those whose sleep was being
  spoilt by those things, and does close to nothing for the
  maintained loop (bed taught to mean waking), which needs the
  restriction-and-retraining method the color holds.
- TRYING HARDER AT SLEEP — the one task effort worsens, by
  mechanism: trying is arousal, arousal forbids it. Every working
  method removes the attempt.
- SLEEPING PILLS AS A LIFE PLAN — under half an hour bought,
  tolerance built, and an exit worse than the entrance; the years on
  them are the receipt.
- WILLPOWER-BUDGET PLANS — see the fuel-tank claim above; the plans
  fail where the finding failed. Drained to arrangement.
- TELLING FACTS TO CHANGE BEHAVIOUR — the information-deficit
  method: campaigns that changed attitudes and not behaviour, on the
  measured gap between intending and doing. Facts move the minority
  whose block was a missing fact. Drained to: cues, defaults,
  friction, and the true-norm figure (the one fact that does move
  behaviour, because it repoints the room).
- THE GROUP BRAINSTORM AS IDEA ENGINE — measured repeatedly: the
  same people working alone then pooling produce more and better
  than the interacting group, where the loudest anchor, the rest
  converge, and the beam queues. Conditions matter: groups WIN at
  judging and combining. The method stands for sifting, fails for
  making.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE POLYGRAPH — fell short as stated in THINGS: measures the
  body's weather, not the lie. Kept in use in places anyway; the
  white holds why the courts mostly refuse it.
- THE SCAN AS MIND-READER — fell short at its own resolution;
  honest as a comparison of blood between tasks, dishonest as a
  window on thought.
- THE WEARABLE AS SLEEP ORACLE — counts movement and heartbeat,
  guesses stages poorly, and its nightly score can itself maintain
  insomnia (the worry it feeds is the arousal that forbids). Fell
  short as instrument; stands as a rough log.
- TRUTH DRUGS — loosen the tongue and the confabulation together;
  fell short for the reason the CLAIMS entry holds, and drains on
  the same condition (accuracy raised, not volume — none does).
- THE MANUAL AS MECHANISM MAP — earns its keep as a dictionary of
  shapes; falls short whenever read as a map of causes. The color
  files its earned place; this side files the misuse.
- NOT YET FOUND: THE IMPAIRMENT GAUGE A TIRED SELF BELIEVES — the
  drink-drive line has a roadside instrument; seventeen-hours-awake,
  measured equal, has none. Would drain on: a bedside or roadside
  reading of readiness that outranks the impaired self-report — the
  want is written in the crash statistics.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- TELL WHICH FLAGGED PERSON IS THE ONE — the base-rate arithmetic
  forbids it at every accuracy yet written; the answered form is
  population-shaped (move the barrier, shrink the packet), and the
  individual form stands open.
- PREDICT THE COURSE FROM THE FIRST EPISODE — courses vary
  enormously and nothing at presentation sorts them; confident early
  prognosis is invention. Would drain on: a marker at first
  presentation that splits the one-episode lives from the recurring
  ones.
- HAND OVER UNDERSTANDING BY TELLING — the click can be
  manufactured by any good explainer; the machine must still be
  built in each head by its own practice, error, and sleep. Teaching
  narrows the gap and cannot close it; the want (installation) may
  be unanswerable in principle, and the phylum keeps it filed rather
  than certified, awaiting a means of writing structure.
- READ A MIND WITHOUT BEHAVIOUR — closed under stated conditions
  (see TELEPATHY); the open remainder is engineering's, not this
  phylum's.
- END SUFFERING WITHOUT A BILL — every dial turned is priced
  somewhere (the flatness, the numbness, the exit rebound); the
  want of a free lever stands open across the whole pharmacy. Would
  drain on: a lever that moves one dial and provably leaves the
  rest untouched — the WHY it hasn't: the transmitters each serve
  many circuits, so a chemical turned up anywhere is turned up
  everywhere it works.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- The asylum ledger: centuries of chains, bleedings, and spinning
  chairs, each certain in its day — then the emptying, promised a
  community care that in large part never came, so the failure
  moved to the street and the cell rather than ending. Both halves
  filed: the cruelty of confident treatment without mechanism, and
  the cruelty of withdrawal without replacement.
- The lobotomy's decade: the field's sharpest lesson that a
  quietened ward is not a healed person, bought at thousands of
  persons flattened and one prize that history reads as a wound.
- The eyewitness convictions: the recording theory of memory died
  in courtrooms, exoneration by exoneration, before the textbooks
  caught up — the innocent served the years that the theory's
  confidence cost.
- The hypnotic-recovery era: families and verdicts broken on
  memories assembled under suggestion — the rebuilding law's price
  paid in public, and the reason the color's trauma method insists
  on safety and pace.
- The replication reckoning: the field turned its own instruments
  on itself and watched celebrated findings shrink or vanish under
  large pre-registered retests. Filed as a success of method paid
  for in humility: the surviving claims are the ones this phylum
  lets bear load.
- Phrenology's skulls: character read from head-bumps, measured
  with calipers and total confidence — wrong, and half-drained in
  the strangest way: localisation itself was real (the maps, the
  hubs), only the reading of it from outside was empty. The first
  overclaim of the physical mind, ancestor of the scan's captions.
- A century of dream keys: symbol dictionaries sold as science,
  the same book giving one meaning to every dreamer. No receipts
  ever; the honest state of dreams is filed above as unsettled.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, REBUILT WITH A SPEED LIMIT: MIND ↔ MEASUREMENT — the
  replication break taught the relation its own law: adopt findings
  at the pace they stop reversing, weight the large and
  pre-registered over the striking, and treat one study as one
  witness. The limit is itself knowledge, filed here.
- BROKEN, REBUILT: MIND ↔ LAW ON THE EYEWITNESS — confidence was
  admitted as proof and convicted the innocent; the rebuilt relation
  carries corroboration rules and the warning in the jury
  instruction.
- FORBIDDEN AS IDENTITY: MIND ↔ COMPUTING — the analogy kept as a
  tool (index, cache, compression travel well) broke as an identity
  claim: no program apart from the machine, no copy, no pause.
  Every upload want and every "just software" argument runs into
  this closed entry; it recomputes only with the substrate condition
  stated in THINGS.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: GUT ↔ MOOD — the traffic runs overwhelmingly
  upward and the correlations are real; the causal claims sold on
  them outrun the mechanism by years. Filed dotted, awaiting the
  intervention studies that move mood by moving the gut alone.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: THE NIGHT'S REPLAY ↔ THE DREAM FELT — the
  filing is measured; whether the dream is that filing experienced
  from inside is a guess. Dotted. Would drain on: a day's material
  cued during sleep (a sound, a smell tied to the learning) showing
  up in the woken dreamer's report above chance, with the next-day
  memory moved to match — the pieces of that test exist separately;
  nobody has yet closed the loop.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: CULTURE ↔ RECOVERY — the poorer-country
  psychosis outcomes stand unexplained (see CLAIMS); if the life
  waiting outside the illness is the active part, the relation is a
  treatment nobody is prescribing. Would drain on: outcome measured
  against the waiting life directly, anywhere.
