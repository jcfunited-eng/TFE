# COOKING — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/cooking/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting
for its drain. Seed authored by Claude and marked as seed.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- A FAT THAT ACTS LIKE BUTTER WITHOUT ITS COSTS — attempted:
  hardened plant oils (margarine). The attempt WORKED as texture
  and FAILED as health: the hardening process built trans shapes
  the body handles worse than what was replaced. Filed with its
  why: the problem was the hardening chemistry, not the want.
  Would drain on: a hardening path that builds no trans shapes
  (partially arrived — the want stands half-drained and open).
- SWEETNESS WITHOUT SUGAR'S BODY — attempted many times (the
  intense sweeteners). Sweetness: delivered. FAILED as
  replacement: sugar in baking is also bulk, browning fuel,
  moisture-keeper, and preservative — the substitutes carry none
  of those four jobs. Would drain on: a substance doing all five
  jobs at once; none is written.
- THE PERFECT ALL-PURPOSE PAN — wanted forever: instant answer AND
  steady mass. CANNOT BE in one plain metal: conduction speed and
  heat-holding mass oppose each other in the same material (the
  numbers are in the color's MEANS). Drained PARTIALLY by
  lamination (copper or aluminum clad in steel) — the compromise
  is the answer so far; a true single material is still white.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "SEARING SEALS IN THE JUICES" — held for a century, measured
  false: a seared crust is porous, not a barrier; seared meat
  loses as much moisture as unseared or more. WHY it was believed:
  the crust's flavor reads as richness, and richness reads as
  juiciness. What actually holds moved to the color: sear for
  browning's flavor, keep juice by the ladder (never pass actin's
  squeeze) and the rest.
- "ALCOHOL ALL COOKS OFF" — false by measurement: ~35% remains
  after 30 minutes of simmering, ~5% after 2.5 hours. The claim
  fails exactly where it matters (feeding those who must not have
  it).
- "SALT MAKES THE WATER BOIL FASTER / HOTTER ENOUGH TO MATTER" —
  false at kitchen doses: ~10 g per liter raises the ceiling
  ~0.17°C. Salt the pasta water for seasoning; the physics claim
  is noise at the tongue's dose.
- "MICROWAVES COOK FROM THE INSIDE OUT" — false: they reach ~1-2 cm
  and cook outside-in like everything else, just starting below
  the skin. The believed version misread the burn pattern.
- "WASHING RAW POULTRY CLEANS IT" — false and backwards: the water
  aerosols the bacteria onto sink and counters; heat is the only
  instrument that finishes the claim. Would never drain: this one
  is a cannot under all kitchen conditions.
- "MSG SICKNESS" — the claimed effect fails blinded tests at meal
  doses; filed as a claim that didn't hold, kept because queries
  still arrive carrying it.
- UNSETTLED: WHY THE MPEMBA ODDITY (hot water sometimes freezing
  faster than cold) HAPPENS — the observation recurs; the
  mechanism is still contested (evaporation's head start,
  dissolved gas, convection's currents, container contact — each
  grips partly). Nothing certain — but here is a possibility:
  several small effects stack, and which wins depends on the
  container and the freezer, which is why the experiment refuses
  to settle. Would drain on: a controlled decomposition that
  predicts WHEN it happens, not just that it can.
- UNSETTLED: WHY FLAVORS PAIR — why some combinations please
  across cultures and others repel. Shared aroma molecules grip
  part of it; culture grips another; no written law predicts a
  new pairing's success. Would drain on: a tested predictor
  beating chance on pairs never tried.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A PREDICTIVE THEORY OF TASTE — the color can build
  flavor (browning, ferment, balance) but cannot COMPUTE a new
  dish's taste before it exists; the five channels plus aroma are
  mapped as parts, not as a working sum. This is the subject's
  largest named absence — the hands can assemble a recipe, and
  only a mouth can yet judge it.
- LACKING: THE FULL CHEMISTRY OF LONG FERMENTATION'S AROMA —
  hundreds of compounds identified, no written account composes
  them into "why sourdough tastes like that"; the lore (cold, slow,
  old starter) outruns the why, as it did for millennia.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: EARLY NUTRITION SCIENCE IN THE KITCHEN — the
  fat-versus-sugar reversals whipsawed cooking advice for decades;
  the lesson filed: population correlations transfer poorly to the
  single pot, and cooking should adopt nutritional claims at the
  speed they stop reversing, not at the speed they publish.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
wings-on-arms entries, waiting for their planet.

- BROWNING IN THE MICROWAVE — impossible UNDER THE CONDITION of
  dielectric heating alone: the energy goes to water, water pins
  the surface near 100°C wet, browning needs ~140°C dry.
  DRAINED PARTIALLY, exactly as the shimmer predicts: add a
  different condition (a susceptor surface that itself grows hot,
  or a joint convection element) and crust returns. The pure-form
  entry stands; the combined-form drain is written.
- FRYING IN WATER — the want: fry's crust without fat. CANNOT BE
  at any safe kitchen pressure: browning needs ~140°C, water's
  ceiling is 100 at one atmosphere and ~121 at a pressure
  cooker's; the gap does not close below pressures no kitchen
  vessel should hold. Recomputes honestly: on some world (or
  vessel) where water stays liquid past 140, the entry drains —
  the wings-on-arms shape, filed with its numbers.
- SEALING LOW-ACID FOOD BY BOILING ALONE — the deadly try: 100°C
  kills the living spoilers but not botulism's spores, which wake
  in the sealed, airless, low-acid dark. Paid for in deaths
  (history's ink). Drained by TWO different roads, both written in
  the color: pressure's 121°C, or acid's pH 4.6 — and the entry
  stands as the reason the two roads are law.
- COOKING BY LORE AT ALTITUDE — sea-level recipes carried up the
  mountain fail on schedule: the ceiling fell ~1°C per 300 m and
  the recipe's clock silently assumed 100. Not a cannot — a
  conditions entry: every wet-method time is a claim ABOUT its
  altitude. Filed because it is the cleanest kitchen proof that a
  method's conditions are part of the method.
- THAWING ON THE COUNTER — tried daily for a century: the outside
  spends hours crossing the danger zone while the center thaws.
  The arithmetic (doubling every ~20 minutes in the warm heart)
  damns it. Drains only to the cold thaw, the cold-water thaw, or
  cooking from frozen — written in the color's safety lines.
- ONE-TEMPERATURE COOKING OF THICK CUTS — fierce heat only, the
  grill's oldest error: the square law and the ladder together
  forbid it (surface passes burning while the center is raw).
  Filed as the mother of the two-zone method — an entry whose
  failure BUILT the method that superseded it.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE SOLAR OVEN, WHERE THE SUN ISN'T — works exactly when and
  where the sky allows; fell short as a general mean, stands as a
  conditional one. Its condition is the weather phylum's to state.
- THE CUP AS A MEASURE OF FLOUR — fell short by up to 30% on
  packing alone; superseded by the scale and kept only where the
  30% cannot hurt. Filed because instrument error this size
  explains a whole class of "the recipe failed" queries.
- NOT YET FOUND: A HOME INSTRUMENT FOR DONENESS WITHOUT PIERCING —
  the thermometer answers by wounding; the want (know the center,
  touch nothing) stands open. Would drain on: a means reading
  internal temperature through intact food at kitchen prices.
- NOT YET FOUND: PRESERVATION WITH ZERO QUALITY COST — every
  written keeper (cold, dry, salt, acid, heat, sugar) trades
  texture or flavor for time; the freezer's ice spears and the
  can's softness are the standing receipts. The want is ancient,
  open, and honestly stated: time still charges for keeping.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- PREDICT A NEW DISH BEFORE IT IS COOKED — compute taste from a
  recipe as physics computes a trajectory. Blocked on the SCIENCE
  absence above (no predictive theory of taste).
- FEED EVERYONE WELL FROM WHAT EACH PLACE GROWS — the thrift
  cookbook scaled to the world; cooking holds its corner (waste
  nothing, stretch, preserve) and the rest lives with farming,
  economy, and weather — a want this phylum can only serve, not
  answer.
- MAKE THE SAFE CHOICE VISIBLE WITHOUT KNOWLEDGE — the safety
  arithmetic protects only those who hold it; the want (a kitchen
  where the danger zone defends itself) is a means-and-engineering
  question still open.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- Preservation's uncounted dead: every keeping method in the color
  was found by populations that buried the failed tries; the
  methods carry no inventor's name because the cost was spread
  across everyone. The color's HISTORY says "found by survivors";
  this page is where the non-survivors are acknowledged as the
  method's true price.
- Canning's ledger, the cleanest written case: sealed food fed
  armies and killed families for a century until the spore and the
  pH line were found. The trying was long, the why arrived late,
  and the law (4.6, or 121°C) is the whole record compressed to
  two numbers.
- Margarine's century: the fix that became the harm that became
  the warning — the fullest kitchen example of a solution filed to
  the color and later partly demoted, exactly the traffic the
  model says must stay possible.
- Lore's long reign and its price: until the thermometer, doneness
  knowledge lived in thumbs and proverbs — it worked, it traveled
  poorly, and it died with its holders. The instrument didn't just
  improve the answer; it made the knowledge FILEABLE. That lesson
  reaches past cooking: what cannot be written into a claim
  cannot be inherited.
