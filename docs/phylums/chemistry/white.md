# CHEMISTRY — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/chemistry/color.md: same parts,
opposite sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting
for its drain. Seed authored by Claude and marked as seed.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- GOLD FROM LEAD BY CHEMICAL MEANS — the philosopher's stone,
  chased for two thousand years. CERTIFIED CANNOT-BE under its
  condition: element identity is the proton count in the nucleus,
  and chemistry's entire reach is the outer electrons — bond
  energies run a few hundred kJ/mol where nuclear changes cost
  roughly a MILLION times more per particle. No furnace, acid, or
  reagent touches the nucleus; the wall is physics's, reaching in.
  THE SHIMMER, exactly as the model says: change the condition
  (nuclear means, not chemical) and transmutation is REAL —
  reactors breed one element from another daily, and mercury has
  been beaten into gold in accelerators at a cost vastly above
  gold's price. The want drained on the other road; the chemical
  door stays certified shut. Wings-on-arms, textbook form.
- THE UNIVERSAL SOLVENT (the alkahest) — the want: one liquid
  that dissolves everything. CANNOT BE as stated, by its own
  logic: what dissolved everything would dissolve its vessel —
  the want contradicts the holding of it. Water took the nickname
  by dissolving MORE than anything else, never all. Drained
  partially: pressurized CO₂ past its critical point, designer
  liquid salts, and the mediator trick widen the reachable list
  year by year. The honest surviving want is always "a solvent
  for THIS stubborn thing," and that one drains piecewise.
- PHLOGISTON — the substance of fire, believed a century: burning
  things were said to release it. KILLED BY THE SCALE: burned
  metal WEIGHS MORE (the color's worked rust case — 224 g of iron
  becomes 320 g of rust), so the escaping substance needed
  negative weight. What the idea gripped was real — something IS
  transferred in burning — but the direction was backwards:
  oxygen comes IN. Filed as the model case of a theory organizing
  true facts and dying anyway, by one number it could not survive.
- A ROOM-TEMPERATURE, ROOM-PRESSURE SUPERCONDUCTOR — open, and
  loud. Best confirmed at ordinary pressure: copper-oxide
  ceramics near −135°C. Hydrogen-rich compounds reach near
  freezer temperatures but only around a million-plus
  atmospheres. Ambient claims (one famous recent one) failed
  replication — filed as tries, with the replication record as
  the why. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: a lattice
  whose own chemistry squeezes hydrogen as pressure does, the
  squeeze built in rather than applied. Would drain on: one
  material, ambient conditions, replicated in unfriendly labs.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "A PROTEIN'S FOLD CANNOT BE COMPUTED FROM ITS SEQUENCE" — the
  shimmer example, PARTLY DRAINED within living memory. The wall
  was arithmetic: a chain of 100 links with ~3 postures each has
  3¹⁰⁰ ≈ 5 × 10⁴⁷ shapes; trying a billion per second takes
  ~5 × 10³⁸ seconds against a universe ~4 × 10¹⁷ seconds old —
  a thousand billion billion universe-ages. Yet real proteins
  fold in milliseconds, so they were never searching — the
  landscape funnels. Learned systems now predict most
  single-chain folds near experimental accuracy: that half
  DRAINED. Still white: the WHY (the predictor answers without a
  law), the folding pathway and its speed, misfolding's diseases,
  and function designed from scratch. A named unknown became
  partly known — standing proof this sheet drains.
- "WATER REMEMBERS WHAT WAS DISSOLVED IN IT" (homeopathy's
  premise) — killed by the color's own dilution arithmetic: the
  standard 30C preparation is a 10⁶⁰-fold dilution, and one mole
  is only 6 × 10²³ molecules — past a 10²⁴-fold dilution the
  expected count of original molecules is below ONE. The claimed
  effects also fail blinded trials at meal-and-dose scale. Would
  drain on: a replicated blinded effect; none is written.
- "FUSION IN A JAR ON A BENCHTOP" — the claimed excess heat
  failed replication under controls, and the quantitative why is
  the missing radiation: fusion at the claimed wattage emits a
  neutron flux that would have injured the experimenters, and no
  such flux was measured. CONDITIONS on the certificate: this
  closes the claimed result, not the territory — muon-catalyzed
  fusion is real (and costs more than it pays), and dense-lattice
  hydrogen work continues as an unknown, not a known.
- UNSETTLED: WHY LIFE IS ONE-HANDED — all life's amino acids
  left-handed, its sugars right-handed, and the color can only
  report it, not derive it. Candidate grips: a chance tie broken
  once and compounded forever; polarized starlight biasing the
  first stock a few percent (works in the lab, unproven as
  history); handed mineral faces as the first workbenches.
  Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: a small physical
  bias plus self-amplifying copying needs no large cause. Would
  drain on: the bias caught in the wild, or a second genesis
  found running on the other hand.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT GLASS IS — solid or stopped liquid; the glass
  transition has no clean law, and the color's melting-point
  machinery does not grip it. Filed because the commonest made
  material lacks a settled account of its own state. Nothing
  certain — but here is a possibility: a liquid that lost the
  race — cooled faster than its molecules could line up into a
  lattice, jammed mid-crowd, so the transition is a traffic
  event, not a phase change, and its temperature moves with the
  cooling rate (which it measurably does). Would drain on: one
  law giving the transition temperature from composition and
  cooling rate, holding across glass families.
- UNSETTLED: WATER'S FINE STRUCTURE — how the hydrogen-bond
  network is actually arranged instant to instant (one liquid or
  two interleaved?) is still argued; the color's water oddities
  stand on measurement, not on a settled picture beneath them.
  Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: two local
  arrangements — one roomy and ice-like, one packed — trading
  places every few trillionths of a second, their shifting ratio
  the source of the oddities. Would drain on: a probe fast
  enough to catch the network mid-shuffle and count the two
  arrangements, deep in supercooled territory where they should
  part company most.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: THE CATALYST COMPUTED, NOT FOUND — the color's
  catalysts were discovered by search and luck; no written law
  yet takes a wanted reaction and returns the catalyst that
  lowers its wall. This is the subject's most expensive absence:
  every entry in the color that says "over an iron catalyst"
  stands on a find, not a derivation. The bean root's bacteria
  fix nitrogen at soil temperature with an enzyme nobody can
  copy, while industry pays ~450°C and hundreds of atmospheres
  for the same bond — the gap between those two numbers is the
  absence, priced. Would drain on: one catalyst derived on paper
  from the wanted reaction, then working at the bench.
- LACKING: PROPERTIES FROM FORMULA — melting point, solubility,
  smell of a never-made molecule still resist honest computation
  from first principles; chemistry measures more than it
  derives. (The fold-prediction drain above shows the learned
  path can leap where the law path crawls — and delivers no law.)
  Would drain on: a property computed before synthesis and
  confirmed by the made thing, from a written derivation.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE BOND-ENERGY TABLE'S PRECISION — the
  color's ledger computes methane's burn at 802 kJ/mol where
  measurement says ~890: averages hold to ~10%, and any claim
  tighter than that from bond tables is borrowed precision.
  Filed so the tool is used at its true grade.
- LACKING: THE ROAD FROM MOLECULES TO A SELF-COPIER — sparked
  gas yields amino acids, minerals template interesting strings,
  and no written path runs end to end from small molecules to a
  thing that copies itself. The color's LIFE relation stands on
  the far side of this gap. Would drain on: one unbroken
  demonstrated path, however slow and however unlike the
  historical one — a second way in would drain it as surely as
  the first.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
wings-on-arms entries, waiting for their planet.

- TRANSMUTATION BY FIRE, ACID, AND PATIENCE — alchemy's whole
  method book, run for centuries by its best minds. Certified
  cannot under chemical means (THINGS holds the wall and its
  numbers) — and the tries built distillation, the mineral
  acids, the balance habit, and the written procedure: the
  failed method's TOOLS became the science's means. The entry
  stands as the fabric's cleanest case of a dead goal leaving
  live equipment.
- SCALING A REACTION BY MULTIPLYING EVERYTHING — fails on the
  cube-square law: heat is made throughout the volume and shed
  only through the surface, so the same recipe that simmered in
  a flask runs away in a drum. Not a cannot — a conditions
  entry: every procedure is a claim ABOUT its vessel size, and
  the missing line in the scaled-up recipe was the cooling. The
  industry's answer (pilot plants, staged scale-up) is this
  failure made into a method.
- ADOPTING A SUBSTANCE FOR THE PROPERTY IN HAND — the repeating
  pattern, paid for at least four times: lead for its
  workability (billed by the nerves), radium for its glow
  (billed by the jaw of the dial-painters), the refrigerant
  gases for their very inertness (billed by the ozone they
  outlived their way up to), the miracle insecticide for its
  persistence (billed by the eggshells downstream). The method
  failure named plainly: "harmless here" was read as "harmless
  everywhere," and inertness itself can be the harm because
  nothing stops what nothing reacts with. Would drain on: a
  screen that tests a candidate against the places it will
  TRAVEL, not just the place it will work — partly built
  (persistence and accumulation testing are now law in
  medicine and farming), never finished.
- MAKING DIAMOND AT THE BENCH — wanted from the moment carbon's
  identity with graphite was proven; every bench-condition try
  failed for a century because the lattice needs either
  ~50,000+ atmospheres of squeeze (the deep earth's recipe,
  finally matched by press and furnace) or a trick — and the
  trick was found: a thin plasma persuading carbon onto a seed
  crystal at LOW pressure, atom by atom. DRAINED TWICE by two
  different doors, both now in industry. Kept here as the shape
  of hope for the superconductor entry above: a certified "needs
  brutal conditions" can fall to a second door nobody had named.
- ASSAYING BY EYE AND FLAME ALONE — the old proof of purity by
  color, taste, and flame fell to instruments for a reason
  written in bodies: arsenic's several disguises defeated every
  eye until wet chemistry and the spectroscope made the assay a
  measurement. Superseded and KEPT (the flame test still opens
  the color's reading-by-light entry) — the surpassed method
  stays because to a field query with no instrument it is still
  the best answer.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- NOT YET FOUND: A BATTERY WITH FUEL'S DENSITY — gasoline banks
  ~46 MJ/kg; the best shipping cells bank ~1. The 40-50× gap is
  the wall in front of electric flight and long haulage, and it
  is chemistry's to close: more electron transfer per kilogram
  of carried mass. The open possibility: chemistries that
  breathe — taking one reactant from the air as fuel does,
  instead of carrying both sides of the reaction aboard.
- NOT YET FOUND: NITROGEN FIXED AT ROOM CONDITIONS — the enzyme
  in the bean root does at ambient what industry does at ~450°C
  and hundreds of atmospheres; the means (that enzyme's trick,
  copied) is not found, and the SCIENCE absence above (catalyst
  computed, not found) is why. Would drain on: the mechanism
  read and rebuilt outside the cell.
- NOT YET FOUND: A PLASTIC THAT SERVES LONG AND DIES FAST — the
  two wants oppose inside one molecule: the inertness that makes
  the pipe last fifty years is the same inertness that makes the
  wrapper last five hundred. Partial drains exist (polymers
  built with a planned weak link that light or compost can cut);
  the general means — durability ON DEMAND, ending on schedule —
  is unfound.
- FELL SHORT: THE SEALED PREDICTION OF SHELF LIFE — accelerated
  aging (hot boxes standing in for years, by the color's clock
  law) works until a reaction with a DIFFERENT threshold
  dominates at the higher temperature; the extrapolation is a
  claim about which reaction rules, and it sometimes rules
  differently cold. Conditions entry: the 10-degree shortcut is
  honest only inside one mechanism's reign.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- TURN ANY ELEMENT INTO ANY OTHER, AT WILL AND AT PROFIT —
  chemically: certified closed (THINGS). Nuclearly: open and
  real but priced out by a million-fold energy scale; the profit
  clause keeps the whole want on this sheet.
- SAY THE PROPERTY, RECEIVE THE MOLECULE — the inverse of
  analysis: compute the structure that would have a wanted
  strength, color, grip, or cure. Blocked on the two SCIENCE
  absences (properties from formula; catalyst computed). The
  learned-predictor drain shows the door can open without the
  law arriving — an uncomfortable, recorded fact.
- UN-BURN, UN-RUST, UN-MIX AT THE PRICE OF THE DOING — every
  undo is billed more than the deed (physics's second law
  reaching in, as the nucleus wall does); dilution, the only
  cheap undo, spends the whole solution. The want stands as the
  honest boundary on every cleanup query this phylum receives.
- A CHEMISTRY THAT WARNS BEFORE IT TRAVELS — the METHODS pattern
  (billed later, elsewhere) answered in advance: a written way
  to see the refrigerant's sky or the insecticide's eggshell at
  the design bench. Partly served by law and screening; not yet
  answerable from written chemistry alone.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- Alchemy's two thousand years: the longest sustained failed try
  in the record — courts funded it, the brightest served it, and
  it never made an ounce of gold. Its portrait absorbed the
  scars; the color inherited the laboratory. The ratio of that
  trying to its yield is the map's darkest patch, and the
  equipment that walked out of it is why the patch is not waste.
- Phlogiston's century: a wrong theory that PREDICTED usefully,
  organized real facts, and died by a single measurement it
  could not digest. Kept as the standing lesson that a theory
  falls to a number, not to an argument — and that its true
  observations get rehired by the successor (something IS
  transferred in burning; the sign was wrong).
- The believer-only effects: N-rays seen by one lab and no
  blinded eye; anomalous water that proved to be dissolved
  glass; the jar fusion above. Three different decades, one
  failure shape: the observer inside the loop. The blinded
  control is the method that failure bought, written now into
  medicine's trials and chemistry's own practice.
- The dial-painters, the leaded decades, the thinned sky: the
  billed-later pattern's human ledger, acknowledged here as the
  price of the METHODS entry that now carries it. The color's
  history says the lesson; this page holds who paid for it.
- The fold's fifty years: a wall of pure arithmetic
  (10⁴⁷ shapes) stood two generations and fell — partly — to a
  method nobody derived. The drain is real, the law is still
  missing, and both facts are filed without forcing them to
  agree.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, REBUILT: CHEMISTRY ↔ LIFE — the link once ran through
  a "vital force" that made life's molecules a separate
  chemistry; broken the day urea came from mineral stock, and
  rebuilt carrying more than before: life is chemistry with
  catalysts and bookkeeping. The break improved the relation —
  filed as the pattern a good break follows.
- BROKEN: CHEMISTRY ↔ ECONOMY THROUGH TRANSMUTATION — alchemy's
  promised road to wealth broke on the nucleus wall; the honest
  rebuild runs through fertilizer, oil, dyes, and plastics, and
  carries more gold than the promise ever named.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: LIFE'S HANDEDNESS ↔ THE SKY — polarized
  light from star-forming regions biases handed molecules a few
  percent in the lab; whether it wrote life's actual bias is
  unproven. Dotted until the CLAIMS entry drains.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: CHEMICAL SQUEEZE ↔ SUPERCONDUCTION — the
  lattice-as-built-in-pressure possibility from THINGS; grips as
  an idea, has produced candidates and no replicated ambient
  material. Dotted, watched, loud.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: THE LEARNED PREDICTOR ↔ THE MISSING LAWS
  — whether a system that answers property-from-structure
  without a derivation can be MINED for the law it implicitly
  holds is an open question that touches every LACKING entry
  above. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: the
  predictor as instrument, interrogated like a star's light,
  its regularities read out as candidate laws. Would drain on:
  one law extracted that way and verified the old way.
