## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or
cannot be.

- THE HOLLOW EARTH — a tried world, believed seriously and
  proposed for exploration. CANNOT BE, on two receipts held in
  the color: the planet weighs 5.5 g/cm³ average against its
  shell's 2.7 — a hollow interior would have to weigh LESS than
  the shell, and this one weighs double — and every quake's
  waves cross the interior and arrive on time at every station,
  which no cavity permits. Filed because the arithmetic that
  kills it is short enough to teach. Would drain only if the
  weighing or the wave arrivals were wrong — and both are
  re-measured every day the network listens.
- A SAMPLE FROM BELOW THE SCRATCH — wanted since drilling began;
  not yet real. The deepest hole stopped at ~12 km of 6,371
  (0.2%) when rock at ~180°C went plastic and squeezed the hole
  shut behind the bit. PARTIALLY DRAINED unasked: volcanoes hand
  up mantle lumps, and iron meteorites are the shattered cores
  of other worlds — core samples held, just never ours.
- A COMPLETE ROCK RECORD — cannot be, by the machine that makes
  the record: rock accumulates only where material arrives, and
  most places most of the time are wearing down — so most of
  time is missing at most places, and the planet's first
  half-billion years left almost no rock at all. The leftovers
  (meteorites, a few zircons) are the only pages of the opening
  chapter.

## CLAIMS
What didn't hold — with why — and what is still unsettled.

- EARTHQUAKE PREDICTION TO THE DAY — certified cannot, under
  these conditions: the break comes when stress passes strength
  at a fault's weakest patch, both quantities buried kilometers
  deep with no instrument that reads them in place; and the
  final trigger is tiny against the stored load, so the day
  carries no signal of its own size. Every offered forerunner
  fails cold testing — animal behavior (never survives blind
  counting), radon, electrical signals, foreshocks (known to be
  foreshocks only afterward). The paid receipts: one famous
  evacuation on foreshocks was followed the next year by a quake
  with no foreshocks that killed ~240,000; a fault watched for a
  promised window let the window close empty and broke a decade
  late. MEANWHILE THE COLOR HOLDS WHAT WORKS: zones, slip
  budgets (~140-year recurrences), hardened buildings, and ~12
  seconds of physics-bought warning. Would drain on: a
  measurable that changes before failure, only before failure,
  readably from the surface — none is written.
- FLOOD GEOLOGY — the tried claim that one recent worldwide
  flood laid all the layers. Paid off with receipts: one flood
  settles ONE graded bed, but cliffs hold thousands of
  alternations with quiet seasons between; single lakes hold
  millions of counted annual layer-pairs; mud cracks,
  footprints, buried soils, and grown reefs sit mid-stack, each
  needing dry air or calm clear water in the middle of the
  claimed year of turbid water. The want — a readable earth —
  was answered instead by the clocks, at a different age. Filed
  as the cleanest case of a reading tested against the rock and
  not held.
- "THE OLDEST ROCK DATES THE PLANET" — didn't hold: the wheel
  recycles the crust, so the oldest surviving rock is far
  younger than the earth. The color's fix: read the age off
  meteorites, the leftovers never reworked.
- "THE DESERT MARCHES IN" — didn't hold: dry land degrades in
  place (overgrazing in good years, ploughing that bares soil,
  wells that let herds stay), and an ordinary drought then finds
  nothing holding the ground. Blaming the desert hid the levers
  that work: cover, delivery, moving on.
- "THE SEA IS DROWNING THE DELTA" — half-held: great deltas sink
  several times faster than the sea rises, because dams stopped
  the mud deliveries and pumping squeezes the pile. The sea gets
  the blame; the working lever is upstream.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT TIMES THE MAGNETIC FIELD'S REVERSALS — the
  dynamo has flipped hundreds of times, at spacings from tens of
  thousands of years to a ~40-million-year quiet stretch, and no
  written law predicts the next. The field weakens ~5% a century
  now — but it has weakened before and recovered without
  flipping. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: the
  flip is the dynamo's own turbulence finding a rare state, a
  flame guttering with nobody blowing on it; simulated dynamos
  flip unprompted the same irregular way. Would settle on: a
  simulation reproducing the recorded spacing statistics AND
  naming a measurable that always precedes its flips.
- UNSETTLED: THE FULL MIX PACING THE ICE AGES — the orbital
  whisper sets the metronome, but the slowest, weakest wobble
  dominates the recent record while stronger ones barely show.
  Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: the feedbacks
  (ice's whiteness, the ocean's carbon grip) choose which
  whisper to amplify, so the pacing belongs to the amplifier.
  Would settle on: a feedback model reproducing WHICH beats won.
- UNSETTLED: WHY THIS PLANET ALONE RUNS PLATES — its twin in
  size next door runs none. Nothing certain — but here is a
  possibility: water is the missing ingredient, softening rock
  enough to bend and lubricating slabs enough to sink; a world
  that lost its ocean locked its shell. Would settle on: finding
  the switch in either planet's record.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A LAW OF FAILURE AT FAULT SCALE — friction laws from
  hand-sized rock in presses do not carry to a thousand square
  kilometers of buried, wet, uneven fault; this missing scale-up
  is the science behind the prediction cannot.
- LACKING: THE DYNAMO'S CLOCK — the reversal record is read
  beautifully (the striped floor) and generated blindly; the
  timing law is absent.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE COOLING-BALL AGE — the era's best
  physics computed the planet's age from its cooling and got
  tens of millions of years: exact, confident, and wrong by a
  factor of fifty to two hundred depending which of his numbers
  you take, because the model lacked a heat source
  (decay) and a heat carrier (creeping rock). The standing
  lesson: an exact number from an incomplete model is exactly
  wrong.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE FAST CLOCK ON OLD THINGS — carbon-14
  past ten half-lives holds under 1/1,000 of its signal, so
  dates claimed beyond ~50,000 years on it were noise dressed as
  numbers; and any date from an unsealed container (a
  sandstone's secondhand grains) reads the wrong event. Both
  misuses published wrong ages before the sealing rule was
  learned.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- PREDICTING BY FORERUNNERS — the tried list is long (animals,
  radon, well levels, electric signals, quake lights, foreshock
  counts) and every one fails blind: each fires without quakes
  and stays silent before real ones. The one honest drain so far
  is not prediction but arithmetic at the event: the fast wave
  outruns the damaging one (~6 vs ~3.5 km/s), so 100 km buys ~12
  seconds — gas valves, brakes, doorways; never evacuations.
- DOWSING FOR WATER — centuries tried, fails every fair blinded
  test; it SEEMS to work because the color's water-table claim
  makes almost any deep-enough guess come up wet. Filed with its
  mechanism of persuasion: a method that half-works on a
  smuggled signal is more dangerous than one that fails
  outright.
- DRILLING TO THE MANTLE — tried at full seriousness; the deep
  try stopped when ~180°C rock flowed shut around the bit faster
  than cutting opened it. Conditions: that hole was sunk in the
  coldest old crust available — 180°C at 12 km is only ~15°C per
  km, half the ordinary gradient — and still hit the wall;
  ordinary crust at ~25-30°C/km reaches ~400°C by 15-16 km, past
  steel's working strength.
  Would drain on: a bit and casing that cut and stand past
  ~300°C — or the ocean route, where the crust is a third as
  thick.
- QUELLING A FAULT BY LUBRICATION — pumping fluid down
  demonstrably TRIGGERS quakes (a city's waste well turned a
  quiet zone seismic; stopping the pump stopped them), but
  nothing controls the size once slipping starts. The draining
  arithmetic forbids the kind version: each magnitude step is
  ~32× energy, so relieving one magnitude-7 costs about a
  thousand magnitude-5s fired under the city on purpose.
  Certified reckless under those numbers; would drain on a way
  to cap an induced slip, which no one has written.
- HOLDING A DELTA WITH LEVEES ALONE — the walls stop the floods
  and thereby the mud deliveries, so the plain sinks while the
  penned river builds its bed upward, raising the stakes of the
  break that comes. The condition is the color's own claim (a
  delta is mud held up by arriving mud); drains only on letting
  mud through — paid diversions, not higher walls.
- STOPPING BEACH LOSS WITH A WALL — the groyne fills upcoast and
  starves everything downcoast, because a beach is a flow, not a
  place; the wall relocates the loss and adds interest.
  Partially drained by feeding the flow (dredged sand poured
  back), which works and never ends — a purchase, not a cure.
- SITING BY LIVING MEMORY — towns placed by the worst anyone
  remembers, on records a century deep, against ground whose
  worst runs on gaps of centuries; the safest-looking site is
  often the one that has not had its turn. Not a cannot — a
  conditions entry: the fix (the rock's longer book) is in the
  color; this stands as why the fix is law.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- NOT YET FOUND: A STRESS GAUGE FOR DEPTH — no instrument reads
  how loaded a buried fault is, in place, kilometers down. This
  single missing instrument is what parts quake forecasting
  (works) from quake prediction (cannot); its arrival would
  drain the largest cannot in this phylum.
- NOT YET FOUND: THE DRILL THAT SURVIVES THE DEEP — conditions
  as filed under METHODS: heat climbs ~25-30°C per km and the
  rock itself begins to flow; everything brought down so far
  softens before the mantle.
- FELL SHORT: THE SHORT RECORD — instruments a century old
  against hazards on thousand-year gaps; honest for weather, a
  trap for ground. Supplemented, never replaced, by the rock's
  own books.
- FELL SHORT: THE MAP AS TESTIMONY — believed the way a sentence
  would not be, though everything on it was chosen and its
  answers change with its scale (no true coastline length).
  Kept as the indispensable mean it is; filed here because its
  confidence outruns its warrant unless the grain is stated.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- NAME THE DAY THE GROUND WILL MOVE — blocked on the missing
  gauge and the missing failure law; the color sells the decade
  instead.
- SEE THE ORE BODY BEFORE THE DRILL — sounding narrows, gravity
  and magnetics hint, only the drill confirms, and most holes
  still miss. Would drain on imaging sharp enough to tell a rich
  crack from a wet one at depth.
- RESTORE A SPENT AQUIFER OR SOIL IN A LIFETIME — the making
  rates forbid it: fossil water fell in another climate and is
  not coming back on any human clock; soil builds at centuries
  per inch. The honest answers are slower spending and imported
  making — both purchases from the color.
- READ THE FIRST HALF-BILLION YEARS — the opening chapter's
  pages were fed back into the wheel; wanting them is wanting
  rock that no longer exists. Drains only through the offsite
  copies (meteorites, the moon's undisturbed face) — a route
  already partly walked.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side.

- The confident wrong age: the best physicist of his century
  computed the planet young, held the number against the
  geologists' plain reading of slow layers, and was wrong
  because his model was missing a term nobody had discovered.
  The geologists could not name his error and were right anyway
  — the rock's testimony outranked the equation's confidence,
  and the missing term arrived within his own lifetime to say
  so.
- The fifty-year rejection: drift was proposed on matching
  coastlines and fossils and rejected for want of an engine —
  and the demand for a mechanism was correct method even though
  the idea was true. The stripes ended it in a decade. Both
  halves are the lesson: suggestive patterns are not tests, and
  sound method can still hold the door shut on a truth for a
  lifetime.
- The prediction embarrassments, paid in full: the evacuated
  city that seemed to prove foreshocks, the unwarned city a year
  later that buried the method with ~240,000, the promised
  window that closed empty. This ledger is why the color's word
  is "forecast" and never "predict."
- The tried worlds: hollow earths and one-flood earths were
  serious readings by serious people working before the
  instruments — filed with respect and their receipts, because
  each was killed by arithmetic anyone can now run in a minute,
  and that arithmetic is the inheritance.
- The scratch: two nations raced to drill through the crust;
  both stopped in the top 0.2%. The interior stayed a place read
  by lamplight — and the reading, tested by prediction, has so
  far beaten the drill at its own game.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, THEN REBUILT HONESTLY: EARTH ↔ BELIEF — scripture read
  as a rock record produced flood geology and young-earth
  counts; the relation broke on the layer arithmetic and stands
  rebuilt with the ground keeping the layers and belief keeping
  the meanings. The receipts stay filed because queries still
  arrive carrying them.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: QUAKE LIGHTS AND SIGNALS ↔ ELECTRONICS —
  reports of glows and radio oddities before quakes recur and
  have never survived blind capture; dotted until an
  instrumented catch, which would upgrade the prediction cannot.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: ANIMAL SENSES ↔ ANIMALS — the fleeing-
  beasts story is ancient, anecdotal, uncounted; every counted
  test says hindsight. Dotted, cheap to test, never yet cashed.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: FLOOD STORIES ↔ STORY — the melt's ~120 m
  sea rise drowned every coastal plain then inhabited, and the
  drowning tales are everywhere; the dates roughly rhyme and
  proof is not written.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: PRESSURE AT STUCK EDGES ↔ CONFLICT — the
  stress-jam-lurch shape recurs in borders and markets as vivid
  picture; whether it carries anything a query can compute with
  beyond the picture is unproven. Dotted, awaiting a query that
  cashes it.
