docs/fabric_phylums/15_building_materials.md,

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE TOWER TO HEAVEN — wanted as long as there have been bricks.
  The ledger says why it stops: base stress = density × g ×
  height, so each material carries a ceiling — solid granite
  crushes itself near ~3.8 km — and long before that the
  square-cube law takes the floor away: lifts, stairs and
  structure grow with the height they serve while the floor plate
  does not, so somewhere past the built ~0.8 km the core eats the
  building it holds up. Wind and money arrive as extra creditors.
  NOT a cannot in general — the shimmer's own shape: it recomputes
  on more strength per weight (the drawn-wire direction, ~6× plain
  steel and climbing) or on a smaller g. On this planet, in these
  materials, at habitable floor fractions: certified closed.
- THE UNSINKABLE AND THE FIREPROOF — a claimed cannot on chance's
  side, tried at full price. The great liner sold as unsinkable
  sank first voyage: its safety was conditional (compartments open
  at the top; a wound longer than the design's worst case) and the
  word had erased the conditions. Theatres and towers advertised
  fireproof burned in their linings and contents — the frame
  survived; the people did not. What drained into the color:
  safety claims are minutes under stated conditions — "holds a
  room's fire one hour," "floats with any two compartments
  flooded" — never absolutes, because chance's tail is longer than
  any design's worst case. The absolute forms stand here as
  certified cannots under all conditions.
- THE INSTANT CITY — tried repeatedly: capitals, districts, new
  towns built in one stroke; the receipts are ghosts — empty
  towers, streets too wide to be places, no worn paths because
  nobody walked. WHY, in the color's own laws: services need a
  quorum of people and people come for services (neither arrives
  alone); desire lines cannot be drawn, only worn; and one
  decade's city files one decade's replacement bill. Drains
  partially on phasing: seed, let the paths wear, build the next
  stage on the evidence of the first.
- THE MAINTENANCE-FREE MATERIAL — wanted by every owner; none is
  written. Disorder wins: every surface is on its way somewhere
  (stone and copper toward dignity, painted render toward
  streaks). Would drain on a surface chemistry that repairs itself
  at ambient energy — nothing certain, but skin heals at body
  temperature, so the want is unbuilt, not unlawful.
- THE TRUE MAP OF THE UNDERGROUND — attempted for a century, never
  real: a record kept by many parties with no shared standard and
  no penalty for being wrong is not a record. Every excavation is
  exploratory; hitting something is normal. Would drain on cheap
  through-ground imaging, or a register with teeth.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "WIDENING THE ROAD CURES THE CONGESTION" — measured false at
  scale: easier driving makes more driving until delay returns at
  a larger size. The road did what it was built to do; the claim
  mistook the queue for the demand. Drains only where demand is
  finite and small — the rural bypass — never in a living city.
- "THE WALL MAKES THE FLOOD IMPOSSIBLE" — false by its own
  arithmetic: a defence raises the size of event needed and
  worsens the one that comes (more built behind; the water arrives
  all at once on overtopping). And the hundred-year label misleads
  twice: 1% a year is 26% per mortgage, and the record behind it
  is usually shorter than the hazard's cycle.
- "FARES SHOULD PAY FOR THE SERVICE" — the spiral, run many times:
  raise fares, cut quiet routes, lose riders, raise cost per
  rider, justify the next cut. Most cost is fixed and much benefit
  lands on people not aboard; the fare cannot measure the worth. A
  metric steers the thing it measures.
- "THE LABELS ON THE PLAN WILL BE OBEYED" — false daily: people
  eat in kitchens, work in bedrooms, wear the shortcut into the
  grass, prop the fire door. Behavior follows effort, permanently;
  the ignored plan is not defeated loudly, it is quietly
  overwritten.
- "SEALING THE BUILDING FIXED IT" — half-true and paid for: the
  gaps lost heat AND carried away moisture; sealing removed both
  jobs, and mold arrived where vapor had nowhere to go. Drains
  only with the deliberate-ventilation clause, now in the color.
- "CONCRETE IS PERMANENT" — believed for a century of ruins: water
  reaches the bars, rust swells severalfold, the cover bursts from
  inside. A conditions entry, not a cannot: it survives where
  water is kept out or the bars protected.
- "THE SAMPLE BOARD SHOWS THE MATERIAL" — it shows day one of
  thirty years; weathering is the real face and the board shows
  none of it. Drains on a sample aged before the choosing — or on
  walking to a wall of the same stuff ten years old.
- "SIGNS WILL FIX THE WAYFINDING" — a building covered in signs is
  confessing its plan does not explain itself; memory holds
  pictures and sequences, not instructions. Drains only where the
  plan itself explains — landmarks and sequence doing the signs'
  work.
- "THE BLIND INDOORS WILL COOL THE ROOM" — the sun crossed the
  glass; the heat is inside and the blind is now a warm object
  indoors. Shade works outside or barely at all. Drains only for
  the light job — glare — never the heat job.
- UNSETTLED: WHEN EXACTLY A CRACK LETS GO — the magnifier law says
  where stress crowds, but fatigue life scatters severalfold
  between twin parts under twin loads. Nothing certain — but here
  is a possibility: the scatter lives in surface flaws below
  inspection's sight, so life is set by the worst flaw nobody saw.
  Would drain on cheap full-field flaw reading — the same drain
  the bridge-walking entry in MEANS waits for.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT MAKES A PLACE LOVED — refuge-with-view,
  enclosure, edges, light from two sides, the body's ruler all
  grip; no written sum predicts a new place's success. Nothing
  certain — but here is a possibility: the gripping parts compose
  most of it and the remainder is people, not geometry (a place is
  loved partly BECAUSE it is used). Would drain on the composed
  sum beating chance on places not yet opened.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A FULL READING OF THE GROUND — the borehole samples a
  column and the building bets a field; between two boreholes
  everything is inference. Foundations carry the largest factors
  and the oldest prayers because the science is thinnest exactly
  where the loads end up. Nothing certain — but a possibility:
  many cheap probes over one dear one, the field sensed instead
  of sampled; the factors would shrink toward the other
  materials'.
- LACKING: A WORKING SUM FOR COMFORT — four channels are known
  (air, surfaces, movement, damp) but bodies differ and the
  thermostat reads one; no sum sets a number that satisfies a
  roomful. The color holds the parts; the composition is white.
  Nothing certain — but a possibility: stop satisfying the
  average body, and let each body trim its own corner of the four
  channels.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: TOWERS-IN-A-PARK PLANNING — light and air
  were computed, correctly; street life was not in the equation,
  and the first three meters died at eye level exactly as the
  color's laws now predict. A model optimizes what it measures,
  and what it omits it quietly destroys.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE CLEAN MAP OF ZONES — a separation
  written for the smoking factory copied itself onto the bakery;
  travel became compulsory, then by car, then parking spread the
  destinations further. A written rule applies to cases it never
  met — paid in city-shape.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- THE FLAT ARCH WITHOUT THE TIE — thrust grows as rise falls (the
  color's worked case: halve the rise, the shove doubles to 125
  kN); walls shoved apart, vaults down. The tallest vault of its
  age fell twice teaching this. Drains with tie rod, buttress, or
  neighbor arch — all in the color.
- THE GLASS BOX EVERYWHERE — one facade for all faces and
  climates: gift on the winter sun side, furnace on the west all
  year, cooling bills forever. Not a cannot — a conditions entry:
  glass is a one-way heat valve, and the method works exactly
  where that valve's direction is wanted.
- BURYING THE FAST LAYER IN THE SLOW — pipes cast into slabs: a
  twenty-year part inside a hundred-year part, dug out at ruin
  prices. Whatever went in first comes out last. Drains on
  separable runs — the color's unknown-tenant method.
- SIZING TO THE AVERAGE — pipes, exits, grids, reservoirs built to
  mean demand fail at every peak; the average says nothing about
  simultaneity or the dry year. The worst hour is the design case.
- DEFERRING THE SMALL BILL — tried by nearly every owner and
  government: the saving is visible now, the cost compounds later
  (~1 of sealing skipped becoming ~4-5 of rebuilding). An
  incentive fault, not ignorance — the cost lands on somebody
  else. Drains only where the later somebody gets a voice now.
- THE EFFICIENCY DRIVE THAT ATE THE SPARE — resilience IS idle
  capacity, so optimization finds and removes it, and the bill
  arrives at the next failure as a cascade through coupled
  systems. Condition for the drain: price the spare as insurance,
  or it will always be spent.
- SKIPPING THE SHAKEDOWN — new systems opened straight into
  service fail most in their first months (wrong settings, factory
  faults, installation errors), and early faults get misfiled as
  design failures. The bathtub curve is not optional.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE BOREHOLE — honest and insufficient: a true word about one
  column of ground, silent about the field. Kept because nothing
  better is written.
- THE PAPER REGISTER OF BURIED SERVICES — fell short for a century
  (see THINGS); the digging proceeds exploratory.
- NOT YET FOUND: AN INSTRUMENT READING A STRUCTURE'S REMAINING
  LIFE — where a bridge has no second load path, a person walks
  the metal forever, because a local defect converts to total
  collapse without warning. The want: see the flaw from a
  distance, at inspection prices. Would drain the walking, never
  the redundancy rule.
- NOT YET FOUND: GRID-SCALE STORAGE AT GRID PRICES — the missing
  piece of every electricity system: the frequency reports the
  balance in real time precisely because almost no buffer stands
  between making and using. Half-draining as batteries arrive; the
  gas network meanwhile stores its buffer in its own pipes and
  rides peaks several times larger with ease — the contrast
  measures what electricity lacks.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- HOUSE EVERYONE WELL WHERE THEY WANT TO BE — the geometry fights:
  corners cost, land sits under parking (a bedroom's floor per
  car, several per household), every network price cross-
  subsidizes, and density's friction arrives with its exchange.
  The color holds each law; no assembly of them yet answers the
  want at the scale it is wanted. Nothing certain — but the
  blockers named in the color are rules and incentives more than
  physics, so the possibility is governance-shaped, not
  geometry-shaped.
- GROW WITHOUT WRITING AN UNBUDGETED BILL — every cohort of pipes
  and bridges is a scheduled future expense nobody schedules; the
  pattern of past building IS the spending forecast, and no city
  yet reads it as one.
- MAKE THE INVISIBLE FUNDABLE BEFORE IT FAILS — attention goes to
  change, and a system performing identically every day emits no
  signal; prevention loses the budget argument to visible things
  almost everywhere. A possibility: fund on measured decay
  instead of failure — the compounding rate is a signal, once
  somebody reads it as one.
- KEEP THE SCREENS WITHOUT THE EXPOSURE — remote control gave one
  operator a hundred sites and gave the network a door nobody
  designed; the efficiency and the exposure are one feature, and
  no arrangement yet keeps the first while closing the second. A
  possibility: let the screens read everything and command
  nothing except down a second, separate path.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side.

- The fallen vaults' tuition: the safety factor is compressed
  grief — centuries of masons finding the edge of the ledger by
  crossing it. The color's clean 1.5-2 is that record reduced to
  one number.
- The sewage ledger: the first great sewers poured the waste into
  the drinking river and death ROSE before it fell — the cleanest
  proof ever paid that the benefit was never the pipe but the
  broken path from waste to mouth. The color's three ordered jobs
  are written in that ink.
- Fire's diary: two ways out, the hour wall, doors opening outward
  under a crowd's push — every clause has a date and a death toll;
  the codes are the disasters, compressed.
- The square window at altitude: the force magnifier collected its
  fee in the first jetliners before the corners were rounded; the
  crack law entered the color at full price.
- The ghost cities' receipts: the instant-city entry above was
  paid for in built, standing, empty concrete — failures measured
  in whole districts, filed with their why.
- The operators' retirements: which valve sticks, which alarm
  lies — knowledge lost as the normal case, because the part that
  mattered was never the part anybody thought to write down.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, THEN REBUILT HONESTLY: BUILDING ↔ TRANSPORT — the old
  relation ("supply more road, get less traffic") ran backwards
  under measurement; rebuilt in the color as behavior-follows-
  effort, with induced demand filed and the junction, not the
  width, holding capacity.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: BUILDING ↔ MIND AS A PREDICTOR — the
  gripping parts are first-class in the color, but composing them
  into a forecast of a place's success is the unsettled claim
  above; the relation stands dotted until it drains.
- NEW AND UNPRICED: BUILDING ↔ COMPUTING — remote control's
  exposure is a live relation with no ledger: the cost has been
  demonstrated, not distributed. Stands here until the trade is
  written with numbers.
- STRAINED, STANDING: BUILDING ↔ GOVERNANCE — concentrated cost
  against diffuse benefit decides where the sewage works goes
  regardless of merits; filed so no crossing mistakes the siting
  record for an engineering answer.
