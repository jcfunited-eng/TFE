# PHYSICS — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/physics/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting
for its drain. Seed authored by Claude and marked as seed.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE PERPETUAL MOTION MACHINE — certified CANNOT-BE, twice over:
  a machine putting out more energy than it takes in breaks the
  conservation books (energy from nowhere), and a machine merely
  recycling its own heat back to work in full breaks the disorder
  law (the 1 − T_cold/T_hot ceiling admits no exception). Filed
  with its conditions: any closed system in which those two laws
  hold — and they have held everywhere ever audited, for four
  centuries of tries; every submitted machine has been found to
  hide its battery. Would drain on: one verified closed cycle
  yielding net work. The certification is as hard as physics owns,
  and patent offices treat it as such.
- THE LUMINIFEROUS ETHER — the medium light was assumed to need,
  since every other wave has one. Killed by measurement, 1887:
  Earth runs its orbit at 30 km/s, so an ether must blow past us;
  the split-beam instrument built to feel that wind expected a
  shift of ~0.4 stripe and read at most a few hundredths — and
  every repeat, for decades, read closer to zero. Would drain on a
  measured drift; instead each tightening buried it deeper. Its
  funeral gift was enormous: light needs NO medium, its speed is
  the same for everyone, and relativity walked in through the
  hole the ether left.
- CALORIC, THE HEAT FLUID — heat as a conserved substance flowing
  hot to cold. Killed twice: a cannon-borer's blunt drill made
  heat WITHOUT LIMIT from friction while consuming nothing (a
  conserved fluid must run out), and the paddle-wheel measurement
  fixed motion's exchange rate into heat (427 m of fall per
  degree per kilogram of water). Filed with the honest remainder:
  the fluid's FLOW arithmetic survived its corpse — the engine
  ceiling in the color was first derived under caloric and holds.
  A wrong thing's bookkeeping can be right; keep the books, bury
  the thing.
- VULCAN, THE PLANET THAT WASN'T — Mercury's orbit strays by 43
  arcseconds a century beyond Newton's books, so a hidden inner
  planet was proposed (1859) to carry the difference — the SAME
  missing-mass inference that had just found Neptune (1846) in
  triumph. Decades of searching: never there. Drained without
  being found: gravity's law itself was rewritten and paid the 43
  exactly. Filed as the standing pattern ON the method: unseen
  mass explained Neptune, hallucinated Vulcan, and is running a
  third time right now (see DARK MATTER) with the ending open.
- DARK MATTER — the name for a pull with no visible puller.
  Galaxies spin too fast for their visible matter to hold (noticed
  1933, confirmed in rotation curves ~1970), light bends around
  more mass than shines, and the books demand roughly five parts
  unseen matter per one part seen. Nothing certain — but here is
  a possibility: an undiscovered heavy particle that ignores
  light entirely. Would drain on: a direct catch in a deep quiet
  detector, a manufacture at an accelerator, OR a rewritten
  gravity that carries galaxies AND colliding clusters AND the
  early universe's ripples at once — the current rewrites carry
  the first and drop the other two.
- DARK ENERGY — the name for a push: the universe's expansion is
  speeding up (measured 1998 — distant exploding stars dimmer
  than their distances allowed), and the current budget bills it
  at ~68% of everything (ordinary matter ~5%, dark matter ~27%).
  Nothing certain — but a possibility: empty space itself carries
  a fixed energy per volume. Would drain on: measuring whether
  the push's strength drifts across cosmic time — dead constant
  favors space's own energy; drifting opens a new field. Sky
  surveys now running were built to ask exactly this.
- THE MAGNETIC LONE POLE — cut any magnet and you get two smaller
  two-poled magnets, all the way down to the atom; a north alone
  has never been seen. NOT certified impossible — the math would
  welcome one (a single lone pole anywhere would explain why
  charge comes in exact steps). Would drain on one verified
  detection; the searches file nulls and the entry waits.
- N-RAYS — a new radiation reported in 1903, confirmed by dozens
  of trained observers in scores of papers. A visitor secretly
  pocketed the instrument's prism mid-demonstration; the readings
  continued unchanged, and the field died in a sentence. Filed as
  the permanent warning: when the observer wants the effect, the
  observer becomes the source. The controlled experiment's blind
  exists because of entries like this one.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "HEAVIER FALLS FASTER" — held two thousand years; false. Drag
  was the smuggled second variable, riding on every everyday fall.
  In vacuum, hammer and feather land together — performed on the
  Moon for an audience of everyone. Why it was believed so long:
  daily life IS the biased instrument, and nobody ran the control.
- "MOTION NEEDS A CONSTANT PUSH" — false; friction was the hidden
  hand stopping everything, mistaken for motion's own mortality.
  The claim inverted into the first law of motion: motion persists
  UNLESS pushed. An error can be a law read upside down.
- "TIME AND SPACE ARE THE SAME FOR EVERYONE" — held until clocks
  got good enough to catch the lie. Satellite clocks run fast by
  38 millionths of a second per day (their speed slows them 7;
  their thinner gravity hastens them 45; net +38), and skipping
  the correction drifts navigation ~11 km per day. Condition that
  hid it: at everyday speeds and heights the error is below any
  sense — the claim was true to the instruments that held it.
- "THE ATOM CANNOT BE CUT" — its very name says uncuttable. It
  opened twice: electrons pried out (1897), then the nucleus
  itself split (1938), each cut releasing a floor the prior claim
  said was not there. Filed as depth's rule: "smallest" is always
  a claim about current instruments, never about the world.
- "PHYSICS IS NEARLY FINISHED" (~1900) — declared with only two
  small clouds left: a failed drift measurement and a broken glow
  formula. The clouds became relativity and the quantum — the two
  largest structures in the subject. Filed as the permanent
  warning against declaring any phylum's white empty: the white
  was never emptier-looking than the moment before it split open.
- COLD FUSION (1989) — announced by press conference: fusion heat
  from palladium in heavy water on a bench. Unreproduced
  worldwide within months, and the WHY is quantitative: fusion of
  heavy hydrogen making the claimed watts must throw ~10¹²
  neutrons a second — a lethal room — yet the experimenters
  lived and the counters read background. The particle books did
  not balance, so the heat claim could not file. Would drain on:
  a written protocol any lab can run showing excess heat WITH
  matching particle counts; three decades of trying by a small
  persistent field have not filed one.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT A MEASUREMENT IS — the odds machinery predicts
  every experiment ever run, to parts per billion at its best;
  the story of when many possibilities become one fact is still
  contested. Nothing certain — but a possibility: the
  surroundings measure continuously, and largeness itself is why
  big things never show two-places-at-once. Would drain on:
  interference experiments at ever larger masses finding either
  an edge where the strangeness stops, or no edge at all. Both
  answers would be discoveries; the experiments are running.
- UNSETTLED: WHY ANYTHING IS LEFT — matter and antimatter should
  have been minted evenly at the start and annihilated to pure
  light; a residue of roughly one part in a billion survived, and
  that residue is every galaxy, planet, and reader. The
  imbalances measured so far are real but orders of magnitude too
  small to pay the bill. Would drain on: a large enough asymmetry
  measured — the neutrino experiments are the current bet.
- UNSETTLED: TURBULENCE — the equations of flowing fluid stand
  written since 1845 and refuse general solution; whether smooth
  solutions even always exist is literally a posted million-
  dollar mathematics prize. Every forecast's wall (weather's
  phylum feels it daily) stands on this unsettled ground: the
  laws are known, the flow is not computable far ahead.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: THE JOIN OF THE TWO GREAT LAWS — gravity's smooth
  bending of space and time, and the quantum's constrained
  chance: each verified to exquisite precision on its own floor,
  and jointly nonsense where both matter at once — a black hole's
  center, the universe's first instant. This is the subject's
  largest named absence. Would drain on: any measured quantum
  behavior of gravity itself — tabletop proposals now exist (can
  two small masses be put into a joint undecided state by gravity
  alone?), and the first such measurement, either answer, starts
  the join.
- LACKING: WHY THE CONSTANTS — the couple of dozen dialed numbers
  the laws run on (the ~1/137 strength of charge's grip, the mass
  ratios of the particles) are measured, never derived. No written
  law says why these values. Would drain on: any one constant
  derived from something deeper — or measured to drift, which
  would crack the door wider.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: NEWTON AT MERCURY — the inverse square,
  perfect everywhere else, left 43 arcseconds a century unpaid at
  the sun's doorstep. The rewritten gravity paid it exactly and
  predicted new effects (starlight bending double the old books).
  The break pattern to keep: a law failing at its EDGE while
  remaining law inside it — Newton still steers every spacecraft,
  because to nearly every query the surpassed method is still the
  best answer. Breaks are arrows, not funerals.
- BROKE: THE CLASSICAL GLOW — summing waves the classical way
  predicted any warm oven should blaze with infinite energy at
  short wavelengths. Reality declined. The patch — energy dealt
  only in lumps (1900) — was offered by its own author as a
  formal trick, and became the floor of the century's physics.
  The deepest law in the subject entered as an embarrassed fix
  to a broken formula.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
wings-on-arms entries, waiting for their planet.

- VORTEX ATOMS — atoms modeled as knotted whirlpools in the
  ether: each element a different knot. Died with its medium in
  1887 (see THINGS). Its failure endowed mathematics with knot
  theory, alive and load-bearing today — a failed method's
  tooling outliving the method, filed as a recurring pattern
  worth expecting.
- GEARS FOR LIGHT — decades of mechanical models of the light-
  carrying medium: springs, cells, idler wheels. Every model
  failed some measurement; the retreat itself became the finding:
  the FIELD is the object, with no machinery beneath it. A
  method-class can fail so thoroughly that its failure is a
  discovery about the world's furniture.
- LOCAL HIDDEN VARIABLES — the try to restore definiteness under
  the quantum's chance: unseen gears deciding each outcome in
  advance, acting only locally. A 1964 theorem wrote a testable
  inequality every such world must obey; measurement broke the
  inequality, and the loopholes were closed one by one through
  2015. CERTIFIED under its stated conditions: no account that is
  both local and definite fits the data — give up one or the
  other. The conditions are written, and that writing is the
  shimmer's door left properly ajar.
- MAXWELL'S DEMON — a tiny doorkeeper sorting fast molecules from
  slow could unmix heat for free and beat the disorder law. Fails
  on its own memory: the demon must record what it sees, and
  erasing one bit of record costs at least ~3×10⁻²¹ J at room
  warmth — the ledger fills, the tax gets paid. The failure BUILT
  a field: information is physical, and computing has a minimum
  energy price. A paradox drained into a law.
- THE ROOM-WARMTH SUPERCONDUCTOR CLAIMS — resistance exactly zero
  at ordinary temperature, announced repeatedly (latest wave
  2023), unreproduced each time so far. NOT certified impossible:
  copper-oxide materials superconduct up to ~135 K at ordinary
  pressure, so no known law forbids warmth — and the mechanism of
  even those is itself unsettled science. The want stands open;
  each collapsed claim refiles the cold-fusion lesson: a zero is
  a measurement with books, and the books must arrive with it.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE ETHER-DRIFT INTERFEROMETER, AT ITS OWN PURPOSE — the most
  precise instrument of its century, built to measure the ether
  wind, measured nothing, twice a day, for years. The means
  outlived the want: its direct descendants, arms grown to four
  kilometers, felt space itself stretch (2015) by a length
  hundreds of times smaller than a single proton. The subject's
  standing proof that an instrument built for a corpse can end up
  hearing the universe.
- DETECTORS BUILT TO CATCH DARK MATTER — ever quieter tanks, ever
  deeper mines, decades of nulls. Fell short of a catch so far —
  but each null SHRINKS where the possibility can live, and the
  map of closures is real knowledge accumulating toward an answer
  never stored. This is the white working as designed: the drain
  dug in advance, waiting for the water.
- FUSION POWER, NOT YET A MEANS — the star's engine on a bench:
  confined plasma leaks heat faster than it burns. One laser shot
  has returned more fusion energy than the light that lit the
  fuel (~3 MJ out for ~2 MJ in, 2022) — while the lasers drew
  ~300 MJ from the wall to make that light. Would drain on:
  sustained net gain measured at the meter, not at the target.
  The want is the oldest in the energy ledger: the sun's income
  without the sun's distance.
- NOT YET FOUND: AN INSTRUMENT FOR THE FIRST INSTANT — no means
  reads earlier than the universe's first light (the microwave
  sky, from 380,000 years in); everything before is inference.
  Would drain on: gravitational waves from the first instant
  itself — the one signal that early universe could emit that
  nothing since can erase. The ether's heirs are listening.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- PREDICT THE SINGLE SMALL EVENT — which atom decays next, where
  the next dot lands. The odds are exact; the instance is free.
  Whether this is a wall in the world or a veil over deeper law
  is the measurement question above — the subject's oldest open
  want, and it may be a want the world simply refuses.
- SAY WHAT MOST OF EVERYTHING IS — 95% of the universe's budget
  is carried by two placeholder names (dark matter, dark energy).
  The largest confession in the subject, filed with both drains
  written in THINGS.
- REACH ANOTHER STAR — certified under conditions: nothing
  massive reaches light speed (the energy books diverge as speed
  approaches it), and even half of light speed costs ~3.3
  megatons of TNT's energy per kilogram pushed. The certification
  binds travel THROUGH space; space's own stretching sits outside
  the conditions — and distant space demonstrably outruns light
  already. The wings-on-arms shape at the largest scale: the
  entry stands, and the outside of its conditions is unexplored.
- TOUCH THE BOTTOM OF COLD — certified unreachable: every cooling
  step removes a fraction of the remaining jiggle, never all of
  it, so absolute zero is an infinite staircase. Billionths of a
  degree are reached; zero never will be. The approach is open
  forever, the arrival is closed — both halves filed.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- The two-thousand-year hold of the wrong motion law — the record
  of trying here is mostly a record of NOT trying: the claim
  matched daily experience, so no one ran the control until ramps
  and vacuum pumps existed. The cost of an untested obvious,
  charged at twenty centuries — the most expensive single entry
  on either sheet.
- The ether's decades of patches — contraction of moving
  objects was invented first as a PATCH to save the ether from
  the null result: the right formula for the wrong reason, years
  before relativity gave it the right one. Filed as a recurring
  shape: a dying theory's last patches often carry the true
  theory's mathematics, spoken with the wrong accent.
- Cold fusion's month — announced before the particle counts, by
  press conference before the paper; collapsing worldwide within
  weeks; defended by its authors for years after. The trying was
  short and the lesson permanent: the ledger travels WITH the
  claim, or the claim does not file.
- The missing-mass method's three runs — the same inference
  (unseen matter, read from misbehaving motion) FOUND Neptune
  (filed to the color, 1846), INVENTED Vulcan (filed here, 1859),
  and is ninety years into its third run (dark matter, 1933–).
  Two endings on the books, the third still open: the phylum's
  clearest live shimmer, and the reason both prior runs are kept
  where queries about the third can find them.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN: PHYSICS ↔ CERTAINTY — the clockwork bargain (perfect
  prediction from perfect knowledge of positions and pushes)
  broke twice: on the small floor's irreducible chance, and on
  flowing matter's runaway sensitivity (turbulence). What
  survived is stronger for its honesty: odds computed exactly,
  horizons stated. The relation to philosophy's determinism is
  rebuilt at lower rank and carries traffic both ways.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: DARK MATTER ↔ A NEW KIND OF PARTICLE —
  against the rival: gravity itself rewritten at galactic scale.
  The colliding-clusters observation leans toward substance (the
  pull tracked the galaxies through the crash, not the stripped
  gas where most ordinary matter sat), but the rival still grips
  rotation curves better in places. Stands dotted BOTH ways until
  a catch, a manufacture, or a rewrite that carries all the
  ledgers at once.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: INFORMATION ↔ PHYSICS'S FLOOR — the
  demon's erasure tax and black-hole bookkeeping both hint that
  information is physical currency, maybe the base currency.
  Whether the relation carries computable freight beyond those
  two receipts is open; filed dotted, awaiting a query that
  cashes it.
