## THINGS
The units this subject is about. Physics sits near the root: almost
every branch of the fabric runs back through these things, so each
names its other homes for the cascade.

- ENERGY — the one conserved currency; every event is energy
  changing hands or changing coat. Also lives in: cooking (heat's
  work), the body (food as fuel), economy (the cost under every
  cost), farming (sunlight caught), engineering.
- MASS — the amount of matter; the stubbornness against being
  pushed AND the handle gravity pulls; also frozen energy (see
  SCIENCE). Also: chemistry (the scale's truth), engineering, body.
- MOTION — position changing with time; never lost on its own,
  only traded. Also: engineering, weather (moving air is the
  weather), life (everything alive moves something).
- FORCE — the push or pull that CHANGES motion; nothing else does.
  Also: engineering (loads), body (muscle), farming (the plow).
- MOMENTUM — motion's quantity, mass times velocity; the thing
  collisions conserve exactly. Also: engineering (impact), body
  (why the catch hurts less when the hands give).
- HEAT — energy in transit from hotter to cooler; the disordered
  jiggling of atoms, not a substance (the mirror holds the fluid
  it was mistaken for). Also: cooking, weather, the body, engines.
- TEMPERATURE — how hard the atoms jiggle, on a scale with a true
  bottom (−273.15°C, where jiggling runs out). Also: cooking,
  weather, medicine (fever), chemistry (rates).
- PRESSURE — force spread on area; in a still fluid it pushes
  every direction alike and stacks with depth. Also: weather (the
  barometer), cooking (the sealed pot), body (blood), engineering.
- THE GAS — matter's loosest state: atoms flying free, pressure
  from their drumming on the walls. Also: weather, chemistry,
  cooking (leavening), engineering (every engine breathes).
- WAVES — traveling disturbance that carries energy without
  carrying the medium; the shape moves, the water stays. Also:
  weather (swell), culture (music), medicine (ultrasound).
- SOUND — pressure waves in matter; no matter, no sound. 343 m/s
  in 20°C air, ~1,480 in water, ~5,900 in steel. Also: body (the
  ear), culture (music), engineering.
- LIGHT — the self-carrying wave of electricity and magnetism;
  needs no medium (paid for in the mirror), one speed in vacuum,
  arrives in countable lumps. Also: life (sight, photosynthesis),
  farming, chemistry, culture.
- CHARGE — matter's second handle, two kinds; like repels, unlike
  attracts; conserved, and dealt only in exact steps. Also:
  chemistry (all bonding is charge), body (nerves), engineering.
- THE MAGNET — moving charge's companion field; poles always in
  pairs (the lone pole waits in the mirror). Also: engineering
  (motor and generator), medicine (imaging), weather (compass).
- ATOMS — matter's discrete kinds, ~90 found in nature; everything
  is arrangements of them. Also: chemistry (its whole alphabet),
  life, medicine, farming (soil is atoms too).
- THE NUCLEUS — the atom's dense heart: nearly all the mass in a
  hundred-thousandth of the width; where the fiercest energy
  ledger is kept. Also: medicine (radiation), economy (power),
  culture (the bomb's shadow).
- FIELDS — the condition of space that carries force without
  touch: gravity's, charge's, the magnet's. The field is the
  thing, not bookkeeping (the mirror records the machinery that
  was tried under it). Also: engineering, weather (maps of
  pressure ARE fields).
- GRAVITY — every mass pulling every other, weakening with
  distance squared; the weakest force and the only one that never
  cancels, so it rules the large. Also: weather (why air thins
  up), body (bones built against it), farming (water runs
  downhill), engineering.
- TIME AND SPACE — the stage that turned out to be an actor:
  clocks run slower when moving fast or sitting deep in gravity
  (measured daily; see PURPOSE, navigation). Also: mathematics,
  culture (the calendar), engineering (every schedule).

## CLAIMS
What holds, written to be tested by a crossing query. Each claim
carries its conditions.

- Energy is never made and never destroyed — only moved between
  holders and changed in form. Condition: count EVERYTHING,
  including the heat that leaks; every audited exception has died
  on an uncounted leak.
- Every energy trade sheds some as heat, and heat flows downhill
  only: hotter to cooler, never the reverse on its own. Reversing
  it (the refrigerator) costs outside work. Disorder wins by
  default; order is always paid for.
- Heat moves three ways: by touch through matter, carried in
  moving matter, and as glow across empty space. Each road has its
  own law with numbers (see SCIENCE); the sun reaches us by the
  third alone.
- A thing in motion keeps its motion — speed and direction — until
  a force changes it. Rest is not the natural state; stopping is
  friction's doing, not motion's death.
- Force equals mass times acceleration: the same push moves the
  light thing more. Condition: speeds far below light's; near
  light speed the books change (see SCIENCE, mass-energy).
- Every push is answered by an equal push back — you cannot touch
  without being touched. This is why guns kick, why rockets work
  in empty space (they push on their own thrown exhaust), and why
  the Earth rises to meet every falling apple, unmeasurably.
- Momentum is conserved in every collision ever measured, gentle
  or violent, no exceptions found. Missing momentum in the books
  is how the invisible is discovered (see METHODS).
- Everything falls at the same rate where drag is absent — hammer
  and feather together. Condition: vacuum, or drag small against
  weight. The mirror holds the two thousand years this was denied.
- Gravity pulls with the product of the masses and fades with the
  distance SQUARED — the same law for the apple and the Moon,
  checked by computation (the founding worked case, in SCIENCE).
- Pressure in a still fluid pushes equally in all directions and
  grows with depth; ten meters of water adds one atmosphere. A
  floating thing displaces exactly its own weight of fluid — which
  is why steel ships float and why ~90% of an iceberg hides.
- A confined gas trades pressure, volume, and temperature by one
  law: pressure times volume follows absolute temperature. Heat a
  sealed can and the pressure climbs with the kelvins.
- Changing form — melt, boil — eats or pays energy at a standstill
  temperature. The toll is large: boiling a kilogram of water away
  costs over five times what heating it from ice-cold to the boil
  cost (numbers in SCIENCE).
- Waves pass through each other unharmed and simply ADD where they
  cross: crest on crest doubles, crest on trough cancels to
  nothing. Silence from two loudspeakers is possible and the
  positions are computable (SCIENCE).
- Light holds one speed in vacuum, 299,792,458 m/s, the same for
  every observer regardless of their own motion — and nothing
  carrying matter or news goes faster. Condition: through space;
  space's own stretching is outside the claim (the mirror keeps
  that door).
- Charge comes in two kinds and exact steps, is conserved
  absolutely, and grips by the same inverse-square shape as
  gravity but enormously harder: two protons repel electrically
  ~10^36 times stronger than they attract by gravity. Matter feels
  neutral only because the two kinds are mixed to a whisker.
- Moving charge makes magnetism; changing magnetism pushes charge.
  One law read both ways is the motor and the generator — and
  light itself is the pair chasing each other through space.
- Matter comes in discrete kinds. ~90 natural atoms, each a fixed
  character; every substance is an arrangement, every chemical
  change a re-arrangement, atoms conserved throughout.
- The very small runs on constrained chance: the odds of each
  outcome are exact and computable to parts per billion, the
  single event is free. Watching costs: to measure a small thing
  is to disturb it, by an amount with a floor, not by clumsiness.
- Mass is frozen energy: E = mc². The exchange rate is so steep
  that a gram pays for a city's destruction (worked in SCIENCE);
  the sun runs on this ledger, spending mass as light.

## SCIENCE
The math and physics held — not mentioned. Each piece is a LAW a
query can compute with, a RULE it can follow, and where it earns
it, a WORKED case with the arithmetic run.

### MECHANICS — motion and its books

LAW: force = mass × acceleration. F = m·a — newtons, kilograms,
  meters per second per second.
RULE: to find what a push does — divide the force by the mass;
  that is the speed gained each second while the push lasts.
WORKED: a 1,000 kg car pushed with 3,000 N: a = 3 m/s². From rest
  to 30 m/s (highway speed) takes 30 ÷ 3 = 10 s. The same numbers
  run backwards are the brakes.

LAW: falling gains 9.8 m/s every second; distance = ½ × 9.8 × t².
RULE: to time a drop — square the seconds, times 4.9, meters.
WORKED: 1 s → 4.9 m, 2 s → 19.6 m, 3 s → 44.1 m. The distances
  run as the squares 1:4:9 — this pattern, read off a slowed-down
  ball on a ramp, is where measured physics began.

LAW: energy of motion = ½ × mass × speed². Height's stored energy
  = mass × 9.8 × height. Falling converts one to the other
  exactly.
RULE: to find landing speed from height — v = √(2 × 9.8 × h).
WORKED: a 1 kg stone off a 5 m wall: stored 1 × 9.8 × 5 = 49 J;
  lands at √(2 × 9.8 × 5) = √98 ≈ 9.9 m/s; motion energy
  ½ × 1 × 98 = 49 J. The books balance to the joule — that
  balance IS the conservation claim, run once.
WORKED, the square's teeth: a 1,000 kg car at 30 m/s carries
  ½ × 1,000 × 900 = 450,000 J; at 60 m/s, 1,800,000 J. Double
  the speed, FOUR times the energy — and four times the braking
  distance. The square law prices every crash.

LAW: momentum = mass × velocity, conserved in total through every
  collision and separation.
RULE: to find a recoil — set the momenta equal and opposite.
WORKED: a 4 kg rifle fires a 10 g bullet at 800 m/s. Bullet
  momentum 0.01 × 800 = 8; rifle recoil 8 ÷ 4 = 2 m/s backwards.
  The shoulder is doing conservation arithmetic.

### GRAVITY — the founding computation

LAW: pull = G × m₁ × m₂ ÷ r², fading with distance SQUARED. At
  Earth's surface it accelerates everything at 9.8 m/s².
RULE: to re-price gravity at another distance — square the ratio
  of distances and divide.
WORKED, the check that joined heaven and earth: the Moon sits 60
  Earth-radii out, so the law demands 9.8 ÷ 60² = 9.8 ÷ 3,600 =
  0.00272 m/s² there. Now measure the Moon directly: orbit radius
  384,000 km, period 27.3 days = 2.36 million s; speed = 2π ×
  3.84×10⁸ ÷ 2.36×10⁶ ≈ 1,022 m/s; needed inward acceleration =
  v² ÷ r = 1,022² ÷ 3.84×10⁸ ≈ 0.00272 m/s². The two numbers
  MATCH. One law, apple and Moon — this single computation is the
  subject's founding receipt.
- Weight is the pull, mass is the amount: a 60 kg person weighs
  60 × 9.8 = 588 N here and about a sixth of that (~98 N) on the
  Moon, mass unchanged. Escape from Earth entirely costs 11.2
  km/s, thrown once — the price every rocket pays.

### HEAT AND DISORDER

LAW: heat to warm a thing = mass × capacity × degrees climbed.
  Q = m·c·ΔT. Capacities (J per kg per °C): water 4,180 · ice
  2,100 · air ~1,000 · iron 450 · copper 385. Water's is the
  giant — the reason coasts have mild weather and radiators are
  filled with it.
RULE: mass in kg, times capacity, times the climb.
WORKED, Joule's waterfall: let 1 kg of water fall 427 m and catch
  every joule as heat: energy = 1 × 9.8 × 427 ≈ 4,185 J; climb =
  4,185 ÷ 4,180 ≈ 1.0°C. Motion and heat trade at a fixed,
  measured rate — the exchange rate that killed the heat-fluid
  (mirror) and completed the conservation law.

LAW: changing form has a fixed toll at a standstill temperature.
  Melting ice: 334,000 J/kg. Boiling water off: 2,257,000 J/kg.
RULE: tolls are paid per kilogram converted; temperature does not
  move until the toll is fully paid.
WORKED: melting 1 kg of ice costs 334,000 J — the very energy
  that would lift the melted kilogram's temperature by 334,000 ÷
  4,180 ≈ 80°C. Boiling that kilogram away costs 2,257,000 J —
  5.4 times the 418,000 J that heating it the whole way from 0 to
  100°C cost. Phase change is where the energy hides: sweat cools
  a body, steam scalds one, and a thawing snowpack holds rivers
  back for weeks, all on this one toll.

LAW: heat through a wall by touch = conductivity × area ×
  temperature difference ÷ thickness. Conductivities (W per m per
  °C): copper 400 · steel ~50 (stainless ~15) · glass 0.8 · wood
  ~0.1 · still air 0.026.
WORKED, with its honest condition: a single-pane window, 1 m² of
  4 mm glass, 20°C inside and 0 outside: 0.8 × 1 × 20 ÷ 0.004 =
  4,000 W — yet real windows lose nearer 100 W. The difference is
  two thin films of still air clinging to the faces: air conducts
  30 times worse than glass, so the films, not the glass, are the
  true wall. The law is right; the layers must ALL be counted.
  Double glazing is the same lesson built on purpose.

LAW: everything glows by its temperature's FOURTH power: radiated
  power = 5.67×10⁻⁸ × area × T⁴ (T in kelvin), and absorbs by the
  same law from its surroundings.
WORKED: bare human skin, 1.7 m² at 306 K, radiates 5.67×10⁻⁸ ×
  1.7 × 306⁴ ≈ 845 W; a 293 K room radiates ≈ 710 W back; net
  loss ≈ 135 W — the scale of the body's whole food budget, which
  is why clothing exists and why a clear night sky (very cold,
  radiating little back) frosts the ground it faces.

LAW: no engine turning heat to work keeps more than
  1 − T_cold ÷ T_hot of it (temperatures in kelvin). The rest
  MUST be dumped as waste heat — disorder's tax, not bad
  engineering.
WORKED: steam at 500 K exhausting to a 300 K river: ceiling =
  1 − 300/500 = 40%. No cleverness passes it at those two
  temperatures; the only doors are hotter steam or a colder sink.
  Every power station's cooling towers are this subtraction made
  visible.

### PRESSURE AND THE GAS

LAW: pressure = force ÷ area. Atmosphere at sea level: ~101,000
  Pa — about a kilogram-weight on every square centimeter.
WORKED: a 60 kg person on one 1 cm² stiletto heel: 588 N ÷
  0.0001 m² = 5,880,000 Pa. A 4,000 kg elephant on four 0.1 m²
  feet: 39,200 N ÷ 0.4 m² = 98,000 Pa. The heel presses SIXTY
  times harder than the elephant — area, not weight, decides.
  The unnoticed version: air pushes on a 1 m² tabletop with
  ~101,000 N — ten tonnes — felt by no one because it pushes
  every direction alike, underside included.

LAW: depth pressure = density × 9.8 × depth.
WORKED: 10 m under water: 1,000 × 9.8 × 10 = 98,000 Pa — one
  extra atmosphere per ten meters, the diver's ruling number and
  the reason dams thicken toward the bottom.

LAW: for a confined gas, pressure × volume ÷ absolute temperature
  stays fixed.
RULE: hold one of the three, and the other two trade in exact
  proportion; temperatures ALWAYS in kelvin.
WORKED, both directions: a sealed can at 293 K tossed in a fire
  at 586 K doubles its pressure — the whole warning label in one
  ratio. A 1.0 L balloon dunked in liquid nitrogen (77 K) shrinks
  toward 1.0 × 77 ÷ 293 ≈ 0.26 L, and swells back on the bench.
  Cooling to zero volume extrapolates every gas line to the same
  floor: −273.15°C — how the true bottom of temperature was READ
  off a graph before anyone could approach it.

### WAVES AND LIGHT

LAW: speed = frequency × wavelength, for every wave.
WORKED: concert A, 440 Hz in 343 m/s air: wavelength 343 ÷ 440 ≈
  0.78 m. Green light at 5.4×10¹⁴ Hz: 3×10⁸ ÷ 5.4×10¹⁴ ≈
  5.6×10⁻⁷ m — half a thousandth of a millimeter, which is why
  light seems to travel in rays until its obstacles shrink to its
  own scale.

LAW: crossing waves add point by point; equal waves half a
  wavelength out of step cancel exactly.
RULE: to find silence or brightness — compare the two path
  lengths; a whole-wavelength difference doubles, a half cancels.
WORKED: two speakers playing 686 Hz (wavelength 343 ÷ 686 =
  0.50 m): stand where one speaker is 0.25 m farther than the
  other and the waves arrive opposed — silence, from two sources
  of sound. Noise-cancelling headphones manufacture this on
  purpose; soap-film colors are the same arithmetic run by light
  against its own reflection.

LAW: glow spreads over the sphere it fills: brightness falls with
  distance SQUARED.
WORKED: Mars orbits 1.52 times our distance from the sun and so
  receives 1 ÷ 1.52² ≈ 43% of our light per square meter — a fact
  every greenhouse-on-Mars plan must budget first.

LAW: light's speed in vacuum, 3×10⁸ m/s, same for all observers.
WORKED: sun to Earth, 1.5×10¹¹ m ÷ 3×10⁸ = 500 s — we see the
  sun as it was eight minutes ago; every look outward is a look
  backward. Sound's version at 343 m/s: thunder arrives ~3 s per
  kilometer behind its lightning — count seconds, divide by
  three, kilometers to the strike.

### CHARGE AND CIRCUITS

LAW: current through a resistance costs voltage: V = I × R.
  Power delivered = V × I, watts.
WORKED: a 230 V kettle drawing 10 A delivers 2,300 W through a
  23 Ω element. Job: 1 kg of water, 20°C to boil: 4,180 × 80 =
  334,400 J; time = 334,400 ÷ 2,300 ≈ 145 s. One law prices the
  wait, the wire's thickness, and the fuse.
- The pairing that electrified the world: spin a coil near a
  magnet and charge is pushed (the generator); push charge
  through a coil near a magnet and it spins (the motor). One law,
  two readings, and the whole grid between them.

### ATOMS AND THE VERY SMALL

LAW: atoms are counted by the mole: 6×10²³ of anything, sized so
  the count in grams equals the atom's own mass number.
WORKED: 18 g of water — one swallow — holds 6×10²³ molecules.
  All the oceans together hold about 8×10²² such swallows — the
  one swallow beats every ocean roughly eight times over. Matter's
  grain is that fine, which is why it took so long to prove it had
  grain at all (HISTORY).

LAW: light's energy comes in lumps: E = h × f, with h =
  6.6×10⁻³⁴ J·s.
WORKED: one green-light lump: 6.6×10⁻³⁴ × 5.4×10¹⁴ ≈ 3.6×10⁻¹⁹
  J. A 1 W green laser therefore fires 1 ÷ 3.6×10⁻¹⁹ ≈ 3×10¹⁸
  lumps per second — so many that light feels continuous, so few
  per event that a dark-adapted eye can register a handful.

LAW: unstable nuclei decay by halves: remaining = start ×
  (½)^(time ÷ half-life). Which atom goes next is pure chance;
  the population's clock is exact — constrained chance in one
  formula.
WORKED: carbon-14, half-life 5,730 years. A bone at one eighth
  its living carbon-14 has sat through exactly three halvings:
  3 × 5,730 = 17,190 years. Archaeology reads clocks physics
  wound.

LAW: mass is energy at rate E = m × c², c² = 9×10¹⁶ J/kg.
WORKED: one gram converted: 0.001 × 9×10¹⁶ = 9×10¹³ J — about
  21,000 tonnes of TNT. The Hiroshima bomb converted less than a
  gram; the sun converts four million tonnes each second and has
  barely dented itself in four billion years.

LAW: the small is chance under exact constraint. Fire single
  electrons, one at a time, at two open slits: each arrives as
  one dot at an unpredictable place, and ten thousand dots build
  the striped adding-pattern of a WAVE that went through both
  slits. The odds are computed exactly (to parts per billion in
  the best-tested cases); the instance is not — and no deeper
  gearing that is both local and definite fits the measurements
  (the mirror holds that closed door and its exact conditions).

## METHODS
How things are done here — each with what governs it and what
breaks it. The prepositioned how-to-build.

- THE CONTROLLED EXPERIMENT: change ONE thing, hold the rest,
  measure what follows; repeat until the wiggle is smaller than
  the effect. Breaks when two things moved together — the mirror
  is a museum of claims that died of a smuggled second variable
  (drag riding on weight for two thousand years).
- IDEALIZE, THEN REPAY: solve the frictionless, airless, point-
  sized version first — the laws show clean — then add the messy
  terms back priced. Breaks when the discarded term WAS the
  story: the feather falls wrong until drag returns to the books.
- CONSERVATION BOOKKEEPING: audit energy, momentum, charge before
  and after. The books MUST balance; a deficit is never a loss —
  it is an arrow pointing at something unseen carrying the
  missing amount. The neutrino was found as exactly this: missing
  momentum billed to an invisible thief, decades before any
  detector caught one.
- FORCE ACCOUNTING: isolate one body on paper, draw every force
  on it and nothing else, sum to get the acceleration. Breaks
  when a force is forgotten (the normal push of the ground is the
  classic omission) or a neighbor's force is double-counted.
- DIMENSION CHECKING: units must balance across every equals
  sign — meters with meters, joules with joules. Costs nothing,
  catches nonsense before arithmetic starts, and can even guess a
  formula's shape from the units alone.
- MEASURING WITH WAVES: split one light beam, send the halves on
  two journeys, recombine and read the stripes; a shift of one
  stripe is one wavelength of difference — lengths read to
  fractions of a millionth of a meter. The subject's most
  delicate ruler (and the mirror holds the null it was built for
  and the triumph that null became).
- SPECTROSCOPY: hot matter glows and cool matter absorbs at
  wavelengths exact to the KIND of atom — a barcode per element.
  Works at any distance light survives: the same lines read in a
  flame and in a star's light is how we know the stars are made
  of our atoms.
- CALORIMETRY: trap a process's heat in a known mass of water,
  read the climb, multiply by 4,180. Turns any reaction, meal, or
  machine into joules on a receipt.
- THE COLLISION PROBE: throw small fast things at what cannot be
  seen and read the bounce pattern. Rare hard bounces off atoms
  revealed the nucleus; the method scaled up is the accelerator
  — faster probe, shorter wavelength, finer sight.
- THE NULL EXPERIMENT: build the instrument to measure NOTHING —
  perfectly balanced arms, perfectly opposed paths — so that any
  reading at all is discovery. The sharpest measurements in the
  subject's history are nulls (the mirror holds the greatest).

## MEANS
What the methods run on, with the numbers that matter.

- CLOCKS AND RULERS: the second is 9,192,631,770 ticks of a
  cesium atom's inner swing; the meter is DEFINED off light's
  fixed speed. Measurement's floor is atomic now — every lab on
  Earth owns identical standards because atoms are identical.
- THE BALANCE: mass is the honest quantity — conserved, additive,
  indifferent to place. Weight varies by planet; the balance
  cancels that and reads mass true.
- THE THERMOMETER: temperature made visible; the instrument that
  turned heat from argument into arithmetic.
- PRISM AND GRATING: light fanned by wavelength — the door to
  every barcode spectroscopy reads.
- LENS AND MIRROR: light bent to magnify — telescope outward,
  microscope inward. Hard limit: nothing finer than the
  wavelength used can be resolved, ~half a millionth of a meter
  for visible light — the wall that forced electron microscopes
  and accelerators, which see with shorter waves.
- THE VACUUM PUMP: subtracting the air; where drag, sound, and
  oxidation all stop and the clean laws show bare. Half the
  founding experiments were impossible until the air could leave.
- MAGNET AND COIL: the generator (motion in, current out), the
  motor (current in, motion out), the transformer (voltage traded
  for current at fixed power) — three windings of one law, and
  the electric grid is the three of them at continental size.
- THE BATTERY: chemistry lending charge on demand — chemistry's
  door into this phylum, and the portable half of everything.
- DETECTORS: from the photographic plate that found
  radioactivity by accident to counters that click for single
  lumps of light or single decaying atoms — the means by which
  constrained chance became countable fact.
- ACCELERATORS: the collision probe industrialized; kilometers of
  ring to make protons fast enough that their wavelength probes
  the width of a nucleus's parts.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject answers — the doors a query enters by.

- PREDICT BEFORE IT HAPPENS: where the ball, the planet, the
  current, the heat will be — computed ahead of the event. The
  subject's oldest promise and still its core trade.
- MOVE WORK OUT OF MUSCLE: the engine, the motor, the turbine —
  heat and current turned to pull. The watt is the wage of the
  modern world, and its ceiling (the disorder tax) is priced in
  SCIENCE, not negotiable.
- SEE THE INVISIBLE: too small (microscope, accelerator), too far
  (telescope), too enclosed (X-ray, ultrasound, magnetic
  imaging), too fast, too faint — physics builds every extended
  eye the other subjects look through.
- POWER AND LIGHT ON DEMAND: the grid, the battery, the lamp; a
  civilization's pulse measured in the units this phylum defined.
- KNOW WHAT EVERYTHING IS MADE OF: the same ~90 atoms here, in
  the sun, and in the farthest star — read by their light. One
  matter, everywhere, provably.
- KEEP TIME AND FIND THE WAY: from the pendulum to the atomic
  clock to satellite navigation — which only works because two
  relativity corrections (+45, −7 millionths of a second per day)
  are applied; skip them and the fix drifts ~11 km per day. The
  deepest theory in the phylum, riding in every pocket.
- BE THE GROUND FLOOR: chemistry, engineering, weather, cooking,
  the body — their laws are this phylum's laws wearing work
  clothes. A root phylum's purpose is to be built upon.

## HISTORY
How the knowledge arose, in what order, at what cost — the order
carries why.

- The sky first: regularity noticed before law existed — seasons,
  eclipses, planets — because the sky is the one lab nobody can
  disturb. Prediction preceded explanation by millennia.
- Galileo slows the fall: balls on ramps, time in heartbeats and
  water-drips — the fall stretched until its 1:4:9 pattern showed.
  Measurement enters, and the two-thousand-year-old motion claims
  die of it (the mirror holds both corpses).
- Newton joins heaven and earth: one gravity for apple and Moon,
  checked by the computation in SCIENCE. The join proved the
  universe runs on ONE set of laws — the license every later
  physicist worked under.
- Heat's identity fight: heat-as-fluid against heat-as-motion,
  settled by a cannon borer's endless friction-heat and a paddle
  wheel's fixed exchange rate (427 m of fall per degree). Energy
  conservation — now the deepest law — was assembled LAST of the
  classical laws, from the wreckage of the fluid (mirror).
- Atoms doubted until counted: discrete kinds explained chemistry
  for a century while eminent physicists still called atoms a
  bookkeeping fiction. Pollen grains jittering in water — atoms'
  drumming made visible — ended the doubt only when the jitter
  was computed and COUNTED out to 6×10²³.
- Light's double answer: waves (two slits, 1801 — the adding
  stripes) and lumps (1905 — light knocking electrons loose only
  in fixed quanta). Both measured beyond dispute; both kept.
  Worth is applicability to the query — the founding case.
- The two clouds: with physics declared nearly finished (~1900),
  two small leftovers — a failed drift measurement and a broken
  glow formula — became relativity and the quantum. The boast is
  filed in the mirror; the lesson is filed everywhere.
- The receipt of E = mc²: a formula on paper in 1905, a city gone
  in 1945. Physics learned its ledger binds the world, and the
  world learned to fund physics — culture's door, both ways.

## RELATIONS
First-class connections — the cascade's paths outward. Each names
what runs along it.

- MATHEMATICS: the language the laws are written in — calculus
  was invented FOR motion; the square, the inverse square, the
  exponential and the wave equation carry every SCIENCE entry
  above. What runs along: formulas outbound, rigor inbound.
- CHEMISTRY: physics hands over the atom's outer electrons, and
  the whole periodic table falls out of the electron rules;
  chemistry hands back the battery, materials, and every reaction
  heat that calorimetry meters.
- COOKING: heat's three roads, the boiling ceiling under
  pressure, phase-change tolls, gas expansion in leavening —
  physics's kitchen branch, restated there in kitchen terms as
  the model orders.
- ENGINEERING: every claim above made load-bearing — bridge,
  turbine, chip, and grid; engineering pays back the instruments
  physics thinks with. The busiest two-way road in the fabric.
- WEATHER: air and water as fluids run on this phylum's pressure,
  convection, and phase tolls — rain repays evaporation's
  ~2,450,000 J/kg (the toll at sea and skin temperature; the
  2,257,000 in SCIENCE is the same toll at the boil — weather
  holds both branches), wind falls down pressure hills, and the
  forecast's wall stands in the mirror (turbulence).
- LIFE: sunlight caught at ~1,000 W/m² peak funds every food
  chain; diffusion, buoyancy, and the square-cube law size every
  creature — why the whale needs water and the insect fears rain.
- BODY: muscle is an engine (~20% efficient, the rest is heat —
  hence sweating); nerves run on charge steps; the eye is a lens,
  the ear a pressure-wave reader; the radiating skin's 135 W is
  computed in SCIENCE.
- MEDICINE: X-rays see with short waves, radiation doses follow
  half-life arithmetic, ultrasound is sonar turned inward,
  magnetic imaging reads the body's own nuclei — every deep
  diagnostic is applied physics.
- FARMING: the sun's square-meter budget caps every field; frost
  physics (the clear-sky glow loss computed in SCIENCE) kills or
  spares harvests; water's tolls set irrigation's arithmetic.
- ECONOMY: energy is the input under every input; the
  kilowatt-hour (3,600,000 J) is physics sold retail; efficiency
  ceilings are thermodynamic law, not market failures — no
  subsidy repeals the 40%.
- CULTURE: the clock ordered the day, the lamp abolished the
  dark, the bomb shadowed a century, the satellite shrank the
  planet; physics's picture of one lawful universe is itself a
  cultural inheritance.
- ANIMALS: scaling laws rule body plans; the flea and the elephant obey one equation.
- ASTRONOMY: the sky is physics' oldest laboratory and hardest test bench.
- BUILDING: statics is physics standing still; every wall balances forces.
- COMPUTING: the transistor and the bit's energy floor — computing is physics constrained.
- CONFLICT: ballistics and blast are physics weaponized.
- CRAFT: heat treatment and material stress — craft obeys what it never wrote down.
- DESIGN: light, contrast, and material behavior bound what design can do.
- EARTH: plate motion, quakes, and the planet's heat engine.
- ELECTRONICS: charge in matter is physics' gift to circuits.
- FLIGHT: lift and drag are fluid physics earning wages.
- MEASUREMENT: units and constants — measurement is physics' contract with the world.
- THE MIND: the brain runs on charge and chemistry within physics' budget.
- MOVEMENT: momentum and levers price every throw and jump.
- MUSIC: the vibrating string wrote the first physics with numbers.
- PHILOSOPHY: what physical law IS — description or decree — is philosophy's question.
- PLANTS: water columns and light capture — the plant is applied physics.
- STORY: the camera's optics and the screen's illusion of motion.
- SURVIVAL: heat loss arithmetic is survival's core physics.
- TRANSPORT: friction, momentum, and drag price all motion.
- UPKEEP: entropy is why upkeep exists — disorder wins unpaid.
