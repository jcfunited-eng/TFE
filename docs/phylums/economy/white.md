# ECONOMY — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/economy/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting
for its drain. Seed authored by Claude and marked as seed.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- PERPETUAL YIELD WITHOUT INPUT — the mirror of physics'
  perpetual motion: "income forever from nothing." CANNOT BE
  under conditions: payouts exceeding what production brings in,
  no outside inflow. Every machine claiming it is the same
  machine in a new coat: early claims paid from late arrivals, so
  arrivals must double on schedule — at a 20%-a-month promise the
  base doubles every ~3.8 months, 33 doublings pass 8.6 billion,
  one mark to the whole species inside ~11 years — and it always
  dies far sooner, because recruits are not the species. Would
  drain on: a real inflow found — which renames it production and
  moves it to the color.
- MONEY NO ONE CAN DILUTE AND NO ONE MUST GUARD — attempted for
  centuries: metal dilutes only at the mine's pace but every
  metal tie snapped under war finance (1914, 1933, 1971);
  fixed-count ledgers lock dilution out by arithmetic, yet the
  ticket's worth still swung with belief. Half-drained: dilution
  can be fenced; keeper-free STEADINESS is not written anywhere.
  Would drain on: a mechanism holding the agreement itself steady
  with no keeper — none is known.
- THE JUST PRICE — one number asked to do two jobs: report
  scarcity and match desert. Centuries of doctrine and decree;
  the jobs part company whenever what is scarce is also unfairly
  held. Half-drained where practiced: let price keep its
  reporting job and do the justice by transfers beside it, never
  through it. The single fair-and-true number remains white.
- A MEASURE OF ENOUGH — the national count adds what is sold and
  is read as welfare; a nursed child, a clean river, a quiet
  night count zero in it. Adjusted indexes grip partly and agree
  poorly. Would drain on: a well-being count two rival offices
  compute to the same number.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "A DECREED PRICE IS A PRICE" — tried at every scale of force.
  Receipts: the Roman maximum-price edict of 301 (~a thousand
  goods capped, death penalty attached — goods left the markets;
  the edict was ignored, then abandoned); the 1970s fuel caps
  (shortage arriving as lines paid in hours); rent caps (offered
  flats shrink, upkeep decays, insiders gain, searchers pay in
  years). WHY, in the color's worked market: cap 10 against a
  crossing of 20 → 80 wanted, 30 brought, 50 short — and the
  missing 10 is still paid, in queue-time, favors, or the black
  market's premium. Certified cannot UNDER CONDITIONS: cap below
  the crossing, sellers free to walk, supply free to shrink.
  Recomputes when conditions move: wartime cards WITH enforced
  supply did clear — a different machine, not a decree alone.
- "A PUBLIC METHOD CAN PREDICT THE MARKET" — the standing cannot,
  with its arithmetic. If a published rule says today's 100 will
  be 110 tomorrow, buyers act today, and today's price is bid to
  ~110 less one day's interest — about a cent and a half at 5% a
  year. The promised rise is spent by the promise: the prediction
  is eaten by its own publication. Certified UNDER CONDITIONS:
  method public, entry free, price free to move. Outside them
  there are real earnings — private facts, closed doors, being
  first — and each un-publics itself as it is copied, eroding to
  ordinary (the color's fading-profit claim seen from the
  winner's side). Would drain: only by breaking a condition;
  while all three hold, the cannot is structural — a man cannot
  lift himself by his own belt.
- "MORE TICKETS MAKE A RICHER TOWN" — held by rulers again and
  again. Receipts: the denarius thinned from ~95% silver toward
  ~5% across three centuries, prices climbing after it; 1923
  Germany (a loaf ~200 billion marks by November; the 1918
  saver's mark worth ~a trillionth of itself); 1946 Hungary
  (prices doubling ~every 15 hours — a week is 168 ÷ 15 ≈ 11.2
  doublings, ~2,300×); 2008 Zimbabwe (~25-hour doubling). WHY: the color's
  island arithmetic — tickets doubled, loaves flat, each ticket
  buys half — run nightly instead of once. Cannot-be under
  condition: goods not growing with the tickets. Drains exactly
  there: new money matched by new goods dilutes nothing — the
  matching is the whole difference between finance and
  counterfeit.
- "THIS TIME IS DIFFERENT" — the mania's password; every entry in
  the ledger below carried it, in those words or a costume of
  them. Never yet held; kept because it arrives fresh with every
  doubling.
- "THE FREE LUNCH EXISTS" — every audited one was a bill moved:
  to another party, a later year, or a commons no one billed.
  The near-misses that survive audit — trade's extra loaf, the
  factory's 240× — are gains of ARRANGEMENT: cooked lunches with
  cooks. Kept because queries arrive carrying it daily.
- UNSETTLED: WHY MANIAS RECUR DESPITE EVERY RECEIPT — receipts
  printed and public since 1637; 1720, 1929, 2000, and 2008
  arrived anyway. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility:
  while the doubling runs, every participant's own eyes
  contradict the warning (each seller so far really was paid),
  and the holders of the last crash's receipts leave the floor at
  about the rate new buyers arrive — the ledger outlives the
  memory but not the temptation; eyes beat ink. Would drain on: a
  forward predictor of the tipping point, tested on manias it had
  not seen; none is written.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT MONEY'S WORTH FINALLY RESTS ON — every form
  (salt, silver, paper, ledger) worked while enough hands agreed
  to take it next; no law of nature props the agreement up.
  Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: money is trust
  made countable, and trust has no conservation law; the
  hyperinflation ledger is its evaporation filmed at speed.
  Would drain on: the keeper-free steadiness named in THINGS.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A UNIT OF WANT — the subject's central quantity has no
  thermometer. Satisfaction has no scale two people share, so
  "the greatest good" can be argued but never computed. The
  largest named absence: the machinery prices what people DO and
  only infers what they feel.
- LACKING: A WORKING SCIENCE OF THE WHOLE AT ONCE — one crossing
  computes (the color holds it); all crossings at once do not.
  The forward record is the receipt: of ~150 recessions across
  the world in two recent decades, consensus forecasts a year
  ahead caught ~5. Would drain on: a method whose forward record
  beats that in public.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: COMPUTING ALL PRICES CENTRALLY — tried at
  civilization scale for most of a century: queues for bread
  beside warehouses of goods no one wanted. WHY: the numbers the
  plan needs (who wants what, at what forgone next-best) are
  CREATED by the trading the plan replaced — silencing the signal
  deleted the plan's own input. The famine ledger sits in HISTORY.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: STILL-WATER MATH IN A STORM — risk models
  assumed blows land independently (the pooling law's condition,
  borrowed as if free). In the crash the crowd moves as one:
  correlations set near zero went toward one in 1987 and 2008,
  exactly when the answer mattered. Filed: the independence
  condition is a condition, not a decoration; crowd feedback
  voids it precisely at the extremes.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
wings-on-arms entries, waiting for their planet.

- DECREEING PRICES AGAINST SCARCITY — the most re-tried failed
  method in the subject (receipts in CLAIMS). Each new try
  believes the last failed only by weak enforcement; Diocletian
  enforced with death, and the goods still walked. The drain —
  enforce supply, not just price — is written, and is a
  different, costlier machine.
- PRINTING AS REVENUE — works once, small, by surprise; repeated,
  prices learn the mint's pace and run ahead of it; the terminus
  is the hyperinflation ledger. Carried condition: dilution taxes
  whoever holds tickets longest (the pensioner) and FORGIVES
  whoever owes them — a saver-to-debtor transfer no vote
  approved, which is why indebted treasuries are its repeat
  customers.
- PEGGING A PRICE AGAINST THE TIDE — a standing promise to hold a
  crossing where the flows no longer cross; every reserve spent
  defending it feeds the other side. Receipts: the pound forced
  off its peg in one day of 1992, ~billions spent defending;
  Argentina, 2001. Condition: a peg holds while the promise sits
  near the true crossing — a smoothing tool, never a levee.
- FORBIDDING INTEREST OUTRIGHT — the usury bans: lending did not
  stop, it hid, and hiding's cost was added to the desperate
  borrower's rate — the ban taxed exactly whom it meant to
  shield. The want stands open and worthy; caps-with-daylight are
  its partial drain where written carefully.
- HOARDING METAL AS NATIONAL WEALTH — the treasure-fleet century:
  prices roughly tripled as the silver landed, the pile diluting
  itself by its own arrival, the goods forgone to fetch it the
  real bill. WHY: wealth is what tickets buy, never the tickets —
  the color's bookkeeping claim, learned at empire scale.
- SELLING THE FUTURE TWICE — the pyramid as method; filed apart
  from its THING because it returns in a new coat each decade,
  and the doubling arithmetic identifies it under any coat: any
  yield whose source is the next arrival IS this method, whatever
  the brochure says.
- BUYING BECAUSE IT ROSE — momentum ridden as if it were law:
  works until the turn, and the turn is not published (the
  standing cannot). Its record is the mania ledger's unwritten
  second column: who was holding when it stopped.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE CRYSTAL BALL — an instrument for tomorrow's price:
  cannot-be under the public-method conditions; every sold
  version either fails audit or, succeeding, un-publics itself
  and stops being a means anyone can buy.
- THE WANT-METER — not found; SCIENCE's first absence made
  concrete. Every proxy (money spent, hours queued, survey
  answers) measures behavior and calls it feeling.
- THE KEEPER-FREE STORE OF WORTH — not found; every written store
  (metal, paper, ledger, grain) needs a guard, a government, or a
  granary roof.
- AN AVERAGE THAT CANNOT MISLEAD — not found: one number hides a
  crowd's shape; the average rises while the middle falls
  whenever the top runs away. Partial drain, written: report the
  middle beside the mean and the spread beside both — three
  numbers where one was wanted.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- END SCARCITY ITSELF — pushed back, never off: every abundance
  so far bred new wants at roughly the pace of the plenty.
  UNSETTLED whether wants-grow-with-means is law or habit.
  Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: wants are
  comparative, measured against neighbors rather than against
  enough, which would make plenty unable to arrive by production
  alone. Would drain on: a society observed satisfied.
- MAKE THE FUTURE CERTAIN — it can pool, price, and split risk;
  it cannot remove it. The pooling law's own boundary is the
  cannot: what strikes everyone at once — flood, war, crash —
  cannot be insured from inside the group that shares it.
- PRICE THE PRICELESS — a grandmother's care, a species, clean
  air: shadow numbers grip poorly and offend twice — the number
  is wrong, AND pricing changes the thing (where blood was paid
  for, the gift of it thinned). Open, its danger documented.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- THE MANIA LEDGER — 1637, tulips: one bulb quoted at the price
  of a fine Amsterdam house. 1720, the South Sea: ~128 in
  January, ~1,000 by August, ~150 by December. 1929: the index
  from 381 to 41, −89%. 2000: 5,048 to ~1,100, −78%. 2008: the
  same shape run on houses. Each printed its receipts in full;
  each next one arrived anyway, carrying the password.
- THE HYPERINFLATION LEDGER — 1923 Germany, 1946 Hungary, 2008
  Zimbabwe (quantities in CLAIMS). Every entry began as
  "temporary finance" for a state that could not tax or borrow
  what it spent; none began as a plan to destroy the money.
- THE PLANNING FAMINES — the century's costliest applied-science
  break: harvests commanded, signals silenced, tens of millions
  dead — ~15-45 million in the largest single case, by differing
  counts. Filed with care and without ornament: the dead are the
  receipt, and the receipt is why every silence-the-signal entry
  above is written in this ink.
- TUITION PAID EVERY GENERATION — cooking's white holds "what
  cannot be written cannot be inherited." This subject holds the
  harder case: here it WAS written — printed, public, taught —
  and failed to transfer anyway, five manias running. The
  sharpened lesson: a receipt transfers only when the reader's
  own eyes are not watching a doubling contradict it. What would
  fix that is exactly the unsettled mania claim above.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, THEN REBUILT WITH A CONDITION: ECONOMY ↔ PHYSICS
  (borrowed stillness) — the crossing's math was borrowed from
  balanced forces and holds for still markets; it broke in storms
  (SCIENCE above). The link stands again carrying its tag: crowd
  feedback voids the stillness, and the borrowed math must state
  the weather it works in.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: MANIAS ↔ EPIDEMICS (life, medicine) —
  contagion curves fit past bubbles beautifully in hindsight and
  have predicted none forward. Dotted until it calls one it has
  not seen.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: WEATHER → PRICE, FORWARD — harvest weather
  moves prices, proven backward; chains predicting prices from
  forecasts decay at the weather phylum's own horizon and then
  strike the public-method cannot. Dotted.
- CASHED, FROM COOKING'S WHITE: cooking filed "fermentation's
  arithmetic ↔ money's compounding" as dotted, awaiting a query
  that cashes it. This phylum cashes it: the law is one law —
  2^(t ÷ doubling time) — and this color runs it on savings,
  debts, prices, and schemes with working numbers. The relation
  carries cargo both ways: the yeast clock and the interest clock
  are the same clock in different media.
