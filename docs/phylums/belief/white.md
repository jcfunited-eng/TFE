## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE PROOF THAT COMPELS — wanted for millennia: an argument
  carrying a neutral reader all the way to the divine. Attempted
  many times (ledger in CLAIMS); none compelled, each for a
  stated reason. Would drain on: premises a neutral reader
  already holds and steps that close.
- THE ARGUMENT THAT CONVERTS — belief installed by reasoning
  alone. The color's law stands against it: conversion runs along
  relationships; belief arrives after the taking-part. Would
  drain on: documented conversions with no relationship channel —
  ask when, not why, and count.
- THE SINCERITY METER — an instrument reading belief from
  outside. Structurally hard: every proxy is a costly signal, and
  costly signals measure cost paid, not belief held; the sincere
  and the well-trained pretender emit the same light. Would drain
  on: a channel the holder cannot school.
- A TYPE SYSTEM THAT SURVIVES BLINDING — a sorting of people that
  still describes them with flattery, vagueness, and the
  subject's cooperation removed. Every tried system (signs, birth
  order, blood types) fails the blind (receipts below); the
  appetite it feeds is real and stays hungry. Would drain on: one
  system matching blinded persons above chance under the
  written-first rule.
- THE FULL SECULAR BUNDLE — one thing carrying all the practice
  carried: calendar, congregation, grief script, account of what
  you are for, hour not for anything else. Nations, movements,
  teams, regimens each carry a part; none carries the whole.
  Open, with the parts list as the specification. Would drain
  on: one thing shown carrying every part at once, held for a
  generation.

## CLAIMS
What didn't hold — with why — and what is still unsettled.

THE TRIED PROOFS LEDGER — each in its strongest form, with why it
did not compel; certified only UNDER the stated conditions.

- FIRST CAUSE: everything needs a cause, so the chain needs a
  first. Leaked because the rule is suspended exactly at the
  conclusion, and a stopping point, if granted, arrives bare — no
  watching, wanting, or judging follows. Would drain on: a shown
  necessity that the exception land on a mind rather than a law.
- DESIGN: order this fine implies an orderer — the watch on the
  heath. The living examples moved out when selection showed fit
  produced without foresight. The remnant is the fine-tuned
  constants: narrow life-permitting windows, one universe
  observed, only survivors ever ask — no probability computable
  from a single draw. Unsettled, three ways open: many draws, a
  deeper law fixing the constants, or design. Would drain on: a
  derivation of the constants, or evidence of other draws.
- ONTOLOGICAL: the greatest conceivable being must exist, since
  existing is greater than not. Leaked because existence adds
  nothing to a concept — the perfect island rides the same rails
  — and no one was ever argued into worship by it. Would drain
  on: a shown reason existence perfects a being and not an
  island.
- MORAL: a shared code implies a lawgiver. The premise is real;
  the color holds the leak: the code recurs among people with
  none of the doctrine, so it points as readily at shared human
  problems. Would drain on: a piece of the code that shared
  human problems cannot reach.
- MIRACLE TESTIMONY: the wonder was witnessed. The color's odds
  law prices it — one 99-in-100 witness moves a millionth to a
  ten-thousandth, and retellings of a shared story are one
  witness wearing many faces. Would drain on: independence —
  separated witnesses, separated channels, priced apart.
- THE WAGER: infinite stakes make belief the only rational bet.
  Leaked three ways: rival gods claim the same bet in opposite
  directions; belief cannot be executed on command — the wager
  buys attendance, not conviction; and that calculated attendance
  is what gets rewarded is smuggled in unargued. Would drain on:
  one bet shown to stand alone, and a way to believe on purpose.
- EXPERIENCE: the deepest reports agree across traditions that
  never met — self gone, all one, certainty, wordlessness. Real,
  and it cuts both ways: the same machinery interrupted predicts
  the same report with no doctrine true. The ineffability clause
  is load-bearing — a report that cannot be examined cannot be
  argued with; the honesty and the immunity are both real. Would
  drain on: a report carrying something checkable the machinery
  could not have supplied.

TESTED AND FAILED — checkable claims that were checked.

- NATAL CHARTS DESCRIBE PERSONS: on the blind test the
  astrologers helped design, they matched charts to blinded
  profiles at one in three against chance of one in three, having
  predicted better than half. Time twins: ~2,000 born minutes
  apart, a hundred-plus measures, no likeness above chance. Birth
  month predicting profession: checked, failed. The color keeps
  what is real — the vocabulary service, the calendar; the sky
  reading persons is certified closed under every blind run yet.
- PROPHECY OF DATES — receipts, each obeying the color's
  strengthening law where its three conditions held:
  · Oct 22, 1844: tens of thousands waited, crops left standing;
    the failure was re-read together as an event in heaven — and
    the body grown from the re-reading now counts ~20 million.
  · Dec 21, 1954: the living-room flood group, observed from
    inside; publicity-shy before the failure, calling newspapers
    after — public, costly, together, belief loudened. The
    founding receipt.
  · 1975: the announced likely year passed; the count dipped a
    few parts in a hundred, then resumed climbing.
  · May 21, 2011: millions spent on ~5,000 billboards; the passed
    date re-read as a spiritual judgment pointing to Oct 21 — the
    exact move — and when THAT passed, the prophet recanted. The
    rare drain case: the joint re-reading withdrawn, the movement
    thinned.

UNSETTLED — filed with a possibility, per the answer rule.

- WHICH WAY BIG GODS AND BIG GROUPS CAUSE EACH OTHER — scaling
  attested, direction not. Possibility: they ratchet, each
  enabling the next size of the other. Would settle on: dated
  sequences where one moved first, across many cases.
- WHY SYNCHRONY BINDS — iron on effect, unsure on mechanism.
  Possibility: the prediction machinery treats what moves with
  you as you. Would settle on: a manipulation breaking the
  binding while keeping the motion.
- THE HOT HAND — the famous null rested on a counting flaw; the
  correction is not in doubt; corrected, small real streak
  effects appear in some settings, and belief in the hand is
  still far larger than any measured effect. Open on size and
  where.
- WHAT MEANING-HUNGER IS — the appetite every entry on both
  sheets feeds: for pattern, a name, a shape to a life,
  mattering. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: a mind
  is a device for reducing surprise, and a life is the largest
  thing it must compress; meaning may be the felt success of that
  compression — a life that fits a story is cheap to carry, and
  the hunger is the cost of carrying an uncompressed one. Would
  begin to settle on: measured relief tracking narrative FIT
  rather than content.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks; where applied science broke.

- LACKING: A PREDICTIVE THEORY OF WHICH MEANINGS TAKE — the slots
  recur everywhere (origin, code, costly demand, calendar,
  specialist, purity line, settlement after death, grief
  procedure); nothing written predicts which CONTENT wins a slot
  in a given place. The slot list is the color's; the filling
  rule is absent.
- LACKING: THE SINCERITY INSTRUMENT — every measure passes
  through behavior, and behavior is exactly what a signal economy
  optimizes (see THINGS).
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE SECULARIZATION FORECAST — belief was to
  fade with education and prosperity on a schedule; practice fell
  in some places, belief lagged it, elsewhere both grew. Adopt
  such forecasts at the speed they stop reversing.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: PRAYING AT A DISTANCE, TRIALED — the large
  heart-surgery trial: strangers' intercession moved nothing
  measured, and the told-they-are-prayed-for arm did slightly
  WORSE (~59 of 100 with complications against ~52). Possibility
  filed with the number: being told reads as being sicker. The
  color's line held — expectation moves what passes through the
  person, whichever way it points.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- ARGUING THE IDENTITY-BEARING BELIEF DOWN — fails in everyone.
  CONDITION: the belief carries people, history, and
  self-account; winning bills all three and no fact covers it.
  Drains only by the color's route: belonging first, a cheap
  exit, self-persuasion — never the frontal fact.
- CORRECTING THE PRACTICE AS IF IT WERE A CLAIM — the fast
  answered with physiology, the blessing with germs: the reply
  lands on a claim not being made and is heard as contempt.
- CONTEMPT AS A TOOL — mocking the believer converts the opinion
  into a badge of loyalty and hands the person a community of the
  also-sneered-at. Reliably strengthens what it aims to remove.
- BANNING BELIEF BY DECREE — suppressed traditions have run
  decades underground and emerged intact. CONDITION of failure:
  the practice can be kept small, indoors, and inherited — the
  household outlasts the decree; persecution also pays the
  costly-signal bill for free.
- BUYING MEANING — the hired mourner, the purchased title, the
  shortcut pilgrimage: empty under the color's law (meaning is
  stored cost; buying is the avoidance of the cost). The only
  drain would be one bought instance arriving full — none on
  record; this may be a true cannot.
- FIXING BOTH TEXT AND READING — snaps at the first serious
  collision with a changed world. MOVING BOTH — dissolves what
  there was to be loyal to. Receipts across traditions; the
  color's two-layer method is the only survivor.
- NAMING THE DATE — the falsifiable move that keeps being made;
  the ledger shows both outcomes: public + costly + joint, the
  failure binds; recanted, it drains. A tradition that wants to
  live avoids dates — and some part of the wanting keeps writing
  them anyway.
- THE DEBUNKER'S SHORTCUT — learning the list of errors and
  producing the feel of having seen through, without the work.
  Debunking is itself a satisfying pattern-story (hidden cause,
  fooled majority, a knower), and the click of understanding does
  not check which side it serves. CONDITION: no written-first
  criterion — the sceptic's goalposts move in the dark exactly
  like the believer's.
- REMOVING THE BELIEF, LEAVING THE JOB — rarely holds: the job
  (comfort, vocabulary, routine, explanation, group) stands
  unfilled and rehires.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- NOT YET FOUND: A STAKE BOTH SIDES ACCEPT — fair tests exist and
  are rarely sat for; the believer's imagined downside has no
  ceiling ("never risk it"), so the test is never run and the
  belief is protected by never being examined. Would drain on: a
  wager whose loss the belief itself prices as bearable.
- FELL SHORT: THE SECULAR GRIEF SCRIPT — pieces exist (ceremony,
  counselor, anniversary), but no widely-known script tells a
  neighbor what to do on the third day. The known-thing-to-do is
  the part most missed when a tradition goes, and least rebuilt.
  Would drain on: a script neighbors know before they need it.
- FELL SHORT: THE BELONGING SUBSTITUTES — the team carries the
  calendar and the chant but not the bedside; the movement the
  moral frame but burns its members; the regimen the discipline
  but meets no one. Filed with which parts each carries — the
  specification for the THINGS entry still open.
- FELL SHORT: "IT CAN'T HURT" AS A PRICE TAG — true only while
  the practice displaces nothing; false the moment it takes the
  place of a treatment, a check, a decision. Where time decides
  the outcome, the months on the inert remedy are the whole harm
  and cannot be got back.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- ANSWER SUFFERING AT ITS SCALE — the hardest standing problem
  for any benevolent design: not the stubbed toe but the child's
  cancer, the earthquake, the century. Every answer buys relief
  by weakening another part — freedom (does not cover
  earthquakes), the greater good (the sufferer was not asked),
  the test (makes the tester the author), the mystery (ends the
  conversation with the problem). None settles it, and the
  difficulty is internal to the account, not imported by critics.
  What traditions DO at the bedside is in the color, and is not
  an argument.
- SETTLE THE UNSETTLEABLE ACCOUNTS IN THIS LIFE — the demand the
  afterlife answers exists whether or not the answer does, and
  does not leave when the belief does.
- MAKE DEATH SURVIVABLE WITHOUT STORY — the color's scripts all
  ride on narrative and witness; whether the bare fact can be
  carried without a story is open — probably the meaning-hunger
  question wearing black.
- GIVE THE LEAVER THE FUNCTIONS — the person out of a tradition
  still needs the calendar, the noticing neighbors, the grief
  script, the hour; serving them without the doctrine they left
  is this phylum's open engineering want.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side.

- The proofs' long trying: each argument in the ledger was
  sharpened for centuries by the best minds of its age, and the
  failures were productive — the tools forged to test them
  (necessity, evidence, probability, what a claim forbids) became
  logic's and measurement's working kit. Other phylums' color was
  paid for on this sheet.
- The dates ledger as a century of natural experiment: 1844,
  1954, 1975, 2011 — the same move run under varying conditions
  until the conditions became the knowledge (public + costly +
  together = stronger; recanted = drained). Nobody designed the
  series; the white collected it.
- The trials of prayer and charts: rare cases where believer and
  tester agreed on a design before the result — and the agreement
  did not survive the result. Lesson filed: the written-first
  criterion binds only those who stay bound; write it, sign it,
  publish it.
- The pigeons and the eyes: the turn in which belief's machinery
  became a measured subject — rituals invented by birds on a
  timer, honesty near-tripled by painted eyes, one flattering
  sketch rated personal by a whole class. The color's receipts
  were minted here, by tries.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, THEN REBUILT HONESTLY: BELIEF ↔ MEASUREMENT — failed
  both ways for centuries (tests refused; tests scored by
  partisans). The standing link now carries its own rule as
  cargo: criterion written first, every attempt counted, the
  scorer blinded — because the scorer is the mechanism.
- BROKEN IN HALF: BELIEF ↔ ASTRONOMY — the calendar half holds;
  the persons half failed every blind test and is certified
  closed under those conditions. The relation stands, carrying
  only its true half.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: BELIEF ↔ BODY — long practice travels with
  measured health differences; belonging, diet, and habit ride
  the same carriage and no one has cleanly unloaded them. Dotted
  until a design separates the passengers.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: BELIEF ↔ GOVERNANCE — whether a civil
  frame can carry the full bundle without a doctrine is the
  THINGS entry still open; every historical try is partial.
  Dotted, awaiting its case.
