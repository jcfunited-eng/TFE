## THINGS
The units this subject is about; other homes named so a cascade
can branch.

- RITUAL — a group moving as one: same words, same instant, same
  motions. The synchrony itself does the binding, beneath any
  doctrine; also: music (the choir is the mechanism bare),
  conflict (drill), play (the chant).
- THE SACRED — the marked-off untouchable: a day, place, name, or
  object fenced from ordinary use. The fence, not the thing,
  makes it sacred; also: law, people, culture.
- COST — the currency meaning is stored in: time, wealth,
  comfort, the best of the flock, given up visibly. A free signal
  carries no information; also: economy (the guarantee), life
  (the peacock's tail, honest because heavy).
- STORY — meaning's shipping container: a garden, a flood, an
  exodus carried three thousand years while a list dies in a
  week; also: story, mind (shapes stored, lists not), teaching.
- THE EXEMPLAR — moral teaching as a person, not a proposition:
  the man who gave everything, the woman who would not bow — a
  character carries the exceptions no rule can state; also:
  teaching, story, play (the copied player).
- RITES OF PASSAGE — birth, adulthood, marriage, death: a slow
  invisible change turned into one dated, witnessed moment; also:
  people (a status is real only when others hold it), law.
- THE CALENDAR WHEEL — festivals on a fixed weekly and yearly
  wheel, bending time from a line into a circle a community can
  stand on; also: astronomy (the first shared clock), farming
  (the harvest made the dates matter).
- THE WATCHING WITNESS — an unseen observer keeping accounts,
  following you where nobody knows your name; also: governance
  (surveillance made literal and expensive), law (courts as the
  secular replacement), mind (conscience).
- THE TEXT AND ITS READING — words written late and under
  pressure, argued into a canon whose choosing disappears into
  the binding, then buried under commentary until what a believer
  follows is the reading. Seed and tree; also: language, law (a
  constitution and its decisions), teaching (a syllabus is a
  canon with a timetable).
- THE CONGREGATION AND THE HOUSEHOLD — the transmission engines:
  the community whose noticing and exclusion are the sanction,
  and the household that installs practice before it is examined;
  also: people, language (mother tongues spread the same way).
- THE SPECIALIST — the full-time religious class surplus makes
  possible, acquiring a class's interests: income, succession,
  buildings, a monopoly on the rites; also: medicine, law,
  governance.
- PRAYER — five actions, not one: asking, thanking, praising,
  confessing, sitting silent; only the first is what outsiders
  mean; also: mind (a thought becomes inspectable when it leaves
  the head), medicine (structured attention, measured).
- THE CHARM, THE OMEN, THE TYPE SYSTEM — anxiety given a location
  and a thing to check; the read coincidence and streak, from a
  detector tuned to fire early (SCIENCE prices it); the recurring
  machines sorting people into a few named kinds, handing you a
  name for what you had already noticed; also: mind, mathematics,
  language.

## CLAIMS
What holds, written to be tested by a crossing query.

- Practice is what a tradition transmits; doctrine is what gets
  said about it. Ask a member and you get beliefs; watch a year
  and you get a diet, a calendar, gestures, a way of naming
  children and burying parents.
- Participation comes first; belief arrives out of the taking
  part. So conversion runs along relationships — a spouse, a
  friend who kept showing up — almost never along arguments: ask
  converts why and you get reasons; ask when and you get a person.
- Religions are inherited far more than chosen: births,
  child-rearing, and intermarriage move the numbers more than
  every doctrinal argument ever made (compounding in SCIENCE).
- Costly demands sort: rules expensive for the half-committed and
  cheap for the committed filter membership without anyone
  judging. The groups that ask most last longest (receipt in
  SCIENCE).
- Membership is worn where it cannot be taken off — dress, marks,
  a diet met three times a day. Frequency is the mechanism: a law
  met at every meal cannot be forgotten or kept private, and it
  draws the boundary on the plate.
- The sacred runs on one-way contact rules: the clean touching
  the unclean becomes unclean, never the reverse — hence the
  washing, the vessels, who may touch what. The same intuition
  runs in people who reject the belief (the refused bedpan-bowl,
  the disliked man's sweater).
- Gods scaled with the group: small societies keep local spirits
  indifferent to strangers; large ones carry watching gods who
  keep accounts — past the size where everyone knows everyone,
  reputation stops policing and something must take over
  (direction of cause unsettled; mirror holds it).
- Being watched changes behavior whether or not anyone watches:
  eyes painted over an honesty box near-tripled its takings. A
  believed enforcer costs nothing — the cheapest law enforcement
  any society has run.
- What religion adds to morality is mostly not content — the
  virtue list recurs among people holding none of the doctrine.
  It adds audience, schedule, memory, penalties, and a community
  that notices: the enforcement, not the code.
- An unchangeable text plus a movable reading is the only
  arrangement that survives a change of world: fix both layers
  and it snaps; move both and there is nothing to be loyal to.
- Interpretation produces hierarchy from necessity: a reading
  with no decider produces as many readings as readers — a
  schism. Splits are normal; every large tradition is a family of
  them. The harshest treatment goes to the nearest neighbor: the
  cousin with a different reading threatens the claim to be the
  true one; the stranger's god threatens nothing.
- The reformer's move is always "back to the source," and the
  result is always new — the original is unrecoverable, so what
  gets built is a modern reading made in a modern argument.
  Prophets come from the edge; the center has offices and incomes
  and produces administrators.
- Traditions absorb local practice everywhere they go — the
  festival on the old date, the shrine reused — and deny it. The
  absorbing is how a tradition arrives somewhere and stays.
- Faith and evidence play different games: "I believe in you" is
  a commitment, not a forecast. Some statements are flags, not
  claims — and the costlier a statement is to hold, the better it
  flags.
- A failed prophecy can leave a group stronger. CONDITIONS, all
  three: public, costly, faced together — then reinterpretation
  is cheaper than retreat, and the joint re-reading binds. Absent
  any condition, belief thins (dated receipts in the mirror,
  including the recant case).
- Arguing down a belief that carries identity fails in everyone —
  winning would cost the holder their people, their history, and
  their account of who they are; no fact covers that bill. When
  people do leave, belonging goes first — a move, a marriage, a
  falling-out — and belief thins afterwards, over years; the
  reasons given later are real and almost never what started it.
- Meaning resists purchase: it is stored cost, so the bought
  version arrives empty — the hired mourner, the purchased title,
  the trophy without the season.
- Ritual under uncertainty scales with the uncertainty: deep-sea
  fishing thick with observances, lagoon fishing bare; batters
  (fail seven in ten) full of routines, fielders few. It delivers
  a controllable action where there is none — worth something
  measurable: steadier hands, more attempts.
- Belief changes outcomes through the believer, both directions:
  the self-declared lucky enter more, persist, notice more offers
  — inaccurate belief, measurably better off. And told a
  treatment causes nausea, people get nauseous; told a thing is
  dangerous, they report pain from a harmless procedure.
- A vague description cannot miss, and the width is felt as
  depth: the specificity in a reading was supplied by the reader,
  from their own store, too fast to notice. Flattery is waved
  through; one well-placed criticism buys credibility for the
  rest.
- Misses leave no mark — a wrong prediction with no stake gets no
  entry — so the record in a head is a record of hits. A fair
  record counts every attempt (the tally law in SCIENCE).
- A practice working does not validate its theory: willow bark
  relieves pain and the humours do not exist; quarantine works
  and had nothing to do with bad air. Separating practice from
  explanation is the most useful move in this subject.
- An unfalsifiable claim and a false one are different animals: a
  claim no test can reach can never become knowledge; a claim
  that forbids something can be checked — and sometimes has been,
  and failed. Likewise a stance ("that research leans" —
  checkable, often right) is not a story with named parts that
  must each carry weight.
- A belief is usually doing a job — comfort at a diagnosis, a
  vocabulary for character, a routine before the frightening
  thing, an explanation for the bad years, a group that meets.
  Remove the belief without noticing the job and the removal
  rarely holds.

## SCIENCE
The math and working knowledge of belief, held — not mentioned.
LAW to compute with, RULE to follow, WORKED run to a real answer.

### MATHEMATICS — weighing a report of the extraordinary

LAW: posterior odds = prior odds × (chance of the report if true
  ÷ chance of the report if false).
RULE: price how often such a witness reports wrongly (error,
  deception, retelling drift); divide honesty by that; multiply
  onto the prior odds.
WORKED: event priced at 1 in 1,000,000; witness right 99 in 100.
  Ratio = 0.99 ÷ 0.01 = 99. Posterior odds = 99 ÷ 1,000,000 ≈
  1 in 10,100 — moved nearly a hundredfold, still one in ten
  thousand. TWO independent witnesses: 99 × 99 = 9,801 → ~1 in
  100. THREE: 99³ = 970,299 → odds 0.97 ≈ 49%. The law states
  what would compel: independence — which retellings of one
  shared story never have; a passed-along story is one witness
  wearing many faces.

### MATHEMATICS — coincidence, priced

LAW: amazement rate = chances per person × people × odds per
  chance. Rare times many equals often.
RULE: price the field of possible hits BEFORE one is picked out;
  the field is never specified in advance, so odds computed after
  the pick are theater.
WORKED: a one-in-a-million-per-day event, 300,000,000 people =
  300 stunned households daily, by arithmetic alone.
WORKED: 23 people; pairs = 23 × 22 ÷ 2 = 253; chance of NO
  shared birthday ≈ (364/365)²⁵³ ≈ 0.50 — a coin flip that two
  match, in a room that would swear the odds were tiny.

### MATHEMATICS — how long a secret lives

LAW: P(held) = p^(n × t) — p each keeper's per-year silence, n
  keepers, t years.
RULE: the size test — count required keepers, multiply by years,
  raise silence to that power; then ask what paperwork had to
  exist.
WORKED: 100 keepers at 99% silence: 0.99¹⁰⁰ ≈ 0.37 after one
  year — likelier blown than held. Ten years: 0.99¹⁰⁰⁰ ≈
  0.00004, about 1 in 23,000. Small, short, few-handed
  conspiracies are common; enormous permanent ones are priced out
  by their own multiplication — and real exposures match the law:
  insiders who talk, an accountant, a defector, documents kept
  for other purposes. Inside the paperwork, every time.

### MATHEMATICS — how a tradition actually grows

LAW: transmission compounds. N = N₀ × (1 + r)^t.
RULE: look for the steady per-generation rate (births, retention,
  intermarriage) before the dramatic event; a mechanism operating
  on everyone born beats one operating on the few persuaded.
WORKED: 40% per decade — documented for one ancient movement's
  first centuries — over 26 decades: 1.4²⁶ ≈ 6,300×. A thousand
  members become ~6.3 million with no mass miracle: per year just
  1.034 — a friend bringing a friend, children kept. Birth over
  argument: a 1% yearly edge is 1.01¹⁰⁰ ≈ 2.7× per century; 2%
  is 1.02¹⁰⁰ ≈ 7.2×.

### MATHEMATICS — runs, and the middle they return to

LAW: chance has no memory; the long run arrives by dilution,
  never correction. After 5 heads, the next 995 fair flips
  average 497.5: (5 + 497.5) ÷ 1,000 = 50.25% — the run is
  drowned, never repaid. "Owed a tail" is a true fact about long
  runs applied where it does not reach.
LAW: extremes are followed by draws nearer the middle, no cause
  needed — and people seek help at their worst.
RULE: to judge any "it worked" — ask what would have happened
  anyway; only a comparison group can see that.
WORKED: pain wobbling 3-9 around a middle of 6; the shrine is
  booked on a 9-day; the average next day is ~6 — a 3-point
  improvement bought by arithmetic. Stack the rest — conditions
  that come and go, attention, expectation, the non-improved who
  leave uncounted — and a shrine compiles a genuine record of
  recoveries with nothing above doing anything. The same stack
  scores most remedies judged without a comparison.

### THE DETECTOR'S PRICE LIST — why the pattern appetite runs high

LAW: the best detector fires when probability × cost-of-miss
  exceeds cost-of-false-alarm: fire at p > C_alarm ÷ C_miss.
WORKED: wind versus predator. A false alarm costs one sprint —
  call it 1; a miss costs everything — 10,000. Fire at any hunch
  above 1 in 10,000; a hunch at 1 in 1,000 clears the bar
  tenfold. A mind wrong 999 times in 1,000 is running its numbers
  CORRECTLY at those prices. Faces in clouds, meaning in
  coincidence, an author behind every big event — a setting, not
  a defect; the modern world changed the prices, not the machine.
- Corollary: a big effect feels owed a big cause, and an intended
  cause arrives faster than an accidental one — intention-reading
  is the fastest thing a social mind does, and it fires on
  markets, institutions, and weather, where there is nobody to
  read.

### THE SIGNAL ARITHMETIC — cost as information

LAW: a signal sorts only while faking costs more than faking
  pays: cost(pretender) − cost(committed) > gain from pretending.
RULE: to read a strange demand — compute who it is expensive for:
  hours, diet, dress, tithe are near-free for the committed (much
  would be given anyway), full-rate for the half-committed.
WORKED: 10 hours a week = 520 hours a year — near a quarter of a
  working year, billed in full only to the free-rider, who
  leaves. RECEIPT: on the 19th-century commune ledger, religious
  communes outlived secular roughly fourfold year by year, and
  among the religious, costlier demands meant longer life (~);
  among secular communes the correlation vanishes — cost without
  a shared frame sorts nothing.
- Token substitution, same law: firstborn → ram → coin → candle,
  and the act stays full — what was needed was a MARKED cost, not
  that particular one; the community sets the price and lowers it
  as it can afford to.

### THE TALLY DISCIPLINE — what a record is worth

LAW: worth = hits ÷ (hits + misses), against the base rate of
  chance hits. A tally recording only hits measures memory.
WORKED: a reader makes 40 statements; 10 land; felt record 10 for
  10; true record 10 ÷ 40 = 25% — below the ~30% that universal
  material hits unaided (nearly everyone has lost an older
  relative, has a scar, keeps a photograph). Worse than guessing,
  experienced as marvelous.
RECEIPT: hand a class one broad, flattering personality sketch;
  ask each to rate its personal accuracy: 39 students, average
  4.3 of 5 — the founding measurement of the description that
  cannot miss.
RULE (the fair test, whole): state the claim so a result could go
  against it; fix what counts as a hit BEFORE anything is drawn;
  blind the judge; count every attempt including the unwanted.
  The written-first criterion binds BOTH sides — the sceptic's
  goalposts move too (the fluke, the flaw found after the fact in
  a design already accepted). Whoever scores the result is the
  mechanism, whichever answer they hoped for.

### THE BODY UNDER BELIEF — the placebo's exact reach

LAW: expectation moves what is reported and felt — pain, nausea,
  fatigue, itch, function — and does not move what is counted
  without the patient: a tumour's size, a bone's knitting, a
  virus's load.
RULE: to credit a remedy — demand an effect on something measured
  without the patient. To design care — spend the real effects
  (attention, explanation, a plan with steps, an unhurried hour)
  on purpose; they are the components under most pressure to cut.
RECEIPTS: the placebo works even told-as-placebo — given openly
  it still moves reported symptoms, so the effect lives in the
  ritual of treatment, not the deception; placebo arms report the
  side effects they were warned of. And the learning machinery is
  bodied: pigeons fed on a blind timer invented turning, bobbing,
  wing-raising — 6 of 8 birds, each its own ritual — reward
  crediting whatever preceded it, link unchecked; people run the
  same rule, then supply a reason.

## METHODS
How belief is built, carried, and honestly tested — followable.

- BINDING BY SYNCHRONY: put the group in one rhythm — same words,
  same instant, same motions, ideally sung. Breaks when done
  alone or unsynchronized — content without timing binds nothing.
- FENCING THE SACRED: mark the thing out of ordinary use, publish
  the contact rules, make the refusals visible and shared. Breaks
  when a crossing costs nothing — an unpunished insult is
  information about the group's strength, which is what blasphemy
  rules actually guard.
- INSTALLING A CODE BY FREQUENCY: attach the law to what happens
  three times a day; the plate beats the pulpit. Breaks when the
  practice can be private — a hidden observance sorts nobody.
- SORTING BY COST: set demands expensive for the half-committed
  (arithmetic above). Breaks when the demand outprices even the
  devoted, or the shared frame is gone.
- PACKAGING IN STORY AND EXEMPLAR: ship the ethic as a person
  choosing under pressure; the origin as a tale landing on
  obligation — origins answer why, not how, and are precise about
  what is now owed. Ship for the medium the audience has:
  windows, songs, processions, a building's shape — the text is
  the specialists' copy of a thing that mostly travels without it.
- SURVIVING THE FOUNDER: convert presence into offices,
  procedures, and a text before the eyewitnesses thin. The
  writing is an emergency act — why texts so often contain the
  disagreements they were written to settle. Breaks when nothing
  inheritable replaces the person.
- FIXING THE TEXT, MOVING THE READING: amendment near impossible,
  interpretation always possible; commentary absorbs the changing
  world; a decider appointed or the split accepted. Breaks both
  ways: both layers fixed snaps; both loose dissolves.
- ABSORBING THE LOCAL: keep the festival on the old date, reuse
  the shrine, admit the local spirit as a minor figure — meeting
  newcomers partway is cheaper than teaching from zero.
- SCRIPTING GRIEF: give the unbearable a shape and a schedule — a
  body to sit with, days counted, tasks assigned, an end; the
  structure holds when feeling cannot. At the bedside: presence,
  a script for when nothing can be said, a task for the hands,
  the promise of not-alone — no argument is in use in that room.
- CONFESSION MACHINERY: make admission survivable — bounded
  audience, script, fixed price, and an END; a wrong with no
  closing procedure is carried indefinitely.
- PILGRIMAGE: a status ceremony with a long approach — costs
  weeks and money (sorting), places the traveler among strangers
  sharing only this (belonging), returns them visibly changed to
  witnesses at home (passage) — none of it needing the
  destination to do anything.
- ASKING ALOUD: force the want into a sentence (its first shape),
  name who is responsible, convert helpless waiting into an act.
  Works on the asker whatever else it does.
- GIVING THE PERFORMER A RITUAL: before an uncontrollable task,
  assign a controllable action — steadier hands, more attempts.
  The target is the performer, not the world; a checklist is the
  same form aimed at a target it does touch.
- COLD READING (filed as what it is): open wide and watch — broad
  statements, read the face for the flicker, keep what lands,
  narrow; rescue misses with "does that mean anything to you?" so
  the sitter builds the hit and files it as one. Side channels:
  universal material (the base rates of a human life) and prior
  research (the queue, the clothes, the obituary page). Many
  practitioners are sincere — the skill trains below words, felt
  as a strengthening gift. Real, followable knowledge; what it is
  evidence OF is settled: the sitter.
- TESTING A CLAIM FAIRLY: the tally rule plus designs that close
  the person-channel — charts matched blind and above chance;
  charms judged where the holder cannot act on the belief;
  remedies moving something measured without the patient. Each
  test names the channel it closes, because each system's real
  mechanism runs through the person.
- MEETING A BELIEF WITHOUT CONTEMPT: find the job the belief does
  before touching it; answer the practice as practice (the fast
  is not a physiology claim — correcting a claim not being made
  lands as contempt and hardens); keep a cheap exit open; replace
  the job if you remove the worker. The mirror holds every way
  this is done wrong.

## MEANS
What the methods run on.

- THE SKY AND ITS CALENDAR: the first shared clock, genuinely
  governing planting, flooding, migration — why looking up was a
  reasonable ancient move, and why the dates still turn the
  festival wheel.
- THE BUILDING, THE IMAGE, THE SONG: transmission for audiences
  without letters — a cathedral's windows as the book for those
  who had none, doctrine memorized because it rhymed. Most
  believers in most centuries could not read; the tradition
  reached them anyway.
- THE TEXT, THE CANON, THE COMMENTARY SHELF: the preserved words,
  the argued edge of what counts, the ever-growing reading.
  Translation is the recurring crisis: sound, rhythm, ambiguity,
  and centuries of reading cannot all survive the crossing, so
  every translation is decisions presented as a copy — and each
  has produced a fight, sometimes a war.
- THE HOUSEHOLD AND THE CONGREGATION: practice installed before
  it is examined; audience, schedule, and noticing supplied
  weekly — a service is spaced repetition.
- MARKS, DRESS, AND DIET: membership continuous and visible —
  recognized by strangers, watched by one's own, unable to slip
  out between Tuesday and Thursday.
- THE CHARM AND THE TOKEN: anxiety given a location; cost given a
  bearer — cheap, portable, honest about their channel: the
  effect travels through the carrier.
- THE HONEST INSTRUMENTS: the written-first criterion, the blind
  judge, the comparison group, the count of every attempt — built
  to defeat the scorer's own eye, including a sceptical one,
  because in this subject the scorer is always the mechanism.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject answers — the doors a query enters by.

- HOLD THE GROUP PAST REPUTATION'S REACH: bind strangers into a
  people — the watching witness, the worn marks, the shared
  calendar, the costly gate.
- MAKE INVISIBLE TRANSITIONS VISIBLE: date and witness the slow
  changes of a life so no one is left unsure they happened.
- CARRY PEOPLE THROUGH MORTALITY: the grief script, the bedside
  practice, the settled account after death — the heaviest floor
  of the load-bearing wall.
- SETTLE ACCOUNTS NOTHING ON EARTH CAN SETTLE: the murderer who
  died comfortable, the child who suffered for nothing — every
  society produces the cases, and the demand that they be
  answered somewhere is among the most durable things about
  people.
- CONVERT HELPLESSNESS INTO ACTION: prayer at the bedside, the
  routine before the at-bat, the charm in the pocket.
- MAKE CHARACTER DISCUSSABLE: a vocabulary for someone's nature
  without accusing anyone — saying a friend is a certain sign
  says what could not be said directly. The service is real and
  has nothing to do with the sky.
- EXPLAIN THE RUN OF BAD YEARS: a shape for what otherwise has
  none. The comfort is not a weakness; people are bewildering.
- LICENSE THE WANTED CHOICE: the reading that says rest, leave,
  speak — an external voice converting a want into permission,
  costing less standing than owning the decision.

## HISTORY
How the knowledge arose, in what order, at what cost.

- The sky first: the calendar genuinely ruled planting and flood;
  extending it from seasons to persons was a natural next step
  for people with no way to test it — an old good idea never
  withdrawn. The test, when it came, is in the mirror.
- Local spirits, then watching gods, as groups outgrew the reach
  of reputation — belief scaling with the census, whichever way
  the cause runs.
- The founder dies, the institution is invented: presence into
  offices, sayings into texts written as eyewitnesses thinned,
  the canon argued for generations then read as one object. Then
  splits, reforms, absorptions: readings diverged with distance,
  no shared decider made them bodies, and reformers built new
  things from unrecoverable sources, believing them old.
- The functions were named by their persistence: where practice
  declined the needs did not, and nations, movements, teams,
  regimens were pressed into the slots. What is actually missed
  is rarely the doctrine: the singing together, the neighbors who
  notice an absence, the known thing to do when somebody dies,
  the hour a week not for anything else.
- Folk practice found real things without knowing why: honey on
  wounds, mould on infection, willow bark, citrus at sea,
  isolating the sick — trial with feedback surfacing effects no
  available theory predicted. Both lessons held: age measures
  usefulness-to-keepers, not truth; and a working practice does
  not validate its theory.
- Belief became a measured subject late: the class rating one
  sketch as personally theirs (1949), pigeons inventing rituals
  (1948), a doomsday living room observed from inside (1954),
  eyes over an honesty box (2006), the hot-hand count corrected
  (2010s) — the machinery of persistence finally weighed instead
  of sneered at. The receipts above date from this turn.

## RELATIONS
First-class connections; each names what runs along it.

- MIND: the load-bearing phylum — memory as reconstruction (the
  reading's specificity supplied by the sitter), the detector's
  price list, expectation shaping symptom, identity deciding
  which reasons get a hearing, the click of understanding
  arriving before understanding.
- PEOPLE: belonging as enforcement, sanction, and the reason
  argument fails; costly signals; the group defined by refusals;
  the standing of being one of the few who see.
- STORY: myth as compressed instruction, the exemplar, origins
  landing on obligation, the anecdote beating the rate because it
  ships in the stored format.
- LANGUAGE: sentences that do rather than report (the flag, the
  vow), vague words with no edges, translation's impossible
  crossing, the unlucky number born of a sound-alike word.
- LAW: canon and precedent, letter and spirit, the decider of
  readings, the plea bargain beside the confessional, exemption
  where two laws claim one person.
- GOVERNANCE: legitimacy from founding stories, surveillance as

- ETHICS: the shared virtue list religion carries but did not

- MEASUREMENT: the fair test, the written-first criterion, the
