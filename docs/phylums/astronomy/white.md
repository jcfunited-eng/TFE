# ASTRONOMY — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/astronomy/color.md: same parts,
opposite sign. Seed authored by Claude from

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or
cannot be under stated conditions.

- THE ETHER — the medium light was supposed to wave in, because
  every other wave has one. The earth orbits at 30 km/s, so
  light timed along the motion and across it had to differ by a
  computable amount; the instrument built to catch it (1887)
  could see far less than the expected effect — and found
  nothing, and repetitions tightened nothing toward zero.
  Certified CANNOT under the condition that defined it: light
  needs no carrying medium. The null became the floor a new
  physics was built on — a well-built zero is a discovery.
- VULCAN — a planet inside Mercury, invented for a real anomaly:
  Mercury's closest point drifts 43 arc-seconds per century more
  than the known planets account for, and the method had just
  triumphed (Neptune predicted from Uranus's wobbles, found
  within one degree of the penciled cross, 1846). Decades of
  searching, sightings claimed at the limit of seeing, no
  planet. Drained by new LAW, not new matter: general relativity
  computes the 43 arc-seconds exactly, nothing added. Half of a
  split verdict — see dark matter below.
- THE CRYSTAL SPHERES — the solid machinery the planets rode on.
  Killed by a comet (1577) measured, by its missing seasonal
  jump, to lie beyond the moon — sailing through where the
  spheres should stand. Certified cannot: the sky holds no
  scaffolding; gravity runs the worlds on nothing at all.
- THE MARTIAN CANALS — straight lines webbing Mars, seen,
  mapped, and named by careful observers for decades, read as
  irrigation and so as engineers. An artifact of eyes at the
  resolution limit — the mind supplies pattern where the
  instrument runs out, in good faith. Killed by better
  resolution: bigger telescopes, then visiting cameras, no lines
  at all. The standing warning for every instrument's edge: the
  last unit of resolution is where wishes live.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- GEOCENTRISM — the subject's longest-held wrong claim: ~1,400
  years from polished form to fall. WHY it held, honestly: the
  ground feels still, everything overhead visibly circles, and
  the killer objection to a moving earth — nearby stars should
  jump against far ones over a year, and none did — was CORRECT
  reasoning on good data, read wrong: the true answer (stars so
  far the jump is under an arc-second, a coin at four
  kilometers) was a distance nobody would grant. THE RECEIPTS,
  in order: Venus shows a full set of phases (1610), impossible
  if it circles between earth and sun; four moons orbit Jupiter
  (1610), so not everything circles the earth; the backward
  loops cost the old model an extra circle per planet and come
  free in the new one as overtaking; the stellar jump itself
  finally caught (1838) — the earth demonstrably moves. Lessons:
  a model that only ACCOMMODATES survives any data if you keep
  paying it machinery — only prediction settles it; and a sound
  objection can point wrong when one quantity is unimaginably
  larger than anyone will assume.
- "THE HEAVENS ARE UNCHANGING AND PERFECT" — killed twice in
  five years: a new star flared (1572) where none had been, and
  the 1577 comet crossed the incorruptible spheres. The sky
  changes on timescales that dwarf a watcher — exactly why the
  claim survived. Its residue, the perfect-circle dogma, cost
  decades more before orbits were let be ellipses and fit.
- "THE SUN BURNS AS A FIRE BURNS" — damned by arithmetic. A sun
  of finest coal: 2 × 10³⁰ kg × 30 million J/kg = 6 × 10³⁷ J; at
  the observed 3.8 × 10²⁶ W, about 5,000 years. The fallback
  (slow gravitational settling) buys tens of millions — still
  far short of what rocks and life demanded. The deficit stood
  two generations, both sides right, physics missing a term;
  drained by mass-as-energy plus the quantum loophole (the
  color's furnace runs to ~10 billion years). The subject's best
  case of the gap BEING the signal.
- THE STEADY-STATE UNIVERSE — eternal, unchanging, matter
  quietly created to fill the stretch. Killed by two
  measurements: the far past holds more bright radio sources
  than the present (so there IS a past), and the ancient glow
  arrived (1965) exactly as a hot beginning demands and a steady
  state cannot make. The geocentrism lesson rerun in a century.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT DARK MATTER IS — the measurement is solid, the
  identity white. Four independent books short by about the same
  figure: galaxies' outskirts turn too fast for the matter that
  shines, clusters move too fast to hold together, light bends
  more than visible mass explains, structure could not have
  grown in the time available. Separate methods, separate
  scales, one missing amount — agreement is what turns a puzzle
  into a measurement. Bounded: it pulls, does not shine, does
  not block light, moves slowly, outweighs the visible ~5 to 1;
  every named candidate has missed where a search could see. The
  Vulcan/Neptune verdict cuts both ways: hidden mass has been
  triumphantly real once and famously fictional once. Nothing
  certain — but here is a possibility: a particle outside the
  known families, interacting only by pull, which is why every
  trap built from the known forces stays empty. Would drain on:
  a catch in any detector; a changed law landing all four books
  at once with nothing added (none yet does); or the missing
  amount failing to appear somewhere it should.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT PUSHES THE EXPANSION — the fixed-fuse
  explosions in far galaxies arrived FAINTER than their stretch
  predicted: farther off than steady slowing allows. The
  expansion is speeding up, and whatever does it is most of the
  energy there is while showing up in no other way at all.
  Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: emptiness itself
  carries a small fixed cost per volume, so more space pushes
  harder as more space exists. Would drain on: any second,
  independent way of seeing it, or a measured drift in its
  strength over cosmic time.
- UNSETTLED: WHY OPPOSITE HORIZONS MATCH — the ancient glow
  holds one temperature on sides of the sky that could never
  have exchanged a signal, and the geometry sits flat to a
  knife's edge when almost any curvature was available. Nothing
  certain — but here is the offered possibility: an instant of
  enormous stretching at the start, blowing a patch small enough
  to have already agreed up into everything seen — stretching
  flattens, as a balloon's skin does. It answers both oddities
  at once (its strength) and has not yet paid a prediction only
  it could pay (why it stays here). Would drain on: a signature
  in the glow that stretching demands and nothing else makes.
- UNSETTLED: WHETHER LIFE EXISTS ELSEWHERE — the galaxy is old
  enough that a civilization spreading at ANY speed could have
  crossed it many times over, so the plain expectation is
  company — and there is none: no visit, no signal, no artifact.
  Every answer costs something — life is rare, or intelligence
  is, or civilizations are brief, or quiet, or the searches too
  thin to count — and nobody knows which; the null's weight is
  exactly the weight of the search behind it, and the searched
  fraction is tiny. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility:
  the rare step is behind us — life's own start — which fits a
  sky full of habitable ground and empty of neighbors. Would
  drain on: ONE independent second start (fossil chemistry on a
  near world, a biosphere gas in a far planet's air — the tools
  are in the color), collapsing "life is rare"; or centuries of
  genuinely wide silence, sharpening toward "rare or brief".
- UNSETTLED: WHY EVERY BIG GALAXY'S CENTRAL BLACK HOLE TRACKS
  ITS GALAXY — millions to billions of suns at the middle, mass
  marching with properties of a galaxy it cannot
  gravitationally reach. A tight relation between things that
  cannot touch points to a shared history, not a shared force —
  but WHICH history is unwritten. Would drain on: catching the
  coupling in the act, far back, where both are growing.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: THE PHYSICS OF THE FIRST INSTANT — the two great
  working theories (the very large, the very small) refuse to
  merge exactly where the universe began and where collapsed
  cores bottom out. The subject computes from the first minutes
  forward, to decimal places of helium; before that it holds no
  law at all. The largest named absence.
- LACKING: A LAW FOR THE MISSING FIVE-SIXTHS — the color
  computes with what shines; the books say what shines is a
  sixth of what pulls. No equation in the color carries the rest
  as anything but a measured placeholder.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE SUN-AGE ARITHMETIC — right method,
  honest numbers, missing physics (the coal sun above). The
  standing caution AND hope: an arithmetic that lands impossibly
  short is not a failure of arithmetic; it is a term announcing
  itself.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: DISTANCE THROUGH DUST — early ladder work
  ignored the thin smoke's tax (dimmer AND redder) and distances
  came out systematically wrong until the bias was modeled. A
  systematic error cannot be averaged away; it must be found and
  carried. The correction is in the color; the era of wrong maps
  is the receipt here.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- ADDING EPICYCLES — paying a model machinery to keep it fit:
  each new observation cost a new circle, nothing was ever
  predicted, and the fit never breaks — exactly the trap.
  Superseded by prediction-first (the color's rule). The pattern
  recurs in every era wearing new clothes; this is its index
  case.
- HUNTING THE ETHER DRIFT — the instrument superb, the thing
  absent. A search can be flawless and its target fictional —
  and the null then outvalues the hoped-for positive.
- MAPPING AT THE LIMIT OF SEEING — the canals method: trained
  eyes, long patience, systematic records — of an artifact.
  Fails under the condition: signal at or below the resolution
  plus an expecting mind. Drains only by more resolution or a
  blind protocol; never by more patience, which just deepens the
  map of the wish.
- BUYING MAGNIFICATION — magnifying delivers a bigger, dimmer,
  blurrier smudge, because detail was fixed by the opening's
  width before the eyepiece saw it. The want (see more) drains
  only through area and width — the color's law.
- WEIGHING A THIN SILENCE AS BROAD — the early signal searches
  covered a sliver of frequencies, directions, and years, and
  the silence was talked about as if it covered the sky. A null
  result's weight is the search behind it, no more. Not a failed
  method — a correctly thin one, filed so the silence stays
  honestly priced.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE EYE AT ITS EDGE — reported canals on Mars and figures in
  every star-scatter; a superb instrument that generates pattern
  when starved. Superseded at the edge by the camera and the
  blind protocol; kept everywhere else.
- THE EMPTY TRAPS — decades of detectors built for the leading
  dark-matter candidates: underground, cooled, exquisitely
  quiet, and empty. Each null year genuinely narrows the bounds
  — the white doing its work — but the catch is not yet found.
- NOT YET FOUND: A SECOND WINDOW ON THE EXPANSION'S PUSH — one
  phenomenon, one signature, everything else inference; would
  transform the dark-energy entry the day it exists.
- NOT YET FOUND: THE CLEAN READ OF A SMALL WORLD'S AIR — the
  difference measurement works (the color holds it), but for
  worlds like this one the signal sits at the raggedest edge of
  today's instruments. The means that drains the life question
  is designed, partly built, not yet delivering.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- SAY WHAT MOST OF EVERYTHING IS — roughly 95% of the energy
  books is the two dark entries; the subject can weigh what it
  cannot name.
- SAY WHETHER ANYONE ELSE IS OUT THERE — the tools are arriving,
  the answer is not; either outcome would be among the largest
  facts ever landed, which is why the want never cools.
- SAY WHAT CAME BEFORE, OR WHETHER "BEFORE" MEANS ANYTHING —
  blocked on the lacking first-instant physics; the question
  cannot yet even be posed in computable form.
- CALL THE SUN'S OUTBURSTS IN ADVANCE — the ~11-year beat is
  known, the individual eruption is not, and a big one aimed
  here would bill the electrical world dearly. Would drain on: a
  working model of the wound field's letting-go.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side.

- The fourteen-century detour is this subject's Dorian portrait:
  the color says "the earth turns" in one line; this page holds
  what the line cost — generations of good observers weighing
  real evidence inside a frame that could absorb any fact for
  the price of another circle. The honest core (the missing
  stellar jump WAS good evidence) is kept on purpose: the lesson
  is not "they were fools" but "a sound objection plus an
  unimaginable quantity reads as refutation."
- The ether century: the finest zero ever measured, taken by
  people expecting a number; physics' deepest reform began as
  this page's entry.
- The sun-age deadlock: two sciences both right and openly
  contradicting for fifty years — a missing term standing in
  plain sight, wearing a paradox's costume.
- Neptune and Vulcan, the split verdict: one method, one triumph
  and one phantom a few decades apart. Every hidden-mass
  argument since — dark matter above all — inherits both halves
  and must say why it resembles the first more than the second.
- The canals: decades of published maps of nothing, drawn in
  good faith by skilled hands — the priced warning that patience
  cannot fix a resolution problem.
- The empty traps' decades: the ongoing try, filed while still
  running — each null tightening the bounds, the accumulated
  closures already the field's real map of where the answer is
  not.

- BROKEN: ASTRONOMY ↔ BELIEF, as fate-telling — the claim that
  birth-sky positions steer lives fails every blinded test:
  matched charts, swapped charts, practitioners at chance. Kept
  because queries still arrive carrying it — and because the

  records the true subject later ran on. The relation broke; the
