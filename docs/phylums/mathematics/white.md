# MATHEMATICS — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/mathematics/color.md: same parts,
opposite sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting
for its drain. Seed authored by Claude and marked as seed.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE INFINITESIMAL — a number smaller than every fraction yet
  more than zero. Used brilliantly for 150 years (calculus ran on
  it) while provably incoherent AS THEN STATED — the same symbol
  treated as zero in one line and nonzero in the next. Banished
  ~1800s in favor of limits; then DRAINED IN PART: a lawful home
  was built for it (1960s, nonstandard analysis) where the old
  reasoning is licensed. Filed as the exemplar: a thing can be
  illegal in one ground and lawful in a richer one.
- THE SET OF ALL SETS — cannot be, under the rule "any property
  names a set": the set of all sets not containing themselves
  can neither contain itself nor not (Russell, 1901). WHY in
  condition: unrestricted collection is the culprit; restrict
  the collection rules and set theory stands (the ground now in
  use). The want (one universe holding everything) stays open.
- THE LARGEST PRIME — cannot be; the color holds the two-line
  proof, ~2,300 years old. Indexed here so a query hunting "the
  biggest" brakes instead of searching.
- A FRACTION EQUAL TO √2 — cannot be (color holds the proof).
  Condition: whole-number ratios; move to decimals that never
  end and the diagonal is welcomed — the cannot named its ground
  and the richer ground drained the want.
- AN EVEN RAFFLE ON ALL COUNTING NUMBERS — cannot be: give every
  number the same chance p; if p > 0 the chances sum past 1
  forever, if p = 0 they sum to 0 — never to 1. "Pick a random
  whole number, all equally likely" is a sentence with no object.
  The cannot is "equal" and "all" together: drop either and it
  drains — a finite range raffles fine, and unequal chances that
  sum to 1 (half, quarter, eighth…) raffle all of them lawfully.
- AN ODD PERFECT NUMBER — wondered about ~2,300 years. The even
  ones march (6, 28, 496, 8,128…); no odd one found below
  ~10^1500, none forbidden. Neither built nor buried: a named
  unknown. Would drain on: one example, or a proof of cannot.
- A FORMULA THAT MINTS THE PRIMES — long wanted, not found in
  usable form. Partial drains exist (sieves list them; tests
  certify one quickly) but no cheap expression writes the nth
  prime to order. Would drain on: exactly that, at tolerable
  cost — or a proof that cost this low cannot be.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "EVERY LENGTH IS A RATIO OF WHOLE NUMBERS" — the Pythagorean
  creed, broken by their own theorem: the square's diagonal
  (color holds the proof). Legend says the discloser drowned;
  what certainly drowned was the claim. First recorded case of a
  subject's foundation failing FROM INSIDE — filed as the pattern
  later repeated by the fifth postulate and by set theory.
- "THE FIFTH POSTULATE MUST FOLLOW FROM THE OTHER FOUR" — 2,000
  years of attempted proofs, all failed, and the failure WAS the
  discovery: deny the postulate and no contradiction comes — you
  get instead the geometry of the sphere (triangles over 180°)
  or the saddle (under). The tries that "proved absurdities" had
  proved true theorems of unbuilt worlds — wings-on-arms flown
  on another surface. Deepest drain on record: the curved case
  is the one real spacetime uses.
- "CHANCE REMEMBERS" (the gambler's ruin) — false: a fair coin
  after five heads is even money still. WHY believed: the long
  run does even out — but by dilution, not correction: a 5-0
  head start followed by 1,000 fair tosses expects 505 heads of
  1,005 ≈ 50.2% — the fraction heals, the lead never repays.
  Quantities in the color's long-run law; the fallacy is that
  law read backwards.
- "STICK OR SWITCH, IT'S 50-50" (the three doors) — false: the
  first pick wins 1/3 of the time, so switching wins 2/3 —
  switching loses only when the first pick was right. Filed
  because trained mathematicians fought it in public and lost;
  intuition about chance mis-prices information arriving.
- "CONFIRMED A MILLION TIMES, THEREFORE TRUE" — broken on the
  record: Fermat's primes died at the sixth case (color holds
  the corpse with its factors); a famous prime-count inequality
  holds for every number ever checked yet provably fails
  somewhere near 10^316 (~). Checking bounds confidence; only
  proof closes.
- "WHAT WINS IN EVERY GROUP WINS OVERALL" — false when group
  sizes differ (Simpson's reversal). WORKED: doctor A cures
  80/90 easy (89%) and 5/10 hard (50%); doctor B cures 9/10 easy
  (90%) and 55/90 hard (61%). B beats A in BOTH kinds — and
  loses overall, 64/100 against A's 85/100, because A got the
  easy load. Averages follow the mix; strip the mix and the
  comparison lies.
- "THE WHOLE IS GREATER THAN THE PART" — broke on the infinite:
  the evens pair one-to-one with all counting numbers (n ↔ 2n).
  Drained by promotion: the breakage became the DEFINITION of
  infinite (color holds it as law). An axiom demoted to a
  condition: true exactly of finite things.
- UNSETTLED: GOLDBACH — every even number from 4 up seems to be
  two primes (4=2+2, 28=5+23, 100=3+97). Checked to ~4×10^18,
  proven never. Would drain on: a proof, or one stubborn even
  number.
- UNSETTLED: TWIN PRIMES — do pairs two apart (11-13, 41-43)
  ever run out? Unknown. PARTIAL DRAIN on record: infinitely
  many primes within 246 of each other is proven (2013-14) —
  the gap fell from infinity to 246 and stuck. Would drain on:
  closing 246 to 2.
- UNSETTLED: COLLATZ — halve if even, triple-and-add-one if odd;
  every start ever tried falls to 1 (checked to ~2^68). No proof
  it always must. A child can state it; no one can close it.
  Would drain on: a proof, or one orbit that escapes or cycles.
- UNSETTLED: P VS NP, in plain words — for puzzles whose answers
  are QUICK TO CHECK (a filled timetable, a solved jigsaw), is
  there always a quick way to FIND the answer? Everyone bets no;
  no one has proven it either way; a standing million-dollar
  bounty says how much the ground is worth. Would drain either
  way — and a "yes" would collapse locks, schedules, and
  secrecy in one stroke (engineering holds the stakes).
- UNSETTLED: RIEMANN, in plain words — primes thin out on a
  known average schedule; the hypothesis says their wobble
  around that schedule stays as small as fair coin-flips allow.
  Checked for ~10^13 cases of its exact form; unproven. Half of
  number theory is written "assuming it" — a fabric of
  conditional color waiting on one white entry. Would drain
  either way: a proof turns that conditional half solid in one
  stroke; one wobble past the bound and it all comes down.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A REASON MATH FITS THE WORLD — the subject's deepest
  named absence. Equations invented for play keep turning out to
  run planets and particles. Nothing certain — but here are the
  standing possibilities: the world is mathematical and we
  transcribe; OR we keep only the math that fits and forget the
  misses (survivor's shelf); OR minds grown in this world can
  only invent world-shaped tools. Would drain on: a case the
  possibilities predict differently — none is written.
- LACKING: A DIFFICULTY METER — no way to price a problem before
  solving it. Fermat's note looked like an afternoon and took
  358 years; the four-color map looked deep and fell to patient
  case-checking. The subject cannot yet compute its own costs;
  every quest is priced by dying on it. Nothing certain — but a
  possibility stands: walls can be priced (the halting cannot
  prices a whole class as unpayable), so a partial meter is not
  forbidden. Would drain on: an instrument that prices even one
  named quest before anyone dies on it.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: INFINITESIMAL CALCULUS AS FIRST STATED —
  150 years of right answers on unsound ground ("ghosts of
  departed quantities"). Paid twice: rebuilt on limits (1800s),
  then the old style licensed in a richer ground (1960s). Lesson
  filed: a method can outrun its own justification and still be
  right — but the bill always arrives, and it compounds.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: NAIVE SET THEORY — "any property names a
  set" seemed self-evident and exploded (THINGS holds the bomb).
  Cost of repair: the ground became a CHOICE of axioms rather
  than an obviousness. Certainty survived; innocence did not.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: PROBABILITY IN THE COURTROOM — a mother
  was convicted when 1-in-8,500 was squared to 1-in-73-million
  by treating two infant deaths in one family as independent;
  shared cause was the whole question, and independence assumed
  it away. Conviction overturned; the lesson is the color's own
  law: chances multiply ONLY when independent, and independence
  is a claim to prove, never a default.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
certified cannots indexed by their tools, waiting where drains
exist.

- SQUARING THE CIRCLE — build a square equal in area to a given
  circle. CERTIFIED CANNOT under the exact conditions: compass
  and unmarked straightedge, finitely many steps. WHY: those
  tools only reach lengths built from whole numbers by towers of
  square roots; π satisfies NO polynomial equation at all
  (proved 1882), so no tower reaches it. SHIMMER: change a tool
  and it drains — a rolling circle or a quadratrix curve squares
  it easily. The certificate names its tools; outside them the
  task was never impossible, only the purity was.
- TRISECTING THE ANGLE — split a GENERAL angle in three with the
  same two tools. CERTIFIED CANNOT (1837): trisecting 60° means
  building cos 20°, a root of 8x³ − 6x − 1 = 0 — depth three,
  and square-root towers only reach depths 1, 2, 4, 8… WHY in
  one line: 3 is not a power of 2. DRAINS on any moved
  condition: a ruler with two marks trisects (antiquity knew);
  paper folding trisects; and particular angles (90°) trisect
  fine — the cannot is about ALL angles with THOSE tools.
- DOUBLING THE CUBE — build a cube of twice the volume: needs
  ∛2, depth three again, same certificate, same drains. The
  three classical cannots are ONE fact about the tools, indexed
  three ways — which is why they fell together after 2,000 years
  of separate trying.
- PROVING EVERYTHING FROM INSIDE ONE SYSTEM — the honest cannot
  (1931): any consistent ground rich enough to carry arithmetic
  holds true sentences it cannot prove, and cannot prove its own
  consistency from inside. CONDITIONS MATTER, so the edges are
  written: grounds too poor for arithmetic can be complete —
  plain school geometry is (every statement in it provable or
  refutable), and so is arithmetic with addition but no
  multiplication. The wall stands exactly where counting meets
  multiplying, and no wider. Would never drain inside its
  conditions; stepping OUTSIDE them (a stronger ground) sees
  each unprovable truth — and grows its own.
- A MACHINE THAT SETTLES EVERYTHING — decide any program's fate
  by inspection. CANNOT (1936): feed the supposed decider a
  program built to do the opposite of whatever is predicted
  about it, and it contradicts itself on itself. The ash this
  grew from is the computer itself — the cannot was
  constructive, and engineering lives in the crater.
- THE DEGREE-FIVE FORMULA — a general recipe in roots for
  equations of degree 5, sought for 300 years after degrees 3
  and 4 fell. CANNOT (1824): the shuffles of five roots do not
  take apart the way four's do. DRAINS in every practical
  direction: degrees up to 4 keep their formulas; many special
  quintics solve; and iteration (color's METHOD) answers to any
  wanted accuracy — the want was answers, and answers survived
  the formula's death.
- THE BETTING SYSTEM AGAINST A FAIR GAME — double after every
  loss and "always" come out ahead. CANNOT: with 127 units you
  can cover 7 straight losses (1+2+4+8+16+32+64 = 127); the 7th
  straight loss arrives one time in 128 — so in 128 runs, expect
  ~127 wins of 1 and one wipe of 127: zero, before the house
  edge. No reshuffling of stakes moves an expected value;
  systems redistribute WHEN you lose, never WHETHER. Condition:
  fair or house-favored game, finite purse. A truly
  player-favored game needs no system — and finding one is not
  mathematics but scouting.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- ROMAN NUMERALS AS A CALCULATING MEAN — fell short absolutely:
  no place value, no carry; XLVII × XXXVI is a wrestling match
  on paper. Europe computed on the abacus for a thousand years
  to route around its own numerals. Kept honestly where it
  serves: faces, dates, order without arithmetic.
- FLOATING POINT AS EXACT ARITHMETIC — falls short BY DESIGN:
  machine decimals are binary underneath, 0.1 has no finite
  binary writing, so 0.1 + 0.2 lands at 0.30000000000000004.
  Harmless where tolerance is stated; lethal where drift
  compounds unwatched (a wartime clock accumulating a tiny
  binary error per tick drifted enough to miss — the receipt is
  engineering's, the WHY is this entry). Condition: floating
  point is a measuring mean, not a counting one; count in
  integers, measure in floats, and never test floats for
  perfect equality.
- A COMPUTABLE MEASURE OF SIMPLICITY — the wanted instrument:
  score any object's true complexity (shortest recipe that
  makes it). The measure EXISTS and is PROVEN uncomputable — no
  machine returns it in general (it inherits the halting wall).
  Every compression tool is a working shadow of it; the
  instrument itself cannot be built. Certified by its
  conditions: general and exact; drains only into
  approximations.
- NOT YET FOUND: PROOF CHEAP ENOUGH TO CHECK BY MACHINE AS A
  HABIT — machine-checked proof exists (the four-color proof
  leaned on it; whole theorems are formalized) but at a
  translation cost so high most working proofs never pay it.
  Would drain on: formalization near the price of writing the
  proof itself — the gap is closing, the entry stands open.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- DECIDE EVERY QUESTION IT CAN POSE — cannot, by its own two
  theorems (METHODS holds them with conditions). The honest
  reframe the subject settled on: each ground has blind spots;
  stronger grounds see them and grow their own. Certainty
  remains real, local, and conditional — absolute completeness
  was the want that died, and knowledge grew at the funeral.
- PREDICT THE SINGLE CASE FROM CHANCE — probability answers
  crowds (the color's long-run law) and stays silent on the next
  toss, the one patient, tomorrow's rain as fact. Likely cannot
  in principle; filed open because the want returns with every
  query that carries one life in it.
- GROUND CERTAINTY WITHOUT CHOOSING GROUND — the want behind the
  1900s program: axioms so self-evident no choice remains.
  Receipt of the failure: the continuum question (is there a
  size of infinity strictly between the counting numbers and
  the decimals?) is PROVEN unanswerable from the standard
  ground — not unsolved: independent (shown unrefutable 1940,
  unprovable 1963). At the foundation you must choose, and own
  the choice. The want stands; the subject now files it
  honestly.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the
story.

- The diagonal's scandal: the first foundation to fail failed
  from inside, by the subject's own proof. Whatever truly
  happened to Hippasus, the pattern he names has repeated twice
  more (fifth postulate, naive sets) and earned a rule: the
  crack is the map — dig where the ground groans.
- The 2,000-year siege of the fifth postulate: generations spent
  whole careers proving it and failing; one besieger derived
  "absurdity after absurdity" and died sure he'd defended
  Euclid — he had written the first theorems of the geometry
  that later carried gravitation. The longest recorded case of
  the white doing its work: every failed proof was a survey of
  the land where the drain finally opened.
- The circle-squarers' flood: so many false squarings arrived
  that the Paris Academy stopped reading them (1775) — a century
  BEFORE the cannot was proven (1882). Filed as the cost of an
  unindexed white: without the certificate, the same dead road
  is walked forever by newcomers who cannot see the closures.
- Negative numbers, the long shame: called absurd and fictitious
  in Europe for centuries WHILE working flawlessly in every
  ledger. The trying here was backwards — the practice was
  right and the permission lagged. Rule earned: when the books
  balance better with the invention than without, the invention
  is telling you what the law is.
- Fermat's margin, 358 years: filed 1637 as a claimed proof "the
  margin is too small to contain"; drained 1995 by machinery
  undreamt for 350 of those years. The white's flagship: an
  entry may outlive its filer's civilization and still drain —
  and the margin's own proof almost certainly never existed,
  which is ALSO knowledge: confidence is not a receipt.
- Hilbert's list, 1900: twenty-three unknowns filed deliberately
  and in public — the most productive white-filing on record.
  Some drained to color, one drained to a certified cannot (the
  tenth: no universal method for whole-number equations, proven
  1970), the deepest (Riemann, in CLAIMS) still stand. Proof
  that NAMING the unknown organizes a century of trying.
- The four-color proof, 1976: true by a checking no single human
  has read whole (~2,000 machine-verified cases). The theorem
  entered the color; what "knowing" must mean strained and
  moved. The argument is not over; filed as the live edge of
  proof-as-chained-certainty — the chain now sometimes passes
  through hands that are not hands.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, REBUILT HONESTLY: MATHEMATICS ↔ SPACE — for 2,000
  years Euclid's geometry was read as FACT about the world, the
  one subject where the map and the land were the same sheet.
  Broke when the other geometries proved consistent and the
  world measurably picked a curved one. Rebuilt as it now
  stands: mathematics writes the menu of consistent worlds;
  measurement, in physics, orders. The relation is stronger for
  the break — but it carries a scar with a name: no theorem
  ever again gets to claim the territory by beauty alone.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: THE PRIMES ↔ PHYSICS — the wobble of the
  primes (Riemann's, in CLAIMS) matches, numerically and
  uncannily, the statistics of energy levels in heavy atomic
  nuclei. Gripping on every computed case, proven on none. If
  the bridge is real, a physical system may someday settle the
  subject's most famous unknown from outside — the fabric's own
  bet, dotted until cashed.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: WHY IT FITS AT ALL — the relation of this
  phylum to EVERY other is itself the standing unknown (SCIENCE
  holds it with its three live possibilities). Every crossing
  the color exports — to cooking's ratios, physics' laws,
  medicine's trials — is evidence in a case that has never
  closed.
- BROKEN AS OFFERED: NUMBER ↔ FATE — numerology's claim that
  the counting carries destinies has never held under any fair
  test; the patterns offered dissolve on fresh data. Kept on
  file, not as a possibility waiting to drain but because
  queries arrive every day still carrying it, and the fabric
  answers best when it holds WHY a thing fails: the hits are
  remembered, the misses are not — the survivor's shelf again,
  the same flaw the SCIENCE entry warns this subject about
  itself.
