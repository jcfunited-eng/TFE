## THINGS
The units this subject is about. Each sits in many phylums at once;
its other homes are named so a cascade can branch.

- NUMBER — the count set free from what was counted: three sheep,
  three days, three stones share one thing, and that thing is the
  unit here; also lives in: economy (the ledger), physics (the
  measure), culture (lucky and unlucky), every phylum that counts.
- THE NUMERAL — the written number: ten digits and the place each
  stands in. The numeral is not the number; it is the number's
  best machine so far; also: history (its slow arrival), economy
  (bookkeeping), engineering (encoding).
- ZERO — the placeholder that became a number: the count of
  nothing, made lawful; also: history (invented, resisted),
  physics (the vacuum, the origin), economy (the empty account).
- NEGATIVES — debts made lawful: numbers below nothing, each the
  undoing of its positive; also: economy (born in ledgers),
  physics (direction, charge), weather (below freezing).
- FRACTION AND RATIO — the number between numbers: a comparison
  holding two counts in one grip; also: cooking (the recipe IS
  ratios), chemistry (fixed proportions), music via culture
  (intervals are ratios), farming (seed per area).
- THE UNKNOWN — a number named before it is known, so the rules
  can work on it anyway: the letter x; also: engineering (the
  design constraint), economy (the price to solve for).
- THE EQUATION — a claimed balance: two writings of one value,
  with the law that what is done to one side must be done to the
  other; also: chemistry (the balanced reaction), physics (law's
  native sentence), economy (the books must balance).
- SHAPE — point, line, angle, circle, triangle: the forms left
  when material is forgotten; also: engineering (the drawing),
  physics (the trajectory), farming (the field), culture (the
  ornament).
- THE SQUARE AND ITS ROOT — a number times itself, and the walk
  back: area's arithmetic and the clock of spreading things; also:
  cooking (thickness squared), physics (diffusion, falling),
  engineering (strength of cross-sections).
- PRIME — a count that will not break into equal smaller piles:
  the atoms of multiplication; also: engineering (codes and
  secrecy run on their hardness), life (cicada years, ~).
- CHANCE — the number on an uncertainty: 0 never, 1 surely,
  between them the law of crowds; also: economy (risk, insurance),
  medicine (the trial), weather (the forecast), physics (the
  small world runs on it).
- THE AVERAGE — many numbers speaking as one, at the price of
  their spread; also: economy (the index), medicine (the normal
  range), weather (the climate vs the day).
- SYMMETRY — a move that leaves the thing looking untouched:
  turn, flip, slide, repeat; also: physics (symmetry pays out
  conservation laws), life (the bilateral body), culture (every
  ornament and rhyme), chemistry (crystal forms).
- INFINITY — the unfinished made workable: not a big number but a
  direction, tamed by rules for approaching it; also: physics
  (the limit), philosophy via culture.
- PROOF — a chain of steps from agreed ground, each link checkable,
  whose end is then held with certainty CONDITIONAL on the ground;
  the subject's own product and instrument at once; also:
  engineering (the machine-checked program), culture (the idea
  that knowing can be owed a warranty).
- THE ALGORITHM — a recipe for computing that a hand or a machine
  can follow without understanding it: the carry, long division,
  the sieve; also: engineering (the program), cooking (the recipe
  is its cousin), economy (the clearing house).

## CLAIMS
What holds, written to be tested by a crossing query. Worth is
never absolute — it is applicability to the query at hand.

- A count does not depend on the order of counting: count the
  flock left to right or right to left, the number is the same.
  This is why number is a THING at all.
- Adding in any order gives the same sum; multiplying in any
  order gives the same product. Subtraction and division refuse
  the swap: 7−3 is not 3−7. Half of arithmetic's traps are this
  one asymmetry.
- Every whole number above 1 breaks into primes in exactly one
  way (order aside): 60 = 2×2×3×5 and nothing else. The
  factorization is the number's fingerprint.
- There is no largest prime — proved, not surveyed; the proof is
  in SCIENCE and has held ~2,300 years.
- The angles of a triangle sum to 180° — CONDITION: on a flat
  surface. On a sphere they sum to more (a triangle from pole to
  equator can carry 90°+90°+90° = 270°); on a saddle, less. The
  condition is part of the claim; forgetting it cost 2,000 years
  (the mirror holds that record).
- The diagonal of a square is no ratio of whole numbers: √2 is
  not a fraction. Proved by contradiction in SCIENCE. First
  crack in the claim that number = ratio (mirror holds the fall).
- Scale a shape by k and lengths go ×k, areas ×k², volumes ×k³ —
  one rule pricing photos, fields, doses, giants, and pans.
- Chance has no memory: a fair coin after five heads is still
  even money. The long run corrects fractions by dilution, not
  the ledger by revenge (the law is in SCIENCE; the false
  version is in the mirror).
- Compounding multiplies where adding merely stacks: steady
  percent growth doubles on a fixed clock, and the doublings,
  not the percent, are what to count.
- One counterexample kills a universal claim; a million
  confirmations do not prove it. The receipt with real numbers
  is in SCIENCE (Fermat's beautiful wrong guess).
- A proof, once sound, stays sound — its certainty is conditional
  on its ground (axioms) and on nothing else: not on fashion,
  majority, or the passage of time. What CAN move is the ground
  (mirror: the fifth postulate).
- Infinity comes in sizes: the counting numbers can be listed;
  the decimals cannot — any claimed complete list is beaten by
  building a decimal that differs from the nth entry at the nth
  digit. Larger still exist above that, without end.
- 0.999… equals 1 exactly — not near, equal: it is the geometric
  series 9/10 + 9/100 + … whose sum law (SCIENCE) gives
  (9/10)/(1 − 1/10) = 1. The discomfort is about notation, not
  number.
- An average without its spread is half a report: 5 and 5 average
  5; 0 and 10 average 5. Any claim built on an average carries
  the condition "and the spread is…" or it is not yet a claim.

## SCIENCE
The mathematics itself, held — not mentioned. Each piece is a LAW
a query can compute with, a RULE it can follow, and where it earns
it, a WORKED case with the arithmetic done and checked.

### ARITHMETIC — place value and the four operations

LAW: a numeral is a sum of digits times powers of ten.
  4,507 = 4×1,000 + 5×100 + 0×10 + 7. The place is worth ten of
  the place to its right — that single convention is the whole
  machine.
RULE (THE CARRY): to add, work right to left, column by column;
  when a column's sum reaches ten, write the remainder and carry
  the 1 into the next column — because ten of a place IS one of
  the next place.
WORKED: 478 + 256 — units: 8+6 = 14, write 4, carry 1; tens:
  7+5+1 = 13, write 3, carry 1; hundreds: 4+2+1 = 7. Answer 734.

RULE (THE BORROW): to subtract, when a column comes up short,
  break one of the next place into ten of this one.
WORKED: 503 − 267 — units: 3−7 won't go; borrow: 13−7 = 6, the
  0 tens became 9 (the 5 hundreds became 4); tens: 9−6 = 3;
  hundreds: 4−2 = 2. Answer 236. Check by the undo:
  267 + 236 = 503. It stands.

RULE (LONG MULTIPLICATION): every digit times every digit, each
  product shifted to its place, then one addition.
WORKED: 47 × 36 = 47×30 + 47×6 = 1,410 + 282 = 1,692.

RULE (LONG DIVISION): deal the dividend out place by place,
  asking each time how many whole times the divisor fits.
WORKED: 986 ÷ 29 — 29 into 98 fits 3 times (87), remainder 11;
  bring down the 6 → 116; 29 into 116 fits 4 times exactly
  (29×4 = 116). Answer 34. Check: 29 × 34 = 986. It stands.

RULE (THE NINES CHECK): sum the digits of each number down to one
  digit; the operation done on these little shadows must match
  the shadow of the answer. Catches most slips; NOT swapped
  digits (a swap keeps the digit sum — state the limit).
WORKED: 478+256 = 734. Shadows: 4+7+8 = 19 → 1; 2+5+6 = 13 → 4;
  1+4 = 5. Answer's shadow: 7+3+4 = 14 → 5. Matches.

RULE (FRACTIONS DIVIDE BY INVERTING): to divide by a fraction,
  multiply by it upside down — "how many 4/5-pieces fit in 2/3?"
WORKED: (2/3) ÷ (4/5) = 2/3 × 5/4 = 10/12 = 5/6.

### PROPORTION — the times rule

LAW: a ratio survives scaling: a:b = na:nb for any n. This is
  why a recipe, a map, a dose, and a model can be resized at all.
RULE (RULE OF THREE): when a/b = c/x, then x = bc/a — three
  knowns buy the fourth.
WORKED: 3 kg costs 12; what do 7 kg cost? 12 × 7 ÷ 3 = 28.
  (Unit check: 4 per kg, times 7 = 28.)

LAW: inverse proportion — when a job is fixed, doers × time is
  the constant. CONDITION: only where the work truly shares;
  some jobs refuse division (the condition travels with the law).
WORKED: 4 workers, 6 days = 24 worker-days; 8 workers → 3 days.

LAW: percent is a ratio to 100, and percent changes MULTIPLY —
  they do not add.
RULE: chain them as factors: +20% then −20% is ×1.2 × 0.8.
WORKED: down 50% then up 50%: 100 → 50 → 75 — a net loss of 25.
  And the undo of a 20% drop is a 25% rise: 100 → 80, and
  80 × 1.25 = 100. Percent has a direction; the mirror holds the
  grief this asymmetry causes.
WORKED (percent of): 15% of 240 — 10% is 24, 5% is 12,
  24 + 12 = 36.

### SQUARES, ROOTS, AND THE SCALING LAWS

LAW: scale by k — length ×k, area ×k², volume ×k³. Surface per
  amount therefore falls as size grows: big things are mostly
  inside.
WORKED: a giant twice a man's height carries 2³ = 8× the weight
  on bones of 2² = 4× the cross-section — each bone bears twice
  its old load, and that is why the giant of the story breaks
  and why no elephant is built like a spider. The same law,
  kitchenward: half-size pieces double the surface per amount
  (cooking holds its own copy, in its terms — that repetition is
  the fabric working, not waste).

LAW (PYTHAGORAS): in a right triangle, a² + b² = c² — the square
  on the long side is the other two squares combined.
WORKED: a 5 m ladder, foot 3 m from the wall: the top stands at
  √(25 − 9) = √16 = 4 m. The 3-4-5 rope with knots built square
  corners for Egyptian fields before the proof existed.

RULE (HERON'S ROOT): to find √N — guess g, average g with N/g,
  repeat; each pass roughly doubles the correct digits.
WORKED: √10 — guess 3; average 3 with 10/3 ≈ 3.333 → 3.1667;
  average 3.1667 with 10/3.1667 ≈ 3.1579 → 3.1623. And
  3.1623² ≈ 10.0001. Two passes, four digits.

LAW (THE DIFFUSION CLOCK): what spreads by wandering reaches
  depth ∝ √time — so doubling the depth costs FOUR times the
  time, and there is no shortcut in the law.
WORKED: a wanderer covering 1 cm in 1 day needs 4 days for 2 cm,
  9 for 3 cm. Cooking's cures, the body's absorbing, chemistry's
  mixing all run on this clock — each phylum holds its own copy
  with its own k.

### EXPONENTS — compounding and its clock

LAW: steady multiplication compounds: N = N₀ × 2^(t ÷ T), where
  T is the doubling time. The ladder of tens for fast reckoning:
  2^10 = 1,024 ≈ a thousand, so every 10 doublings ≈ ×1,000.
RULE (RULE OF 70): doubling time ≈ 70 ÷ percent-per-period.
WORKED: 7% a year doubles in ~10 years (exactly: 1.07^10 ≈ 1.97).
  1,000 at 7% for 10 years ≈ 1,967 — the doubling, in money.
  At 35 years: 3.5 doublings, 2^3.5 ≈ 11× — not 3.45×; adding
  where the law multiplies is the classic misread.

WORKED (THE CHESSBOARD): one grain on the first square, doubled
  each square. Square 64 alone holds 2^63 ≈ 9.2×10^18 grains —
  nine billion billion; the board altogether 2^64 − 1 ≈ 1.8×10^19.
  The king who granted it learned what every lender and every
  culture of bacteria knows.
WORKED (THE FOLD): paper 0.1 mm thick, folded 42 times:
  0.1 mm × 2^42 ≈ 4.4×10^11 mm = 440,000 km — past the Moon
  (~384,000 km). The arithmetic is exact; only the folding is
  impossible (~7 folds is what paper physically grants — the
  limit belongs to physics, the law stands).
BACKWARDS: the same law halves — decay, depreciation, cooling
  toward the room. Count halvings the way you count doublings.

### PROBABILITY — the long-run law

LAW: when outcomes are equally likely, the chance of an event is
  favorable ÷ total. Independent chances MULTIPLY.
WORKED: two dice — 36 equally likely pairs. Sum 7: six ways
  (1-6, 2-5, 3-4, 4-3, 5-2, 6-1) = 6/36 = 1/6. Sum 2: one way
  = 1/36. The middle is common because it has more roads in.

RULE (AT LEAST ONCE): the chance of at least one hit in n
  independent tries is 1 − (1−p)^n — compute the miss, then flip.
WORKED (the wager that started the science, 1654): at least one
  six in 4 rolls: 1 − (5/6)^4 = 1 − 625/1296 = 671/1296 ≈ 0.518
  — a paying bet. At least one double-six in 24 rolls of two
  dice: 1 − (35/36)^24 ≈ 0.491 — a losing one. The Chevalier
  felt the difference in his purse before Pascal and Fermat
  wrote the law.

LAW (THE LONG RUN): the FRACTION of hits settles toward the
  probability as trials grow; the COUNT of excess does not — it
  wanders wider, ∝ √n.
WORKED: 100 fair tosses: expect 50 heads give or take ~5 — a
  10% wobble. 10,000 tosses: 5,000 give or take ~50 — a 1%
  wobble. The fraction tightened tenfold; the absolute wobble
  GREW tenfold. Both halves matter; quoting only one is how
  casinos and fallacies eat.

RULE (SAMPLE TO KNOW THE WHOLE): a fair sample of n answers
  within roughly 1/√n of the truth, whatever the population size.
WORKED: 1,000 people polled → ~±3% (1/√1000 ≈ 0.032). To halve
  the error, quadruple the sample — the √ law again.

LAW (EXPECTED VALUE): the long-run worth of a gamble is the sum
  of each outcome × its chance.
WORKED: single number at European roulette — pays 35-to-1, 37
  slots: EV = (1/37)(35) − (36/37)(1) = −1/37 ≈ −2.7% of every
  stake. The house edge is not luck; it is this subtraction,
  collected forever.

WORKED (THE SCREENING TRAP — carried by medicine, held here):
  a disease touches 1 in 1,000; a test errs 1% both ways. Test
  1,000 people: ~1 sick person flags true; ~10 of the 999
  healthy flag false. A positive is therefore sick only ~1 time
  in 11 (~9%) — the test is 99% right and the positive is 91%
  wrong, because the base rate multiplies first. Chances
  multiply; intuition adds.
NOTE (crowded coincidences): ~23 people make even odds of a
  shared birthday — 23 people carry 23×22÷2 = 253 pairs, and
  253 lottery tickets against 365 days is no long shot.

### PROOF — chained certainty

LAW: a proof is a finite chain from agreed ground (axioms) by
  agreed moves (logic); the conclusion inherits exactly the
  certainty of the ground — no more, and no less.

WORKED PROOF (NO LARGEST PRIME, Euclid): suppose the primes were
  finite; multiply them all and add 1. That number leaves
  remainder 1 on division by every listed prime, so its prime
  factors are new — the list was incomplete, always. With real
  numbers: 2×3×5×7 + 1 = 211, itself prime. But note the honest
  edge: 2×3×5×7×11×13 + 1 = 30,031 = 59 × 509 — the construction
  promises a NEW prime, not that the number itself is one.

WORKED PROOF (√2 IS NO FRACTION): suppose √2 = p/q in lowest
  terms. Then p² = 2q², so p² is even, so p is even: p = 2r.
  Then 4r² = 2q², so q² = 2r², so q is even too — but p and q
  shared no factor. The supposition breaks; the diagonal is no
  ratio. This is contradiction as a method: assume it, break it.

RULE (INDUCTION — the ladder): prove the claim for 1; prove that
  holding at n forces holding at n+1; then it holds for every
  rung, forever, by finite work.
WORKED: claim 1+2+…+n = n(n+1)/2. Base: 1 = 1×2/2. Step: given
  the sum to n, add n+1: n(n+1)/2 + (n+1) = (n+1)(n+2)/2 — the
  same shape with n+1 in n's place. Spot-check n=100:
  100×101/2 = 5,050 — the schoolroom sum done in one line.

LAW (THE COUNTEREXAMPLE): universal claims die of one instance.
WORKED RECEIPT: Fermat guessed every 2^(2^n) + 1 is prime, and
  the first five agree: 3, 5, 17, 257, 65,537 — all prime. The
  sixth: 2^32 + 1 = 4,294,967,297 = 641 × 6,700,417 (Euler,
  1732). Five confirmations, one corpse. Checking is evidence;
  only proof is proof — and the unsettled giants this law
  governs are filed in the mirror (Goldbach and kin).

LAW (SUM OF A GEOMETRIC SERIES): a + ar + ar² + … = a/(1−r)
  when |r| < 1 — infinitely many terms, one finite answer.
WORKED: 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 + … = (1/2)/(1 − 1/2) = 1. The runner
  crosses the room after all; Zeno's infinite errand has a
  finite bill. Same law: 0.999… = (9/10)/(9/10) = 1.

### THE LAWFUL INVENTIONS — zero, negatives, the tamed infinite

LAW: zero is the number that changes no sum (a + 0 = a);
  negatives complete subtraction: for every a there is −a with
  a + (−a) = 0. Both were INVENTED, resisted, and admitted
  because the books balance better with them (history below,
  scars in the mirror).
WORKED (WHY MINUS TIMES MINUS IS PLUS — a proof, not a decree):
  0 = (−1) × 0 = (−1) × (1 + (−1)) = (−1) + (−1)(−1).
  So (−1)(−1) must be +1, or the distributive law dies. The rule
  of signs is the PRICE of keeping the laws whole, not a whim.
LAW: dividing by zero stays forbidden — if 1/0 were a number z,
  then 0×z = 1; but 0×z = 0 always, and with 0 = 1 every number
  equals every other. The ban is load-bearing.
LAW (CLOCK ARITHMETIC): counting can lawfully wrap: on a 12-hour
  face, 9 + 5 = 2. Remainders form a complete little arithmetic
  of their own — the nines check above runs on the 9-face, and
  modern secrecy (engineering) runs on faces with hundreds of
  digits.
LAW (SIZES OF THE INFINITE): pairing is the test of same-size —
  and the even numbers pair perfectly with ALL numbers (n ↔ 2n),
  so an infinite thing can match its own half. What broke the
  old rule "the whole is greater than the part" (mirror) became
  the DEFINITION of infinite here.

### SYMMETRY — the moves that change nothing

LAW: the symmetries of a thing are all the moves that leave it
  looking untouched; they compose (two moves make a move), each
  undoes (every move has its reverse), and "do nothing" counts.
  Anything obeying those three laws — moves, numbers, shuffles —
  is one kind of object, and facts proved once transfer whole.
WORKED: an equilateral triangle has 6 symmetries (3 turns
  counting rest, 3 flips); a square has 8; a regular n-gon 2n.
  Count them by where a marked corner can land (n places) times
  face-up or flipped (2).
LAW (SYMMETRY AS ECONOMY): what is symmetric need only be solved
  once per family — one octant of the circle draws all eight.
  Physics holds the deep copy: each symmetry of its laws pays
  out a conserved quantity (its file, its terms).

## METHODS
How things are done and built here, followable — the
prepositioned how-to-build.

- COUNT BY GROUPING: tally, then bundle — fives, tens, sixties;
  place value is this method frozen into notation. Works on
  anything discrete; breaks on the continuous, where measuring
  (ratio to a unit) takes over.
- ESTIMATE, THEN BRACKET: round the inputs, compute the easy
  neighbor, and state the direction of the error — 47×36 is
  "under 50×36 = 1,800, over 45×36 = 1,620." An answer with a
  bracket beats a naked exact answer that might be a slip.
- CHECK BY THE UNDO: subtraction re-adds, division re-multiplies,
  roots re-square; every inverse pair is a free proofreader.
  Add the nines check for long chains; know it misses swaps.
- THE RULE OF THREE: set the known ratio against the wanted one
  and cross-multiply — the workhorse of doses, prices, maps,
  recipes; breaks the moment the relation is not actually
  proportional (state the condition, then use it).
- NAME THE UNKNOWN: call it x, write what is claimed about it as
  an equation, and do to both sides only what preserves balance
  until x stands alone. Algebra is bookkeeping with a suspect
  in custody.
- DRAW THE PICTURE: a line for a quantity, a square for a
  product, similar triangles for the unreachable — the shadow
  method: a 1 m stick casts 1.5 m, the tree casts 12 m, so the
  tree is 12 ÷ 1.5 = 8 m. Geometry answers what no ladder
  reaches.
- WORK BACKWARDS: from the wanted end, invert each step to the
  start — the method of mazes, of untangling, of "what must the
  price have been."
- CLIMB BY INDUCTION: prove the first rung and the climb, own
  the whole ladder (SCIENCE holds the rule and a worked case).
- ASSUME IT AND BREAK IT: contradiction — grant the doubted
  thing, follow the laws to a collision, conclude it was never
  true (the √2 proof is the exemplar).
- COUNT WITHOUT COUNTING: pair things off (Gauss's 50 pairs of
  101 = 5,050); multiply choices (3 shirts × 4 trousers = 12
  outfits); subtract the unwanted from the total. Counting
  cleverly is the difference between arithmetic and combinatorics.
- SAMPLE THE WHOLE: when the population is unreachable, draw
  fairly and let 1/√n price the honesty of the estimate. Breaks
  when the draw is not fair — a big biased sample is precision
  about the wrong thing.
- ITERATE TO THE ANSWER: guess, improve by a rule, repeat until
  the change is below care — Heron's root is the pattern; most
  modern computing is this method wearing different rules.
- SURPASSED AND KEPT: Roman numerals still number the faces of
  things; the abacus still teaches place value through fingers;
  log tables and the slide rule still show WHY multiplying is
  adding in disguise. Inferior for computing, best for some
  queries — the model says they stay, so they stay.

## MEANS
What the methods run on, with the numbers that matter.

- FINGERS AND TALLY — the first instruments; base ten is the
  hands' signature written into the numeral.
- THE ABACUS — place value in beads; carried the arithmetic of
  empires for ~2,000 years and still beats a phone for a
  practiced hand on plain sums.
- POSITIONAL NUMERALS — ten digits and the zero: the single
  greatest computing device ever adopted; it made the carry RULE
  possible on paper, and paper cheaper than a counting board.
- PAPER AND THE ALGORITHM — the four operations as marching
  orders (SCIENCE holds them worked); a clerk who understands
  nothing and follows exactly still gets the right answer — that
  discovery is the seed of the machine below.
- COMPASS AND STRAIGHTEDGE — the honest pair: they draw every
  circle and line, bisect anything, build square roots at will —
  and each step can only solve a square-root-deep equation, a
  limit that certifies three famous cannots (mirror holds them
  indexed by this very condition).
- THE EQUALS SIGN AND THE LETTER x — notation as a tool: "="
  (1557, two lines "because no two things can be more equal")
  and the unknown as a letter made equations writable; what can
  be written compactly can be manipulated lawfully.
- LOG TABLES AND THE SLIDE RULE — multiplication turned into
  addition (log ab = log a + log b); three digits of accuracy,
  which built most of the pre-electronic world. Surpassed, kept.
- THE CALCULATOR AND THE COMPUTER — exact within limits; the
  limit is real: machine decimals are binary underneath and 0.1
  has no finite binary form, so 0.1 + 0.2 lands a hair off 0.3.
  Condition on the mean: state the tolerance or inherit the
  mirror's floating-point entry.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject answers — the doors a query enters by.

- COUNT AND KEEP TRACK: herds, grain, debts, days — the birth
  purpose; writing itself began as accounting marks (history).
- DIVIDE FAIRLY: land, inheritance, bread — fractions and ratios
  exist because "equal shares" had to be computable and provable
  to the disputants.
- MEASURE THE UNREACHABLE: the tree by its shadow, the earth by
  two shadows — one summer noon, a well at one city and a 7.2°
  shadow at another: 7.2° is 1/50 of the circle, the cities lie
  ~800 km apart, so the earth is ~40,000 km around (Eratosthenes,
  ~250 BC, right to within a few percent with sticks).
- PREDICT: the calendar, the eclipse, the tide, the trajectory —
  write the law as an equation, turn the crank, know the future
  where the law holds.
- PRICE RISK: odds, insurance, the fair premium — expected value
  turns uncertainty from fate into arithmetic (SCIENCE holds the
  casino's exact edge).
- COMPRESS: a formula is a warehouse — n(n+1)/2 holds every
  triangle-sum ever needed; a good notation carries a method to
  strangers unborn. Knowledge that cannot be written compactly
  travels poorly (cooking's mirror learned the same lesson from
  lore).
- CERTAINTY ITSELF: proof answers a want no experiment can —
  knowing that no case, ever, anywhere, will disobey. Bought at
  the price of conditionality on the ground (the mirror sells
  the honest version).
- PLAY: puzzles, perfect numbers, magic squares — wants with no
  use that repeatedly grew uses (primes were play for 2,000
  years; now they lock every message; engineering holds that
  copy).

## HISTORY
How the knowledge arose, in what order, at what cost — the order
carries why.

- Tally before writing: notched bones (~20,000 years old) carry
  counts; when marks on clay tokens became marks about tokens,
  accounting became writing — number is older than the sentence.
- Base sixty in Babylon, base ten nearly everywhere hands
  counted; place value invented rarely — Babylon had it without
  a true zero and suffered ambiguous numerals for centuries.
- Egypt and the rope: 3-4-5 corners, land re-measured after each
  flood — geometry ("earth-measure") named by its purpose.
- Greece turns rules into proofs (~300 BC Euclid compiles): the
  novelty was not the facts but the WARRANTY — each fact chained
  to agreed ground. The diagonal's scandal (√2, mirror) had
