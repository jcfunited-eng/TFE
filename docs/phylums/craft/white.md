# CRAFT — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The anti-mirror of
docs/phylums/craft/color.md: same parts, opposite sign. What didn't hold
carries its WHY in quantities and conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns
move a condition. Nothing here is a tombstone; every entry is a possibility
waiting for its drain. Seed authored by Claude from the same source files as
the color.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot be under
stated conditions.

- DAMASCUS STEEL, THE ORIGINAL — the phylum's shimmer case. Wootz blades
  carried a watered pattern and a temper no European forge matched; the art
  faded out by around 1800 when the particular ore and the unbroken chain of
  hands both ran out. LOST because the knowledge lived in two unfileable
  places at once: a supply chain (that ore carried trace metals — vanadium
  among them — that band the carbides into the pattern) and the hands (a
  forging rhythm nobody wrote down). PARTLY DRAINED: modern analysis found
  the bands and their trace-element condition, and a metallurgist and a smith
  together have forged blades that band and behave like the old ones. Still
  white: the original process chain — we can make A Damascus; nobody can
  certify it is THE Damascus. Filed as the standing warning: what is not
  written dies with its holders.
- ROMAN CONCRETE — harbors two thousand years in seawater while modern marine
  mixes spall in decades; lost with the empire's supply chains and unwritten
  practice. PARTLY DRAINED, recently: the volcanic ash reacts with lime and
  seawater to grow interlocking minerals in the cracks, and the lumps of
  unmixed quicklime — long read as sloppy work — are self-healing reservoirs:
  water reaching a crack wakes them and the crack seals itself. The color's
  law that a repair relocates the fault is what this material answered: it
  repairs itself IN PLACE. Still white: whether modern mixes get the healing
  without the two-thousand-year proof — now under test in the sea.
- GOLD FROM LEAD BY CRAFT — certified impossible UNDER THE CONDITIONS of
  every craft means, and the why is a number. Every tool of the trade —
  fire, hammer, acid — moves whole atoms by pushing on their outer electrons,
  at energies near one electron-volt per bond. Lead to gold means changing
  the NUCLEUS — removing three protons — held at energies about a million
  times larger; the hottest forge (~2,000°C) hands each particle ~0.2
  electron-volts, millions of times short, and no cleverness of arrangement
  closes an energy gap — arrangement was craft's whole power, and the nucleus
  does not care how the electrons are arranged. THE SHIMMER, exactly: move
  the condition and it drained — reactors and accelerators, which buy the
  million-fold energies, have made real gold from mercury and bismuth in
  atom-counts, at costs millions of times the metal's worth. Impossible BY
  CRAFT stands; impossible IN GENERAL fell.
- THE MACHINE THAT REPLACES THE HAND ENTIRELY — tried every generation;
  every generation drained PART. The record, exactly: every set of MOTIONS
  ever fully written has fallen to a machine — loom, lathe, press, numeric
  mill — without exception. The remainder that resists is not motion; it is
  three unwritten things: (1) READING THIS PIECE — a machine executes a
  specification, and this board's grain, this clay's wetness, the flaw an
  inch under this face are not in it; a sensor helps only after someone
  writes which reading means which correction, and that writing is the
  missing thing itself. (2) RECOVERY — when the material disagrees mid-job,
  the plan must be remade inside the window, on the material's clock.
  (3) THE CROOKED ALCOVE — a specification is a description, scribing is a
  copy of reality, and where description fails its executor fails with it.
  Would drain on sensing at the hand's resolution (fingertips read textures
  finer than a thousandth of a millimeter) PLUS set-points acquired from
  demonstration instead of transcription — both arriving in pieces, neither
  whole. The honest ledger: the remainder shrinks every generation and has
  never once reached zero.
- THE UNIVERSAL MATERIAL — hard AND tough AND stiff AND light, wanted
  forever. CANNOT BE in one plain substance: hardness comes from stopping the
  slip inside a material, toughness from allowing it — one mechanism pulled
  two ways. DRAINED PARTIALLY the only way the law permits: by arrangement —
  hard skin on tough core, layers trading duties, fibers in glue each doing
  one job. The compromise IS the answer so far; a single substance stays
  white.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is unsettled.

- "GLASS IS A SLOW LIQUID — OLD WINDOWS SAGGED" — false: cathedral panes are
  thicker at the bottom because hand-spun glass came out uneven and glaziers
  set the heavy edge down. Glass at room heat flows on timescales past the
  age of the universe; it breaks because every surface carries invisible
  scratches and a crack starts at the worst one.
- "A BETTER GRADE OF STEEL WILL STIFFEN IT" — false: all steels share one
  stiffness; grade buys strength, never flex. The sagging shelf is cured by
  depth's cube law or another material.
- "THE HARD STONE IS THE DURABLE STONE" — false: hardness resists the
  scratch, toughness the blow; flint is glass-hard and shatters, soft
  limestone takes a hammer all day.
- "THREAD COUNT MEASURES QUALITY" — fails past the weave's geometric ceiling:
  threads have thickness and must cross, so only so many fit an inch; numbers
  above that count the plies inside each thread, and many thin weak plies
  make a WORSE cloth. A claim above a ceiling has redefined its terms — the
  trades' cleanest gamed metric.
- "GENUINE LEATHER MEANS GOOD LEATHER" — the label names the LAYER, not the
  quality: strength lives in the tight outer grain; a split hide's fuzzy
  underside coated in plastic and embossed with a printed grain is still the
  weak half.
- UNSETTLED: WHETHER THE HAND'S KNOWLEDGE IS UNWRITABLE, OR ONLY UNWRITTEN —
  the trades say it cannot be put in words; the record says only that it
  never has been. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: the settings
  are continuous and coupled (pressure with angle with speed, each
  conditioned on the felt reply), while words hand over one discrete thing at
  a time — transcription fails where demonstration succeeds, which would mean
  the wall is the CHANNEL, not the knowledge. Would settle on: one full trade
  skill acquired from recorded demonstration alone, no standing-next-to.
- UNSETTLED: LOST SECRETS VERSUS LOST HOURS — the violin, the blade, the
  glaze each carry a legend of a single vanished trick. The Damascus drain
  found a CONDITION (the ore), not a trick. Possibility: most lost arts are
  lost supply chains plus lost hours, and the romance of the recipe hides the
  price list. Would drain per art, the way Damascus half-did.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied science broke.

- LACKING: A WRITTEN FUNCTION FROM TOUCH TO CORRECTION — the color states
  every law of the cut; no written account computes what the fingers do with
  the feedback: which felt change commands which correction, at what gain.
  The subject's largest named absence — why the apprentice hours cannot be
  compressed and why the machine's remainder stands. Would drain the day the
  function is written down and a novice runs it cold; the
  recorded-demonstration test in CLAIMS is the same door from the other side.
- LACKING: A BENCH-PRICE READING OF THE INSIDE — the flaw an inch under the
  stone's face, the cut core behind the rope's intact sheath, the split under
  the composite's unmarked skin. Tapping and listening is the poor hand's
  version, and it is real; instruments that see inside exist at industrial
  prices. White at the workshop's price point; drains on cheap imaging.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE SAMPLE'S NUMBER ON THE REAL PART — strength figures
  transferred from clean coupons to scratched, notched, welded, cold,
  repeatedly-loaded reality; the ships that split in winter seas were this
  transfer's invoice. Lesson filed: a measurement is a statement about its
  own conditions, and the conditions are part of the number.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- FORCING THE BAD FIT — the hammer as corrector fails always: matter cannot
  share space, so force does not reshape a wrong part, it wounds two right
  ones. Drains only backward, to remaking the fit.
- SKIPPING A GRADE IN THE ABRASIVE QUEUE — coarse scratches hide under fine
  shine until the finish lights them up. No shortcut UNDER THE CONDITION that
  each grade removes only the previous grade's depth — which is what a grade
  IS.
- QUENCH WITHOUT TEMPER — the edge left glass-hard and strained cracks in the
  night with nothing having touched it. Paid for centuries before the
  two-step became law: the quench buys the ceiling, the temper buys survival;
  the second step is not optional.
- GLUE ON END GRAIN — the open straws drink the glue inward and the surface
  is never wetted. Drains only by turning the meeting into long grain plus a
  lock — the failure BUILT the joinery.
- SEALING A POROUS WALL TO PROTECT IT — hard render or waterproof coat on old
  stone: drying moves further in, salts crystallize BEHIND the seal, and the
  face blows off in slabs carrying the repair. Backwards under its own
  condition — the wall needed to breathe. Drains to breathable finishes:
  lime, not cement.
- FIRING HOPE INSTEAD OF DRYNESS — damp ware, sealed hollows, uneven walls,
  the door opened early: each a bomb or a crack with its law in the color. No
  drain; these failures are why the schedule is the method.
- EACH GENERATION'S FULL-REPLACEMENT TRY — the automatic workshop, promised
  with the loom, the mill, the printer of parts: each took its motions and
  left the remainder (stated in THINGS). Filed as the map, not mockery: what
  fell was always the written part, so the frontier is precisely the writing.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE ALL-PURPOSE MATERIAL — fell short by law (hard against tough); stands
  as layered and fibered compromises. The color holds the compromises; the
  single substance stays here.
- A TOOL THAT READS THE BOLT'S STRETCH, NOT ITS TWIST — the torque wrench
  measures a proxy and friction eats ~90% at a slippery rate. PARTLY DRAINED:
  stretch-reading fasteners exist for the critical few at many times the
  cost; the ordinary bolt still trades in the proxy, under the color's rules.
- AN INSTRUMENT FOR THE WINDOW — nothing at workshop price reads
  "leather-hard now," "gone off enough to trowel," "the glass has gone
  stiff": the states are read by hand and eye at apprentice prices. Would
  drain on a cheap probe per state; none is written. The cones are the one
  great exception — heat-work made visible for pennies — proving the want is
  answerable.
- A FINISH THAT ENDS THE UPKEEP — every seal is re-fought: paint chalks,
  varnish lifts at the weakest layer beneath, oil walks out of leather. The
  want (protection that does not consume itself) stands open; the Roman lime
  that heals its own cracks is the one entry pointing at how it might drain.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- HAND THE SKILL OVER WITHOUT THE YEARS — 13,440 hours is the standing
  tuition and no written shortcut has ever held. Blocked on the SCIENCE
  absence (no touch-to-correction function); the unsettled claim above names
  what would drain it.
- THE REPAIR THAT DOES NOT RELOCATE THE FAULT — every strong patch moves the
  weak point next door. Answered so far only by choosing WHERE it moves (the
  color's sacrificial designs) and, once, by the self-healing Roman lime —
  the sole entry that repairs in place.
- UNMAKE THE COMPOSITE — glued laminates and fibered plastics cannot be
  separated back into their parts at any price yet written: undoing a mixture
  costs more than making it, and these were made never to come apart.
  Certified under present means; until new chemistry, every permanent joint
  is a decision about a landfill, made at the bench.
- GOLD FROM THE FURNACE — the oldest want at the workshop door, answered
  NEVER at craft energies (the arithmetic in THINGS). Kept as purpose because
  the want built real knowledge: the assay, the acids, the balances —
  alchemy's white funded chemistry's color.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- The lost-arts ledger: Damascus, the Roman sea-concrete, and a long tail of
  glazes, varnishes, and tempers that died when a chain of standing-next-to
  broke — plague, war, a closed trade route, an exhausted ore. The mechanism
  is always the same and is the phylum's central warning: knowledge below
  words has no backup copy. The color's "instruments made settings fileable"
  is the counter-move history finally made.
- The uncounted spoiled work: every law in the color was paid for in ruined
  pieces nobody tallied — quenches that cracked, kilns that took a month's
  ware in one burst, blocks split on a hidden vein a stroke from finished.
  The methods carry no inventor's name because the price was spread across
  every bench for centuries; this page is where that spending is acknowledged
  as the method's true cost.
- Alchemy's centuries: the grandest failed try in craft's record —
  transmutation pursued with furnace and acid for a thousand years. It never
  once worked, and the trying built the assay balance, the mineral acids, and
  the habit of the repeated experiment: the white shaping the next color,
  exactly as the model says. The impossibility was finally CERTIFIED
  (energies, in THINGS) rather than merely despaired of — and the
  certification is knowledge the despair never was.
- The machine's generations: each replacement try filed with what it took and
  what it left, the remainder shrinking but never zero. The record refuses
  both romances — the hand was not sacred (its motions fell), the machine was
  not complete (the reading never fell). The frontier has held one shape the
  whole time: whatever gets truly written, falls.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, THEN REFOUNDED: CRAFT ↔ BELIEF — transmutation was a belief wearing
  an apron; the relation broke on the energy arithmetic and refounded as
  chemistry. The lesson runs both ways: a craft claim is testable or it is a
  creed.
- RAN BACKWARD FOR MILLENNIA, NOW REBUILT: CRAFT ↔ PHYSICS AND CHEMISTRY —
  the how outran the why from the first kiln to the modern forge; the
  relation now carries traffic both directions, and the color's SCIENCE
  section is that road, paved.
- SUSPECTED, HALF-PROVEN: CRAFT ↔ COMPUTING — the jig grew into the program
  and the program keeps reaching for the whole hand; every written motion has
  crossed, the reading of the material has not. Dotted at exactly the
  unsettled claim above; drains — or is certified — with it.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: CRAFT ↔ MIND — whether the reflex below words is a
  different KIND of knowledge or only a different encoding. The trades'
  teaching economy (years, standing-next-to, watching not enough) is evidence
  of something; which thing, nothing certain — the possibility and its test
  are filed in CLAIMS, and mind's phylum holds the other end of the wire.
