# ELECTRONICS — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/electronics/color.md: same parts,
opposite sign. Seed authored by Claude from
docs/fabric_phylums/145_deep_electronics.md and
docs/fabric_phylums/85_deep_heat_light_electricity.md. What didn't
hold carries its WHY in quantities and conditions, so it recomputes
when new knowns move a condition. Nothing here is a tombstone.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE PERPETUAL BATTERY (and every free-energy circuit) — CANNOT
  under the condition that energy is never created. A battery is a
  chemical tank, not a spring of charge; a coil-and-magnet loop
  cannot feed itself because whatever is induced OPPOSES its cause —
  if it helped its cause, the loop would run for free, and the
  induction law is built so it does not. Every measured claim in the
  long parade has resolved into a hidden source (a warm room, a
  mains trickle, a wound spring) or a meter reading only part of the
  loop. Would drain on: overturning conservation itself, of which
  nothing is written in any phylum. Filed prominently because the
  want never dies and the queries keep arriving.
- THE ROOM-TEMPERATURE, ORDINARY-PRESSURE SUPERCONDUCTOR — OPEN,
  with the gap stated exactly. That superconduction is real is
  settled color (zero toll, currents persisting for years). The
  records: at ordinary pressure the best ceramics superconduct near
  −140 °C — about 160 degrees short of a 20° room. Near room warmth,
  hydrogen-rich compounds have reached about −20 °C, but only
  squeezed to roughly 1.7 million atmospheres — a pressure no wire
  or grid can live at. One material must hold BOTH conditions at
  once, and none is written; a famous ambient claim collapsed under
  replication within weeks (see HISTORY). The prize is computable
  from the color: line loss goes as I² × R, and R = 0 zeroes the
  term — power shipped, stored, and magnets run without the heat
  tax. Would drain on: the double condition met; every year of new
  materials re-asks it.
- THE NOISELESS AMPLIFIER — CANNOT above the cold floor: noise is
  warm matter jostling, present in a perfect circuit — a plain
  10,000-ohm resistor at room warmth hisses about two millionths of
  a volt across hearing's band before any amplifier touches it.
  PARTIALLY DRAINED as the shimmer predicts: chill the first stage
  and the floor sinks — radio astronomy bathes its receivers near
  the cold floor to hear faint stars. Even there charge arrives in
  lumps and the graininess remains; the full drain is not written.
- THE PERFECT SWITCH — zero drop closed, zero leak open, zero time
  to change, forever: every real switch fails at least one. Contacts
  bounce and wear; transistors leak open, drop closed, and spend
  heat crossing the middle. The transistor is the near-miss that
  built the world anyway; the remainder of the want stands open.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- "TURN IT UP AND YOU'LL HEAR IT" — false always: gain multiplies
  signal and noise by the same number, and a ratio is unmoved by
  multiplying both halves. The whisper drowned at the cable's far
  end is drowned at every volume. What holds moved to the color:
  collect more at the source, keep noise out on the way in.
- "GROUND IS ZERO EVERYWHERE" — false in every real circuit: ground
  is metal with resistance carrying every return current, so two
  points both called ground sit at different voltages, and a signal
  measured against the wrong one has the difference added straight
  in. The commonest fault in the subject BECAUSE the schematic draws
  ground as a symbol with no properties — attention follows what is
  drawn.
- "MORE BITS MEANS MORE ACCURATE" — false: bits state how finely the
  ruler divides, not whether its marks are in the right places. A
  sixteen-bit reading from a drifting reference is honestly computed
  to fifteen millionths of a volt and may be true to only a
  thousandth. Precision and accuracy never imply each other.
- "IT'S A SOFTWARE BUG" — often false, filed with its why: a supply
  dipping on a heavy edge, a reset arriving before the voltage is
  up, a noisy input read as a keypress — the program obeys perfectly
  a lie the hardware told. This claim's failure built the color's
  debug-downhill-from-the-supply method.
- "DIGITAL IS CLEAN" — false underneath, always: every edge is an
  analogue voltage climbing a real slope, ringing, reflecting off
  the track's far end. The verdict holds only while the value
  behaves; a flaky board is this claim failing slowly.
- "SAMPLING A BIT TOO SLOW GIVES A ROUGH VERSION" — false, and a
  certified cannot: below two looks per wiggle the machine reports a
  fast thing as a slow one with total confidence — looked at 40,000
  times a second, a 30,000-per-second tone returns as 40,000 −
  30,000 = 10,000, inside the honest band. CONDITIONS: once
  recorded, nothing separates the invented tone from a real one; the
  cure exists only BEFORE the converter. No new known can move
  this — it is arithmetic.
- UNSETTLED: WHY THE WARM SUPERCONDUCTORS SUPERCONDUCT — the cold
  kind is settled color (pairs glued by lattice vibration); the
  ceramics run far too warm for that glue as understood, and after
  decades the mechanism is still argued. Nothing certain — but here
  is a possibility: the pairing glue is magnetic ordering rather
  than lattice vibration, which fits how these materials sit next to
  magnetic phases. Would drain on: a theory that predicts a NEW
  material's transition temperature before it is made — prediction,
  not explanation after the fact — and its draining is the likeliest
  road to the room-temperature entry above.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: A THEORY THAT DESIGNS A SUPERCONDUCTOR — materials are
  found by search and luck, not computed in advance; the missing
  mechanism above is the missing science here. The subject can state
  the prize to the watt and cannot write the recipe.
- LACKING: A DIRECT READING OF A BATTERY'S REMAINING CHARGE — no
  wire carries the answer; only a voltage that sags under load,
  droops with cold, and sits nearly flat across most of the
  discharge of the chemistries used most. Every fuel gauge is a
  model inferring a hidden state, carrying every assumption it was
  built with — why the percentage jumps, lies in winter, and ages
  badly. Would drain on: a cheap probe of the chemistry itself;
  nothing is written.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE ANALOGUE COPY — every stage adds noise, so
  every copy of a copy is worse, and generations of recordings
  decayed to hiss. Drained not by better analogue but by changing
  the question: carry verdicts instead of values (the digital turn),
  at the stated price of throwing the shape away at the door. The
  failure and its price are both knowledge.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- THE SINGLE THRESHOLD ON A NOISY SIGNAL — chatters, necessarily: a
  slow noisy signal crosses one line many times on the way through,
  under the condition that noise rides every signal. DRAINED by two
  thresholds — one up, a lower one back; the failure BUILT the
  color's commit-before-changing-your-mind method, the thermostat's
  gap, every clean button.
- RESCUING THE CABLE'S FAR END WITH GAIN — fails by the ratio law
  (see CLAIMS); drained only by moving the first stage to the
  sensor. Filed because the try recurs in every generation.
- KILLING THE COIL'S SPARK BY OPENING THE SWITCH FASTER — exactly
  backwards: the coil's push is L × (change ÷ time), so halving the
  time DOUBLES the spike; the color's worked case makes 50,000 V
  from a 12-volt circuit in a millionth of a second. Drained by the
  flyback diode: give the current an exit, not a shorter argument.
- ELIMINATING METASTABILITY — CANNOT under its conditions: a signal
  free to change at any instant, sampled by a clock that is a
  stranger to it, can catch the sampler on its balance point, where
  it hangs undecided for an unbounded time — a device with two
  stable states HAS a balance point between them; that is what
  two-state means. Drained in practice, never in principle: each
  added beat of waiting multiplies the odds against enormously, so
  the failure is made rarer than the hardware's lifetime — bought
  with beats, not abolished.
- THE FILTER BOTH SHARP AND QUICK — CANNOT: telling two nearby
  rhythms apart requires watching enough cycles for their difference
  to show, so every gain in steepness is paid in delay and
  edge-wobble; a boundary ten times finer costs about ten times the
  watch. Filed beside its cousin, the amplifier's fixed
  gain-times-speed budget.
- BOTH GAIN AND SPEED FROM ONE STAGE — refused by that budget;
  PARTIALLY DRAINED by cascading stages, each spending its own — at
  the price that each stage's noise enters before the next stage's
  gain. That price is why amplify-at-the-source leads the color's
  METHODS.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- THE DIODE'S FIXED TOLL — a little under a volt taken from every
  crossing (at 2 A: 0.7 × 2 = 1.4 W of pure loss per diode, twice
  that through a bridge). PARTIALLY DRAINED twice: the Schottky
  junction halves the toll (~0.3 V), and synchronous rectification
  replaces the diode with a driven switch dropping almost nothing —
  at the cost of the drive's complexity. The free one-way door
  itself: not written.
- THE UNIVERSAL MEMORY — fast as the cross-coupled pair, dense and
  cheap as the leaky bucket, permanent as a carving: every written
  family fails at least one (the pair is greedy, the bucket forgets
  thousands of times a second, the durable kinds write slowly and
  wear out). Candidates circle; none has taken the crown. Would
  drain on: one cell holding its state unpowered and switching in a
  beat at bucket prices.
- WIRELESS POWER AT ROOM DISTANCE — falls short by geometry: the
  near field works at touching distance (the toothbrush, the phone
  pad — a transformer with its halves in two devices), but an
  unguided field spreads by the square of distance, and a beam tight
  enough to be efficient is tight enough to be dangerous to whatever
  crosses it. Open, with its condition named: efficiency and safety
  pull opposite ways.
- THE INSTRUMENT THAT DOES NOT INTRUDE — every meter takes current,
  every probe hangs capacitance; every measurement is of instrument
  and subject together. Fell short in principle, managed in practice
  by budgeting the intrusion; the zero-touch probe at bench prices
  is not found.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- SHIP AND USE POWER WITHOUT THE HEAT TAX — blocked squarely on the
  superconductor entry: until both conditions meet in one material,
  every amp pays I² × R, and the grid's whole design is the
  arithmetic of minimizing a tax it cannot abolish.
- STORE POWER AT THE GRID'S OWN SCALE — the grid is a market for a
  good that must be made as it is consumed; batteries hold towns for
  hours, not countries for seasons. The want sits jointly with
  chemistry and economy; this phylum holds its corner (conversion,
  regulation) and cannot answer alone.
- A CIRCUIT THAT EXPLAINS ITS OWN FAULT — debugging is still
  inference from outside; wounds are latent (the spark entry), and
  the hottest point is computed, never read. The self-reporting
  circuit is a want, not a method.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- THE FREE-ENERGY PARADE — centuries long, still marching: wheels,
  magnets, and circuits claimed to give more than they take, every
  one resolving under measurement into a hidden source or a partial
  meter. The parade's lesson is the color's law read backwards, and
  it is knowledge: the meter must enclose the WHOLE loop before a
  claim exists at all.
- THE WAR OF THE CURRENTS — direct current lost the grid to the
  transformer's need for change, publicly and bitterly; then the
  surpassed method came back where its virtues rule (undersea links,
  every chip's insides). The subject's cleanest proof of the ruling
  that nothing surpassed is discarded.
- THE AMBIENT-SUPERCONDUCTOR CLAIM THAT DIED IN WEEKS — a material
  (LK-99) announced as the room-temperature answer; laboratories
  worldwide replicated within days, the signals resolved into known
  impurities, and the claim closed in a single season. Filed NOT as
  embarrassment but as the white working at full speed: a drain
  attempted, tested at once, refused with reasons — search never to
  be repeated on that material, the want above still open and
  sharper for it.
- THE INVISIBLE WOUND'S LONG DENIAL — boards failed in service for
  years while handling looked harmless; the spark that wounds
  without killing was doubted precisely because the part still
  worked at test. The strap-and-mat ritual is the record of that
  denial ending; the latent failure entered the color as method only
  after the field paid for the proof.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN AS A NAIVE MAP, REBUILT HONESTLY: ELECTRONICS ↔ MIND — "the
  brain is a circuit" fails on inspection: nerves are slow chemical
  one-way pulses, not wires; memory is not addressed; the clock is
  nowhere. The relation stands rebuilt on what genuinely crosses —
  signals over noise, thresholds, feedback, oscillators — carrying
  its warning label against the wiring-diagram picture.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: THE PRESSURE ROAD TO AMBIENT SUPERCONDUCTION
  — the squeezed hydrides show room-warmth superconduction is not
  forbidden, only expensive; whether the road through a million
  atmospheres leads anywhere ordinary pressure can follow is
  unproven. The relation to chemistry's high-pressure work stands
  dotted, awaiting a material.
- LEAKY BY NATURE: ELECTRONICS ↔ COMPUTING — the clean digital
  abstraction leaks downward whenever the analogue underneath
  misbehaves; computing's correctness proofs are conditional on
  voltages nobody proves. The relation is first-class in the color
  and carries, here, its permanent asterisk.
