## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE RELIABLE JOKE MACHINE — attempted many times (pun
  generators, formula writing rooms); hit rates stay low. WHY: the
  color's arithmetic sizes a snap (bits) and times it (the beat)
  but cannot point it — no written law says WHICH second readings
  amuse a given room rather than confuse, offend, or bore. Would
  drain on: a predictor beating a working comedian's hit rate on
  new material for a known room.
- A GAME FUN FOR EVERYONE — the ancient open want. Every written
  game picks a skill and a temperament, and the seam sits at a
  different height per player; chance flattens skill but bores the
  skilled. Partially drained by machines: video games quietly tune
  difficulty per player. Still white: the shared table where all
  sit at their own seam inside the SAME game. Would drain on: a
  handicap that tunes itself without hiding (a hidden handicap
  breaks the frame — the color's method says so).
- A METER FOR BEAUTY — no instrument reads beauty off an object;
  averaged preferences exist, the unit does not. WHY: the parts
  are mapped (pattern, symmetry, surprise), no working sum
  composes them. Would drain on: a measure predicting a NEW
  object's reception better than a poll of ten strangers.

## CLAIMS
What didn't hold — with why — and what is still unsettled. Every
unsettled entry carries a possibility, as the answer rule demands.

- "THE GOLDEN RATIO IS WHY THINGS ARE BEAUTIFUL" — didn't hold:
  asked to pick the most pleasing rectangle, people do not pick
  1.618 from its neighbors beyond chance. Kept because queries
  arrive carrying it; what holds moved to the color —
  pattern-with-surprise and symmetry-slightly-broken carry the
  load the ratio was credited with.
- "EXPLAIN THE JOKE AND THE LAUGH WILL COME" — cannot, UNDER THE
  CONDITION explanation-before-crossing: explaining raises the
  second reading's expectedness toward 1, and log2(1) = 0 — the
  snap arrives carrying zero bits. The dissection kills the frog;
  the frog is made of the crossing. Stated with a smile: this page
  can compute exactly why it cannot make you laugh. The drain is
  real and in daily use: explain a DIFFERENT joke's mechanism,
  then hand over a fresh one of the same family — teaching comedy
  is precisely this detour.
- "YOU CAN TICKLE YOURSELF" — cannot, UNDER THE CONDITION
  self-caused-and-instantly-predicted: the mind subtracts what it
  predicted from what it feels; zero bits arrive and no rule is
  violated — nothing wrong, no laugh. Drains at the edges exactly
  as the shimmer promises: ~0.2 s of machinery delay between
  command and touch and the tickle partly returns (measured);
  another's hand removes the prediction entirely. The condition,
  not the skin, is the wall.
- UNSETTLED: WHY THE SEAM FEELS LIKE JOY — where it sits is iron
  (color); why joy, nothing certain — but a possibility: the
  feeling may BE the learning meter, pleasure paid where
  information gained per attempt peaks, which is why the seam and
  the fastest learning share an address. Would drain on: felt fun
  tracking measured gain-per-try across tasks.
- UNSETTLED: WHY SYMMETRY PLEASES — the health-signal story grips
  partly, unproven as mechanism; the preference is iron. A second
  possibility: mirrored halves cost half to take in, and saved
  effort reads as pleasure — which would also cover why a SLIGHT
  break pleases more (a small surcharge of news on a cheap
  pattern). Would drain on: preference tracking measured
  processing cost across novel shapes.
- UNSETTLED: WHY CUTENESS COMPELS — partly settled: the trigger
  list is mapped (big eyes, big head, round cheek — the infant's
  proportions), the response crosses species lines, designers
  steer by it (a famous cartoon mouse was redrawn younger for
  decades). Open: why the pull spills onto cars, kettles, and
  typefaces that can never be cared for, and why the urge to
  SQUEEZE rides along with the urge to protect. Possibility: a
  care reflex keyed to proportion rather than kinship, the squeeze
  its overshoot. Would drain on: measurements separating the care
  from the grip on objects outside all possible care.
- UNSETTLED: WHAT LAUGHTER ORIGINALLY WAS — the cousin species
  pant in rough play where people ha-ha; possibility: laughter is
  the play-pant made into a signal, the frame given a sound. Grips
  as anatomy and observation, unproven as lineage. Would drain on:
  comparative work across the ape line tying pant to laugh.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: THE DIRECTION OF FUNNY — the color gives a reveal's
  size (bits) and moment (the beat) and cannot give its direction:
  no law selects which violations read benign to a given room. The
  subject's largest named absence — and the one this phylum is
  honestly glad of, since the fully written law would end the
  profession it describes. The limit and the smile file together.
- LACKING: A SUM FOR BEAUTY — parts mapped, no working sum; the
  same shape as cooking's missing theory of taste (its white names
  it too — both fields build well and predict poorly). The two
  drain together if either finds how mapped parts compose into one
  felt verdict.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE INSTRUMENT DISTURBS THE PLAY — put a
  lab's frame around play and the play thins; observed play is
  part performance, mandated play none at all. The receipts that
  survived are those taken from outside the frame — chirps,
  budgets, deprivation damage. Standing caution: in this subject,
  method design must protect the frame or it measures something
  else.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- FUN BY MANDATE — the scheduled morale event, attendance taken:
  play is chosen or it is not play; the sign-up sheet deletes the
  choosing, and the overjustification arithmetic (color: rewarded
  drawing fell to ~half) runs on whatever fun was left. CANNOT
  under the condition of obligation — and drains the instant the
  choosing is real: the same picnic, optional and unscored, can be
  play. The condition is the whole entry.
- THE LAUGH TRACK — engineering on the true contagion law:
  recorded company for the solitary viewer. Worked for decades,
  then rooms learned to hear the can, and counterfeit safety began
  reading as a broken frame — the method aged into its own joke.
  The contagion stays iron in the color; the counterfeit's failure
  stays here. Would drain on company that is real (watching
  together at a distance) — which is not a laugh track at all.
- CHASING THE HIT BY FORMULA — sequels, joke templates, cloned
  games: the formula carries the pattern half and cannot carry the
  surprise half; by the compression law every faithful copy raises
  p and drops the bits toward wallpaper. A description already
  given pays no news. No drain while the law of surprise stands;
  the honest road is new material on old bones — the callback, the
  variation — which is the color's method, not this one.
- WINNING BY SOLVING — solve the game and you end it: tic-tac-toe
  in every childhood, checkers by years of computation. The
  solution is real knowledge and files as such; what failed is the
  want behind it — winning AT PLAY by removing the play. The
  warning shape: some wants consume their object. Recomputes per
  game by the counting law: a game near 10⁴⁴ positions is safe
  from this fate for any lifetime that will query this fabric.
- BEATING THE HOUSE BY STAKING PATTERN — the doubling system:
  double after each loss so the first win repays all. The
  arithmetic that buries it: after 10 straight losses the stakes
  laid are 1+2+4+…+512 = 1,023 units with 1,024 now demanded —
  against a table cap and a finite purse, while expected value
  stays −1/6 (or the house's chosen edge) on every roll regardless
  of history. WHY it seduces: a run of ten feels impossible, yet
  at even odds it arrives about once per thousand sequences, and
  the player who meets it loses more than all prior winnings.
  CANNOT under negative-EV-per-play + finite purse; no staking
  pattern moves an expected value. Would drain only on a game
  whose EV is not negative — which the house, by construction,
  does not offer.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- NOT YET FOUND: A SEAM METER — no gauge reads too-easy / right /
  too-hard off a player at the table. Video games infer it from
  deaths-per-minute and retune (partial drain, machine-side); the
  tabletop, classroom, and workbench still tune by argument. Would
  drain on: an engagement reading taken without disturbing the
  frame — this want and SCIENCE's instrument caution are one item.
- FELL SHORT: THE UNIVERSAL PARTY GAME BOX — every fun-for-all
  title in fact picks its temperament and its seam; the box ships
  a compromise wearing the open want's label (the want is filed
  under THINGS). Kept because "why was game night miserable"
  queries land here: the box was never the failure — the pairing
  was.
- FELL SHORT: PROPORTION TABLES AS BEAUTY'S RECIPE — academies
  legislated beauty with measured canons; each works inside its
  own walls and dies outside them. WHY: a canon is pure pattern,
  and the live zone needs the surprise half, which no table can
  hold — tabled, it is expected. The pattern half codifies; the
  surprise half never has.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- MAKE THIS PERSON LAUGH, GUARANTEED — cannot as stated: needs the
  direction-of-funny law (lacking) plus a frame no stranger fully
  grants on demand. The working approximation — know the room, lay
  the frame, spend callbacks — is the color's craft, and it tops
  out at likely, never guaranteed.
- BOTTLE THE SEAM — the vanished-hours feeling on demand, without
  task or skill. Chemistry can force pleasure's neighbors and each
  forcing collects its debt (medicine's ledger); the honest road
  remains a real task at the real seam. Possibility: the seam may
  be unbottleable in principle, because the feeling IS the doing
  at the right height (the joy-meter possibility in CLAIMS); if
  that drains true, this want closes as a cannot.
- END BOREDOM FOR GOOD — boredom returns at every exhausted
  pattern, by the compression law. Open — and possibly a cannot
  worth keeping: a possibility, filed — boredom is the hunger that
  makes the next pattern worth finding; remove it and play's
  engine loses its fuel. Would drain on: sustained play
  demonstrated with the hunger removed.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- The long dismissal: "just playing" kept play unstudied for
  generations while it carried the species' rehearsal; the cost of
  not looking left no record of what wasn't found. The correction
  came by instruments (the chirp above our hearing) and by
  deprivation damage. The color holds the arrival; this page holds
  the delay.
- Beauty's rulebooks, drawered: every era's measured canon, tried
  in earnest, kept as style history — the pattern half codifying
  while the surprise half escapes, again and again; the repetition
  is itself the evidence that the escape is structural (see
  MEANS).
- The gamblers' graveyard: centuries of staking systems against a
  fixed negative edge, each buried by the same arithmetic, most
  rediscovered fresh by every generation. The mathematics that
  ended the argument was born at the same table — play handed the
  serious world the tool that condemned play's worst habit. The
  color banks the gift; this page keeps the graves, with their
  1,023.
- The laugh track's full arc: honest engineering, decades of
  service, slow death by audience learning — a method aged not
  because its law broke but because the room changed. Standing
  proof that in play the audience is part of the machine, and a
  method can be outlived by its own success.
