# UPKEEP — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/upkeep/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign; every entry carries its WHY in quantities and conditions so it
recomputes when new knowns move a condition. Seed authored by Claude
from the same four source files as the color.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE MAINTENANCE-FREE MACHINE — certified CANNOT under conditions:
  moving parts in contact, under load, of finite material. Wear
  removes atoms at every touch (the color's half-nanometer per
  footstep) and no material has infinite atoms; sealed bearings and
  better oil stretch the interval, never abolish it. DRAINS where a
  condition falls: remove the contact (magnetic bearings,
  no-moving-parts electronics) and the interval leaps outward —
  then the drains' own fine print arrives: capacitors dry, fans
  spin, contacts corrode. The wage is moved, not ended.
- THE SELF-CLEANING HOME — the standing want, partially drained
  three ways, whole in none: the self-cleaning oven burns its dirt
  near 500°C (the whole box becomes the cleaner, at a real energy
  bill); the robot vacuum drains only the open floor; the
  grime-shedding coating works while intact and is a mask —
  scratch is a door. The full want cannot close under the founding
  law: dirt is matter out of place, and sorting matter takes
  directed work. Would drain on: cheap general directed work — a
  hand that sorts. Factories have such hands under fixed
  conditions; the home's endless variety is the unmet condition.
- THE PERMANENT COATING — paint that seals forever. Cannot as a
  mask: iron's hunger never leaves, every skin eventually cracks,
  and the flaw recruits. DRAINED PARTIALLY by changing principle:
  galvanizing's zinc volunteers to corrode first — a paid decoy,
  consumed on schedule — which is upkeep again, longer interval.

## CLAIMS
What didn't hold — with why — and what is still unsettled.

- "BUY IT ONCE AND BE DONE" — never held. WHY: ownership charges a
  continuous cost (storage, care, deciding) daily whether used or
  not, and the thing wears by the atoms-per-touch law.
- "STAINLESS CANNOT RUST" — fails under salt plus scratching: its
  self-healing skin needs oxygen's reach and no crowding salts;
  spray and scratch revive the slow fire. Stain-LESS, not never.
- "THE RESTART FIXES IT" — overreached: holds only while sickness
  lives in short-term state. Written to the bones (corrupted store,
  worn part), the restart is a ritual. The condition is the claim.
- "MORE WILLPOWER NEXT TIME" — measured false daily: a resolution
  is a plan to be a different person under identical conditions.
  Drains only by moving the conditions — the biscuits elsewhere,
  the fence instead of the watching.
- "MORE SHELVES WILL FIX THE CLUTTER" — false by the container law:
  bounded space, unbounded inflow, fills at any size. Only a rule
  at the door holds; the bigger house is the same arithmetic,
  slower.
- UNSETTLED: THE RIGHT INSPECTION INTERVAL FOR A NEW MACHINE — too
  seldom buys the dear repair; too often introduces the faults it
  hunts (seals re-seated badly, threads worn by the wrench).
  Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: set the first
  interval by consequence (the color's replace-or-run law), then
  let counted failures shorten or stretch it — the schedule as a
  measurement in progress. Would settle on: failure counts per
  interval for the machine's family.
- UNSETTLED: IS SLEEP THE BODY'S NIGHTLY RESTART — the shape
  matches (throw the day's tangle away, re-walk from the rehearsed
  beginning); the mechanism is unproven. Nothing certain — but here
  is a possibility: waking refreshed is short-term state cleared,
  and what sleep cannot fix is what is written to the bones — the
  restart's own boundary. Would drain on: the body's mechanism
  evidence.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks; where applied science broke.

- LACKING: A LAW FOR WHEN THIS ONE FAILS — the field computes
  populations (intervals, odds, rates) and cannot compute this
  bearing's Tuesday; single-thing failure stays a chance number,
  not a trajectory. Partial drain in progress: instruments that
  listen to a machine's vibration are dragging single-failure
  prediction toward measurement in industry — not yet at household
  prices. Would drain on: the cheap ear.
- LACKING: AN ARITHMETIC OF ATTENTION — the color runs on "noticing
  is the real work" without a unit for it; interruption cost and
  decision fatigue are measured only crudely and cannot be budgeted
  as money and hours are. Until the unit exists, the invisible work
  stays invisible in every ledger. Would drain on: the unit — a
  price per interruption and per open loop that a household ledger
  could carry next to its dollars and hours.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: EFFICIENCY THAT REMOVED ALL SLACK — schedule
  science stripped the buffers because idle looked like waste; then
  one late thing passed its delay downstream in full and the day
  collapsed. The variation math was in the books and left out of
  the plan. Filed: slack is load-bearing.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions.

- THE ANNUAL BLITZ AS A SYSTEM — the great purge as the whole of
  upkeep. Fails on arithmetic: disorder accrues daily, the blitz
  runs yearly on a good week, and between them the deferral factor
  multiplies. Stands only as a reset, never as the wage.
- STREAK-KEEPING — never-miss-once as the rule. One break destroys
  the identity the rule was built on, and destroyed identities do
  not restart. Superseded by never-miss-twice (the color holds it).
- THE HEAD AS THE LIST — tried by everyone first. The unwritten
  item keeps announcing itself, and the announcing is the cost;
  recall reconstructs, worst on the worst day. Drains only to paper
  — and only with the reread, or the paper is where things go to
  be lost.
- MORE TAPE ON THE PEELING PATCH — fails by geometry: a peel
  attacks one thin line at a time whatever the thickness; only
  turning the load flat, or a through-fastener, changes it.
- FORCING THE SEIZED BOLT COLD — the snapped-stud drawer. Fails
  when the rust bond outholds the bolt's waist; the drains (oil
  plus time, tap, heat) are the color's method, and this entry is
  why.
- ORGANIZING BY PURCHASE — bins and apps instead of a door-rule and
  a habit. Containers without a rule relocate the pile, now with a
  receipt. Would drain on nothing; this one is arithmetic.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- NOT YET FOUND: AN INSTRUMENT FOR HIDDEN WATER — damp in the wall
  announces late, after the rot; meters exist but the wall must
  already be suspected. The want: a house that says where it leaks
  before the ceiling does. Partial drains: leak sensors under the
  known risks (heater pan, washer hose).
- FELL SHORT: THE UNIVERSAL TOOL — one tool carries every job
  badly: grip geometry and force-per-area oppose universality. Kept
  under its true condition: in the pocket it beats the toolbox
  left at home.
- FELL SHORT: PAYING TO STOP THINKING ABOUT IT — the subscription
  as a memory replacement. The renewal drifts upward (the loyalty
  penalty is designed around the not-looking), so the mean quietly
  bills for the attention it promised to remove. Works only WITH
  the annual review it was bought to replace.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- END THE WAGE — upkeep abolished: the want behind the
  maintenance-free machine and the self-cleaning home. The field's
  own founding law forbids its dearest wish: disorder wins unless
  directed work is paid, and every drain found so far (coatings,
  robots, ovens, decoys) MOVES the wage — to energy, money, a
  longer interval — and none ends it. The shimmer applies only
  where the condition (cheap directed work) moves.
- MAKE NOTICING TRANSFERABLE — hand over a household without
  handing over the head. Blocked on the lacking arithmetic of
  attention; partially drained by the written binder (accounts,
  renewals, one place per kind), which transfers the facts and
  still not the noticing.
- WHY ENTROPY FEELS PERSONAL — filed with a smile, honestly. The
  house does not know you; the socks are not conspiring; the law is
  indifferent. Yet every generation of householders reports being
  singled out. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: order
  carries your signature (the one arrangement in ninety-five
  trillion you paid hours for) and mess carries none, so disorder
  can only ever destroy something of yours and never make you a
  gift — every undirected change reads as theft because, in your
  house, you are the only one pushing the other way. The physics is
  settled; the feeling belongs to mind, and this entry is the
  bridge filed from this side.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side.

- WD-40's thirty-nine dead formulas — the can carries its white in
  its own name: water displacement, attempt forty. The cleanest
  small proof that the color's conveniences stand on filed
  failures.
- The intervals are written in wreckage: hours-between-inspections,
  the wired fastener, the checklist — each bought by machines that
  shook apart, burned, or fell with their crews before the rule
  existed. The schedule's numbers are the crash record, compressed.
- The checklist was resisted first — it read as an insult to
  professional memory — and the resisting years were paid in
  flights and operations until the record won. Every household
  re-runs the resistance in miniature.
- Deferral is re-learned personally, every generation: the cheap
  year of skipping feels like proof that skipping is safe, so the
  compounding lesson inherits poorly — its early evidence points
  the wrong way. The color's 80× receipt is the inheritance this
  entry keeps trying to hand over.
