## THINGS
The units this subject is about; other homes named for the cascade.

- DIRT AND DUST — matter out of place (soil in the field is wealth,
  on the carpet filth; cleaning re-sorts, never destroys) and the
  air's slow river of shed skin and soot, landing wherever air goes
  still. Also: farming, chemistry, weather, astronomy.
- WEAR AND RUST — the two slow eaters: every use rubs a little off,
  parts made to touch eat each other; oxygen patiently marries iron
  back to ore — burning without flame. Also: physics, chemistry,
  body (cartilage is the body's bearing), earth.
- WATER — the house's patient enemy and best cleaner: leak, damp,
  fog on cold glass. Also: weather, building, physics, cooking.
- OIL AND SOAP — oil's whole job is that moving parts never quite
  touch; soap is the paid mediator, one molecule-end holding water,
  the other fat. Also: chemistry, engineering, cooking, medicine.
- THE FASTENER — screw, bolt, tape, zipper: force parked and held;
  a screw is a wound spring on a ramp, parked by friction alone.
  Also: engineering, building, physics.
- THE TOOL — intention given shape: a hand's force delivered
  exactly where the job requires — a decision made once, in metal,
  for all later hands. Also: craft, design, engineering.
- THE SPARE — the failure answered in advance: bulb, fuse, key,
  bought BEFORE the Sunday night that needs them. Also: survival,
  medicine (the rescue dose), transport (the sea kit).
- THE LIST — memory moved to paper so the head goes quiet:
  checklist, routine, the one shared calendar. Also: mind, flight,
  medicine, teaching.
- THE HOUR — the one stock that leaves and does not come back:
  money returns, things are replaced, the hour is not. Also:
  economy, physics (time runs one way), mind.
- THE EARLY WARNING — new noise, damp patch, hairline crack: most
  failures announce themselves early and quietly. Also: medicine,
  measurement.

## CLAIMS
What holds, written to be tested by a crossing query.

- Left alone, everything drifts toward mess — dust settles, edges
  dull, weeds return. Order is not a state but an upkeep, bought
  and bought again; SCIENCE holds the arithmetic. And cleaning
  moves matter, never erases it: "away" is always a place — the
  bin, the drain, someone's river.
- Failures announce early, so scheduled looking buys the cheap
  repair; a skipped repair is a loan from the machine at ruinous
  interest — the leak rots the beam, the beam shifts the wall.
  Prevention beats cure by orders of magnitude, because failure
  cascades and prevention does not.
- The flaw recruits: a scratch in paint is a door, and rust born
  there takes several times the room of the metal it ate, heaving
  the paint at its own edges — one family with infection through
  broken skin and cracks starting at corners.
- Vibration loosens screws: each shake is a split second of
  weightlessness for the threads; stored tension walks the screw a
  hair back, and a million small slips is a loose bolt — why
  aircraft wire fasteners shut: slips forbidden by wire, not hope.
- A cheap tool hides its true price: it dies sooner (buy twice) and
  damages the work first — rescuing a chewed bolt is never sold
  cheap.
- Righty-tighty is agreement hardened into hardware — true by
  unanimity alone; exceptions sit where a machine's own spin would
  unscrew it (the bike's left pedal tightens as you ride).
- Cold slows chemistry: a chilled battery holds charge it cannot
  serve fast — dead at forty percent in the snow, awake indoors.
- Urgent means noisy now; important means it changes something
  later. Almost nothing is both, the noisy wins by default — so
  maintenance loses to today's job unless it is scheduled. And a
  house has a maintenance schedule whether or not anyone writes
  one: each interval is kept or ignored, and ignoring is choosing
  to pay later at a much worse rate.
- Noticing is the real work of a household — milk low, form due,
  seal weeping. Invisible, rarely counted, untransferred with the
  task; kept in one head it is a single point of failure.
- What you paid is gone and says nothing about keeping: the
  expensive mistake is kept BECAUSE it was expensive. Gifts differ:
  the object stands in for a person, and that meaning is what is
  kept.
- Storage is fixed, acquisition is not: a container fills to any
  size given (shed, disk, new shelf). Only a rule at the door —
  one in, one out — holds.
- Every estimate imagines the clean run — the only version anyone
  can picture — while the record says otherwise; work expands to
  fill the time given; and a day packed edge to edge cannot absorb
  one late thing — slack is not waste, it converts a problem into
  an inconvenience.
- Standards slip one notch at a time: each thing left undone makes
  the next easier to leave; the standard is kept in the small
  cases, where it feels like it does not matter.
- Systems beat resolutions: intentions renew daily, arrangements
  are made once and keep working on days nobody is trying. A system
  is only real if it works in a bad week; and one missed day is
  weather, two is a new pattern — never miss twice.

## SCIENCE
The math, physics, chemistry of upkeep, held — not mentioned: LAW
to compute with, RULE to follow, WORKED with the arithmetic run.

### PHYSICS — why mess wins

LAW: order is few arrangements, mess is many; a change nobody
  directs lands among the many. Tidy-by-chance odds = 1 over (easy
  places per thing)^(number of things).
WORKED: 20 things, each with 5 easy landing places and 1 right one:
  (1/5)^20 = 1 in 95,367,431,640,625 — one day in ~95 trillion of
  random put-downs ends tidy. Mess needs no help; order needs a
  hand, on a schedule, forever. This field is that law's wage.

### THE ARITHMETIC OF DEFERRAL — cheap insurance

LAW: while damage recruits neighbors, the bill grows by a FACTOR
  per stage: cost after n skipped stages ≈ first cost × g^n, g
  ~4-5 in wood-and-water cases.
RULE: pay the smallest bill on schedule; a skipped interval
  multiplies the bill, never adds.
WORKED (~rough by nature): gutters cleaned ~$150. Skip a year:
  fascia soaked, ~$600 (×4). Another: rot in soffit and wall top,
  ~$3,000 (×5). Another: water in the basement, ~$12,000 (×4).
  Model: 150 × 4³ = 150 × 64 = $9,600, tracking the ~$12,000
  receipt; end to end, 12,000 ÷ 150 = 80 first-bills.
LAW: a risk's yearly price = odds × consequence; prevention is
  bought insurance. WORKED: oil change ~$60; skipping risks even
  ~5%/yr of a ~$6,000 engine: 0.05 × 6,000 = $300 expected against
  $60 paid — five to one. The smoke alarm runs the same law with a
  life on the consequence side, unpriced.

### CHEMISTRY — cleaning is matching the dirt

LAW: like dissolves like; grease refuses water. Soap holds both —
  fat-end buried in grease, water-end out — so grease rides away
  inside soap's escorts.
LAW: acid eats mineral — limescale is chalk-kin, unmade into
  dissolved salt and gas (the fizz is the crust leaving). Base eats
  grease — a strong base splits fat into soap and glycerin: the
  oven cleaner turns burnt grease into soap that washes itself off.
RULE: mineral crust → acid (vinegar, lemon); grease → base (soda,
  oven cleaner); everyday film → soap and water. NEVER mix bleach
  with acid or ammonia: the mixture exhales poison gas.
LAW: cleaning speed ~doubles per 10°C. WORKED: dishwater 60°C vs
  20°C: 2^((60−20)/10) = 2^4 = 16× faster on grease — why
  dishwater is hot.
WORKED (dilution): bleach ~5%; sanitizing wants ~0.05% — factor
  5 ÷ 0.05 = 100, so 1,000 ml ÷ 100 = 10 ml per liter. Stronger is
  not cleaner, just more poison to rinse.

### PHYSICS — wear and friction

LAW: material worn grows with load × distance rubbed; halve either,
  halve the wear. Oil makes the metal-on-metal distance near zero.
WORKED: a stone step dished 2 cm in 600 years: 20 mm ÷ 600 ≈ 0.033
  mm/yr. At ~200 footfalls/day, 200 × 365 = 73,000/yr: 33 µm ÷
  73,000 ≈ 0.00045 µm ≈ half a nanometer — one or two atom-layers
  per footfall. Wear is atoms leaving at every touch; it never
  rests, so neither does the schedule.
LAW: a thread is a ramp wrapped in a circle, and only friction
  parks a load on a ramp: it holds while the ramp's slope stays
  under friction's grip. WORKED: a common bolt climbs ~1.5 mm per
  ~28 mm lap — slope ~3°, needing grip ~0.05 against dry metal's
  ~0.15: parked three times over. Each shake unloads the threads
  for a split second, grip toward zero — and the slope is still
  there. That is the vibration claim, computed.
LAW: narrow gaps pull liquids in — the tighter, the harder: for
  water, climb ≈ 15 mm ÷ gap-in-mm. WORKED: a 1 mm gap lifts
  ~15 mm; a 0.1 mm hairline ~15 cm; a micron crack ~15 m. Why
  penetrating oil creeps uphill into a rusted joint (a crowbar one
  molecule wide), why solder wicks, why a crack drinks rain.

### DIAGNOSIS — halving the search

LAW: a test splitting the suspects in half cuts the search in two;
  n such tests tell apart 2^n causes.
RULE: ask the question that splits nearest to half; a test that
  only confirms what you believe removes nothing. The swap test is
  a ready halver: exchange suspect part with known-good — the fault
  follows the part or stays with the socket.
WORKED: 64 lights, dead. One-by-one: worst 63 checks, average ~32.
  Halving: 64 → 32 → 16 → 8 → 4 → 2 → 1 — SIX checks. And 2^10 =
  1,024: ten good questions sort a thousand causes — why the calm
  fixer beats the fast one.

### THE MONEY OF THINGS

LAW: true price = (price + upkeep) ÷ uses; frequency decides, not
  the ticket. WORKED: $120 boots, ~300 wears/yr × 3 yr = 900:
  120 ÷ 900 ≈ 13¢ a wear. $40 boots dead at ~100 wears: 40¢ a wear
  — and over 900 wears, 9 pairs = $360: triple money plus eight
  failures at bad moments. Quality for the daily-touched; cheap or
  hired for the once-a-year (ownership charges storage and care
  daily, used or not).
LAW: repair-or-replace compares price PER YEAR BOUGHT. WORKED:
  $200 repair buying ~2 yr = $100/yr vs $600 new lasting ~10 =
  $60/yr — replace; but $80 buying 4 yr on a sound machine =
  $20/yr — repair. A cheap repair near the end just postpones the
  same decision.
LAW: replace-on-schedule vs run-to-failure is decided by the
  consequence alone. WORKED: cambelt ~$400 planned, or ~$3,000
  engine when it snaps — schedule it; the wiper blade fails cheap
  and visible — run it to death.
LAW: a fixed setting-up cost divided across a batch shrinks per
  item — under batching, errand routes, and the two-minute rule
  (below some size, handling costs more than doing). WORKED: six
  errands, 30-min round trip + 20 min each: separately 6 × 50 =
  300 min; one route 30 + 120 + 25 between = 175 min — 125 min
  cheaper, the fixed cost paid once, not six times.
LAW: two INDEPENDENT copies multiply safety: ~10%/yr loss each,
  both lost = 0.1 × 0.1 = 1%/yr. CONDITION: independence AND
  checking — a silently-stopped copy is 100% failed and multiplies
  nothing; the restore test converts belief into the 1%.
LAW: the emergency fund is months, not a round number: outgoings ×
  months. WORKED: $2,500 × 6 = $15,000 — sized to the interruption
  (illness, lost work), not any single shock. Kin: insure ruin,
  carry annoyance — cover must also pay administration and profit.

## METHODS
How upkeep is done — what governs each, and what breaks it.

- SCHEDULED LOOKING: walk the whole holding on a fixed round,
  hunting early warnings. Breaks when the round only visits what is
  already trusted.
- CLEANING BY MATCHING: soap for film, acid for crust, base for
  grease, hot for speed; never bleach-plus-acid. Wipe the spill now
  — a stain is a deferral bill in miniature.
- LUBRICATION: oil so parts never touch; grease where oil runs off;
  penetrating oil PLUS TIME for the seized (the narrow-gap pull
  works overnight, not on demand); tap to crack the rust bond; heat
  to swell the nut. Forced cold, it snaps.
- READING THE HOUSE — symptoms are physics speaking: window fog is
  the room's breath landing on the coldest surface; the
  summer-stuck door is wood drinking the damp months; banging pipes
  are moving water — a ram — stopped at once (close valves gently);
  fridge cycling is heat leaking back in, pumped uphill in bursts;
  the wet finger finds the draft, because evaporation is a cooler
  and moving air its accelerant.
- THE RESTART: most machine sickness lives in short-term state, not
  the bones — throw the tangle away and re-walk the one path
  rehearsed a million times, the path from the beginning. First
  move for anything with a memory; breaks when the sickness is in
  the bones — then diagnosis's turn.
- DIAGNOSIS BY HALVING: split, swap, change ONE thing at a time —
  two changes un-halve the world. The jiggled key is the humble
  edition: a blind search of nearby positions until the worn pins
  line up — sampling, not strength.
- PATCHING: duct tape is a liquid handshake with a cloth spine —
  mighty dragged flat, feeble peeled: patch so the load drags flat.
  Glue: clean, close, clamped — touch and teeth. Paint: thin coats
  cure through (a thick coat skins over, sealing its wet middle
  in); and paint is a mask, not a cure — keep the skin unbroken or
  renew it.
- SPARE SHELF AND TESTED BACKUP: a spare is only a spare before the
  failure — buy it on an ordinary day; restore one real file from
  the backup on a schedule, because copying fails silently and an
  untested copy is a belief. Sort parts by consequence: calendar
  the cambelts, ignore the bulbs.
- CAPTURE AND REVIEW: write it down — the value is the quiet, not
  the record (unwritten tasks keep announcing themselves, and the
  announcing is the cost); then a fixed weekly moment: each item
  done, dropped, delegated, or dated — an unreread list is where
  things go to be lost.
- CHECKLIST OR GROOVE: checklist for the rare-and-fatal (thinking
  done once, calmly, by someone with time); routine for the daily
  (a decision made once, collected repeatedly for nothing).
- ARRANGEMENT OVER RESOLVE: change conditions, not the person —
  put-away easier than put-down; one landing place for arrivals,
  emptied by habit; one in, one out; automatic payment for the
  must-never-miss bill; money in separate pots so commitments stop
  looking spendable; the shopping list written at home by a calmer
  person. Effort decides behavior more reliably than intention.
- GUARDING THE HOURS: batch like with like; protect the few good
  thinking hours (an open calendar is filled by whoever asks
  first); leave slack; count hidden days (a trip costs a day each
  end, a commute ten unpaid hours a week); book early where
  rationing is by queue; decide meals once, not thrice daily at
  the worst hour.
- DELEGATION AND HANDOVER: handing a task means handing standard,
  deadline, and reasons — mostly never in words, so first times
  cost more than doing it yourself; the case rests on repetition.
  Write the household's knowledge OUT of the one head.
- THE ANNUAL REVIEW: once a year ask every arrangement whether it
  still answers a question anyone still has — renewals drift, the
  loyal pay more than newcomers, and a default decides for all who
  don't act; the pricing was designed around that.

## MEANS
What the methods run on.

- THE TOOLBOX: few and good beats a drawer of junk — priced by
  cost-per-use, sized to what the hand touches often.
- THE OIL FAMILY: light oil, grease, penetrating oil (WD stands for
  water displacement — its first job was shoving water off metal).
- THE CLEANING SHELF: soap; vinegar the acid; soda and oven cleaner
  the bases; bleach diluted 1-in-100, never mixed.
- THE PAPER BRAIN: one shared calendar (two private calendars
  cannot coordinate); one known place per kind of document
  (findability is a rule, not tidiness); checklist; written list.
- THE SPARE SHELF: bulbs, fuses, batteries, key copy, charger —
  trivial any ordinary day, unobtainable Sunday night.
- THE SENTRIES: the smoke alarm tested (untested it is the bilge
  pump nobody switched on), the leak pan, the restored backup.
- THE WET FINGER: evaporation read as an instrument — the oldest
  draft gauge.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject answers — the doors a query enters by.

- KEEP WHAT IS BUILT: hold disorder off at wage rates — the
  standing argument the phylum is named for.
- KEEP IT CHEAP: the small bill on schedule, never the compounded
  one (the 80× arithmetic).
- KEEP IT SAFE: the tested alarm, the unmixed bleach, the wired
  fastener — consequence-first care.
- KEEP IT FINDABLE AND THE HEAD QUIET: one place per thing, the
  written list, knowledge out of the single head.
- KEEP THE HOURS: the one stock that never returns — batching,
  slack, routine, the protected morning.
- KEEP IT RUNNING IN A BAD WEEK: arrangements that hold under
  illness, travel, and disorder — the true test of any system.

## HISTORY
How the knowledge arose, and at what cost.

- Upkeep predates writing: thatch renewed, edges resharpened, grain
  kept dry — the wage paid before the law had a name.
- Handwashing beat plagues before anyone knew why: soap's germ-kill
  ran generations ahead of its explanation — method outran science
  and saved lives anyway.
- The checklist was born from a crash: a bomber too complex for
  memory killed its pilots on a skipped step; the answer was a list
  read aloud every time — medicine later borrowed it. Lock-wire
  wrote the vibration lesson in hardware, and flight formalized the
  schedule: lives measured in hours between inspections. The
  household inherits all three, mostly unwritten.
- WD-40 carries its history in its name: water displacement,
  formula forty — thirty-nine failures behind every can (the
  mirror holds that side).
- The machines took the scrubbing and left the noticing: washer and
  dishwasher shrank the visible chores; the invisible work stayed,
  and stayed uncounted.

## RELATIONS
First-class connections — what runs along each.

- PHYSICS: disorder's arithmetic, friction and wear, the thread's
  ramp, water hammer, heat pumped uphill, the narrow-gap pull,
  time's arrow. Upkeep is physics's bill, paid at home.
- CHEMISTRY: soap's treaty, acid-on-mineral, base-on-grease, rust
  as slow fire, cold-dawdling batteries, the bleach prohibition.
- COOKING: the kitchen dirties at the speed of its use; the knife
  is the tool law's home edition; setting out before the pan is
  batching; the cut apple browns by rust's own slow fire.
- BODY: living IS continuous self-repair; cartilage wears; a limp
  reshapes the knee, the knee the hip — deferral in flesh;
  alertness rhythms make hours non-interchangeable.
- MEDICINE: the checkup is scheduled looking — tumors and cracks
  obey one law; prevention beats cure; infection through broken
  skin is scratch-is-a-door; the pill organiser is arrangement
  over resolve.
- FARMING: the untended field walks back to wilderness; machinery
  serviced before the season; the fence that beats watching.
- WEATHER: damp swells the door and fogs the glass; seasons set the
  calendar; wind reads on a wet finger.
- ECONOMY: depreciation is wear in the ledger; compound interest
  runs both ways; sunk cost; cost-per-use; queues as rationing;
  every yes is spending from a budget already allocated.
- ENGINEERING AND BUILDING: design judged under load; surge tanks;
  shear versus peel; fail-safe over vigilance; cracks start at
  corners; maintenance losing to today's job; deck boards gapped so
  wood can breathe.
- COMPUTING: the restart — a system knows its start better than any
  middle; the backup's silent failure; the disk that fills.
- FLIGHT: checklist, lock-wire, hours between inspections — upkeep
  at its most disciplined, because the consequence is total.
- MIND: attention goes to the noisy present; unwritten tasks keep
  announcing; deciding is a limited stock; choices are shaped by
  the room they are made in.
- PEOPLE: the mental load and who carries it; the shared calendar;
  the one-head single point of failure; a household teaches itself
  what it tolerates.
- GOVERNANCE AND LAW: standards slip one notch at a time in streets
  as in sculleries; rules unenforced decay like fences
  (law-strength is the mirror's to question); righty-tighty and
  drive-on-the-right are one species — rules true by unanimity.
- DESIGN AND CRAFT: defaults decide for everyone; storage placed
  where the hand reaches; fit before force; clean-close-clamped;
  the master's few good tools.
- TEACHING: delegation is teaching — the hour of explanation that
  saves a year; standards never fully in words.
- MEASUREMENT: the interval, the round, the restore test — trust in
  any store depends on it being checked.
- TRANSPORT: timetable slack as recovery time; the commute's unpaid
  hours; the kit carried where no shops are.
- MATHEMATICS: halving the search, multiplying independent
  safeties, cost divided by uses, growth by factors — the
  arithmetic spine.
