# MEDICINE — THE WHITE
Built 2026-08-30 to the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md). The
anti-mirror of docs/phylums/medicine/color.md: same parts, opposite
sign. What didn't hold carries its WHY in quantities and
conditions, so it recomputes when new knowns move a condition.
Nothing here is a tombstone; every entry is a possibility waiting
for its drain. Seed authored by Claude and marked as seed.

## THINGS
Units wondered about or attempted that are not yet real, or cannot
be under stated conditions.

- THE FOUR HUMOURS — blood, phlegm, yellow bile, black bile: the
  attempted units of all disease for ~2,000 years. Never real: no
  measurement ever found them, and every therapy built on
  rebalancing them (bleeding, purging) failed the count when the
  count finally arrived. Filed as the standing example of a unit
  invented to make treatment logical instead of found by looking —
  the shape of the error matters more than the error. Would drain
  only on some measurable quantity standing behind the four names;
  none has ever been found. The nearest modern echo is a rebuke,
  not a rescue: the body DOES have balances that make disease when
  they tip — sodium, sugar, thyroid hormone, iron — and every one
  of them was found by measuring a real thing, never by naming a
  fluid and reasoning from the name.
- MIASMA — "bad air" as the carrier of epidemic disease. Killed by
  the pump-handle map: the 1854 deaths followed the WATER lines,
  not the smells, and the beer-drinking brewery workers in the
  foulest air were spared. WHY it held so long: smell correlates
  with filth and filth correlates with disease — a true
  correlation hiding the wrong mechanism. The shimmer: the fixes
  miasma inspired (sewers, ventilation) worked anyway, and some
  diseases DO ride air — the wrongness was claiming ALL of them
  did. Right actions from a wrong reason are still on the books;
  the reason is what got buried.
- THE PANACEA — the cure-all, wanted in every century, sold in
  most. CANNOT BE under the condition that diseases have different
  mechanisms: a microbe to kill, a rebellion of one's own cells, a
  worn joint, a missing molecule — no single key turns four
  different locks. Would drain only if the condition moved: a
  lever below all disease (none is written). Every tonic era is
  this entry resold.
- A DRUG WITH NO SIDE EFFECT — CANNOT BE under the condition that
  drugs travel by blood: the whole body receives what one organ
  needed, and any molecule active enough to change its target is
  active enough to touch look-alike targets elsewhere. Drains
  PARTIALLY with aim (targeted delivery, local injection — the
  botulinum entry in the color is placement beating circulation);
  a fully clean systemic drug remains white.

## CLAIMS
What was claimed and didn't hold — with why — and what is still
unsettled.

- BLOODLETTING REBALANCES THE SICK — held some 2,000 years, from
  the Hippocratic writers through Galen to the 1800s, which is
  roughly eighty generations of doctors in a row being wrong in
  the same direction. Why it was believed, honestly: most patients
  recover anyway (the body's work, credited to the lancet);
  bleeding visibly DOES something (calm, pallor — read as the
  disease leaving); and the humoural theory made it logical. What
  killed it: counting. When outcomes were finally tallied
  (Louis, 1830s, pneumonia), the bled-early died MORE often than
  the bled-late; the trial arithmetic in the color's SCIENCE is
  the instrument this failure forged. George Washington was
  drained of roughly 2.5 liters — near half his blood — in his
  final hours, by the best doctors of his day, by the book. The
  shimmer, exact, with the quantities that make it exact:
  bloodletting is TODAY'S correct treatment for two named
  conditions. For iron overload, one drawn unit (~450 mL)
  carries ~200-250 mg of iron out of a body that has no other
  way to dump it, so a weekly draw removes ~1 g a month and the
  overload is measured down. For blood grown too thick, draws
  hold the packed-cell fraction under ~45%, below which clots
  stop forming. Note what changed: not the act, but the arrival
  of a measurement telling you when to stop. The method drained
  for those two, and stays white for the ten thousand other
  conditions it was used on.
- SICKNESS RIDES ON SMELLS (miasma's claim form) — see THINGS;
  filed twice deliberately: once as a false unit, once as the
  claim that a true correlation (filth↔disease) does not certify
  the mechanism riding it. That lesson is load-bearing across
  every phylum that measures.
- ULCERS COME FROM STRESS AND LIFESTYLE — held for decades, and
  the standard of care (antacids forever, bland diets, no cure)
  was built on it. Didn't hold: a bacterium (H. pylori) sits in
  most ulcers; one researcher drank a culture in 1984, grew the
  gastritis, and antibiotics now CURE what was managed for life.
  WHY the truth was resisted: "no microbe survives stomach acid"
  was treated as a wall instead of a condition to test. Filed as
  the modern Semmelweis — the claim that couldn't be true until
  someone ran it.
- "VACCINES CAUSE AUTISM" — a claim that never held: born in a
  fabricated 12-child series (1998, retracted 2010), and tested
  against millions of counted children since — a single Danish
  cohort of ~657,000 children found the same autism rate in the
  vaccinated and the unvaccinated. Put the two counts on one
  line: 12 children, no control arm, versus 657,000 children
  with one. By the √N law in the color, a count that size can
  see a difference of a fraction of a point; there was none to
  see. Kept on file because queries still arrive carrying it,
  and because it is the cleanest live example of the trial
  arithmetic running in reverse.
- "BLOOD IS BLOOD" — transfusion tried for centuries and it
  killed unpredictably, so it was banned, revived, banned. The
  hidden condition: the ABO groups (A, B, O found 1901, AB the
  year after); across the wrong pair the recipient's antibodies
  shred the gift — and with four groups mixed blind, better
  than a third of pairings are wrong, which is exactly the
  ragged pattern that looked like bad luck. Drained
  fully by typing — the color holds transfusion as routine — and
  filed here as the pattern: a method that fails RANDOMLY is
  usually a method with an unmeasured condition.
- UNSETTLED: THE PLACEBO EFFECT'S REACH — the ritual of treatment
  measurably moves reported pain, nausea, and mood; it does not
  shrink tumors or kill microbes. Nothing certain on mechanism —
  but here is a possibility: expectation and conditioning drive
  real brain chemistry for the symptoms the brain itself
  generates, and cannot reach past them. Would drain on: trials
  that map exactly WHICH symptoms answer to ritual, with the
  brain chemistry read alongside.
- UNSETTLED: WHY SLEEP IS REQUIRED AT ALL — total deprivation is
  fatal in animals; every human spends about a third of life —
  some 25 years of a 75-year life — lying still, deaf and
  defenseless, which is an enormous price for evolution to keep
  paying. The function is still argued: repair, waste clearance,
  memory filing, each gripping partly. Nothing certain — but
  here is a possibility: these are one job, not three. The brain
  may be unable to run its own maintenance while it is being
  used, the way a road cannot be resurfaced under traffic, so
  the traffic is stopped nightly and everything deferred is done
  at once. Would drain on: an animal made to run the clearance
  and the memory filing while awake, and shown to live without
  sleeping. Filed because medicine prescribes sleep daily on
  evidence of WHAT without a settled WHY — a method-outruns-why
  running inside every ward.

## SCIENCE
The science this subject still lacks, named; and where applied
science broke.

- LACKING: WHY ANESTHESIA WORKS AT DEPTH — used ten thousand
  times a day; mechanism unsettled after ~180 years. The one
  strong regularity (potency tracks fat-solubility across a
  ~100,000-fold range) suggested "it dissolves into the cell's
  fat" — then broke: some highly fat-soluble compounds
  anesthetize nothing, and xenon — a noble gas with almost no
  chemistry at all — anesthetizes fine. Current grip: specific
  proteins on nerve cells, several at once, no single switch
  found. The subject's largest working mystery: consciousness is
  switched off and back on daily, on schedule, safely, by
  monitoring rather than by understanding. Would drain on: a
  mechanism that PREDICTS a new anesthetic's potency before it
  is tried.
- LACKING: A SCIENCE OF THE INDIVIDUAL DOSE — the color's
  mg-per-kg is the average patient; real patients clear the same
  drug at speeds varying severalfold by their enzyme variants,
  age, and organ state. The half-life in the color is a
  population number wearing a lab coat. Would drain on: cheap
  reading of each patient's own clearing speed before the first
  dose (gene panels grip a corner of it already).
- LACKING: THE MECHANISM OF MOST MENTAL ILLNESS — depression's
  once-taught story ("low serotonin, topped up") did not survive
  as a full account: the drugs move the chemical in hours, the
  relief arrives in weeks, and direct tests of the low-serotonin
  claim came back empty. The drugs help many (the counted trials
  hold); WHY they help is not settled. Filed exactly as that:
  working method, absent mechanism, and a warning against
  teaching the mechanism story as if it were the evidence. The
  weeks-not-hours gap is the clue the story could not cover, and
  it points somewhere else: at slow rebuilding — new connections
  between nerve cells — rather than at a chemical level topped
  up. Would drain on: a measurement taken before treatment that
  predicts WHICH patient this drug will help, which no theory of
  mental illness can yet do for any of them.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: THE CORRELATION FED AS A PILL — people
  who eat plenty of vegetables get less cancer; the isolated
  molecule (beta-carotene) was trialed as a supplement in
  smokers and the treated arm got MORE lung cancer. Two big
  counts, both pointing the wrong way: ~29,000 male smokers,
  ~18% more lung cancer in the supplemented arm; ~18,000
  smokers and asbestos workers, ~28% more, and that trial was
  halted about two years early because the harm was already
  clear. Note the direction of the error — the expected answer
  was fewer, and the counted answer was more, which is a
  correlation not merely failing to transfer but reversing.
  The break, stated as law: a nutrient's company is not the
  nutrient; a population correlation does not survive
  extraction into a capsule, and the sign of the effect is not
  guaranteed to survive either. Cooking's white holds the same
  lesson from its side of the table — the two entries brace
  each other.
- BROKE WHEN APPLIED: RADIATION AS TONIC — radium water and
  radium salves were sold as vigor for two decades before the
  dose arithmetic was run; one famous devotee drank ~1,400
  bottles over about three years and died with his jaw
  crumbling. The condition nobody had written: radium is
  chemically a cousin of calcium, so the body files it in BONE
  and it never leaves — a half-life of ~1,600 years against a
  human lifetime, which means every swallow adds to a total
  that only ever goes up. That is why the color's half-life law
  gave no protection here: five half-lives is the rule for a
  drug the body clears, and this is the case where the body
  clears nothing. Radiation stayed in medicine — as a SCALPEL,
  dosed and aimed, killing what it is pointed at — which is the
  drain: the same physics filed under the dose law instead of
  under enthusiasm.

## METHODS
What was tried and didn't work, carrying its conditions — the
wings-on-arms entries, waiting for their planet.

- BLEED-PURGE-BLISTER (heroic medicine) — the humoural toolkit,
  applied for centuries at full confidence. Failed because its
  unit was never real (THINGS) and its receipts were the body's
  own recoveries. Its true product was the counting that ended
  it: the trial method in the color is this method's tombstone
  turned instrument. Residual drain: the bleed survives for two
  named diseases (CLAIMS); the purge survives as poisoning
  triage; the confidence survives nowhere.
- ANTIBIOTICS AGAINST VIRUSES — tried daily, still, worldwide.
  CANNOT WORK under the condition that defines both parties: the
  drug's targets (cell wall, bacterial ribosome) do not exist in
  a virus; a virus is instructions riding OUR machinery, and
  what would poison it poisons us. The cost is not neutral:
  every futile course runs the resistance selection (color,
  SCIENCE) and charges it to everyone. Would drain only per
  target found — a viral part unlike any human part — which is
  what the few real antivirals each are: narrow keys, one lock,
  no broad spectrum written.
- BOILING THE INSTRUMENTS — surgery's honest try before the
  autoclave: 100°C kills the living microbes and leaves the
  spores. Run the color's numbers and the failure is exact —
  each tenfold cut in the hardiest spores takes ~190 minutes at
  a boil, so a certified instrument would need ~38 hours in the
  kettle, which nobody ever gave it. Wounds kept infecting
  through clean-LOOKING steel, and the surgeon had no way to
  see the difference between clean and sterile. Drained by
  pressure's 121°C, where the same twelve cuts take 18 minutes
  — cooking's canner lesson and this one are a single law paid
  for twice, in botulism and in gangrene.
- LOBOTOMY — cutting the brain's forward connections to quiet
  the incurable; tens of thousands performed, a Nobel awarded
  (1949). It "worked" by the wrong measure: distress fell
  because personhood fell — the outcome counted was ward
  quietness, not the patient's life. Superseded within a decade
  by the first psychiatric drugs. Filed with its condition
  bared: an outcome measure chosen by the institution will be
  optimized INTO, and the method is the permanent warning label
  on every outcome measure since. The shimmer, and it is
  uncomfortable: cutting into the brain to treat the mind did
  not stay closed. It came back narrow — a few millimeters of
  one circuit, or a wire left in place and switchable — for
  severe illness that answers to nothing else, in small counted
  numbers, with the patient's own reported life as the outcome
  instead of the ward's quiet. What was condemned was never
  "touching the brain"; it was touching it blind, at scale, and
  scoring the wrong thing.
- MERCURY AS MEDICINE — calomel purges and syphilis salves,
  centuries of them: a real poison producing dramatic effects
  (salivation, purging) that were READ as potency by humoural
  bookkeeping. The condition that hid the failure: no control
  arm existed anywhere. Stands as the cleanest case of "visible
  action mistaken for benefit" — the same misread that kept
  bloodletting alive. The partial drain, stated exactly:
  mercury as a general medicine is gone, but mercury compounds
  DID work as water-pills for failing hearts and were standard
  into the 1950s, retired only when safer ones arrived — not
  when the theory died. A poison with a real narrow action
  keeps that action after its bad reasoning is buried, which is
  why "it was mercury, therefore it did nothing" is the wrong
  lesson to take from this entry.
- THALIDOMIDE, TESTED IN THE WRONG BODY — a sedative safe in the
  adult by every test then run; in the first-trimester womb it
  cost ~10,000 babies their limbs before withdrawal. The failure
  was a CONDITIONS failure: safety was certified in a body the
  drug was never confined to, and pregnancy was outside the
  test. The drain is double and exact: (1) it rebuilt the world's
  licensing gauntlet — trial phases, pregnancy testing — and
  (2) the molecule itself returned, decades later, as a working
  treatment for leprosy's flare and a blood cancer, under
  absolute no-pregnancy control. Wings-on-arms in full: the
  method failed at one gravity and flies at another, and the
  condition is written on the label in black borders.
- THE MIRACLE TONIC (patent medicine at scale) — opium, cocaine,
  and alcohol sold as cures for everything by testimonial;
  heroin was launched as a cough remedy. The mechanism of the
  deception is in the color as a law: things that quiet
  SYMPTOMS collect testimony that they cured, because the body
  meanwhile did the curing. Died of the first food-and-drug laws
  plus the trial method; reborn every decade in new packaging —
  the entry stays open because the want it exploits (PURPOSE,
  below) stays open.

## MEANS
Means that fell short, and means not yet found.

- NO INSTRUMENT READS PAIN — the alarm wire has no thermometer;
  medicine borrows a 0-10 self-report, which fails exactly where
  it matters most (the unconscious, the very young, the
  disbelieved). Would drain on: an objective correlate that
  tracks the self-report where the self-report works, then
  stands in where it doesn't.
- THE BROAD ANTIVIRAL — the "penicillin for viruses" — not
  found, and the METHODS entry explains why the search is hard,
  not that it is closed: each real antiviral is one lock's key.
  Would drain on: machinery common to whole viral families and
  absent in us (a few candidate doors are being tried; nothing
  general is written).
- THE ANTIBIOTIC PIPELINE ITSELF — a mean that is eroding while
  in use: resistance compounds live (color, SCIENCE) while
  genuinely new drug classes arrive at a trickle. The gap has a
  name and a length — roughly thirty years passed between one
  new class reaching patients and the next, while resistance
  never paused for a day. The arithmetic of the race is
  unfavorable and counted: ~1.3 million deaths a year are laid
  directly at resistant bacteria, with ~5 million deaths a year
  having a resistant infection somewhere in them (2019 count,
  rounded, and the trend is upward). Would drain on: new
  classes, or non-drug kills (the phage road — viruses that eat
  bacteria — stands half-explored since the 1920s), or simply
  using the
  existing means at the discipline the resistance arithmetic
  demands.
- THE COLD CHAIN, WHERE THERE IS NO COLD — vaccines that die at
  room temperature fail exactly where the diseases are worst.
  Fell short as a global mean; drains vaccine by vaccine as
  heat-stable forms are built (some now travel dried).
- DIAGNOSIS WITHOUT A LABORATORY — the culture plate takes days
  and a building; most of the world's fevers are treated by
  guess. Wanted: the mouth-of-the-clinic test at pennies. Drains
  test by test (rapid malaria and strep exist); the general mean
  is still white.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject cannot yet answer.

- CURE THE COMMON COLD — the oldest open want in the file.
  Blocked by arithmetic, not mystery: ~200 distinct viruses
  across several families cause "a cold," and the poster method
  needs a face — two hundred posters, several of them reprinting
  yearly. Nothing certain — but here is a possibility: a shared
  doorknob (many rhinoviruses enter by one receptor) or a
  primed general defense could cover families at a stroke.
  Would drain on: one target common across the families, proven
  in the counted way.
- CURE MOST CANCERS — the honestly-shaped entry: cancer is not
  one disease but hundreds of ways one's own cells rewrite
  their rules, and every weapon that kills them borders on
  killing the patient (the dose margin from the color, at its
  cruelest). The drained part is real and must be said:
  the commonest childhood leukemia went from ~1-in-10 survival
  to ~9-in-10 within living memory; testicular cancer and
  Hodgkin's are mostly won; overall five-year survival across
  all cancers is now roughly 2 in 3 — a rounded figure that
  swings hard with the country counting it and with which
  cancers that country finds early. The white part is named per
  condition, not in general: pancreatic cancer still runs near
  ~1 in 8 alive at five years, the worst brain tumor nearer
  ~1 in 15, and for most cancers the spread stage remains the
  wall — a cancer caught in one place is a different problem
  from the same cancer seeded through a body, and the second is
  where nearly all the deaths are. Would drain on: telling self
  from almost-self — the immune-system road (teach the army to
  read the rebellion's uniform) is the current live possibility,
  already cashing in a minority of patients.
- STOP OR REVERSE AGING ITSELF — medicine treats aging's
  products (the clogged vessel, the worn joint, the failing
  filter) and cannot touch the process; whether aging is
  damage's accumulation or a program that could be paused is
  unsettled science (its entry belongs jointly to life's
  phylum). Would drain on: any intervention moving the whole
  curve in people, counted, not in worms and mice, where curves
  have already moved.
- REGROW WHAT IS LOST — the salamander regrows its limb; the
  map of how exists in life's phylum and our own cells built us
  once. Medicine can knit bone and skin but not spinal cord,
  not the lost limb, not the dead heart muscle at scale. Would
  drain on: reading the builder's plan back into adult tissue
  (early corners — grown skin, tracheas — exist).
- MAKE THE TRUE TREATMENT REACH EVERY BODY THAT NEEDS IT — the
  want medicine can serve but not answer alone: the solved
  diseases still kill by distribution failure (economy,
  engineering, culture hold the other ends). Filed because a
  cure that does not arrive is, to the patient, exactly a cure
  not yet found.

## HISTORY
The record of the trying itself — the portrait's side of the story.

- The twenty humoural centuries: the longest confident error in
  the file. Its cost is uncountable and its lesson is counted:
  the era ended not when someone argued better but when someone
  TALLIED — the control arm is the tombstone of two thousand
  years of eloquence.
- Semmelweis's rejection: the true claim, with receipts, refused
  for ~20 years — because it arrived without a mechanism (germs
  were not yet shown), carried an insult (the healers' own hands
  as the carriers), and its author burned his bridges defending
  it. He died in an asylum before Pasteur's work made his
  numbers respectable. Filed as the standing lesson that truth's
  reception depends on more than truth — mechanism, manners,
  and whose pride it gores — and that the numbers were right
  the whole time anyway.
- The counting resistance: "my patients are not statistics" was
  the honest objection of good doctors for a century; the
  answer, learned slowly, is that WITHOUT the count every doctor
  is fooled by the body's own recoveries — the count is how the
  individual patient stops inheriting the profession's
  illusions.
- Thalidomide's ledger: ~10,000 named babies bought the modern
  trial gauntlet; one regulator's stubbornness (holding the US
  door on inadequate evidence) is the counter-entry showing the
  gauntlet working before it was law. Every "why does approval
  take years" query lands here.
- Smallpox's grave: the one bottom-drained entry — centuries of
  variolation deaths (the old method gave people real smallpox
  on purpose and killed perhaps 1 in 50 of them, accepted
  because the disease itself killed nearer 1 in 3), then
  Jenner's safer swap, then a global campaign's worth of tries,
  and then zero, forever. Read the two rates together and the
  bargain is visible: ~2% against ~30% is why a method that
  killed people was the RIGHT method for two centuries, and why
  it was abandoned the moment something better existed. Kept in
  the white's history as proof of what a fully paid white looks
  like: the color holds the method; this page holds the price.
- The resistance era, being written now: the antibiotic age's
  own white, accumulating in real time — every needless course
  files another line. This is not a warning about the future;
  the deaths are already counted, ~1.3 million a year laid
  directly at bacteria the drugs no longer beat, which is more
  than malaria kills. Note what kind of white entry this is:
  most of this file records knowledge that was never true. This
  one records knowledge that WAS true and is being spent — a
  color entry draining backwards, the only mechanism in the
  file that runs that direction. It grows unless the means
  entry above drains.

## RELATIONS
Connections that broke, or are suspected and unproven.

- BROKEN, THEN REBUILT: MEDICINE ↔ AUTHORITY — thirteen
  centuries of Galen's texts outranking the opened body; the
  relation broke at Vesalius and Harvey and was rebuilt with the
  direction of trust reversed: the body outranks the book,
  observation outranks the elder. The rebuilt relation carries
  that clause as its cargo, and every era tests it again.
- BROKEN, CARRYING A WARNING: MEDICINE ↔ THE MARKET — the patent
  medicine century proved that testimony plus commerce
  reliably manufactures false cures; the relation now runs
  through the trial gauntlet by law. The warning that travels
  this link: wherever the gauntlet is bypassed (supplements,
  miracle clinics), the patent-medicine pattern regrows within
  a decade, every time.
- SUSPECTED, UNPROVEN: GUT MICROBES ↔ THE MIND — the resident
  bacteria correlate with mood and grip firmly in mouse work;
  transplant experiments in people are young and mixed. Filed
  dotted: a possible new organ of psychiatry, or the decade's
  best correlation-mistaken-for-mechanism — the beta-carotene
  entry above says which error to guard against while it
  settles. Would drain on: counted human trials moving illness,
  not mood scores alone.
- SUSPECTED, HALF-PROVEN: THE BODY'S CLOCK ↔ THE DOSE — the same
  drug at morning versus night differs in effect and harm in
  scattered counted cases (some blood-pressure and chemo
  timing); whether timing belongs on every label is unproven.
  Filed dotted with its drain named: trials that randomize the
  CLOCK, not just the molecule.
