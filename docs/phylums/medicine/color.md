## THINGS
The units this subject is about. Each sits in many phylums at once;
its other homes are named so a cascade can branch.

- THE MICROBE — bacterium, virus, fungus, parasite: the invisible
  causes that germ theory made countable; also lives in: life
  (evolution's fastest runners), cooking (hired labor or enemy),
  farming, weather (the seasonal diseases).
- THE DOSE — a quantity of substance per quantity of body; the unit
  that divides medicine from poison; also: chemistry (concentration),
  mathematics (the mg-per-kg proportion), farming (pesticide).
- THE DRUG — a molecule sent into the body to change one thing and
  asked politely to change nothing else; also: chemistry (it IS a
  molecule), economy (priced), farming (grown, once — bark and leaf).
- THE IMMUNE SYSTEM — the body's standing army with a memory:
  recognizes, attacks, and REMEMBERS every invader's face; also: the
  body, life (it evolves within one lifetime).
- THE VACCINE — the invader's face without its weapon, shown early;
  also: life (attenuation), engineering (the cold chain), culture
  (uptake rides on trust).
- BLOOD — the body's freight system: oxygen out, waste back, ~5
  liters circulating whole every minute; also: the body, physics
  (pressure, flow).
- THE WOUND — the breach in the wall where the doubling law starts
  its clock; also: the body (healing), cooking's mirror image (the
  cut surface that spoils first).
- PAIN — the body's alarm wire, a signal carrying location and
  urgency; also: the body (nerves), culture (what may be said of it).
- FEVER — the body cooking its invaders: a deliberate thermostat
  raise, weapon and signal at once; also: the body, cooking (the
  same protein ladder sets its ceiling).
- THE CELL — the unit all tissue is made of and the unit cancer is
  a rebellion of; also: life, chemistry.
- THE ANTIBODY — a molecule shaped to grip exactly one invader's
  surface, the wanted poster made physical; also: life, chemistry
  (lock and key).
- THE TRIAL — a designed comparison that lets a treatment be told
  apart from luck; also: mathematics (probability made procedure),
  economy (what evidence costs).
- THE EPIDEMIC — disease compounding through a population, one case
  seeding the next; also: mathematics (the exponential), weather,
  economy, culture.
- THE HOSPITAL AND THE CLINIC — where the methods concentrate, and
  where the microbes do too (its own hygiene arithmetic); also:
  engineering, economy, culture.
- CLEAN WATER AND THE SEWER — the largest medicines ever deployed,
  neither of them a drug; also: engineering (they built it), weather,
  economy.

## CLAIMS
What holds, written to be tested by a crossing query. Worth is
never absolute — it is applicability to the query at hand.

- Specific diseases have specific invisible living causes. Not bad
  air, not imbalance — a particular microbe for a particular
  disease, visible under glass, countable, killable. Condition: this
  covers the infectious diseases; the body's own failures (cancer,
  worn joints, clogged vessels) answer to other laws.
- The dose makes the poison — EVERYTHING is toxic at some dose and
  most poisons are safe below some dose, water and oxygen included.
  A substance is never safe or dangerous by name; only by mg per kg
  (see SCIENCE for the arithmetic).
- Microbes compound. One becomes two becomes a crowd on the same
  doubling law as cooking's spoilage — which is why hours matter in
  an infected wound and why "wait and see" has a price written in
  powers of two.
- Killing microbes trades time for temperature — the same law the
  kitchen holds, and medicine and cooking learned it from the same
  teacher (Pasteur). Milder heat held longer equals fiercer heat
  held briefly; spores are the hard case that sets the top of the
  scale (see SCIENCE).
- Antibiotics are targeted layoffs: they break machinery bacteria
  have and human cells lack — the cell wall, the bacterial ribosome.
  That is why they barely touch us, and why they do NOTHING to a
  virus, which owns no machinery at all and borrows ours.
- Resistance is evolution run live in the patient: any big enough
  crowd of bacteria already contains a few mutants the drug cannot
  kill; the drug fires the rest and the mutants inherit the
  territory. Every use, needed or not, runs this selection —
  resistance is a cost charged to everyone (arithmetic in SCIENCE).
- A vaccine is a wanted poster: show the immune system the face
  before the criminal arrives and the response starts from memory —
  days of head start against an enemy that doubles by the hour. The
  race arithmetic is in SCIENCE; the head start is the whole game.
- Hands carry disease between bodies. The claim cost its discoverer
  his career and is now the cheapest life-saver in the building:
  soap, water, seconds (receipts in SCIENCE and HISTORY).
- Pain is a signal, not the enemy: it reports damage with location
  and urgency, guards the injury while it heals, and the rare
  children born without it die young of unnoticed wounds. Treat the
  pain AND read the message; silencing the alarm does not put out
  the fire.
- Fever is a weapon within limits: the body raises its own
  thermostat to speed its army and slow some invaders. The ceiling
  is the protein ladder cooking knows — the body's own proteins
  begin to fail past ~41-42°C, so the weapon has a redline.
- The body does most of the healing; medicine clears the road.
  Wounds close, bones knit, most infections end, most colds pass —
  which is exactly why worthless remedies collect testimonials (the
  trial exists to subtract this self-healing; see SCIENCE).
- A treatment is proven only against a comparison group, blinded:
  the control arm subtracts self-healing and the blind subtracts
  hope — the patient's and the doctor's. Testimony is not evidence,
  however sincere, because recovery-anyway is the default.
- Prevention beats cure by orders of magnitude where it exists:
  clean water, sewers, vaccines, and washed hands have saved more
  lives than every drug and every surgery combined. Order of
  magnitude, so the claim can be checked rather than admired:
  smallpox alone was killing on the order of 300 million people
  a century when the campaign closed it; childhood vaccination
  since the 1970s is credited with well over 100 million deaths
  averted; no drug and no operation has a number in that range,
  because a drug treats one patient at a time and a sewer treats
  a city forever.
- Diagnosis before treatment: name the cause, then choose the
  weapon. The same cough can be virus (wait), bacterium (specific
  layoffs), or something growing (an entirely different war) — and
  the treatments for one worsen another.
- A screening test's meaning depends on how rare the disease is —
  a highly accurate test for a rare disease is wrong most of the
  times it says yes (the arithmetic is in SCIENCE and it surprises
  every generation of doctors).

## SCIENCE
The math, physics, and chemistry of medicine, held — not mentioned.
Each piece is a LAW a query can compute with, a RULE it can follow,
and where it earns it, a WORKED case proving the rule runs.

### THE DOSE ARITHMETIC — poison and medicine on one line

LAW: dose scales by body mass. dose = (mg per kg) × (kg of body).
  A drug is characterized by two numbers on that line: the dose
  that works and the dose that harms; the ratio between them is
  its margin of safety.
RULE: to size a dose — take the body's mass in kg, multiply by the
  drug's mg-per-kg; to judge a drug's danger — divide its harm
  dose by its working dose and read the margin.
WORKED: paracetamol (acetaminophen), the common fever-and-pain
  drug: works at ~15 mg/kg per dose; a single swallow of
  ~150 mg/kg poisons the liver. A 20 kg child: working dose
  15 × 20 = 300 mg; poison line 150 × 20 = 3,000 mg — just six
  ordinary 500 mg tablets. A 70 kg adult: poison line
  150 × 70 = 10,500 mg — 21 tablets. Margin of safety:
  150 ÷ 15 = 10. Ten. That is why the child's bottle has a
  child-proof cap and the adult's has a daily ceiling printed on
  it — the arithmetic, not caution for its own sake.
WORKED, both directions of the law: botulinum toxin is the
  deadliest molecule known — around a billionth of a gram per kg
  kills — and medicine injects a measured fraction of that into a
  single overactive muscle as treatment. Same molecule; the dose
  and the placement decide which name it wears. And water, the
  safest substance there is, has killed at the other end: several
  liters forced down in an hour or two dilute the blood's salt
  past what nerves tolerate. NOTHING is safe by name.

LAW: the body clears most drugs exponentially — each half-life
  halves what remains. amount = A₀ × (1/2)^(t ÷ half-life).
RULE: to know what is still aboard — count the half-lives that
  fit in the elapsed time, halve that many times. To know when a
  drug is effectively gone — five half-lives leaves ~3%
  (1/32). To keep a drug's level steady — re-dose on the rhythm
  of its half-life, not on convenience.
WORKED: caffeine's half-life ≈ 5 hours. A 200 mg mug at 3 pm, at
  10 pm bedtime (7 h = 1.4 half-lives): 200 × (1/2)^1.4 ≈ 76 mg —
  more than a third of the mug still circulating at lights-out.
  Same law, medical direction: an antibiotic with a 6-hour
  half-life is down to ~3% after 30 hours (five half-lives) —
  effectively gone — which is why it is prescribed every 6-8
  hours and not once a day: skip doses and the level sags below
  what kills the microbes while staying high enough to select
  the resistant ones — the worst of both, a dose that teaches
  without killing. The half-life IS the schedule.

### THE MICROBE ARITHMETIC — the invisible made countable

LAW: microbes compound. N = N₀ × 2^(t ÷ doubling time). Fast
  bacteria in a warm wound or bloodstream double in ~20-40
  minutes; viruses in cells run their own fast clocks.
RULE: to price a delay — count the doublings that fit in it,
  raise 2 to that power.
WORKED: one bacterium, doubling every 30 minutes, left unopposed
  for 24 hours: 48 doublings = 2^48 ≈ 2.8 × 10^14 — more bacteria
  than the body has cells, from ONE, in a day. The immune system
  is why this does not happen every time skin breaks; sepsis is
  what it looks like when the race is being lost, and it is why
  sepsis is treated by the clock — each hour of delay measurably
  raises the odds of death.

LAW: infection needs a big enough landing party — the infectious
  dose, and it varies enormously by microbe and by route.
  Dysentery's shigella: ~10-100 swallowed organisms suffice.
  Cholera: ~100 million swallowed, because stomach acid kills
  almost all of them.
RULE: to guess a disease's road, read its dose — a tiny dose
  travels on hands and doorknobs; a huge dose needs a delivery
  system that ships volume, which means the water supply.
WORKED: this one line explains the pump-handle map (HISTORY):
  cholera rode London's water because only water delivers a
  hundred million organisms in a glass; and it explains why
  handwashing crushes dysentery but sewage treatment was what
  crushed cholera. The dose arithmetic picks the countermeasure.

LAW: killing microbes with heat trades time for temperature, and
  at a fixed temperature killing is exponential — each "D"
  minutes cuts survivors tenfold. Hotter shrinks D roughly
  tenfold every 5-10°C depending on the microbe; bacterial SPORES
  are the hardest case and set the top of the scale.
RULE: to certify a kill — choose the tenfold-drops you owe
  (pasteurization owes ~5-7 against the target microbe; surgical
  sterility owes 12 against the hardiest spores), multiply by D
  at your temperature.
WORKED: an instrument carrying a million spores (10^6), D at the
  autoclave's 121°C ≈ 1.5 minutes for the hardiest: sterility
  demands survivors down past one-in-a-million odds — 12 tenfold
  drops — so 12 × 1.5 = 18 minutes, and there is the standard
  ~15-20 minute autoclave cycle, derived, not decreed.
WORKED, why the kettle could never do it: run the same law
  downward. For spores the D-per-degree step is ~10°C, so
  dropping from 121°C to 100°C multiplies D by 10^(21÷10) ≈ 126:
  1.5 × 126 ≈ 190 minutes, over three hours for ONE tenfold
  drop. The twelve drops sterility owes would need
  12 × 190 = 2,280 minutes ≈ 38 hours at a rolling boil. That
  is the whole reason the kettle never made surgery safe and the
  pressure vessel did — not squeamishness, a factor of 126
  (cooking holds the same law at pH 4.6 and the canner — same
  teacher, same numbers).
WORKED, the gentle end: milk pasteurized at 63°C for 30 minutes
  or ~72°C for ~15 seconds — the dairy's actual pair — kills the
  tuberculosis and typhoid the milk once carried while leaving
  the milk milk. Medicine's interest and cooking's are the same
  numbers wearing two coats.

### THE RESISTANCE ARITHMETIC — evolution, worked live

LAW: mutation supplies resistance BEFORE the drug arrives:
  roughly one cell in 10^7-10^9 already carries, by copying
  error, a change that defeats a given drug. Selection does the
  rest: kill the susceptible and the resistant inherit the
  patient.
RULE: to estimate the mutants present — multiply the crowd's size
  by the mutation rate. To deny them the inheritance — either
  finish the crowd entirely (complete the course while levels are
  lethal) or demand two independent miracles at once (combination
  therapy).
WORKED: an established infection carries ~10^9 bacteria. At one
  resistant mutant per 10^8: expect 10^9 × 10^-8 = ~10 resistant
  cells ALREADY THERE on day one. One drug kills the
  999,999,990 others and hands those ten an empty body. Two
  drugs with independent targets: a cell resisting both needs
  10^-8 × 10^-8 = 10^-16 luck, and 10^9 × 10^-16 = one chance in
  ten million that even ONE such cell exists. That arithmetic is
  the entire design of tuberculosis therapy — never one drug,
  always a cocktail — and it is why half-finished antibiotic
  courses are a factory for resistant strains: they kill enough
  to select and not enough to finish.

### THE EPIDEMIC ARITHMETIC — one case seeds the next

LAW: each case infects R others on average; cases compound by
  R per generation: cases ≈ R^generations. R above 1 grows,
  below 1 dies out. Immunity subtracts: an outbreak stalls when
  the immune fraction exceeds 1 − 1/R (herd immunity).
RULE: to find the wall an outbreak breaks against — compute
  1 − 1/R; vaccinate past it and the chain of transmission
  starves even among the unvaccinated.
WORKED: measles, R ≈ 15 in an open population: wall at
  1 − 1/15 ≈ 93% — which is why measles returns the moment
  vaccination slips a few points, and why "most people are
  vaccinated" is not enough for THIS disease. Seasonal flu,
  R ≈ 1.5: wall at 1 − 1/1.5 ≈ 33% — a far lower bar, met some
  years by vaccination plus survivors. Growth itself: R = 3
  with weekly generations, ten weeks unchecked:
  3^10 = 59,049 cases from one — the reason "only a few cases"
  is a statement about TODAY.

LAW: vaccination moves the start line of a race between two
  exponentials. First meeting with a microbe: the immune response
  takes ~1-2 weeks to arm. Second meeting (or vaccinated):
  memory cells arm in ~1-3 days.
RULE: to price a vaccine's head start — count the microbe's
  doublings inside the unprimed delay, count them again inside
  the primed delay, and divide. The answer is how many times
  smaller the enemy is when the fight starts.
WORKED: a virus doubling every 8 hours grows 2^21 ≈ 2,000,000×
  in the 7 days (21 doublings) an unprimed defense needs. The
  primed defense meets it after 2 days — 6 doublings — at
  2^6 = 64× instead: 2,097,152 ÷ 64 ≈ 33,000 times fewer
  virus particles to clear. Same body, same virus, same
  immune system — the poster on the wall is worth a factor of
  tens of thousands in enemy headcount. That is the whole
  mechanism; everything else is bookkeeping.
  Same rule, the other way: a microbe that doubles SLOWLY —
  tuberculosis takes ~15-20 hours per generation, against a
  fast wound bacterium's 30 minutes — gives even an unprimed
  defense time to arm before it is overrun, which is why TB is
  a standoff measured in months rather than a crisis measured
  in hours. The head start a poster buys is worth most against
  the fastest doublers.

### THE TRIAL ARITHMETIC — telling treatment from luck

LAW: most conditions improve untreated (self-healing, and the
  worst days are followed by average ones simply because they
  were the worst — regression to the mean). Therefore a
  treatment's effect = treated outcome MINUS untreated outcome,
  never treated outcome alone.
RULE: to test a remedy — split comparable patients by lot
  (randomize), treat one arm, give the other the ritual without
  the substance, let neither patients nor scorers know who got
  which (double blind), and subtract.
WORKED: 100 patients on the drug, 60 recover; 100 on placebo,
  50 recover. The drug's own contribution is 10 per 100 — not
  60. Number needed to treat = 1 ÷ (0.60 − 0.50) = 10: treat ten
  to help one. Without the control arm the drug claims all 60
  and a useless remedy claims 50 — this single subtraction is
  why medicine measured its own history and found (mirror:
  bloodletting) that it had been claiming the body's work as the
  treatment's for two thousand years.

LAW: chance's noise shrinks with the square root of the count —
  a proportion measured on N patients wobbles by roughly
  ±√(0.25 ÷ N) one standard step.
RULE: to size a trial — the smaller the true difference you must
  see, the more patients you owe; halving the noise costs 4× the
  patients.
WORKED: on 100 patients the wobble is √(0.25/100) = 5 points —
  a real 10-point drug barely clears one wobble and can vanish
  into luck. On 10,000 patients the wobble is 0.5 points and the
  same drug stands 20 wobbles clear. This is why small trials
  contradict each other, why big ones settle questions, and why
  a modest-but-real effect (most of medicine's honest effects
  are) can ONLY be seen through a large counted trial.

LAW: a test's verdict depends on the disease's rarity (the
  base-rate law): positive predictive value =
  true positives ÷ (true positives + false positives).
WORKED: a test that catches 99% of cases and false-alarms on 1%
  of the healthy, screening a disease carried by 1 in 1,000.
  Test 100,000 people: 100 carriers → 99 caught; 99,900 healthy
  → 999 false alarms. A positive result is 99 ÷ (99 + 999) ≈ 9%
  likely to be real — the test is "99% accurate" and its yes is
  wrong 10 times in 11. RULE derived: screen the rare only in
  high-risk groups (raise the base rate), and confirm every
  screening yes with an independent second test — the second
  test's arithmetic starts from 9%, not 0.1%. Run it:
  0.09 × 0.99 = 0.0891 true, 0.91 × 0.01 = 0.0091 false, so
  0.0891 ÷ (0.0891 + 0.0091) ≈ 91%. One test moved the answer
  from 0.1% to 9%; the second moved it from 9% to 91%. Note
  what that means and say it out loud: even two agreeing tests
  leave about one yes in eleven wrong, which is why a screening
  program owes the patient a THIRD step — a look at the thing
  itself — before anyone is treated.

### THE BODY'S OWN NUMBERS — the flow that had to be a loop

LAW: the heart moves stroke × rate: ~70 mL × ~72 beats/min ≈
  5 liters per minute — the body's whole blood volume, every
  minute, at rest.
RULE: to find any heart's output — multiply what one beat
  ejects by the beats per minute. To find how hard a body is
  working — divide that output by the resting 5 L/min and read
  the multiple (hard exercise reaches 4-6×, mostly by rate).
  To spot a failing heart — the same arithmetic run backwards
  from a measured output that will not rise.
WORKED: per day that is 5 × 60 × 24 = 7,200 liters through a
  body that holds five. Harvey ran this arithmetic (with cruder
  numbers) and the conclusion forced itself: no body makes tons
  of new blood a day, so the SAME blood must go around and
  around — circulation, proven by multiplication before any
  microscope saw a capillary. The oldest worked case in this
  file, and the founding proof that arithmetic can see inside
  the body where eyes cannot.

### THE REHYDRATION RECIPE — chemistry as a life raft

LAW: the gut absorbs water only by riding it on salt and sugar —
  the sodium-glucose pump pulls both in together and water
  follows the salt. Plain water runs through a cholera-struck
  gut; salt-sugar water is PUMPED in by it.
RULE: oral rehydration solution — per liter of clean water:
  the standard packet's measured mix (~13.5 g glucose, ~2.6 g
  table salt, plus potassium and citrate), or the home version:
  ~6 level teaspoons of sugar and ~half a teaspoon of salt.
  Too much salt is worse than none: taste it — no saltier
  than tears.
WORKED: cholera can void liters a day; death is by drying, not
  by the microbe's poison. Matching the outflow with this
  solution, mouth against gut, drops cholera's untreated death
  rate from ~half to ~1 in 100 — a packet of sugar and salt
  standing where an intravenous ward would otherwise have to.
  Judged by lives per coin, likely the best medicine of its
  century; the arithmetic of the pump made it, and it needs no
  refrigerator, no needle, no doctor.

## METHODS
How things are done here — each with what governs it, when it
applies, and what breaks it. The prepositioned how-to-build.

- HYGIENE AND ASEPSIS (never let them in): soap dismantles the
  fatty skin many microbes wear and the greasy film the rest
  ride on (chemistry: soap's two-ended molecule pries fat from
  water — one end holds grease, the other holds water, and the
  rinse carries both away); alcohol at 60-90% unravels their
  proteins; sterile field and instrument
  keep the wound's landing party at zero, where the doubling law
  starts from nothing. Breaks when: rushed (seconds matter),
  skipped once (one seeding suffices — see the doubling law), or
  aimed at the wrong strength (pure alcohol works WORSE than 70%;
  denaturing needs water present).
- VACCINATION (the poster before the criminal): deliver the face
  — killed whole, weakened live, or a fragment — and let memory
  form in peace. Governed by the race arithmetic and the herd
  wall. Breaks when: the microbe changes its face faster than
  posters print (flu's yearly reissue; the cold's two hundred
  faces — mirror holds that entry), or the cold chain breaks and
  the poster spoils, or trust breaks and arms are never offered.
- THE ANTIBIOTIC COURSE (targeted layoffs, finished): match drug
  to microbe (culture it, test it against the drug panel), dose
  by weight, re-dose by half-life, run the full course while
  levels stay lethal. Governed by the resistance arithmetic.
  Breaks when: aimed at a virus (no target exists — pure
  selection cost, zero benefit), stopped at "feeling better"
  (selects the survivors), or dosed below the kill line.
- THE CONTROLLED TRIAL (the method for knowing itself): randomize,
  control, blind, count, subtract. Medicine's only instrument for
  telling a real cure from the body's own work plus hope. Breaks
  when: arms too small (the √N law), the blind leaks, or the
  question is one no one may ethically randomize (there the
  method yields to careful observation, and certainty drops a
  grade — said aloud).
- DIAGNOSIS BY ELIMINATION (the differential): list what could
  produce the picture, order the list by danger and likelihood,
  test to strike entries until one stands. Governed by the
  base-rate law at every test. Breaks when: the list was never
  full (the unconsidered cause is invisible), or a test's yes is
  trusted without its base-rate arithmetic.
- SURGERY UNDER ANESTHESIA AND ASEPSIS (the paired method):
  neither half works alone — pain control made surgery bearable
  and slow-able; asepsis made it survivable. Anesthesia is dosed
  by weight and vigil (its full WHY is still white — the method
  outruns the mechanism and is used ten thousand times a day
  anyway, monitored because not fully understood). Breaks when:
  either half is skimped — speed-era surgery killed by shock and
  sepsis alike.
- ISOLATION AND QUARANTINE (starve the chain): remove links until
  R falls below 1; the epidemic arithmetic does the rest. The
  oldest method against the invisible, run on pure logic
  centuries before germs were seen. Breaks when: the disease
  spreads before it shows (the presymptomatic leak), or
  compliance breaks — a method half-followed fails by the same
  compounding it fights.
- REHYDRATION FIRST (treat the dying, then the disease): most
  deaths in diarrheal disease are by drying; the sugar-salt pump
  (SCIENCE) holds the patient alive while the microbe burns out
  or is beaten. The general form: stabilize the failing system
  before hunting the cause — airway, breathing, circulation, in
  that order, because minutes kill in that order.
- TRIAGE (arithmetic under scarcity): when hands are fewer than
  wounds, sort by salvageability, not severity alone — the worst
  case that CAN be saved outranks the worst case that cannot.
  Brutal, counted, and it saves the most lives per hand; breaks
  hearts by design and lives by neglect of it.
- WATCHFUL WAITING (time as a method): most colds, most backs,
  many lumps and fevers resolve; the method is scheduled
  re-examination with named tripwires ("return if..."). Governed
  by self-healing rates. Breaks when: waiting is unwatched — no
  tripwires, no schedule — or applied to the fast compounders
  (sepsis, some cancers) where the doubling law owns the clock.
- SCREENING THE WELL (hunt before symptoms): apply a cheap test
  to a chosen group, confirm positives with an independent
  second. Governed entirely by the base-rate law — choose the
  group to raise the base rate. Breaks when: applied to
  everyone for everything, where the false alarms outnumber the
  finds ten to one and the harms of chasing them outweigh the
  cures.
- PUBLIC HEALTH ENGINEERING (medicine without patients): sewer,
  water treatment, food inspection, vaccination law. Acts on the
  dose arithmetic wholesale — cut the delivered dose below the
  landing-party line for a whole city at once. The
  highest-lives-per-effort method in this file; it breaks only
  when it is defunded, and its success is invisible, which is
  exactly what keeps threatening its funding.

## MEANS
What the methods run on, with the numbers that matter.

- THE MICROSCOPE — made the enemy visible after all of history
  blind; germ theory's instrument the way the thermometer is
  cooking's.
- THE THERMOMETER — fever made a number; 37°C the set point,
  ~38 the flag, ~41-42 the redline the protein ladder sets.
- SOAP, ALCOHOL, BLEACH — chemistry's three cheap executioners:
  soap dismantles (and merely LIFTS what it doesn't kill —
  the mechanics of the 20 seconds matter), alcohol at 60-90%
  denatures, dilute bleach oxidizes; between them they cover
  hands, skin, and surfaces for pennies.
- THE AUTOCLAVE — cooking's pressure canner in a white coat:
  121°C, ~15-20 minutes, 12 tenfold drops, spores included;
  the kettle never could (SCIENCE holds the derivation).
- THE NEEDLE AND THE LINE — the road past the gut for drugs the
  stomach would destroy or the emergency cannot wait on; also
  the road infections take if the hygiene method slips.
- THE CULTURE PLATE AND THE DRUG PANEL — grow the microbe, try
  the layoffs against it in glass before betting the patient.
- IMAGING — X-ray, ultrasound, and their heirs: physics lending
  medicine eyes that see through the wall (each with its own
  dose arithmetic — the X-ray is itself a dose).
- THE CUFF AND THE STETHOSCOPE — pressure and sound: the
  circulation's numbers (~120/80 mmHg the textbook resting
  pair) read from outside, no wound needed.
- THE COLD CHAIN — vaccines are proteins; the ladder that cooks
  an egg spoils a vaccine; an unbroken line of refrigeration
  from factory to arm is the poster's supply line.
- THE PHARMACY — molecules by class: the layoffs (antibiotics),
  the alarm-quieters (analgesics), the thermostat-lowerers,
  the pump-and-pressure tuners, the immune-brakes, the
  replacement shipments (insulin, thyroxine — what the body
  stopped making, mailed in on the half-life schedule).
- THE RECORD AND THE COUNT — the ledger of cases and outcomes:
  medicine's memory, and the raw material every trial and every
  Snow-style map is built from. What is not counted cannot be
  learned from; the count is an instrument, same rank as the
  microscope.

## PURPOSE
The wants this subject answers — the doors a query enters by.

- KEEP ALIVE: turn back the death that arrives by microbe,
  wound, or failing organ — the founding want.
- RELIEVE SUFFERING: pain, nausea, fear, breathlessness — worth
  answering even when nothing can be cured; half of medicine is
  this half.
- RESTORE FUNCTION: the mended bone that bears weight, the
  cleared artery, the lens replaced — repair, not just rescue.
- PREVENT: the cheapest door — vaccines, clean water, washed
  hands, the habits; every case prevented is a cure that never
  had to be afforded.
- FORETELL: prognosis — what will happen, how fast, with what
  odds; the want behind every "how long do I have," answered
  honestly with counted distributions, never with a single
  confident number.
- EASE THE DYING: when cure is spent, comfort remains —
  palliation is medicine keeping its second purpose after its
  first is lost, not medicine giving up.

## HISTORY
How the knowledge arose, in what order, at what cost — the order
carries why.

- Method outran why for most of history: cinchona bark broke
  fevers (quinine, malaria), citrus prevented scurvy, variolation
  preceded any theory of immunity — each worked for centuries
  unexplained, proof that HOW can be held and used before WHY
  arrives (cooking's fermentation tells the same story).
- Harvey multiplied the heart's output (1628) and circulation
  fell out of the arithmetic — the first great win of counting
  over authority, against fourteen centuries of Galen's texts
  outranking the body on the table.
- Lind split twelve scorbutic sailors into six pairs (1747),
  gave each pair a different remedy, and only the citrus pair
  walked — the trial's ancestor, run on twelve men because
  twelve was what he had. The Navy did not issue lemon juice
  fleet-wide until 1795: forty-eight years between the answer
  and the acting on it, and scurvy killed through every one of
  them. The gap between knowing and doing is itself a recurring
  historical object, and it is usually measured in decades.
- Jenner turned the milkmaids' lore into the first vaccine
  (1796); smallpox — which killed ~300 million people in the
  20th century alone before the campaign closed — was declared
  eradicated in 1980: the only human disease ever deliberately
  made extinct, and the standing proof that a white entry can be
  drained to the very bottom.
- Semmelweis counted the two maternity wards (1847): the
  doctors' ward killed ~1 mother in 10, the midwives' ~1 in 25,
  and the difference walked in on the doctors' hands from the
  autopsy room. Chlorine washing dropped the doctors' ward to
  ~1 in 80. Run the subtraction: 1/10 − 1/80 = 10% − 1.25% =
  8.75%, and on ~3,000 births a year that is ~260 mothers a
  year, saved by a basin. Two wards in one building, differing
  in one habit, is the control arm arriving before anyone had
  named it. The claim was rejected in his lifetime anyway
  (the mirror holds that record and its lesson).
- Snow mapped the cholera deaths of 1854 around the Broad Street
  pump — ~600 dead in a neighborhood, the brewery workers
  (who drank beer) spared — and the handle came off. Miasma
  died of a map and a dose arithmetic (the mirror holds the
  body).
- Pasteur and Koch made germ theory stand (1860s-80s): the
  swan-neck flask ended spontaneous generation, the postulates
  tied one microbe to one disease, and safety in kitchen and
  clinic alike became arithmetic. Lister carried it into surgery
  with carbolic acid, and counted it: 16 deaths in 35
  amputations before, 6 in 40 after — ~46% down to ~15%, which
  is two deaths in every three simply gone.
- Anesthesia arrived (ether, 1846) and surgical skill stopped
  being a footrace; paired with asepsis it opened the body's
  interior to repair at all. How it works at depth is STILL not
  settled — the longest-running method-outruns-why in modern
  medicine, filed white.
- Insulin (1922) converted type 1 diabetes from months-to-live
  into a managed lifetime — the replacement-shipment template:
  what the body stopped making, made outside and mailed in on
  the half-life schedule.
- Penicillin, found by accident (1928) and industrialized under
  war (1943-45), opened the antibiotic age; streptomycin's 1948
  tuberculosis trial — patients assigned by sealed random
  numbers, a control arm on bed rest alone, and the chest films
  read by judges who did not know which arm they were scoring —
  was the modern trial's debut. (The blinding was partial: only
  the scorers were kept in the dark, since the control arm got
  no injection. Full double blinding came later; this was the
  first rung.) The two arrivals mark medicine's two modern
  pillars: the targeted layoff and the honest count.
- Oral rehydration therapy (1960s-70s) put the sugar-salt pump
  in a packet and has saved tens of millions since — the
  century's reminder that the cheapest arithmetic, not the
  newest machine, often buys the most lives.

## RELATIONS
First-class connections — the cascade's paths outward. Each names
what runs along it.

- COOKING: the shared kill trade (time-for-temperature, D-drops,
  the spore ceiling), pasteurization's numbers, the danger-zone
  doubling law, preservation as microbe denial, nutrition's
  supply line. Same teacher — Pasteur — two classrooms.
- CHEMISTRY: every drug is a molecule with a shape, a solubility,
  a half-life; soap and bleach and alcohol are reactions; pH
  gates the stomach's own defense; the sugar-salt pump is
  chemistry read as a life raft.
- LIFE / BIOLOGY: evolution runs resistance in real time; the
  microbe, the cell, and the immune system are its objects;
  attenuation (weakening a live vaccine) is directed evolution in
  a bottle.
- BODY: medicine is the body's repair manual and the body is the
  senior partner — most healing is its work; anatomy, the
  ladder of its proteins, the circulation's arithmetic.
- PHYSICS: imaging's rays and echoes, the pressure in the cuff
  and the vessel, diffusion across the lung's surface, radiation
  as both scalpel and dose.
- MATHEMATICS: the exponential (dose decay, microbe growth,
  epidemics), probability as the trial's skeleton, the base-rate
  law, √N noise, the counted ledger.
- FARMING: antibiotics fed to livestock select resistance that
  walks to the clinic; diseases jump from animals (the zoonoses);
  food safety is the shared border post with cooking.
- WEATHER: malaria follows the mosquito's map, flu the winter,
  cholera the flood; heat and cold kill directly at the body's
  redlines.
- ECONOMY: prevention is the cheapest life ever purchased and is
  perpetually underbought because its success is invisible;
  drug prices ration cures; the poor still die of the solved
  diseases — a distribution failure, not a knowledge failure.
- CULTURE: trust decides whether the poster reaches the arm and
  the cure reaches the mouth; ritual carries real comfort (and
  real placebo effect); what pain may be spoken of decides what
  pain gets treated.
- ENGINEERING: the sewer and the waterworks — the biggest
  medicines ever built; the autoclave, the imaging machines, the
  cold chain; the hospital itself as a machine for concentrating
  care without concentrating contagion (a fight it never fully
  wins).
