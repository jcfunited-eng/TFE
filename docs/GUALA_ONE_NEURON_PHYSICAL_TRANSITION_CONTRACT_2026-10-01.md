### Decisive counterexamples, not output tuning

Swapping an isolated nonzero D fact with an M fact yields exactly the same
concatenated digit stream. Values 2 and 1/2 have the same trit multiset after
numerator/denominator concatenation; values 10 and 12 differ in positional
placement but also have the same multiset. An unordered scalar sum cannot
recover the discarded structure. Signed numerator trits DO preserve numeric
sign; the original chat wording about sign loss was incorrect.

For the rejected fixed reversal source,
$$
\dot E_{cap}=-gV(V-E_c).
$$
This can be positive for `0 < V < E_c`, even without new external trits.
Stored chemical energy and the source's work cannot be omitted. For a
constant-conductance passive specialization with `E_c=0`, explicit Euler
gives `V_next=(1-g*dt/C)*V`; non-increasing capacitor energy requires
`0 <= g*dt/C <= 2`. Accepting every positive finite dt does not prove this.
Clipping dt/tau is a changed law, not a stability derivation.

A scalar phase of `1e308` is finite, but subtracting `2*pi` does not change
its binary64 representation. The retired normalizer's saturating quotient
followed by repeated subtraction therefore can hang. No stress test is needed
to justify deleting that path.
