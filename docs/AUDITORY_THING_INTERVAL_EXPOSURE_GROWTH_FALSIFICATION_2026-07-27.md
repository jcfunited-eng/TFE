# Auditory THING Interval Exposure-Growth Falsification — 2026-07-27

## Architecture honesty

- Requested architecture: determine whether substantially more repeated,
  varied, physically custodied experience makes the already frozen local
  interval-neuron law robust.
- Current code reality: the law grows exact per-cell coordinate intervals,
  but canonical recurrence and cross-THING quiescence can remove cells.
- Conflict: yes. The resulting whole receptive population is not monotone:
  its surviving cell population shrank as exposure increased.
- Mechanisms not extended: the law itself, L0-L4, LoomNeuron, chi,
  MapInject, STDP, scores, thresholds, labels, waveform matching, ML, and
  post-oracle feature selection.
- Single tested item: cumulative 5/10/20/40/80 independent speakers per
  causal THING against one fixed untouched 20-speaker-per-THING held-out set.
- Full-field evaluation: the byte-frozen law retained complete local
  prior/following D/M/R/U/C/P/B tuples, duration, physical topology,
  co-occurrence edges, and source witnesses.
- Declared reduction: each local cell used coordinate intervals and therefore
  did not retain covariance inside the cell.

## What experience growth proved

The learned intervals themselves expanded from additional lived values.
However, the complete mosaic did not expand monotonically. As the episode
count grew, canonical recurrence retained fewer local cell kinds, and
cross-THING recurrence made additional cells quiescent. Ordered edge
structure disappeared entirely after the five-exposure rung.

The result is therefore not “too little experience.” Eighty independent
speakers per THING made this fixed mechanism less selective than twenty.
Changing recurrence, retaining union cells, widening individual intervals,
or reducing the complete local tuple after seeing this curve would define a
new recognizer and is prohibited in this audit.
