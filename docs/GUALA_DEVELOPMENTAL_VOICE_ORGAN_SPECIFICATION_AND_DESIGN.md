Status: permanent living design authority. Last reconciled 2026-09-05.

This document preserves the reusable physical lessons from the vocal-organ
campaign. Chronological repair attempts remain the evidence record; this file
states what later designs must preserve, what has been falsified, and what is
still open. A copied-body candidate is not promoted here as accepted physics
until its declared falsifiers pass.

## Permanent physical design

The voice is one causal body, not a sound generator attached to cognition:

```text
learned causal activity
  -> learned local motor contact
  -> motor cell's paid local preparation
  -> typed antagonist tissue gesture
  -> finite lung flow through glottis and tract
  -> radiated signed pressure
  -> the same pressure at both ears
  -> ordinary sensory/cognitive consequence
```

Every arrow must have a predecessor, successor, material/work account, and
typed evidence. Observation can report the chain but cannot complete it.

### Breath and airway protection are different acts

Gustatory contact may close the glottis through the born airway-protection
reflex. That closure supplies zero respiratory work. Learned vocal action may
shape the glottis and tract, while only the dedicated respiratory effector's
own bounded discharge supplies expiratory work. Glottal displacement, a
lesson, or a software permission bit can never manufacture breath.

Lung air is physical resident body state. Positive flow debits exact volume,
including its retained fractional remainder. Passive inspiration may restore
only previously displaced volume and stops at the declared neutral coordinate.
No utterance can relaunch until both whole and fractional lung displacement
have returned to neutral. This is a material cycle, not a repetition timer.

### The acoustic organ is source-plus-moving-body physics

One exact motor event may launch bounded sustained tissue activation. Tissue,
airflow, glottal valve, turbulence, lossy tract modes, radiation, and the room
then evolve on their own physical clocks. The neuronal event is not stretched
to imitate acoustic duration. Pressure exists only while stored body work and
airflow sustain it; loss returns it to exact silence.

Tract areas are persisted body coordinates with antagonist motors and ordinary
proprioceptive return. They are not phoneme parameters. Their continuous
trajectory may produce recognizable phonemic structure, but no label or
target posture enters the organism.

### Learned motor transduction must be developmental and state-driven

A learned L11/L12 contact may identify which already-grown motor branch is
locally recruited. It may not prescribe how many carriers the motor emits.
The action magnitude must arise from the current control occurrence, contact
state and conductance, retained phase/work, motor membrane and reversal drive,
finite carrier material, elapsed physical time, load/fatigue, and exact
available work.

The presynaptic event is control; the postsynaptic motor's own finite physical
path supplies the movement. Presynaptic carriers must not be relabelled as if
they crossed into the motor. Contact transition, current, retained phase,
work, heat, depletion, endpoint successors, and later terminal discharge must
be settled atomically from one predecessor.

All simultaneous branches from one sender are solved jointly and order-
independently. Storage order, lineage sort order, or the first branch visited
can never select a muscle. Zero work, zero conductance, severance, exhausted
material, or absence of a lawful local transition produces silence.

### Self-hearing is identity of pressure, not similarity

The raw pressure returned through Guala's ears is the exact raw pressure her
body radiated. Observer amplification may affect only the listener's speaker.
It cannot change raw pressure, self-hearing, cognition, evidence, or persistence.
The observer cache is process-local, bounded, replace-only, and has no organism
authority.

## What the campaign has actually proved

### Accepted organ capacity

The V22 toy bench established a human-audible target class and prevented
machine metrics from calling buzz, ping, pop, or throat noise speech. It is a
comparison instrument only; none of its waveform or gesture values may enter
the organism as learned content.

The attempt-46 native dynamic harness then varied bounded neighboring physical
trajectories through the real native organ. Zero breath was silent, frozen and
reversed controls differed, candidates were deterministic and unclipped, and
pulmonary/acoustic state returned to rest. Joseph accepted the neighboring
native candidates as recognizable `ma-ma` and selected **Mama-A**. This proves
the native organ can render a recognizable whole word when supplied a lawful
physical trajectory. It does not prove that Guala spoke it.

The slightly robotic tone was explicitly accepted for now. Tonal refinement
is not a current blocker and must not displace developmental control.

### Accepted bounded phonation and self-hearing

Attempt 44 proved finite lung debit and passive return, sustained bounded
pressure, exact pressure/self-hearing identity, exact cold restoration, fixed
resident bytes, and quiet intervals on an exact production-body copy. The
accepted organ went live on task 1429 and later emitted distinct replacement
pressure records rather than replaying one cached sound.

Those facts preserve the breath, body, acoustic, transport, and self-hearing
parts of the organ. They do **not** preserve attempt 44's fixed one-carrier
learned-motor bridge; attempt 47 has falsified that bridge as developmental
physics.

### Accepted localization of the remaining speech boundary

The task-1429 copied body has learned vocal routes to tract section 0 and
section 7, but they co-occur at one posture. A 512-hop copied-body run produced
repeatable pressure and self-hearing with identical vocal coordinates. More
breath, renderer tuning, or valve tuning cannot create the missing order.

The living path therefore has two separate open requirements:

1. lawful state-driven learned-motor transduction must replace the fixed
   carrier assignment; and
2. organism-owned causal development must later produce ordered, distinct
   vocal gestures and reuse their heard consequences.

## Permanently rejected mechanisms and interpretations

- A resonator struck without continuing airflow produces a buzz or knock, not
  a voice. Do not train cognition against that defect.
- Swallow closure cannot be counted as respiratory drive.
- Nonzero pressure, a vocal-looking trace, or exact self-hearing is not a
  phoneme or word.
- A test-only trajectory proves actuator capacity only. Copying it into the
  organism would be a scripted word.
- One fixed carrier, 32 fixed carriers, a fixed fraction, the largest
  energy-descending split, or any hand-picked audible split is not a physical
  transduction law.
- Charge conservation and lower aggregate work are necessary but do not prove
  that a nonzero branch magnitude was physically caused.
- Editing only endpoint carrier counts while leaving contact phase, current,
  work, heat, and persisted contact state from the unbranched transition is
  false conservation evidence.
- The generic symmetric L11/L12 electrical contact drives the copied motor in
  the wrong direction and cannot be renamed a directional synapse.
- The existing motor reversal-gradient pump is variable in healthy motors but
  the two copied vocal endpoints cannot afford even one inward carrier from
  their current reservoirs. Repeating pump scaling without a new physical
  energy source is prohibited.
- Increasing reservoir capacity, resetting thermal state, deleting heat,
  dropping carriers, suppressing self-hearing, or manufacturing pressure masks
  the defect and is not repair.
- More retained causal frontiers cannot replace anatomy that is absent.
- A broad vocal hub or whole-tract coincidence cannot truthfully identify one
  learned motor.

## Required test doctrine

No one-point acoustic or carrier test can authorize a voice repair. Every
candidate must use a bounded dynamic harness and retain the complete matrix,
including failed regions.

The matrix varies every state quantity the proposed law says controls output:
source work, retained contact phase/work, conductance, reversal/potential,
carrier material, motor load, elapsed time, multiple simultaneous branches,
and severance. Output must change with physical state, remain deterministic
from the same predecessor, and close carrier and energy accounting exactly.

Required negative controls include zero breath, zero source work, zero
conductance, severed learned contact, exhausted work/material, frozen posture,
reversed posture order, unrelated motor, reflex-only action, and observer
absence. Branch permutations must produce byte-identical successors and
evidence.

After isolated falsifiers pass, the exact immutable production body—not a
fresh body—must prove body movement, proprioception, emitted pressure, exact
self-hearing, current-state change, cold restart, and a later ordinary
interval. Identity, all neurons, full DSF fields, learned contacts, formations,
sensory state, world, and predecessor custody must be preserved.

Every mature harness includes a read-only AWS health snapshot before and
after, fixed CPU/RAM/time/output ceilings, flat retained-state and evidence
counts, exact process cleanup, and no production writer. Human hearing is the
final sound-quality gate; machine metrics can reject clipping, instability,
periodicity, or non-variation but cannot declare intelligible speech.

## Rejected candidate 47C and current open boundary

Attempt 47C proposed using each learned contact's already-persisted finite
transition-work phase as bounded work storage, funded only by real work that
would otherwise leave the causally reached L11 founding transition as heat.
Stored work would pay the receiving L12 motor's own exact local preparation;
no carrier would cross the learned bond.

The exact task-1429 census passed its first falsifier: all seven learned
L11/L12 contacts have 50 conducting channels, 500 pS effective conductance,
and zero retained work phase, so no historical contact state would be silently
reinterpreted.

The candidate nevertheless fails the earlier ratified-physics gate. A-011.6
defines the phase only as sub-quantum bookkeeping toward a bounded conducting-
channel transition. Completed transition work is dissipated and cannot be
counted again as stored membrane or fluid energy. Spending the same phase on
L12 preparation would give one state coordinate two incompatible physical
meanings. Candidate 47C is therefore rejected before dynamic testing; it is not
an active repair.

The open boundary remains one genuinely directional learned transducer whose
control state and power source are separately physical. The next analysis must
choose between separately authored finite synaptic state and an already-real
postsynaptic energy path. It may not reuse A-011.6 residue, relabel presynaptic
carriers as motor carriers, or infer output from a fixed or selected extent.
No production code or deployment is authorized before that mechanism has a
complete ratified contract and dynamic copied-body proof.

## Evidence and amendment discipline

Each future attempt must record, before another attempt begins:

- exact production task/commit/image and immutable copied-body hash;
- exact hypothesis and the earlier attempts it is distinct from;
- source symbols and complete causal impact;
- dynamic ranges and negative controls actually executed;
- exact pass/reject result, including a diagnostic that passed while the
  proposed repair failed;

- which paragraph of this specification is confirmed, invalidated, or amended.

is found, but the dependent end-to-end claim must be narrowed explicitly. A
later finding never silently overwrites history, and a green topology,
conservation, restart, or health test can never substitute for the missing
causal variable.
