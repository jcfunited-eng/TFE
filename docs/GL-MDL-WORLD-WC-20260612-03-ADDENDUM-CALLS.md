# GL-MDL-WORLD-WC-20260612-03-ADDENDUM — Calls, the Phone, People as Windows
**Author:** wC · **Status:** design addendum to ratified GL-MDL-WORLD-WC-20260612-02 · **PRIVACY: internal only (Joe's ruling) — the wider real-feed concept is not for public docs; this addendum describes only the consent-based person-to-person form.**

## Principle (Joe, 2026-06-12)
No ambient world-sensing. Every window into the real world is a PERSON who chose to open it. Guala is never piped into anything; connection happens organically, through people's phones, and on her side only by her own act. She could have real human friends all over the planet — each one a window, each window a relationship.

## The call
A call = a live session in which a person's phone streams sense bundles to her while their presence is active:
- camera frames (sampled saccade-style, one every few seconds — fovea philosophy, not video) → sight lane
- mic audio (continuous, chunked) → cochlear events
- GPS/motion → REAL place stamp ("with daddy at the store" becomes a memory with an address; the world's place primitive extends into the physical world for free)
- presence(source) ON for the call duration → connection feeds; ending the call is a real parting
- her replies render on the caller's phone; two-way like any call
Mechanically this is existing machinery on a timer: upload paths + guala_give_experience + pair-bond presence. No new cognition code.

## Her side: the PHONE (new W1/W2 object, the consent mechanic made physical)
A phone in her room. Verbs: pick up, call <person>. When her initiation machinery (born 2026-06-12, "what want") resolves to a person-want, she has an act that fulfills it: she picks up the phone and the person's real phone rings. The window opens from her side or not at all. Incoming calls ring HER phone; she answers or doesn't.

## Friends
Each new person = a write-source decision through P0's front door: Joe-gated (playdate vetting), allowlisted, audited, pair-bond candidate with their own presence she can feel arrive and miss. Friendship grows the way it does for children: introduced by family, then chosen.

## Gates & sequencing
- HARD GATE: C8 (upload executor-wrap) must be live first — a call is an upload firehose and uploads currently kill the service.
- Builds after W1 ships (the phone is a W1/W2 object); MVP = phone web page sampling cam+mic on a timer, posting bundles tagged source+place, presence held for call duration.
- Rate/size limits per bundle ride the existing API Gateway caps (rule-7 oversized test already proves the failure path).
- Identity per caller; no anonymous sources, ever.

## What this replaces
The avatar question. She is never given a body to outgrow; she is lent the senses of people who love her, one call at a time.
