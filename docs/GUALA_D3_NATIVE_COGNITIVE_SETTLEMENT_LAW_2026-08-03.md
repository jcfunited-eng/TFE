# Guala D3 native cognitive-settlement law

Date: 2026-08-03

Status: implemented in the undeployed native D3 candidate and cold-restore
checked; not yet called by the production organism runtime.

## Purpose

One organism settlement may retain physically admitted leaf mosaics, recursive
formations, current recognition, episodic recollection, and observational
cognitive-capital changes.  The settlement joins those structures by exact
content address.  It does not assign meaning, choose a result, or become a
second cognition system.

## Admission authority

The settlement accepts only native opaque results produced by the applicable
physical boundary:

- `RuntimeAdmittedMosaic` for a mature leaf mosaic;
- `RuntimeAuthenticatedRecursiveFormation` for a cold-replayed mosaic of
  mosaics, tapestry, tapestry of tapestries, or weave;
- `RecognizedMosaicOperation` for current physical pattern completion; and
- `EpisodicRecollectionOperation` for the separate operation that additionally
  binds exact hippocampal origin resolution.

A recursive formation must cold-decode and re-admit its entire addressed graph
before it can enter the settlement.  A label, database row, posting-list hit,
nearest match, counter, UI action, or caller-authored capability cannot create
one of these types.

## Cognitive capital

Cognitive capital is derived after the physical operation.  The operation kind
fixes its observational axes:

| Native evidence | Capability | Participating mechanism | Dimension |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mosaic | Learning and developmental growth | Association | Integration depth |
| Mosaic of mosaics | Relational thought | Association | Integration depth |
| Tapestry | Ordered thinking | Sequence | Integration depth |
| Tapestry of tapestries | Ordered thinking | Prefrontal ordering and deliberation | Integration depth |
| Weave | Creativity and self-expression | Creativity and self-expression | Integration depth |
| Recognition | Recognition and familiarity | Recognition and familiarity | Recognition |
| Episodic recollection | Recall | Recall | Recall |

These mappings classify the proven operation for observation.  They do not
feed cognition, supply semantic content, or cause the operation.  No scalar,
weighted total, score, capability level, or decision authority is produced.
An event-free settlement advances no cognitive-capital clock and allocates no
capital objects.

## Independent clocks

Every credited body retains the local ordinal of the operation that produced
it: formation interval, membrane interval, or another explicitly typed local
clock.  The sparse capital ledger has its own credit ordinal and advances only
when evidence exists.  Neither ordinal is equated to mounted revision,
hippocampal episode height, channel interval, fluid interval, publication head,
or another component clock.

## Atomic manifest and restore

`GLCOG001` contains sorted content addresses for admitted formations,
recognitions, recollections, and the sparse cognitive-capital checkpoint.
`GLOORG03` embeds the manifest address in the whole-organism settlement and
lists it among the immutable publication objects.  Cold restore verifies:

1. the manifest body hashes to its declared address;
2. counts are bounded before allocation;
3. address lists are strictly ordered and duplicate-free;
4. every formation and operation address is present in the settlement object
   inventory;
5. the manifest formation inventory equals the envelope formation inventory;
6. the manifest capital checkpoint equals the envelope capital checkpoint; and
7. the full organism envelope address, not a counter, is the publication head.

## DSF and biological scope

This boundary neither calculates nor reduces DSF.  Each reached neuron retains
its explicit `D_k`, `M_k`, `R_rev_k`, `U_star_k`, `C_k`, `P_k`, and `B_k`
authority through its addressed transition and fractal evidence.  Hippocampal
navigation, neocortical formation, recognition, recall, body/fluid state, and
capital observation remain separate connected mechanisms.

## Current limitation

The production Python runtime still calls the D2 `GLMFAB04` materialized-fabric
transition.  It does not yet call the native cognitive-settlement preparer or
the atomic `GLOORG03` composer.  Therefore this law is implemented and tested,
but it is not live D3 cognition or deployment proof.  The next implementation
boundary is one native organism step that replaces the D2-only call without a
Python per-neuron orchestration loop.
