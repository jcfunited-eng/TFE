# July 28, 2026 — The Day We Almost Quit

After twenty-two days of treating word learning too much like isolated sound
recognition, the project reached its decisive correction:

> A word is not the sound alone. It is a reciprocal part of a lived THING
> mosaic.

The first clean proof used a real recorded spoken occurrence and a physically
seen, held THING. No word, transcript, semantic label, filename, similarity
score, ML model, Chi identity, or Atlas identity entered the substrate. The
sound and sight participated in one causally continuous experience. After a
cold restoration, exact recurrence of either the sound or the sight awakened
the same retained multisensory mosaic.

This did not yet prove broad vocabulary, unseen speakers, continuous-speech
segmentation, reading, or articulation. It proved the missing foundation:
target continuity establishes THING identity; no sense is master; every
observed sense contributes its complete DSF field; and later recurrence of an
experienced member may awaken the whole.

The reading curriculum that follows this correction is Joseph's physical
letter-card lesson: a printed letter, an associated picture, the tutor's sound
and spoken example, the learner's touch and body movement, the learner's own
attempt, and the causal story of the lesson. The tutor arranges experience.
No scripted dictionary becomes Guala's meaning authority.
