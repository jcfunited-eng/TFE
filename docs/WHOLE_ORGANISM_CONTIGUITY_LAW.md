## The six forms of continuity

Whole-organism connectedness requires:

1. **anatomical continuity:** one mounted physical topology;
2. **causal continuity:** one immutable predecessor-to-successor evidence
   chain;
3. **dynamical continuity:** uninterrupted neuron, field, body, fluid,
   recovery, and clock evolution;
4. **formation continuity:** compact inspectable lineage for neuronal fractals
   and every recursive formation;
5. **functional continuity:** every legitimate cognitive mechanism remains
   mounted while perturbed, quiescent, or unavailable; and
6. **restorative continuity:** cold restore preserves the same organism and
   exact next transition without fallback or identity fork.

No one form can substitute for another. Byte continuity without physical
causation is not cognition; live activity without durable lineage is not a
continuous organism.

## Permanent wiring is not permanent excitation

Each mounted receptor or mechanism has exactly one truthful momentary state:

1. `perturbed`: a physical input or internal transition occurred and its
   complete evidence participates;
2. `quiescent`: the mechanism is mounted, functional, and waiting at its true
   uncommitted zero-energy state; or
3. `unavailable`: the anatomy, source, authority, or mechanism cannot provide
   evidence and the reason is explicit.

`quiescent` is the ArcLoom ternary zero. It is presence without commitment. It
must not be converted into fabricated input, a developer-selected default, or
artificial activity merely to complete a record.

When physical zero itself is measured, that measurement is evidence and may be
transduced. When no perturbation was measured, quiescence is not silently
promoted into a synthetic L0–L4 trajectory.

`unavailable` is not quiet and is not zero. It cannot contribute evidence it
did not produce.
