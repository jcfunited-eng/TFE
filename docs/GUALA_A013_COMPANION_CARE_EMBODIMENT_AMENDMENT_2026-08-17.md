## Smallest lawful physical increment

The first increment is not named `hug` or `comfort`. It is one physical
body-surface contact occurrence:

```text
authenticated guardian surface trajectory
  + authenticated Guala cutaneous site geometry
  + explicit surface material and temperature
  + exact causal duration and normal/tangential work
  -> sparse local contact settlement
  -> reciprocal world/body successor
  -> tactile + thermal + proprioceptive receptor occurrence
  -> unchanged full DSF and complete-neuron settlement
  -> one current-only committed successor
```

Required causative state:

- stable body and surface-site identities;
- world-space centre/orientation/extent for each reached site;
- surface material, temperature, compliance, and friction evidence;
- exact contact normal, compression, normal/tangential load, duration, work,
  heat transfer, and any slip or separation;
- sparse ancestry from guardian actuation to guardian surface displacement;
- sparse ancestry from any Guala action to her surface displacement;
- returned cutaneous, thermal, and proprioceptive consequences; and
- one bounded current contact frontier, not a contact history.

No default physical coefficient is permitted. Every value must be derived
from the declared virtual morphology/material lattice or, later, measured from
the selected physical body. Missing anatomy makes the contact unavailable.

The guardian may initiate a low-energy physical offer because the guardian is
an external participant, not Guala's cognitive controller. No `accepted` or
`consented` bit enters physics. Guala's ongoing body motion remains causally
effective: orientation, approach, maintained contact, separating motion,
stillness, later return, and distress/body overload are retained separately.
An observer may describe willing participation only from that trajectory; the
contact name cannot manufacture it.

## How named care becomes evidence later

The following names remain external descriptions of complete physical
episodes. They never become motor commands or substrate meanings.

| Care description | Minimum physical facts before the description is truthful |
|---|---|
| Hug | multiple torso/arm cutaneous contacts, bounded pressure/work, guardian and Guala body trajectories, breath/posture consequences, free separation, and later reciprocal or voluntary continuation evidence |
| Kiss | world-projected oral/perioral surface contact with another explicit cutaneous site, material/thermal contact, bounded work, and returned oral/facial/tactile consequence; sound or animation is insufficient |
| Lift/hold/carry | body masses, gravity, vertical geometry, support/contact wrenches, load transfer, stable reciprocal support, root trajectory, vestibular/proprioceptive/tactile return, and safe release |
| Draw/paint together | held tool with tip geometry, hand/tool contact, mutable bounded surface cells, finite pigment/graphite material, contact work and transfer, rendered optical consequence, and each participant's actual actions |
| Bedtime | a supportable bed surface, Guala's body-on-bed contact and posture, covers/light/sound as physical world state, guardian reading as actual acoustic pressure, and sleep arising from Guala's body/organism state rather than a bedtime command |
| Bathtime | vessel and finite water mass, temperature/heat exchange, flow/drainage, wet skin/contact, buoyancy/support, soap or other material only when modeled, body sensory return, and guardian/Guala actions with bounded safety energy |

Holding Guala while the guardian performs another activity additionally
requires simultaneous carried-body support and independent guardian
manipulation. A location update attached to the guardian is not carrying.

## Pure-physics and resource boundary

- Authenticate once before contact settlement and once after it.
- Settle only reached surface sites and contact edges:
  `O(reached surfaces + reached contacts)`.
- No whole-body, whole-world, or neuron scan is permitted.
- Opposing work cancels or transfers through the contact law; it cannot create
  displacement, pressure, heat, or material.
- Retain current surface/contact/material state only. No per-touch log, queue,
  image sequence, or duplicated body snapshot may accumulate.
- Observation receipts describe the physical transition but never cause it.

## Disposition

No production code was added in this amendment. That is deliberate: the first
required physical input—world-space body-surface morphology with derived
material/contact coefficients—does not exist, and an unmounted arithmetic
helper would repeat the exact A-013 mistake already removed from the tree.

Recommended next action: integrate the single body-to-body cutaneous contact
increment only after the concurrent native root-action/world work is settled,
then use that exact contact law as the shared physical basis for affectionate
touch before adding gravity/support, art materials, water, or named care
episodes.
