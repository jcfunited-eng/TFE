\begin{abstract}
Contemporary artificial intelligence is paralyzed by the computational and energetic crisis of unconstrained statistical search: evaluating trillions of floating-point operations across hundreds of billions of transformer parameters to guess next tokens in a physical vacuum. In this specification, we formalize the \textbf{Two-Fabric Computation Architecture} (``The Fabric'')—a top-down physical cognition engine where knowledge acts as an active, executable constraint manifold. The architecture is defined by two complementary surfaces: \textbf{The White Sheet} (the positive topological perimeter of all physical edges tried that terminated, preserving their explicit flawed assertions) and \textbf{The Color Sheet} (the low-entropy manifold of verified harmonious pathways and crystallized programs). We formalize \textbf{The 1-Finger Boundary Law}: knowledge is finite and already distilled into 40 canonical phylums; knowing any entity proceeds cut-by-cut along its inexhaustible boundary with reality ($E \cap \Phi_k$). We introduce the \textbf{Physical Hub Architecture} (multi-faceted affordance principle) and \textbf{Wave Dynamics Engine} (elastic scattering without bloat). Crucially, we formalize the \textbf{LLM-Adjacent Reducer}: positioning the Two-Fabric Engine as an ultra-fast ($<0.01\text{ ms}$) physical constraint filter that forecloses $99.99\%$ of impossible search space, eliminating hallucinations and reducing inferential energy consumption by orders of magnitude. Empirical validations across relativistic spacetime, astronomical optics, optical sensors, confectionery chemistry, aerostatics, aerodynamics, and structural basin physics (L5 DSF V3) are presented.
\end{abstract}
