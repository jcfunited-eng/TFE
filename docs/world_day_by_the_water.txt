the wide ocean moves slow under the gray morning sky
the cold water touches her bare warm feet
she stands on the soft wet sand near the loud ocean
the salty smell of the ocean fills her nose
a small white bird flies above the moving water
the deep ocean is dark and wide and full
she finds a smooth gray stone on the wet sand
the stone is cold and smooth and round in her hand
she holds the small stone up to the bright sun
the wind moves her hair and the smell of the sea is strong
a small fish moves quick in the cool clear water
she watches the fish until it swims away into the dark
the soft wind carries the cool air across her warm face
she sits down on the warm dry sand far from the cold water
the gray sky opens and the warm sun comes through
the bright light shines on the wide moving water
small waves move slowly toward the soft yellow sand
she finds a small white shell beside the cold stone
the shell is smooth and small and white in her hand
she puts the soft shell to her ear and listens
inside the shell she hears the quiet voice of the ocean
a brown dog runs along the wet sand with quick happy feet
the dog stops and looks at the small girl with bright eyes
she throws the round stone and the dog runs after it
the dog brings the stone back and drops it at her feet
the warm sun moves slowly across the wide blue sky
the day by the ocean is full and long and good
she walks home with the smell of the ocean in her clothes
the small stone and the white shell are warm in her pocket
