HRB and MTD: price and the nine values, day by day and gate by gate

Joe, 2026-09-30: the field is the whole bar — Date, Open, High, Low, Close, Volume — not the close. The last two charts for each ticker feed the 5-vector (O, H, L, C, V), raw, to the literal kernel as found and to the kernel written from the canon (L0/L1 on the vector with the gate's own mean; L2/L3/L4 formulas from uf_core with no history-wide mean). Every value is unrounded.

Two kernels are drawn first for each ticker. Production (uf_core): the field is log(close), the gate threshold 0.20 is on log units, and the L2 contrast is taken against the mean of all gates in the history. Literal (standalone_truth_kernel.py, run as found): the field is the close in dollars, the threshold 0.20 is twenty cents, the contrast is against the gate's own mean. Same 2,700 closes into both.

Each day's nine values are what the production adapter (compute_uf_structural_state) returns on the closes through that day: the last gate's D_k, M_k, R_rev_k, U*_k, C_k, P_k, B_k as raw doubles, plus S_UF and R_UF. Two of the nine are averages by the adapter's own definition: S_UF = ½·(share of gates with D_k = 0) + ½·(share with no reversal), R_UF = mean R_k over all gates. The other seven are one gate's record. The second chart for each ticker shows every gate's record over its whole life, the object the spec calls the evolution.
