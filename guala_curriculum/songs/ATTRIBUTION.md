“Alphabet song.ogg,” recorded and published by Wikimedia Commons contributor
CambridgeBayWeather as their own work under CC BY-SA 3.0:

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alphabet_song.ogg
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

## Count-up and count-down songs

`count-up-one-to-ten-v1.wav` and `count-down-ten-to-one-v1.wav` use the
following human number recordings from Wikimedia Commons:

- One through nine: recordings attributed by Commons to
  `NumberOne~commonswiki`, each dedicated to the public domain.
- Ten: recording attributed by Commons to TheDaveRoss under CC BY-SA 3.0.

Each number occupies an exact 1.2-second slot. The source voice was resampled
to mono signed 16-bit PCM at 16 kHz and mixed with a short, low-level ascending
or descending sine-tone pulse. The tone sequence and arrangement were created
for this project. The two derivative recordings are distributed under
CC BY-SA 3.0.
