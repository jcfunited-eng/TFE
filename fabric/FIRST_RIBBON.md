# THE FIRST RIBBON — the language program, and what the fabric
# already forbids

Joe's instruction: the knowledge to build this is in the fabric,
and so are the walls that say which routes are dead. Read the
walls first, then build. This file is what the walls said.

WHAT THE FABRIC FORBIDS (each is a wall already written in the
language files; do not spend a night re-proving any of them)

1. No reading the structure off word order alone. The same words
   nest two ways and mean two things. So: build a nesting, not a
   left-to-right scan.
2. No listing the sentences of a language. The set has no end.
   So: nothing may be matched against stored sentences.
3. No understanding a new sentence except by building its meaning
   from its parts. So: composition is compulsory, and nothing can
   be all lookup.
4. No listing all the senses of a common word — the extension is
   live and new senses are made when needed. So: a sense must be
   PRODUCED from the company present, not fetched from a table.
   (This kills the sense-lookup file as a method. It stays as
   knowledge; it cannot be the mechanism.)
5. No sorting words by what they mean — the school definitions
   fail on half the vocabulary. So: a word's class comes from how
   it distributes, not from its meaning.
6. No listing the sentence-fitting forms in a dictionary — they
   are produced by rule. So: morphology by rule.
7. No fluent speech at conversational speed from full
   construction — there is not time. So: ready-made chunks must
   be carried, and only the joints built fresh.
8. No long sentence understood without its grouping. So: group
   before parsing.
9. No marked member without a plain one to be marked against.
   So: meaning is carried by contrast, not by presence.
10. No translating a term without checking its neighbours. So:
    a word's value is relational — what it beat, not what it is.

WHAT THAT LEAVES — the shape of the first ribbon
  group the sentence; build a nesting; take word behaviour from
  distribution; produce the sense from the company at hand;
  carry chunks; keep the contrast, not just the content.

WHAT THE RIBBON IS
  not a program I write and run over the knowledge. The ribbon
  carries data in, crosses the language knowledge, and that
  crossing assembles the handler for that particular sentence.
  The data (searches, results, particulars) never enters the
  sheets — it rides, is consumed or added to, and leaves.

STATE WHEN THIS WAS WRITTEN

  not exist: any parsing at all. That is the next build and it is
  the first ribbon.
