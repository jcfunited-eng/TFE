FABRIC WORLDS
Name a city. The name becomes the asking; six taught families answer at once — districts, heights, commerce, parks, character, style. Streets, shops, citizens and weather all fall out of that one answer. No two names build the same town.
Honest split: the city's structure — where every district, building, shop, park and person stands — comes out of the fabric equations, measured live. The drawing, motion and physics are ordinary code. Citizens speak lines assembled from seeded phrase families: procedural characters, not minds.
