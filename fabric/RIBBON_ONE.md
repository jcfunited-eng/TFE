# RIBBON ONE — COMMUNICATION
Design under the ratified model (fabric/MODEL.md), 2026-08-30.
Governed by the ten walls in FIRST_RIBBON.md — none is relaxed here.

## What it is under the model

The first program the fabric assembles: the one that understands
being told, answers, says the answer back in language, and asks its
own questions. Per the model, this ribbon is not a program anyone
writes per task — the LANGUAGE PHYLUM (building now, deep, holding
the ten walls as claims and the reading procedures as followable
METHODS) is the knowledge, and the ribbon's handler for any one
sentence is assembled by crossing it. The data — the sentence, its
particulars, the answer — rides the ribbon and leaves. Nothing of it
is stored.

Its means, named by Joe and mapped to mechanisms:
  syntax        grouping and nesting from the language phylum's laws
  cognition     the crossing: meaning composed from parts under
                constraint, then worked with
  thinking      internal ribbons running unprompted (re-walks,
                gap-noticing, proposals)
  discernment   consult the white before answering; know know from
                don't-know from can't-be
  self-aware    the fabric's phylum of itself records every crossing
  creativity    assembly never stored; possibilities offered from
                the white

## What already exists (the seed, measured)

- fabric/wanting.py — reads a sentence into a want: KIND (what
  would count as an answer, from the asking words), ABOUT, FORBIDDEN
  (constraints on the making), TURNS-ON (the doing, by contrast).
  Retrieves nothing; says which part it could not read.
- fabric/first_ribbon.py — grouping and nesting per wall 1 and 8.
- The one untiltable bench: told a task in plain English, the fabric
  finds its own written procedure and does the arithmetic — 16/22
  end to end at last measure.
- Bench law (earned the hard way, kept): hand labelling is
  forbidden; every bench runs through the shipping path; a case
  pruned to pass is a hand label in different clothes.

## The increments, each with a bench that cannot be tilted

1. UNDERSTAND — re-ground the want-reader on the language phylum
   (not the old numbered files), so the reading takes its frame,
   asking words, and procedures from the phylum's METHODS. Bench:
   the existing doing-test suite must not regress; unread parts must
   be NAMED, never filled.
2. ANSWER — want → living searches across the phylums → assembled
   answer. Bench: the doing test driven from 16/22 toward 22/22,
   and the making test on every answer: was the output already in
   the corpus before the question? (If yes, it was retrieval.)
3. SAY BACK — the answer composed into English by the same language
   knowledge run forward (composition, never templates, no stored
   sentences). Bench: round-trip — the ribbon's own reader must
   read the ribbon's sentence into the same want/claim structure it
   spoke from. Identity is arithmetic, not opinion.
4. ASK BACK — when a part is unread or nothing grips: the answer
   rule holds — "nothing is certain here, but here is a
   possibility" — and the ribbon asks its own question for the
   missing part. Bench: on a suite of deliberately incomplete
   tellings, the question asked must name exactly the unread part.
5. DISCERN — before answering, the crossing consults the white;
   a certified silence answers FROM the white; a new silence FILES
   to it with its why. Bench: re-asking a filed question must not
   re-walk the territory (the white is the speed).
6. RECORD — every crossing lands in the self-aware phylum: its
   existence, shape, outcome. Never its cargo. Bench: ask the
   fabric what it just did; the answer must come from that phylum
   by the same crossing machinery as any other question.

## The line it must never cross

No stored sentences. No ranking of entries. No sense fetched from a
table. No per-task handler written by hand. The moment any of those
appears, it is the turn-based chatbot again — stop and re-read the
walls.

## Order of work

The language phylum lands first (wave 2, running). Then increment 1
on top of it, then 2 through 6 in order, each behind its bench.
Nothing reported as working without its bench number stated.
