# THE LAWBOOK — laws the doing element walks with

Every law here DESCENDS from a floor in docs/fabric_phylums/ —
its parent is named. Laws are knowledge, not code: the one
walking rule in fabric_do.py reads them and lets them kill
candidates. No law is a routine; no task owns a law.

LAW column-count
  FROM: mathematics — "no column's sum may disagree with counting
  the pile one by one" (the bundles floor), standing on "no honest
  recount disagrees" (the counting floor).
  SAYS: in any column, the digits put in plus the carry brought in
  must equal the digit written down plus ten times the carry sent
  left. A digit that breaks this is dead on arrival.

LAW no-fat-place
  FROM: mathematics — "no place holds ten" (the bundles floor).
  SAYS: every written place holds 0 through 9, nothing else.

LAW times-is-copies
  FROM: mathematics — "no product escapes its addition" (the
  copies floor).
  SAYS: a times b is a laid down b times and counted as one pile.
  When the copies are many, the bundles floor lets them go down in
  bundles — each place of the multiplier lays its own copies,
  shifted to its place, and the piles are counted together.

LAW one-body-one-place
  FROM: physics — "two solids cannot share a space" (the
  exclusion floor).
  SAYS: one seat takes one guest; one guest takes one seat.
  Anything standing in two places, or two things in one, is dead.

LAW the-count-must-balance
  FROM: physics — "the universe keeps its books" (the conservation
  floor), through money's bookkeeping thread; and the counting
  floor's forced recount.
  SAYS: what the pieces count up to must equal what was asked for.
  A pile that counts to anything else is dead.

Instance papers (fabric/instances/) may declare their OWN cannots
— this party's feud, this purse's coins. The walker treats a
declared cannot and a floor-descended law identically: both kill.
The difference is honesty of parentage, and both are written down.
