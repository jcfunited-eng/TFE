ASKING: go more again think more about you

Answer — from my knowledge:
  (sports games) practice buys automation, and automation frees attention — the stroke rehearsed to self-running costs no thought, and the freed thought is what plays strategy; amateurs think about their feet, masters about the opponent.
  (teaching learning) skill climbs in stages — rules first (slow, spoken, one step at a time), then feel (the rules melt together), then flow (the doing runs wordless); no stage can be skipped.
