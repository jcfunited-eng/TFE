ASKING: what don't you know

Nothing I hold answers this directly. The nearest knowledge, in case it helps:
  (physics) the very small runs on chance constrained by law — until asked, a particle holds possibilities, not positions; the asking settles it.
  (sports games) home teams win more, everywhere ever measured (roughly 54-60% across sports) — the home side knows the bounce, the light, the wind, sleeps in its own bed, and the crowd leans on the referee's close calls.
  (law agreements) "I did not know it was forbidden" excuses no one — if ignorance excused, ignorance would become the thing to buy, and everyone would arrange to know nothing.
