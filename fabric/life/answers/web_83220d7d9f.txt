ASKING: take the physics you know and fix the things that are wrong

Nothing I hold answers this directly. The nearest knowledge, in case it helps:
  (law agreements) "I did not know it was forbidden" excuses no one — if ignorance excused, ignorance would become the thing to buy, and everyone would arrange to know nothing.
  (image design) depth on a flat page is drawn by shrinking — far things sit smaller and higher, parallel edges lean together toward a vanishing point, and the eye reads distance it knows is not there.
  (teaching learning) experts cannot remember not-knowing — once a thing turns obvious, the mind cannot simulate the student to whom it is not; the best knowers are often the worst explainers.
