What DSF-AI Detects in Battery Data

Cathode Phase Transitions

Detects ordering transitions in NMC, LFP, and other cathode materials during charge/discharge. Identifies the voltage plateaus where the crystal structure reorganizes.

Degradation Onset

Finds the cycle number or voltage where capacity fade begins accelerating. Early warning before the cell fails — analogous to finding precursors before a phase transition.

SEI Layer Changes

Detects impedance transitions associated with solid electrolyte interphase evolution during formation and aging. Identifies when the SEI destabilizes.

Thermal Stability

Analyzes DSC data from cathode materials to find exothermic decomposition onsets and thermal runaway precursors. Same analysis used in pharmaceutical polymorph screening.

Why Not Use an In-House Script?

Most battery labs have Python scripts for dQ/dV and basic peak finding. DSF-AI finds what those scripts miss:

  • Precursors — subtle changes before a transition that indicate the material is approaching failure
  • Broad transitions — gradual changes that don't produce sharp peaks in dQ/dV
  • Stability boundaries — where the data shifts from one behavior to another, even without an obvious transition
  • Consistency — automated detection removes human judgment from the analysis, making results reproducible across analysts

Questions

Is my data stored?
No. Your CSV is processed in memory and deleted immediately after analysis. We cannot recover your data or results.
Can I automate this with my cycling setup?
Yes — Academic and Industry plans include API access. POST a CSV to the API endpoint, get JSON results back. Integrate with your data pipeline.
How is this different from dQ/dV analysis?
dQ/dV finds peaks in the derivative. DSF-AI finds transitions and stability boundaries in the raw data — including gradual changes, precursors, and broad transitions that don't produce sharp dQ/dV features. The analysis method is proprietary.
What format does the data need to be in?
Two columns, CSV. Column 1 is the independent variable (voltage, cycle number, temperature), column 2 is the measurement (capacity, impedance, heat flow). Headers are ignored.
