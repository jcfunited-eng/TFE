Case Study: Battery Cycling Degradation Detection

DSF-AI identified the onset of accelerated capacity fade in lithium-ion cycling data — the "knee point" where the cell begins failing rapidly.

The Data

Capacity (mAh/g) vs. cycle number for a simulated NMC lithium-ion cell over 500 charge/discharge cycles. The cell exhibits three distinct aging phases: normal aging, accelerated degradation (first knee), and rapid failure (second knee).

What This Means

The "knee point" in battery cycling — where capacity fade suddenly accelerates — is one of the most important features in cell lifetime prediction. DSF-AI detected the transition from stable aging to rapid failure automatically, without any battery-specific parameters or models.

The VOLATILE region at the end of life indicates the cell is no longer degrading smoothly — it's becoming unstable. This is the kind of early warning that allows a battery management system to flag cells before they fail catastrophically.

Honest limitation: The first knee (gradual acceleration around cycle 150) was too subtle for the analysis to flag as a distinct transition — it appeared as a smooth curve change within the DEGENERATE region. The second knee (rapid failure) was detected clearly.
